S1 [apply]: Because the candle burns from 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM on the same day; and since for any candle x, any numbers s, e, d: if x burns from s:00 PM to e:00 PM on the same day and d = e - s, then x burns for a total of d hours: the candle burns for a total of 4 hours (5 - 1 = 4).
S2 [apply]: Because the candle melts by 2 centimeters for every hour that it burns; and the candle burns for a total of 4 hours; and since for any candle x, any numbers r, t, m: if x melts by r centimeters for every hour that it burns and x burns for a total of t hours and m = r × t, then x becomes m centimeters shorter over its total burning time: the candle becomes 8 centimeters shorter over its total burning time (2 x 4 = 8).
