### ## FACTS
- **Fact-1:** Bob is furry.
  
### ## RULES
- **Rule-1:** If someone is nice, then they are green.

### ## INFERENCES
- **Inference-1:** Bob is green :: (∀x (Nice(x) → Green(x))) ∧ Bob is furry.
  
This inference combines the given facts that Bob is furry and applies the rule that if someone is nice, then they are green. The conjunction ensures both conditions are met.
