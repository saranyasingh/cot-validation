```plaintext
- All cold, young people are white.
```

```plaintext
- If someone is furry and big then they are cold.
- If someone is big then they are furry.
```

#### **INFERENCES**

**Inference 1:** 
```plaintext
:: ALL(COLD AND YOUNG) -> WHITE
```
This inference is derived from the given fact: "All cold, young people are white."

**Inference 2:** 
```plaintext
:: IF BOB IS COLD THEN BOB IS YOUNG
```
This inference comes from applying the rule "If someone is furry and big then they are cold." Since Bob is cold, it follows that he must be big (because all cold people are big). And since all big people are furry (from the second rule), Bob must be furry.

**Conclusion:**
Since Bob is young (as inferred from his being both cold and young), the statement "Bob is not cold" contradicts the given facts. Therefore, the statement is false.
