### ## RULES

- **Rule 2:** All quiet, white things are rough.
- **Rule 3:** All white things are young.

### ## INFERENCES
- **Inference 1:** Since Charlie is big (Fact 1), and all quiet, white things are rough (Rule 2), Charlie cannot be white (contradiction).
