### CHAIN OF THOUGHT REASONING TO CONVERT

**FACTS**
- Harry is white.
- Harry is young.
- Harry is kind.

**RULES**
- If someone is furry and big then they are cold.
- If someone is big then they are furry.

**INFERENCES**
1. **Fact:** Harry is smart.
   - Reasoning: Harry is kind, which implies he could potentially be smart according to the rule "If someone is smart, then they are furry."

2. **Rule:** If someone is smart and furry, then they are fuzzy.
   - Reasoning: From Fact 1, we know Harry is smart. According to the rule, since Harry is smart and furry, he must be fuzzy.

Thus, based on the given information and rules, we can infer that Harry is fuzzy. Therefore, the answer is True.
