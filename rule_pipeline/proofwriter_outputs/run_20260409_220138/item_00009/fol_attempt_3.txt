### ## FACTS
- The bear is green.
- The bear needs the mouse.
- The bear needs the cow.
- The bear sees the cow.
- The bear sees the mouse.
- The mouse likes the cow.
- The mouse sees the rabbit.
- The rabbit is young.
- Young people are kind.

### ## RULES
- If someone is kind then they need the bear.
- If someone sees the cow then they are cold.
- If someone is cold then they like the rabbit.
- If someone needs the bear then the bear is young.
- If someone is round then they like the mouse.
- If someone likes the bear and they like the mouse then the bear likes the mouse.
- If someone likes the cow then the cow likes the bear.
- If someone is round and they do not need the bear then they see the rabbit.

### ## INFERENCES
- **Inferencing 1:** 
  - From Fact-1 and RULE-1: If the bear is green, then the bear needs the mouse.
  - From Fact-2 and RULE-2: If the bear needs the mouse, then the bear is round.
  - Combining these, we get: If the bear is green, then the bear is round and needs the mouse.
  
- **Inferencing 2:** 
  - From Inference 1: The bear is round and needs the mouse.
  - From RULE-3: If someone needs the bear, then the bear is young.
  - Therefore, the bear is young.
  
- **Inferencing 3:** 
  - From Fact-5 and RULE-5: The rabbit is young.
  - From RULE-4: If someone is cold then they like the rabbit.
  - Since the bear is young, it follows that the bear is cold.
  
- **Inferencing 4:** 
  - From Fact-4 and RULE-4: The bear sees the rabbit.
  - From Inference 3: The bear is cold.
  - From RULE-5: Young people are kind.
  - Therefore, the bear is kind.
