### ## RULES
- If someone is kind then they need the bear.
- If someone is round then they like the mouse.
- If someone likes the bear and they like the mouse then the bear likes the mouse.

### ## INFERENCES
- **Inference 1:** The mouse likes the bear.
  - From INF-1: The mouse likes the bear.

- **Inference 2:** The mouse does not need the bear.
  - From INF-2: The mouse does not need the bear.

- **Inference 3:** The mouse sees the rabbit.
  - From INF-3: If someone is round and they do not need the bear then they see the rabbit,
    - And from Rule 6: If someone is round and they do not need the bear then they see the rabbit,
      - Therefore, the mouse is round and does not need the bear, hence the mouse sees the rabbit.

- **Conclusion:** The mouse does not see the rabbit.
  - From the above inferences, we know the mouse sees the rabbit but does not need the bear, which contradicts the requirement for Rule 6 to apply. Hence, the mouse does not see the rabbit.
