### ## FACTS
- Anne is green.
- Bob is kind.

### ## RULES
- If someone is nice then they are green.
- If Bob is kind, then Bob is green.

### ## INFERENCES
- From Fact 2 (Anne is green) and Rule 5 (If someone is nice then they are green):
  - Conclusion: Anne is green.

- From Fact 3 (Bob is kind) and Rule 5:
  - Conclusion: Bob is green.

Therefore, the final inference is:
- Bob is green.
