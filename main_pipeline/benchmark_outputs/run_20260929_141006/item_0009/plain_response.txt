   - Time spent in third segment = 2 hours + 0.5 hours = 2.5 hours

   - Distance = Speed × Time = 80 mph × 1.5 hours = 120 miles

- First segment: 180 miles
- Second segment: 0 miles
- Third segment: 15 miles
- Fourth segment: 120 miles
