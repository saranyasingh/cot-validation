To determine how much Kylar needs to pay for 16 glasses, we first need to calculate the cost of each type of glass and then sum these costs.

1. The first glass costs $5.
2. Each subsequent glass costs 60% of the previous glass's price. So, the second glass will cost:
   \[
   5 \times 0.6 = 3 \text{ dollars}
   \]
3. Similarly, the third glass will cost:
   \[
   3 \times 0.6 = 1.8 \text{ dollars}
   \]
4. Following this pattern, the fourth glass will cost:
   \[
   1.8 \times 0.6 = 1.08 \text{ dollars}
   \]

Now, let's calculate the total cost:

- First glass: $5
- Second glass: $3
- Third glass: $1.8
- Fourth glass: $1.08

Summing these amounts gives us the total cost:
\[
5 + 3 + 1.8 + 1.08 = 10.88 \text{ dollars}
\]

Therefore, Kylar needs to pay $\boxed{10.88}$ dollars for all 16 glasses.
