EXPLANATION
To determine how much Kylar needs to pay for 16 glasses, we first need to calculate the cost per glass and then multiply that by the total number of glasses.

1. **Calculate the cost per glass**: The problem states that one glass costs $5.
2. **Determine the discounted price for every second glass**: Every second glass costs 60% of the original price. Since 60% is equivalent to 0.6 when converted to decimal form, this means the price of every second glass is \( \$5 \times 0.6 = \$3 \).
3. **Calculate the total cost for odd-numbered glasses (1, 3, 5, ..., 15)**: There are 8 odd-numbered glasses (1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13, 15). Each of these glasses costs $5. Therefore, the total cost for the odd-numbered glasses is \( 8 \times \$5 = \$40 \).
4. **Calculate the total cost for even-numbered glasses (2, 4, 6, ..., 16)**: There are also 8 even-numbered glasses (2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16). Each of these glasses costs $3. Therefore, the total cost for the even-numbered glasses is \( 8 \times \$3 = \$24 \).

Finally, add the total costs for both types of glasses to find the overall amount Kylar needs to pay:
\[ \$40 + \$24 = \$64 \]

Therefore, Kylar needs to pay $64 for 16 glasses. The answer is: 64.
