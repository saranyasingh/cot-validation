EXPLANATION
To determine Josh's profit from flipping the house, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the initial total cost**: 
   - Initial purchase cost: $80,000
   - Repair cost: $50,000
   - Total initial cost: $80,000 + $50,000 = $130,000

2. **Determine the new value of the house**:
   - The house's value increased by 150% due to repairs.
   - New value calculation: 
     \[
     \text{New value} = \text{Original value} \times (1 + \text{percentage increase})
     \]
     Here, the percentage increase is 150%, so:
     \[
     \text{New value} = \$80,000 \times (1 + 1.5) = \$80,000 \times 2.5 = \$200,000
     \]

3. **Calculate the profit**:
   - Profit is calculated as the difference between the final sale price and the total initial cost.
   - Sale price: $200,000
   - Total initial cost: $130,000
   - Profit: 
     \[
     \text{Profit} = \text{Sale price} - \text{Total initial cost}
     \]
     \[
     \text{Profit} = \$200,000 - \$130,000 = \$70,000
     \]

Therefore, the correct profit Josh made is **$70,000**.
