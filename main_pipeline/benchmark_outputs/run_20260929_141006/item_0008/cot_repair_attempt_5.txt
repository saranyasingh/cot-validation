EXPLANATION
To determine how long it will take Carla to download the 200 GB file after the restart, we need to break down the process into two parts:

1. **First 40% of the Download**:
   - Carla initially downloads at a rate of 2 GB per minute.
   - She covers 40% of the 200 GB file in 200 GB * 40% = 80 GB.
   - The time required to download the first 40% of the file is 80 GB / 2 GB/min = 40 minutes.

2. **Restarts and Remaining Download**:
   - After the restart, Carla starts over with the original speed of 2 GB per minute.
   - Since the restart resets Carla's progress, there is no reduction in her initial download speed.
   - There are now only 120 GB left to download after the restart (since 200 GB - 80 GB = 120 GB).
   - The time required to download the remaining 120 GB is 120 GB / 2 GB/min = 60 minutes.

Adding these times together gives the total download time:
\[ 40 \text{ minutes} + 60 \text{ minutes} = 100 \text{ minutes} \]

Therefore, the answer is: 100
