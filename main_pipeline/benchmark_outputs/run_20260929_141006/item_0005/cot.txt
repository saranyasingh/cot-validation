EXPLANATION

Let's break down the information given in the problem step-by-step.

Firstly, we know that Wendi feeds each of her 20 chickens 3 cups of mixed chicken feed per day. This means that the total amount of feed needed for all the chickens combined over the course of a day is calculated as follows:

Total daily feed = Number of chickens × Feed per chicken per day
= 20 × 3
= 60 cups

Next, we are informed about how much feed Wendi actually gives away during different parts of the day:

- In the morning, she gives 15 cups.
- In the afternoon, she gives 25 cups.

To find out how much feed is left after these two portions, we subtract the amounts given in the morning and afternoon from the total daily requirement:

Remaining feed = Total daily feed - (Morning feed + Afternoon feed)
= 60 - (15 + 25)
= 60 - 40
= 20 cups

Therefore, the remaining amount of feed that needs to be given to the chickens in the final meal of the day is 20 cups.

The answer is: 20
