To determine the total number of bolts of fiber needed to make a robe, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Identify the given information:**
   - A robe requires 2 bolts of blue fiber.
   - It also requires half as much white fiber.

2. **Calculate the amount of white fiber required:**
   - Since the amount of white fiber is half the amount of blue fiber, we calculate:
     \[
     \text{White fiber} = \frac{1}{2} \times \text{Blue fiber}
     \]
     Substituting the known value (2 bolts of blue fiber):
     \[
     \text{White fiber} = \frac{1}{2} \times 2 = 1 \text{ bolt}
     \]

3. **Sum up all the fibers needed:**
   - Total bolts of fiber = Blue fiber + White fiber
     \[
     \text{Total bolts} = 2 + 1 = 3 \text{ bolts}
     \]

**Explanation:**

- We started with the fact that the robe needs 2 bolts of blue fiber.
- Then, based on the condition that it needs half as much white fiber as blue fiber, we calculated how much white fiber is required.
- Finally, we added both quantities together to find out the total number of bolts needed.

Therefore, the total number of bolts of fiber needed to make the robe is **3**.
