   \[
   \text{Blue fiber} = 2 \text{ bolts}
   \]

2. **White Fiber**: The problem specifies that the robe uses half as much white fiber as blue fiber. Therefore, we need to find half of the blue fiber requirement.
   \[
   \text{White fiber} = \frac{\text{Blue fiber}}{2} = \frac{2 \text{ bolts}}{2} = 1 \text{ bolt}
   \]

3. **Total Bolts**: To find the total number of bolts needed for one robe, add the bolts of blue fiber and white fiber together.
   \[
   \text{Total bolts} = \text{Blue fiber} + \text{White fiber} = 2 \text{ bolts} + 1 \text{ bolt} = 3 \text{ bolts}
   \]
