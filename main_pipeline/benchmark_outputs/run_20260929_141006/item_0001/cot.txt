EXPLANATION

1. **First Premise:** Janet's ducks lay 16 eggs per day.
   - This establishes that there are a constant number of eggs available for sale throughout the day.

2. **Second Premise:** Janet eats three eggs for breakfast every morning.
   - This indicates an ongoing consumption of eggs during the day.

3. **Third Premise:** Janet bakes muffins for her friends using four eggs per day.
   - This suggests another continuous use of eggs outside of the main sales activity.

4. **Fourth Premise:** Janet sells the remaining eggs at the farmers' market for $2 per egg.
   - This specifies how the unsold eggs are sold and at what price.

5. **Fifth Premise:** The total number of eggs laid per day minus the ones eaten for breakfast minus the ones used to bake muffins equals the number of eggs left for sale.
   - This accounts for all the eggs not consumed or used in baking, which form the basis of the farmer's market sales.

Calculation:
- Total eggs laid per day = 16
- Eggs eaten for breakfast per day = 3
- Eggs used to bake muffins per day = 4
- Remaining eggs for sale per day = 16 - 3 - 4 = 9

Price per egg = $2

Total earnings per day = Number of eggs for sale × Price per egg
= 9 × $2 = $18

Therefore, Janet makes $18 every day at the farmers' market.

The answer is: 18
