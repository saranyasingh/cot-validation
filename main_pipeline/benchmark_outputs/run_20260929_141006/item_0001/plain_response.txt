To determine how much Janet makes every day at the farmers' market, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the total number of eggs laid each day.
2. Subtract the number of eggs eaten for breakfast.
3. Subtract the number of eggs used to bake muffins for her friends.
4. Determine the remaining number of eggs that can be sold at the farmers' market.
5. Calculate the earnings from selling the remaining eggs.

Let's go through each step:

1. **Total number of eggs laid each day**:
   \[
   16 \text{ eggs/day}
   \]

2. **Eggs eaten for breakfast**:
   \[
   3 \text{ eggs/day} 
   \]
   
3. **Eggs used to bake muffins**:
   \[
   4 \text{ eggs/day}
   \]

4. **Remaining eggs after eating for breakfast and baking muffins**:
   \[
   16 - 3 - 4 = 9 \text{ eggs/day}
   \]

5. **Earnings from selling the remaining eggs**:
   Each egg sells for $2, so:
   \[
   9 \text{ eggs} \times \$2/\text{egg} = \$18/\text{day}
   \]

Therefore, Janet makes \$18 every day at the farmers' market. 

The answer is: 18
