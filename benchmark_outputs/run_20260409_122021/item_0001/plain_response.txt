To determine how much Janet makes every day at the farmers' market, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the total number of eggs laid per day.
2. Subtract the number of eggs eaten for breakfast.
3. Subtract the number of eggs used to bake muffins.
4. Calculate the remaining eggs that can be sold.
5. Determine the revenue from selling the remaining eggs.

Let's go through each step in detail:

1. Total eggs laid per day = 16 eggs

2. Eggs eaten for breakfast = 3 eggs/day

   Remaining after breakfast = 16 - 3 = 13 eggs

3. Eggs used to bake muffins = 4 eggs/day

   Remaining after baking = 13 - 4 = 9 eggs

4. Price per egg = $2

   Revenue from selling the eggs = 9 eggs * $2/egg = $18

Therefore, Janet makes $18 every day at the farmers' market.

The answer is: 18
