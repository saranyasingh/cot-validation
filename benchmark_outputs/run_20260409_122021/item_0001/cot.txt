Explanation:

To determine how much Janet makes every day at the farmers' market, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate total daily production**: 
   - Janet's ducks lay 16 eggs per day.

2. **Subtract eggs eaten for breakfast**:
   - She eats 3 eggs for breakfast.
   - Eggs left after breakfast = Total eggs - Eggs eaten for breakfast
     \[
     16 - 3 = 13 \text{ eggs}
     \]

3. **Subtract eggs used for baking muffins**:
   - She uses 4 eggs to bake muffins for her friends.
   - Eggs left after baking = Eggs left after breakfast - Eggs used for baking muffins
     \[
     13 - 4 = 9 \text{ eggs}
     \]

4. **Determine earnings from selling the remaining eggs**:
   - Each egg sold brings in $2.
   - Total earnings from selling remaining eggs = Remaining eggs × Price per egg
     \[
     9 \times 2 = \$18
     \]

Therefore, Janet makes $\boxed{18}$ dollars every day at the farmers' market.
