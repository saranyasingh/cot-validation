To determine how many boxes of pizza Marie ordered, we need to calculate the total cost of the items she already bought and then subtract this from the total amount she paid.

First, let's calculate the cost of the chicken meal:
\[ \text{Cost of chicken meal} = \$12 \]

Next, let's calculate the cost of the 5 packs of milk:
\[ \text{Cost of milk} = 5 \times \$3 = \$15 \]

Then, let's calculate the cost of the 4 apples:
\[ \text{Cost of apples} = 4 \times \$1.50 = \$6 \]

Now, add up all these costs:
\[ \text{Total cost of chicken meal, milk, and apples} = \$12 + \$15 + \$6 = \$33 \]

Marie paid a total of $50, so we can find out the cost of the pizzas by subtracting the cost of the other items from the total amount:
\[ \text{Total cost of pizzas} = \$50 - \$33 = \$17 \]

Since each box of pizza costs $8.50, we divide the total cost of the pizzas by the price per box to find out how many boxes Marie ordered:
\[ \text{Number of boxes of pizza} = \frac{\$17}{\$8.50} = 2 \]

Therefore, Marie ordered 2 boxes of pizza. The answer is: 2.
