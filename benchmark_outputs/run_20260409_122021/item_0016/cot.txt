To determine the profit maximization strategy, we need to compare the future values of both purchases after accounting for their respective expected increases.

**Premise 1:** The merchant has two options - purchasing jewelry worth $5,000 or electronic gadgets worth $8,000.
- **Premise 2:** The jewelry market is expected to increase by 2.5%.
- **Premise 3:** The electronic gadgets market is expected to increase by 1.2%.

We calculate the future value of each option based on these expected increases.

**Calculation for Jewelry:**
Future Value = Current Value * (1 + Rate of Increase)
\[ \text{Future Value}_{\text{jewelry}} = \$5,000 \times (1 + 0.025) \]
\[ \text{Future Value}_{\text{jewelry}} = \$5,000 \times 1.025 \]
\[ \text{Future Value}_{\text{jewelry}} = \$5,125 \]

**Calculation for Electronic Gadgets:**
Future Value = Current Value * (1 + Rate of Increase)
\[ \text{Future Value}_{\text{gadgets}} = \$8,000 \times (1 + 0.012) \]
\[ \text{Future Value}_{\text{gadgets}} = \$8,000 \times 1.012 \]
\[ \text{Future Value}_{\text{gadgets}} = \$8,096 \]

Now, let's find the difference in their future values to determine which investment provides more profit.

**Profit Difference Calculation:**
\[ \text{Profit} = \text{Future Value}_{\text{gadgets}} - \text{Future Value}_{\text{jewelry}} \]
\[ \text{Profit} = \$8,096 - \$5,125 \]
\[ \text{Profit} = \$2,971 \]

Therefore, the merchant should choose the electronic gadgets to maximize his profit at the end of this month by making a choice, resulting in an additional profit of $2,971 compared to purchasing jewelry.
