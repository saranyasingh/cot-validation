## FACTS
- Premise 1: The merchant has two options - purchasing jewelry worth $5,000 or electronic gadgets worth $8,000.
- Premise 2: The jewelry market is expected to increase by 2.5%.
- Premise 3: The electronic gadgets market is expected to increase by 1.2%.

## RULES
- Future Value = Current Value * (1 + Rate of Increase)

## INFERENCES
- Future Value_{jewelry} = $5,000 * (1 + 0.025)
- Future Value_{jewelry} = $5,000 * 1.025
- Future Value_{jewelry} = $5,125
- Future Value_{gadgets} = $8,000 * (1 + 0.012)
- Future Value_{gadgets} = $8,000 * 1.012
- Future Value_{gadgets} = $8,096
- Profit = Future Value_{gadgets} - Future Value_{jewelry}
- Profit = $8,096 - $5,125
- Profit = $2,971
