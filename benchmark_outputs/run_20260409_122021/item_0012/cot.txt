EXPLANATION

1. **First Premise**: Toula bought 3 dozen donuts at $68 per dozen.
   - This gives us the cost for the donuts.

2. **Second Premise**: She also bought 2 dozen mini cupcakes at $80 per dozen.
   - This gives us the cost for the cupcakes.

3. **Third Premise**: Additionally, she bought 6 dozen mini cheesecakes at $55 per dozen.
   - This gives us the cost for the cheesecakes.

4. **Fourth Premise**: To find the total cost, we need to add up all these individual costs.

**Step-by-Step Calculation**

- Cost of donuts: \( 3 \times 68 = \$204 \)
- Cost of cupcakes: \( 2 \times 80 = \$160 \)
- Cost of cheesecakes: \( 6 \times 55 = \$330 \)

Now, summing these amounts together:

\[
\$204 + \$160 + \$330 = \$794
\]

Therefore, the total cost for all the pastries is **$794**.

THE ANSWER IS: 794
