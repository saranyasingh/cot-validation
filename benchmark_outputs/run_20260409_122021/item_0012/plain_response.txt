To calculate the total cost, we need to multiply the quantity of each item by its respective price and then sum up all the costs.

1. Donut cost:
   - Toula bought 3 dozen donuts at $68 per dozen.
   - Cost = 3 * $68 = $204

2. Mini Cupcake cost:
   - Toula bought 2 dozen mini cupcakes at $80 per dozen.
   - Cost = 2 * $80 = $160

3. Mini Cheesecake cost:
   - Toula bought 6 dozen mini cheesecakes at $55 per dozen.
   - Cost = 6 * $55 = $330

Now, let's add these amounts together:

Total cost = Cost of donuts + Cost of mini cupcakes + Cost of mini cheesecakes
           = $204 + $160 + $330
           = $694

Therefore, the total cost was $694.

The answer is: $694
