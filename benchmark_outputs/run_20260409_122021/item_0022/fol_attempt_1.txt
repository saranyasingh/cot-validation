### RULES
RULE-1: If someone becomes a father at an age x, they were x years old when their child was born :: ∀x (Fathered(x) → ChildBornAtAge(x))

RULE-2: If someone has a child at an age y, the child was y years old when the parent was x :: ∀y (ChildBornAtAge(y,x) → ParentWasBornAtAge(y,x))
