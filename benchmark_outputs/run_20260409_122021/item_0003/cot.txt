EXPLANATION
First, we need to determine the original cost of the house after all expenses have been paid. Josh bought the house for $80,000 but spent an additional $50,000 on repairs. Therefore, the total initial investment was:
\[ \text{Initial Investment} = \$80,000 + \$50,000 = \$130,000 \]

Next, we calculate the increase in value due to the renovations. The problem states that the house's value increased by 150% of its original purchase price ($80,000). To find this amount, we multiply the original purchase price by 150%, which is equivalent to multiplying by 2.5 (since 150% = 1.5):
\[ \text{Increase in Value} = \$80,000 \times 2.5 = \$200,000 \]

Now, we add the increase in value to the initial investment to find the total profit:
\[ \text{Total Profit} = \text{Initial Investment} + \text{Increase in Value} = \$130,000 + \$200,000 = \$330,000 \]

The answer is: 330000
