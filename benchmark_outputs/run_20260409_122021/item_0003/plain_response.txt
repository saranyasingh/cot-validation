To determine Josh's profit from flipping the house, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the new value of the house after the renovations.
2. Determine the total cost incurred (initial purchase price + repair costs).
3. Subtract the total cost from the new value of the house to find the profit.

Step 1: Calculate the new value of the house after the renovations.
- The initial purchase price was $80,000.
- The house increased in value by 150% due to the renovations.
- To find 150% of $80,000:
  \[
  150\% \text{ of } \$80,000 = 1.5 \times \$80,000 = \$120,000
  \]
- Adding this increase to the original purchase price gives:
  \[
  \$80,000 + \$120,000 = \$200,000
  \]

Step 2: Determine the total cost incurred.
- Initial purchase price: $80,000
- Repair costs: $50,000
- Total cost:
  \[
  \$80,000 + \$50,000 = \$130,000
  \]

Step 3: Calculate the profit.
- New value of the house: $200,000
- Total cost: $130,000
- Profit:
  \[
  \$200,000 - \$130,000 = \$70,000
  \]

Therefore, the profit Josh made from the house flip is $\boxed{\$70,000}$.
