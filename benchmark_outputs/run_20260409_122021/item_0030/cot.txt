EXPLANATION

1. **Premise 1:** Gloria has to choose between the boots and two pairs of high heels.
2. **Premise 2:** Together, the two pairs of high heels cost five dollars less than the boots.
3. **Premise 3:** One pair of heels costs $33.
4. **Premise 4:** The other pair of heels costs twice as much as the first.

Now let's use these premises to find out the price of the boots.

5. **Step 1:** Calculate the total cost of the two pairs of high heels.
   - Cost of first pair: $33
   - Cost of second pair: \(2 \times 33 = 66\)
   - Total cost of both pairs: \(33 + 66 = 99\) dollars

6. **Step 2:** According to Premise 2, this total (99) is five dollars less than the cost of the boots.
7. **Step 3:** Let the cost of the boots be denoted as \(B\).
8. **Step 4:** Therefore, we have the equation: \(B - 5 = 99\).

Solving for \(B\):

9. **Step 5:** Add 5 to both sides of the equation:
   \[
   B - 5 + 5 = 99 + 5 \\
   B = 104
   \]

Thus, the boots cost $104.

The answer is: 104
