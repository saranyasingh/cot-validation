Let's denote the price of the boots as \( B \).

According to the problem, the total cost of both pairs of heels is $5 less than the cost of the boots. So we can write this relationship as:
\[ 33 + 2 \times 33 = B - 5 \]

Simplifying the left side:
\[ 33 + 66 = B - 5 \]
\[ 99 = B - 5 \]

Now, solving for \( B \):
\[ B = 99 + 5 \]
\[ B = 104 \]

Therefore, the boots cost $104.
