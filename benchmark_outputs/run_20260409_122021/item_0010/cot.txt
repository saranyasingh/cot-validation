To determine Eliza's total earnings for the week, we need to break down her work into two parts: regular hours and overtime.

1. **Regular Hours (First 40 hours):**
   - Regular rate = $10/hour
   - Number of regular hours = 40 hours
   - Earnings from regular hours = \( 40 \times 10 = \$400 \)

2. **Overtime Hours:**
   - Overtime rate = \( 10 \times 1.2 = \$12 \) per hour
   - Number of overtime hours = Total hours worked - Regular hours
     = 45 - 40 = 5 hours
   - Earnings from overtime hours = \( 5 \times 12 = \$60 \)

3. **Total Earnings:**
   - Total earnings = Earnings from regular hours + Earnings from overtime hours
     = \( 400 + 60 = \$460 \)

Therefore, Eliza's total earnings for the week are \$460.
