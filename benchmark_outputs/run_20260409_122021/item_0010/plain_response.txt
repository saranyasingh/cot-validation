To calculate Eliza's earnings for the week, we need to break down her work into two parts: regular hours and overtime hours.

1. **Regular Hours (First 40 Hours):**
   - Regular rate = $10/hour
   - Number of regular hours = 40 hours

   Earnings from regular hours:
   \[
   \text{Earnings} = \text{Rate} \times \text{Hours}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Earnings from regular hours} = \$10 \times 40 = \$400
   \]

2. **Overtime Hours (Above 40 Hours):**
   - Overtime rate = \(1.2 \times \text{Regular Rate}\)
   - Overtime rate = \(1.2 \times \$10 = \$12\) per hour
   - Number of overtime hours = \(45 - 40 = 5\) hours

   Earnings from overtime hours:
   \[
   \text{Earnings from overtime hours} = \text{Overtime Rate} \times \text{Overtime Hours}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Earnings from overtime hours} = \$12 \times 5 = \$60
   \]

3. **Total Earnings:**
   Sum up the earnings from regular and overtime hours.
   \[
   \text{Total Earnings} = \text{Earnings from regular hours} + \text{Earnings from overtime hours}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Total Earnings} = \$400 + \$60 = \$460
   \]

Therefore, Eliza's total earnings for the week are $\boxed{\$460}$.
