EXPLANATION
To determine how much Kylar needs to pay for 16 glasses, we first need to calculate the cost of each glass based on the given pricing structure.

Step 1: Calculate the cost of the first glass.
- Each glass costs $5.

Step 2: Determine the cost of the second glass.
- Every second glass costs 60% of the original price.
- 60% of $5 = 0.60 * $5 = $3.

Step 3: Sum up the total cost for all 16 glasses.
- For the first 8 glasses (odd-numbered positions), each costs $5.
- For the remaining 8 glasses (even-numbered positions), each costs $3.
- Total cost for odd-numbered glasses = 8 * $5 = $40.
- Total cost for even-numbered glasses = 8 * $3 = $24.
- Grand total = $40 + $24 = $64.

Therefore, Kylar needs to pay $64 for the 16 glasses.
