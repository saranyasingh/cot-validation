Explanation:
To determine how much Cynthia will spend on ice cream after 60 days, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the total number of servings of ice cream Cynthia consumes in 60 days.
2. Determine how many cartons of ice cream she needs based on the number of servings per carton.
3. Calculate the total cost of the cartons.

Step 1: Calculate the total number of servings of ice cream consumed in 60 days.
- Cynthia eats 1 serving of ice cream every night.
- There are 60 days in the period.
- Therefore, the total number of servings = 1 service/night * 60 nights = 60 servings.

Step 2: Determine how many cartons of ice cream are needed.
- Each carton contains 15 servings.
- To find out how many cartons are needed, divide the total number of servings by the number of servings per carton.
- Number of cartons = Total servings / Servings per carton = 60 servings / 15 servings/carton = 4 cartons.

Step 3: Calculate the total cost of the cartons.
- Each carton costs $4.00.
- Since she needs 4 cartons, multiply the number of cartons by the cost per carton.
- Total cost = Cost per carton * Number of cartons = $4.00/carton * 4 cartons = $16.00.

Therefore, the answer is: 16
