To determine how much Cynthia will spend on ice cream after 60 days, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the total number of servings of ice cream Cynthia consumes in 60 days.
2. Determine how many cartons of ice cream she needs to buy.
3. Calculate the total cost based on the number of cartons needed and the price per carton.

Step 1: Calculate the total number of servings of ice cream consumed in 60 days.
- Cynthia eats 1 serving of ice cream every night.
- There are 60 days.
Total servings = 1 serving/night * 60 nights = 60 servings

Step 2: Determine how many cartons of ice cream are needed.
- Each carton contains 15 servings.
Number of cartons = Total servings / Servings per carton = 60 servings / 15 servings/carton = 4 cartons

Step 3: Calculate the total cost.
- The cost per carton is $4.00.
Total cost = Number of cartons * Cost per carton = 4 cartons * $4.00/carton = $16.00

Therefore, the answer is: $16.00.
