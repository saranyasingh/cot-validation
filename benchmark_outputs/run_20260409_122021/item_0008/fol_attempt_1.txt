### Explanation

2. **Normal Download Rate**: Carla’s normal download rate is 2 GB per minute.
3. **First Phase of Download**: Carla downloads at her normal speed until she has completed 40% of the file. To find out how long this took, we calculate 40% of 200 GB and divide by her download rate.
4. **Restart Due to Update Installation**: There is an additional delay of 20 minutes after resuming the download.
5. **Second Phase of Download**: After the delay, Carla starts again at her initial download rate but now needs to download the remaining portion of the file, which is 120 GB. Again, we calculate the duration needed to download this amount.
6. **Total Time Calculation**: Adding up all these durations gives us the total time taken to download the file.
