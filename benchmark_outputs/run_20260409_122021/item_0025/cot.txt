EXPLANATION
To determine the original price of the book before the discount, we need to work backwards from the sale price. Here’s how we can do it using logical steps.

Step 1: Identify the sale price.
The sale price Kyle paid is $19.50 after a 25% discount.

Step 2: Calculate the amount saved on the original price.
Since there was a 25% discount, this means that $19.50 represents 75% (or 100% - 25%) of the original price. We can set up an equation to find the original price \( P \) as follows:
\[ 0.75P = 19.50 \]

Step 3: Solve for the original price \( P \).
To isolate \( P \), divide both sides of the equation by 0.75:
\[ P = \frac{19.50}{0.75} \]
\[ P = 26 \]

Therefore, the original price of the book before any discount was applied was $26.
