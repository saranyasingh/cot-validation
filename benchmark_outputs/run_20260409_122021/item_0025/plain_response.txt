To find the original price of the book before the discount, we can use the relationship between the discounted price, the original price, and the discount rate. Let's denote:

- \( P \) as the original price,
- \( D \) as the discount amount (which is 25% or 0.25 in decimal form),
- \( CP \) as the cost price after applying the discount ($19.50).

Given that Kyle paid $19.50 after a 25% discount, this means he paid 75% of the original price because 100% - 25% = 75%. We can express this mathematically as:
\[ CP = P \times (1 - D) \]
where \( D = 0.25 \). Substituting the known values into the equation gives us:
\[ 19.50 = P \times (1 - 0.25) \]
\[ 19.50 = P \times 0.75 \]

Now, to solve for \( P \), we divide both sides of the equation by 0.75:
\[ P = \frac{19.50}{0.75} \]
\[ P = 26 \]

Therefore, the original price of the book was $26. The answer is: 26.
