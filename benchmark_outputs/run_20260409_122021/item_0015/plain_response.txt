To determine the percentage of students enrolled in hip-hop dance, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the number of students enrolled in contemporary dance.
2. Determine the number of students who did not enroll in contemporary dance.
3. Calculate the number of students enrolled in jazz dance from those who did not take contemporary dance.
4. Find out how many students remain for hip-hop dance enrollment.
5. Finally, calculate the percentage of students enrolled in hip-hop dance.

Let's go through each step:

1. The number of students enrolled in contemporary dance:
\[ 20\% \text{ of } 20 = \frac{20}{100} \times 20 = 4 \]

2. The number of students who did not enroll in contemporary dance:
\[ 20 - 4 = 16 \]

3. The number of students enrolled in jazz dance:
\[ 25\% \text{ of } 16 = \frac{25}{100} \times 16 = 4 \]

4. The number of students remaining after accounting for contemporary and jazz dance enrollments:
\[ 16 - 4 = 12 \]

5. The percentage of students enrolled in hip-hop dance:
\[ \left( \frac{12}{20} \right) \times 100 = 60\% \]

Therefore, the percentage of students enrolled in hip-hop dance is \(60\%\).
