To determine the percentage of students enrolled in hip-hop dance, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the number of students enrolled in contemporary dance:**
   - Total students = 20
   - Percentage enrolled in contemporary dance = 20%
   \[
   \text{Number of contemporary dancers} = 20\% \times 20 = 0.20 \times 20 = 4
   \]

2. **Determine the number of students remaining after contemporary dance enrollment:**
   - Students remaining after contemporary dance = Total students - Number of contemporary dancers
   \[
   \text{Students remaining} = 20 - 4 = 16
   \]

3. **Calculate the number of students enrolled in jazz dance:**
   - Percentage of remaining students enrolled in jazz dance = 25%
   \[
   \text{Number of jazz dancers} = 25\% \times 16 = 0.25 \times 16 = 4
   \]

4. **Determine the number of students enrolled in hip-hop dance:**
   - Students remaining after both contemporary and jazz dance enrollments = Students remaining - Number of jazz dancers
   \[
   \text{Students remaining after jazz} = 16 - 4 = 12
   \]
   - Since there are no other categories mentioned, all remaining students are enrolled in hip-hop dance.

5. **Calculate the percentage of students enrolled in hip-hop dance:**
   - Percentage of hip-hop dancers = (Number of hip-hop dancers / Total students) × 100%
   \[
   \text{Percentage of hip-hop dancers} = \left(\frac{12}{20}\right) \times 100\% = 0.60 \times 100\% = 60\%
   \]

**Explanation:**

- We started by calculating how many students were enrolled in contemporary dance.
- Then, we found out how many students remained after that group was accounted for.
- Next, we calculated how many of those remaining students were enrolled in jazz dance.
- Finally, since all remaining students were enrolled in hip-hop dance, we determined their percentage relative to the total student body.

Therefore, the answer is: 60
