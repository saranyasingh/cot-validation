To determine how many centimeters shorter a candle will be after burning from 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM, we need to calculate the total time the candle burns during this period.

### Step-by-Step Logical Reasoning:

**Premise 1:** A candle melts by 2 centimeters every hour.
This establishes the rate at which the candle melts per hour.

**Premise 2:** We want to know how much shorter the candle will be after burning from 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM.
We need to find out the duration of the burn.

**Premise 3:** From 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM is a span of 4 hours (from 1 pm to 2 pm, then from 2 pm to 3 pm, then from 3 pm to 4 pm, and finally from 4 pm to 5 pm).
This provides us with the total time the candle will be burning.

**Premise 4:** If the candle melts by 2 centimeters every hour, then over 4 hours, the total melting would be \( 2 \text{ cm/hour} \times 4 \text{ hours} = 8 \text{ cm}\).
This calculates the total amount of wax melted over the entire burning period.

**Conclusion:** Therefore, after burning from 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM, the candle will be 8 centimeters shorter than its original length.

**Answer:** 8
