To determine how many centimeters shorter the candle will be after burning from 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM, we need to calculate the total time the candle has been burning and then use this information to find out how much of its original height has melted away.

First, let's figure out the duration of the candle burning:
- From 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM is a span of 4 hours (since 5:00 PM is one full hour after 1:00 PM).

Next, we know that the candle melts at a rate of 2 centimeters per hour. Therefore, over 4 hours, the amount of the candle that has melted can be calculated as follows:

\[ \text{Total melting} = \text{Melting rate} \times \text{Time} \]
\[ \text{Total melting} = 2 \, \text{cm/hour} \times 4 \, \text{hours} \]
\[ \text{Total melting} = 8 \, \text{cm} \]

So, the candle will be 8 centimeters shorter after burning from 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM.

The answer is: 8
