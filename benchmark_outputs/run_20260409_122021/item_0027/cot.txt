EXPLANATION
To determine how much Mishka spent on all the clothing items, we need to calculate the total cost for each type of clothing separately and then sum these amounts.

First, let's find out how much Mishka spent on shorts.
- Mishka bought 3 pairs of shorts at $16.50 per pair.
- Therefore, the total cost for shorts = \( 3 \times \$16.50 = \$49.50 \).

Next, let's calculate the cost for pants.
- Mishka bought 3 pairs of pants at $22.50 per pair.
- Thus, the total cost for pants = \( 3 \times \$22.50 = \$67.50 \).

Now, let's find the cost for shoes.
- Mishka bought 3 pairs of shoes at $42.00 per pair.
- Hence, the total cost for shoes = \( 3 \times \$42.00 = \$126.00 \).

Finally, we add up the totals for shorts, pants, and shoes to get the overall amount Mishka spent.
- Total cost = Cost of shorts + Cost of pants + Cost of shoes
- Total cost = \$49.50 + \$67.50 + \$126.00

Adding these amounts together:
\[ \$49.50 + \$67.50 + \$126.00 = \$243.00 \]

Therefore, Mishka spent a total of **\$243.00** on all the clothing items.
