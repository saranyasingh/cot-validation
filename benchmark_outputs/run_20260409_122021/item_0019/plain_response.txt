To determine how many dozen eggs Claire eats in 4 weeks, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the number of eggs Claire eats each day.
2. Determine the total number of days in 4 weeks.
3. Find out how many dozen eggs that corresponds to.

Step 1: Number of eggs per day
Claire makes a 3-egg omelet every morning.

Step 2: Total number of days in 4 weeks
There are 7 days in a week, so:
\[ 4 \text{ weeks} \times 7 \text{ days/week} = 28 \text{ days} \]

Step 3: Convert the total number of eggs into dozens
Since there are 12 eggs in a dozen:
\[ \frac{28 \text{ eggs}}{12 \text{ eggs/dozen}} = 2.33 \text{ dozens} \]

Therefore, Claire eats approximately 2.33 dozen eggs in 4 weeks.

The answer is: 2.33
