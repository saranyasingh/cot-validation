EXPLANATION
To determine how many dozen eggs Claire eats in 4 weeks, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Determine the number of days in 4 weeks**: There are 7 days in a week, so in 4 weeks there are \(4 \times 7 = 28\) days.

2. **Calculate the total number of eggs eaten in 28 days**: Since Claire makes a 3-egg omelet every day, over 28 days she will make \(28 \times 3 = 84\) eggs.

3. **Convert the total number of eggs into dozens**: There are 12 eggs in a dozen. Therefore, to find out how many dozens of eggs she eats, we divide the total number of eggs by 12: \( \frac{84}{12} = 7 \) dozens.

**Conclusion**: Claire will eat 7 dozen eggs in 4 weeks.

The answer is: 7
