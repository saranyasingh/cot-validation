EXPLANATION

1. Let's denote the total number of vacuum cleaners Melanie started with as \( T \).
2. According to the problem, Melanie sold a third of her vacuum cleaners at the green house.
3. This means that after selling to the green house, she had two-thirds of her initial amount left.
4. After selling to the green house, she then sold an additional 2 vacuum cleaners to the red house.
5. Next, she sold half of the remaining vacuum cleaners at the orange house.
6. We know that after all these transactions, Melanie had 5 vacuum cleaners left.

Now let's translate this into a series of equations based on our steps:

- Initially, \( \frac{2}{3}T = \text{remaining after green house sale} \)
- Then, \( \frac{2}{3}T - 2 = \text{remaining after red house sale} \)
- Finally, \( \left( \frac{2}{3}T - 2 \right) - \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{2}{3}T - 2\right) = 5 \)

Simplifying the equation:
\[ \left( \frac{2}{3}T - 2 \right) - \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{2}{3}T - 2\right) = 5 \]
\[ \frac{2}{3}T - 2 - \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{2}{3}T + \frac{1}{2} \cdot 2 = 5 \]
\[ \frac{2}{3}T - \frac{1}{3}T - 2 + 1 = 5 \]
\[ \frac{1}{3}T - 1 = 5 \]

Adding 1 to both sides:
\[ \frac{1}{3}T = 6 \]

Multiplying both sides by 3:
\[ T = 18 \]

Therefore, Melanie started with 18 vacuum cleaners.

The answer is: 18
