Explanation:

1. John drives for 3 hours at 60 mph.
   - Distance covered = Speed × Time = 60 mph × 3 hours = 180 miles.

2. John stops due to forgetting something important at home.
   - No distance covered during this period.

3. John starts his return trip after realizing he forgot something.
   - Total time available = 4 hours (but he already spent some time on the way).

4. For the first 2 hours, he experiences standstill traffic.
   - Distance covered = 0 miles (since it's standstill).

5. He then drives at a speed of 30 mph for half an hour.
   - Distance covered = Speed × Time = 30 mph × 0.5 hours = 15 miles.

6. After that, he drives at a speed of 80 mph for the remaining 2 hours.
   - Distance covered = Speed × Time = 80 mph × 2 hours = 160 miles.

7. Summing up all distances:
   - Initial 3 hours at 60 mph: 180 miles
   - Standstill traffic for 2 hours: 0 miles
   - Driving at 30 mph for 0.5 hours: 15 miles
   - Driving at 80 mph for 2 hours: 160 miles

Total distance = 180 + 0 + 15 + 160 = 355 miles.

Answer: 355
