To determine how many cups of feed Wendi needs for the final meal, we first need to calculate the total amount of feed given over the course of the day. We know that:

- Each chicken gets 3 cups of feed per day.
- There are 20 chickens in total.

First, let's find out the daily feed requirement for all the chickens:
\[ \text{Total feed for one chicken per day} = 3 \text{ cups} \]
\[ \text{Number of chickens} = 20 \]

So, the total feed required for all the chickens per day is:
\[ \text{Total feed per day} = 3 \times 20 = 60 \text{ cups} \]

Next, we sum up the feed given during the morning and afternoon sessions:
- Morning feed: 15 cups
- Afternoon feed: 25 cups

Adding these together gives us the total feed given so far:
\[ \text{Total feed already given} = 15 + 25 = 40 \text{ cups} \]

Now, subtract this from the total daily requirement to find out how much more feed is needed for the final meal:
\[ \text{Feed needed for the final meal} = 60 - 40 = 20 \text{ cups} \]

Therefore, Wendi needs to give her chickens 20 cups of feed in the final meal of the day. The answer is: 20.
