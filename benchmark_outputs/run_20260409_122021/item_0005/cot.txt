EXPLANATION

To determine how much feed Wendi needs for her chickens in the final meal of the day, we first calculate the total amount of feed given to all the chickens throughout the day.

1. **Total Feed Given in the Morning**: Wendi gives 15 cups of feed to all 20 chickens.
   \[
   \text{Total morning feed} = 15 \times 20 = 300 \text{ cups}
   \]

2. **Total Feed Given in the Afternoon**: Wendi gives 25 cups of feed to all 20 chickens.
   \[
   \text{Total afternoon feed} = 25 \times 20 = 500 \text{ cups}
   \]

3. **Total Feed Given Throughout the Day**: We add the morning and afternoon amounts together.
   \[
   \text{Total daily feed} = 300 + 500 = 800 \text{ cups}
   \]

4. **Feed per Chicken per Meal**: Each chicken receives 3 cups of feed per meal.
   \[
   \text{Feed per chicken per meal} = 3 \text{ cups}
   \]

5. **Number of Meals per Chickens**: Since there are 20 chickens, they receive their feed in three separate meals (morning, afternoon, evening).
   \[
   \text{Meals per chicken} = 3
   \]

6. **Total Feed Per Chicken for the Day**: Multiply the feed per chicken per meal by the number of meals per chicken.
   \[
   \text{Total feed per chicken for the day} = 3 \times 3 = 9 \text{ cups}
   \]

7. **Total Feed Needed for All Chickens**: Finally, multiply the total feed per chicken for the day by the number of chickens.
   \[
   \text{Total feed needed for all chickens} = 9 \times 20 = 180 \text{ cups}
   \]

Therefore, Wendi needs to give her chickens 180 cups of feed in the final meal of the day.

The answer is: 180
