To determine Jill's annual salary, we need to calculate her earnings from both teaching and coaching separately and then sum them up.

**Premise 1:** Jill earns $20 per hour for teaching.
- This is the rate at which Jill earns money while teaching.

**Premise 2:** Jill works 35 hours per week as a teacher.
- This gives us the total number of hours she spends working as a teacher each year.

**Premise 3:** She works 50 weeks a year.
- This provides the total number of weeks in a year that she works as a teacher.

**Premise 4:** Jill earns $30 per hour for being a cheerleader coach.
- This is the rate at which Jill earns money while coaching.

**Premise 5:** Jill works 15 hours per week as a cheerleader coach.
- This gives us the total number of hours she spends working as a cheerleader coach each year.

**Premise 6:** There are 52 weeks in a year.
- This is a general fact about time, not directly related to this calculation but useful for understanding the timeframe.

Now let's break down the calculations for her annual earnings from teaching and coaching.

### Earnings from Teaching
First, calculate the weekly earnings from teaching:
\[ \text{Weekly earnings from teaching} = \text{Hourly wage} \times \text{Hours worked per week} \]
\[ \text{Weekly earnings from teaching} = \$20 \times 35 \]
\[ \text{Weekly earnings from teaching} = \$700 \]

Next, calculate the annual earnings from teaching:
\[ \text{Annual earnings from teaching} = \text{Weekly earnings from teaching} \times \text{Number of weeks worked per year} \]
\[ \text{Annual earnings from teaching} = \$700 \times 50 \]
\[ \text{Annual earnings from teaching} = \$35,000 \]

### Earnings from Coaching
First, calculate the weekly earnings from coaching:
\[ \text{Weekly earnings from coaching} = \text{Hourly wage} \times \text{Hours worked per week} \]
\[ \text{Weekly earnings from coaching} = \$30 \times 15 \]
\[ \text{Weekly earnings from coaching} = \$450 \]

Next, calculate the annual earnings from coaching:
\[ \text{Annual earnings from coaching} = \text{Weekly earnings from coaching} \times \text{Number of weeks worked per year} \]
\[ \text{Annual earnings from coaching} = \$450 \times 50 \]
\[ \text{Annual earnings from coaching} = \$22,500 \]

### Total Annual Salary
Finally, add the annual earnings from teaching and coaching to get the total annual salary:
\[ \text{Total annual salary} = \text{Annual earnings from teaching} + \text{Annual earnings from coaching} \]
\[ \text{Total annual salary} = \$35,000 + \$22,500 \]
\[ \text{Total annual salary} = \$57,500 \]

Thus, Jill's annual salary is **$57,500**.
