To calculate Jill's annual salary, we need to determine how much she earns from each job separately and then sum them up.

First, let's calculate her earnings from teaching:
- She teaches for 50 weeks a year.
- She works 35 hours a week.
- Her hourly rate for teaching is $20.

Earnings from teaching = Weekly teaching hours * Hourly rate * Weeks worked in a year
= 35 hours/week * $20/hour * 50 weeks/year
= $7,000

Next, let's calculate her earnings from being a cheerleader coach:
- She coaches for 50 weeks a year.
- She works 15 hours a week.
- Her hourly rate for coaching is $30.

Earnings from coaching = Weekly coaching hours * Hourly rate * Weeks worked in a year
= 15 hours/week * $30/hour * 50 weeks/year
= $4,500

Now, add both earnings together to find the total annual salary:

Total annual salary = Earnings from teaching + Earnings from coaching
= $7,000 + $4,500
= $11,500

Therefore, Jill's annual salary is $11,500. The answer is: 11,500
