EXPLANATION
To determine how many bolts of fabric are needed to make a robe, we need to calculate the amount of each color required and then sum them up.

1. **Step 1:** Determine the amount of blue fiber needed.
   - A robe requires 2 bolts of blue fiber.

2. **Step 2:** Calculate the amount of white fiber needed.
   - White fiber is half the amount of blue fiber.
   - Therefore, the amount of white fiber needed = \( \frac{1}{2} \times 2 \) bolts = 1 bolt.

3. **Step 3:** Sum the amounts of both colors to find the total bolts of fabric.
   - Total bolts of fabric = Blue fiber + White fiber = 2 bolts + 1 bolt = 3 bolts.

Therefore, the total number of bolts of fabric needed to make the robe is 3.

The answer is: 3
