To determine how many years it will take Carlos to start earning money from his lemon tree, we need to calculate both the profit per year and then find out when this profit becomes positive.

First, let's calculate the annual profit:
- Annual growth of lemons = 7 lemons
- Selling price per lemon = $1.5
- Cost to buy one lemon = $2 (since it costs $1.5 to sell)
- Profit per lemon = Selling price - Buying price = $1.5 - $1 = $0.5

Total annual profit = Number of lemons * Profit per lemon
= 7 lemons * $0.5/lemon
= $3.5

Next, we account for the expenses:
- Annual watering and feeding cost = $3

Net annual profit after accounting for expenses = Total annual profit - Annual cost
= $3.5 - $3
= $0.5

Since the net annual profit is $0.5, which is less than zero, Carlos does not start earning money until at least the first year because there is no way to make up for the initial investment through sales alone without additional income sources or increased production that isn't mentioned in the problem.

Therefore, the answer is: 1 year.
