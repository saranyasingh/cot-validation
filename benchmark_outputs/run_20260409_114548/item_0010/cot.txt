EXPLANATION

1. First, we need to determine Eliza's regular weekly earnings before considering any overtime.
   
   - Regular rate = $10/hour
   - Number of regular hours worked = 40 hours
   
   Regular earnings = Regular rate × Number of regular hours
                    = $10/hour × 40 hours
                    = $400

2. Next, we calculate Eliza’s total earnings including overtime.

   - Overtime rate = 1.2 × Regular rate
                  = 1.2 × $10/hour
                  = $12/hour
   
   - Total overtime hours worked = Total hours worked - Regular hours
                                = 45 hours - 40 hours
                                = 5 hours
   
   - Earnings from overtime = Overtime rate × Overtime hours
                           = $12/hour × 5 hours
                           = $60

3. Finally, we add the regular earnings and the overtime earnings together to find the total earnings for the week.

   Total earnings = Regular earnings + Earnings from overtime
                 = $400 + $60
                 = $460

Therefore, Eliza's earnings for this week are $460.

The answer is: 460
