To calculate Eliza's earnings for the week, we need to break down her work into two parts: the regular hours and the overtime hours.

1. **Regular Hours**: For the first 40 hours, her rate is $10 per hour.
   \[
   \text{Earnings from regular hours} = 40 \times \$10 = \$400
   \]

2. **Overtime Hours**: The total number of hours worked is 45, so the overtime hours are:
   \[
   \text{Overtime hours} = 45 - 40 = 5 \text{ hours}
   \]
   Her overtime pay is 1.2 times her regular hourly rate, which is:
   \[
   \text{Overtime pay rate} = \$10 \times 1.2 = \$12 \text{ per hour}
   \]
   Therefore, the earnings from overtime hours are:
   \[
   \text{Earnings from overtime hours} = 5 \times \$12 = \$60
   \]

3. **Total Earnings**: Adding the earnings from regular hours and overtime hours gives us the total earnings for the week:
   \[
   \text{Total earnings} = \$400 + \$60 = \$460
   \]

Thus, Eliza's earnings for this week amount to $\boxed{\$460}$.
