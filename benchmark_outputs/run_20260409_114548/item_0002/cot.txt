EXPLANATION
Let's break down the information given and use it to form our logical arguments.

1. **First Premise**: A robe requires 2 bolts of blue fiber.
   - This establishes the starting point for blue fiber usage.

2. **Second Premise**: It also needs half as much white fiber as there is blue fiber.
   - From the first premise, we know that there are 2 bolts of blue fiber.
   - Therefore, the amount of white fiber required is \( \frac{2}{2} = 1 \) bolt.

3. **Third Premise**: To find the total number of bolts used, we add together the blue and white fibers.
   - Blue fibers: 2 bolts
   - White fibers: 1 bolt
   - Total bolts: \( 2 + 1 = 3 \)

4. **Conclusion**: Based on these premises, the total number of bolts needed for the robe is 3.
