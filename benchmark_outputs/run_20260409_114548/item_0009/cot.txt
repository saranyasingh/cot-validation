Explanation:
1. John drives for 3 hours at 60 mph.
   - Distance = Speed × Time = 60 mph × 3 hours = 180 miles.

2. After realizing he forgot something, he tries to get home in 4 hours.
   - However, he spends the first 2 hours stuck in traffic.
     - Remaining distance to cover = Total distance - Distance covered initially = 180 miles - (60 mph × 2 hours) = 120 miles.
   
3. In the next half hour, he drives at 30 mph.
    - Distance driven in this period = 30 mph × 0.5 hours = 15 miles.

4. For the remaining 4 hours, he drives at 80 mph.
    - Distance covered during these 4 hours = 80 mph × 4 hours = 320 miles.

5. Total distance traveled so far = Distance covered initially + Distance driven in traffic + Distance driven in the last 4 hours.
    - Total distance = 180 miles + 120 miles + 320 miles = 620 miles.

6. Since John started at home and drove out to his destination, he must have reached that point when he was done traveling for 4 hours.
   - Therefore, he is 620 miles away from home at the end of those 4 hours.

Answer: 620
