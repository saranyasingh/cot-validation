To determine how many cups of feed Wendi needs for the final meal, we first calculate the total amount of feed given to all the chickens throughout the day.

Wendi has 20 chickens, and each chicken receives 3 cups of feed per day. Therefore, the total daily feed requirement for all chickens is:
\[ 20 \text{ chickens} \times 3 \text{ cups/chicken} = 60 \text{ cups/day} \]

Next, we add up the amounts of feed given during the morning and afternoon:
- Morning feed: \( 15 \text{ cups} \)
- Afternoon feed: \( 25 \text{ cups} \)

Total feed given over two days:
\[ 15 \text{ cups (morning)} + 25 \text{ cups (afternoon)} = 40 \text{ cups} \]

Now, subtract this from the total daily feed requirement to find out how much feed is left for the final meal:
\[ 60 \text{ cups/day} - 40 \text{ cups} = 20 \text{ cups} \]

Therefore, Wendi needs to give her chickens 20 more cups of feed for the final meal of the day. The answer is: 20
