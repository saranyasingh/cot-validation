To determine how much Kylar needs to pay for 16 glasses, we can break down the problem into steps:

1. Calculate the cost of one glass.
2. Determine if any of the glasses qualify for the discounted price.
3. Compute the total cost based on whether all or some glasses get the discount.

Step 1: The cost of one glass is $5.

Step 2: Since every second glass gets a 60% discount, out of the 16 glasses, half will be at full price and half at a discount. Therefore, there will be 8 glasses at full price ($5 each) and 8 glasses at 60% (or $3 per glass).

Step 3: Calculate the total cost:
- Cost of full-price glasses: \(8 \times \$5 = \$40\)
- Cost of discounted glasses: \(8 \times \$3 = \$24\)

Total cost: \(\$40 + \$24 = \$64\)

Therefore, the answer is: Kylar needs to pay $\$64$ for 16 glasses.
