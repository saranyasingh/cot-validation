Explanation:

First, let's break down the problem into smaller parts.

**Premise 1:** Kylar buys 16 glasses.
- This tells us that the total quantity of glasses is known.

**Premise 2:** Every second glass costs only 60% of the original price.
- This indicates a discount on every other glass, which affects our calculation.

**Premise 3:** Each glass originally costs $5.
- This gives us the base price per glass.

To find out how much Kylar needs to pay for all 16 glasses, we'll calculate the cost for each group of glasses (even-numbered or odd-numbered) separately and then sum these amounts.

**Step-by-step Calculation:**

1. **Calculate the cost for the first 8 glasses (since they're not discounted):**
   - Price per glass = $5
   - Number of glasses = 8
   - Total cost for even-numbered glasses = \( 8 \times 5 = \$40 \)

2. **Calculate the cost for the remaining 8 glasses (which are discounted):**
   - Discount rate = 60%
   - Cost per discounted glass = \( 5 \times 0.60 = \$3 \)
   - Number of discounted glasses = 8
   - Total cost for discounted glasses = \( 8 \times 3 = \$24 \)

3. **Sum up the costs from both groups:**
   - Total cost = Cost for even-numbered glasses + Cost for discounted glasses
   - Total cost = \( 40 + 24 = \$64 \)

Therefore, the total amount Kylar needs to pay for 16 glasses is $64.

**Answer:** 64
