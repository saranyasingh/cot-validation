Explanation:
1. James runs 3 sprints per session.
2. Each sprint is 60 meters long.
3. He repeats this process 3 times a week.

To find the total distance he runs in a week, we need to multiply the number of sprints per session by the length of each sprint, then multiply that result by the number of sessions per week.

Calculation:
Total meters = (Number of sprints/session) × (Length of each sprint) × (Number of sessions/week)

Substitute the given values:
Total meters = 3 × 60 × 3

Perform the multiplication:
Total meters = 180 × 3

Calculate the final result:
Total meters = 540

Therefore, the answer is: 540.
