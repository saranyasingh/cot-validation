EXPLANATION
To determine how much shorter the candle will be after burning from 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM, we need to calculate the total time the candle has been burning.

First, let's find out how long the candle has been burning. From 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM spans 4 hours (since there are 60 minutes in an hour).

Next, we know that the candle melts at a rate of 2 centimeters per hour. To find the total melting length over these 4 hours, we multiply the hourly melting rate by the total time:

Melting rate = 2 cm/hour
Total time = 4 hours
Total melting length = Melting rate × Total time
                      = 2 cm/hour × 4 hours
                      = 8 cm

Therefore, the candle will be 8 cm shorter after burning from 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM.

The answer is: 8
