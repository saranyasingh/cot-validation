Explanation:

1. Let's denote the number of downloads in the first month as \(D_1\).
2. According to the problem, \(D_1 = 60\).

3. In the second month, the number of downloads is three times that of the first month:
   \[
   D_2 = 3 \times D_1 = 3 \times 60 = 180
   \]

4. In the third month, the number of downloads is 70% (or 0.7) of what it was in the second month because it reduces by 30%. Thus,
   \[
   D_3 = 0.7 \times D_2 = 0.7 \times 180 = 126
   \]

5. To find the total number of downloads over the three months, we sum up all the individual monthly downloads:
   \[
   \text{Total Downloads} = D_1 + D_2 + D_3 = 60 + 180 + 126 = 366
   \]

Therefore, the total number of downloads over the three months is 366.

The answer is: 366
