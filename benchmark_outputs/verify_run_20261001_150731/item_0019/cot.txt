EXPLANATION
To determine how many dozen eggs Claire will consume in 4 weeks, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Determine the daily consumption**: Claire makes a 3-egg omelet every day.

3. **Calculate monthly consumption**: Assuming there are approximately 4 weeks in a month (a common approximation), the total number of eggs consumed in one month is \(21 \text{ eggs/week} \times 4 \text{ weeks/month} = 84 \text{ eggs/month}\).
4. **Calculate yearly consumption**: There are approximately 52 weeks in a year, so the total number of eggs consumed in one year is \(84 \text{ eggs/month} \times 52 \text{ months/year} = 4464 \text{ eggs/year}\).

Now, let's convert the annual consumption into dozens of eggs:
\[ \frac{4464 \text{ eggs}}{12 \text{ eggs/dozen}} = 372 \text{ dozen eggs/year} \]
