EXPLANATION
To determine the original price of the book before the 25% discount, we need to work backwards through the information provided.

1. **Identify the given information:**  
   - Kyle paid $19.50 after a 25% discount.
   
2. **Understand the relationship between the discounted price and the original price:**  
   A 25% discount means that Kyle paid 75% (or 0.75) of the original price because 100% - 25% = 75%.

3. **Set up an equation to find the original price:**  
   Let \( P \) represent the original price of the book. Since Kyle paid 75% of the original price, we can write this as:
   \[
   0.75P = \$19.50
   \]

4. **Solve for \( P \):**  
   To isolate \( P \), divide both sides of the equation by 0.75:
   \[
   P = \frac{\$19.50}{0.75}
   \]
   \[
   P = \$26
   \]

Therefore, the original price of the book was $\boxed{26}$.
