## VOCABULARY
Predicates:
PaidAmount(x,y) — x paid amount y for the book after the discount
DiscountPercent(x,y) — x received a y% discount on the book
PaidFraction(x,y) — x paid fraction y (as a decimal) of the original price
OriginalPrice(x,y) — y is the original price of the book that x bought

Constants:
kyle, 19.50, 25, 0.75, 26

## FACTS
FACT-1: Kyle paid $19.50 for the book after the discount :: PaidAmount(kyle, 19.50)
FACT-2: Kyle received a 25% discount on the book :: DiscountPercent(kyle, 25)

## RULES
RULE-1: A 25% discount means the buyer pays 75% (0.75) of the original price :: ∀x (DiscountPercent(x,25) → PaidFraction(x,0.75))
RULE-2: The original price equals the amount paid divided by the fraction of the original price that was paid :: ∀x∀y∀f ((PaidAmount(x,y) ∧ PaidFraction(x,f)) → OriginalPrice(x, y/f))

## INFERENCES
INF-1: Kyle paid a fraction of 0.75 of the original price, from FACT-2 and RULE-1 by Universal Instantiation and Modus Ponens :: PaidFraction(kyle, 0.75)
INF-2: The original price of the book is $26 (19.50/0.75 = 26), from FACT-1, INF-1, and RULE-2 by Universal Instantiation and Modus Ponens :: OriginalPrice(kyle, 26)
