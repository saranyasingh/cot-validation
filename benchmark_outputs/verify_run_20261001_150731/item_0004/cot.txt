EXPLANATION
To determine how many total meters James runs per week, we need to break down the problem into smaller parts and then combine those parts.

1. **Identify the components**: James runs three sprints each time he exercises.
2. **Determine the distance of each sprint**: Each sprint is 60 meters long.
3. **Calculate the distance for one complete exercise session (sprint + rest)**: Since there are three sprints, he runs \(3 \times 60 = 180\) meters during one complete session.
4. **Consider the frequency of his workouts**: James performs this exercise regimen 3 times a week.
5. **Calculate the total weekly mileage**: Multiply the distance covered in one session by the number of sessions per week: \(180 \text{ meters/session} \times 3 \text{ sessions/week} = 540 \text{ meters/week}\).

Therefore, the total number of meters James runs per week is 540 meters. The answer is: 540.
