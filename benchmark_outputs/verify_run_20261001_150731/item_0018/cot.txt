To determine Jill's annual salary, we need to calculate her earnings from both teaching and coaching separately and then sum them up.

**Premise 1:** Jill earns $20 per hour as a teacher.
- This gives us her weekly earning from teaching at $20/hour * 35 hours/week = $700/week.

**Premise 2:** Jill earns $30 per hour as a cheerleading coach.
- This gives us her weekly earning from coaching at $30/hour * 15 hours/week = $450/week.

**Premise 3:** There are 50 weeks in a year.
- Therefore, the total weekly earnings are $700 + $450 = $1150/week.

**Premise 4:** She works these same numbers (weekly) for an entire year.
- Thus, her annual salary is $1150/week * 50 weeks/year = $57,500/year.

**Conclusion:** Jill's annual salary is $57,500.

Therefore, the answer is: 57500.
