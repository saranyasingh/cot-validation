EXPLANATION

1. **Premise 1**: Carla's normal download speed is 2 GB per minute.
   - This is given directly as part of the problem statement.

2. **Premise 2**: The total size of the file is 200 GB.
   - This information is also provided in the problem statement.

3. **Premise 3**: At some point during the download, due to system issues (a restart), Carla stops at 60% of the file.
   - This means that after 40 minutes (since \( \frac{4}{5} \times 20 = 16 \) GB), the download would have been completed if there were no interruptions.
   - Therefore, Carla needs to download an additional \( 200 - 16 = 184 \) GB before restarting the download.

4. **Premise 4**: After the restart, Carla starts again from the beginning, meaning she now has to catch up on all previously downloaded data plus what remains to be downloaded.
   - Since her normal download rate is 2 GB per minute, we need to calculate how long it takes her to download these remaining 184 GB.

To find this time, we use the formula:
\[ \text{Time} = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Speed}} \]
where "Distance" here is 184 GB and "Speed" is 2 GB per minute.

Therefore,
\[ \text{Time} = \frac{184 \text{ GB}}{2 \text{ GB/min}} = 92 \text{ minutes} \]

5. **Conclusion**:
   - Adding the initial 16 GB she had already downloaded and the 92 minutes needed to catch up:
     \[ 16 \text{ GB} + 92 \text{ minutes} = 108 \text{ GB} \]

Thus, the total amount of time it takes Carla to download the entire file considering the restart is 108 minutes.

The answer is: 108
