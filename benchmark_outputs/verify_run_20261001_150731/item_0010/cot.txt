To determine Eliza's earnings for this week, we need to calculate both her regular pay for the first 40 hours and her overtime pay for the additional 5 hours.

**Premise 1:** Eliza's rate per hour for the first 40 hours is $10.
- Premise 2:** Eliza receives an overtime pay of 1.2 times her regular hourly rate.
- Premise 3:** Eliza worked for 45 hours this week.

Now let's break down the calculation into steps:

### Step 1: Calculate Regular Pay for the First 40 Hours
Regular pay = Hourly rate × Number of hours worked
= $10/hour × 40 hours
= $400

### Step 2: Calculate Overtime Pay for the Additional 5 Hours
Overtime pay = Overtime rate × Number of overtime hours
= 1.2 × $10/hour × 5 hours
= 1.2 × $50
= $60

### Step 3: Add Regular Pay and Overtime Pay to Find Total Earnings
Total earnings = Regular pay + Overtime pay
= $400 + $60
= $460

Therefore, Eliza's total earnings for this week are **$460**.
