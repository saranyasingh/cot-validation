OvertimeMultiplier(x, n) — x's overtime pay rate is n times x's regular hourly rate
Hours(x, k, n) — x worked n hours of kind k (k is a kind constant: regular_hours, overtime_hours, or total_hours)
Pay(x, k, n) — x's pay of kind k equals n dollars (k is a kind constant: regular_pay, base_overtime, overtime_pay, or total_pay)
Calc(op, a, b, c) — applying arithmetic operation op to a and b yields c (op is the constant times or plus)
