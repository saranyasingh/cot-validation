## RULES
RULE-1: If x has fraction f of water and volume v, and multiplying f and v yields w, then x contains w liters of water :: ∀x,f,v,w (WaterFraction(x,f) ∧ Volume(x,v) ∧ Computes(multiply,f,v,w) → Water(x,w))
RULE-2: If x has volume v and s liters spill from x, and subtracting s from v yields r, then the resulting entity y has volume r :: ∀x,y,v,s,r (Volume(x,v) ∧ Spilled(x,s) ∧ Computes(subtract,v,s,r) → Volume(y,r))
RULE-3: If x has water amount a and y has water amount b, and adding a and b yields c, then the combined entity z has water amount c :: ∀x,y,z,a,b,c (Water(x,a) ∧ Water(y,b) ∧ Computes(add,a,b,c) → Water(z,c))
RULE-4: If x has volume a and y has volume b, and adding a and b yields c, then the combined entity z has volume c :: ∀x,y,z,a,b,c (Volume(x,a) ∧ Volume(y,b) ∧ Computes(add,a,b,c) → Volume(z,c))
