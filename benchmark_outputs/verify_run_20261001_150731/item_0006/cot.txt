To determine how much Kylar needs to pay for 16 glasses, we'll break down the problem into steps.

### Step 1: Determine the cost of the first glass.
- The first glass costs $5.

### Step 2: Calculate the cost of the remaining glasses.
- Every second glass costs 60% of the original price.
- Since there are 16 glasses in total, half of them would be 8 glasses (because \( \frac{16}{2} = 8 \)).
- Therefore, the cost of these 8 glasses is \( 8 \times \$5 \times 0.60 \).

Let's calculate this step-by-step:

\[
\text{Cost of 8 glasses} = 8 \times \$5 \times 0.60
\]

First, multiply the price per glass by 8:

\[
8 \times \$5 = \$40
\]

Next, multiply that result by 0.60:

\[
\$40 \times 0.60 = \$24
\]

So, the cost for the 8 glasses is \$24.

### Step 3: Add the cost of the first glass to the cost of the 8 glasses.
- The first glass costs $5.
- The 8 glasses cost $24.

Therefore, the total cost for all 16 glasses is:

\[
\$5 + \$24 = \$29
\]

### Conclusion:
Kylar needs to pay **$29** for the 16 glasses.
