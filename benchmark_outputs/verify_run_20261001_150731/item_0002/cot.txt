Explanation:
1. We know that a robe requires 2 bolts of blue fiber.
2. Additionally, we're told that it needs half as much white fiber as blue fiber.
3. To find out how much white fiber is needed, we need to calculate half of the amount of blue fiber required.
4. Half of 2 bolts (the amount of blue fiber) is calculated as \( \frac{2}{2} = 1 \).
5. Therefore, the robe also requires 1 bolt of white fiber.

Now let's sum up the total number of bolts used:

- Blue fiber: 2 bolts
- White fiber: 1 bolt

Total bolts = 2 + 1 = 3 bolts

The answer is: 3
