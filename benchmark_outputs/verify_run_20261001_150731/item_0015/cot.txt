EXPLANATION

1. First, calculate the number of students enrolled in contemporary dance.
   - 20% of 20 students = \( \frac{20}{100} \times 20 = 4 \) students.

2. Next, determine how many students remain after those enrolled in contemporary dance have been subtracted.
   - Total students - Students in contemporary dance = Remaining students
   - 20 students - 4 students = 16 students.

3. Then, find out how many of these remaining students enrolled in jazz dance.
   - 25% of the remaining students = \( \frac{25}{100} \times 16 = 4 \) students.

4. Finally, calculate how many students enrolled in hip-hop dance.
   - Remaining students - Students in jazz dance = Hip-hop dancers
   - 16 students - 4 students = 12 students.

5. To find the percentage of the total students that enrolled in hip-hop dance, use the formula:
   Percentage = (Number of hip-hop dancers / Total number of students) × 100%
   - \( \frac{12}{20} \times 100\% = 60\% \)

THE ANSWER IS: 60
