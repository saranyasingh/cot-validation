To determine which purchase plan maximizes profit, we need to calculate the expected value (EV) of both options after accounting for their respective price increases.

### Step-by-Step Logical Reasoning

**Premise 1:** The current values of the items are as follows:
- Jewelry: $5,000
- Electronic gadgets: $8,000

**Premise 2:** The jewelry market is predicted to increase by 2.5%.
- Increase in jewelry value = \( 5000 \times 0.025 = 125 \)
- New value of jewelry = \( 5000 + 125 = 5125 \)

**Premise 3:** The electronic gadgets market is predicted to increase by 1.2%.
- Increase in electronic gadgets value = \( 8000 \times 0.012 = 96 \)
- New value of electronic gadgets = \( 8000 + 96 = 8096 \)

**Premise 4:** To find the profit, subtract the original cost from the new value.

**Premise 5:** Calculate the profit for the jewelry option:
\[ \text{Profit}_{\text{jewelry}} = 5125 - 5000 = 125 \]

**Premise 6:** Calculate the profit for the electronic gadgets option:
\[ \text{Profit}_{\text{electronic gadgets}} = 8096 - 8000 = 96 \]

**Conclusion:** By comparing the profits calculated from Premises 5 and 6, it is clear that the jewelry option results in a higher profit (\( \$125 \)) compared to the electronic gadgets option (\( \$96 \)).

Therefore, based on this analysis, the merchant should choose to buy the jewelry to maximize his profit.

The answer is: 125
