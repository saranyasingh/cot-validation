To determine the total cost of Toula's purchases at the bakery, we need to calculate the cost of each type of pastry separately and then sum these costs.

1. **Cost calculation for donuts:**
   - Toula bought 3 dozen donuts.
   - Each dozen donuts costs $68.
   - Total cost for donuts = \( 3 \times 68 = 204 \) dollars.

2. **Cost calculation for mini cupcakes:**
   - Toula bought 2 dozen mini cupcakes.
   - Each dozen mini cupcakes costs $80.
   - Total cost for mini cupcakes = \( 2 \times 80 = 160 \) dollars.

3. **Cost calculation for mini cheesecakes:**
   - Toula bought 6 dozen mini cheesecakes.
   - Each dozen mini cheesecakes costs $55.
   - Total cost for mini cheesecakes = \( 6 \times 55 = 330 \) dollars.

4. **Total cost calculation:**
   - Sum the costs of all items purchased.
   - Total cost = Cost of donuts + Cost of mini cupcakes + Cost of mini cheesecakes
   - Total cost = \( 204 + 160 + 330 = 794 \) dollars.

Therefore, the total cost of Toula's purchases at the bakery is \( \boxed{794} \).
