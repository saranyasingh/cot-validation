EXPLANATION
To determine how many years it will take Carlos to start earning money from his lemon tree, we need to calculate the net profit per year after accounting for both the initial investment and ongoing expenses.

1. **Initial Investment**: The cost to plant the lemon tree is $90.
2. **Annual Revenue**: Each year, Carlos sells 7 lemons at $1.5 each, so the annual revenue is \(7 \times 1.5 = \$10.5\).
3. **Annual Expenses**: The yearly cost to water and feed the tree is $3.

Now, let's find out the net profit per year by subtracting the annual expenses from the annual revenue:
\[ \text{Net Profit} = \text{Annual Revenue} - \text{Annual Expenses} \]
\[ \text{Net Profit} = 10.5 - 3 = \$7.5 \]

This means that each year, Carlos earns an additional $7.5 in profit beyond covering the initial cost of planting the tree. To break even (start earning money), Carlos needs to cover the initial $90 investment plus the additional $7.5 per year.

4. **Total Initial Cost + Net Profit Needed**: \(90 + 7.5 = \$97.5\).

Since Carlos only makes a net profit of $7.5 per year, it would take him more than one year to reach the total $97.5 needed to break even. Therefore, we need to determine how many years it takes to accumulate this amount.

5. **Years to Break Even**: Divide the total amount needed to break even by the net profit per year:
\[ \text{Years to Break Even} = \frac{\$97.5}{\$7.5} = 13.33 \]

Since Carlos cannot have a fraction of a year in practical terms, we round up to the next whole number because he must earn at least $97.5 to break even over multiple years.

6. **Final Calculation**:
   - First year: Breaks even after $97.5 / $7.5 = 13.33 years.
   - Second year: Earns $7.5 more, totaling $105 ($97.5 + $7.5) and breaks even exactly.

Therefore, it will take Carlos 14 years to start earning money on the lemon tree.

The answer is: 14
