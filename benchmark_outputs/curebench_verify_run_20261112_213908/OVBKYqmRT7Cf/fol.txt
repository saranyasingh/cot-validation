## VOCABULARY
Predicates:
Treats(d, c) — drug d is indicated to treat condition c
Indicates(s, p) — symptom s indicates problem p
RecommendedAction(p, a) — action a is the usual recommended action for problem p
Describes(o, a) — answer option o describes action a
Answer(o) — option o is the correct answer

Constants:
lamotrigine, epilepsy, worsening_seizure_control, inadequate_efficacy, switch_aed, option_d

## RULES

RULE-2: An option describing the appropriate action for worsening seizure control is the answer :: ∀o∀a (Describes(o, a) ∧ RecommendedAction(worsening_seizure_control, a) → Answer(o))
