Encompasses(o, c) — answer option o encompasses condition c
Answer(o) — option o is the correct answer

## RULES
RULE-1: An option describing a condition that is a listed contraindication is ruled out :: ∀o∀c (Describes(o, c) ∧ Contraindicated(terbinafine, c) → ¬Answer(o))
RULE-2: A prior allergic reaction to a drug means the patient should avoid that drug :: ∀d∀c (PriorReaction(d, c) → Contraindicated(d, c))
RULE-3: The most comprehensive option that encompasses two conditions, one of which is contraindicated, is the answer :: ∀o∀x∀y (Encompasses(o, x) ∧ Encompasses(o, y) ∧ Contraindicated(terbinafine, y) → Answer(o))
