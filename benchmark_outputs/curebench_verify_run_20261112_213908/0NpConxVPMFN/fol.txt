Safe(t) — temperature t is safe and appropriate for storing the medication
Answer(o) — option o is the correct answer

## RULES
RULE-1: A temperature within the commonly accepted range is safe and appropriate for storage :: ∀t (InRange(t) → Safe(t))
RULE-2: A temperature outside the commonly accepted range is not safe for storage :: ∀t (¬InRange(t) → ¬Safe(t))
RULE-3: An option describing a safe storage temperature is the answer :: ∀o∀t ((Describes(o, t) ∧ Safe(t)) → Answer(o))
RULE-4: An option describing an unsafe storage temperature is not the answer :: ∀o∀t ((Describes(o, t) ∧ ¬Safe(t)) → ¬Answer(o))
