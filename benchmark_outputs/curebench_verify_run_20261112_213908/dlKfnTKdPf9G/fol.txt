## VOCABULARY
Predicates:
OnDrug(p, d) — patient p is taking drug d
SevereAllergy(p) — patient p experiences severe allergic reactions
NeedsEmergency(p) — patient p requires immediate emergency medical care
Describes(o, a) — answer option o describes action a
Answer(o) — option o is the correct answer
Constants:
patient, pertzye, seek_emergency_treatment, option_b

## FACTS
FACT-1: The patient is taking PERTZYE :: OnDrug(patient, pertzye)
FACT-2: The patient experiences severe allergic reactions :: SevereAllergy(patient)
FACT-3: Option B is seeking emergency treatment immediately :: Describes(option_b, seek_emergency_treatment)

## OPERATIONS

## RULES
RULE-1: A patient with a severe allergic reaction requires immediate emergency medical care :: ∀x (SevereAllergy(x) → NeedsEmergency(x))
RULE-2: If a patient needs emergency care, an option describing seeking emergency treatment is the answer :: ∀x∀o (NeedsEmergency(x) ∧ Describes(o, seek_emergency_treatment) → Answer(o))

## INFERENCES
