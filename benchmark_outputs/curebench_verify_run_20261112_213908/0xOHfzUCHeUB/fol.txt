## VOCABULARY
Predicates:
Studied(d) — drug d has been extensively studied pharmacologically
BindingRange(d, a, b) — the protein binding percentage of drug d generally ranges between a and b percent
Describes(o, a, b) — answer option o states a protein binding of a ± b percent
WithinRange(o, d) — the value stated by option o falls within the general protein binding range of drug d
Answer(o) — option o is the correct answer
Constants:
venlafaxine, 30, 60, 50, 5, option_a
