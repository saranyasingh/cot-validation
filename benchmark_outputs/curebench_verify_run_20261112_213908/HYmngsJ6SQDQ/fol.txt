Distinct(f, g) — frequencies f and g are clearly distinct from each other
MostAccurate(f) — frequency f is the most accurate representation based on standard clinical practice and user expectations
Answer(o) — option o is the correct answer

option_a, option_b, option_c, option_d, once_daily, twice_daily, three_times_daily, every_four_hours
