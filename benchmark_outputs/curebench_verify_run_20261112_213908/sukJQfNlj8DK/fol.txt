## VOCABULARY
Predicates:
Causes(d, c) — drug d is labeled as able to cause condition c
RequiresAttention(c) — condition c requires immediate medical attention and can lead to death
Consider(d, c) — a history of condition c must be considered before prescribing drug d
Describes(o, c) — answer option o describes condition c
Answer(o) — option o is the correct answer
Constants:
pemetrexed, severe_hypersensitivity, option_a
