- Premise 3: The book has a total of 594 pages. (T = 594)
- Premise 4 (from Premises 1 and 2): Since there are C = 3 chapters each with P pages, the total pages T = C × P = 3P.

- Premise 6 (solve for P): P = 594 / 3 = 198.
