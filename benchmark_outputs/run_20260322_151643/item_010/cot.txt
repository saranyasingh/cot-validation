1. There are 34 books in total. (Given)
2. There are 2 bookshelves. (Given)
3. The 34 books are distributed equally between the 2 bookshelves. (Given)
4. When items are distributed equally among k containers, each container holds total items divided by k; here, each bookshelf holds 34 ÷ 2 items. (Definition of equal distribution)
5. 34 ÷ 2 = 17. (Arithmetic)
6. Therefore, each bookshelf has 17 books. (Conclusion)

The answer is: 17
