To calculate the profit Josh made from flipping the house, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Determine the total cost**: The initial purchase price of the house plus the amount spent on repairs.
   \[
   \text{Total Cost} = \$80,000 + \$50,000 = \$130,000
   \]

2. **Calculate the increase in value due to renovation**: Since the value increased by 150%, this means that the new value of the house after renovations is:
   \[
   \text{Increase in Value} = \$130,000 \times 1.5 = \$195,000
   \]

3. **Find the original value before renovations**: Subtract the repair costs from the total cost to find out what the house was worth before any renovations were made.
   \[
   \text{Original Value} = \$130,000 - \$50,000 = \$80,000
   \]

4. **Calculate the profit**: Subtract the original value of the house (before renovations) from the new value after renovations.
   \[
   \text{Profit} = \$195,000 - \$80,000 = \$115,000
   \]

Therefore, the profit Josh made from flipping the house is $\boxed{\$115,000}$.
