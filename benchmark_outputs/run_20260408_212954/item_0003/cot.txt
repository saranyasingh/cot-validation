EXPLANATION
To determine Josh's profit after flipping the house, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total cost of buying and fixing the house:**
   - Cost of the house: $80,000
   - Amount spent on repairs: $50,000
   - Total initial investment = $80,000 + $50,000 = $130,000

2. **Determine the increase in value due to the renovations:**
   - Value increase: 150% of the original purchase price.
   - Increase amount = 150% of $80,000 = 1.5 * $80,000 = $120,000

3. **Calculate the net profit by subtracting the total initial investment from the increase in value:**
   - Net profit = Increase in value - Initial investment
   - Net profit = $120,000 - $130,000 = -$10,000

4. **Interpret the result:**
   - Since the net profit is negative ($-10,000), this indicates that Josh lost money.

Therefore, the answer is: -10

This negative value represents the loss Josh incurred when he flipped the house.
