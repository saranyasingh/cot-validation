To calculate Eliza's earnings for the week when she worked 45 hours, we need to consider both her regular work and any overtime she may have done.

First, let's determine her earnings from her regular work:
- Regular work rate = $10/hour
- Regular hours = 40 hours

Regular earnings = Regular rate * Regular hours
= $10/hour * 40 hours
= $400

Next, let's calculate her earnings from overtime:
- Overtime pay rate = 1.2 * Regular rate
= 1.2 * $10/hour
= $12/hour

Overtime hours = Total hours - Regular hours
= 45 hours - 40 hours
= 5 hours

Overtime earnings = Overtime pay rate * Overtime hours
= $12/hour * 5 hours
= $60

Finally, we add up the earnings from regular work and overtime to get the total weekly earnings:

Total earnings = Regular earnings + Overtime earnings
= $400 + $60
= $460

Therefore, Eliza's earnings for this week amount to **$460**.
