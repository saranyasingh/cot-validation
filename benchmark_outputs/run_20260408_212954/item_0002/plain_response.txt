To determine the total number of bolts of fiber required for one robe, we need to follow these steps:

1. Identify the amount of blue fiber needed.
   - The problem states that a robe takes 2 bolts of blue fiber.

2. Calculate the amount of white fiber needed.
   - It is stated that a robe requires half as much white fiber as blue fiber.
   - Therefore, the amount of white fiber needed is \( \frac{1}{2} \times 2 = 1 \) bolt.

3. Sum up the total number of bolts of fiber.
   - Blue fiber: 2 bolts
   - White fiber: 1 bolt

Total number of bolts:
\[ 2 + 1 = 3 \]

Thus, the total number of bolts of fiber required for one robe is 3.
