Let's convert the chain-of-thought reasoning into structured First-Order Logic (FOL):

### FACTS
1. A robe takes 2 bolts of blue fiber.
   - Fact-1: RobeBlueFiber(2)
2. A robe takes half that much white fiber.
   - Fact-2: RobeWhiteFiber(RobeBlueFiber(2)/2)

### RULES
1. The total number of bolts needed for the robe can be calculated by adding the number of blue bolts to the number of white bolts.
   - Rule-1: TotalBolts(RobeBlueFiber(2), RobeWhiteFiber(RobeBlueFiber(2)/2)) = RobeBlueFiber(2) + RobeWhiteFiber(RobeBlueFiber(2)/2)

### INFERENCES
1. From Fact-1 and Rule-1, we can infer the total number of bolts needed for the robe.
   - Infer-1: TotalBolts(RobeBlueFiber(2), RobeWhiteFiber(RobeBlueFiber(2)/2)) = 4

Therefore, the total number of bolts of fiber needed to make the robe is 4.
