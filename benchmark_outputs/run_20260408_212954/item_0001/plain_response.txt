To determine how much Janet makes every day at the farmers' market, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the total number of eggs laid each day.
2. Determine how many eggs she consumes for breakfast.
3. Subtract the eggs consumed from the total eggs to find out how many are left for selling.
4. Calculate the revenue from selling the remaining eggs.

Step 1: Total eggs laid each day = 16 eggs

Step 2: Eggs consumed for breakfast = 3 eggs/day

Step 3: Remaining eggs after breakfast = Total eggs - Breakfast eggs
Remaining eggs = 16 - 3 = 13 eggs

Step 4: Revenue from selling remaining eggs = Remaining eggs * Price per egg
