To determine how much Kylar needs to pay for 16 glasses, we first need to understand the pricing structure:

1. The cost of one glass is $5.
2. Every second glass costs only 60% of its original price.

Let's break down the calculation step-by-step:

### Step 1: Calculate the Cost of Each Glass
- The price of the first glass is $5.
- Since every second glass costs 60%, we calculate:
  \[
  60\% \text{ of } \$5 = 0.60 \times \$5 = \$3
  \]
  So, the price of each subsequent glass (every other glass) is $3.

### Step 2: Determine the Number of Glasses at Each Price
- There are 16 glasses in total.
- Half of these glasses will be the cheaper ones (costing $3), and the other half will be the more expensive ones (costing $5).

### Step 3: Calculate the Total Cost
- The number of cheap glasses is \( \frac{16}{2} = 8 \).
- The number of expensive glasses is also \( \frac{16}{2} = 8 \).

Now, let's calculate the total cost:
- For the cheap glasses: 
  \[
  8 \text{ glasses} \times \$3/\text{glass} = \$24
  \]
- For the expensive glasses:
  \[
  8 \text{ glasses} \times \$5/\text{glass} = \$40
  \]

Finally, add the two amounts together:
\[
\$24 + \$40 = \$64
\]

Therefore, the total amount Kylar needs to pay for all 16 glasses is **$64**.
