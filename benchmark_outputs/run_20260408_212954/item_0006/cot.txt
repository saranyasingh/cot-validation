EXPLANATION

Let's break down the problem into steps using logical reasoning.

Step 1: Determine the cost of the first glass.
- Cost of one glass = $5

Step 2: Calculate the cost of the second glass.
- Since every second glass costs 60% of the original price,
- Second glass cost = \( \frac{60}{100} \times 5 = 3 \) dollars

Step 3: Identify the pattern in the costs.
- We see that the cost alternates between $5 (for odd-numbered glasses) and $3 (for even-numbered glasses).

Step 4: Determine how many glasses fall into each category.
- There are 16 glasses in total.
- Half of these glasses will be odd-numbered and half will be even-numbered.
- Therefore, there are 8 odd-numbered glasses and 8 even-numbered glasses.

Step 5: Calculate the total cost.
- For the odd-numbered glasses: \( 8 \times 5 = 40 \) dollars
- For the even-numbered glasses: \( 8 \times 3 = 24 \) dollars

Step 6: Add the costs together to find the total amount Kylar needs to pay.
- Total cost = \( 40 + 24 = 64 \) dollars

Therefore, the answer is: 64.
