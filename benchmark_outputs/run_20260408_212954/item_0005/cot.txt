EXPLANATION
To determine how many cups of feed Wendi needs to give her chickens in the final meal of the day, we first need to calculate the total amount of feed given over the course of the day.

Step 1: Calculate the total amount of feed given throughout the day.
- In the morning, she gives 15 cups of feed.
- In the afternoon, she gives 25 cups of feed.

Total feed given = Morning feed + Afternoon feed
= 15 cups + 25 cups
= 40 cups

Step 2: Determine the daily feed requirement per chicken.
- Each chicken receives 3 cups of feed per day.

Step 3: Calculate the total number of chickens.
- Wendi has a flock of 20 chickens.

Step 4: Calculate the total daily feed requirement for all chickens.
- Total daily feed requirement = Daily feed per chicken × Number of chickens
= 3 cups/chicken × 20 chickens
= 60 cups

Step 5: Subtract the feed given during the morning and afternoon from the total daily requirement.
- Feed left for the final meal = Total daily feed requirement - (Morning feed + Afternoon feed)
= 60 cups - 40 cups
= 20 cups

Therefore, Wendi needs to give her chickens 20 cups of feed in the final meal of the day.
