### Facts
FACT-1: MorningFeed = 15 cups
FACT-2: AfternoonFeed = 25 cups

### Rules
RULE-1: TotalFeed = MorningFeed + AfternoonFeed
RULE-2: ChickensPerDay = 3 cups/chicken
RULE-3: TotalChickens = 20 chickens
RULE-4: FinalMealFeed = ChickensPerDay * TotalChickens

### Inferences
INF-1: From FACT-1, RULE-1: TotalFeed = 15 cups + 25 cups = 40 cups
INF-2: From RULE-2, RULE-3: ChickensPerDay = 3 cups/chicken
INF-3: From RULE-4, RULE-5: TotalDailyFeedRequirement = 3 cups/chicken × 20 chickens = 60 cups
INF-4: From RULE-1, RULE-2, RULE-5: TotalDailyFeedRequirement - (MorningFeed + AfternoonFeed) = 60 cups - 40 cups = 20 cups
INF-5: From RULE-4, RULE-5: FinalMealFeed = 60 cups - 40 cups = 20 cups
