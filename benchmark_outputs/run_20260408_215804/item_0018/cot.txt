To calculate Jill's annual salary, we need to determine how much she earns from teaching and coaching separately, then add those amounts together.

First, let's find out how many hours Jill works as a teacher in a year.
She teaches 35 hours a week for 50 weeks a year.
So, the total number of hours worked as a teacher = 35 hours/week * 50 weeks/year = 1750 hours/year.

Next, let's calculate her earnings from being a teacher.
She gets paid $20 per hour for teaching.
Her yearly earnings from teaching = 1750 hours/year * $20/hour = $35,000/year.

Now, let's figure out how many hours she works as a cheerleading coach in a year.
She coaches 15 hours a week for 50 weeks a year.
So, the total number of hours worked as a cheerleader = 15 hours/week * 50 weeks/year = 750 hours/year.

Next, let's calculate her earnings from being a cheerleader.
She gets paid $30 per hour for coaching.
Her yearly earnings from coaching = 750 hours/year * $30/hour = $22,500/year.

Finally, let's sum up her earnings from both jobs to get her annual salary.
Total annual salary = Earnings from teaching + Earnings from coaching
                   = $35,000 + $22,500
                   = $57,500/year.
