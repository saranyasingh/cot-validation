To calculate Jill's annual salary, we need to first determine her earnings from each job separately and then sum them up.

1. **Earnings from teaching**:
   - Hourly rate: $20/hour
   - Hours worked per week: 35 hours
   - Weeks worked in a year: 50

   Earnings from teaching = Hourly rate × Hours worked per week × Weeks worked in a year
   \[
   \text{Earnings from teaching} = \$20 \times 35 \times 50 = \$35,000
   \]

2. **Earnings from coaching**:
   - Hourly rate: $30/hour
   - Hours worked per week: 15 hours
   - Weeks worked in a year: 50

   Earnings from coaching = Hourly rate × Hours worked per week × Weeks worked in a year
   \[
   \text{Earnings from coaching} = \$30 \times 15 \times 50 = \$22,500
   \]

3. **Total annual salary**:
   Sum of earnings from both jobs.
   \[
   \text{Total annual salary} = \$35,000 + \$22,500 = \$57,500
   \]

Therefore, Jill's annual salary is $\boxed{\$57,500}$.
