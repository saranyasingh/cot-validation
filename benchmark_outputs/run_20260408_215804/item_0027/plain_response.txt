To determine how much Mishka spent in total, we need to calculate the cost for each type of clothing item and then sum them up.

First, let's calculate the cost for the shorts:
- Mishka bought 3 pairs of shorts.
- Each pair of shorts costs $16.50.

\[
\text{Cost of shorts} = 3 \times \$16.50 = \$49.50
\]

Next, let's calculate the cost for the pants:
- Mishka bought 3 pairs of pants.
- Each pair of pants costs $22.50.

\[
\text{Cost of pants} = 3 \times \$22.50 = \$67.50
\]

Then, let's calculate the cost for the shoes:
- Mishka bought 3 pairs of shoes.
- Each pair of shoes costs $42.

\[
\text{Cost of shoes} = 3 \times \$42 = \$126
\]

Now, we add up the costs of all the items:

\[
\text{Total cost} = \$49.50 + \$67.50 + \$126 = \$243
\]

Therefore, Mishka spent a total of $243 on all the clothing items.

The answer is: 243
