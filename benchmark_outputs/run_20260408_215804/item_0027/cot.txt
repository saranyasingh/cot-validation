EXPLANATION
To determine how much Mishka spent on all the clothing items, we need to calculate the total cost of each type of item separately and then sum these amounts.

First, let's find out how much Mishka spent on shorts:
- Mishka bought 3 pairs of shorts.
- Each pair of shorts costs $16.50.
So, the total cost for the shorts is \(3 \times \$16.50 = \$49.50\).

Next, let's calculate the cost of the pants:
- Mishka bought 3 pairs of pants.
- Each pair of pants costs $22.50.
Therefore, the total cost for the pants is \(3 \times \$22.50 = \$67.50\).

Finally, let's compute the cost of the shoes:
- Mishka bought 3 pairs of shoes.
- Each pair of shoes costs $42.
Hence, the total cost for the shoes is \(3 \times \$42 = \$126\).

Now that we have calculated the individual costs, we can add them together to get the total amount Mishka spent:
Total cost = Cost of shorts + Cost of pants + Cost of shoes
= \$49.50 + \$67.50 + \$126

Adding these amounts gives us:
\$49.50 + \$67.50 + \$126 = \$243

Thus, Mishka spent a total of \$243 on all the clothing items.

THE ANSWER IS: 243
