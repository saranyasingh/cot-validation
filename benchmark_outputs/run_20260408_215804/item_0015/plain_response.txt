To determine the percentage of students enrolled in hip-hop dance, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the number of students enrolled in contemporary dance.
2. Determine the number of students who did not enroll in contemporary dance.
3. Calculate the number of students enrolled in jazz dance from the remaining students.
4. Find out how many students were left for hip-hop dance.
5. Convert this number into a percentage of the total student population.

Let's go through each step in detail:

1. **Calculate the number of students enrolled in contemporary dance:**
   \[
   \text{Number of contemporary dancers} = 20\% \times 20 = 0.20 \times 20 = 4
   \]

2. **Determine the number of students who did not enroll in contemporary dance:**
   \[
   \text{Remaining students} = 20 - 4 = 16
   \]

3. **Calculate the number of students enrolled in jazz dance:**
   \[
   \text{Number of jazz dancers} = 25\% \times 16 = 0.25 \times 16 = 4
   \]

4. **Find out how many students were left for hip-hop dance:**
   \[
   \text{Students left for hip-hop dance} = 16 - 4 = 12
   \]

5. **Convert this number into a percentage of the total student population:**
   \[
   \text{Percentage of students in hip-hop dance} = \left( \frac{12}{20} \right) \times 100\% = 0.60 \times 100\% = 60\%
   \]

Therefore, the percentage of the entire students enrolled in hip-hop dance is \(60\%\). The answer is: 60%
