EXPLANATION
1. First, let's calculate how many students enrolled in contemporary dance.
   - There were 20 students in total.
   - 20% of them enrolled in contemporary dance.
   - \( 20 \times 0.20 = 4 \) students.

2. Next, we need to find out how many students remain after those enrolled in contemporary dance have been accounted for.
   - Total students: 20
   - Students in contemporary dance: 4
   - Remaining students: \( 20 - 4 = 16 \)

3. Of these remaining students, 25% enrolled in jazz dance.
   - 25% of the remaining 16 students enrolled in jazz dance.
   - \( 16 \times 0.25 = 4 \) students.

4. Now, we subtract the number of students who enrolled in both contemporary and jazz dances (jazz dancers) from the total number of students to determine how many enrolled in hip-hop dance.
   - Total students: 20
   - Contemporary dancers: 4
   - Jazz dancers: 4
   - Hip-hop dancers: \( 20 - 4 - 4 = 12 \)

5. To find the percentage of the entire student body that enrolled in hip-hop dance, we use the formula:
   - Percentage = (Number of hip-hop dancers / Total number of students) * 100%
   - Percentage = \( \frac{12}{20} \times 100\% = 60\% \)

Therefore, the answer is: 60
