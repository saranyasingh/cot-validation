To determine Josh's profit, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total cost**: First, we find out how much Josh spent on buying the house and making repairs.
   - Cost of the house: $80,000
   - Repair costs: $50,000
   - Total cost = $80,000 + $50,000 = $130,000

2. **Determine the increase in value**: The problem states that the repair increased the value of the house by 150%.
   - Increase in value = 150% of $80,000
   - 150% as a decimal is 1.5
   - Increase in value = 1.5 * $80,000 = $120,000

3. **Calculate the net profit**: To find the net profit, subtract the total cost from the increase in value.
   - Net profit = Increase in value - Total cost
   - Net profit = $120,000 - $130,000 = -$10,000

4. **Interpret the result**: A negative net profit indicates a loss.

**Conclusion**: Josh made a loss of $10,000.

The answer is: 10
