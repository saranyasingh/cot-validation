To determine Josh's profit from flipping the house, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the new value of the house after the repairs.
2. Determine the total cost of buying and repairing the house.
3. Subtract the total cost from the new value of the house to find the profit.

Step 1: Calculate the new value of the house after the repairs.
- Initial purchase price: $80,000
- Amount spent on repairs: $50,000
- Increase in value due to repairs: 150% of the initial purchase price

First, calculate 150% of the initial purchase price:
\[ 150\% \times \$80,000 = 1.5 \times \$80,000 = \$120,000 \]

Add this increase to the original purchase price to get the new value of the house:
\[ \$80,000 + \$120,000 = \$200,000 \]

Step 2: Determine the total cost of buying and repairing the house.
- Cost of purchasing the house: $80,000
- Amount spent on repairs: $50,000

Total cost:
\[ \$80,000 + \$50,000 = \$130,000 \]

Step 3: Subtract the total cost from the new value of the house to find the profit.
\[ \$200,000 - \$130,000 = \$70,000 \]

Therefore, Josh made a profit of $\boxed{70000}$.
