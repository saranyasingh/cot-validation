To determine how much Janet makes every day at the farmers' market, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the total number of eggs laid each day.
2. Subtract the number of eggs eaten for breakfast.
3. Subtract the number of eggs used to bake muffins for her friends.
4. Determine the remaining number of eggs that can be sold at the farmers' market.
5. Calculate the revenue from selling these eggs.

Let's go through each step in detail:

1. **Total number of eggs laid each day**:
   - Eggs per day = 16

2. **Eggs eaten for breakfast**:
   - Breakfast eggs = 3

3. **Eggs used to bake muffins for her friends**:
   - Muffin eggs = 4

4. **Remaining eggs for sale**:
   - Remaining eggs = Total eggs - Breakfast eggs - Muffin eggs
   - Remaining eggs = 16 - 3 - 4 = 9

5. **Revenue from selling the eggs**:
   - Revenue per egg = $2
   - Revenue from 9 eggs = 9 * $2 = $18

Therefore, Janet makes $18 every day at the farmers' market. The answer is: 18
