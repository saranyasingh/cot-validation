EXPLANATION
To determine how much Janet makes every day at the farmers' market, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total number of eggs laid per day:** 
   - Janet's ducks lay 16 eggs per day.

2. **Subtract the eggs eaten for breakfast:**
   - She eats 3 eggs for breakfast.
   - Remaining eggs after breakfast = \( 16 \text{ eggs} - 3 \text{ eggs} = 13 \text{ eggs} \).

3. **Subtract the eggs used for baking muffins:**
   - She uses 4 eggs for baking muffins.
   - Remaining eggs after baking = \( 13 \text{ eggs} - 4 \text{ eggs} = 9 \text{ eggs} \).

4. **Determine the amount earned from selling the remaining eggs:**
   - Each egg is sold for $2.
   - Total earnings from selling the eggs = \( 9 \text{ eggs} \times \$2/\text{egg} = \$18 \).

5. **Summarize the calculation:**
   - The total amount made per day at the farmers' market = $18.

Thus, Janet makes $18 every day at the farmers' market.

The answer is: 18
