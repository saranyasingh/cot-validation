To determine the total distance covered by each train over the two days, we need to calculate the distances traveled on each leg of their journey and then sum them up.

### Day 1:
- Distance traveled westward: 80 miles

### Day 2:
- Distance traveled northward: 150 miles

Now, let's add these distances together:

Total distance = Distance on Day 1 + Distance on Day 2
Total distance = 80 miles + 150 miles
Total distance = 230 miles

Thus, the total distance covered by each train in the two days is **230 miles**.
