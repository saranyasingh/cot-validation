To determine how many boxes of pizza Marie ordered, we need to calculate the total cost of the other items she bought and then find out what fraction of her total payment went towards the pizzas.

First, let's calculate the cost of the chicken meal:
\[ \text{Cost of chicken meal} = \$12 \]

Next, we calculate the cost of the 5 packs of milk:
\[ \text{Cost of milk} = 5 \times \$3 = \$15 \]

Then, we calculate the cost of the 4 apples:
\[ \text{Cost of apples} = 4 \times \$1.50 = \$6 \]

Now, let's add up the cost of these three items:
\[ \text{Total cost of chicken meal, milk, and apples} = \$12 + \$15 + \$6 = \$33 \]

Marie paid a total of \$50 for all her orders. To find out how much was spent on the pizzas, we subtract the cost of the chicken meal, milk, and apples from the total amount paid:
\[ \text{Total cost of pizzas} = \$50 - \$33 = \$17 \]

Each box of pizza costs \$8.50. To find out how many boxes of pizza Marie ordered, we divide the total cost of the pizzas by the price per box:
\[ \text{Number of boxes of pizza} = \frac{\$17}{\$8.50} = 2 \]

Therefore, Marie ordered 2 boxes of pizza.
