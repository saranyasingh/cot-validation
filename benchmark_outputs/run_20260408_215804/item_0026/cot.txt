EXPLANATION

To determine how many boxes of pizza Marie ordered, we need to calculate the total cost of all items she purchased except for the boxes of pizza.

First, let's list out the known quantities and their respective prices:
- Chicken meal: $12
- Packs of milk: 5 packs at $3 each = 5 * $3 = $15
- Apples: 4 apples at $1.50 each = 4 * $1.50 = $6
- Boxes of pizza: Let's denote the number of boxes as x.

Next, we add up the costs of these items:
Total cost of chicken meal + Total cost of milk + Total cost of apples = Total cost of other items
$12 + $15 + $6 = $33

Now, we know that Marie paid a total of $50. Therefore, the remaining amount after subtracting the cost of chicken meal, milk, and apples from the total payment must have been spent on the boxes of pizza.
$50 - $33 = $17

Finally, we divide this remaining amount by the price per box of pizza to find out how many boxes Marie ordered.
$x = \frac{17}{8.50}$

Calculating this gives us:
$x = 2$

Therefore, Marie ordered 2 boxes of pizza.
