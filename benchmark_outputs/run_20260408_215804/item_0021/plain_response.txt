To determine how much water is in the remaining 24 liters after spilling one liter of the orange drink, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the amount of water in each type of drink before any is spilled.
2. Determine the total volume of each drink left after spilling.
3. Calculate the amount of water in the remaining drinks.

**Step 1: Calculate the amount of water in each type of drink before any is spilled**

- **Orange Drink**: 
  - Initial volume = 10 liters
  - Water content = \(\frac{2}{3} \times 10 = \frac{20}{3}\) liters

- **Pineapple Drink**: 
  - Initial volume = 15 liters
  - Water content = \(\frac{3}{5} \times 15 = \frac{45}{5} = 9\) liters

**Step 2: Determine the total volume of each drink left after spilling**

- After spilling 1 liter from the orange drink:
  - Orange Drink remaining = \(10 - 1 = 9\) liters
  - Pineapple Drink remains unchanged at 15 liters

**Step 3: Calculate the amount of water in the remaining drinks**

- **Water in Orange Drink remaining**: 
  - Remaining volume = 9 liters
  - Water content = \(\frac{20}{3} \div 9 = \frac{20}{27}\) liters (since we divided the initial water content by the new volume)

- **Water in Pineapple Drink**: 
  - Remains unchanged at 9 liters

**Total Water Calculation**
- Total water = Water in Orange Drink + Water in Pineapple Drink
- Total water = \(\frac{20}{27} + 9\)
- Convert 9 to a fraction with denominator 27: \(9 = \frac{243}{27}\)
- Add the fractions: \(\frac{20}{27} + \frac{243}{27} = \frac{263}{27}\) liters

Thus, the total amount of water in the remaining 24 liters is \(\frac{263}{27}\) liters or approximately 9.81 liters.

The answer is: \(\frac{263}{27}\) liters
