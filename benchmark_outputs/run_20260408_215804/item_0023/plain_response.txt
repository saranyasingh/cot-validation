To determine how many DVDs Billy sold on Tuesday, let's break down the information given:

1. **First 3 Customers**: Each of these 3 customers buys 1 DVD.
   - Number of DVDs sold to the first 3 customers = \( 3 \times 1 = 3 \) DVDs

2. **Next 2 Customers**: Each of these 2 customers buys 2 DVDs.
   - Number of DVDs sold to the next 2 customers = \( 2 \times 2 = 4 \) DVDs

3. **Last 3 Customers**: These 3 customers do not buy any DVDs.
   - Since no DVDs were bought from this group, they do not contribute to the total number of DVDs sold.

Now, we sum up all the DVDs sold:
- Total DVDs sold = DVDs from the first 3 customers + DVDs from the next 2 customers
- Total DVDs sold = \( 3 + 4 = 7 \) DVDs

Therefore, the total number of DVDs Billy sold on Tuesday is 7. 

The answer is: 7
