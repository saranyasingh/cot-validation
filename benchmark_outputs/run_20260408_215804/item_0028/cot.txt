EXPLANATION
To determine how much Cynthia will spend on ice cream after 60 days, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total number of servings of ice cream consumed in 60 days**: Since Cynthia eats one serving of ice cream every night, over 60 nights, she will consume \(60 \times 1 = 60\) servings.

2. **Determine the number of cartons needed**: Each carton contains 15 servings. To find out how many cartons are required to get 60 servings, divide the total servings by the number of servings per carton: \(\frac{60}{15} = 4\) cartons.

3. **Calculate the total cost**: Each carton costs $4.00. Therefore, for 4 cartons, the total cost is \(4 \times 4.00 = 16.00\).

**Conclusion**: Cynthia will spend $16.00 on ice cream after 60 days.
