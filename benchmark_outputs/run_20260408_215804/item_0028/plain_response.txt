To determine how much Cynthia will spend on ice cream after 60 days, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the number of servings Cynthia consumes in 60 days:**
   - Cynthia eats 1 serving of ice cream every night.
   - There are 30 nights in a month (assuming no leap year).
   - Therefore, in 60 days, she will consume \( 60 \times 1 = 60 \) servings.

2. **Determine the number of cartons needed:**
   - Each carton contains 15 servings.
   - To find out how many cartons are needed for 60 servings, divide the total servings by the servings per carton:
     \[
     \frac{60}{15} = 4 \text{ cartons}
     \]

3. **Calculate the total cost:**
   - Each carton costs $4.00.
   - Multiply the number of cartons by the cost per carton:
     \[
     4 \text{ cartons} \times \$4.00/\text{carton} = \$16.00
     \]

Therefore, after 60 days, Cynthia will spend \(\boxed{\$16}\) on ice cream.
