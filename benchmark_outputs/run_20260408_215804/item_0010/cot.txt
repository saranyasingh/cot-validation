EXPLANATION

To determine Eliza's total earnings for the week, we need to break down her work into two parts: her regular hours and her overtime hours.

1. **Calculate Regular Earnings:**
   - Eliza's regular rate is $10 per hour.
   - She worked for 40 hours at her regular rate.
   - Therefore, her regular earnings = \( 40 \text{ hours} \times \$10/\text{hour} = \$400 \).

2. **Calculate Overtime Pay:**
   - For the remaining 5 hours (since she worked a total of 45 hours), she earns time-and-a-half on top of her regular rate.
   - Her overtime rate is \( 1.2 \times \$10 = \$12/\text{hour} \).
   - Therefore, her overtime earnings = \( 5 \text{ hours} \times \$12/\text{hour} = \$60 \).

3. **Calculate Total Earnings:**
   - Total earnings = Regular earnings + Overtime pay
   - Total earnings = \( \$400 + \$60 = \$460 \).

Thus, Eliza's total earnings for the week are $460.

The answer is: 460
