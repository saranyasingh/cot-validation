To calculate Eliza's earnings for the week, we need to consider both her regular hours and her overtime hours.

1. **Regular Hours Earnings**:
   - Regular hours = 40 hours
   - Regular rate = $10/hour

   Regular earnings = 40 hours * $10/hour = $400

2. **Overtime Hours Earnings**:
   - Overtime hours = Total hours worked - Regular hours
   - Overtime hours = 45 hours - 40 hours = 5 hours

   Overtime rate = Regular rate * 1.2
   Overtime rate = $10/hour * 1.2 = $12/hour

   Overtime earnings = 5 hours * $12/hour = $60

3. **Total Earnings**:
   - Total earnings = Regular earnings + Overtime earnings
   - Total earnings = $400 + $60 = $460

Therefore, Eliza's total earnings for the week are $460. The answer is: 460
