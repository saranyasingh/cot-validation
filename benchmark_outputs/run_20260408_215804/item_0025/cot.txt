To find the original price of the book before the discount, we need to work backwards from the sale price.

**Premise 1:** Kyle bought the book for $19.50 after a 25% discount.
- Let \( P \) represent the original price of the book.

Since the discount is 25%, this means that the book was sold at 75% (or 0.75 times) its original price because 100% - 25% = 75%.

We can set up an equation based on this information:
\[ 0.75P = \$19.50 \]

**Step 1:** To solve for \( P \), we divide both sides of the equation by 0.75:
\[ P = \frac{\$19.50}{0.75} \]

**Step 2:** Perform the division:
\[ P = \frac{19.50}{0.75} = 26 \]

So, the original price of the book was **\$26**.

Therefore, the answer is: 26.
