To find the original price of the book before the discount, we need to work backwards from the discounted price using the given information that this price represents 75% of the original price (since a 25% discount means Kyle paid 75% of the original price).

Let's denote the original price as \( P \). According to the problem, \( 0.75P = \$19.50 \).

To solve for \( P \), we divide both sides of the equation by 0.75:

\[
P = \frac{19.50}{0.75}
\]

Now let's perform the division:

\[
P = 26
\]

So, the original price of the book was **$26**.
