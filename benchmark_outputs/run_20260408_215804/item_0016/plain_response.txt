To determine which purchase plan maximizes profit for the merchant, we need to calculate the potential profits based on the expected price increases for both the jewelry and electronic gadgets markets.

First, let's calculate the increase in value for each item:

1. **Jewelry**:
   - Initial value: $5,000
   - Expected increase: 2.5%
   \[
   \text{Increase} = 5000 \times 0.025 = 125
   \]
   \[
   \text{New Value} = 5000 + 125 = 5125
   \]

2. **Electronic Gadgets**:
   - Initial value: $8,000
   - Expected increase: 1.2%
   \[
   \text{Increase} = 8000 \times 0.012 = 96
   \]
   \[
   \text{New Value} = 8000 + 96 = 8096
   \]

Now, let's compare the new values after the expected increases:

- Jewelry: $5,125
- Electronic Gadgets: $8,096

Since $8,096 > $5,125, purchasing the electronic gadgets offers a higher profit.

Next, we calculate the profit from each option:

1. **Profit from Jewelry Purchase**:
   - Cost: $5,000
   - New Value: $5,125
   \[
   \text{Profit} = 5125 - 5000 = 125
   \]

2. **Profit from Electronic Gadgets Purchase**:
   - Cost: $8,000
   - New Value: $8,096
   \[
   \text{Profit} = 8096 - 8000 = 96
   \]

Comparing the two profits:
- Jewelry Profit: $125
- Electronic Gadgets Profit: $96

Therefore, the merchant should choose to purchase the electronic gadgets for a profit of $96. The answer is: 96
