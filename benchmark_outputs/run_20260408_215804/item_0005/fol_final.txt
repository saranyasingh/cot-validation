LABEL: ## RULES :: 
1. **Daily Feed Calculation**:
   - If each chicken gets 3 cups of feed per day, then the total daily feed required can be calculated by multiplying the number of chickens by the feed requirement per chicken.
   ```plaintext
   Rule-1: Total_daily_feed_required = Number_of_chickens * Feed_per_chicken_per_day
   ```
2. **Morning Feed Calculation**:
   - Given morning feed: 15 cups
   ```plaintext
   Rule-2: Morning_feed_given = 15 cups
   ```

3. **Afternoon Feed Calculation**:
   - Given afternoon feed: 25 cups
   ```plaintext
   Rule-3: Afternoon_feed_given = 25 cups
   ```

LABEL: ## INFERENCES :: 
1. **Remaining Feed Needed**:
   - The remaining feed needed after giving morning and afternoon feeds is calculated by subtracting the sum of morning and afternoon feeds from the total daily feed required.
   ```plaintext
   Rule-4: Remaining_feed_needed = Total_daily_feed_required - (Morning_feed_given + Afternoon_feed_given)
   ```
   Substituting the values:
   ```plaintext
   Rule-4: Remaining_feed_needed = 60 - (15 + 25) = 60 - 40 = 20 cups
   ```
