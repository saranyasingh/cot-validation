EXPLANATION

1. Let's start by determining how much feed Wendi provides to all her chickens per day.
   - Each chicken gets 3 cups of feed per day.

2. We know that there are 20 chickens.
   - Therefore, total daily feed required = Number of chickens × Feed per chicken
   - Total daily feed required = 20 × 3 = 60 cups

3. Now we need to calculate how much feed has already been given out during the day.
   - Morning feed: 15 cups
   - Afternoon feed: 25 cups
   - Total feed given so far = Morning feed + Afternoon feed
   - Total feed given so far = 15 + 25 = 40 cups

4. To find out how much more feed is needed for the evening (final) meal, subtract the amount of feed given from the total daily requirement.
   - Remaining feed needed = Total daily feed required - Total feed given so far
   - Remaining feed needed = 60 - 40 = 20 cups

Therefore, Wendi needs to give her chickens 20 cups of feed in the final meal of the day.

The answer is: 20
