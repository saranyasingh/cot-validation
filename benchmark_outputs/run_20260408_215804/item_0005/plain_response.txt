To determine how many cups of feed Wendi needs for her flock in the final meal of the day, we can follow these steps:

1. Calculate the total amount of feed given in the morning and afternoon:
   - Morning feed: 15 cups
   - Afternoon feed: 25 cups

2. Add the morning and afternoon feed amounts together:
   \[
   15 + 25 = 40 \text{ cups}
   \]

3. Determine the number of chickens Wendi has:
   \[
   \text{Number of chickens} = 20
   \]

4. Divide the total amount of feed by the number of chickens to find out how much feed each chicken gets in one meal:
   \[
   \frac{40}{20} = 2 \text{ cups per chicken}
   \]

5. Since the problem states that the feed is divided into three separate meals, calculate the amount of feed needed for the final meal:
   \[
   2 \text{ cups/chicken} \times 20 \text{ chickens} = 40 \text{ cups}
   \]

Therefore, the answer is: The answer is: 40.
