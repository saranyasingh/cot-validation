Let's break down the information given:

1. Seattle has 20 sheep.
2. Charleston has 4 times as many sheep as Seattle.
3. Toulouse has twice as many sheep as Charleston.

First, let's calculate how many sheep Charleston has:
Charleston = 4 * Seattle
Charleston = 4 * 20
Charleston = 80 sheep

Next, we'll find out how many sheep Toulouse has:
Toulouse = 2 * Charleston
Toulouse = 2 * 80
Toulouse = 160 sheep

Now, to find the total number of sheep for all three cities, we add them up:
Total = Seattle + Charleston + Toulouse
Total = 20 + 80 + 160
Total = 260 sheep
