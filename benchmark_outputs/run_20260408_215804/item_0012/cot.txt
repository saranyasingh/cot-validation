EXPLANATION
To find the total cost of Toula's purchases at the bakery, we need to calculate the cost of each type of pastry separately and then sum these costs.

1. **Cost of Donuts**:  
   - Toula bought 3 dozen donuts.
   - Each dozen donuts costs $68.
   - Total cost of donuts = \( 3 \times 68 \).

2. **Cost of Mini Cupcakes**:  
   - Toula bought 2 dozen mini cupcakes.
   - Each dozen mini cupcakes costs $80.
   - Total cost of mini cupcakes = \( 2 \times 80 \).

3. **Cost of Mini Cheesecakes**:  
   - Toula bought 6 dozen mini cheesecakes.
   - Each dozen mini cheesecakes costs $55.
   - Total cost of mini cheesecakes = \( 6 \times 55 \).

4. **Total Cost Calculation**:
   - Sum the individual costs to get the total amount spent.

Let's perform the calculations step-by-step:

1. **Donuts Cost**:
   - \( 3 \times 68 = 204 \)

2. **Mini Cupcakes Cost**:
   - \( 2 \times 80 = 160 \)

3. **Mini Cheesecakes Cost**:
   - \( 6 \times 55 = 330 \)

Now, add all three amounts together to find the total cost:

- Total cost = Donut cost + Mini cupcake cost + Mini cheesecake cost
- Total cost = \( 204 + 160 + 330 \)
- Total cost = \( 794 \)

Therefore, the total cost of Toula’s purchase at the bakery is **$794**.
