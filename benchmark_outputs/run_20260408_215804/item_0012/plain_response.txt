To determine the total cost of Toula's purchases at the bakery, we need to calculate the cost for each type of pastry separately and then sum them up.

1. **Donuts**: 
   - Each dozen costs $68.
   - She bought 3 dozens.
   \[
   \text{Cost of donuts} = 3 \times 68 = \$204
   \]

2. **Mini Cupcakes**:
   - Each dozen costs $80.
   - She bought 2 dozens.
   \[
   \text{Cost of mini cupcakes} = 2 \times 80 = \$160
   \]

3. **Mini Cheesecakes**:
   - Each dozen costs $55.
   - She bought 6 dozens.
   \[
   \text{Cost of mini cheesecakes} = 6 \times 55 = \$330
   \]

Next, we add the costs together to find the total amount spent:

\[
\text{Total cost} = 204 + 160 + 330 = \$794
\]

Therefore, the total cost of Toula’s purchases is $\boxed{\$794}$.

The answer is: 794
