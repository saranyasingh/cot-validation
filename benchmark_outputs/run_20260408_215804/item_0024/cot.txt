EXPLANATION

To determine how much shorter a candle will be after burning from 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM, we need to calculate the total time the candle burns during this period.

1. **Identify the duration**: From 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM is a span of 4 hours.
2. **Determine the melting rate**: The candle melts at a rate of 2 centimeters per hour.
3. **Calculate the total melted length**: Multiply the melting rate by the total time burned.
   \[
   \text{Total melted length} = \text{Melted rate} \times \text{Duration}
   \]
   Substituting the given values:
   \[
   \text{Total melted length} = 2 \, \text{cm/hour} \times 4 \, \text{hours} = 8 \, \text{centimeters}
   \]

**Conclusion**: After burning from 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM, the candle will be 8 centimeters shorter than when it started.

Therefore, the answer is: 8
