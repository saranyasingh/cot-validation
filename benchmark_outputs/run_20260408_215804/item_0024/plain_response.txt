To determine how much shorter the candle will be after burning from 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM, we need to calculate the total time the candle has been burning and then use the given rate of melting.

1. Calculate the duration of the burning period:
   - The candle was burned from 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM.
   - This period spans 4 hours (from 1:00 PM to 3:00 PM) plus an additional half-hour (from 3:00 PM to 5:00 PM).
   - Total burn time = 4 hours + 0.5 hours = 4.5 hours.

2. Determine the amount of melting during this period:
   - The candle melts at a rate of 2 centimeters per hour.
   - Therefore, in 4.5 hours, the candle will melt \( 2 \text{ cm/hour} \times 4.5 \text{ hours} = 9 \text{ cm} \).

Thus, the candle will be 9 centimeters shorter after burning for 4.5 hours from 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM.

The answer is: 9
