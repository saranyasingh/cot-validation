EXPLANATION

1. First, we need to calculate Carlos's initial investment in the lemon tree.
   - Cost to plant = $90
   
2. Next, we determine how much Carlos earns per year from selling lemons.
   - He sells 7 lemons at $1.5 each.
   - Total earnings per year = 7 * $1.5 = $10.5
   
3. Now, we need to account for the annual expenses related to watering and feeding the tree.
   - Annual expense = $3
   
4. We then subtract the annual expenses from the annual earnings to find out his net profit per year.
   - Net profit per year = Earnings - Expenses = $10.5 - $3 = $7.5
   
5. To find out when Carlos starts earning money, we divide the total cost of planting by the net profit per year.
   - Number of years until start earning money = Total cost / Net profit per year = $90 / $7.5 ≈ 12

Therefore, Carlos will start earning money on the lemon tree after 12 years.

The answer is: 12
