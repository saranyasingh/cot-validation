To determine how many years it will take Carlos to start earning money from his lemon tree, we need to calculate the net profit per year and then find out how many years it takes to cover the initial investment of $90.

First, let's calculate the annual profit:
- Annual revenue from selling lemons = 7 lemons * $1.5/lemon = $10.5
- Annual expenses (watering and feeding) = $3

Net annual profit = Annual revenue - Annual expenses
= $10.5 - $3 = $7.5

Now, we need to find out how many years it will take for this net annual profit to equal the initial investment of $90.

Years needed to recover investment = Initial investment / Net annual profit
= $90 / $7.5 = 12 years

Therefore, it will take Carlos 12 years to start earning money on the lemon tree.

The answer is: 12
