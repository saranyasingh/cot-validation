Explanation:

1. Henry's total trip distance is 60 miles.
   Premise: Total trip length = 60 miles.

2. Henry first stopped at 20 miles into his trip.
   Premise: First stop = 20 miles.

3. His second stop was 15 miles before the end of the trip.
   Premise: Second stop = 45 miles (since it was 15 miles before the end).

4. To find out how far he traveled between his first and second stops,
   we need to calculate the difference between these two points on the trip.
   Calculation: Distance between first and second stops = 45 - 20 = 25 miles.

Therefore, the answer is: 25.
