No rules are needed here since all facts provide direct information without requiring any logical derivation.

### Inferences
We need to derive the birth year of Raymond's son. Given that Samantha is currently 31 years old and assuming the current year is \(Y\), we can calculate her birth year as \(Y - 31\).

Since Raymond's son was born when Samantha was 23 years old, and Raymond's son is 23 years older than Samantha, we add 23 years to Samantha's current age to get the birth year of Raymond's son.

Let's denote the current year as \(Y\). Therefore, the birth year of Raymond's son is calculated as:
\[ \text{BirthYear(Raymond's Son)} = Y - 31 + 23 = Y - 8 \]
