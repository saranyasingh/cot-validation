EXPLANATION
To determine how many dozen eggs Claire eats in 4 weeks, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the total number of days in 4 weeks.
2. Determine how many eggs Claire eats per day.
3. Find out how many eggs she consumes in 4 weeks.
4. Convert the total number of eggs into dozens.

Step 1: Total number of days in 4 weeks
There are 7 days in a week, so in 4 weeks there would be:
\[ 4 \text{ weeks} \times 7 \text{ days/week} = 28 \text{ days} \]

Step 2: Eggs consumed per day
Claire makes a 3-egg omelet every morning. Therefore, she eats:
\[ 3 \text{ eggs/day} \]

Step 3: Total eggs eaten in 4 weeks
Now that we know she eats 3 eggs per day over 28 days, the total number of eggs consumed in 4 weeks is:
\[ 3 \text{ eggs/day} \times 28 \text{ days} = 84 \text{ eggs} \]

Step 4: Converting eggs to dozens
Since 1 dozen equals 12 eggs, we convert the total number of eggs to dozens:
\[ \frac{84 \text{ eggs}}{12 \text{ eggs/dozen}} = 7 \text{ dozens} \]

Therefore, the answer is: 7.
