To determine how many dozen eggs Claire will consume in 4 weeks, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the number of omelets made per week.
2. Determine the total number of omelets over 4 weeks.
3. Convert the total number of omelets into dozens of eggs.

Step 1: Number of omelets per day
Claire makes 1 omelet each day.

Step 2: Total number of omelets in 4 weeks
Since there are 7 days in a week:
\[ \text{Total omelets} = 1 \text{ omelet/day} \times 7 \text{ days/week} \times 4 \text{ weeks} = 28 \text{ omelets} \]

Step 3: Convert omelets to dozens
There are 12 eggs in one dozen:
\[ \text{Number of dozens} = \frac{28 \text{ omelets}}{12 \text{ eggs/dozen}} \approx 2.33 \text{ dozens} \]

Therefore, Claire will eat approximately 2.33 dozen eggs in 4 weeks.

The answer is: 2.33
