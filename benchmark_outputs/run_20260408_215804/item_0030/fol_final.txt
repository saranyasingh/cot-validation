### ## FACTS
## FACT-1: Heels cost 33 dollars each :: HeelPrice(33)
## FACT-2: Second pair of heels costs twice the first pair :: HeelPrice(66)

### ## RULES
## RULE-1: Boots cost the sum of the prices of two pairs of heels :: BootsCost(B) ↔ HeelPrice(33) + HeelPrice(66)

### ## INFERENCES
## INF-1: Total cost of heels plus $5 equals cost of boots :: BootsCost(HeelPrice(33) + HeelPrice(66)) + 5 = BootsCost(B)
