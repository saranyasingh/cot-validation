EXPLANATION

To calculate Jill's annual salary, we need to consider both her hourly earnings as a teacher and her weekly earnings as a cheerleader.

First, let's calculate her total earnings as a teacher:
- Hourly rate = $20/hour
- Hours worked per week as a teacher = 35 hours
- Weeks worked in a year = 50 weeks

Total earnings as a teacher = Hourly rate × Hours worked per week × Weeks worked in a year
= $20/hour × 35 hours/week × 50 weeks/year
= $7,000/year

Next, let's calculate her total earnings as a cheerleader:
- Hourly rate = $30/hour
- Hours worked per week as a cheerleader = 15 hours
- Weeks worked in a year = 50 weeks

Total earnings as a cheerleader = Hourly rate × Hours worked per week × Weeks worked in a year
= $30/hour × 15 hours/week × 50 weeks/year
= $22,500/year

Now that we have calculated her earnings for both roles, we can find her total annual salary by adding these amounts together:
Total annual salary = Earnings as a teacher + Earnings as a cheerleader
= $7,000/year + $22,500/year
= $29,500/year

Therefore, Jill's annual salary is $29,500.
