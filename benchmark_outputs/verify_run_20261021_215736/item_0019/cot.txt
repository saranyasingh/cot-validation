To determine how many dozens of eggs Claire will consume in 4 weeks, we need to break down the problem into smaller steps.

**Step 1:** Determine how many eggs Claire eats per week.
- Claire makes a 3-egg omelet every morning.
- There are 7 days in a week.
So, the total number of eggs consumed per week = 3 eggs/omelette * 7 days/week = 21 eggs/week.

**Step 2:** Calculate the total number of eggs consumed in 4 weeks.
- We know that there are 21 eggs consumed per week.
- To find out how many eggs are consumed in 4 weeks, multiply the weekly consumption by the number of weeks.
Total eggs consumed in 4 weeks = 21 eggs/week * 4 weeks = 84 eggs.

**Step 3:** Convert the total number of eggs into dozens.
- One dozen equals 12 eggs.
To convert 84 eggs into dozens, divide the total number of eggs by 12.
Eggs in dozens = Total eggs / 12 = 84 eggs / 12 eggs/dozen = 7 dozens.

Therefore, the total number of dozens of eggs Claire will eat in 4 weeks is **7**.

The answer is: 7
