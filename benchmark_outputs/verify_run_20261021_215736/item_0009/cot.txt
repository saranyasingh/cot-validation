   \]

3. **Third Segment (Reduced Speed):**
   - Distance = Speed × Time
   - John drove for 0.5 hours at 30 mph.
   \[
   \text{Distance} = 30 \, \text{mph} \times 0.5 \, \text{hours} = 15 \, \text{miles}
   \]

4. **Fourth Segment (Higher Speed):**
   - Distance = Speed × Time

   \[
   \text{Distance} = 80 \, \text{mph} \times 2 \, \text{hours} = 160 \, \text{miles}

\[
