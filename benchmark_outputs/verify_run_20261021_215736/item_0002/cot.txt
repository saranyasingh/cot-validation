EXPLANATION
Let's break down the information given to form our logical syllogism.

1. **Premise 1**: A robe requires 2 bolts of blue fiber.
   - This tells us directly about the amount of blue fiber needed.

2. **Premise 2**: A robe also requires half as much white fiber as blue fiber.
   - Since we know there are 2 bolts of blue fiber, then half of this would be \( \frac{2}{2} = 1 \) bolt of white fiber.
   
3. **Conclusion**: To find the total number of bolts required, we add the blue fiber (2 bolts) and the white fiber (1 bolt).
   - Total bolts = Blue fiber + White fiber = 2 + 1 = 3 bolts.

Therefore, the answer is: 3
