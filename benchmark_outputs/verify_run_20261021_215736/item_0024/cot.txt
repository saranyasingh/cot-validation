EXPLANATION
To determine how much shorter the candle will be after burning from 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM, we need to calculate the total time the candle has been burning during this period.

First, let's identify the start and end times:
- Start time: 1:00 PM
- End time: 5:00 PM

Next, we calculate the duration of the burn:
\[ \text{Duration} = \text{End Time} - \text{Start Time} = 5:00 PM - 1:00 PM = 4 \text{ hours} \]

Now, we know that the candle melts at a rate of 2 centimeters per hour. To find out how much shorter the candle will be after 4 hours of burning, we multiply the melting rate by the duration of burning:
\[ \text{Shortening} = \text{Melting Rate} \times \text{Duration} = 2 \text{ cm/hour} \times 4 \text{ hours} = 8 \text{ cm} \]

Therefore, the candle will be 8 centimeters shorter after burning from 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM.

The answer is: 8
