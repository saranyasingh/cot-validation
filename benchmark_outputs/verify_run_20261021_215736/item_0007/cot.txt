Explanation:

1. We know that Seattle has 20 sheep.
   Premise: Seattle has 20 sheep.

2. Charleston has four times as many sheep as Seattle.
   Premise: Since Seattle has 20 sheep, we calculate Charleston's sheep count as \( 20 \times 4 = 80 \) sheep.

3. Toulouse has twice as many sheep as Charleston.
   Premise: Charleston has 80 sheep, so we calculate Toulouse's sheep count as \( 80 \times 2 = 160 \) sheep.

4. To find the total number of sheep across all three locations, we add their counts together:
   Total = Seattle + Charleston + Toulouse
   Total = 20 + 80 + 160
   Total = 260 sheep

Therefore, the answer is: 260
