EXPLANATION
Let's break down the problem into clear steps using a logical syllogism.

**Premise 1:** Kyle paid $19.50 after a 25% discount on the original price.
- This tells us that the discounted price ($19.50) represents 75% of the original price (since 100% - 25% = 75%).

**Premise 2:** Let \( P \) represent the original price of the book.
- We need to find \( P \).

**Step-by-step Reasoning:**
1. **Calculate the percentage relationship between the discounted price and the original price:**
   Since 25% off means paying 75%, we have:
   \[
   0.75P = 19.50
   \]

2. **Solve for \( P \):**
   To isolate \( P \), divide both sides by 0.75:
   \[
   P = \frac{19.50}{0.75}
   \]
   
3. **Perform the division:**
   \[
   P = 26
   \]

Therefore, the original price of the book was $26.

**Answer:** The answer is: 26.
