EXPLANATION

1. Janet's ducks lay 16 eggs per day.
   Premise: This establishes the total production rate of eggs.

2. She eats three eggs for breakfast every morning.
   Premise: This reduces the available eggs to those left after eating.

3. She also bakes muffins for her friends every day with four eggs.
   Premise: This further reduces the available eggs based on baking needs.

4. To find out how many eggs are left after these activities, we subtract what she consumes from the total production.
   Calculation: \(16 - (3 + 4) = 16 - 7 = 9\) eggs remaining.

5. These leftover eggs are sold at the farmers' market for $2 per egg.
   Premise: This sets the price per egg that Janet earns from selling them.

6. To calculate the total earnings, we multiply the number of eggs left by the price per egg.
   Calculation: \(9 \times 2 = \$18\).

Therefore, Janet makes $18 every day at the farmers' market.

The answer is: 18
