To determine which purchase plan maximizes profit at the end of the month, we need to calculate the future value of both purchases considering their respective growth rates.

### Step 1: Calculate Future Value of Jewelry Purchase Plan
- **Initial Price**: $5,000
- **Growth Rate**: 2.5%
- **Future Value Calculation**:
  \[
  \text{Future Value}_{\text{jewelry}} = \text{Initial Price} \times (1 + \text{Growth Rate})
  \]
  \[
  \text{Future Value}_{\text{jewelry}} = 5000 \times (1 + 0.025)
  \]
  \[
  \text{Future Value}_{\text{jewelry}} = 5000 \times 1.025
  \]
  \[
  \text{Future Value}_{\text{jewelry}} = 5125
  \]

### Step 2: Calculate Future Value of Electronic Gadgets Purchase Plan

  \[
  \text{Future Value}_{\text{gadgets}} = \text{Initial Price} \times (1 + \text{Growth Rate})
  \]
  \[
  \text{Future Value}_{\text{gadgets}} = 8000 \times (1 + 0.012)
  \]
  \[
  \text{Future Value}_{\text{gadgets}} = 8000 \times 1.012
  \]
  \[
  \text{Future Value}_{\text{gadgets}} = 8096
  \]

- **Future Value of Jewelry**: $5,125
