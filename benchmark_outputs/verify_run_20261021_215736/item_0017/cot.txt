To determine the total distance covered by each train over the two days, we need to break down the problem into smaller parts.

### Step-by-Step Logical Reasoning (Logical Syllogism)

**Premise 1:** Two trains leave San Rafael at the same time.
   - This establishes the starting point for our calculations.

**Premise 2:** Both trains start their journey westward.
   - This specifies the direction of travel for the first leg of their trip.

**Premise 3:** Each train travels for 80 miles westward.
   - This provides the distance traveled during the initial phase of their journey.

**Premise 4:** On the second day, both trains change direction and travel northward.
   - This introduces the new direction after the first 80 miles.

**Premise 5:** Each train covers an additional 150 miles northward on the second day.
   - This states the distance traveled during the subsequent phase of their journey.

**Conclusion:**
To find the total distance covered by each train over the two days, we add up the distances traveled in both directions.

**Calculation:**
- Distance traveled westward on the first day = 80 miles
- Distance traveled northward on the first day = 80 miles
- Total distance traveled westward = 80 + 80 = 160 miles
- Distance traveled northward on the second day = 150 miles
- Total distance traveled northward = 150 miles
- Total distance covered by each train = 160 miles (west) + 150 miles (north) = 310 miles

Therefore, the distance covered by each train in the two days is **310 miles**.

The answer is: 310
