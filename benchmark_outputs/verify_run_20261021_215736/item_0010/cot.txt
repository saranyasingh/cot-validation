EXPLANATION

To determine Eliza's earnings for the week, we need to break down her work into two parts: regular hours and overtime.

1. **Regular Hours Calculation**: 
   - Regular hours = 40 hours (as stated in the problem)
   - Regular rate = $10/hour

   Earnings for regular hours = Regular hours * Regular rate
   \[
   \text{Earnings for regular hours} = 40 \times 10 = \$400
   \]

2. **Overtime Hours Calculation**:
   - Overtime hours = Total hours - Regular hours
   \[
   \text{Overtime hours} = 45 - 40 = 5 \text{ hours}
   \]
   
   Overtime rate = Regular rate * 1.2
   \[
   \text{Overtime rate} = 10 \times 1.2 = \$12/\text{hour}
   \]

   Earnings for overtime hours = Overtime hours * Overtime rate
   \[
   \text{Earnings for overtime hours} = 5 \times 12 = \$60
   \]

3. **Total Earnings**:
   - Total earnings = Earnings for regular hours + Earnings for overtime hours
   \[
   \text{Total earnings} = 400 + 60 = \$460
   \]

Therefore, Eliza's total earnings for this week are $460.

The answer is: 460
