To determine the total cost of Toula's purchases at the bakery, we need to calculate the cost of each type of pastry separately and then sum these costs.

1. **Calculate the cost of the donuts:**
   - Toula bought 3 dozen donuts.
   - Each dozen donuts costs $68.
   \[
   \text{Cost of donuts} = 3 \times 68 = 204 \text{ dollars}
   \]

2. **Calculate the cost of the mini cupcakes:**
   - Toula bought 2 dozen mini cupcakes.
   - Each dozen mini cupcakes costs $80.
   \[
   \text{Cost of mini cupcakes} = 2 \times 80 = 160 \text{ dollars}
   \]

3. **Calculate the cost of the mini cheesecakes:**
   - Toula bought 6 dozen mini cheesecakes.
   - Each dozen mini cheesecakes costs $55.
   \[
   \text{Cost of mini cheesecakes} = 6 \times 55 = 330 \text{ dollars}
   \]

4. **Sum up all the individual costs to get the total cost:**
   \[
   \text{Total cost} = \text{Cost of donuts} + \text{Cost of mini cupcakes} + \text{Cost of mini cheesecakes}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Total cost} = 204 + 160 + 330 = 794 \text{ dollars}
   \]

**Explanation:** This process involves breaking down the problem into smaller parts (calculating the cost of each item), applying basic arithmetic operations (multiplication and addition), and finally summing up all the calculated costs to find the total expenditure. Each step logically leads to the next, ensuring that the calculation is accurate and comprehensive.
