EXPLANATION
Let's break down the problem into steps.

1. **Initial Purchase Price**: Josh bought the house for $80,000.
2. **Repairs Cost**: Josh spent $50,000 on repairs.
3. **Total Initial Investment**: Adding the purchase price and repair costs gives us $80,000 + $50,000 = $130,000.
4. **Value Increase Percentage**: The house's value increased by 150%.
5. **Increase in Value Calculation**:
   - A 150% increase means the house's new value is 150% more than its original value.
   - To find this amount, we calculate 150% of the initial purchase price: \( 150\% \times \$80,000 = 1.5 \times \$80,000 = \$120,000 \).
6. **New House Value**: Adding the increase in value to the initial purchase price gives us the new value of the house: \( \$80,000 + \$120,000 = \$200,000 \).

7. **Profit Calculation**: Profit is calculated as the difference between the selling price (new value) and the total investment (initial purchase price plus repairs cost). Therefore, the profit is:
   - Selling price: $200,000
   - Total investment: $130,000
   - Profit: \( \$200,000 - \$130,000 = \$70,000 \).

Therefore, the answer is: 70
