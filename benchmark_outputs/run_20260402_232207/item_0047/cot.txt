1. Let's start by defining the variables:
   - Let \( J \) be the number of peaches Jake has.
   - Let \( S \) be the number of peaches Steven has.
   - Let \( G \) be the number of peaches Jill has.

2. We know from the problem statement that Steven has 8 more peaches than Jill:
   \[
   S = G + 8
   \]
   Since we're given that Steven has 5 peaches:
   \[
   5 = G + 8
   \]

3. Solving for \( G \):
   \[
   G = 5 - 8
   \]
   \[
   G = -3
   \]

4. Now, let's find out how many peaches Jake has:
   \[
   J = S - 3
   \]
   Given \( S = 5 \), we substitute it into the equation:
   \[
   J = 5 - 3
   \]
   \[
   J = 2
   \]

5. Finally, to determine how many more peaches Jake has compared to Jill, we subtract Jill's peaches from Jake's peaches:
   \[
   \text{Difference} = J - G
   \]
   Substituting the values we found:
   \[
   \text{Difference} = 2 - (-3)
   \]
   Simplifying this expression:
   \[
   \text{Difference} = 2 + 3
   \]
   \[
   \text{Difference} = 5
   \]

6. Therefore, the answer is: 
   \[
   \boxed{5}
   \]
