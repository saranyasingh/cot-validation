Let's break down this problem into a logical syllogism:

**Premise 1:** Jessie lost 126 kilograms after starting to jog daily.
- This establishes the net weight loss over time.

**Premise 2:** Jessie's current weight is 66 kilograms.
- This gives us her present state.

**Conclusion:** We need to determine how much Jessie weighed before she started jogging.

To find out how much she weighed initially, we'll subtract the total weight loss (126 kg) from her current weight (66 kg).

**Logical Syllogism:**
1. Let \( W \) represent Jessie's initial weight.
2. After jogging daily, she lost 126 kg.
3. Her current weight is 66 kg.
4. Therefore, her initial weight minus the weight loss equals her current weight:
   \[
   W - 126 = 66
   \]
5. Solving for \( W \):
   \[
   W = 66 + 126
   \]
6. Adding these values together:
   \[
   W = 192
   \]

Thus, **the answer is: 192 kg.**

Jessie originally weighed 192 kilograms before she started jogging daily and began losing weight at that rate.
