To determine how many more boys than girls the school has, we need to compare the number of boys to the number of girls. Here's the logical reasoning broken down into a syllogism:

**Premise 1:** There are 34 girls in the school.
- This establishes the total count of girls available.

**Premise 2:** There are 841 boys in the school.
- This provides us with the total count of boys available.

**Conclusion:** To find out how many more boys there are compared to girls, subtract the number of girls from the number of boys:
\[ \text{Difference} = \text{Number of Boys} - \text{Number of Girls} \]
\[ \text{Difference} = 841 - 34 \]

Let's perform this subtraction:

\[ 841 - 34 = 807 \]

Therefore, the school has 807 more boys than girls.

The answer is: 807
