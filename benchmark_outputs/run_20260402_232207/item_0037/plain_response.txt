To find the length of Jesse's room, we can use the formula for the area of a rectangle:

\[ \text{Area} = \text{Length} \times \text{Width} \]

Given:
- The area (A) of the carpet needed is 14 square feet.
- The width (W) of the room is 7 feet.

We need to find the length (L). Rearranging the formula to solve for length gives us:

\[ L = \frac{\text{Area}}{\text{Width}} \]

Plugging in the given values:

\[ L = \frac{14 \text{ square feet}}{7 \text{ feet}} \]

\[ L = 2 \text{ feet} \]

So, the length of Jesse's room is 2 feet.
