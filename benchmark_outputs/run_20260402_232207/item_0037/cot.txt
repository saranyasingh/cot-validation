Premise 1: Jesse's room is 7 feet wide.
Premise 2: She needs a carpet of size 14 square feet.
Premise 3: The area of a rectangle (which we assume is the shape of the room) is calculated as width times length (A = w * l).

To find the length of Jesse's room:

Step 1: Apply the formula for the area of a rectangle to find the length.
Step 2: Rearrange the formula to solve for the length (l).
Step 3: Plug in the known values into the rearranged formula and calculate.

The calculation:
Area (A) = 14 square feet
Width (w) = 7 feet

Length (l) = A / w
l = 14 / 7
l = 2 feet

Therefore, the answer is: The length of Jesse's room is 2 feet.
