To determine how many packs of DVDs you can buy with $104 when each pack costs $26, follow these steps:

1. **Identify the total amount of money available:** 
   - You have $104.

2. **Identify the cost per DVD pack:**
   - Each pack costs $26.

3. **Calculate the number of packs that can be bought:**
   - Divide the total amount of money by the cost per pack:
     \[
     \frac{104}{26} = 4
     \]

Therefore, you can buy 4 packs of DVDs with $104. The answer is: 4.
