Premise 1: Every day Ryan spends 7 hours on learning English.
Premise 2: Every day Ryan spends 4 hours on learning Spanish.
Conclusion: Ryan spends 3 more hours on learning English than he does on learning Spanish.

Here's my logical reasoning:
- From Premise 1, we know that Ryan spends 7 hours per day on learning English.
- From Premise 2, we know that Ryan spends 4 hours per day on learning Spanish.
- To find out how much more time Ryan spends on learning English compared to Spanish, we need to subtract the number of hours spent on Spanish from the number of hours spent on English: 7 - 4 = 3.
Therefore, the conclusion is that Ryan spends 3 more hours on learning English than he does on learning Spanish.

The answer is: The answer is: 3.
