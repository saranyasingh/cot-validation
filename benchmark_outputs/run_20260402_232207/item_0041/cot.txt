To determine how many green and yellow peaches are in the basket, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Identify the total number of peaches**: 
   - We know there are 5 red peaches.
   - There are 14 yellow peaches.
   - There are 6 green peaches.

2. **Calculate the total number of peaches**:
   \[
   \text{Total number of peaches} = \text{Red peaches} + \text{Yellow peaches} + \text{Green peaches}
   \]
   Substituting the given numbers:
   \[
   \text{Total number of peaches} = 5 + 14 + 6 = 25
   \]

3. **Determine the sum of green and yellow peaches**:
   \[
   \text{Sum of green and yellow peaches} = \text{Number of green peaches} + \text{Number of yellow peaches}
   \]
   Substituting the known values:
   \[
   \text{Sum of green and yellow peaches} = 6 + 14 = 20
   \]

Therefore, the number of green and yellow peaches combined is 20.

The answer is: 20
