Premise 1: The grasshopper jumped 25 inches.
Premise 2: The frog jumped 18 inches farther than the grasshopper.
Premise 3: The mouse jumped 2 inches farther than the frog.

Let's break down the problem:

Step 1: Determine how far the frog jumped.
- From Premise 1 and Premise 2, we know that the frog jumped 18 inches farther than the grasshopper.
- Therefore, the frog jumped \( 25 \text{ inches} + 18 \text{ inches} = 43 \text{ inches} \).

Step 2: Determine how far the mouse jumped.
- From Premise 3, we know that the mouse jumped 2 inches farther than the frog.
- Therefore, the mouse jumped \( 43 \text{ inches} + 2 \text{ inches} = 45 \text{ inches} \).

Conclusion:
The mouse jumped 45 inches. Thus, the answer is: 45.
