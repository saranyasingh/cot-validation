1. **Premise 1:** Jerry originally had 4 action figures.
   - This statement establishes the initial count of action figures Jerry had before adding any more.

2. **Premise 2:** Jerry later added 6 more action figures to the shelf.
   - This statement indicates that an additional quantity (6) was added to the original amount (4).

3. **Conclusion derived from Premises 1 and 2:** 
   - To find the total number of action figures now, we add the original amount to the new addition:
     \[
     4 + 6 = 10
     \]
   - Therefore, there are 10 action figures on the shelf now.

The answer is: 10.
