To determine how many days it took to harvest the oranges, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Identify the total number of sacks of oranges harvested**: 
   - Total sacks of oranges = 54

2. **Determine the rate at which the oranges were harvested**:
   - Rate of harvesting = 18 sacks per day

3. **Calculate the time required to harvest all the sacks using the formula**:
   \[
   \text{Time} = \frac{\text{Total quantity}}{\text{Rate}}
   \]

4. **Substitute the known values into the formula**:
   \[
   \text{Time} = \frac{54 \text{ sacks}}{18 \text{ sacks/day}}
   \]

5. **Perform the division**:
   \[
   \text{Time} = 3 \text{ days}
   \]

Therefore, it took 3 days to harvest all the oranges.

The answer is: It took 3 days to harvest the oranges.
