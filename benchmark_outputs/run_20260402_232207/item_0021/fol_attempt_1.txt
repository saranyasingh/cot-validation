## FACTS
FACT-1: Total sacks of oranges = 54 :: TotalOranges(54)
FACT-2: Rate of harvesting = 18 sacks per day :: HarvestRate(18)

## RULES
RULE-1: Time = Quantity / Rate :: Time(TotalQuantity, HarvestRate)

## INFERENCES
INF-1: Time = 54 / 18 :: Time(54, 18)

INF-2: From INF-1, Rule-1 by Division: It took 3 days to harvest the oranges :: Time(54, 18) :: Days(3)
