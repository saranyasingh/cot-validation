To determine how many more emails Jack received in the morning than in the afternoon, we need to follow these steps:

1. Identify the number of emails received in the morning.
   - Jack received 6 emails in the morning.

2. Identify the number of emails received in the afternoon.
   - Jack received 2 emails in the afternoon.

3. Calculate the difference between the number of emails received in the morning and the number of emails received in the afternoon.
   - Difference = Number of emails in the morning - Number of emails in the afternoon
   - Difference = 6 - 2

4. Perform the subtraction.
   - 6 - 2 = 4

Therefore, Jack received 4 more emails in the morning than in the afternoon.

The answer is: 4
