To determine how many books are in each bookshelf when Bryan distributes 34 books equally between 2 shelves:

1. **Premise 1:** Bryan has a total of 34 books.
   - This establishes the quantity we need to distribute among the bookshelves.

2. **Premise 2:** The books are distributed equally among 2 bookshelves.
   - This indicates that we must divide the total number of books (34) by the number of shelves (2).

3. **Conclusion:** To find out how many books are on each shelf, perform the division:
   \[
   \frac{34}{2} = 17
   \]
   
Therefore, each bookshelf contains 17 books.

The answer is: Each bookshelf contains 17 books.
