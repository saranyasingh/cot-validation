To determine how many books are in each bookshelf when Bryan distributes his 34 books equally among 2 shelves, we need to perform a simple division operation.

Here's the calculation:

\[ \text{Number of books per shelf} = \frac{\text{Total number of books}}{\text{Number of shelves}} \]

Plugging in the values given:

\[ \text{Number of books per shelf} = \frac{34}{2} \]

Now let's do the division:

\[ \frac{34}{2} = 17 \]

So, each bookshelf will have 17 books.

Therefore, the answer is: 17
