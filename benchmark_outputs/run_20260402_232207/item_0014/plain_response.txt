To determine how many more books than action figures Jerry has on his shelf, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the total number of books and action figures Jerry has after adding the new action figures.
2. Subtract the total number of action figures from the total number of books.

First, let's find out how many action figures Jerry has now:
- Initially, Jerry had 3 action figures.
- He added 2 more action figures later.
- So, the total number of action figures now is \(3 + 2 = 5\).

Next, we know that Jerry still has 7 books on his shelf.

Now, we subtract the total number of action figures from the total number of books:
\[ \text{Number of books} - \text{Number of action figures} = 7 - 5 = 2 \]

Therefore, there are 2 more books than action figures on Jerry's shelf.

The answer is: 2
