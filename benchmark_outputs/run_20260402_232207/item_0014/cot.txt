To determine how many more books than action figures Jerry has on his shelf, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Identify the initial number of items on the shelf:**
   - Books: 7
   - Action figures: 3

2. **Add the additional action figures to the shelf:**
   - Additional action figures: 2
   - New total of action figures: \(3 + 2 = 5\)

3. **Calculate the difference between the number of books and the new total of action figures:**
   - Number of books: 7
   - Number of action figures (new total): 5
   - Difference: \(7 - 5 = 2\)

Thus, there are 2 more books than action figures on Jerry's shelf.

The answer is: 2
