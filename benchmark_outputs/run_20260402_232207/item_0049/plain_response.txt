To determine how many hours Ryan spends on learning English and Chinese over 5 days, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the total number of hours Ryan spends learning English each day.
2. Calculate the total number of hours Ryan spends learning Chinese each day.
3. Add the daily totals from both subjects together.
4. Multiply the combined daily total by the number of days (which is 5).

Let's go through this step-by-step:

1. **English Learning**: 
   - Hours spent per day = 6 hours

2. **Chinese Learning**:
   - Hours spent per day = 7 hours

3. **Daily Total**:
   - Total hours spent per day = 6 hours (English) + 7 hours (Chinese) = 13 hours

4. **Total Over 5 Days**:
   - Total hours over 5 days = 13 hours/day × 5 days = 65 hours

Therefore, the answer is: The total time Ryan spends learning English and Chinese over 5 days is 65 hours.
