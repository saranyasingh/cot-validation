To determine how many emails Jack sent in the day, we need to add up the number of emails he received and sent during both the morning and the afternoon.

In the morning:
- Received: 4 emails
- Sent: 2 emails

Total emails received or sent in the morning = 4 + 2 = 6 emails

In the afternoon:
- Received: 6 emails
- Sent: 8 emails

Total emails received or sent in the afternoon = 6 + 8 = 14 emails

Now, we sum the total emails from both times:

Total emails sent in the day = Total emails received or sent in the morning + Total emails received or sent in the afternoon
Total emails sent in the day = 6 + 14 = 20 emails

Therefore, the answer is: 20
