1. **Identify the relevant information**: We need to determine how many emails Jack sent in total over the course of the day.
   
2. **Break down the problem into parts**:
   - In the morning, Jack received 4 emails and sent 2 letters.
   - In the afternoon, Jack received 6 emails and sent 8 letters.
   
3. **Calculate the number of emails sent in the morning**:
   - Jack sent 2 letters in the morning but these do not directly contribute to the count of emails he sent that day.
   - Therefore, we only consider the emails received and sent during the morning: \(4\) emails (received) + \(2\) letters (sent).
   
4. **Calculate the number of emails sent in the afternoon**:
   - Jack sent 8 letters in the afternoon but again, these do not directly contribute to the count of emails he sent that day.
   - Therefore, we only consider the emails received and sent during the afternoon: \(6\) emails (received) + \(8\) letters (sent).

5. **Sum up all emails sent throughout the day**:
   - Emails sent in the morning: \(4\)
   - Letters sent in the afternoon: \(8\)

Thus, the total number of emails Jack sent in the day is:

\[ \text{Total emails} = 4 (\text{morning}) + 8 (\text{afternoon}) = 12 \]

The answer is: **12**
