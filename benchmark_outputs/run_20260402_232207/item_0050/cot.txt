Let's break down the problem into steps using a logical syllogism:

**Premise 1:** Jack received 10 emails in the morning.
- This establishes the total number of emails received during the morning.

**Premise 2:** Jack received 5 emails in the afternoon.
- This gives us the number of emails received specifically during the afternoon.

**Premise 3:** Jack received 4 emails in the evening.
- This provides the count of emails received specifically during the evening.

To find out how many more emails Jack received in the afternoon compared to the evening:
- We need to subtract the number of emails received in the evening from the number of emails received in the afternoon.

**Step-by-step Logical Reasoning:**

1. **Identify the numbers involved:**
   - Emails in the afternoon = 5
   - Emails in the evening = 4

2. **Perform the subtraction:**
   - Difference = Emails in the afternoon - Emails in the evening
   - Difference = 5 - 4
   - Difference = 1

3. **Interpret the result:**
   - The difference indicates that Jack received 1 more email in the afternoon than in the evening.

Therefore, the answer is: The answer is: 1.
