To determine how many more emails Jack received in the afternoon than in the evening, we need to subtract the number of emails he received in the evening from the number of emails he received in the afternoon.

Number of emails received in the afternoon = 5
Number of emails received in the evening = 4

Difference = Number of emails in the afternoon - Number of emails in the evening
           = 5 - 4
           = 1

Therefore, Jack received 1 more email in the afternoon than in the evening.

The answer is: 1
