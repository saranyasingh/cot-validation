To determine how many more crayons Paul gave to his friends compared to those he lost, we need to follow these steps:

1. Identify the number of crayons Paul gave away.
2. Identify the number of crayons Paul lost.
3. Subtract the number of crayons lost from the number of crayons given away to find out how many more crayons were given away than lost.

Step 1: Number of crayons given away = 308

Step 2: Number of crayons lost = 231

Step 3: Difference in the number of crayons given away and lost = 308 - 231 = 77

Therefore, Paul gave away 77 more crayons than he lost.

The answer is: 77
