To determine how many more crayons Paul gave to his friends than those he lost, we need to follow these steps:

**Premise 1:** Paul initially had a box of 267 crayons.
   - This establishes our starting point and gives us a total number of crayons available before any loss or giving away occurs.

**Premise 2:** Paul lost 231 crayons during the school year.
   - This information tells us about the reduction in the number of crayons due to an event (presumably an accident).

**Premise 3:** Paul gave away 308 crayons to his friends.
   - This indicates that some crayons were no longer in his possession after this action.

**Premise 4:** We need to find out how many more crayons he gave to his friends than those he lost.
   - To do this, we must calculate both quantities and then compare them.

Let's perform the calculations:
- Number of crayons given away = 308
- Number of crayons lost = 231

Now, subtract the number of crayons lost from the number of crayons given away:
\[ \text{Difference} = 308 - 231 \]

Performing the subtraction:
\[ 308 - 231 = 77 \]

Therefore, Paul gave away 77 more crayons than he lost.

The answer is: 77
