To solve this problem logically, we need to break it down into steps:

- This is our starting point and assumption.

**Premise 2:** Paul gave away 90 crayons during the school year.
- We know how many crayons were given away, so let's subtract that number from the initial amount.

**Calculation Step:** 
\[ \text{Crayons left after giving away} = \text{Initial crayons} - \text{Crayons given away} \]
\[ \text{Crayons left after giving away} = 110 - 90 = 20 \]

**Premise 3:** Paul lost 412 crayons during the school year.
- We have been told about the total loss of crayons.

**Calculation Step:** 
\[ \text{Total crayons lost} = 412 \]

**Premise 4:** To find out how many more crayons he lost than those he gave to his friends, we compare the two values directly.
- This means we need to subtract the number of crayons given away from the total number of crayons lost.

**Final Calculation:**
\[ \text{Difference} = \text{Total crayons lost} - \text{Crayons given away} \]
\[ \text{Difference} = 412 - 90 = 322 \]
