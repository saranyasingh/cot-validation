Let's break down this problem using a logical syllogism:

Premise 1: There are many different books in the 'crazy silly school' series.
Premise 2: You have read 13 of these books.
Premise 3: You are yet to read 8 more books.

Conclusion: Therefore, there must be 21 books in the 'crazy silly school' series.

Here’s how we arrive at our conclusion through the steps of a logical syllogism:

Step 1:
- From Premise 1, we know that there are "many" books in the series.
  
Step 2:
- We also know that you have already read 13 books out of the total number of books in the series (from Premise 2).
  
Step 3:
- Lastly, it's stated that you still need to read 8 more books before completing the series (from Premise 3).

Now, let's add up what we know so far:
- Books read = 13
- Books left to read = 8

To find the total number of books in the series, we simply add these two numbers together:
\[ \text{Total books} = \text{Books read} + \text{Books left to read} \]
\[ \text{Total books} = 13 + 8 \]
\[ \text{Total books} = 21 \]

Therefore, the answer is: The series has 21 books.
