## FACTS
FACT-1: There are many books in the 'crazy silly school' series :: ManyBooksInSeries

## RULES
RULE-1: You have read 13 of these books :: Read(books, 13)

RULE-2: You are yet to read 8 more books :: YetToRead(books, 8)

## INFERENCES
INF-1: From FACT-1, RULE-1 by Modus Ponens: There are 13 books in the series :: TotalBooks = Read(books, 13)

INF-2: From FACT-1, RULE-2 by Modus Ponens: There are 8 books remaining to be read :: TotalBooks = YetToRead(books, 8)

INF-3: Summing the books read and remaining to be read gives us the total number of books :: TotalBooks = Read(books, 13) + YetToRead(books, 8)

INF-4: Combining all parts yields the final count of books in the series :: TotalBooks = 21

Therefore, the total number of books in the 'crazy silly school' series is 21.
