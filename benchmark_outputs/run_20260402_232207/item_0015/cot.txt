Premise 1: Melissa scored a total of 91 points.
Premise 2: She played in 13 games.
Premise 3: She scored the same number of points in each game.

To logically conclude how many points Melissa scored in each game:

Step 1: Let's denote the number of points she scored in each game as P.
Step 2: Since she played in 13 games and scored the same amount in each game, we can express her total score as:
\[ \text{Total Points} = \text{Number of Games} \times \text{Points per Game} \]
\[ 91 = 13P \]

Step 3: To find out what \( P \) is, we need to solve this equation for \( P \).
\[ P = \frac{\text{Total Points}}{\text{Number of Games}} \]
\[ P = \frac{91}{13} \]

Step 4: Perform the division:
\[ P = 7 \]

Therefore, Melissa scored 7 points in each game.

The answer is: 7.
