**Logical Syllogism**

1. **Premise 1**: Danny initially had 71 bottle caps and 24 wrappers.
   - This establishes the initial quantities of bottle caps and wrappers that Danny started with.

2. **Premise 2**: After finding additional items at the park, Danny has 28 bottle caps and 16 wrappers in his collection.
   - This statement provides information about how many bottle caps and wrappers Danny has after collecting new ones.

3. **Conclusion**: We need to determine how many more bottle caps than wrappers Danny currently has.
   - To do this, we subtract the number of wrappers from the number of bottle caps.

Let's perform the calculation:

- Number of bottle caps = 71
- Number of wrappers = 24

\[ \text{Difference} = \text{Number of bottle caps} - \text{Number of wrappers} \]

\[ \text{Difference} = 71 - 24 \]

\[ \text{Difference} = 47 \]

Therefore, Danny has 47 more bottle caps than wrappers now.

The answer is: 47
