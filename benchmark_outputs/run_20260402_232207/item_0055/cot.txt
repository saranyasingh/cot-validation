Let's break down the problem into a series of logical steps:

**Premise 1:** Danny collected bottle caps and wrappers.
- This establishes that we're dealing with two types of items: bottle caps and wrappers.

**Premise 2:** Danny found 11 bottle caps and 28 wrappers at the park.
- This provides us with initial counts of both types of items Danny had before collecting any further.

**Premise 3:** Now Danny has 68 bottle caps and 51 wrappers in his collection.
- This tells us what he currently owns after some additional collection activity.

**Step-by-step Logical Reasoning:**

1. **Initial Count (Premise 2):**
   - Bottle Caps Found: 11
   - Wrappers Found: 28

2. **Current Count (Premise 3):**
   - Bottle Caps: 68
   - Wrappers: 51

3. **Determine Total Collection Added:**
   - Total Bottle Caps Collected: \( 68 - 11 \)
   - Total Wrappers Collected: \( 51 - 28 \)

4. **Calculate Additional Bottle Caps:**
   - Additional Bottle Caps = \( 68 - 11 = 57 \)

5. **Calculate Additional Wrappers:**
   - Additional Wrappers = \( 51 - 28 = 23 \)

6. **Compare Additional Wrappers to Additional Bottle Caps:**
   - Difference = Additional Wrappers - Additional Bottle Caps
   - Difference = 23 - 57

Since this results in a negative number:
\[ 23 - 57 < 0 \]

Therefore, Danny actually lost more bottle caps than wrappers during his collection trip.
