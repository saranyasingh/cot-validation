Let's break down this problem using a logical syllogism:

**Premise 1:** Marco's dad's strawberries weighed 11 pounds.
- This establishes the weight of Marco's dad's strawberries as a specific value (11 pounds).

**Premise 2:** Together their strawberries weighed 30 pounds.
- This statement combines the weights of both Marco's and his dad's strawberries into a single total weight.

**Conclusion:** Therefore, we need to find out how much Marco's strawberries weighed individually.

**Step-by-step logical reasoning:**

1. Let \( M \) represent the weight of Marco's strawberries.
2. According to Premise 2, the combined weight of Marco's and his dad's strawberries is 30 pounds:
   \[
   M + 11 = 30
   \]
3. To isolate \( M \), subtract 11 from both sides of the equation:
   \[
   M + 11 - 11 = 30 - 11
   \]
4. Simplifying the equation gives us:
   \[
   M = 19
   \]

Therefore, the conclusion derived from these premises is that Marco's strawberries weighed **19 pounds**.

The answer is: 19
