Let's denote the number of hours Ryan spends on learning Chinese as \( C \) and the number of hours he spends on learning English as \( E \).

According to the problem:
1. Ryan spends 5 hours on learning Chinese.
2. He spends 2 hours more on learning English than on learning Chinese.

We can set up the equation based on this information:
\[ E = C + 2 \]

Since we know \( C = 5 \):
\[ E = 5 + 2 \]
\[ E = 7 \]

So, Ryan spends 7 hours on learning English.

Therefore, the answer is: The answer is: 7.
