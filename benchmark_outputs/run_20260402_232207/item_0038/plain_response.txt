To find out how much Jessie weighs now after losing 20 kilograms, we need to subtract that amount from her original weight:

Original weight of Jessie = 71 kg
Weight loss after jogging = 20 kg

Now, let's calculate:
\[ \text{Current weight} = \text{Original weight} - \text{Weight loss} \]
\[ \text{Current weight} = 71 \, \text{kg} - 20 \, \text{kg} \]
\[ \text{Current weight} = 51 \, \text{kg} \]

So, the current weight of Jessie is 51 kilograms.

The answer is: 51
