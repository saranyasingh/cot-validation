To determine how much Edward had before spending his money, we need to consider both steps that occurred after he initially spent some amount and ended up with $7. Let's break this down into a logical syllogism:

- This is our given information.

**Premise 2:** After receiving $10 from his friend, Edward had $7 left.
- We know that before receiving the $10, he must have had more than $7 because adding $10 to $7 gives us $17 (the amount he initially spent).

**Conclusion:** Therefore, Edward had $17 before he spent any money.
