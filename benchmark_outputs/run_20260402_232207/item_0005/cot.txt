To determine how much money Olivia has left after spending 15 dollars from her original amount of 78 dollars, we need to perform subtraction. Here’s the logical syllogism:

1. **Premise**: Olivia started with $78.
   - This establishes the initial total amount of money Olivia had before any expenditures.

2. **Premise**: Olivia spent $15 at a supermarket.
   - This introduces an expenditure that reduces the total amount of money Olivia had.

3. **Logical Reasoning**:
   - To find out how much money Olivia has left, we subtract the amount spent ($15) from the initial total ($78).
   - Therefore, the remaining amount = Initial Total - Amount Spent

4. **Calculation**:
   - Remaining Amount = $78 - $15
   - Performing the subtraction gives us: 
     \[
     78 - 15 = 63
     \]

Therefore, the answer is: Olivia has $63 left.
