To determine how much money Olivia has left after spending some at the supermarket, we can follow these steps:

1. Start with the initial amount of money in Olivia's wallet.
   - Olivia initially had $78.

2. Subtract the amount of money she spent at the supermarket from her initial amount.
   - Olivia spent $15 at the supermarket.

3. Perform the subtraction:
   \[
   78 - 15 = 63
   \]

Therefore, after spending $15 at the supermarket, Olivia has $63 left. 

The answer is: 63
