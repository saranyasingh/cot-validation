Let's break down the problem step by step:

1. We know that after buying toys, Dave has 14 tickets left.
2. We also know that he spent 12 tickets on toys.
3. To find out how many tickets Dave originally won, we need to add the number of tickets he spent (12) and the number of tickets he has left (14).

So, let's do the calculation:
\[ \text{Original Tickets} = \text{Tickets Spent} + \text{Tickets Left} \]
\[ \text{Original Tickets} = 12 + 14 \]
\[ \text{Original Tickets} = 26 \]

Therefore, the total number of tickets Dave won at the arcade was 26.
