\text{AvailableWax} ::= AvailableWax(t_0) \land WaxAmount(557)

\text{RequiredWax} ::= RequiredWax(t_0) \land WaxAmount(17)

These are universally quantified implications based on external or linguistic knowledge that apply to all times.

**Rule-1:** The amount of wax used equals the difference between the total required wax and the available wax.
\[
\forall t (AvailableWax(t) \rightarrow \text{UsedWax}(t))

\text{RemainingWax} ::= RemainingWax(t_0) \land \text{AvailableWax}(t_0) \land \text{UsedWax}(t_0)
