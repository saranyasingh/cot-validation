**Logical Syllogism**

1. **Premise 1:** Each basket contains 19 red peaches and 4 green peaches.
   - This establishes the number of fruits per basket.

2. **Premise 2:** There are 15 baskets.
   - This introduces the total count of baskets to be considered.

3. **Conclusion:** Therefore, the total number of peaches in all the baskets is calculated as:
   \[
   \text{Total Peaches} = (\text{Number of Red Peaches per Basket}) + (\text{Number of Green Peaches per Basket})
   \]
   \[
   \text{Total Peaches} = 19 + 4 = 23
   \]

4. **Final Answer:** 
   - The total number of peaches in the 15 baskets is \(23\).

The answer is: 23.
