To determine how many more emails Jack received in the morning than in the afternoon, we need to follow these steps:

### Step 1: Calculate the total number of emails Jack received in the morning.
- Jack received 10 emails in the morning.

### Step 2: Calculate the total number of emails Jack received in the afternoon.
- Jack received 3 emails in the afternoon.

### Step 3: Determine the difference between the number of emails received in the morning and the afternoon.
- Number of emails received in the morning = 10
- Number of emails received in the afternoon = 3

The difference is calculated as:
\[ \text{Difference} = \text{Morning Emails} - \text{Afternoon Emails} \]
\[ \text{Difference} = 10 - 3 \]
\[ \text{Difference} = 7 \]

Therefore, Jack received **7 more emails** in the morning than in the afternoon.

The answer is: 7
