To determine how many more emails Jack received in the morning compared to the afternoon, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the total number of emails Jack received in the morning.
2. Calculate the total number of emails Jack received in the afternoon.
3. Subtract the total number of emails received in the afternoon from the total number of emails received in the morning.

Let's start with step 1:
Jack received 10 emails in the morning.

Now for step 2:
Jack received 3 emails in the afternoon.

For step 3:
We subtract the number of emails received in the afternoon from the number of emails received in the morning:
\[ 10 - 3 = 7 \]

So, Jack received 7 more emails in the morning than in the afternoon.

Therefore, the answer is: 7
