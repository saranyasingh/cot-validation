To determine how many seats the Ferris wheel has, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Identify the total number of people that can ride the Ferris wheel simultaneously**: According to the problem, 18 people can ride the Ferris wheel at the same time.
2. **Determine the capacity of each seat on the Ferris wheel**: Each seat can hold 9 people.
3. **Calculate the number of seats needed to accommodate all the people**:
   - We divide the total number of people (18) by the capacity per seat (9).

So, let's perform this calculation:

\[ \text{Number of seats} = \frac{\text{Total number of people}}{\text{Capacity per seat}} \]
\[ \text{Number of seats} = \frac{18}{9} \]

4. **Perform the division**:
   - \( 18 \div 9 = 2 \)

Therefore, the Ferris wheel must have 2 seats to accommodate 18 people at once.

The answer is: 2
