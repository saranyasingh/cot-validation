Let's break down this problem into a series of logical steps:

**Premise 1:** Dave initially had 15 apps on his phone.
- This sets our starting point and establishes how many apps were present before any changes occurred.

**Premise 2:** Dave then added 71 new apps to his existing collection.
- This introduces an increase in the number of apps through addition.

**Premise 3:** After adding these new apps, Dave had a total of 86 apps (15 initial + 71 added).
- We combine the initial count with the newly added ones to get the current total.

**Premise 4:** After deleting some apps, Dave was left with only 14 apps on his phone.
- This represents the state after some deletions have been made.

**Premise 5:** To find out how many apps were deleted, we subtract the remaining number of apps (14) from the total number of apps at that moment (86).
- This gives us the number of apps removed.

To calculate the exact difference between the number of apps deleted and added:
- Subtracting the final number of apps from the total after additions gives us: 
  \[ 86 - 14 = 72 \]
  
This means Dave deleted 72 apps overall.

Now, let's determine how many more apps he deleted compared to those he originally added:
- Adding up the numbers of apps added and deleted:
  \[ 71 \text{ (added)} + 72 \text{ (deleted)} = 143 \]

Therefore, Dave deleted **143 more apps** than he added.

The answer is: 143
