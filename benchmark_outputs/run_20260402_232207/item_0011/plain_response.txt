To determine how many more apps Dave deleted than he added, we can follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total number of apps after adding and before any deletion:**
   - Initially, Dave had 15 apps.
   - He then added 71 new apps.
   - Total apps after addition = 15 + 71 = 86 apps.

2. **Determine the number of apps remaining after deleting some:**
   - After deleting some apps, Dave had 14 apps left.

3. **Find out how many apps were deleted:**
   - Number of apps deleted = Total apps after addition - Remaining apps = 86 - 14 = 72 apps.

4. **Calculate the difference between the number of apps deleted and the number of apps added:**
   - Difference = Number of apps deleted - Number of apps added = 72 - 71 = 1 app.

Therefore, Dave deleted 1 more app than he added. The answer is: 1
