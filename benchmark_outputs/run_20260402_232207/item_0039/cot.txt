Let's break down this problem into a logical syllogism:

**Premise 1:** Frank puts 11 pieces of candy in each bag.
- This tells us the number of candies per bag.

**Premise 2:** He has 22 pieces of candy.
- This gives us the total number of candies available.

**Conclusion:** Therefore, he would have 2 bags.
- We deduce this because if we divide the total number of candies (22) by the number of candies per bag (11), we get 2 as an integer result. This means it's possible to evenly distribute all the candies into bags without any leftovers or needing additional bags.

The answer is: 2 bags.
