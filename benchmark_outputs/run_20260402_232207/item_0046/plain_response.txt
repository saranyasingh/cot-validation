To determine how many more bird families flew away to Africa than those that flew away to Asia, we need to subtract the number of bird families flying to Asia from the number of bird families flying to Africa.

Number of bird families flying to Africa = 42
Number of bird families flying to Asia = 31

Difference in numbers = Number of bird families flying to Africa - Number of bird families flying to Asia
