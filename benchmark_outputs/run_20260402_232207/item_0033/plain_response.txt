To determine how many houses are in a block given that the mailman gives 2 junk mails per house and 14 pieces of junk mail total, we can set up an equation based on these values:

Let \( h \) represent the number of houses in the block.

According to the problem:
\[ 2h = 14 \]

To find \( h \), we divide both sides of the equation by 2:
\[ h = \frac{14}{2} \]
\[ h = 7 \]

Thus, the number of houses in the block is 7. 

Therefore, the answer is: The answer is: 7.
