Let's break down the problem using a logical syllogism:

1. **Premise 1:** The chef started with 40 apples.
   - This establishes the initial quantity of apples available to be used or discarded.

2. **Premise 2:** The chef ended up with 39 apples after making some pies.
   - This indicates that some apples were not used but rather were consumed (or discarded) during the pie-making process.

3. **Conclusion:** To find out how many apples the chef used, we need to subtract the number of apples left from the number of apples initially available.
   
Therefore, the number of apples used = Initial quantity of apples - Apples left

Applying this formula:
- Initial quantity of apples = 40
- Apples left = 39

Using the formula:
\[ \text{Apples used} = 40 - 39 \]
\[ \text{Apples used} = 1 \]

**The answer is:** 1.
