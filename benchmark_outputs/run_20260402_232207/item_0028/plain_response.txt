To solve this problem, we can break it down into two parts:

1. Calculate how many nails are needed in total.
2. Determine how many planks are required based on the number of nails.

Step 1: Calculate the total number of nails needed.
- Each plank requires 74 nails.
- There are 8 planks used for the walls.

Total nails = Number of planks × Nails per plank
Total nails = 8 × 74

Let's do the multiplication:
Total nails = 592

Step 2: Determine the number of planks needed.
- We know there are 592 nails in total and each plank uses 74 nails.

Number of planks = Total nails ÷ Nails per plank
Number of planks = 592 ÷ 74

Now, let's perform the division:
Number of planks = 8

Therefore, John needs 8 planks for the house wall.

The answer is: 8
