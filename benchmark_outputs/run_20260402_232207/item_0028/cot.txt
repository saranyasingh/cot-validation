To determine how many planks John needs for the house wall, we can break down the problem into two parts and use logical reasoning to connect them:

1. **Step 1:** Determine the total number of nails needed.
2. **Step 2:** Calculate the number of planks required based on the given information about the planks used and their nail requirement.

**Logical Syllogism:**

- Premise 1 (Given): "John uses 8 large planks of wood."
- Premise 2 (Given): "Each plank of wood needs 74 pieces of nails."

Now let's analyze these premises further:

- From Premise 1, we know that there are 8 planks of wood being used.
- From Premise 2, we know that each plank requires 74 nails.

To find out how many nails are needed in total:
\[ \text{Total Nails} = \text{Number of Planks} \times \text{Nails per Plank} \]
\[ \text{Total Nails} = 8 \times 74 \]

Performing this multiplication:
\[ 8 \times 74 = 592 \]

So, John needs 592 nails in total.

Next, we need to determine how many planks are required if each plank has 74 nails. We do this using another logical syllogism:

- Given: "If each plank of wood needs 74 pieces of nails."
- Conclusion: "Therefore, to secure 592 nails, John needs \( \frac{\text{Total Nails}}{\text{Nails per Plank}} \) planks."

Let's calculate the number of planks required:
\[ \text{Planks Needed} = \frac{\text{Total Nails}}{\text{Nails per Plank}} \]
\[ \text{Planks Needed} = \frac{592}{74} \]

Performing this division:
\[ \frac{592}{74} = 8 \]

Thus, John needs 8 planks of wood to ensure all 592 nails are secured.

The answer is: 8
