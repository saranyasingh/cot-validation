To determine how many weeks the harvest lasted, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Identify the given information**: Lewis earns $2 every week.
2. **Determine the total earnings**: Lewis earned a total of $178.
3. **Calculate the number of weeks**: To find out how many weeks the harvest lasted, we divide the total amount earned by the weekly earning rate.

The formula to calculate the number of weeks (W) is:
\[ W = \frac{\text{Total Earnings}}{\text{Weekly Earning Rate}} \]

Substituting the given values into the formula:
\[ W = \frac{178}{2} \]

Performing the division:
\[ W = 89 \]

Therefore, the harvest lasted for 89 weeks.

**Conclusion**: The answer is: 89 weeks.
