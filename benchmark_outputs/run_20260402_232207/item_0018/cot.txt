To determine how many more baseball cards than Ace cards Nell has after giving some away, we need to follow these steps:

### Step 1: Calculate the total number of cards Nell originally had.
- Baseball cards: 309
- Ace cards: 356

Total original cards = Baseball cards + Ace cards
\[ \text{Total original cards} = 309 + 356 = 665 \]

### Step 2: Determine the total number of cards Nell has left after giving some to Jeff.
- Baseball cards left: 415
- Ace cards left: Let's call this \( A \)

Total remaining cards = Baseball cards left + Ace cards left
\[ \text{Total remaining cards} = 415 + A \]

Since all the cards she gave away must be either baseball or ace cards (since that’s what she received), let's denote the number of cards given as \( G \).

So:
\[ \text{Total original cards} - G = \text{Total remaining cards} \]
\[ 665 - G = 415 + A \]

### Step 3: Solve for \( A \) (the number of Ace cards).
Rearrange the equation to solve for \( A \):
\[ 665 - 415 = G + A \]
\[ 250 = G + A \]

We know that the sum of baseball and ace cards initially was 665, so if we subtract the remaining baseball cards from the total initial count:
\[ A = 356 - (665 - 415) \]
\[ A = 356 - 250 \]
\[ A = 106 \]

Thus, Nell has 106 Ace cards left.

### Step 4: Calculate how many more baseball cards than Ace cards Nell has.
\[ \text{More baseball cards than Ace cards} = 415 - 106 \]
\[ \text{More baseball cards than Ace cards} = 309 \]

Therefore, the answer is: 309.
