To determine how many rows Faye can make with her pencils, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total number of pencils Faye has:**
   - Faye has 35 packs of pencils.
   - Each pack contains 4 pencils.
   - Therefore, the total number of pencils is \( 35 \times 4 = 140 \) pencils.

2. **Determine how many rows can be made with 2 pencils per row:**
   - We have 140 pencils and want to place them in rows with 2 pencils in each row.
   - To find out how many rows this would create, we divide the total number of pencils by the number of pencils per row:
     \[
     \frac{140}{2} = 70
     \]

Thus, Faye can make 70 rows with her pencils.

The answer is: 70
