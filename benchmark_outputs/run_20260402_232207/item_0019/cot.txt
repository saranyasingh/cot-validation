**Logical Syllogism**

1. **Premise 1:** There are 270 students in the school.
   - This establishes the total student count.

2. **Premise 2:** Each grade has 5 students.
   - This specifies the student distribution per grade.

3. **Conclusion:** Therefore, to find out how many grades there are, we divide the total number of students by the number of students per grade.
   - Calculation: \( \frac{270}{5} = 54 \).

Thus, the answer is:
The answer is: 54

This conclusion is reached through simple division based on the given premises that establish both the total student count and the student distribution per grade.
