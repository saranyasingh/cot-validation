To determine the total number of grades in the school, we need to divide the total number of students by the number of students per grade. Here's the step-by-step process:

1. The total number of students in the school is given as 270.
2. Each grade has 5 students.
3. To find the total number of grades, we divide the total number of students by the number of students per grade:
   \[
   \text{Total number of grades} = \frac{\text{Total number of students}}{\text{Number of students per grade}}
   \]
   Substituting the given values:
   \[
   \text{Total number of grades} = \frac{270}{5}
   \]

4. Performing the division:
   \[
   \frac{270}{5} = 54
   \]

Therefore, the total number of grades in the school is **54**.
