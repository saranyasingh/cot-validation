To determine how much money was made from selling t-shirts during the Texas Tech game, we need to follow these steps:

### Step 1: Determine the number of t-shirts sold during the Texas Tech game.
We know that the total number of t-shirts sold over both games is 186. We also know that during the Arkansas game, 172 t-shirts were sold. Therefore, the number of t-shirts sold during the Texas Tech game can be calculated as follows:
\[ \text{Number of t-shirts sold during Texas Tech game} = \text{Total t-shirts sold} - \text{T-Shirts sold during Arkansas game} \]
\[ \text{Number of t-shirts sold during Texas Tech game} = 186 - 172 \]
\[ \text{Number of t-shirts sold during Texas Tech game} = 14 \]

### Step 2: Calculate the revenue generated from selling t-shirts during the Texas Tech game.
Given that the profit per t-shirt is $78, we calculate the revenue by multiplying the number of t-shirts sold during the Texas Tech game by the profit per t-shirt:
\[ \text{Revenue from Texas Tech game} = \text{Number of t-shirts sold during Texas Tech game} \times \text{Profit per t-shirt} \]
\[ \text{Revenue from Texas Tech game} = 14 \times 78 \]

### Step 3: Perform the multiplication to find the revenue.
\[ 14 \times 78 = 1092 \]

Therefore, the amount of money made from selling t-shirts during the Texas Tech game is \( \$1092 \).
