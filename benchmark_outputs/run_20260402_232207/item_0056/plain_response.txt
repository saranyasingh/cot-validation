To determine how many letters Jack received in the day, we need to consider both the morning and afternoon sessions separately and then sum them up.

First, let's calculate the number of letters Jack received in the morning:
- Morning: 6 letters

Next, let's calculate the number of letters Jack received in the afternoon:
- Afternoon: 7 letters

Now, add the total number of letters from both sessions:
\[ \text{Total letters} = \text{Morning letters} + \text{Afternoon letters} \]
\[ \text{Total letters} = 6 + 7 \]
\[ \text{Total letters} = 13 \]

Therefore, the total number of letters Jack received in the day is 13.

The answer is: 13
