Premise 1: After a typhoon, 13 trees in Haley's backyard died.
Premise 2: She had grown 3 trees initially.
Conclusion: Therefore, 10 more trees died in the typhoon than those that survived.

Here’s how we arrive at this conclusion through a series of logical steps:

1. **Identify the total number of trees that died**: We know that after the typhoon, 13 trees died. This is our first piece of information and it sets up the basis for comparison.

2. **Consider the initial growth of trees**: We also know that Haley had grown 3 trees initially. This means before the typhoon, there were 3 new trees added to her original collection.

3. **Calculate the number of trees that survived**: Since 13 trees died out of an initial total of 3 + 13 (the additional trees plus the existing ones), we subtract the number of dead trees from the initial total:
   \[
   \text{Number of surviving trees} = (\text{Initial trees}) - (\text{Trees that died})
   \]
   \[
   \text{Number of surviving trees} = (3) - (13)
   \]

4. **Perform the subtraction**:
   \[
   \text{Number of surviving trees} = 3 - 13 = -10
   \]

5. **Interpret the result**: A negative number indicates that there were actually fewer trees surviving than those that died. To clarify, since we cannot have a negative number of trees surviving, this implies that all but 3 of the initial trees must have been lost during the typhoon, meaning only 3 trees remained alive.

6. **Determine the difference between those that died and those that survived**: Now, we need to find out how many more trees died compared to those that survived. Since all but 3 trees remained after the typhoon, we calculate the difference as follows:
   \[
   \text{Difference} = \text{Total trees that died} - \text{Surviving trees}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Difference} = 13 - (-3)
   \]
   Simplifying the expression inside the parentheses gives us:
   \[
   \text{Difference} = 13 + 3 = 16
   \]

7. **Conclude the final statement**: There were indeed 16 more trees that died in the typhoon than those that survived. 

The answer is: 16
