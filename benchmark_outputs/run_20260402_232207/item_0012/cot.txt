**Logical Syllogism**

1. **Premise 1:** Adam has 4 more apples than Jackie.
   - Let's denote the number of apples Jackie has as \( J \).
   - Therefore, Adam has \( J + 4 \) apples.

2. **Premise 2:** Together, Adam and Jackie have 14 apples.
   - This means \( (J + 4) + J = 14 \).

3. **Solve for \( J \):**
   - Combine like terms: \( 2J + 4 = 14 \).
   - Subtract 4 from both sides: \( 2J = 10 \).
   - Divide by 2: \( J = 5 \).

4. **Determine Adam’s Apples:**
   - Since \( J = 5 \), Adam has \( 5 + 4 = 9 \) apples.

5. **Calculate Total Apples for Adam and Jackie:**
   - Together they have \( 5 + 9 = 14 \) apples.

6. **Premise 3:** Bob has 6 apples more than Adam and Jackie together.
   - Let's denote the number of apples Bob has as \( B \).
   - Therefore, \( B = 14 + 6 \).

7. **Calculate Bob’s Apples:**
   - \( B = 14 + 6 = 20 \).

**Conclusion:**
The answer is: Bob has 20 apples.
