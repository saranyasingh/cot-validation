1. **Premise 1:** Paco initially had 8 sweet cookies.
   - Premise 2:** Paco also had 6 salty cookies.
   
2. **Premise 3:** Paco ate 20 sweet cookies.
   - Premise 4:** Paco ate 34 salty cookies.

3. **Logical Reasoning:**
   - From Premise 1 and Premise 3, we know that after eating, Paco has:
     - \( 8 \text{ (initial sweet cookies)} - 20 \text{ (eaten sweet cookies)} = -12 \) sweet cookies left.
     - Since it's not possible to have negative cookies, this suggests there was an error in interpretation or data given.
   - However, let's proceed assuming the problem intends for us to interpret the numbers as actual quantities eaten rather than remaining.
   
4. **Conclusion:**
   - Therefore, Paco ate all his initial sweet cookies first before moving on to the salty ones.
   - So, he ate exactly 8 sweet cookies.
   
5. **Comparison:**
   - Paco ate 34 salty cookies but only consumed 8 sweet cookies.
   - To find how many more salty cookies he ate compared to sweet cookies:
     - Saltier cookies eaten minus sweeter cookies eaten:
       \( 34 - 8 = 26 \)

**The answer is: 26**

This conclusion assumes that the initial count of cookies was meant to be interpreted literally as actual quantities eaten, which leads to the surprising result of consuming significantly more salty cookies than sweet ones.
