To determine how many pages are in each chapter, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total number of pages per day**: 
   - We know that the book has a total of 594 pages and it took Frank 607 days to finish.
   - Therefore, the average number of pages Frank read per day is:
     \[
     \text{Pages per day} = \frac{\text{Total pages}}{\text{Number of days}} = \frac{594}{607}
     \]

2. **Simplify the fraction**:
   - Perform the division:
     \[
     \frac{594}{607} \approx 0.9807
     \]
   - This means Frank reads approximately 0.9807 pages per day on average.

3. **Determine the number of pages per chapter**:
   - Since the book has 3 chapters with an equal number of pages, we divide the total number of pages by the number of chapters:
     \[
     \text{Pages per chapter} = \frac{\text{Total pages}}{\text{Number of chapters}} = \frac{594}{3}
     \]

4. **Perform the division**:
   - Calculate the exact value:
     \[
     \frac{594}{3} = 198
     \]

Therefore, the number of pages in each chapter is \( \boxed{198} \).
