**Logical Syllogism**

1. **Premise 1:** A book has 2 chapters.
   - This establishes that we are dealing with a book divided into two distinct sections.

2. **Premise 2:** The second chapter is 36 pages long.
   - This gives us information about the length of the second part of the book.

3. **Premise 3:** There are a total of 67 pages in the book.
   - This provides the total number of pages across both chapters.

4. **Conclusion:**
   - To find out how many pages are in the first chapter, we subtract the number of pages in the second chapter from the total number of pages in the book.

**Step-by-Step Reasoning:**

- Let's denote the number of pages in the first chapter as \( P_1 \) and the number of pages in the second chapter as \( P_2 \).
  
- From Premise 1, we know:
  \[
  P_1 + P_2 = 67
  \]

- From Premise 2, we know:
  \[
  P_2 = 36
  \]

- Substitute \( P_2 \) into the equation from Premise 1:
  \[
  P_1 + 36 = 67
  \]

- Solve for \( P_1 \):
  \[
  P_1 = 67 - 36
  \]
  \[
  P_1 = 31
  \]

Therefore, the number of pages in the first chapter is:

The answer is: 31 pages.
