**Logical Syllogism**

1. **Premise 1:** Kelly starts with 106 Nintendo games.
   - This establishes the initial quantity of games Kelly possesses before any transactions occur.

2. **Premise 2:** Kelly decides to give away 64 games.
   - This introduces an action (giving away) that changes her possession of games.

3. **Conclusion:** After giving away 64 games, how many games does Kelly have left?

- To determine this, we subtract the number of games given away from the total number of games Kelly originally had:
  \[
  106 - 64 = 42
  \]

Therefore, after giving away 64 games, Kelly will have 42 games left.

The answer is: 42
