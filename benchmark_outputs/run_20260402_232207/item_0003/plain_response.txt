To determine how much each book cost, we can follow these steps:

1. **Identify the total expenditure and remaining balance**: Edward initially had some money (which we'll call \( B \)) and spent $6 on books. After buying the books, he has $12 left.
   
   Therefore:
   \[
   B - 6 = 12
   \]

2. **Solve for the initial amount of money \( B \)**:
   \[
   B = 12 + 6 = 18
   \]
   So, Edward originally had $18.

3. **Calculate the cost per book**: Edward bought 2 books for a total of $6.
   
   The cost of one book is then:
   \[
   \text{Cost per book} = \frac{\$6}{2} = \$3
   \]

Therefore, the cost of each book is $\boxed{3}$.
