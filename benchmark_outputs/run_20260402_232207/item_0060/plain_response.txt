Let's denote the number of children who got on the bus as \( x \).

Initially, there were 21 children on the bus. After 10 children got off, the number of children left on the bus was:
\[ 21 - 10 = 11 \]

After some additional children got on the bus, the total number of children became 16. So, we can set up the equation:
\[ 11 + x = 16 \]

To find \( x \), we subtract 11 from both sides of the equation:
\[ x = 16 - 11 \]
\[ x = 5 \]

Therefore, 5 children got on the bus at the bus stop.

The answer is: 5
