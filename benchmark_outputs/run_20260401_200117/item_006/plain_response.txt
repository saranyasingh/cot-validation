To determine how many cups of sugar are needed when using 9 cups of flour, we can set up a proportion based on the given ratio of flour to sugar in the recipe.

The original recipe ratio is:
Flour : Sugar = 3 : 2

If we let \( x \) represent the number of cups of sugar needed for 9 cups of flour, then we have:
\[ \frac{3}{2} = \frac{9}{x} \]

Now, solve for \( x \):
1. Cross-multiply to get rid of the fractions:
\[ 3x = 2 \times 9 \]
\[ 3x = 18 \]

2. Divide both sides by 3 to isolate \( x \):
\[ x = \frac{18}{3} \]
\[ x = 6 \]

Therefore, if you use 9 cups of flour, you will need 6 cups of sugar.

The answer is: 6
