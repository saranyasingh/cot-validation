To determine the amount of sugar needed when using 9 cups of flour, we can follow these steps:

1. **Understand the given ratio**: The recipe calls for 3 cups of flour for every 2 cups of sugar.
   - Premise: For every 3 parts of flour, there are 2 parts of sugar.

2. **Set up a proportion**: We know that if we have 9 cups of flour, we need to find out what fraction this represents of the total ingredients.
   - Let's denote the number of cups of sugar as \( x \).

3. **Determine the relationship between flour and sugar**:
   - Since the ratio of flour to sugar is 3:2, we can set up a proportion based on this ratio.
   - Therefore, \( \frac{3}{2} = \frac{9}{x} \).

4. **Solve the proportion for \( x \)**:
   - Cross-multiply to solve for \( x \):
     \[
     3x = 2 \times 9
     \]
   - Simplify the right side:
     \[
     3x = 18
     \]
   - Divide both sides by 3 to isolate \( x \):
     \[
     x = \frac{18}{3}
     \]
     \[
     x = 6
     \]

5. **Conclusion**: Using 9 cups of flour means you would need 6 cups of sugar to maintain the correct proportions according to the recipe.

The answer is: 6 cups of sugar.
