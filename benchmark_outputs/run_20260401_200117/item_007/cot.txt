To determine how many miles a car travels in 2.5 hours at a speed of 60 miles per hour, we need to use the relationship between distance, rate (speed), and time. This relationship is given by the formula:

\[ \text{Distance} = \text{Rate} \times \text{Time} \]

Here's the step-by-step logical reasoning based on this formula:

1. **Identify the known quantities**: 
   - Rate (speed) = 60 miles per hour
   - Time = 2.5 hours

2. **Substitute these values into the formula**:
   - Distance = \( 60 \, \text{miles/hour} \times 2.5 \, \text{hours} \)

3. **Perform the multiplication**:
   - First, multiply 60 by 2.5:
     \[
     60 \times 2.5 = 150
     \]

4. **Conclusion**:
   - Therefore, the car travels 150 miles in 2.5 hours.

The answer is: 150 miles.
