To find out how many miles the car travels in 2.5 hours, you can use the formula:

Distance = Speed × Time

Given:
Speed = 60 miles per hour
Time = 2.5 hours

Plugging these values into the formula gives:

Distance = 60 miles/hour × 2.5 hours = 150 miles

Therefore, the car travels 150 miles in 2.5 hours.

The answer is: 150 miles
