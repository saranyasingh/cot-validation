To determine how many bags of oranges the farmer needs, we start by identifying the total number of oranges available. The problem states that the farmer has 72 oranges and each bag can hold 8 oranges. We need to find out how many full bags of 8 oranges can be made from 72 oranges.

We do this by dividing the total number of oranges by the number of oranges per bag:
\[ \text{Number of bags} = \frac{\text{Total number of oranges}}{\text{Number of oranges per bag}} = \frac{72}{8} \]

Performing the division gives us:
\[ \frac{72}{8} = 9 \]

So, the farmer needs 9 bags of oranges. Therefore, the answer is:

The answer is: 9
