Let's break down this problem using a logical syllogism:

1. **Premise 1**: The farmer has 72 oranges.
   - This establishes the total number of oranges available.

2. **Premise 2**: Each bag contains 8 oranges.
   - This specifies the capacity of each bag.

3. **Conclusion**: To find out how many bags are needed to pack all the oranges, we divide the total number of oranges by the capacity of each bag:
   
   \[
   \text{Number of bags} = \frac{\text{Total number of oranges}}{\text{Capacity per bag}}
   \]

4. **Calculation**:
   \[
   \text{Number of bags} = \frac{72}{8}
   \]

5. **Division**:
   \[
   \frac{72}{8} = 9
   \]

Therefore, the farmer needs 9 bags of oranges to pack all his oranges.

The answer is: 9.
