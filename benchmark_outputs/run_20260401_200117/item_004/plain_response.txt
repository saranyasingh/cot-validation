To determine the total number of students, we need to calculate the number of students carried by the buses and the number of students carried by the vans separately, and then add these two totals together.

First, let's calculate the number of students on the buses:
- There are 5 buses.
- Each bus carries 30 students.
So, the total number of students on the buses is \(5 \times 30 = 150\).

Next, let's calculate the number of students in the vans:
- There are 3 vans.
- Each van carries 12 students.
So, the total number of students in the vans is \(3 \times 12 = 36\).

Now, we add the number of students from the buses and the vans to find the total number of students:
\[150 + 36 = 186\]

Therefore, the total number of students is 186.

The answer is: 186
