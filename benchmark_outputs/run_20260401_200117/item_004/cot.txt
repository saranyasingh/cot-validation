Let's break down this problem using a logical syllogism:

**Premise 1:** There are 5 buses.
- Premise 2: Each bus carries 30 students.
- Conclusion: Therefore, 5 buses carry \(5 \times 30 = 150\) students.

**Premise 3:** There are 3 vans.
- Premise 4: Each van carries 12 students.
- Conclusion: Therefore, 3 vans carry \(3 \times 12 = 36\) students.

**Conclusion:** To find the total number of students, we add the students carried by the buses and the vans together:
\[150 + 36 = 186\]

Therefore, the total number of students is **186**.

The answer is: 186
