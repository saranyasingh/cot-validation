To determine how many books are on each shelf, we need to divide the total number of books by the number of shelves. Here's the step-by-step process:

1. Identify the total number of books in the library.
   - The total number of books is 360.

2. Identify the number of shelves.
   - The number of shelves is 9.

3. Divide the total number of books by the number of shelves to find out how many books are on each shelf.
   \[
   \frac{360}{9} = 40
   \]

Therefore, there are 40 books on each shelf.

The answer is: 40
