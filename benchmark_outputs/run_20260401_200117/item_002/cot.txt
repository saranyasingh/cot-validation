Premise 1: A library has 360 books.
Premise 2: These books are divided equally among 9 shelves.
Premise 3: We need to determine how many books are on each shelf.

Logical Syllogism:
Step 1: If we divide 360 books equally among 9 shelves, then each shelf would receive an equal share of the total number of books.
Step 2: To find out how many books are on each shelf, we need to perform division.
Step 3: Perform the division: \( \frac{360}{9} = 40 \).

Therefore, there are 40 books on each shelf.

The answer is: 40
