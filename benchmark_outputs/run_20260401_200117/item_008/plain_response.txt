To determine how many groups of 4 can be formed from 32 students, we need to divide the total number of students by the number of students per group:

\[
\text{Number of groups} = \frac{\text{Total number of students}}{\text{Number of students per group}}
\]

Substituting the given values:

\[
\text{Number of groups} = \frac{32}{4}
\]

Performing the division:

\[
\text{Number of groups} = 8
\]

Therefore, the answer is: 8.
