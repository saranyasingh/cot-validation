To determine how much Sarah spends on 16 pencils at $0.25 each, we need to perform a simple multiplication based on the given information.

### Logical Syllogism:

**Premise 1:** A pencil costs $0.25.
**Premise 2:** Sarah wants to buy 16 pencils.
**Conclusion:** Therefore, Sarah will spend \(16 \times 0.25\) dollars.

Now let's break it down further:

- **Step 1:** Identify the cost per item (pencil).
  - Cost per pencil = $0.25

- **Step 2:** Count the number of items (pencils) Sarah wants to buy.
  - Number of pencils = 16

- **Step 3:** Multiply the cost per item by the number of items to find the total cost.
  - Total cost = \(16 \times 0.25\)

Let's calculate this:
\[ 16 \times 0.25 = 4 \]

So, Sarah spends **$4** on 16 pencils.

The answer is: X = $4
