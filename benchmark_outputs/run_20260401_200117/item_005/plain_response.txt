To determine how much Sarah spends on 16 pencils, we can use the formula:

\[ \text{Total Cost} = \text{Number of Pencils} \times \text{Cost per Pencil} \]

Given that Sarah buys 16 pencils and each pencil costs $0.25, we substitute these values into the formula:

\[ \text{Total Cost} = 16 \times 0.25 \]

Now, let's perform the multiplication:

\[ 16 \times 0.25 = 4 \]

So, Sarah spends **$4** on 16 pencils.
