To determine how many bananas are in each group when they are organized into 2 groups, we need to divide the total number of bananas by the number of groups.

Philip has 290 bananas, and these are divided into 2 groups. So, we perform the division:

\[ \text{Number of bananas per group} = \frac{\text{Total number of bananas}}{\text{Number of groups}} = \frac{290}{2} \]

Now, let's calculate this step-by-step:
1. Divide 290 by 2.
   - \( 290 \div 2 = 145 \)

So, each group of bananas contains 145 bananas.

Therefore, the answer is: The answer is: 145
