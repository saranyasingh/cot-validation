To determine how many bananas are in each group when they are organized into 2 groups, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total number of bananas:**
   Philip has 290 bananas.
   
2. **Determine the number of groups of bananas:**
   The bananas are organized into 2 groups.
   
3. **Divide the total number of bananas by the number of groups:**
   \[
   \frac{290}{2} = 145
   \]
   
So, each group of bananas contains 145 bananas.

The answer is: Each group of bananas contains 145 bananas.
