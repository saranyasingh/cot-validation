WhiteBolts(x, n) — robe x requires n bolts of white fiber
TotalBolts(x, n) — robe x requires n bolts of fiber in total
Half(n, m) — m is half of n
Sum(a, b, c) — a + b = c

robe, n1, n2, n3

RULE-2: The total bolts in a robe are the blue bolts plus the white bolts :: ∀x ∀y ∀z ∀w ((BlueBolts(x, y) ∧ WhiteBolts(x, z) ∧ Sum(y, z, w)) → TotalBolts(x, w))
