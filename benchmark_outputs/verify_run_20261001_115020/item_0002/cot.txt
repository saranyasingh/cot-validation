EXPLANATION
To determine how many bolts of fabric are needed to make a robe, we need to break down the given information into clear steps.

1. **Identify the quantity of blue fiber**: The problem states that a robe requires 2 bolts of blue fiber.
   
2. **Calculate the quantity of white fiber**: It's specified that the amount of white fiber used is half that of the blue fiber. Since 2 bolts of blue fiber are required, then half of this would be:
   \[
   \frac{2}{2} = 1 \text{ bolt}
   \]

3. **Sum up all fibers**: Now we add both quantities together:
   - Blue fiber: 2 bolts
   - White fiber: 1 bolt
   
   Total bolts required:
   \[
   2 + 1 = 3 \text{ bolts}
   \]

Therefore, the total number of bolts of fabric needed to make the robe is: 3.
