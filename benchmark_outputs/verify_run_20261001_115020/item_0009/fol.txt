## VOCABULARY
Predicates:
Speed(x, s): leg x is driven at speed s (mph)
Duration(x, t): leg or period x lasts t hours
Distance(x, d): leg x covers d miles; for john, john is d miles from home
Product(a, b, c): a × b = c
Total(a, b, c, d, r): a + b + c + d = r

Constants:
john: the driver
leg1: first leg, 3 hours at 60 mph
leg2: standstill traffic
leg3: half hour at 30 mph
leg4: remaining time at 80 mph
four_hour_period: the 4-hour period
0, 0.5, 1.5, 2, 3, 4, 15, 30, 60, 80, 120, 180, 315: numeric values
