EXPLANATION
To determine how far John is from home at the end of his journey, we need to calculate the total distance he traveled during the 4-hour period.

1. **First leg of the trip (3 hours at 60 mph):**
   - Distance = Speed × Time
   - Distance = 60 mph × 3 hours = 180 miles

2. **Second leg of the trip (first 2 hours in standstill traffic):**
   - Since this part of the trip does not contribute to travel distance, it adds 0 miles to his total distance.
   
3. **Third leg of the trip (half an hour at 30 mph):**
   - Distance = Speed × Time
   - Distance = 30 mph × 0.5 hours = 15 miles
   
4. **Fourth leg of the trip (remaining time at 80 mph):**
   - Total time spent on the fourth leg = 4 hours - 2 hours (standstill) - 0.5 hours (at 30 mph) = 1.5 hours
   - Distance = Speed × Time
   - Distance = 80 mph × 1.5 hours = 120 miles

Now, summing up all these distances gives us the total distance traveled:
- Total distance = 180 miles + 0 miles + 15 miles + 120 miles = 315 miles

Therefore, John is 315 miles away from home at the end of those 4 hours.
