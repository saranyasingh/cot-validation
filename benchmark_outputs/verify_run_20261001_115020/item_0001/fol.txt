## VOCABULARY
Predicates:
EggsProduced(x, n) — x's ducks produce n eggs per day
EggsUsed(x, n) — x uses n eggs per day for her own consumption or baking
EggsLeft(x, n) — x has n eggs left per day after the uses accounted for so far
Price(x, n) — x sells each egg for n dollars
DailyRevenue(x, n) — x makes n dollars per day at the farmers' market

janet, n2, n3, n4, n9, n13, n16, n18

## RULES
RULE-1: If 16 eggs are produced and 3 are eaten, 16 - 3 = 13 eggs are left :: ∀x ((EggsProduced(x, n16) ∧ EggsUsed(x, n3)) → EggsLeft(x, n13))

RULE-3: If 9 eggs are left and sold at $2 each, revenue is 9 × 2 = $18 :: ∀x ((EggsLeft(x, n9) ∧ Price(x, n2)) → DailyRevenue(x, n18))
