To determine how much Janet makes every day at the farmers' market, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the total number of eggs laid each day.
2. Determine how many eggs are eaten for breakfast.
3. Subtract the eggs used for breakfast from the total to find out how many are left.
4. Calculate the value of the remaining eggs sold at the market.

Step 1: Total eggs laid per day = 16

Step 2: Eggs eaten for breakfast = 3

Step 3: Remaining eggs after breakfast = Total eggs - Eggs for breakfast = 16 - 3 = 13

Step 4: Value of eggs sold at the market = Remaining eggs * Price per egg = 13 * $2 = $26
