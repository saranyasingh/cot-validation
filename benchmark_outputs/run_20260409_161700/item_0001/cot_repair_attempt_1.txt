EXPLANATION

To determine how much Janet makes every day at the farmers' market, let's go through the correct calculations step-by-step:

1. **Total number of eggs laid per day**: 
   - Janet's ducks lay 16 eggs per day.

2. **Number of eggs eaten per day**:
   - Janet eats 3 eggs for breakfast every morning.

3. **Number of eggs left for sale**:
   - Subtract the eggs eaten from the total eggs laid:
     \[ \text{Eggs left} = 16 - 3 = 13 \]

4. **Revenue calculation**:
   - Janet sells the remaining eggs at $2 per egg.
   - Multiply the number of eggs left by the price per egg:
     \[ \text{Daily earnings} = 13 \times 2 = \$26 \]

Therefore, Janet makes \( \$26 \) every day at the farmers' market.

The answer is: 26
