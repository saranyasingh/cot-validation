EXPLANATION
To determine Josh's profit, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the total cost after purchasing the house and putting in repairs.
2. Determine the selling price of the house.
3. Calculate the profit by subtracting the total cost from the selling price.

Let's go through each step carefully:

### Step 1: Total Cost After Purchasing and Repairs
- Initial purchase price of the house: $80,000
- Amount spent on repairs: $50,000

\[
\text{Total Cost} = 80,000 + 50,000 = 130,000
\]

### Step 2: Selling Price
Since the problem doesn't explicitly mention the selling price, we'll assume it was sold at the increased value due to repairs. 

Given that the value of the house increased by 150%, we can calculate the increased value as follows:

\[
\text{Increased Value} = 80,000 \times 1.5 = 120,000
\]

So, the selling price would be the total cost plus the increased value:

\[
\text{Selling Price} = 130,000 + 120,000 = 250,000
\]

### Step 3: Calculating Profit
Profit is calculated as the selling price minus the total cost:

\[
\text{Profit} = \text{Selling Price} - \text{Total Cost}
\]

Substituting the values we have:

\[
\text{Profit} = 250,000 - 130,000 = 120,000
\]

Therefore, the profit made by Josh is $120,000.

The answer is: 120,000
