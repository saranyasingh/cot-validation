To calculate Josh's profit from flipping the house, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the new selling price after the repairs:** 
   - The original cost of the house was $80,000.
   - The amount spent on repairs was $50,000.
   - Therefore, the total initial investment (cost + repair costs) is:
     \[
     \$80,000 + \$50,000 = \$130,000
     \]
   - The increase in value due to the repairs is 150% of the initial investment. So, we calculate:
     \[
     150\% \text{ of } \$130,000 = 1.5 \times \$130,000 = \$195,000
     \]
   - Adding this increase to the initial investment gives us the new selling price:
     \[
     \$130,000 + \$195,000 = \$325,000
     \]

2. **Calculate the profit:**
   - The profit is the difference between the selling price and the initial investment:
     \[
     \$325,000 - \$130,000 = \$195,000
     \]

So, Josh made a profit of $195,000 from flipping the house. 

The answer is: 195000
