To determine how many cups of feed Wendi needs for the final meal of the day, we first calculate the total amount of feed given out during the two earlier meals.

In the morning:
\[ \text{Morning feed} = 15 \text{ cups} \]

In the afternoon:
\[ \text{Afternoon feed} = 25 \text{ cups} \]

Adding these together gives the total amount of feed given out before the final meal:
\[ \text{Total feed before final meal} = 15 + 25 = 40 \text{ cups} \]

Since there are 20 chickens and they receive 3 cups of feed per day, the total amount of feed required for all chickens for one day is:
\[ \text{Total feed needed for all chickens} = 20 \times 3 = 60 \text{ cups} \]

Now, subtracting the total feed given out before the final meal from the total feed needed for all chickens provides the amount of feed needed for the final meal:
\[ \text{Final meal feed} = 60 - 40 = 20 \text{ cups} \]

Thus, Wendi needs to give her chickens 20 cups of feed in the final meal of the day.

The answer is: 20
