EXPLANATION
Let's break down the problem step-by-step to determine Eliza's total earnings for the week.

1. **Identify the base pay**: Eliza earns $10 per hour for the first 40 hours.
   - Base Pay = Hourly Rate × Hours Worked
   - Base Pay = $10/hour × 40 hours
   - Base Pay = $400

2. **Calculate the overtime pay**:
   - Overtime Pay = Overtime Rate × (Hours Worked - Regular Hours)
   - Overtime Rate = 1.2 × Regular Rate ($10/hour)
   - Overtime Rate = 1.2 × $10/hour
   - Overtime Rate = $12/hour
   - Overtime Pay = $12/hour × (45 hours - 40 hours)
   - Overtime Pay = $12/hour × 5 hours
   - Overtime Pay = $60

3. **Total Earnings Calculation**:
   - Total Earnings = Base Pay + Overtime Pay
   - Total Earnings = $400 + $60
   - Total Earnings = $460

Therefore, Eliza’s earnings for the week amount to $460.
