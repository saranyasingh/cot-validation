To calculate Eliza's earnings for the week when she worked 45 hours, we need to break down her earnings into two parts:

1. Earnings from the first 40 hours at her regular rate.
2. Earnings from the overtime hours (the remaining 5 hours) at her overtime rate.

First, let's calculate the earnings from the first 40 hours:
- Regular hourly rate = $10/hour
- Number of regular hours worked = 40 hours

Regular earnings = Regular hourly rate × Number of regular hours
= $10/hour × 40 hours
= $400

Next, let's calculate the earnings from the overtime hours:
- Overtime hourly rate = Regular hourly rate × 1.2
= $10/hour × 1.2
= $12/hour
- Number of overtime hours worked = 5 hours

Overtime earnings = Overtime hourly rate × Number of overtime hours
= $12/hour × 5 hours
= $60

Finally, add both amounts together to get total earnings for the week:
Total earnings = Regular earnings + Overtime earnings
= $400 + $60
= $460

Therefore, Eliza's earnings for this week amount to **$460**.
