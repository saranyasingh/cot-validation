To determine the total number of bolts of fiber needed to make one robe, we can follow these steps:

1. Identify how many bolts of blue fiber are required for one robe.
   - The problem states that one robe requires 2 bolts of blue fiber.

2. Determine how many bolts of white fiber are required for one robe.
   - The problem states that one robe requires half as much white fiber as blue fiber.
   - Since 2 bolts of blue fiber are used, half of this amount would be \( \frac{2}{2} = 1 \) bolt of white fiber.

3. Calculate the total number of bolts of fiber needed for one robe.
   - Add the number of bolts of blue fiber (2) to the number of bolts of white fiber (1).
   - This gives us \( 2 + 1 = 3 \) bolts of fiber per robe.

Therefore, the answer is: 3
