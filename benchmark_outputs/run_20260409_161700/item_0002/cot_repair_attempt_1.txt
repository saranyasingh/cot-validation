To solve this problem, let's break down the information given:

1. A robe requires 2 bolts of blue fiber.
2. It also needs half as much white fiber as blue fiber.

Let's calculate the total number of bolts required step-by-step:

1. **Blue Fiber Calculation**:
   - We know that 2 bolts of blue fiber are needed for the robe.

2. **White Fiber Calculation**:
   - Since the white fiber requirement is half of the blue fiber,
     \[
     \text{White Fiber} = \frac{\text{Blue Fiber}}{2} = \frac{2}{2} = 1 \text{ bolt}
     \]

3. **Total Bolts Calculation**:
   - Adding both types of fibers together,
     \[
     \text{Total Bolts} = \text{Blue Fiber} + \text{White Fiber} = 2 + 1 = 3 \text{ bolts}
     \]

Therefore, the total number of bolts required to make the robe is:

Answer: 3
