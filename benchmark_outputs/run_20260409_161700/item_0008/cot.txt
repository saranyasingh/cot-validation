To determine how long it takes Carla to download the 200 GB file after restarting, we need to break down the problem into several steps.

**Step 1:** Calculate the time taken to download the first 80% of the file before the restart.
- Carla's normal speed is 2 GB per minute.
- First, find out what 80% of the total file size (200 GB) is:
  \[
  0.80 \times 200 = 160 \text{ GB}
  \]
- Time to download 160 GB at her normal speed:
  \[
  \frac{160 \text{ GB}}{2 \text{ GB/min}} = 80 \text{ minutes}
  \]

**Step 2:** Account for the downtime during the restart.
- The restart lasts 20 minutes.

**Step 3:** Calculate the remaining time needed to complete the download after the restart.
- Subtract the time spent downloading the first part and the downtime from the total time required to download the entire file.
  - Total file size: 200 GB
  - Downloaded so far: 160 GB
  - Downtime: 20 minutes
  - Remaining time:
    \[
    200 \text{ GB} - 160 \text{ GB} = 40 \text{ GB}
    \]
    \[
    \frac{40 \text{ GB}}{2 \text{ GB/min}} = 20 \text{ minutes}
    \]

**Step 4:** Add the downtime back into the calculation.
- Since the restart took place midway through the initial download, we need to add this downtime back into the total time.
  \[
  80 \text{ minutes} + 20 \text{ minutes} = 100 \text{ minutes}
  \]

**Conclusion:**
The total time it takes Carla to download the 200 GB file after restarting is **100 minutes**.
