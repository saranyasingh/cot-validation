To determine how many sheep Toulouse, Charleston, and Seattle have together, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Determine the number of sheep in Charleston based on Seattle's count**.
   - Given that Seattle has 20 sheep, and Charleston has 4 times as many sheep as Seattle.
     \[
     \text{Number of sheep in Charleston} = 4 \times 20 = 80
     \]

2. **Determine the number of sheep in Toulouse based on Charleston's count**.
   - Given that Toulouse has twice as many sheep as Charleston.
     \[
     \text{Number of sheep in Toulouse} = 2 \times 80 = 160
     \]

3. **Calculate the total number of sheep for all three locations**.
   - Add the number of sheep in Toulouse, Charleston, and Seattle.
     \[
     \text{Total number of sheep} = 160 + 80 + 20 = 260
     \]

Thus, the total number of sheep in Toulouse, Charleston, and Seattle together is **260**.

The answer is: 260
