EXPLANATION
To calculate the total cost for 16 glasses, we start with the base cost of the first glass, which is $5. Since each subsequent glass costs 60% of the previous glass’s price, we use the formula for the sum of a geometric series to find the total cost.

1. **Base Cost**: The first glass costs $5.

2. **Geometric Series**: The cost of each subsequent glass forms a geometric series where each term is 60% of the previous term.

   Let \( P_n \) represent the cost of the \( n \)-th glass. Then,
   \[
   P_n = P_{n-1} \times 0.60
   \]
   Given that the first term \( P_1 = \$5 \),
   \[
   P_2 = 5 \times 0.60 = \$3
   \]
   \[
   P_3 = 3 \times 0.60 = \$1.80
   \]
   Continuing this process, we get:
   \[
   P_4 = 1.80 \times 0.60 = \$1.08
   \]
   \[
   P_5 = 1.08 \times 0.60 = \$0.648
   \]
   \[
   P_6 = 0.648 \times 0.60 = \$0.3888
   \]
   And so on...

3. To find the total cost, we sum up the infinite geometric series starting at 5 and having a common ratio of 0.60. However, since we are dealing with 16 glasses, we need to consider the partial sum of the series up to the 16th term. 

   Using the formula for the sum of the first \( n \) terms of a geometric series:
   \[
   S_n = P_1 \left( \frac{1 - r^n}{1 - r} \right)
   \]
   where \( P_1 = 5 \), \( r = 0.60 \), and \( n = 16 \):
   \[
   S_{16} = 5 \left( \frac{1 - (0.60)^{16}}{1 - 0.60} \right)
   \]
   Simplifying further:
   \[
   S_{16} = 5 \left( \frac{1 - (0.60)^{16}}{0.40} \right)
   \]
   Calculating \( (0.60)^{16} \approx 0.0799 \):
   \[
   S_{16} = 5 \left( \frac{1 - 0.0799}{0.40} \right)
   \]
   \[
   S_{16} = 5 \left( \frac{0.9201}{0.40} \right)
   \]
   \[
   S_{16} = 5 \times 2.30025
   \]
   \[
   S_{16} \approx 11.50125
   \]

Therefore, Kylar needs to pay approximately $\boxed{11.50}$ dollars for 16 glasses.
