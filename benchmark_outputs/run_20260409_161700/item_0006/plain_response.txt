To determine how much Kylar needs to pay for 16 glasses, we first calculate the cost of each glass and then sum up the total cost.

1. The cost of one glass is given as $5.
2. Every second glass costs 60% of the original price. Therefore, the cost of each second glass is:
   \[
   5 \times 0.6 = 3 \text{ dollars}
   \]

Next, we calculate the number of second glasses Kylar will buy (which is half of the total number of glasses):
\[
\frac{16}{2} = 8 \text{ glasses}
\]

Now, we can find the total cost by adding the cost of all 16 glasses together:
\[
(16 - 8) \times 5 + 8 \times 3 = 8 \times 5 + 8 \times 3
\]
\[
= 40 + 24 = 64 \text{ dollars}
\]

Therefore, Kylar needs to pay $\boxed{64}$ dollars for the glasses.
