1) The problem states: house uses 20 blocks. (Given)
2) The problem states: tower uses 50 blocks. (Given)
3) “How many more blocks the tower uses than the house” equals the difference 50 − 20. (Definition of "more" as a difference)
4) Compute 50 − 20 = 30. (Arithmetic)
5) Therefore, the tower used 30 more blocks than the house.

The answer is: 30
