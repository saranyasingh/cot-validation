- Premise 1: Edward spent a total of $6 to buy 2 books and afterwards has $12 left.
- Premise 2: The two books cost the same amount each.
- Premise 3: Therefore the total cost of the two books is $6 (from Premise 1).
- Premise 4: Since the two books have equal price, the price per book is the total cost divided by 2: 6 ÷ 2.
- Premise 5: Compute 6 ÷ 2 = 3.
- Conclusion: Therefore each book costs $3.
