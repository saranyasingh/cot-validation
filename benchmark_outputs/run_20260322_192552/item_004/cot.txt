1) Premise: The book has 3 chapters. (Given)
2) Premise: Each chapter has the same number of pages. (Given)
3) Definition: Let p be the number of pages per chapter. Then the total pages T satisfy T = 3 * p. (From 1 and 2)
4) Premise: The total number of pages in the book is 594. (Given)
5) Therefore 3 * p = 594. (From 3 and 4)
6) Arithmetic: p = 594 / 3 = 198. (From 5)
7) Therefore each chapter has 198 pages. (From 6)
8) Note: The fact that Frank took 607 days to finish the book does not affect the page count. (Observation)
9) The answer is: 198
