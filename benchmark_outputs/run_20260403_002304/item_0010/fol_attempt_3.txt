### ## FACTS
1. Total number of books: \( B = 34 \).
2. Number of bookshelves: \( S = 2 \).

### ## RULES
There is no additional rule provided.

### ## INFERENCES
Given:
- \( |B| = 34 \)
- \( |S| = 2 \)

Calculate:
\[ \text{Number of books per shelf} = \frac{|B|}{|S|} = \frac{34}{2} = 17 \]

Thus, the number of books per shelf is 17.
