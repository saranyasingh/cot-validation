To determine how many tickets Dave won at the arcade, we can set up an equation based on the information given:

Let's denote the number of tickets Dave won as \( x \).

According to the problem:
- Dave used 12 tickets to buy toys.
- After buying the toys, he still has 14 tickets left.

This means that the total number of tickets he won minus the tickets he spent equals the tickets he has left:
\[ x - 12 = 14 \]

Now, we solve for \( x \):
\[ x = 14 + 12 \]
\[ x = 26 \]

Therefore, the answer is: 26.
