Let's break down the information given and use it to construct a logical argument:

**Premise 1:** Jessie started jogging daily and lost 126 kilograms.
- This tells us that her weight decreased over time due to jogging.

**Premise 2:** Jessie’s current weight is 66 kilograms.
- We know exactly how much she weighs now.

**Conclusion:** To find out how much Jessie weighed before starting to jog, we need to add back the total weight loss (126 kg) to her current weight (66 kg).

**Logical Syllogism:**

1. **Jessie started jogging daily and lost 126 kilograms.**
   - Premise 1

2. **She currently weighs 66 kilograms.**
   - Given fact

3. **To find her original weight, we must account for both her current weight and the weight loss.**
   - Logical deduction based on Premises 1 and 2

4. **Add the weight loss (126 kg) to her current weight (66 kg).**
   - Calculation to determine the original weight

5. **The calculation gives us:**
   - \( 66 \text{ kg} + 126 \text{ kg} = 192 \text{ kg} \)
   - Conclusion: Jessie originally weighed 192 kilograms.
