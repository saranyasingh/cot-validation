**Premise 1:** Dave initially had 15 apps.
- **Premise 2:** Dave then added 71 new apps.
- **Premise 3:** After adding these new apps, Dave had 86 apps (since 15 + 71 = 86).
- **Premise 4:** Dave deleted some apps and ended up with 14 apps remaining.
- **Premise 5:** Therefore, Dave deleted \(86 - 14 = 72\) apps in total.

To find out how many more apps Dave deleted than he added:
- We know he added 71 apps.
- We calculated that he deleted 72 apps in total.

Thus,
\[ \text{Number of apps deleted} - \text{Number of apps added} = 72 - 71 = 1 \]

Therefore, the answer is: 1. 

The difference between the number of apps deleted and the number of apps added is exactly 1.
