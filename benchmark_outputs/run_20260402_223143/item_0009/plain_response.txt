To determine how many tickets Dave won at the arcade, we need to consider the information given:

1. Dave used 12 tickets to buy toys.
2. After buying the toys, he still has 14 tickets left.

We can set up an equation to represent this situation:
\[ \text{Total Tickets} - \text{Tickets Used} = \text{Tickets Left} \]

Substituting the known values into the equation:
\[ \text{Total Tickets} - 12 = 14 \]

Solving for Total Tickets:
\[ \text{Total Tickets} = 14 + 12 \]
\[ \text{Total Tickets} = 26 \]

Therefore, Dave won 26 tickets at the arcade. The answer is: 26
