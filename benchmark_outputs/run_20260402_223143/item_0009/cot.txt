Premise 1: At the arcade, Dave had won some tickets.
Premise 2: Dave used 12 tickets to buy some toys.
Premise 3: After buying the toys, Dave still has 14 tickets left.

We need to determine how many tickets Dave won initially.

Let's break down the information given:
- Let \( W \) represent the total number of tickets Dave won initially.
- We know that after using 12 tickets, Dave has 14 tickets left.

From Premise 3, we have:
\[ W - 12 = 14 \]

To find \( W \), we add 12 to both sides of the equation:
\[ W = 14 + 12 \]
\[ W = 26 \]

Therefore, Dave won 26 tickets at the arcade.

The answer is: 26
