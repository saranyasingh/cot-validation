To determine how many more crayons Paul lost than those he gave to his friends, we need to follow these steps:

### Step 1: Calculate the total number of crayons given away and lost.
- **Crayons given to friends:** 90
- **Crayons lost:** 412

Total crayons accounted for:
\[ \text{Total} = \text{Crayons given to friends} + \text{Crayons lost} \]
\[ \text{Total} = 90 + 412 \]

Calculate the sum:
\[ \text{Total} = 502 \]

So, Paul has used or lost 502 crayons.

### Step 2: Determine the difference between the crayons lost and the crayons given away.
We need to find out how many more crayons were lost than given away.
\[ \text{Difference} = \text{Crayons lost} - \text{Crayons given to friends} \]
\[ \text{Difference} = 412 - 90 \]

Perform the subtraction:
\[ \text{Difference} = 322 \]

Thus, Paul lost 322 more crayons than he gave to his friends.

### Conclusion
The answer is: 322
