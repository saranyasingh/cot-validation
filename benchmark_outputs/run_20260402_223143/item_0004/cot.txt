To determine how many pages are in each chapter, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total number of pages per chapter**:
   - We know that the book has a total of 594 pages.
   - There are 3 chapters in the book.
   - Therefore, the number of pages per chapter is calculated as follows:
     \[
     \text{Pages per chapter} = \frac{\text{Total pages}}{\text{Number of chapters}} = \frac{594}{3}
     \]
   - Performing the division:
     \[
     \frac{594}{3} = 198
     \]

2. **Verify the calculation**:
   - To ensure our result is accurate, let's check if multiplying the number of pages per chapter by the number of chapters equals the total number of pages:
     \[
     198 \times 3 = 594
     \]
   - This confirms that our calculation is correct.

Therefore, the number of pages in each chapter is 198.

The answer is: 198
