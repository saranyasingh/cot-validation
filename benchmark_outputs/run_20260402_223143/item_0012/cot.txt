**Logical Syllogism**

1. **Premise 1:** Adam has 4 more apples than Jackie.
   - This tells us that if we know how many apples Jackie has, we can find out how many apples Adam has.

2. **Premise 2:** Together Adam and Jackie have 14 apples.
   - If we add up all the apples they have together, it equals 14.

3. **Conclusion:** Let's denote the number of apples Jackie has as \( J \) and the number of apples Adam has as \( A \).
   - From Premise 1, \( A = J + 4 \).

4. **Substitution:** Since \( A = J + 4 \), substitute this into the total equation:
   - \( J + (J + 4) = 14 \)

5. **Simplify Equation:** Combine like terms on the left side:
   - \( 2J + 4 = 14 \)

6. **Isolate Variable:** Subtract 4 from both sides to isolate the term with \( J \):
   - \( 2J = 10 \)

7. **Solve for \( J \):**
   - Divide both sides by 2:
     \[
     J = 5
     \]

8. **Find \( A \):**
   - Substitute \( J = 5 \) back into the expression for \( A \):
     \[
     A = J + 4 = 5 + 4 = 9
     \]

9. **Determine Bob’s Apples:**
   - Bob has 6 apples more than Adam and Jackie together:
     \[
     B = A + J + 6 = 9 + 5 + 6 = 20
     \]

Therefore, the answer is: Bob has 20 apples.
