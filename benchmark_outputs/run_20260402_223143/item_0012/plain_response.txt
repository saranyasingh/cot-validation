Let's denote the number of apples Jackie has as J.

According to the problem:
- Adam has 4 more apples than Jackie, so Adam has J + 4 apples.
- Together, Adam and Jackie have 14 apples, which means (J + 4) + J = 14.

Now we can solve for J:
2J + 4 = 14
2J = 10
J = 5

So Jackie has 5 apples.

Next, we need to find out how many apples Adam has:
Adam has 4 more apples than Jackie, so Adam has J + 4 = 5 + 4 = 9 apples.

Finally, we calculate how many apples Bob has:
Bob has 6 apples more than Adam and Jackie together, so Bob has (J + 4) + J + 6 = (5 + 4) + 5 + 6 = 9 + 5 + 6 = 20 apples.

Therefore, Bob has 20 apples.

The answer is: 20
