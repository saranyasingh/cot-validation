Let's break down this problem into a logical syllogism:

1. **Premise**: Bryan took a look at his books.
   - This establishes that Bryan is aware of the number and distribution of his books.

2. **Premise**: He has 34 books distributed equally in 2 bookshelves.
   - This statement directly provides us with the total number of books (34) and how these books are divided among two bookshelves.

3. **Premise**: To find out how many books are in each bookshelf, we need to divide the total number of books by the number of bookshelves.
   - We apply basic arithmetic division here:
     \[
     \text{Number of books per bookshelf} = \frac{\text{Total number of books}}{\text{Number of bookshelves}}
     \]
   - Substituting the given values:
     \[
     \text{Number of books per bookshelf} = \frac{34}{2}
     \]

4. **Calculation**:
   - Performing the division:
     \[
     \frac{34}{2} = 17
     \]

5. **Conclusion**:
   - Therefore, there are 17 books in each bookshelf.
