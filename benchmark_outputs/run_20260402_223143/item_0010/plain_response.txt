To determine how many books are in each bookshelf when Bryan distributes his 34 books equally among 2 shelves, we can use simple division:

\[ \text{Number of books per shelf} = \frac{\text{Total number of books}}{\text{Number of shelves}} \]

Plugging in the values given:

\[ \text{Number of books per shelf} = \frac{34}{2} = 17 \]

So, there are 17 books in each bookshelf. The answer is: 17.
