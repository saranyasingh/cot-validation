**Logical Syllogism**

1. **Premise 1:** Marco's dad's strawberries weighed 11 pounds.
   - This establishes the weight of Marco's dad's strawberries as 11 pounds.

2. **Premise 2:** Together, their strawberries (including Marco's) weighed 30 pounds.
   - This provides the total combined weight of both sets of strawberries.

3. **Conclusion:** To find out how much Marco's strawberries weighed, we need to subtract the weight of Marco's dad's strawberries from the total combined weight.
   
4. **Calculation:**
   - Total combined weight = 30 pounds
   - Weight of Marco's dad's strawberries = 11 pounds
   
5. **Subtraction:**
   \[
   \text{Weight of Marco's strawberries} = \text{Total combined weight} - \text{Weight of Marco's dad's strawberries}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Weight of Marco's strawberries} = 30 \text{ pounds} - 11 \text{ pounds} = 19 \text{ pounds}
   \]

6. **Final Answer:** Therefore, Marco's strawberries weighed 19 pounds.

The answer is: 19
