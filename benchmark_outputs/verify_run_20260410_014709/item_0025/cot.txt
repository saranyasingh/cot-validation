EXPLANATION
To find the original price of the book, we need to reverse the effect of the 25% discount that Kyle paid.

Step 1: Understand the relationship between the discounted price and the original price.
A 25% discount means Kyle paid 75% of the original price because 100% - 25% = 75%.

Step 2: Set up an equation based on this information.
Let \( P \) represent the original price of the book.
After applying the 25% discount, Kyle paid \( 0.75P \).

Step 3: We know the discounted price Kyle paid ($19.50).
So, we have the equation:
\[ 0.75P = 19.50 \]

Step 4: Solve for \( P \), which gives us the original price of the book.
Divide both sides by 0.75:
\[ P = \frac{19.50}{0.75} \]
\[ P = 26 \]

Therefore, the original price of the book was $26.

The answer is: 26
