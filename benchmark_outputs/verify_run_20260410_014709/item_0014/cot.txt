EXPLANATION

1. Let's denote the total number of vacuum cleaners Melanie started with as \( x \).
   
2. After selling a third of her vacuum cleaners at the green house, she had \( \frac{2}{3}x \) left.
   
3. Then, she sold 2 more vacuum cleaners at the red house, so she had \( \frac{2}{3}x - 2 \) left.
   
4. At the orange house, she sold half of what was left after the red sale, which means she sold \( \frac{1}{2}(\frac{2}{3}x - 2) = \frac{1}{3}x - 1 \). Therefore, she had \( \frac{2}{3}x - 2 - (\frac{1}{3}x - 1) = \frac{1}{3}x - 1 \) left.

5. According to the problem, this remaining amount is equal to 5 vacuum cleaners. Thus, we have the equation:
   \[
   \frac{1}{3}x - 1 = 5
   \]

6. Solving this equation for \( x \):
   \[
   \frac{1}{3}x - 1 + 1 = 5 + 1 \\
   \frac{1}{3}x = 6 \\
   x = 6 \times 3 \\
   x = 18
   \]

Therefore, the answer is: 18.
