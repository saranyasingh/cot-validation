EXPLANATION

Let's break down the problem step-by-step.

Firstly, we know that Wendi has 20 chickens, and each chicken receives 3 cups of feed per day. Therefore, the total amount of feed given to all chickens per day would be:

Total feed = Number of chickens × Feed per chicken
           = 20 × 3
           = 60 cups

Next, let's look at how much feed was given in the morning and afternoon:

Morning feed = 15 cups
Afternoon feed = 25 cups

To find out how much feed was given before the final meal, we add these two amounts together:

Pre-final-meal feed = Morning feed + Afternoon feed
                    = 15 + 25
                    = 40 cups

Now, since the total daily feed needed is 60 cups (as calculated earlier), and we've accounted for 40 cups already (morning and afternoon), the remaining feed required for the final meal must be:

Final meal feed = Total daily feed - Pre-final-meal feed
                = 60 - 40
                = 20 cups

Therefore, Wendi needs to give her chickens 20 cups of feed in the final meal of the day.

The answer is: 20
