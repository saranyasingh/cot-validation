EXPLANATION

To determine how much shorter the candle will be after burning from 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM, we need to calculate the total time during which the candle was burning and then use this information to find out how much shorter it became.

1. **Identify the duration**: From 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM is a span of 4 hours.
   
2. **Understand the melting rate**: The candle melts at a rate of 2 centimeters per hour.

3. **Calculate the total melting amount**: Multiply the duration (in hours) by the rate of melting (in centimeters per hour).
   \[
   \text{Total melting} = 4 \, \text{hours} \times 2 \, \text{cm/hour} = 8 \, \text{centimeters}
   \]

Therefore, the candle will be 8 centimeters shorter after burning from 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM.

The answer is: 8
