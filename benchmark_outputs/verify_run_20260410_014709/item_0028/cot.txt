EXPLANATION

To determine how much Cynthia will spend on ice cream after 60 days, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the total number of servings of ice cream Cynthia consumes over 60 days.
2. Determine how many cartons of ice cream she needs to buy.
3. Calculate the total cost based on the number of cartons needed.

Step 1: Calculate the total number of servings of ice cream consumed in 60 days.
- Cynthia eats 1 serving of ice cream every night.
- There are 60 days in her period.
- Therefore, the total number of servings consumed = \( 1 \text{ serving/day} \times 60 \text{ days} = 60 \text{ servings} \).

Step 2: Determine how many cartons of ice cream are needed.
- Each carton contains 15 servings.
- To find out how many cartons are required, divide the total number of servings by the number of servings per carton.
- Number of cartons needed = \( \frac{60 \text{ servings}}{15 \text{ servings/carton}} = 4 \text{ cartons} \).

Step 3: Calculate the total cost.
- Each carton costs $4.00.
- Multiply the number of cartons needed by the cost per carton.
- Total cost = \( 4 \text{ cartons} \times \$4.00/\text{carton} = \$16.00 \).

Therefore, Cynthia will spend $16.00 on ice cream after 60 days.

The answer is: 16
