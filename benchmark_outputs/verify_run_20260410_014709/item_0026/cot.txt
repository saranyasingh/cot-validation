To determine how many boxes of pizza Marie ordered, we need to first calculate the total amount spent on the items she already knows about.

   \[
   1 \text{ meal} \times \$12 = \$12

   \[

3. Calculate the total cost of the 4 apples:
   \[
   4 \text{ apples} \times \$1.50 = \$6
   \]

Now, let's sum up these amounts:
\[
\$12 + \$15 + \$6 = \$33
\]

Marie paid a total of $50, so the remaining amount must have been spent on the pizzas:
\[
\$50 - \$33 = \$17
\]

Since each box of pizza costs $8.50, we can find out how many boxes were purchased by dividing the remaining amount by the price per box:
\[
\frac{\$17}{\$8.50} = 2
\]
