EXPLANATION
To determine how much Mishka spent on all the clothing items, we need to calculate the cost of each type of item separately and then sum these amounts.

First, let's find out how much Mishka spent on shorts:
- Mishka bought 3 pairs of shorts.
- Each pair of shorts costs $16.50.

\[
\text{Cost of shorts} = 3 \times \$16.50 = \$49.50
\]

Next, let's calculate the total cost of pants:
- Mishka bought 3 pairs of pants.
- Each pair of pants costs $22.50.

\[
\text{Cost of pants} = 3 \times \$22.50 = \$67.50
\]

Now, let's find the total cost of shoes:
- Mishka bought 3 pairs of shoes.
- Each pair of shoes costs $42.

\[
\text{Cost of shoes} = 3 \times \$42 = \$126
\]

Finally, we add up the costs of all the items to get the total amount Mishka spent:

\[
\text{Total cost} = \text{Cost of shorts} + \text{Cost of pants} + \text{Cost of shoes}
\]
\[
\text{Total cost} = \$49.50 + \$67.50 + \$126 = \$243
\]

Therefore, the total amount Mishka spent on all the clothing items is:

The answer is: 243
