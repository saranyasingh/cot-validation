To determine how many years Carlos needs to start earning money on his lemon tree, we need to calculate the net profit per year after accounting for both the initial investment and ongoing expenses.

### Step-by-Step Logical Reasoning:

**Premise 1:** The total cost to plant the lemon tree is $90.
   - This is our first expense.

**Premise 2:** Each year, the tree grows 7 lemons, which can be sold for $1.5 each.
   - This gives us the revenue generated per year.

**Premise 3:** It costs $3 annually to water and feed the tree.
   - This is another recurring expense.

**Premise 4:** We need to find out when the annual revenue exceeds the combined annual expenses (initial investment + annual watering).

**Step 1:** Calculate the annual revenue.
\[ \text{Annual Revenue} = 7 \text{ lemons} \times \$1.5/\text{lemon} = \$10.5 \]

**Step 2:** Calculate the annual net profit.
\[ \text{Annual Net Profit} = \text{Annual Revenue} - \text{Annual Expenses} = \$10.5 - \$3 = \$7.5 \]

**Step 3:** Determine how many years it takes to recover the initial investment ($90).
\[ \text{Years to Recover Investment} = \frac{\text{Initial Investment}}{\text{Annual Net Profit}} = \frac{\$90}{\$7.5} = 12 \]

**Conclusion:** After 12 years, Carlos will have earned enough money from selling the lemons to cover the initial planting cost and break even financially.

Therefore, the answer is: 12
