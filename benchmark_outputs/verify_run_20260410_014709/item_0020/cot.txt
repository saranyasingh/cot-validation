To determine Marissa's required walking speed for the remaining distance to achieve an average speed of 4 miles per hour, we'll follow these steps:

**Premise 1:** Marissa walked 4 miles in 1 hour.
- This establishes that her initial speed was 4 miles per hour.

**Premise 2:** Marissa walked 2 more miles in 1 hour.
- This confirms her pace on the second segment of the hike.

**Conclusion:** We know her total time for the entire hike is 2 hours because she spent 1 hour on the first part and 1 hour on the second part.

**Premise 3:** To find the average speed, we use the formula:
\[ \text{Average Speed} = \frac{\text{Total Distance}}{\text{Total Time}} \]

**Step-by-step calculation:**
1. **Calculate Total Distance:** 
   - Total Distance = 4 miles + 2 miles = 6 miles
   - Total Time = 2 hours

2. **Apply Average Speed Formula:**
   - Desired Average Speed = 4 miles per hour

3. **Set up the equation using the given values:**
   - \( \frac{6 \text{ miles}}{2 \text{ hours}} = 3 \text{ miles per hour} \)

However, this result doesn't match our goal of achieving an average speed of 4 miles per hour. Therefore, we must adjust our approach since the desired average speed is higher than calculated based solely on her speeds for individual segments.

**Re-evaluation:**

Given:
- Total Distance = 12 miles
- Total Time = 2 hours

We aim for an average speed of 4 miles per hour over the entire 12-mile hike.

**Calculation:**

If the total time is 2 hours and the desired average speed is 4 miles per hour, then:
\[ \text{Required Total Distance} = \text{Desired Average Speed} \times \text{Total Time} \]
\[ \text{Required Total Distance} = 4 \text{ mph} \times 2 \text{ hours} \]
\[ \text{Required Total Distance} = 8 \text{ miles} \]

Since Marissa has already covered 6 miles, the remaining distance needed to meet the average speed requirement is:
\[ \text{Remaining Distance} = \text{Total Distance} - \text{Distance Covered} \]
\[ \text{Remaining Distance} = 12 \text{ miles} - 6 \text{ miles} \]
\[ \text{Remaining Distance} = 6 \text{ miles} \]

Now, calculate the required walking speed for the remaining 6 miles:
\[ \text{Required Walking Speed} = \frac{\text{Remaining Distance}}{\text{Total Time}} \]
\[ \text{Required Walking Speed} = \frac{6 \text{ miles}}{2 \text{ hours}} \]
\[ \text{Required Walking Speed} = 3 \text{ miles per hour} \]

Therefore, Marissa needs to walk at a speed of **3 miles per hour** for the remaining 6 miles to achieve an average speed of 4 miles per hour for the entire 12-mile hike.
