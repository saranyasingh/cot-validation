EXPLANATION
First, we need to determine the initial cost of the house including the purchase price and repair costs. Josh bought the house for $80,000 and spent an additional $50,000 on repairs.

Initial Cost = Purchase Price + Repair Costs
           = $80,000 + $50,000
           = $130,000

Next, we calculate the increase in the house's value due to the repairs. The problem states that the repairs increased the value of the house by 150%.

Increase in Value = Initial Cost * Increase Percentage
                = $130,000 * 150%
                = $195,000

This increase in value represents the total amount of money Josh made after accounting for his investment (purchase price plus repair costs).

Now, to find out how much profit Josh made, we subtract the original investment (purchase price plus repair costs) from the total increase in value.

Profit = Total Increase in Value - Original Investment
       = $195,000 - $130,000
       = $65,000

Therefore, the profit Josh made is $65,000.

The answer is: 65
