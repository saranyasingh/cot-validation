To determine how many years ago Raymond's son was born, we need to follow these steps:

**Premise 1:** Raymond was born 6 years before Samantha.
- Premise 1 directly states that the difference in their birth years is 6 years.

**Premise 2:** Raymond had a son when he was 23 years old.
- This tells us about Raymond's current age (23) and his age at the time of having the son (which must have been less than or equal to 23).

**Premise 3:** Samantha is currently 31 years old.
- We know this information because it provides the current age of Samantha, which helps us establish her chronological timeline relative to Raymond and his son.

**Step-by-step Reasoning:**

1. **Determine Raymond’s Current Age:**
   - Given that Samantha is currently 31 and Raymond was born 6 years before her, Raymond must be \(31 + 6 = 37\) years old.

2. **Identify When Raymond's Son Was Born:**
   - Raymond has a son who was born when he was 23 years old.
   - Since Raymond is now 37, the son was born 37 - 23 = 14 years ago.

**Conclusion:**
Raymond's son was born 14 years ago.

**Answer:**
The answer is: 14
