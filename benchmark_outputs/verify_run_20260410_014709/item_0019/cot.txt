EXPLANATION
To determine how many dozens of eggs Claire eats in 4 weeks, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the total number of eggs Claire eats per week.
2. Multiply that number by 4 to get the total for 4 weeks.

Step 1: Determine the number of eggs eaten per day.
- Claire makes a 3-egg omelet every morning.
- There are typically 7 days in a week (including weekends).
So, the number of eggs eaten per week = 3 eggs/day * 7 days/week = 21 eggs/week.

Step 2: Calculate the total number of eggs eaten over 4 weeks.
- Total eggs eaten in 4 weeks = Eggs per week * Weeks = 21 eggs/week * 4 weeks = 84 eggs.

Step 3: Convert the total number of eggs into dozens.
- Since there are 12 eggs in a dozen,
   - Total dozens of eggs = Total eggs / Dozen size = 84 eggs / 12 eggs/dozen = 7 dozens.

Therefore, the answer is: 7.
