To determine the total distance covered by each train over the two days, we need to break down the problem into smaller steps.

### Step 1: Calculate the distance covered on the first day.
- Both trains start at the same location (San Rafael).
- On the first day, they travel 80 miles westward.

### Step 2: Calculate the distance covered on the second day.
- After the first day, the trains continue their journey.
- On the second day, they travel an additional 150 miles northward.

Since both trains have been traveling continuously since the beginning, including the second day, the total distance traveled on the second day remains the same as the initial segment.

Therefore, the distance covered on the second day by each train is also **150 miles**.

### Step 3: Summarize the total distances covered by each train.
- Total distance covered by each train = Distance covered on the first day + Distance covered on the second day

\[
\text{Total distance} = 80 \text{ miles} + 150 \text{ miles}
\]

\[

\]
