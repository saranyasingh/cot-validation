Explanation:

1. Toula bought 3 dozen donuts at $68 per dozen.
   - Total cost for donuts = 3 × $68 = $204.

2. She also bought 2 dozen mini cupcakes at $80 per dozen.
   - Total cost for cupcakes = 2 × $80 = $160.

3. Lastly, she bought 6 dozen mini cheesecakes at $55 per dozen.
   - Total cost for cheesecakes = 6 × $55 = $330.

4. To find the total cost, we sum up all these amounts:
   - Total cost = Cost for donuts + Cost for cupcakes + Cost for cheesecakes
                = $204 + $160 + $330 = $794.

Therefore, the total cost of all the items Toula bought is $794.

The answer is: 794
