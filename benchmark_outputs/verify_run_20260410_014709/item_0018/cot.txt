EXPLANATION
To determine Jill's annual salary, we need to calculate her earnings from both her teaching job and her coaching job separately, then sum them up.

First, let's calculate her weekly earnings from teaching.
- She earns $20 per hour.
- She teaches for 35 hours per week.
- Therefore, her weekly earnings from teaching = $20/hour * 35 hours/week = $700/week.

Next, let's calculate her weekly earnings from coaching.
- She earns $30 per hour.
- She coaches for 15 hours per week.
- Therefore, her weekly earnings from coaching = $30/hour * 15 hours/week = $450/week.

Now, let's find out how much she earns in total per week from both jobs combined.
- Total weekly earnings = Weekly earnings from teaching + Weekly earnings from coaching = $700/week + $450/week = $1150/week.

Finally, to find her annual salary, we multiply her total weekly earnings by the number of weeks she works in a year.
- Number of weeks worked in a year = 50 weeks/year.
- Therefore, her annual salary = Total weekly earnings * Weeks per year = $1150/week * 50 weeks/year = $57,500/year.
