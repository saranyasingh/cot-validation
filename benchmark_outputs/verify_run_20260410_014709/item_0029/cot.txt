EXPLANATION
To determine how many miles Henry traveled between his first and second stops, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Identify the total distance of the trip**: Henry's entire bike trip covers 60 miles.
   
2. **Determine the location of the first stop**: Henry made his first stop at 20 miles into his journey.

3. **Calculate the remaining distance until the end of the trip**:
   - Total distance = 60 miles
   - Distance already covered (first stop) = 20 miles
   - Remaining distance = Total distance - First stop distance
   \[
   60 \text{ miles} - 20 \text{ miles} = 40 \text{ miles}
   \]

4. **Locate the second stop on the route**:
   - Second stop is 15 miles before the end of the trip.
   - Since the end of the trip is at 60 miles, subtracting 15 miles gives us the position of the second stop.
   \[
   60 \text{ miles} - 15 \text{ miles} = 45 \text{ miles}
   \]
   Therefore, the second stop is at 45 miles from the start.

5. **Find the distance between the first and second stops**:
   - First stop = 20 miles
   - Second stop = 45 miles
   - Distance between stops = Second stop - First stop
   \[
   45 \text{ miles} - 20 \text{ miles} = 25 \text{ miles}
   \]

CONCLUSION
Thus, the distance Henry traveled between his first and second stops is 25 miles.

THE ANSWER IS: 25
