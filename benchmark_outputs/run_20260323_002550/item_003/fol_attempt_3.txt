## FACTS
FACT-1: Edward spent $6 on 2 books :: spent(edward, 6, books) ∧ quantity(books, 2)
FACT-2: Both books cost the same amount :: same_price(books)

## RULES
RULE-1: If a total amount is spent on a number of items and the items have the same price, then the price per item is the total amount divided by the number of items :: ∀a ∀t ∀i ∀n ((spent(a, t, i) ∧ quantity(i, n) ∧ same_price(i)) → price_per_item(i, $quotient(t, n)))

## INFERENCES
INF-1: From FACT-1 by Simplification: Edward spent $6 on the books :: spent(edward, 6, books)
INF-2: From FACT-1 by Simplification: There are 2 books :: quantity(books, 2)
INF-3: From INF-1, INF-2, FACT-2, and RULE-1 by Universal Instantiation and Modus Ponens: The price per book is $6 divided by 2 :: price_per_item(books, $quotient(6, 2))
INF-4: From INF-3 by Arithmetic: Each book costs $3 :: price_per_item(books, 3)
