## FACTS
FACT-1: Edward spent $6 on 2 books :: Spent(edward, 6, 2_books)
FACT-2: Both books cost the same amount :: SamePrice(2_books)

## RULES
RULE-1: If a total amount is spent on a number of items and the items have the same price, then the price per item is the total amount divided by the number of items :: ∀a ∀n ∀i ((Spent(a, n, i) ∧ SamePrice(i)) → PricePerItem(i, a / n))

## INFERENCES
INF-1: From FACT-1, FACT-2, RULE-1 by Universal Instantiation and Modus Ponens: The price per book is $6 divided by 2 :: PricePerItem(2_books, 6 / 2)
INF-2: From INF-1 by Arithmetic: Each book costs $3 :: CostPerBook(3)
