Let’s reason step by step.  

**Step 1 – Understanding the total spent on books**  
Edward spent $6 on 2 books.  
So total cost for 2 books = $6.  

**Step 2 – Cost per book**  
Both books cost the same amount.  
If 2 books cost $6, then 1 book costs $6 ÷ 2 = $3.  

**Step 3 – Checking the irrelevant information**  
The problem says: “Now he has $12.”  
This is extra information not needed to find the cost per book, because the cost per book is determined solely by the total spent ($6) and the number of books (2).  

**Step 4 – Logical syllogism**  
1. Edward spent $6 on 2 books of equal price.  
2. Therefore, the total price of the 2 books is $6.  
3. If 2 books cost $6, then 1 book costs $6 ÷ 2.  
4. $6 ÷ 2 = $3.  
5. Therefore, each book costs $3.  

The answer is: 3
