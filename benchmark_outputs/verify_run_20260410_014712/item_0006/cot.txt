EXPLANATION
To determine how much Kylar needs to pay for 16 glasses, we first need to identify the cost structure of the glasses.

1. **Identify the cost of the first glass:** This is given as $5.
2. **Determine the cost of subsequent glasses:** Every second glass costs 60% of the original price.
   - Calculate 60% of $5:
     \[
     0.60 \times 5 = 3
     \]
   - Therefore, each second glass costs $3.
   
Now that we know both the initial cost and the reduced cost for every other glass, let's calculate the total amount Kylar needs to pay for 16 glasses.

**Total Cost Calculation:**
- For the first glass: $5
- For the remaining 14 glasses (since every second glass counts): \(14 \times 3\)

First, calculate the cost of the remaining 14 glasses:
\[
14 \times 3 = 42
\]

Next, add this to the cost of the first glass:
\[
5 + 42 = 47
\]

Thus, Kylar needs to pay a total of $47 for the 16 glasses.

THE ANSWER IS: 47
