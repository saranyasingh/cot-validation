EXPLANATION
1. First, we need to calculate how much of the 200 GB file Carla has downloaded before the restart.
   - 40% of 200 GB = 80 GB (since 200 * 0.40 = 80)
   
2. Next, we determine how long it took Carla to download these 80 GB at her normal speed.
   - 80 GB / 2 GB/minute = 40 minutes
   
3. After the restart, Carla needs to start again from the beginning.
   - This means she needs to download the remaining 120 GB (200 GB - 80 GB) at her normal speed.

4. Calculate the time required to download the remaining 120 GB.
   - 120 GB / 2 GB/minute = 60 minutes
   
5. Add up all the time needed:
   - Time to download 80 GB + Restart time + Time to download 120 GB
   - 40 minutes + 20 minutes + 60 minutes = 120 minutes

Therefore, the total time it takes Carla to download the entire file is 120 minutes.
