To determine how much Janet makes every day at the farmers' market, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total number of eggs laid per day:** 
   - Janet's ducks lay 16 eggs per day.

2. **Subtract the eggs eaten for breakfast:**
   - Janet eats 3 eggs for breakfast every morning.
   - Eggs left after breakfast = Total eggs - Breakfast eggs
     \[
     16 - 3 = 13
     \]

3. **Subtract the eggs used for baking muffins:**
   - Janet uses 4 eggs to bake muffins for her friends every day.
   - Eggs left after baking = Remaining eggs - Muffin eggs
     \[
     13 - 4 = 9
     \]

4. **Determine the revenue generated from selling the remaining eggs:**
   - Janet sells the remaining eggs at $2 per fresh duck egg.
   - Revenue from selling remaining eggs = Remaining eggs * Price per egg
     \[
     9 \times 2 = 18
     \]

Therefore, the amount Janet makes every day at the farmers' market is \( \$18 \).

The answer is: 18
