To determine Josh's profit from flipping the house, we need to follow these steps:

**Premise 1:** Josh bought a house for $80,000.
- This establishes the initial cost of the house.

**Premise 2:** Josh put in $50,000 in repairs.
- This accounts for the total amount spent on improving or fixing the house.

**Premise 3:** The increase in the value of the house was 150%.
- A 150% increase means the house's new value is 160% (or 1 + 1.5) of its original price because 100% represents the original value.

**Step-by-step calculation:**
1. Calculate the new value of the house after improvements:
   \[
   \text{New Value} = \text{Original Price} \times (1 + \text{Percentage Increase})
   \]
   Substituting the values:
   \[
   \text{New Value} = \$80,000 \times (1 + 1.5)
   \]

2. Perform the multiplication:
   \[
   \text{New Value} = \$80,000 \times 2.5
   \]
   \[
   \text{New Value} = \$200,000
   \]

3. Subtract the total investment made ($50,000) from the new value of the house to find the profit:
   \[
   \text{Profit} = \text{New Value} - \text{Total Investment}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Profit} = \$200,000 - \$50,000
   \]
   \[
   \text{Profit} = \$150,000
   \]

Therefore, Josh made a profit of **$150,000** from his house flip.
