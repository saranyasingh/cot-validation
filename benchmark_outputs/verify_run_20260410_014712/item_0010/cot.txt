EXPLANATION

To determine Eliza's earnings for the week, we need to calculate both her regular earnings (for the first 40 hours) and her overtime earnings (for any hours beyond 40).

1. **Regular Earnings Calculation**:
   - Regular hourly rate = $10/hour
   - Number of regular hours worked = 40 hours
   - Regular earnings = Regular hourly rate × Number of regular hours
     \[
     \text{Regular earnings} = \$10 \times 40 = \$400
     \]

2. **Overtime Earnings Calculation**:
   - Overtime hourly rate = Regular hourly rate × 1.2
     \[
     \text{Overtime hourly rate} = \$10 \times 1.2 = \$12/\text{hour}
     \]
   - Hours worked beyond 40 = Total hours worked - Regular hours
     \[
     \text{Hours worked beyond 40} = 45 - 40 = 5 \text{ hours}
     \]
   - Overtime earnings = Overtime hourly rate × Hours worked beyond 40
     \[
     \text{Overtime earnings} = \$12 \times 5 = \$60
     \]

3. **Total Earnings Calculation**:
   - Total earnings = Regular earnings + Overtime earnings
     \[
     \text{Total earnings} = \$400 + \$60 = \$460
     \]

Therefore, Eliza's total earnings for the week are $460.

The answer is: 460
