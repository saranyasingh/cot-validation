To determine how many more apps Dave deleted than he added, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the total number of apps after adding the new ones.
2. Determine how many apps were deleted from that total.
3. Find the difference between the number of apps deleted and the number of apps added.

Step 1: Calculate the total number of apps after adding the new ones.
Dave initially had 15 apps and added 71 new apps:
\[ 15 + 71 = 86 \]

Step 2: Determine how many apps were deleted from that total.
After adding the new apps, Dave had 14 apps left:
\[ 86 - 14 = 72 \]
So, Dave deleted 72 apps in total.

Step 3: Find the difference between the number of apps deleted and the number of apps added.
Dave added 71 apps and deleted 72 apps:
\[ 72 - 71 = 1 \]

Therefore, Dave deleted 1 more app than he added.

The answer is: 1
