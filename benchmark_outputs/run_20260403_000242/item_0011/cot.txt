Let's break down this problem into a logical syllogism:

**Premise 1:** Dave initially had 15 apps on his phone.
- This establishes our starting point and gives us a baseline number to work with.

**Premise 2:** He added 71 new apps.
- This introduces an increase in the total count that we need to account for later.

**Premise 3:** After adding these new apps, Dave had 14 apps left.
- This shows us how much the total increased after the addition process.

Now, let's combine these premises to find out how many apps Dave deleted:
- **Step 1:** We start with 15 apps.
- **Step 2:** Adding 71 new apps brings the total to \(15 + 71 = 86\) apps.
- **Step 3:** After deleting some apps, Dave has 14 apps left.
- **Conclusion:** To determine how many apps were deleted, subtract the remaining number (14) from the initial total (86):
  \[
  86 - 14 = 72
  \]
  
So, Dave deleted 72 apps.
