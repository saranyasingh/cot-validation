Let's break down the problem:

1. Dave initially won some tickets.
2. He spent 12 tickets on toys.
3. After spending, he has 14 tickets left.

To find out how many tickets he won initially, we need to add the number of tickets he spent and the number of tickets he has left.

So, if he had \( x \) tickets initially and then spent 12 tickets, we can write the equation:
\[ x - 12 = 14 \]

Now, let's solve for \( x \):
\[ x = 14 + 12 \]
\[ x = 26 \]

Therefore, the total number of tickets Dave won at the arcade was 26. 

The answer is: 26
