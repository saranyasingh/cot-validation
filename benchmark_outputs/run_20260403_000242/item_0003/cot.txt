1. **Premise 1:** Edward bought two books.
   - This establishes that we have information about the number of items (books) and their total expenditure ($6).

2. **Premise 2:** Each book costs the same amount of money.
   - This indicates that all books share the same price point per unit.

3. **Premise 3:** After spending $6 on two books, Edward still has $12 left.
   - This provides us with his current financial status after making the purchase.

4. **Conclusion:** Therefore, each book must have cost $3.
