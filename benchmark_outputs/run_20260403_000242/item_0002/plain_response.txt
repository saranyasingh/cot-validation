To find out how much Marco's strawberries weighed, we can set up an equation based on the information given:

Let \( M \) represent the weight of Marco's strawberries in pounds.

According to the problem:
- Marco's dad's strawberries weighed 11 pounds.
- Together, their strawberries weighed 30 pounds.

We know that Marco's strawberries plus his dad's strawberries equals the total weight:
\[ M + 11 = 30 \]

To solve for \( M \), we subtract 11 from both sides of the equation:
\[ M = 30 - 11 \]
\[ M = 19 \]

Therefore, Marco's strawberries weighed 19 pounds.

The answer is: 19
