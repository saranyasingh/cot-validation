To determine how much Jessie weighed before starting to jog, we need to consider her current weight and the amount of weight she has lost through jogging.

1. Identify Jessie's current weight:
   \[
   \text{Current weight} = 66 \text{ kg}
   \]

2. Determine the total weight loss after starting to jog every day:
   \[
   \text{Total weight loss} = 126 \text{ kg}
   \]

3. Calculate Jessie's original weight by adding the total weight loss to her current weight:
   \[
   \text{Original weight} = \text{Current weight} + \text{Total weight loss}
   \]
   Substituting the values:
   \[
   \text{Original weight} = 66 \text{ kg} + 126 \text{ kg} = 192 \text{ kg}
   \]

Therefore, Jessie originally weighed 192 kilograms before starting to jog daily.
