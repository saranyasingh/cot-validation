1. **Premise**: Bryan has a total of 34 books.
   - This establishes the total number of books available to be distributed among the bookshelves.

2. **Premise**: These books are distributed equally in two bookshelves.
   - This indicates that the distribution method divides the total number of books evenly between the two shelves.

3. **Conclusion**: To find out how many books are on each shelf, we need to divide the total number of books (34) by the number of bookshelves (2).
   - Mathematically, this division would be \( \frac{34}{2} = 17 \).

Therefore, based on these premises, the number of books on each bookshelf is 17.

The answer is: 17.
