EXPLANATION

To determine how much Janet makes every day at the farmers' market, we need to calculate the total number of eggs laid per day, subtract the number eaten or used up, and then multiply the remaining quantity by the price per egg.

1. **Total Eggs Laid Per Day**: Janet's ducks lay 16 eggs per day.
2. **Eggs Eaten Every Morning**: Janet eats 3 eggs for breakfast every morning.
   - Remaining eggs after breakfast = Total eggs - Eggs eaten
   - Remaining eggs = 16 - 3 = 13 eggs
3. **Eggs Used for Muffins**: Janet uses 4 eggs to bake muffins for her friends every day.
   - Remaining eggs after baking muffins = Remaining eggs - Eggs used for muffins
   - Remaining eggs = 13 - 4 = 9 eggs
4. **Earnings from Selling Eggs**: Janet sells the remainder of the eggs at $2 per egg.
   - Daily earnings = Remaining eggs * Price per egg
   - Daily earnings = 9 * 2 = $18

Therefore, Janet makes $18 every day at the farmers' market.
