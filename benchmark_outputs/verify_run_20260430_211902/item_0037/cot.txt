To determine how much Terry spends on yogurt over 30 days, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total number of yogurts Terry consumes in 30 days**:
   - Terry eats 2 yogurts per day.
   - Over 30 days, the total number of yogurts consumed is \( 2 \text{ yogurts/day} \times 30 \text{ days} = 60 \text{ yogurts} \).

2. **Determine the cost per unit (yogurt) based on the sale price**:
   - The sale offers 4 yogurts for $5.00.
   - Therefore, the cost per yogurt is \( \frac{\$5.00}{4 \text{ yogurts}} = \$1.25 \text{/yogurt} \).

3. **Calculate the total cost for 60 yogurts**:
   - Using the cost per yogurt calculated above, the total cost for 60 yogurts is \( 60 \text{ yogurts} \times \$1.25/\text{yogurt} = \$75.00 \).

Thus, Terry spends a total of \$75.00 on yogurt over 30 days.

The answer is: 75
