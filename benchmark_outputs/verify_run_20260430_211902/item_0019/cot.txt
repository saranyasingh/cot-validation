Explanation:

1. Claire makes a 3-egg omelet every morning.
2. We need to determine how many dozen eggs she eats in 4 weeks.

Let's break this down further:

Step 1: Calculate the total number of eggs consumed per day.
- Since there are 7 days in a week,
- Claire makes a 3-egg omelet every morning,
- Therefore, she consumes \( 3 \times 7 = 21 \) eggs per week.

Step 2: Calculate the total number of eggs consumed over 4 weeks.
- She consumes 21 eggs per week,
- Over 4 weeks, she would consume \( 21 \times 4 = 84 \) eggs.

Step 3: Convert the total number of eggs into dozens.
- There are 12 eggs in a dozen,
- To find out how many dozens she consumes, we divide the total number of eggs by 12,
- Thus, she consumes \( \frac{84}{12} = 7 \) dozens of eggs.

Therefore, the answer is: 7.
