To determine Eliza's earnings for the week when she worked 45 hours, we need to break down the calculation into parts based on her regular work hours and overtime hours.

**Premise 1:** Eliza's rate per hour for the first 40 hours is $10.
- This means that if Eliza works up to 40 hours, she earns $10 per hour.

**Premise 2:** Eliza receives an overtime pay of 1.2 times her regular hourly rate.
- Overtime pay = Regular hourly rate × 1.2

**Step 1:** Calculate the total earnings for the first 40 hours.
\[ \text{Earnings for 40 hours} = \text{Regular hourly rate} \times 40 \]
\[ \text{Earnings for 40 hours} = \$10 \times 40 \]
\[ \text{Earnings for 40 hours} = \$400 \]

**Step 2:** Calculate the overtime pay for the additional 5 hours (since 45 - 40 = 5).
\[ \text{Overtime pay} = \$10 \times 1.2 \]
\[ \text{Overtime pay} = \$12 \]

**Step 3:** Add the earnings from the first 40 hours to the overtime pay to get the total earnings for the week.
\[ \text{Total earnings} = \text{Earnings for 40 hours} + \text{Overtime pay} \]
\[ \text{Total earnings} = \$400 + \$12 \]
\[ \text{Total earnings} = \$412 \]

**Conclusion:** Eliza's earnings for this week are $412.

Therefore, the answer is: 412.
