EXPLANATION
Let P represent the original price of the book.

We know that Kyle paid $19.50 after a 25% discount, which means he paid 75% of the original price (since 100% - 25% = 75%).

So we have:
\[ \text{Discounted Price} = \text{Original Price} \times \left(1 - \frac{\text{Discount Percentage}}{100}\right) \]

Substituting the known values:
\[ 19.50 = P \times \left(1 - \frac{25}{100}\right) \]
\[ 19.50 = P \times 0.75 \]

To find the Original Price (P), divide both sides by 0.75:
\[ P = \frac{19.50}{0.75} \]
\[ P = 26 \]

Therefore, the original price of the book was $26.00.

The answer is: 26
