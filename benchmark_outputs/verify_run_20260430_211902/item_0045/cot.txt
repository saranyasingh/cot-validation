EXPLANATION
Let's break down Charlie's situation into parts and use logical steps to determine his net profit.

1. **Cost per Pound of Beeswax and Wicks**:
   - Cost = $10.00 (for 1 pound of beeswax + wicks)

2. **Profit per Candle**:
   - Selling price per candle = $2.00
   - Profit per candle = Selling price per candle - Cost per pound of beeswax and wicks
     \[
     \text{Profit per candle} = \$2.00 - \$10.00 = -\$8.00
     \]
   This means that for each candle sold, Charlie incurs a loss of $8.00 on average due to the high cost of raw materials.

3. **Number of Candles Sold**:
   - Number of candles sold = 20

4. **Total Loss from Sales**:
   - Total loss = Profit per candle * Number of candles sold
     \[
     \text{Total loss} = (-\$8.00) \times 20 = -\$160.00
     \]

5. **Net Profit Calculation**:
   - Net profit = Total revenue from sales - Total cost
     \[
     \text{Net profit} = (\$2.00 \times 20) - \$10.00 = \$40.00 - \$10.00 = \$30.00
     \]

Therefore, the net profit if Charlie makes and sells 20 candles is $30.00.

The answer is: 30
