To determine how many pieces of pie were taken by the guests, we need to follow these steps:

**Premise 1:** Grandma Jones baked 5 apple pies.
- This establishes the total number of pies.

**Premise 2:** Each pie was cut into 8 pieces.
- This gives us the size of each piece.

**Premise 3:** There were 5 pies initially.
- This confirms the quantity of pies.

**Premise 4:** After serving, there were 14 pieces left.
- This indicates how many pieces were consumed.

**Premise 5:** We need to find out how many pieces were taken by the guests.
- This is our goal.

Let’s use the premises to reason through this problem:

1. **Total Number of Pieces Initially:**
   - Since there were 5 pies and each pie was cut into 8 pieces,
   \[
   \text{Total pieces} = 5 \times 8 = 40
   \]

2. **Number of Pieces Left:**
   - There were 14 pieces remaining at the end of the evening.

3. **Pieces Taken by Guests:**
   - To find out how many pieces were taken, subtract the number of pieces left from the initial total.
   \[
   \text{Pieces taken} = \text{Total pieces} - \text{Pieces left}
   \]
   Substituting the values:
   \[
   \text{Pieces taken} = 40 - 14 = 26
   \]

Therefore, the number of pieces of pie that were taken by the guests is 26.

**The answer is: 26**
