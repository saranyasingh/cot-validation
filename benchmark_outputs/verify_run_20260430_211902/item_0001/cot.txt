EXPLANATION
To determine how much Janet makes every day at the farmers' market, we need to calculate the total number of eggs laid by her ducks per day and then find out how many eggs remain after accounting for what she eats and uses to bake muffins.

1. **Total eggs laid per day**: Janet's ducks lay 16 eggs per day.
2. **Eggs eaten for breakfast**: Janet eats 3 eggs for breakfast every morning.
   - Remaining eggs after breakfast = Total eggs - Eggs eaten for breakfast = 16 - 3 = 13 eggs.
3. **Eggs used to bake muffins**: Janet bakes muffins for her friends using 4 eggs every day.
   - Remaining eggs after baking muffins = Remaining eggs after breakfast - Eggs used for muffins = 13 - 4 = 9 eggs.
4. **Earnings from selling eggs**: Janet sells the remaining eggs at $2 per fresh duck egg.
   - Earnings from selling eggs = Remaining eggs × Price per egg = 9 × $2 = $18.

Therefore, Janet makes $18 every day at the farmers' market.

The answer is: 18
