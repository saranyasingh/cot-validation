EXPLANATION
To determine how many hours John spends walking and caring for his dogs per week, we need to break down the problem into smaller steps.

1. **Calculate the daily time spent on each dog:**  
   - Each dog takes 0.5 hours a day.
   - Since there are 10 dogs, the total daily time spent on all dogs is \(10 \times 0.5 = 5\) hours.

2. **Convert the daily time to weekly time:**  
   - There are 7 days in a week.
   - Therefore, the total weekly time spent on all dogs is \(5 \times 7 = 35\) hours.

Thus, John spends 35 hours a week taking care of his dogs.

The answer is: 35
