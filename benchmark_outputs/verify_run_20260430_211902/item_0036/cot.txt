Explanation:
1. Mike played ping pong for 40 minutes.
2. In the first 20 minutes, he scored 4 points.
3. For the next 20 minutes (second half), he scored 25% more points than in the first 20 minutes.

To find out how many points he scored in the second 20 minutes, we need to calculate 25% of the initial 4 points and add it to his initial score.
Step-by-step calculation:
- Calculate 25% of 4 points: \( 4 \times 0.25 = 1 \)
- Add this value to the initial score: \( 4 + 1 = 5 \)

Therefore, in the second 20 minutes, Mike scored 5 additional points.

Now, let's sum up all the points:
- Points scored in the first 20 minutes: 4 points
- Points scored in the second 20 minutes: 5 points

Total points scored = 4 + 5 = 9 points

The answer is: 9
