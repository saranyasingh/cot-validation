To determine how much shorter a candle will be after burning from 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM, we need to calculate the total time the candle has been burning.

**Premise 1:** A candle melts by 2 centimeters every hour.
- This tells us the rate at which the candle loses height over time.

**Premise 2:** We want to find out how many centimeters shorter the candle will be after burning from 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM.
- To do this, we first need to figure out how long the candle was burning during this period.

**Step-by-step Calculation:**
1. **Determine the duration of burning:**
   - From 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM is a span of 4 hours (since there are 60 minutes in an hour).
   
2. **Calculate the total loss of height:**
   - Since the candle melts 2 cm per hour, over 4 hours it would lose \(4 \times 2 = 8\) centimeters.

Therefore, the candle will be 8 centimeters shorter after burning from 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM.

**Answer:** 8

The answer is: 8
