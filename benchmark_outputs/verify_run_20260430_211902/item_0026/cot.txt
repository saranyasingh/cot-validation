EXPLANATION
To determine how many boxes of pizza Marie ordered, we need to first calculate the total cost of the items she already knows about (the chicken meal, milk, and apples). Then, subtract this amount from her total payment to find out how much was spent on the pizzas.

Step 1: Calculate the total cost of the chicken meal.
- Chicken meal costs $12.
  
Step 2: Calculate the total cost of the milk.
- 5 packs of milk at $3 each = 5 * $3 = $15.

Step 3: Calculate the total cost of the apples.
- 4 apples at $1.50 each = 4 * $1.50 = $6.

Now, add these amounts together to get the subtotal for the known items:
- Subtotal = $12 + $15 + $6 = $33.

Step 4: Subtract the subtotal from the total payment to find the cost of the pizzas.
- Total payment = $50.
- Cost of pizzas = $50 - $33 = $17.

Step 5: Divide the cost of the pizzas by the price per box to find the number of boxes ordered.
- Each box of pizza costs $8.50.
- Number of boxes = $17 / $8.50 ≈ 2.

Therefore, Marie ordered approximately 2 boxes of pizza.
