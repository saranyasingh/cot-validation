EXPLANATION
To determine how much Kylar needs to pay for 16 glasses, we first identify the cost structure described in the problem. Specifically, the price for the first glass is $5, and each subsequent glass costs 60% of the price of the preceding glass.

Let's break this down step-by-step:

1. **Identify the base cost**: The cost of the first glass is $5.
2. **Calculate the cost of the second glass**: Since it costs 60% of the first glass, the cost of the second glass is \(5 \times 0.6 = \$3\).
3. **Generalize the pattern**: Each subsequent glass will have its price calculated as 60% of the previous glass’s price. This means that if the nth glass has a price of P_n, then P_{n+1} = P_n * 0.6.

Now let's calculate the total cost:

4. **Sum up all the costs**:
   - Cost of the first glass: $5
   - Cost of the second glass: $3
   - Cost of the third glass: \(3 \times 0.6 = \$1.80\)
   - Cost of the fourth glass: \(1.80 \times 0.6 = \$1.08\)

We continue this process until we reach the 16th glass. However, instead of manually calculating each individual glass price, we can use an arithmetic progression formula to find the sum easily.

5. **Use the arithmetic series sum formula**:
   For an arithmetic sequence, the sum S_n of the first n terms is given by:
   \[
   S_n = \frac{n}{2} [2a + (n-1)d]
   \]
   Here,
   - \(n = 16\) (number of terms),
   - \(a = 5\) (first term),
   - \(d = -0.6\) (common difference).

Plugging these values into the formula gives us:
\[
S_{16} = \frac{16}{2} [2(5) + (16-1)(-0.6)]
\]

Calculating inside the brackets:
\[
= 8 [10 + 15(-0.6)]
\]
\[
= 8 [10 - 9]
\]
\[
= 8[1] = 8
\]

Therefore, the total amount Kylar needs to pay for 16 glasses is $\boxed{8}.
