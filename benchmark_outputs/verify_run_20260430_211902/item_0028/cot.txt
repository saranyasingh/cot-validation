To determine how much Cynthia will spend on ice cream after 60 days, we need to follow these steps:

**Premise 1:** Cynthia eats one serving of ice cream every night.

**Premise 2:** She buys cartons of ice cream with 15 servings of ice cream per carton.

**Premise 3:** Each carton costs $4.00.

**Premise 4:** We want to find out how much she spends over 60 days.

Let's break it down further:

- **Step 1:** Calculate the total number of servings of ice cream Cynthia consumes in 60 days.
  - Since she eats one serving per day, in 60 days she will eat \(60 \times 1 = 60\) servings.

- **Step 2:** Determine how many cartons of ice cream she needs to buy to get 60 servings.
  - Since each carton has 15 servings, we divide the total servings needed (60) by the servings per carton (15).
  - \( \frac{60}{15} = 4 \) cartons.

- **Step 3:** Calculate the total cost based on the number of cartons needed.
  - Each carton costs $4.00, so for 4 cartons, the total cost would be \(4 \times 4.00 = 16.00\).

Therefore, the total amount Cynthia will spend on ice cream after 60 days is $16.00.

**The answer is: 16**
