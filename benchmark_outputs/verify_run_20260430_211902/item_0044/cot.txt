To determine how many grams of chips we can eat while staying within our daily calorie limit, let's break down the problem into steps using deductive reasoning.

**Premise 1:** A bag of chips contains 250 calories per serving.
- This tells us that each serving provides 250 calories.

**Premise 2:** A 300g bag contains 5 servings.
- This means the total weight of the bag (300g) corresponds to 5 servings.

**Premise 3:** Our daily calorie target is 2000 calories.
- This sets the upper limit for our caloric intake for the day.

**Premise 4:** We have already consumed 1800 calories.
- This indicates that we have exceeded our daily calorie goal by 200 calories (since 2000 - 1800 = 200).

**Step-by-step Reasoning:**

1. **Determine the total calories from the bag:**
   Since there are 5 servings in the bag and each serving is 250 calories,
   \[
   \text{Total calories} = 5 \times 250 = 1250 \text{ calories}
   \]

2. **Calculate remaining calories needed after consumption:**
   Given we've already consumed 1800 calories out of our daily goal of 2000,
   \[
   \text{Remaining calories needed} = 2000 - 1800 = 200 \text{ calories}
   \]

3. **Determine the number of additional servings based on remaining calories:**
   Each serving contains 250 calories,
   \[
   \text{Number of servings} = \frac{\text{Remaining calories}}{\text{Calories per serving}} = \frac{200}{250} = 0.8 \text{ servings}
   \]
   However, since partial servings don't exist, this calculation doesn't apply directly; instead, it implies we can consume approximately half a serving more.

4. **Convert remaining calories to grams:**
   First, convert 200 calories to grams assuming the density of chips similar to water or light snacks:
   \[
   \text{Density of chips} \approx 0.9 \text{ g/cm}^3
   \]
   Volume of 200 calories:
   \[
   \text{Volume} = \frac{200 \text{ calories}}{4186.8 \text{ calories/g}} \approx 0.0479 \text{ cm}^3
   \]
   Mass (in grams):
   \[
   \text{Mass} = \text{Volume} \times \text{Density} = 0.0479 \text{ cm}^3 \times 0.9 \text{ g/cm}^3 \approx 0.0431 \text{ g}
   \]

Therefore, the amount of chips we can eat to stay within our daily calorie limit is approximately **0.0431 grams**.

The answer is: 0.0431
