1. Candice started with 80 post-it notes.
2. She added some more post-it notes after purchasing a package.

To find out how many post-it notes were in the package she purchased, we need to calculate the total number of post-it notes she used plus the ones she had initially minus the ones she has leftover.

Step 1: Calculate the total number of post-it notes used.
- She placed one post-it note on each of 220 cups of coffee.
- Therefore, she used 220 post-it notes for the coffee cups.

Step 2: Add the initial number of post-it notes.
- She started with 80 post-it notes.
- Adding the 220 post-it notes used for the coffee cups gives us:
  80 + 220 = 300 post-it notes so far.

Step 3: Subtract the leftover post-it notes.
- She had 23 post-it notes remaining.
- Therefore, the number of post-it notes in the package is:
  300 - 23 = 277 post-it notes.

Therefore, the answer is: 277.
