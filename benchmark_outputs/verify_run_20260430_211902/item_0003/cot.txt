To determine Josh's profit after buying the house and making repairs, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total cost**: First, we add the purchase price of the house to the amount spent on repairs.
2. **Determine the increase in value**: Since the house increased in value by 150%, this means it now worth three times its original value (because \(100\% + 150\% = 250\%\)).
3. **Calculate the selling price**: To find out how much the house was sold for, we multiply its new value by 3 (since it was worth three times more).
4. **Compute the profit**: Profit is calculated as the difference between the selling price and the initial investment.

Let's go through these calculations step-by-step:

**Step 1: Calculate the total cost**
- Purchase price of the house: $80,000
- Amount spent on repairs: $50,000
- Total cost = Purchase price + Repair costs
\[ \text{Total Cost} = \$80,000 + \$50,000 = \$130,000 \]

**Step 2: Determine the increase in value**
- Increase in value: 150%
- Original value of the house: $80,000
- New value = Original value * (100% + 150%) = $80,000 * 250% = $200,000

**Step 3: Calculate the selling price**
- Selling price = New value * 3
\[ \text{Selling Price} = \$200,000 * 3 = \$600,000 \]

**Step 4: Compute the profit**
- Profit = Selling price - Initial investment
\[ \text{Profit} = \$600,000 - \$130,000 = \$470,000 \]

Therefore, the profit made by Josh is $\boxed{470000}$.
