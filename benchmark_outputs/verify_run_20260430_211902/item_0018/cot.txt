To determine Jill's annual salary, we need to calculate her earnings from both teaching and coaching separately, then sum them up.

**Premise 1:** Jill earns $20 per hour for teaching.
- This establishes the hourly wage for teaching.

**Premise 2:** Jill teaches for 50 weeks a year, working 35 hours a week.
- This provides the total hours worked per year in teaching.

**Premise 3:** Jill earns $30 per hour for being a cheerleader coach.
- This establishes the hourly wage for coaching.

**Premise 4:** Jill coaches for 15 hours a week.
- This provides the total hours worked per year in coaching.

**Step-by-step Calculation:**

First, calculate Jill's yearly earnings from teaching:
\[ \text{Earnings from Teaching} = (\text{Hourly Wage}) \times (\text{Total Weeks Worked}) \]
\[ \text{Earnings from Teaching} = \$20/hour \times 50 \text{ weeks/year} \]

Next, calculate Jill's yearly earnings from coaching:
\[ \text{Earnings from Coaching} = (\text{Hourly Wage}) \times (\text{Total Weeks Worked}) \]
\[ \text{Earnings from Coaching} = \$30/hour \times 15 \text{ weeks/year} \]

Now, add the two amounts together to find Jill's total annual salary:
\[ \text{Annual Salary} = \text{Earnings from Teaching} + \text{Earnings from Coaching} \]
\[ \text{Annual Salary} = (20 \times 50) + (30 \times 15) \]

Let's perform these calculations:

\[ 20 \times 50 = 1000 \]
\[ 30 \times 15 = 450 \]

Finally, add these amounts:
\[ 1000 + 450 = 1450 \]

Therefore, Jill's annual salary is:
The answer is: 1450
