EXPLANATION
Let's break down the problem into logical steps.

Step 1: Understand the given information.
- Darrell and Allen's ages are in the ratio of 7:11.
- Their total current age is 162.

Step 2: Set up an equation to represent the ratio of their ages.
Let D be Darrell's age and A be Allen's age. According to the ratio, we have:
\[ \frac{D}{A} = \frac{7}{11} \]

Step 3: Express D in terms of A using the ratio.
From Step 2, we know that \( D = \frac{7}{11}A \).

Step 4: Use the total age to set up another equation.
We also know that their combined age is 162, so:
\[ D + A = 162 \]

Step 5: Substitute the expression for D from Step 3 into the total age equation.
Substituting \( D = \frac{7}{11}A \) into \( D + A = 162 \), we get:
\[ \frac{7}{11}A + A = 162 \]

Step 6: Solve for A.
To solve this, first combine like terms on the left side:
\[ \frac{7A}{11} + \frac{11A}{11} = 162 \]
\[ \frac{18A}{11} = 162 \]

Now multiply both sides by 11 to clear the fraction:
\[ 18A = 162 \times 11 \]
\[ 18A = 1782 \]

Divide both sides by 18:
\[ A = \frac{1782}{18} \]
\[ A = 99 \]

So, Allen's current age is 99.

Step 7: Calculate Allen's age 10 years from now.
Add 10 years to Allen's current age:
\[ 99 + 10 = 109 \]

Therefore, Allen's age 10 years from now will be 109.

The answer is: 109
