To solve this problem, we need to calculate the total cost of all the items Toula purchased at the bakery.

1. **Calculate the cost of donuts**:  
   - Toula bought 3 dozen donuts.
   - Each dozen donuts costs $68.
   - Therefore, the total cost for donuts = \(3 \times 68 = 204\) dollars.

2. **Calculate the cost of mini cupcakes**:  
   - Toula bought 2 dozen mini cupcakes.
   - Each dozen mini cupcakes costs $80.
   - Therefore, the total cost for mini cupcakes = \(2 \times 80 = 160\) dollars.

3. **Calculate the cost of mini cheesecakes**:  
   - Toula bought 6 dozen mini cheesecakes.
   - Each dozen mini cheesecakes costs $55.
   - Therefore, the total cost for mini cheesecakes = \(6 \times 55 = 330\) dollars.

4. **Sum up the total cost**:  
   - Total cost = Cost of donuts + Cost of mini cupcakes + Cost of mini cheesecakes
   - Total cost = \(204 + 160 + 330\)

Now, let's add these amounts together:

\[204 + 160 = 364\]
\[364 + 330 = 694\]

Therefore, the total cost of all the items Toula bought is **$694**.

The answer is: 694
