EXPLANATION
To determine how long it takes Carlos to start earning money from his lemon tree, we need to consider both the initial investment (planting the tree) and the ongoing expenses (watering and feeding the tree).

1. **Initial Investment:** The tree costs $90 to plant.
2. **Annual Profit Calculation:** For each year, Carlos sells 7 lemons at $1.5 each, so he earns \(7 \times 1.5 = \$10.5\) per year from selling lemons. However, he also spends $3 annually on watering and feeding the tree.

So, the net profit per year after accounting for the annual expense is \(10.5 - 3 = \$7.5\).

3. **Years to Break Even:** To find out how many years it takes for Carlos to earn back the $90 investment, we divide the total cost by the net profit per year:
   \[
   \text{Number of years} = \frac{\$90}{\$7.5} = 12
   \]

Therefore, it will take Carlos 12 years to start earning money on the lemon tree.

The answer is: 12
