To determine the total number of bolts required to make a robe, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Identify the amount of blue fiber needed**: According to the problem, a robe requires 2 bolts of blue fiber.

2. **Calculate the amount of white fiber needed**: The problem states that the amount of white fiber needed is half the amount of blue fiber. Therefore, we calculate:
   \[
   \text{Amount of white fiber} = \frac{\text{Amount of blue fiber}}{2} = \frac{2}{2} = 1 \text{ bolt}
   \]

3. **Sum the total number of bolts**: To find the total number of bolts needed, we add the bolts of blue fiber and the bolts of white fiber together:
   \[
   \text{Total bolts} = \text{Bolts of blue fiber} + \text{Bolts of white fiber} = 2 + 1 = 3 \text{ bolts}
   \]

**Explanation**:
- We started by identifying the specific amounts given (2 bolts of blue fiber).
- Then, using the information provided about the relationship between blue and white fibers (half as much white fiber), we calculated the exact quantity.
- Finally, adding these two quantities gave us the total number of bolts required.

Therefore, the answer is: 3
