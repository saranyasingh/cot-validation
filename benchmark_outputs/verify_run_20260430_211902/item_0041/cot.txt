EXPLANATION

Let's break down the information given to form a logical syllogism.

1. **Premise 1:** "Suzy's iPhone is 1 year old."
   - This establishes the age of Suzy's iPhone.
   
2. **Premise 2:** "Ben's iPhone is two times older than Suzy's iPhone."
   - Since Suzy's iPhone is 1 year old, we calculate Ben's iPhone age by multiplying this by 2.
   - Calculation: \(1 \text{ year} \times 2 = 2 \text{ years}\).

3. **Premise 3:** "Brandon's iPhone is four times as old as Ben's iPhone."
   - We know Ben's iPhone is 2 years old, so we multiply this by 4 to find Brandon's iPhone age.
   - Calculation: \(2 \text{ years} \times 4 = 8 \text{ years}\).

**Conclusion:** Therefore, Brandon's iPhone is 8 years old.

The answer is: 8
