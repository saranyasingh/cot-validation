To determine how much Mishka spent on all the clothing items, we need to calculate the total cost for each type of item and then sum these amounts.

**Premise 1:** Mishka bought 3 pairs of shorts.
- Each pair of shorts costs $16.50.
- Total cost for shorts = \( 3 \times 16.50 = \$49.50 \).

**Premise 2:** Mishka bought 3 pairs of pants.
- Each pair of pants costs $22.50.
- Total cost for pants = \( 3 \times 22.50 = \$67.50 \).

**Premise 3:** Mishka bought 3 pairs of shoes.
- Each pair of shoes costs $42.
- Total cost for shoes = \( 3 \times 42 = \$126 \).

**Conclusion:** To find the total amount Mishka spent, we add up the totals for each type of clothing item.
- Total spending = Cost of shorts + Cost of pants + Cost of shoes
- Total spending = \( 49.50 + 67.50 + 126 \)

Let's perform the addition step-by-step:
1. Add the cost of shorts and pants:
   - \( 49.50 + 67.50 = 117.00 \)
2. Add the result to the cost of shoes:
   - \( 117.00 + 126 = 243.00 \)

Therefore, the total amount Mishka spent on all the clothing items is **$243.00**.

The answer is: 243
