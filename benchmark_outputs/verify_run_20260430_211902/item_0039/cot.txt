Explanation:

1. John runs 60 miles a week.
2. He runs 3 days a week.
3. He runs 3 hours on the first day.
4. On the other two days, he runs half as much time.

Let's break down his running schedule:

- First day: 3 hours
- Second day: \( \frac{3}{2} = 1.5 \) hours
- Third day: \( \frac{3}{2} = 1.5 \) hours

Next, let's calculate his total running time per week:

\[
3 + 1.5 + 1.5 = 6 \text{ hours}
\]

Now, we know that he runs 60 miles in 6 hours. To find out how many miles he runs per hour, we divide the total distance by the total time:

\[
\frac{60 \text{ miles}}{6 \text{ hours}} = 10 \text{ miles/hour}
\]

Therefore, John runs at a speed of 10 miles per hour.

The answer is: 10
