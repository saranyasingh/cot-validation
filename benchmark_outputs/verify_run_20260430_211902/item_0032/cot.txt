EXPLANATION
Let's denote the number of jelly beans in the jar as \( J \).

1. The first friend guesses that there are 80 jelly beans.
   - Premise: Friend 1 guesses 80 jelly beans.

2. The second friend guesses that there are 20 more than half the amount guessed by the first friend.
   - Calculation: Half of 80 = 40. Add 20: 40 + 20 = 60.
   - Premise: Friend 2 guesses 60 jelly beans (half of 80 plus an additional 20).

3. The third friend guesses that there are 25% more than the amount guessed by the first friend.
   - Calculation: 25% of 80 = 20. Add this to 80: 80 + 20 = 100.
   - Premise: Friend 3 guesses 100 jelly beans (80 plus 25%).

To find the average guess, we sum up all the guesses and divide by the number of friends.

Summing up the guesses:
\[ 80 + 60 + 100 = 240 \]

There are three friends, so we divide the total by 3:
\[ \frac{240}{3} = 80 \]

Therefore, the average guess is 80 jelly beans.

The answer is: 80
