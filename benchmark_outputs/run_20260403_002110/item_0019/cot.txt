Let's break down this problem using a logical syllogism:

1. **Premise 1**: There are 270 students in the school.
   - This establishes the total number of students available to be distributed among the grades.

2. **Premise 2**: Each grade has an equal number of students.
   - This tells us that the distribution of students across all grades must be uniform.

3. **Premise 3**: There are 5 students in each grade.
   - This specifies how many students are in each individual grade.

4. **Conclusion**:
   - To find out how many grades there are, we need to divide the total number of students (270) by the number of students per grade (5).
   
So, logically deduced:

- Let \( G \) represent the number of grades.
- We know \( 5G = 270 \).

To solve for \( G \):

\[ G = \frac{270}{5} \]

Performing the division:

\[ G = 54 \]

Therefore, the answer is: There are 54 grades in total.
