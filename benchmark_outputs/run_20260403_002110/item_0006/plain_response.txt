To determine how many more crayons Paul lost than those he gave to his friends, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total number of crayons Paul had initially:**
   \[
   \text{Initial crayons} = 110
   \]

2. **Subtract the number of crayons given away from the initial amount:**
   \[
   \text{Crayons after giving away} = 110 - 90 = 20
   \]

3. **Determine the number of crayons lost:**
   \[
   \text{Crayons lost} = 412
   \]

4. **Find out how many more crayons were lost compared to what was given away:**
   \[
   \text{Difference} = \text{Crayons lost} - \text{Crayons given away}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Difference} = 412 - 90 = 322
   \]

Thus, Paul lost 322 more crayons than he gave away to his friends. The answer is:
\[
\boxed{322}
\]
