Let's break down the problem into a logical syllogism:

**Premise 1:** Paul received a box containing 110 crayons as a gift.
- This is our starting point and represents the total number of crayons Paul had initially.

**Premise 2:** Paul gave away 90 crayons during the school year.
- This indicates an action taken by Paul that led to a reduction in the crayon count.

**Premise 3:** Paul lost 412 crayons.
- This describes another loss that occurred after giving away some crayons.

Now we need to determine how many more crayons Paul lost than those he gave away:

**Step 1:** Calculate the net loss (crayons given away minus crayons lost).
\[ \text{Net Loss} = 90 - 412 \]

**Step 2:** Perform the subtraction:
\[ 90 - 412 = -322 \]
- Since this result is negative, it means Paul actually gained 322 crayons instead of losing them. 

To find out how many more crayons he lost than those he gave away:
\[ \text{More Lost Than Given Away} = | -322 | = 322 \]

Therefore, Paul lost 322 more crayons than he gave away.

The answer is: The answer is: 322
