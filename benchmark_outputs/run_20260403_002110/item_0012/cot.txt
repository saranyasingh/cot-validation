**Logical Syllogism**

1. **Premise**: Adam has 4 more apples than Jackie.
   - Let \( J \) represent the number of apples Jackie has.
   - Therefore, Adam has \( J + 4 \) apples.

2. **Premise**: Together, Adam and Jackie have 14 apples.
   - So, we set up the equation:
     \[
     (J + 4) + J = 14
     \]

3. **Solve for \( J \)**:
   \[
   2J + 4 = 14 \\
   2J = 10 \\
   J = 5
   \]
   Thus, Jackie has 5 apples.

4. **Determine Adam's apples**:
   - Adam has \( J + 4 \):
     \[
     J + 4 = 5 + 4 = 9
     \]
   So, Adam has 9 apples.

5. **Calculate the total apples Adam and Jackie have together**:
   - They both have 14 apples.

6. **Bob has 6 apples more than Adam and Jackie together**:
   - Bob’s apples (\( B \)) can be calculated as:
     \[
     B = 14 + 6 = 20
     \]

7. **Conclusion**:
   - Bob has 20 apples.

The answer is: \( B = 20 \).
