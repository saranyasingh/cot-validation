**Premise:** Together, Adam and Jackie have 14 apples.
So, we set up the equation:
\[
(J + 4) + J = 14
\]

   \[
   2J + 4 = 14

   \[
   J = 5

4. Calculate Adam's apples:

   J + 4 = 5 + 4 = 9

   - Bob has 20 apples.
