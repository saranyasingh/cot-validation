To determine how many weeks the harvest lasted, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Identify the given information:**
   - Lewis earns $2 every week.
   - He earned a total of $178 over the duration of the harvest.

2. **Set up an equation based on the given information:**
   Let \( w \) represent the number of weeks the harvest lasted.
   
   Since Lewis earns $2 per week, his earnings over \( w \) weeks would be:
   \[
   2w = 178
   \]

3. **Solve for \( w \):**
   To find \( w \), divide both sides of the equation by 2:
   \[
   w = \frac{178}{2}
   \]
   Simplifying this gives:
   \[
   w = 89
   \]

4. **Conclusion:**
   The harvest lasted for 89 weeks.

The answer is: 89
