**Logical Syllogism**

1. **Premise 1:** A book has two chapters.
   - This statement establishes that our book consists of exactly two distinct parts or sections.

2. **Premise 2:** The second chapter of the book is 36 pages long.
   - This statement provides specific information about the content of one of the chapters within the book.

3. **Premise 3:** There are a total of 67 pages in the book.
   - This statement gives us the cumulative number of all pages across both chapters.

4. **Conclusion:** We need to find out how many pages are in the first chapter.
   - To solve this, we subtract the length of the second chapter from the total number of pages in the book.

5. **Calculation:**  
   Total pages = 67  
   Pages in the second chapter = 36  
   Pages in the first chapter = Total pages - Pages in the second chapter

   Therefore,
   Pages in the first chapter = 67 - 36 = 31

The answer is: **31** pages are in the first chapter.
