Let's break down the problem using a logical syllogism:

Premise 1: Each basket contains 19 red peaches and 4 green peaches.
Premise 2: There are 15 such baskets.
Premise 3: We need to find out how many peaches are in all the baskets together.

The logical structure:
1. Every basket has 19 + 4 = 23 peaches.
2. Since there are 15 baskets, we multiply the number of peaches per basket by the total number of baskets.

Therefore, the answer is: 
The total number of peaches in the baskets is 15 * 23 = 345 peaches.
