### FACTS
FACT-1: After Jessie started jogging daily, she lost 126 kilograms. :: LosingWeight(126, AfterJogging)
FACT-2: Her current weight is 66 kilograms. :: CurrentWeight(66)

### RULES
RULE-1: If someone loses weight, their current weight is less than their original weight. :: LadderOfWeighing(LosingWeight, LessThan, CurrentWeight)

### INFERENCES
INF-1: From FACT-1, RULE-1 by Transitivity: Jessie's original weight was more than 66 kilograms. :: OriginalWeightGreaterThanCurrentWeight
INF-2: Subtracting the weight lost from the current weight gives her original weight. :: SubtractionToFindOriginalWeight(INF-1, OriginalWeight)

INF-3: Calculate the original weight by subtracting the weight lost from the current weight. :: CalculationForOriginalWeight(INF-1, 66, 126)
INF-4: The result of the calculation is 192 kilograms. :: ResultIs192(kg)
