**Logical Syllogism**

1. **Premise 1**: After Jessie started jogging daily, she lost 126 kilograms.
   - This statement establishes the relationship between her weight loss and her jogging routine.

2. **Premise 2**: Her current weight is 66 kilograms.
   - This provides us with her current state, which helps in calculating how much she weighed before starting to jog.

3. **Conclusion**: To find out how much she weighed before starting to jog, we need to add back the 126 kilograms she lost to her current weight.
   - Therefore, if she currently weighs 66 kilograms after losing 126 kilograms, her original weight was \( 66 + 126 \).

4. **Calculation**:
   - \( 66 + 126 = 192 \) kilograms.

**Final Answer**: The answer is: 192 kilograms.
