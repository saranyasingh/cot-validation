To determine how many packs of DVDs you can buy, we need to divide the total amount of money you have by the cost per DVD pack. 

Here's the calculation:

\[ \text{Number of packs} = \frac{\text{Total money}}{\text{Cost per pack}} \]

Given:
- Total money = $104
- Cost per pack = $26

\[ \text{Number of packs} = \frac{104}{26} \]

Performing the division:

\[ \text{Number of packs} = 4 \]

Therefore, the answer is: 4.
