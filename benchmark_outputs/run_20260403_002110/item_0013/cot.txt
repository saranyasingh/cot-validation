To determine how many DVDs you can buy with $104 when each DVD costs $26, we need to follow these steps:

### Step 1: Understand the total amount available.
- Total money available: $104

### Step 2: Identify the cost per item.
- Cost of one DVD: $26

### Step 3: Determine how many items (DVDs) can be bought with the given amount.
We use division to find out how many DVDs can be purchased:
\[ \text{Number of DVDs} = \frac{\text{Total Money}}{\text{Cost per DVD}} \]
\[ \text{Number of DVDs} = \frac{104}{26} \]

### Step 4: Perform the division.
\[ \frac{104}{26} = 4 \]

Therefore, you can buy **4** packs of DVDs with $104.

The answer is: 4
