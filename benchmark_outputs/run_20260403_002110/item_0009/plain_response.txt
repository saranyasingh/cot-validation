To determine how many tickets Dave won at the arcade, we can set up an equation based on the information given:

Let \( x \) be the number of tickets Dave initially won.

According to the problem:
- Dave spent 12 tickets buying toys.
- After spending these tickets, he has 14 tickets left.

This means that the total number of tickets he initially won minus the 12 tickets he spent equals the 14 tickets he has left. We can express this as:
\[ x - 12 = 14 \]

To find \( x \), we add 12 to both sides of the equation:
\[ x - 12 + 12 = 14 + 12 \]
\[ x = 26 \]

So, Dave won 26 tickets at the arcade.

The answer is: 26
