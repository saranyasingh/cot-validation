To determine how many tickets Dave won at the arcade, we need to follow these steps:

**Premise 1:** At the arcade, Dave initially won some tickets.
- This establishes that there was an initial quantity of tickets available before any were spent or given away.

**Premise 2:** Dave used 12 tickets to buy some toys.
- This indicates that out of the initial number of tickets, 12 were consumed during his purchase.

**Premise 3:** After buying the toys, Dave still has 14 tickets left.
- This implies that after spending 12 tickets on the toys, the remaining number of tickets equals 14.

**Conclusion:** To find the total number of tickets Dave originally won, we add the tickets used (12) and the tickets remaining (14).

Therefore, the calculation is:
\[ \text{Initial Tickets} = \text{Tickets Used} + \text{Tickets Left} \]
\[ \text{Initial Tickets} = 12 + 14 \]
\[ \text{Initial Tickets} = 26 \]

The answer is: Dave won 26 tickets at the arcade.
