To find out how many books are in each bookshelf, we need to divide the total number of books by the number of bookshelves.

Given:
- Total number of books = 34
- Number of bookshelves = 2

Now, let's perform the division:

\[ \text{Number of books per shelf} = \frac{\text{Total number of books}}{\text{Number of shelves}} \]

\[ \text{Number of books per shelf} = \frac{34}{2} \]

\[ \text{Number of books per shelf} = 17 \]

Therefore, there are 17 books in each bookshelf.

The answer is: 17
