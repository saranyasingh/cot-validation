1. **Premise**: Bryan took a look at his books.
   - This statement introduces the subject and action but does not provide any numerical data or distribution details about the books.

2. **Premise**: He has 34 books distributed equally in 2 bookshelves.
   - This statement provides key information that all books are evenly divided among two shelves.

3. **Premise**: To find out how many books are in each bookshelf, we need to divide the total number of books (34) by the number of bookshelves (2).
   - Mathematically, this can be expressed as \( \frac{34}{2} \).

4. **Calculation**:
   - Perform the division: \( \frac{34}{2} = 17 \).
   - Therefore, each bookshelf contains 17 books.

5. **Conclusion**: Each bookshelf contains 17 books.

The answer is: 17
