\[ \text{Total kids} = \text{Kids going to camp} + \text{Kids staying at home} + \text{Outside county attendees} \]

\[ \text{Total kids} = 61,0769 + 59,0796 + 22 \]

Now add these numbers together:

\[ 61,0769 + 59,0796 = 120,1565 \]
\[ 120,1565 + 22 = 120,1787 \]

\[ \text{Lawrence County population} = \text{Total kids} - \text{Outside county attendees} \]

\[ \text{Lawrence County population} = 120,1787 - 22 \]

\[ 120,1787 - 22 = 120,1765 \]
