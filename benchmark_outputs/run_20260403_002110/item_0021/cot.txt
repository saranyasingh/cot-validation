Premise 1: He harvested 54 sacks of oranges.
Premise 2: They harvested 18 sacks of oranges per day.
Premise 3: We need to find out how many days it took to harvest all the oranges.

To determine this, we use the information given in Premises 1 and 2.

Step-by-step logical reasoning:

1. From Premise 1, we know the total number of sacks harvested is 54.
2. From Premise 2, we know the rate at which the oranges were harvested is 18 sacks per day.
3. To calculate the number of days required to harvest all the oranges, we divide the total number of sacks by the daily harvest rate:
   \[
   \text{Number of days} = \frac{\text{Total number of sacks}}{\text{Daily harvest rate}}
   \]
   Substituting the values:
   \[
   \text{Number of days} = \frac{54}{18}
   \]

4. Performing the division:
   \[
   \frac{54}{18} = 3
   \]

Therefore, the answer is: It took 3 days to harvest the oranges.
