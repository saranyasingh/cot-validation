To determine how many more boys than girls the school has, we need to compare the number of boys to the number of girls. Here's the logical reasoning:

**Premise 1:** There are 34 girls in the school.
**Premise 2:** There are 841 boys in the school.

The goal is to find out how many more boys there are compared to girls.

**Step 1:** Calculate the difference between the number of boys and girls.
\[ \text{Difference} = \text{Number of Boys} - \text{Number of Girls} \]

**Step 2:** Substitute the given numbers into the equation.
\[ \text{Difference} = 841 - 34 \]

**Step 3:** Perform the subtraction.
\[ 841 - 34 = 807 \]

Therefore, the school has 807 more boys than girls.

The answer is: 807
