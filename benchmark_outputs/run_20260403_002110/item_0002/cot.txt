To determine how much Marco's strawberries weighed, we need to follow these steps:

**Premise 1:** Marco's dad's strawberries weighed 11 pounds.
- This is given directly in the problem statement.

**Premise 2:** Together their strawberries weighed 30 pounds.
- This information is also provided in the problem statement.

**Step-by-step Logical Reasoning:**

1. **Total weight of both fathers' strawberries:** 
   - Given: Total = 30 pounds

2. **Weight of Marco's dad's strawberries:**
   - Given: Dad's strawberries = 11 pounds

3. **Calculate the remaining weight:**
   - Since the total weight (30 pounds) includes both Marco's and his dad's strawberries,
     \[
     \text{Remaining weight} = \text{Total weight} - \text{Dad's strawberries}
     \]
     Substituting the values:
     \[
     \text{Remaining weight} = 30 \text{ pounds} - 11 \text{ pounds} = 19 \text{ pounds}
     \]

4. **Conclusion:**
   - Therefore, Marco's strawberries must have weighed 19 pounds.

The answer is: The answer is: 19 pounds.
