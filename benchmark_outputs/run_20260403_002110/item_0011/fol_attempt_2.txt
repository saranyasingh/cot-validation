## FACTS
**Fact-1:** Dave initially had 15 apps.
\[
\text{Apps}_{\text{initial}}(Dave) ::= Apps(Dave) = 15
\]

**Fact-2:** Dave added 71 new apps.
\[
\text{AddedApps}(Dave) ::= AddedApps(Dave) = 71
\]

## RULES
**Rule-1:** Adding and then deleting apps maintains the total count.
\[
\forall x (\text{Apps}(Dave) + \text{AddedApps}(Dave) = \text{DeletedApps}(Dave))
\]

**Rule-2:** Deletion alone results in fewer apps than initial plus added.
\[
\forall x (\text{Apps}(Dave) + \text{AddedApps}(Dave) > \text{DeletedApps}(Dave))
\]

## INFERENCES
**Inference-1:** Total number of apps after adding minus added equals current total apps.
\[
\begin{align*}
&\text{TotalAppsAfterAdding} := \text{Apps}(Dave) + \text{AddedApps}(Dave) \\
&\text{CurrentTotalApps} := \text{Apps}(Dave) + \text{DeletedApps}(Dave)
\end{align*}
\]

**Inference-2:** Given total apps after adding equals current total apps.
\[
\begin{align*}
&\text{TotalAppsAfterAdding} = \text{CurrentTotalApps} \\
&\Rightarrow \text{Apps}(Dave) + \text{AddedApps}(Dave) = \text{Apps}(Dave) + \text{DeletedApps}(Dave)
\end{align*}
\]

**Inference-3:** Given current total apps is greater than initial plus added.
\[
\begin{align*}
&\text{CurrentTotalApps} > \text{Apps}(Dave) + \text{AddedApps}(Dave) \\
&\Rightarrow \text{Apps}(Dave) + \text{DeletedApps}(Dave) > \text{Apps}(Dave) + \text{AddedApps}(Dave)
\end{align*}
\]

**Inference-4:** Current total apps less initial plus added equals deleted minus added.
\[
\begin{align*}
&\text{CurrentTotalApps} - \text{Apps}(Dave) = \text{DeletedApps}(Dave) - \text{AddedApps}(Dave)
\end{align*}
\]

**Inference-5:** Difference between deleted and added apps.
\[
\begin{align*}
&(\text{CurrentTotalApps} - \text{Apps}(Dave)) - (\text{Apps}(Dave) + \text{AddedApps}(Dave)) = (\text{DeletedApps}(Dave) - \text{AddedApps}(Dave)) \\
&\Rightarrow \text{DeletedApps}(Dave) - \text{AddedApps}(Dave) = \text{DeletedApps}(Dave) - \text{AddedApps}(Dave)
\end{align*}
\]

**Inference-6:** Since both sides cancel out, we conclude:
\[
\boxed{0}
\]
