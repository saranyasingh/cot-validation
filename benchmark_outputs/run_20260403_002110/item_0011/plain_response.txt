To determine how many more apps Dave deleted than he added, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Initial number of apps**: Dave initially had 15 apps.
2. **Apps added**: He added 71 new apps.
3. **Final number of apps**: After adding and deleting some, he had 14 apps left.

First, calculate the total number of apps after adding:
\[ \text{Total apps after adding} = 15 + 71 = 86 \]

Next, subtract the number of apps he has left from this total to find out how many apps he deleted:
\[ \text{Apps deleted} = 86 - 14 = 72 \]

Now, compare the number of apps deleted to the number of apps added:
\[ \text{Difference} = \text{Apps deleted} - \text{Apps added} = 72 - 71 = 1 \]

Therefore, Dave deleted 1 more app than he added. The answer is: 1
