To determine how many more apps Dave deleted than he added, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total number of apps before deletion:**
   - Initially, Dave had 15 apps.
   - He added 71 new apps.
   - Therefore, the total number of apps after adding is:
     \[
     15 + 71 = 86
     \]

2. **Determine the number of apps left after deletion:**
   - After deleting some apps, Dave had 14 apps left.

3. **Find out how many apps were deleted:**
   - Subtract the number of apps left (14) from the total number of apps after adding (86):
     \[
     86 - 14 = 72
     \]
   So, Dave deleted 72 apps.

4. **Compare the number of apps deleted and added:**
   - Number of apps added: 71
   - Number of apps deleted: 72

5. **Calculate the difference between the number of apps deleted and added:**
   - Subtract the number of apps added from the number of apps deleted:
     \[
     72 - 71 = 1
     \]

Therefore, Dave deleted 1 more app than he added.

The answer is: Dave deleted 1 more app than he added.
