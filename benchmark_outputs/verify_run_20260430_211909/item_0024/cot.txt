EXPLANATION
To determine how many centimeters shorter a candle will be after burning from 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM, we need to calculate the total time the candle has been burning.

First, let's identify the start and end times of the burning period:
- Start time: 1:00 PM
- End time: 5:00 PM

Next, we find the duration of the burning period:
- Duration = End time - Start time
- Duration = 5:00 PM - 1:00 PM
- Duration = 4 hours

Now, we know that the candle melts at a rate of 2 centimeters per hour. To find out how much the candle melts over the course of 4 hours, we multiply the melting rate by the duration:
- Total melting = Melting rate × Duration
- Total melting = 2 cm/hour × 4 hours
- Total melting = 8 cm

Therefore, the candle will be 8 centimeters shorter after burning from 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM.

The answer is: 8
