Explanation:

1. Let's denote the price of the boots as B.
2. We know that Gloria needs to choose between the boots and two pairs of high heels.
3. One pair of heels costs $33, so let's call this pair H1.
4. The second pair of heels costs twice as much as H1, which means it costs 2 * $33 = $66, and we'll call this pair H2.
5. Together, the two pairs of heels cost $33 + $66 = $99.
6. According to the problem, these two pairs of heels together cost five dollars less than the boots, so we have the equation:
   B - ($99) = $5
7. Solving for B gives us:
   B = $99 + $5 = $104.

Therefore, the boots cost $104.

The answer is: 104
