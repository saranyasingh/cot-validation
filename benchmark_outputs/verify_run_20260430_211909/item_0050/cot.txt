EXPLANATION

To determine the total number of unoccupied units in Richard's apartment building, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the total number of units in the building.
2. Determine how many units are occupied based on the given occupancy rate.
3. Subtract the number of occupied units from the total number of units to find the number of unoccupied units.

Step 1: Calculate the total number of units in the building.
- There are 15 floors in the building.
- Each floor has 8 units.
- Therefore, the total number of units is \( 15 \times 8 = 120 \) units.

Step 2: Determine how many units are occupied.
- Three-quarters (3/4) of the building is occupied.
- To find the number of occupied units, multiply the total number of units by 3/4:
\[ 120 \times \frac{3}{4} = 90 \]

Step 3: Subtract the number of occupied units from the total number of units to find the number of unoccupied units.
- Number of unoccupied units = Total number of units - Number of occupied units
\[ 120 - 90 = 30 \]

Therefore, the total number of unoccupied units in the building is 30. 

The answer is: 30
