Explanation:
1. First, we need to determine how many students enrolled in contemporary dance.
   - 20% of 20 students = \( \frac{20}{100} \times 20 = 4 \) students.

2. Next, we calculate the number of students who did not enroll in contemporary dance.
   - Total students - Contemporary dancers = Remaining students
   - 20 students - 4 students = 16 students.

3. Now, we find out how many of these remaining students enrolled in jazz dance.
   - 25% of 16 students = \( \frac{25}{100} \times 16 = 4 \) students.

4. Finally, we calculate the number of students who enrolled in hip-hop dance.
   - Remaining students after contemporary and jazz dancers = Hip-hop dancers
   - 16 students - 4 students = 12 students.

5. To find the percentage of students enrolled in hip-hop dance, we use the formula:
   - (Number of hip-hop dancers / Total number of students) × 100%
   - \( \frac{12}{20} \times 100\% = 60\% \)

Therefore, the answer is: 60.
