To determine how much Terry spends on yogurt over 30 days, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total number of yogurts Terry consumes per day:**  
   Terry eats 2 yogurts per day.

2. **Determine the total number of yogurts consumed over 30 days:**  
   Total yogurts = Number of yogurts per day × Number of days
   Total yogurts = 2 yogurts/day × 30 days = 60 yogurts

3. **Find out how many sets of 4 yogurts Terry buys:**  
   Since the store sells 4 yogurts for $5.00, we divide the total number of yogurts by 4.
   Sets of 4 yogurts = Total yogurts / Yogurt packs per set
   Sets of 4 yogurts = 60 yogurts / 4 yogurts/set = 15 sets

4. **Calculate the total cost for the sets of 4 yogurts:**  
   Cost = Number of sets × Price per set
   Cost = 15 sets × $5.00/set = $75.00

**Explanation:**

- We first calculated the daily consumption of yogurts to establish a baseline.
- Then, we determined the weekly consumption (30 days) to ensure accuracy.
- Next, we found out how many complete sets of 4 yogurts Terry could buy within those 30 days.
- Finally, we calculated the total expenditure based on the price of each set of 4 yogurts.

Therefore, the answer is: 75
