To determine Josh's profit after his house flips, we need to follow these steps:

**Premise 1:** Josh bought a house for $80,000.
- Premise 2:** After repairs, the house's value increased by 150%.
- **Conclusion:** Therefore, the increase in value due to the repairs is calculated as \(80,000 \times 1.5 = 120,000\).

**Step-by-step reasoning:**

1. **Initial Purchase Price:** Josh bought the house for $80,000.
2. **Repairs Cost:** Josh spent an additional $50,000 on repairs.
3. **Increase in Value:** The total cost of the repairs (initial purchase price + repairs) resulted in a 150% increase in the house’s value.

Now, let's calculate the new value of the house after the renovations:
\[ \text{New Value} = \text{Initial Purchase Price} + (\text{Initial Purchase Price} + \text{Repairs}) \]
\[ \text{New Value} = \$80,000 + (\$80,000 + \$50,000) \]
\[ \text{New Value} = \$80,000 + \$130,000 \]
\[ \text{New Value} = \$210,000 \]

4. **Profit Calculation:** Profit is the difference between the selling price (or market value) and the original cost.
   - Selling Price or Market Value: \$210,000
   - Original Cost: \$80,000
   - Profit = Selling Price - Original Cost
   - Profit = \$210,000 - \$80,000
   - Profit = \$130,000

Therefore, the profit made by Josh is $\boxed{130000}$.
