1. Janet's ducks lay 16 eggs per day.
   Premise: This establishes the total number of eggs produced daily.

2. She eats three eggs for breakfast every morning.
   Premise: This reduces the number of eggs available for sale later in the day.

3. She bakes muffins for her friends every day using four eggs.
   Premise: Another reduction in the number of eggs available for sale.

4. She sells the remaining eggs at the farmers' market for $2 per egg.
   Premise: This provides the price at which the remaining eggs are sold.

5. To calculate her earnings, we need to subtract the eggs eaten for breakfast and the eggs used for baking muffins from the total number of eggs laid.
   Calculation: \( \text{Total Eggs} - (\text{Eggs for Breakfast} + \text{Eggs for Muffins}) = \text{Remaining Eggs} \)

6. We then multiply the number of remaining eggs by the price per egg to find the total earnings.
   Calculation: \( \text{Remaining Eggs} \times \$2/\text{egg} = \text{Daily Earnings} \)

7. Substitute the values into the equation:
   \( (16) - (3 + 4) \times \$2 \)
   
8. Simplify inside the parentheses first:
   \( 16 - 7 \times \$2 \)

9. Perform multiplication next:
   \( 16 - 14 \)

10. Finally, perform subtraction:
    \( 16 - 14 = 2 \)
