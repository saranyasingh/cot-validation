To determine how many sheep Toulouse, Charleston, and Seattle have together, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Identify the given information:**
   - Seattle has 20 sheep.
   - Charleston has 4 times as many sheep as Seattle.

2. **Calculate the number of sheep Charleston has:**
   \[
   \text{Number of sheep in Charleston} = 4 \times \text{Number of sheep in Seattle}
   \]
   Substituting the value for Seattle:
   \[
   \text{Number of sheep in Charleston} = 4 \times 20 = 80
   \]

3. **Determine the number of sheep Toulouse has:**
   - Toulouse has twice as many sheep as Charleston.
   \[
   \text{Number of sheep in Toulouse} = 2 \times \text{Number of sheep in Charleston}
   \]
   Substituting the value for Charleston:
   \[
   \text{Number of sheep in Toulouse} = 2 \times 80 = 160
   \]

4. **Sum the total number of sheep:**
   \[
   \text{Total number of sheep} = \text{Number of sheep in Toulouse} + \text{Number of sheep in Charleston} + \text{Number of sheep in Seattle}
   \]
   Substituting the values calculated:
   \[
   \text{Total number of sheep} = 160 + 80 + 20 = 260
   \]

**Conclusion:**

The total number of sheep that Toulouse, Charleston, and Seattle have together is \(260\).

The answer is: 260
