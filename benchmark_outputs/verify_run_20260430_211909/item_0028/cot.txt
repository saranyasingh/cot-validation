EXPLANATION

To determine how much Cynthia will spend on ice cream after 60 days, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Determine the total number of servings consumed over 60 days**:
   - Cynthia eats 1 serving of ice cream every night.
   - There are 30 nights in a month (assuming an average of 30 days per month).
   - Over 60 days, there are \( \frac{60}{30} = 2 \) months.

   Therefore, the total number of servings consumed in 60 days is:
   \[
   1 \text{ serving/night} \times 2 \text{ months} = 2 \times 15 \text{ servings/carton} = 30 \text{ servings}
   \]

2. **Calculate the number of cartons needed**:
   - Each carton contains 15 servings.
   - To find out how many cartons are required, divide the total number of servings by the number of servings per carton:
   \[
   \frac{30 \text{ servings}}{15 \text{ servings/carton}} = 2 \text{ cartons}
   \]

3. **Calculate the total cost**:
   - Each carton costs $4.00.
   - Multiply the number of cartons by the price per carton to get the total cost:
   \[
   2 \text{ cartons} \times \$4.00/\text{carton} = \$8.00
   \]

Therefore, Cynthia will spend a total of $8.00 on ice cream after 60 days. The answer is: 8
