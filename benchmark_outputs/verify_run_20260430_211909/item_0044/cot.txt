To determine how many grams of chips you can consume given your daily calorie target and the amount you've already eaten, we need to follow these steps:

**Premise 1:** A 300g bag of chips contains 5 servings.
- This establishes that there are 5 servings per 300g.

**Premise 2:** Each serving has 250 calories.
- This tells us the calorie content of each individual serving.

**Premise 3:** Your daily calorie target is 2000 calories.
- This sets the upper limit for your total calorie intake for the day.

**Premise 4:** You have already consumed 1800 calories.
- This indicates the current calorie consumption.

Now, let's calculate how many more calories you can consume before reaching your daily target:

1. **Total Calories Allowed Before Target:**
   - Subtract the calories already consumed (1800) from the total allowed calories (2000).
   \[
   2000 - 1800 = 200 \text{ calories}
   \]

2. **Number of Additional Servings You Can Eat:**
   - Divide the remaining calories (200) by the calories per serving (250).
   \[
   \frac{200}{250} = 0.8 \text{ servings}
   \]

3. **Calculate Total Weight of Chips You Can Eat:**
   - Multiply the additional servings (0.8) by the weight of one serving (300g).
   \[
   0.8 \times 300 = 240 \text{ grams}
   \]

Therefore, based on the provided information and calculations, you can eat up to 240 grams of chips while staying within your daily calorie target.

The answer is: 240
