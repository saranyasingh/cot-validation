EXPLANATION
To determine the original price of the book before any discounts were applied, we need to work backwards using the information provided.

First, let's denote the original price as \( P \).

We know that Kyle paid $19.50 after receiving a 25% discount on the original price. A 25% discount means he paid 75% (or 0.75) of the original price because 100% - 25% = 75%.

So, we can set up the equation based on this relationship:
\[ 0.75P = 19.50 \]

Next, we solve for \( P \) by dividing both sides of the equation by 0.75:
\[ P = \frac{19.50}{0.75} \]

Performing the division gives us:
\[ P = 26 \]

Therefore, the original price of the book was $26.

The answer is: 26
