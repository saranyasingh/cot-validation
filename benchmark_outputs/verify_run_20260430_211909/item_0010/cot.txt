EXPLANATION

To determine Eliza's earnings for the week, we need to break down her work into two parts: the regular hours (first 40 hours) and the overtime hours (above 40 hours). We'll calculate her earnings for both parts separately and then sum them up.

- Regular hourly rate = $10
- Number of regular hours = 40
- Earnings for regular hours = Regular hourly rate × Number of regular hours
\[ \text{Earnings for regular hours} = \$10 \times 40 = \$400 \]

\[ \text{Overtime pay rate} = \$10 \times 1.2 = \$12 \]

\[ \text{Number of overtime hours} = 45 - 40 = 5 \]
- Earnings for overtime hours = Overtime pay rate × Number of overtime hours
\[ \text{Earnings for overtime hours} = \$12 \times 5 = \$60 \]

Step 3: Sum the earnings for regular and overtime hours to get the total earnings for the week.
\[ \text{Total earnings} = \text{Earnings for regular hours} + \text{Earnings for overtime hours} \]
\[ \text{Total earnings} = \$400 + \$60 = \$460 \]
