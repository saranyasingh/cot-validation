Explanation:
1. John runs 60 miles a week.
2. He runs 3 days a week.
3. On the first day, he runs 3 hours.
4. On the second and third days, he runs half as much time as the first day.

To find out how fast John runs, we need to calculate his total running distance per hour over all three days.

First, let's determine how many miles he runs on the second and third days combined:
- Since he runs half as much time on the second and third days compared to the first day,
- We multiply the time he runs on the first day (3 hours) by 0.5 to get the time for the second and third days together: \(3 \times 0.5 = 1.5\) hours.

Next, add up the total time spent running over the three days:
- First day: 3 hours
- Second day: 1.5 hours
- Third day: 1.5 hours
- Total time: \(3 + 1.5 + 1.5 = 6\) hours

Now that we have the total time, we can divide the total distance (60 miles) by the total time (6 hours) to find the average speed:
\[ \text{Speed} = \frac{\text{Total Distance}}{\text{Total Time}} = \frac{60 \text{ miles}}{6 \text{ hours}} = 10 \text{ miles per hour} \]

Therefore, the answer is: 10.
