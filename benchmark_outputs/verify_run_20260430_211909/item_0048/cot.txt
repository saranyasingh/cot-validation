EXPLANATION

1. John spends $200 on blue ties at $40 each.
   - Total cost of blue ties = Quantity * Price per tie
   - Let's denote the quantity of blue ties as \( q \) and their price as \( p \).
   - From the given information, we know \( p = \$40 \).

2. Calculate the total cost of blue ties.
   - Total cost of blue ties = \( q \times 40 \)
   - Given total cost = $200
   - Therefore, \( 40q = 200 \)

3. Solve for \( q \), the quantity of blue ties.
   - Divide both sides by 40 to find \( q \):
     \[
     q = \frac{200}{40} = 5
     \]
   - Thus, John bought 5 blue ties.

4. Determine how many red ties John bought.
   - John buys twice as many red ties as blue ties.
   - If he bought 5 blue ties, then he bought \( 2 \times 5 = 10 \) red ties.

5. Find the cost of one red tie.
   - Red ties cost 50% more than blue ties.
   - Cost of one blue tie = $40
   - Cost increase due to the markup (50%) = \( 0.5 \times 40 = 20 \)
   - Cost of one red tie = \( 40 + 20 = 60 \)

6. Calculate the total cost of red ties.
   - Total cost of red ties = Number of red ties × Cost per red tie
   - Total cost of red ties = \( 10 \times 60 \)

7. Sum up the costs of all the ties.
   - Total cost of blue ties = $200
   - Total cost of red ties = \( 10 \times 60 = 600 \)
   - Total amount spent = Cost of blue ties + Cost of red ties
   - Total amount spent = \( 200 + 600 = 800 \)

THE ANSWER IS: 800
