EXPLANATION
1. Let's denote the amount of blue fiber as B and the amount of white fiber as W.
2. According to the problem statement, B = 2 bolts (blue).
3. W = 0.5 * B (half the amount of blue fiber).
4. Substituting the value of B into equation 3 gives us: W = 0.5 * 2 = 1 bolt (white).
5. To find the total number of bolts, we add B and W together: Total = B + W = 2 + 1 = 3 bolts.

Therefore, the answer is: 3.
