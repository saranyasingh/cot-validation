EXPLANATION
To determine how much Mishka spent on all the clothing items, we need to calculate the cost of each type of item separately and then sum them up.

First, let's find the total cost of the shorts.
- Mishka bought 3 pairs of shorts at $16.50 each.
- Total cost for shorts = \( 3 \times \$16.50 = \$49.50 \).

Next, let's calculate the total cost of the pants.
- Mishka bought 3 pairs of pants at $22.50 each.
- Total cost for pants = \( 3 \times \$22.50 = \$67.50 \).

Finally, let's determine the total cost of the shoes.
- Mishka bought 3 pairs of shoes at $42.00 each.
- Total cost for shoes = \( 3 \times \$42.00 = \$126.00 \).

Now, we add up the totals for all the items to find the overall expenditure.
Total cost = Cost of shorts + Cost of pants + Cost of shoes
Total cost = \( \$49.50 + \$67.50 + \$126.00 \)

Adding these amounts together gives us the total amount Mishka spent on all the clothing items.
\( \$49.50 + \$67.50 + \$126.00 = \$243.00 \).

Therefore, Mishka spent a total of $243.00 on all the clothing items.
