To determine how many dozen eggs Claire eats in 4 weeks, we need to break down the problem into smaller steps.

1. **Calculate the total number of eggs Claire eats per week:**
   - Claire makes a 3-egg omelet every morning.
   - There are 7 days in a week.
   - Therefore, the number of eggs she eats per day is \(3 \times 7 = 21\) eggs.
   - Over 4 weeks, the total number of eggs she eats is \(21 \times 4 = 84\) eggs.

2. **Convert the total number of eggs into dozens:**
   - One dozen equals 12 eggs.
   - To find out how many dozens of eggs she eats, divide the total number of eggs by 12.
   - Therefore, the number of dozens of eggs she eats in 4 weeks is \(84 / 12 = 7\).

So, the final answer is: 7.

**Explanation:**

First, we calculated the daily consumption of eggs, then multiplied it by the number of days in a week to get the weekly consumption. Next, we scaled up this weekly amount to the number of weeks (4 weeks) to find the monthly consumption. Finally, we converted the total number of eggs into dozens by dividing by 12, which gives us the final count of dozens of eggs consumed over 4 weeks.
