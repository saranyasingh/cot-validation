EXPLANATION
1. Candice started with 80 post-it notes.
2. She bought another package containing an unknown quantity of post-it notes.
3. After buying this new package, she added these to her original 80 post-it notes.
4. At work, she used 220 post-it notes, placing one on each cup of coffee.
5. We know that after all this usage, she still had 23 post-it notes left.

Let's denote the number of post-it notes in the newly purchased package as \( x \).

From steps 2-5, we have the following equation based on the total number of post-it notes she had initially, plus what she got from the purchase, minus what she used (which includes both the ones she originally had and those used at work), and finally having 23 left:

\[ 80 + x - 220 = 23 \]

Solving for \( x \):

\[ x - 140 = 23 \]
\[ x = 163 \]

Therefore, the number of post-it notes in the package that she purchased was 163.

THE ANSWER IS: 163
