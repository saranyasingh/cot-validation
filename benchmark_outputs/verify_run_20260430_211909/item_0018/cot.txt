EXPLANATION
To calculate Jill's annual salary, we need to consider both her hourly earnings from teaching and her weekly earnings from being a cheerleading coach.

First, let's calculate her weekly earnings from teaching.
- She earns $20 per hour.
- She teaches for 35 hours a week.
So, her weekly earnings from teaching = $20/hour * 35 hours/week = $700/week.

Next, let's calculate her weekly earnings from coaching.
- She earns $30 per hour.
- She coaches for 15 hours a week.
So, her weekly earnings from coaching = $30/hour * 15 hours/week = $450/week.

Now, let's add these two amounts together to get her total weekly earnings.
Total weekly earnings = Earnings from teaching + Earnings from coaching
= $700/week + $450/week = $1150/week.

Since there are 50 weeks in a year, we multiply her weekly earnings by 50 to find her annual earnings.
Annual earnings = Total weekly earnings * Weeks per year
= $1150/week * 50 weeks/year = $57,500/year.

Therefore, Jill's annual salary is $57,500.
