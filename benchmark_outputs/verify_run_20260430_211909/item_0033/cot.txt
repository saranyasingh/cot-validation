EXPLANATION
To determine how many hours John spends walking and caring for his dogs per week, we need to break down the problem into smaller steps.

Step 1: Calculate the daily time spent on each dog.
Each dog requires 0.5 hours per day for walking and taking care of their business. Therefore, John spends \(0.5 \text{ hours/day} \times 10 \text{ dogs}\) on walking each day.

\[
0.5 \text{ hours/dog} \times 10 \text{ dogs} = 5 \text{ hours/day}
\]

Step 2: Calculate the weekly time spent on each dog.
There are 7 days in a week, so we multiply the daily time by 7 to find out how much time is spent on all dogs combined each week.

\[
5 \text{ hours/day} \times 7 \text{ days/week} = 35 \text{ hours/week}
\]

Therefore, John spends 35 hours a week taking care of his dogs.

The answer is: 35
