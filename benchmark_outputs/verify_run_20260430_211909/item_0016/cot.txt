To determine which purchase plan maximizes profit, we need to compare the future values of both purchases after accounting for their respective growth rates.

Firstly, let's calculate the future value of the jewelry purchase if it goes up by 2.5%:

Future Value (FV) = Present Value * (1 + Growth Rate)
FV_Jewelry = $5,000 * (1 + 0.025)
FV_Jewelry ≈ $5,125

Now, let's calculate the future value of the electronic gadgets purchase if they go up by 1.2%:

Future Value (FV) = Present Value * (1 + Growth Rate)
FV_Gadgets = $8,000 * (1 + 0.012)
FV_Gadgets ≈ $8,169

Next, we find out how much more money the merchant will have by choosing the electronics over the jewelry:

Profit Difference = FV_Gadgets - FV_Jewelry
Profit Difference ≈ $8,169 - $5,125
Profit Difference ≈ $3,044

Therefore, by choosing the electronic gadgets purchase plan, the merchant would gain approximately $3,044 more than choosing the jewelry plan.
