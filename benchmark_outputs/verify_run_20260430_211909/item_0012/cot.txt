EXPLANATION
To find out the total cost Toula spent at the bakery, we need to calculate the cost of each type of pastry separately and then sum them up.

1. **Cost of Donuts**: 
   - Toula bought 3 dozen donuts.
   - Each dozen costs $68.
   - Therefore, the cost for 3 dozen donuts = \( 3 \times 68 = \$204 \).

2. **Cost of Mini Cupcakes**:
   - Toula bought 2 dozen mini cupcakes.
   - Each dozen costs $80.
   - Therefore, the cost for 2 dozen mini cupcakes = \( 2 \times 80 = \$160 \).

3. **Cost of Mini Cheesecakes**:
   - Toula bought 6 dozen mini cheesecakes.
   - Each dozen costs $55.
   - Therefore, the cost for 6 dozen mini cheesecakes = \( 6 \times 55 = \$330 \).

Now that we have calculated the individual costs:
- Cost of donuts = $204
- Cost of mini cupcakes = $160
- Cost of mini cheesecakes = $330

Finally, we add these amounts together to get the total cost:
\[ 204 + 160 + 330 = \$794 \]

Therefore, the total cost Toula spent on her purchase is $794.
