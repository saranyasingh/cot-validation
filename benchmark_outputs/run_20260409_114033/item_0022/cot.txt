Premise 1: Raymond was born 6 years before Samantha.
Premise 2: Raymond has a son who was born when he was 23.

From Premise 2, we know that Raymond's son was born when he was 23, which means his son was born in 25 + 23 = 48.

Therefore, Raymond's son was born 17 years ago.
