To determine the total cost of Toula's purchases at the bakery, we need to calculate the cost of each type of pastry separately and then sum them up.

1. **Donuts**:
   - Cost per dozen = $68
   - Number of dozens = 3
   - Total cost for donuts = \( 3 \times 68 = \$204 \)

2. **Mini Cupcakes**:
   - Cost per dozen = $80
   - Number of dozens = 2
   - Total cost for mini cupcakes = \( 2 \times 80 = \$160 \)

3. **Mini Cheesecakes**:
   - Cost per dozen = $55
   - Number of dozens = 6
   - Total cost for mini cheesecakes = \( 6 \times 55 = \$330 \)

Now, let's add up all the costs:

- Total cost for donuts: $204
- Total cost for mini cupcakes: $160
- Total cost for mini cheesecakes: $330

Total cost = \( 204 + 160 + 330 = \$694 \)

Therefore, the total cost for Toula's purchases is $\boxed{694}$. The answer is: 694.
