To solve this problem, we need to determine the total cost of the shoes Gloria wants to buy. We know that one pair of heels costs $33, and another pair costs twice as much, so it would be:

\[ 2 \times 33 = 66 \]

Together, these two pairs of heels cost:

\[ 33 + 66 = 99 \]

According to the problem, these two pairs of heels cost five dollars more than the boots. Let's denote the cost of the boots as \( B \). Therefore, we have:

\[ B + 5 = 99 \]

Now, solving for \( B \):

\[ B = 99 - 5 \]
\[ B = 94 \]

So, the boots cost \( \$94 \).

Therefore, the answer is: The boots cost $94.
