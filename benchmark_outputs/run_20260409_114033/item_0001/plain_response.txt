To determine how much Janet makes every day at the farmers' market, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the total number of eggs laid each day.
2. Subtract the number of eggs eaten for breakfast.
3. Subtract the number of eggs used for baking muffins.
4. Determine the remaining number of eggs available for sale.
5. Calculate the revenue from selling the remaining eggs.

Let's go through each step:

1. Total number of eggs laid each day:
   \[
   16 \text{ eggs/day}
   \]

2. Number of eggs eaten for breakfast (3 eggs):
   \[
   16 - 3 = 13 \text{ eggs left after breakfast}
   \]

3. Number of eggs used for baking muffins (4 eggs):
   \[
   13 - 4 = 9 \text{ eggs left after baking}
   \]

4. Remaining number of eggs for sale:
   \[
   9 \text{ eggs}
   \]

5. Revenue from selling the eggs:
   \[
   9 \text{ eggs} \times \$2/\text{egg} = \$18
   \]

Therefore, Janet makes \$18 every day at the farmers' market.

The answer is: \(18\)
