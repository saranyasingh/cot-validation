To determine Marissa's required walking speed for the remaining portion of the trail, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total time available for the hike:**  
   - Total distance = 12 miles
   - Time taken for the first part = 1 hour + 1 hour = 2 hours
   - Remaining distance = 12 miles - 4 miles - 2 miles = 6 miles
   - Therefore, total time needed = 2 hours + 0.5 hours (for the last 6 miles)

2. **Determine the total time required to reach the desired average speed:**  
   - Desired average speed = 4 miles/hour
   - To find the total time needed at this speed: \( \text{Total time} = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Speed}} = \frac{12 \text{ miles}}{4 \text{ mph}} = 3 \text{ hours} \)

3. **Calculate the additional time needed:**  
   - Total time available = 2 hours + 0.5 hours = 2.5 hours
   - Additional time needed = 3 hours - 2.5 hours = 0.5 hours

4. **Find the required walking speed for the remaining distance:**  
   - Distance remaining = 6 miles
   - Required time = 0.5 hours
   - Speed required = \( \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Time}} = \frac{6 \text{ miles}}{0.5 \text{ hours}} = 12 \text{ mph} \)

- We calculated that Marissa has already spent 2 hours on the first part of the trail.
- For the remaining 6 miles, she needs an additional 0.5 hours to achieve her goal of averaging 4 miles per hour over the entire trip.
- Using the formula for speed (\( \text{Speed} = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Time}} \)), we determined that she needs to walk at 12 miles per hour for the remaining 6 miles to maintain her desired average speed.
