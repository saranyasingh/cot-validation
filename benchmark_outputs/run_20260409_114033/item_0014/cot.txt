EXPLANATION

1. Let's denote the total number of vacuum cleaners Melanie started with as \( x \).

2. According to the problem, Melanie sold a third of her vacuum cleaners at the green house. Therefore, the remaining vacuum cleaners after selling at the green house would be:
   \[
   x - \frac{x}{3} = \frac{2x}{3}
   \]

3. Next, she sold 2 more vacuum cleaners to the red house. Thus, the new count becomes:
   \[
   \frac{2x}{3} - 2
   \]

4. Then, she sold half of the remaining vacuum cleaners at the orange house. After this sale, the number of vacuum cleaners left would be:
   \[
   \left(\frac{2x}{3} - 2\right) - \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{2x}{3} - 2\right)
   \]
   
5. Simplifying the expression inside the parentheses:
   \[
   \left(\frac{2x}{3} - 2\right) - \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{2x}{3} - 2\right) = \left(1 - \frac{1}{2}\right)\left(\frac{2x}{3} - 2\right) = \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{2x}{3} - 2\right)
   \]

6. We know that after these sales, Melanie had 5 vacuum cleaners left. Hence,
   \[
   \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{2x}{3} - 2\right) = 5
   \]

7. Solving for \( x \):
   \[
   \frac{2x}{3} - 2 = 10 \\
   \frac{2x}{3} = 12 \\
   2x = 36 \\
   x = 18
   \]

Therefore, Melanie started with 18 vacuum cleaners.

THE ANSWER IS: 18
