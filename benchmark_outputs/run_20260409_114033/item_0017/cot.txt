To determine the total distance covered by each train over the two days, we need to break down the problem into smaller steps.

### Step 1: Calculate the distance traveled on the first day.
- Each train travels 80 miles westward.
- Therefore, the total distance traveled by each train on the first day is \( 80 \text{ miles} \).

### Step 2: Calculate the distance traveled on the second day.
- On the second day, each train travels 150 miles northward.
- Therefore, the total distance traveled by each train on the second day is \( 150 \text{ miles} \).

### Step 3: Determine the total distance traveled by each train over the two days.
- Total distance = Distance traveled on the first day + Distance traveled on the second day.
- Total distance = \( 80 \text{ miles} + 150 \text{ miles} = 230 \text{ miles} \).

### Conclusion:
Each train covers a total distance of **230** miles over the two days.

Therefore, the answer is: 230
