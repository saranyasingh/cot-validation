To determine how many cups of feed Wendi needs for her chickens in the final meal of the day, we can follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total amount of feed given in the morning:**
   - Wendi gives 15 cups of feed in the morning.
   
2. **Calculate the total amount of feed given in the afternoon:**
   - Wendi gives 25 cups of feed in the afternoon.
   
3. **Sum the amounts of feed given in both meals:**
   \[
   15 \text{ cups} + 25 \text{ cups} = 40 \text{ cups}
   \]

4. **Determine the number of chickens:**
   - There are 20 chickens in Wendi’s flock.

5. **Calculate the amount of feed needed per chicken per meal:**
   - Each chicken receives 3 cups of feed per meal.
   - Therefore, the total amount of feed needed per chicken across all three meals is:
     \[
     3 \text{ cups/meal} \times 3 \text{ meals} = 9 \text{ cups/chicken}
     \]

6. **Calculate the total amount of feed needed for all chickens:**
   - Since there are 20 chickens, the total amount of feed needed is:
     \[
     9 \text{ cups/chicken} \times 20 \text{ chickens} = 180 \text{ cups}
     \]

7. **Subtract the amount of feed already given from the total amount needed:**
   - The total amount of feed already given (morning + afternoon) is 40 cups.
   - Therefore, the remaining amount of feed needed is:
     \[
     180 \text{ cups} - 40 \text{ cups} = 140 \text{ cups}
     \]

Thus, the amount of feed Wendi needs to give her chickens in the final meal of the day is \( \boxed{140} \) cups.
