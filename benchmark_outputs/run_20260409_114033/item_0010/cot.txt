2. **Premise 2:** For any hours worked beyond the initial 40 hours, she receives an overtime pay of 1.2 times her regular hourly rate.
   - This accounts for the additional hours above the base limit, increasing her earning potential based on the extra time worked.

3. **Premise 3:** Eliza worked a total of 45 hours this week.
   - This gives us the total hours worked.

  \[
  \text{Regular Earnings} = 40 \text{ hours} \times \$10/\text{hour} = \$400
  \]

  \[
  \text{Overtime Hours} = 45 \text{ hours} - 40 \text{ hours} = 5 \text{ hours}
  \]

- **Overtime Pay:** Calculate the overtime pay by multiplying the overtime hours by the overtime rate, which is 1.2 times the regular rate ($10).

  \[
  \text{Overtime Pay} = 5 \text{ hours} \times 1.2 \times \$10/\text{hour} = 60 \times \$10/\text{hour} = \$600
  \]

- **Total Earnings:** Add the regular earnings to the overtime pay to get the total weekly earnings.

  \[
  \text{Total Earnings} = \text{Regular Earnings} + \text{Overtime Pay} = \$400 + \$600 = \$1000
