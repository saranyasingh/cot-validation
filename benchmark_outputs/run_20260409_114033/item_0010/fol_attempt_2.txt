## FACTS
- Eliza worked 40 hours at her regular rate of $10 per hour.
- Eliza worked 5 hours at her overtime rate of 1.2 times her regular rate.

## RULES
- If Eliza works more than 40 hours, she earns overtime pay at 1.2 times her regular rate.

## INFERENCES
- Regular Earnings = 40 hours * $10/hour
- Overtime Hours = 45 hours - 40 hours
- Overtime Pay = 5 hours * 1.2 * $10/hour
- Total Earnings = Regular Earnings + Overtime Pay
