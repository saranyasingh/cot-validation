To calculate Eliza's total earnings for the week, we need to consider both her regular work and any overtime hours she worked.

First, let's calculate her earnings from her regular work:
- Regular rate = $10/hour
- Regular hours = 40 hours

Earnings from regular hours = Regular rate * Regular hours
= $10/hour * 40 hours
= $400

Next, let's calculate her earnings from overtime hours:
- Overtime rate = 1.2 times regular rate
- Overtime hours = Total hours - Regular hours
= 45 hours - 40 hours
= 5 hours

Overtime earnings = Overtime rate * Overtime hours
= (1.2 * $10) * 5 hours
= $12 * 5 hours
= $60

Finally, add the earnings from regular and overtime hours together:

Total earnings = Earnings from regular hours + Overtime earnings
= $400 + $60
= $460
