To determine the total number of downloads over the three months, we need to calculate the number of downloads for each month and then sum them up.

**Premise 1:** A new program had 60 downloads in the first month.
- This is our starting point, which gives us the initial count of downloads.

**Premise 2:** The number of downloads in the second month was three times as many as the downloads in the first month.
- If there were 60 downloads in the first month, then in the second month there would be \(3 \times 60 = 180\) downloads.

**Premise 3:** In the third month, the number of downloads was reduced by 30% compared to the second month.
- To find out how many downloads were left after reducing by 30%, we calculate 70% (since 100% - 30% = 70%) of the second-month downloads.
- Therefore, the number of downloads in the third month would be \(180 \times 0.7 = 126\).

Now, let's summarize the calculations:

1. First month: 60 downloads
2. Second month: 180 downloads
3. Third month: 126 downloads

Adding these together to get the total number of downloads over the three months:

\[ 60 + 180 + 126 = 366 \]

Thus, the total number of downloads over the three months is 366.

The answer is: 366
