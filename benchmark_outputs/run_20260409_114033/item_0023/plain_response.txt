To determine how many DVDs Billy sold on Tuesday, we need to calculate the total number of DVDs sold to each group of customers and then sum these amounts.

1. The first 3 customers each bought 1 DVD:
   \(3 \text{ customers} \times 1 \text{ DVD/customer} = 3 \text{ DVDs}\)

2. The next 2 customers each bought 2 DVDs:
   \(2 \text{ customers} \times 2 \text{ DVDs/customer} = 4 \text{ DVDs}\)

3. The last 3 customers didn't buy any DVDs:
   \(0 \text{ DVDs/customer} \times 3 \text{ customers} = 0 \text{ DVDs}\)

Now, we add up all the DVDs sold:
\(3 \text{ DVDs} + 4 \text{ DVDs} + 0 \text{ DVDs} = 7 \text{ DVDs}\)

Therefore, the total number of DVDs Billy sold on Tuesday is 7.

The answer is: 7
