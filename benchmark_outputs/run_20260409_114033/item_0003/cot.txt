EXPLANATION
Let's break down the problem step-by-step using logical reasoning.

1. **Initial Purchase Price**: Josh bought the house for $80,000.
2. **Repairs Cost**: He spent $50,000 on repairs.
3. **Total Initial Investment**: The total amount Josh invested (initial purchase price + repair cost) is $80,000 + $50,000 = $130,000.
4. **Value Increase**: The house's value increased by 150% after repairs were made.
5. **New Value Calculation**: To find out how much the house was worth after the increase, we calculate 150% of the initial purchase price ($80,000).  
   - 150% of $80,000 = \( \frac{150}{100} \times \$80,000 = 1.5 \times \$80,000 = \$120,000 \).
6. **Net Profit Calculation**: The net profit is calculated as the new value minus the total investment.
   - Net Profit = New Value - Total Investment = \$120,000 - \$130,000 = -$10,000.

**Conclusion**: Since the result is negative (-$10,000), this indicates that Josh lost money on the transaction.
