To determine how many miles Henry traveled between his first and second stops, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the total distance from the start of the trip to the second stop.
2. Subtract this distance from the total trip distance to find the distance between the first and second stops.

First, let's identify the distances:
- The total trip distance is 60 miles.
- The second stop is 15 miles before the end of the trip.

So, the distance from the start of the trip to the second stop is:
\[ \text{Distance} = 60 - 15 = 45 \text{ miles} \]

Next, we subtract the distance from the start to the first stop (which is given as 20 miles) from the distance from the start to the second stop (which is 45 miles):
\[ \text{Distance between first and second stops} = 45 - 20 = 25 \text{ miles} \]

Therefore, Henry traveled 25 miles between his first and second stops.
