To calculate Jill's annual salary, we need to determine how much she earns from each job separately and then add them together.

First, let's calculate her earnings from teaching:
- She teaches for 35 hours a week.
- Her hourly rate for teaching is $20.
- Therefore, her weekly earnings from teaching = 35 hours/week * $20/hour = $700/week

Next, let's calculate her earnings from being a cheerleader:
- She coaches for 15 hours a week.
- Her hourly rate for coaching is $30.
- Therefore, her weekly earnings from coaching = 15 hours/week * $30/hour = $450/week

Now, let's find out how many weeks she works in a year:
- There are 50 weeks of teaching + 50 weeks of coaching = 100 weeks/year

Finally, let's calculate her total annual salary:
- Total weekly salary = Earnings from teaching + Earnings from coaching = $700/week + $450/week = $1150/week
- Annual salary = Weekly salary * Weeks worked per year = $1150/week * 100 weeks/year = $115,000/year

Therefore, Jill's annual salary is $115,000.
