EXPLANATION
To determine Jill's annual salary, we need to calculate her earnings from both teaching and coaching separately, then sum them up.

First, let's calculate her weekly earnings from teaching.
- She earns $20 per hour.
- She teaches for 35 hours a week.
Her weekly earning from teaching = \( 20 \times 35 = 700 \) dollars.

Next, let's calculate her weekly earnings from coaching.
- She earns $30 per hour.
- She coaches for 15 hours a week.
Her weekly earning from coaching = \( 30 \times 15 = 450 \) dollars.

Now, let's find out how much she earns in a year from each activity.
- There are 50 weeks in a year.
Her annual earning from teaching = \( 700 \times 50 = 35000 \) dollars.
Her annual earning from coaching = \( 450 \times 50 = 22500 \) dollars.

Finally, we add her earnings from both activities to get her total annual salary.
Total annual salary = Earnings from teaching + Earnings from coaching
= \( 35000 + 22500 = 57500 \) dollars.

Therefore, Jill's annual salary is 57500 dollars. The answer is: 57500.
