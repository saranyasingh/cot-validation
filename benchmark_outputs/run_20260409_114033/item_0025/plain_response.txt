To find the original price of the book before the discount, we can use the information that Kyle paid $19.50 after a 25% discount. Let's denote the original price as \( P \).

A 25% discount means Kyle paid 75% of the original price because 100% - 25% = 75%. So, we can write the equation:

\[ 0.75P = 19.50 \]

Now, to solve for \( P \), we divide both sides of the equation by 0.75:

\[ P = \frac{19.50}{0.75} \]

Calculating this gives us:

\[ P = 26 \]

Therefore, the original price of the book was $26. The answer is: 26.
