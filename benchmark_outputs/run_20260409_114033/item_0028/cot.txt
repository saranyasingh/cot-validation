Explanation:

1. Cynthia eats one serving of ice cream every night.
2. She has been eating ice cream for 60 days.
3. Therefore, she has consumed \(60 \times 1 = 60\) servings of ice cream over these 60 days.

Next, we need to determine how many cartons of ice cream she needs to buy. Each carton contains 15 servings.

Calculation:

\[
\text{Number of cartons needed} = \frac{\text{Total servings}}{\text{Servings per carton}} = \frac{60}{15} = 4
\]

So, Cynthia needs to buy 4 cartons of ice cream.

Now, let's calculate the total cost:

\[
\text{Cost per carton} = \$4.00
\]
\[
\text{Total cost} = \text{Number of cartons} \times \text{Cost per carton} = 4 \times 4 = \$16.00
\]

Therefore, after 60 days, Cynthia will spend $16.00 on ice cream.

The answer is: 16
