To determine how much Cynthia will spend on ice cream after 60 days, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the total number of servings of ice cream Cynthia consumes in 60 days.
2. Determine how many cartons of ice cream she needs to buy based on the total servings.
3. Calculate the total cost for the required number of cartons.

Step 1: Calculate the total number of servings in 60 days.
Since Cynthia eats one serving every night and there are 60 days:
\[ \text{Total servings} = 60 \text{ days} \times 1 \text{ serving/day} = 60 \text{ servings} \]

Step 2: Determine how many cartons of ice cream are needed.
Each carton contains 15 servings:
\[ \text{Number of cartons} = \frac{\text{Total servings}}{\text{Servings per carton}} = \frac{60}{15} = 4 \text{ cartons} \]

Step 3: Calculate the total cost.
Each carton costs $4.00:
\[ \text{Total cost} = \text{Number of cartons} \times \text{Cost per carton} = 4 \text{ cartons} \times \$4.00/\text{carton} = \$16.00 \]

Therefore, the total amount Cynthia will spend on ice cream after 60 days is:
The answer is: \$16.00
