Let's break down the given chain of thought into structured First-Order Logic (FOL):

### Facts
1. **Cynthia eats one serving of ice cream every night.**
   - Fact-1: EatingIceCream(serving, night)

2. **She has been eating ice cream for 60 days.**
   - Fact-2: EatingIceCream(days, 60)

### Rules
1. **If she eats ice cream on a particular day, then she ate ice cream on all previous days.**
   - Rule-1: IfEatingIceCream(day, eaten), ThenEatingIceCream(prevDay, eaten)

### Inferences
1. **From Fact-2, infer that she ate ice cream each night for those 60 days.**
   - Rule-2: EatingIceCream(night, 60)

2. **Calculate the total number of servings consumed over 60 nights.**
   - Rule-3: TotalServings(60)

3. **Determine the number of cartons needed if each carton contains 15 servings.**
   - Rule-4: CartonsNeeded(servings, carton)

4. **Calculate the cost per carton.**
   - Rule-5: CostPerCarton(carton)

5. **Calculate the total cost based on the number of cartons needed.**
   - Rule-6: TotalCost(cartons, cost)

6. **Combine the above rules to get the final result.**
