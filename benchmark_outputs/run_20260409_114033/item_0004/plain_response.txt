To calculate the total number of meters James runs in a week, we need to multiply the distance of one sprint by the number of sprints and then by the frequency of running:

Distance per sprint = 60 meters
Number of sprints per session = 3
Frequency of sessions per week = 3

Total meters per week = Distance per sprint × Number of sprints per session × Frequency of sessions per week

Let's do the math:
Total meters per week = 60 meters/sprint × 3 sprints/session × 3 sessions/week
Total meters per week = 180 meters/session × 3 sessions/week
