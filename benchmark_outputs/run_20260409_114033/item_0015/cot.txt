To determine the percentage of students enrolled in hip-hop dance, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the number of students enrolled in contemporary dance:**
   - Total number of students = 20
   - Percentage enrolled in contemporary dance = 20%
   \[
   \text{Number of students in contemporary dance} = 20 \times \frac{20}{100} = 4
   \]

2. **Calculate the number of remaining students after contemporary dance enrollment:**
   - Remaining students = Total students - Students in contemporary dance
   \[
   \text{Remaining students} = 20 - 4 = 16
   \]

3. **Calculate the number of students enrolled in jazz dance:**
   - Percentage of remaining students enrolled in jazz dance = 25%
   \[
   \text{Number of students in jazz dance} = 16 \times \frac{25}{100} = 4
   \]

4. **Calculate the number of students enrolled in hip-hop dance:**
   - Remaining students after contemporary and jazz dance enrollments = Remaining students - Students in jazz dance
   \[
   \text{Students in hip-hop dance} = 16 - 4 = 12
   \]

5. **Calculate the percentage of total students enrolled in hip-hop dance:**
   - Percentage = (Number of students in hip-hop dance / Total number of students) × 100
   \[
   \text{Percentage of students in hip-hop dance} = \left(\frac{12}{20}\right) \times 100 = 60\%
   \]

**Explanation:** This process involves breaking down the problem into smaller parts—first determining who is enrolled in each type of dance based on given percentages, then subtracting those numbers from the total to find out how many are left over, which represent the group that has not been accounted for yet. Finally, calculating the percentage of the original student population that corresponds to this remaining group.

The answer is: 60
