To find out what percentage of the entire students enrolled in hip-hop dance, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the number of students enrolled in contemporary dance.
2. Determine the number of students left after accounting for those enrolled in contemporary dance.
3. Calculate the number of students enrolled in jazz dance from the remaining students.
4. Find the number of students enrolled in hip-hop dance from the remaining students.
5. Calculate the percentage of the total students that enrolled in hip-hop dance.

Step 1: Number of students enrolled in contemporary dance = 20% of 20 students
   \( \frac{20}{100} \times 20 = 4 \) students

Step 2: Remaining students after contemporary dance enrollment
   Total students - Students in contemporary dance = 20 - 4 = 16 students

Step 3: Number of students enrolled in jazz dance = 25% of the remaining students
   \( \frac{25}{100} \times 16 = 4 \) students

Step 4: Remaining students after jazz dance enrollment (those who enroll in hip-hop)
   Remaining students - Students in jazz dance = 16 - 4 = 12 students

Step 5: Percentage of students enrolled in hip-hop dance
   \( \frac{\text{Number of students in hip-hop}}{\text{Total number of students}} \times 100\% = \frac{12}{20} \times 100\% = 60\% \)

Therefore, the percentage of the entire students enrolled in hip-hop dance is 60%. The answer is: 60.
