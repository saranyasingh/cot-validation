Explanation:
To determine how much shorter a candle will be after burning from 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM, we need to calculate the total time the candle has been burning and then use this information to find out how much it has melted.

Step 1: Determine the duration of the burning period.
- From 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM is a span of 4 hours (5:00 - 1:00 = 4 hours).

Step 2: Calculate the total melting length based on the rate of melting.
- The candle melts at a rate of 2 centimeters per hour.
- Over 4 hours, the total melting length would be \(4 \text{ hours} \times 2 \text{ cm/hour} = 8 \text{ cm}\).

Therefore, the candle will be 8 centimeters shorter after burning from 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM.

The answer is: 8
