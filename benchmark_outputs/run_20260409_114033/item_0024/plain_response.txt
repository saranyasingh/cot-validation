To determine how much shorter the candle will be after burning from 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM, we need to calculate the total time the candle has been burning and then use the given rate of melting.

First, let's find out how long the candle was burning:
- From 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM is a span of 4 hours (since there are 60 minutes in an hour).

Next, we know the candle melts at a rate of 2 centimeters per hour. Therefore, over the course of 4 hours, the candle will have melted:
\[ \text{Total melting} = \text{Rate of melting} \times \text{Time} \]
\[ \text{Total melting} = 2 \, \text{cm/hour} \times 4 \, \text{hours} \]
\[ \text{Total melting} = 8 \, \text{centimeters} \]

Thus, the candle will be 8 centimeters shorter after burning from 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM.

The answer is: 8
