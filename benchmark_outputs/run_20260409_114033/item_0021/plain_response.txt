To determine how much water is in the remaining mixture after spilling one liter of orange drink, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the initial amount of water in each type of drink:**
   - Orange drink: \( \frac{2}{3} \) of the total volume is water.
     - Total volume of orange drink = 10 liters.
     - Volume of water in orange drink = \( \frac{2}{3} \times 10 = \frac{20}{3} \approx 6.67 \) liters.
   - Pineapple drink: \( \frac{3}{5} \) of the total volume is water.
     - Total volume of pineapple drink = 15 liters.
     - Volume of water in pineapple drink = \( \frac{3}{5} \times 15 = 9 \) liters.

2. **Account for the spilled orange drink:**
   - Initially, there was 1 liter of orange drink spilled.
   - After spilling, the volume of orange drink left is \( 10 - 1 = 9 \) liters.

3. **Determine the new volumes of water in both types of drinks:**
   - Remaining orange drink: 9 liters (all water since it was initially pure).
   - New volume of water from orange drink = 1 liter (spilled out).

   - Pineapple drink remains unchanged:
     - Volume of water in pineapple drink = 9 liters.

4. **Sum the volumes of water in both types of drinks:**
   - Total volume of water = Water from orange drink + Water from pineapple drink
     - Total volume of water = \( 1 + 9 = 10 \) liters.

Thus, the total volume of water in the remaining 24 liters is 10 liters. The answer is: 10.
