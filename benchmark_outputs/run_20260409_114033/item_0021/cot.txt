EXPLANATION
To determine how much water remains after spilling one liter of the orange drink, we first need to calculate the amount of water in both drinks before any spills occur.

Step 1: Calculate the initial amounts of water in each drink.
- Orange drink contains \( \frac{2}{3} \) water out of 10 liters. Therefore, the volume of water in the orange drink is:
\[ 10 \times \frac{2}{3} = \frac{20}{3} \text{ liters} \]
- Pineapple drink contains \( \frac{3}{5} \) water out of 15 liters. Therefore, the volume of water in the pineapple drink is:
\[ 15 \times \frac{3}{5} = 9 \text{ liters} \]

Step 2: Determine the total amount of water before spilling.
- Total water before spilling:
\[ \frac{20}{3} + 9 = \frac{20}{3} + \frac{27}{3} = \frac{47}{3} \text{ liters} \]

Step 3: Account for the spilled orange drink.
- One liter of orange drink is spilled, which means 1/10th of the orange drink's water content is lost. Therefore, the water lost from the orange drink is:
\[ \frac{1}{10} \times \frac{20}{3} = \frac{20}{30} = \frac{2}{3} \text{ liters} \]

Step 4: Calculate the remaining water after spilling.
- Remaining water:
\[ \frac{47}{3} - \frac{2}{3} = \frac{45}{3} = 15 \text{ liters} \]

Therefore, the answer is: 15
