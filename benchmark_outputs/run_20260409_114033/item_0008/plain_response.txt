   - Therefore, the actual time needed to complete the second half is \( 100 \text{ GB} / 2 \text{ GB/min} = 50 \text{ minutes} \).
   - Adding this to the 50 minutes already lost due to the restart, the total additional time required is \( 50 + 50 = 100 \text{ minutes} \).

3. **Calculate the total time including the restart.**
