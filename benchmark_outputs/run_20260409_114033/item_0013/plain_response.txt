To determine how many years Carlos needs to start earning money from his lemon tree, we need to calculate the net profit per year after accounting for the initial investment and ongoing expenses.

First, let's find out how much Carlos earns in revenue each year:
- He sells 7 lemons at $1.5 each.
- Total revenue = \( 7 \times \$1.5 = \$10.5 \) per year.

Next, let's consider the annual expenses:
- Watering and feeding the tree costs $3 per year.

Now, subtract the annual expenses from the total revenue to get the net profit per year:
- Net profit per year = Revenue - Expenses
- Net profit per year = \( \$10.5 - \$3 = \$7.5 \) per year.

Carlos needs to cover the initial planting cost of $90. To find out how many years it takes to earn this amount, divide the planting cost by the net profit per year:

\[ \text{Number of years} = \frac{\$90}{\$7.5} \]

Perform the division:
\[ \text{Number of years} = 12 \]

Therefore, it will take Carlos 12 years to start earning money on the lemon tree.

The answer is: 12
