To determine how many bolts of fabric are needed to make a robe, we need to break down the information given into clear steps.

**Premise 1:** A robe requires 2 bolts of blue fiber.
- This establishes the quantity required for the blue color of the robe.

**Premise 2:** Half as much white fiber is needed compared to the blue fiber.
- Since 2 bolts are used for blue fiber, half of this amount would be \( \frac{2}{2} = 1 \) bolt of white fiber.

**Conclusion:** Adding the blue and white fibers together gives us the total number of bolts needed.
- Total bolts = Blue fiber + White fiber = 2 bolts (blue) + 1 bolt (white)

Therefore, the total number of bolts of fabric needed is **3**.

The answer is: 3
