To determine the total number of bolts needed for the robe, we start by understanding the given information:

1. A robe requires 2 bolts of blue fiber.
2. The same amount of white fiber as there is of blue fiber is required.

Since the problem states "half that much" white fiber, we can calculate the amount of white fiber needed:
\[ \text{Amount of white fiber} = \frac{1}{2} \times \text{Amount of blue fiber} \]
\[ \text{Amount of white fiber} = \frac{1}{2} \times 2 = 1 \text{ bolt} \]

Now, to find the total number of bolts needed:
\[ \text{Total bolts} = \text{Bolts of blue fiber} + \text{Bolts of white fiber} \]
\[ \text{Total bolts} = 2 + 1 = 3 \]

Therefore, the answer is: 3.
