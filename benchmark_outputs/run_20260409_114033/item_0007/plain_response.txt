Let's break down the information given:

1. Seattle has 20 sheep.
2. Charleston has 4 times as many sheep as Seattle.
3. Toulouse has twice as many sheep as Charleston.

First, we calculate how many sheep Charleston has:
\[ \text{Charleston} = 4 \times \text{Seattle} = 4 \times 20 = 80 \]

Next, we calculate how many sheep Toulouse has:
\[ \text{Toulouse} = 2 \times \text{Charleston} = 2 \times 80 = 160 \]

Now, to find out how many sheep they all have together, we add them up:
\[ \text{Total} = \text{Seattle} + \text{Charleston} + \text{Toulouse} = 20 + 80 + 160 = 260 \]

Therefore, the total number of sheep that Toulouse, Charleston, and Seattle have together is **260**. The answer is: 260.
