To determine how many dozens of eggs Claire will eat in 4 weeks, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the number of eggs Claire eats each day.
2. Determine the total number of days in 4 weeks.
3. Multiply the daily egg consumption by the total number of days in 4 weeks.
4. Convert the result into dozens of eggs.

Step 1: Claire makes a 3-egg omelet every morning. Therefore, she consumes:
\[ 3 \text{ eggs/day} \]

Step 2: There are 7 days in a week and she does this for 4 weeks. So, the total number of days in 4 weeks is:
\[ 4 \text{ weeks} \times 7 \text{ days/week} = 28 \text{ days} \]

Step 3: Now, multiply the daily egg consumption by the total number of days in 4 weeks:
\[ 3 \text{ eggs/day} \times 28 \text{ days} = 84 \text{ eggs} \]

Step 4: To convert the total number of eggs from individual units (eggs) to dozens (where one dozen equals 12 eggs), divide the total number of eggs by 12:
\[ \frac{84 \text{ eggs}}{12 \text{ eggs/dozen}} = 7 \text{ dozens} \]

Therefore, the total number of dozens of eggs Claire will eat in 4 weeks is:
\[ \boxed{7} \]
