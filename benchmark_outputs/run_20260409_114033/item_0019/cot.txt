EXPLANATION

To determine how many dozen eggs Claire consumes over four weeks, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the total number of days in 4 weeks.
2. Determine how many omelets she makes per day.
3. Find out how many eggs she uses per omelet.
4. Multiply the daily egg usage by the total number of days to get the weekly egg count.
5. Finally, convert the weekly egg count into dozens.

Let's break it down:

**Step 1:** Calculate the total number of days in 4 weeks.
\[ \text{Total days} = 4 \times 7 = 28 \]

**Step 2:** Determine how many omelets she makes per day.
\[ \text{Omelets per day} = 1 \]

**Step 3:** Find out how many eggs she uses per omelet.
\[ \text{Eggs per omelet} = 3 \]

**Step 4:** Multiply the daily egg usage by the total number of days to get the weekly egg count.
\[ \text{Weekly eggs} = 1 \times 3 \times 28 = 84 \]

**Step 5:** Convert the weekly egg count into dozens.
Since there are 12 eggs in a dozen,
\[ \text{Dozens of eggs} = \frac{84}{12} = 7 \]

Therefore, Claire eats 7 dozen eggs in 4 weeks.

The answer is: 7
