EXPLANATION
To determine how much Mishka spent on all the clothing items, we need to calculate the total cost for each type of clothing separately and then sum them up.

- Mishka bought 3 pairs of shorts.

- Mishka bought 3 pairs of pants.
- Each pair of pants costs $22.50.

Finally, let's compute the total cost for shoes:
- Mishka bought 3 pairs of shoes.
- Each pair of shoes costs $42.
- Therefore, the total cost for shoes = 3 × $42 = $126.

Now that we have the individual totals, we add them together to get the overall expenditure:
Total cost = Cost of shorts + Cost of pants + Cost of shoes
Total cost = $49.50 + $67.50 + $126

   - $49.50 + $67.50 = $117.00
2. Now add the cost of shoes:
