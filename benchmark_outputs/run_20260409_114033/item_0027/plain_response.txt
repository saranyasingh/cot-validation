To determine how much Mishka spent in total, we need to calculate the cost for each type of clothing item and then sum them up.

First, let's find the cost for the shorts:
- Mishka bought 3 pairs of shorts.
- Each pair of shorts costs $16.50.
\[
\text{Cost of shorts} = 3 \times 16.50 = 49.50 \text{ dollars}
\]

Next, let's find the cost for the pants:
- Mishka bought 3 pairs of pants.
- Each pair of pants costs $22.50.
\[
\text{Cost of pants} = 3 \times 22.50 = 67.50 \text{ dollars}
\]

Now, let's find the cost for the shoes:
- Mishka bought 3 pairs of shoes.
- Each pair of shoes costs $42.
\[
\text{Cost of shoes} = 3 \times 42 = 126 \text{ dollars}
\]

Finally, we add up the costs of all the items:
\[
\text{Total cost} = \text{Cost of shorts} + \text{Cost of pants} + \text{Cost of shoes}
\]
\[
\text{Total cost} = 49.50 + 67.50 + 126 = 243 \text{ dollars}
\]

Therefore, Mishka spent a total of \(243\) dollars on all the clothing items. The answer is: 243
