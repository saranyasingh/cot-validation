To determine which purchase plan maximizes profit for the merchant, we need to calculate the potential profits from both options considering their respective growth rates.

### Jewelry Purchase Plan:
- Initial value: $5,000
- Growth rate: 2.5%

First, calculate the value after one month:
\[ \text{Value after one month} = \$5,000 \times (1 + 0.025) = \$5,000 \times 1.025 = \$5,125 \]

Next, calculate the value after two months:
\[ \text{Value after two months} = \$5,125 \times (1 + 0.025) = \$5,125 \times 1.025 = \$5,256.25 \]

Profit from the jewelry purchase plan:
\[ \text{Profit} = \$5,256.25 - \$5,000 = \$256.25 \]

### Electronic Gadgets Purchase Plan:
- Initial value: $8,000
- Growth rate: 1.2%

First, calculate the value after one month:
\[ \text{Value after one month} = \$8,000 \times (1 + 0.012) = \$8,000 \times 1.012 = \$8,096 \]

Next, calculate the value after two months:
\[ \text{Value after two months} = \$8,096 \times (1 + 0.012) = \$8,096 \times 1.012 = \$8,178.34 \]

Profit from the electronic gadgets purchase plan:
\[ \text{Profit} = \$8,178.34 - \$8,000 = \$178.34 \]

Comparing the profits from both options:

- Jewelry purchase plan: \$256.25
- Electronic gadgets purchase plan: \$178.34

The jewelry purchase plan yields a higher profit (\$256.25) compared to the electronic gadgets purchase plan (\$178.34).

Therefore, the answer is: The jewelry purchase plan would yield a profit of \$256.25.
