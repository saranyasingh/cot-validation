EXPLANATION

Let's break down the problem into steps.

**Step 1:** Determine the total time the candle burns.
- From 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM is a span of 4 hours (since there are 60 minutes in an hour).

**Step 2:** Calculate how much the candle melts during this period.
- According to the information given, the candle melts at a rate of 2 centimeters per hour.
- Therefore, over 4 hours, the candle will melt \(4 \times 2 = 8\) centimeters.

**Step 3:** Conclude the final length of the candle.
- If the original length of the candle was L cm, after melting for 4 hours, its new length would be \(L - 8\) cm.

However, since we don't have the initial length of the candle, let’s assume it starts at some arbitrary length L_cm.

Given only the melting rate, we cannot determine the exact length before burning but know that the melted portion is always 8 cm regardless of the starting point.

Therefore, the candle becomes 8 cm shorter than its original length.

**Answer:** The candle will be 8 cm shorter after burning from 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM.
