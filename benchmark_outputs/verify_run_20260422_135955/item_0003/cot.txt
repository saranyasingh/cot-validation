EXPLANATION
To determine Josh's profit, we need to calculate both his initial investment and the total amount spent on the house, including the increase in value due to renovations.

1. **Initial Investment**:
   - Josh bought the house for $80,000.
   - He also invested an additional $50,000 in repairs.
   - Therefore, his total initial investment is:
     \[
     80,000 + 50,000 = 130,000
     \]

2. **Increase in Value**:
   - The problem states that the renovations (or flip) increased the value of the house by 150%.
   - To find the increase in value, we calculate 150% of the original purchase price ($80,000):
     \[
     1.50 \times 80,000 = 120,000
     \]
   - Adding this increase to the original purchase price gives us the new market value:
     \[
     80,000 + 120,000 = 200,000
     \]

3. **Total Amount Invested After Flip**:
   - Josh's total investment after the renovation includes not only the initial purchase price but also the additional repair costs.
   - Therefore, the total amount invested after the flip is:
     \[
     130,000 + 50,000 = 180,000
     \]

4. **Profit Calculation**:
   - Profit is calculated as the difference between the total amount invested after the flip and the original purchase price.
   - Thus, Josh's profit is:
     \[
     200,000 - 80,000 = 120,000
     \]

Therefore, the answer is: 120,000.
