To calculate the total number of hours Meredith spent writing articles over the three days, we need to follow these steps:

1. Determine the number of articles written each day.
2. Multiply the number of articles by the time it takes to write them (4 hours per article).
3. Sum up the total hours for all three days.

Let's break this down into logical steps:

### Step 1: Determine the number of articles written each day
- **Monday**: She wrote 5 articles.
- **Tuesday**: She wrote \( \frac{2}{5} \) times more articles than on Monday. Therefore, the number of articles written on Tuesday is:
  \[
  \text{Articles on Tuesday} = 5 + \left( \frac{2}{5} \times 5 \right)
  \]
  Simplifying inside the parentheses first:
  \[
  \text{Articles on Tuesday} = 5 + 2 = 7
  \]

- **Wednesday**: She wrote twice the number of articles she wrote on Tuesday. Therefore, the number of articles written on Wednesday is:
  \[
  \text{Articles on Wednesday} = 2 \times 7 = 14
  \]

### Step 2: Calculate the total hours spent writing each day
- **Monday**: 5 articles * 4 hours/article = 20 hours
- **Tuesday**: 7 articles * 4 hours/article = 28 hours
- **Wednesday**: 14 articles * 4 hours/article = 56 hours

### Step 3: Sum up the total hours for all three days
\[
\text{Total hours} = 20 + 28 + 56 = 104 \text{ hours}
\]

Therefore, the total number of hours Meredith spent writing articles in the three days is:
**The answer is: 104**

This completes our logical reasoning process.
