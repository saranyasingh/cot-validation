To determine how many pieces of pie were taken by the guests, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the total number of pieces of pie initially available.
2. Determine how many pieces were left after the guests ate some.

**Step 1: Calculate the initial number of pieces of pie**

- Grandma Jones baked 5 apple pies.
- Each pie was cut into 8 pieces.
- Therefore, the total number of pieces of pie is \( 5 \times 8 = 40 \).

**Step 2: Determine how many pieces were taken**

- After the guests took and ate their pieces, there were 14 pieces remaining.
- This means that the guests took \( 40 - 14 = 26 \) pieces.

Thus, the number of pieces taken by the guests is **26**.
