To determine the percentage of students enrolled in hip-hop dance, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the number of students enrolled in contemporary dance:**
   - Total students = 20
   - Percentage enrolled in contemporary dance = 20%
   \[
   \text{Number of contemporary dancers} = 20\% \times 20 = 0.20 \times 20 = 4
   \]

2. **Determine the number of students who did not enroll in contemporary dance:**
   \[
   \text{Remaining students} = 20 - 4 = 16
   \]

3. **Calculate the number of students enrolled in jazz dance:**
   - Percentage of remaining students enrolled in jazz dance = 25%
   \[
   \text{Number of jazz dancers} = 25\% \times 16 = 0.25 \times 16 = 4
   \]

4. **Determine the number of students enrolled in hip-hop dance:**
   - Remaining students after accounting for contemporary and jazz dancers
   \[
   \text{Remaining students} = 16 - 4 = 12
   \]
   - Since all remaining students are enrolled in hip-hop dance,
   \[
   \text{Number of hip-hop dancers} = 12
   \]

5. **Calculate the total percentage of students enrolled in hip-hop dance:**
   \[
   \text{Percentage of hip-hop dancers} = \left(\frac{\text{Number of hip-hop dancers}}{\text{Total students}}\right) \times 100\% = \left(\frac{12}{20}\right) \times 100\% = 60\%
   \]

Therefore, the percentage of students enrolled in hip-hop dance is \(60\%\).

**The answer is:** 60
