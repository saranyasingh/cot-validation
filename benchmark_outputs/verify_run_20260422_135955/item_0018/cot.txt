EXPLANATION
To calculate Jill's annual salary, we need to determine her earnings from both teaching and coaching separately, then sum them up.

1. **Calculate weekly earnings from teaching:**
   - Jill earns $20 per hour.
   - She teaches for 35 hours a week.
   
   Weekly earning from teaching = Hourly rate × Hours worked per week
                              = $20 × 35
                              = $700
   
2. **Calculate weekly earnings from coaching:**
   - Jill earns $30 per hour.
   - She coaches for 15 hours a week.
   
   Weekly earning from coaching = Hourly rate × Hours worked per week
                               = $30 × 15
                               = $450
   
3. **Total weekly earnings:**
   - Total weekly earnings = Earnings from teaching + Earnings from coaching
                         = $700 + $450
                         = $1150
   
4. **Annual earnings calculation:**
   - There are 50 weeks in a year.
   - We multiply the total weekly earnings by the number of weeks in a year.
   
   Annual salary = Total weekly earnings × Weeks in a year
                 = $1150 × 50
                 = $57,500
   
Therefore, Jill's annual salary is $57,500.
