To find Charlie's net profit, we need to consider both his costs and revenue.

First, let's calculate the total amount spent on materials (beeswax and wicks) for making 20 candles.
- Each candle requires 1 pound of beeswax.
- Therefore, 20 candles require \( 20 \times 1 = 20 \) pounds of beeswax.
- Additionally, it costs $10.00 for 1 pound of beeswax.
- Thus, the total cost for beeswax is \( 20 \times 10 = \$200.00 \).
- Since Charlie uses 1 pound of wax per candle, he also needs 20 wicks at $10.00 each.
- Hence, the total cost for wicks is \( 20 \times 10 = \$200.00 \).

Next, let's determine the revenue generated from selling 20 candles.
- Each candle is sold for $2.00.
- Therefore, the total revenue is \( 20 \times 2 = \$40.00 \).

Now, we can calculate the net profit by subtracting the total cost from the total revenue.
- Net Profit = Revenue - Cost
- Net Profit = \( 40 - 200 = -\$160.00 \)

Therefore, Charlie's net loss is $160.00 when he makes and sells 20 candles.
