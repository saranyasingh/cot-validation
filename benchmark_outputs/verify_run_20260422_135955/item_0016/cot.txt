- Initial cost of gadgets = $8,000
- Expected increase in price = 1.2%
- Final price of gadgets = $8,000 * (1 + 0.012) = $8,000 * 1.012 = $8,096

Step 3: Calculate the potential profit if the merchant buys the jewelry.
- Profit from jewelry = Final price - Initial cost = $5,125 - $5,000 = $125

Step 4: Calculate the potential profit if the merchant buys the electronic gadgets.
- Profit from gadgets = Final price - Initial cost = $8,096 - $8,000 = $96

Step 5: Compare the profits.
- Jewelry profit: $125
- Gadgets profit: $96
