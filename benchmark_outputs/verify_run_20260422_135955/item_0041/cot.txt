Explanation:

Let's denote the ages of the iPhones as follows:
- \( B \) = age of Ben's iPhone
- \( S \) = age of Suzy's iPhone
- \( R \) = age of Brandon's iPhone

From the problem statement, we have the following relationships:
1. Brandon's iPhone is four times as old as Ben's iPhone.
   This translates to: \( R = 4B \)

2. Ben's iPhone is two times older than Suzy's iPhone.
   This translates to: \( B = 2S \)

We are given that Suzy’s iPhone is 1 year old, so:
\[ S = 1 \]

Now, substituting \( S = 1 \) into the equation \( B = 2S \):
\[ B = 2 \times 1 \]
\[ B = 2 \]

Next, using \( B = 2 \) in the first relationship \( R = 4B \):
\[ R = 4 \times 2 \]
\[ R = 8 \]

Therefore, Brandon’s iPhone is 8 years old.

The answer is: 8
