EXPLANATION
To determine how much Janet makes every day at the farmers' market, we need to calculate the total number of eggs laid per day, subtract the number of eggs eaten for breakfast, and then subtract the number of eggs used for baking muffins. Finally, we'll multiply the remaining number of eggs by their value.

1. **Total Eggs Laid Per Day**: Janet's ducks lay 16 eggs per day.
   Premise: Janet's ducks lay 16 eggs per day.

2. **Eggs Eaten for Breakfast**: Janet eats 3 eggs for breakfast every morning.
   Premise: Janet eats 3 eggs for breakfast every morning.

3. **Remaining Eggs After Breakfast**: Subtract the eggs eaten for breakfast from the total eggs laid.
   Remaining Eggs = Total Eggs - Eggs for Breakfast
   Remaining Eggs = 16 - 3 = 13 eggs
   Reasoning: This calculation shows how many eggs remain after accounting for the ones eaten for breakfast.

4. **Eggs Used for Muffins**: Janet uses 4 eggs for baking muffins every day.
   Premise: Janet uses 4 eggs for baking muffins every day.

5. **Remaining Eggs After Baking**: Subtract the eggs used for baking muffins from the remaining eggs.
   Remaining Eggs = 13 - 4 = 9 eggs
   Reasoning: This calculation shows how many eggs remain after accounting for the ones used for baking muffins.

6. **Value of Remaining Eggs**: Each fresh duck egg is sold for $2.
   Premise: Each fresh duck egg is sold for $2.

7. **Daily Revenue**: Multiply the number of remaining eggs by the price per egg to find out how much Janet makes every day.
   Daily Revenue = Remaining Eggs * Price per Egg
   Daily Revenue = 9 * $2 = $18
   Reasoning: This calculation shows the total amount Janet earns from selling the remaining eggs.
