EXPLANATION
1. Terry consumes 2 yogurts per day.
2. Over 30 days, Terry would consume \(2 \times 30 = 60\) yogurts.
3. Yoghurt is sold in packs of 4 for $5.00.
4. To find out how many packs Terry needs to buy, we divide the total number of yogurts needed (60) by the number of yogurts per pack (4). This gives us \(60 / 4 = 15\) packs.
5. Each pack costs $5.00, so for 15 packs, the cost is \(15 \times 5 = \$75.00\).

Therefore, the answer is: 75
