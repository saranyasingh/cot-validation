EXPLANATION
To determine how much Kylar needs to pay for 16 glasses, we first calculate the cost of one glass and then apply that to the total number of glasses.

Step 1: Calculate the cost of one glass.
The original price of one glass is $5.

Step 2: Determine the discount on each subsequent glass.
Every second glass costs only 60% of the original price. This means it costs \( 5 \times 0.6 = 3 \) dollars.

Step 3: Calculate the cost for 8 glasses at the discounted price.
Since there are 16 glasses in total and they come in pairs (every other glass), there will be 8 glasses at the discounted price and 8 glasses at the original price.

Cost of 8 discounted glasses:
\( 8 \times 3 = 24 \) dollars

Cost of 8 original glasses:
\( 8 \times 5 = 40 \) dollars

Step 4: Sum up the costs.
Total cost = Cost of 8 discounted glasses + Cost of 8 original glasses
\( 24 + 40 = 64 \) dollars

Therefore, Kylar needs to pay $64 for 16 glasses.

The answer is: 64
