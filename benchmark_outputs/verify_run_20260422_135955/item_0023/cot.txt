EXPLANATION
To determine how many DVDs Billy sold on Tuesday, we need to calculate the total number of DVDs sold to all his customers.

1. **First 3 customers**: Each buys 1 DVD.
   - Total DVDs sold = 3 × 1 = 3 DVDs

2. **Next 2 customers**: Each buys 2 DVDs.
   - Total DVDs sold = (2 × 2) + (1 × 2) = 4 + 2 = 6 DVDs

3. **Last 3 customers**: None buy DVDs.
   - Total DVDs sold = 0 DVDs

Now, let's sum up all the DVDs sold:
- First group: 3 DVDs
- Second group: 6 DVDs
- Third group: 0 DVDs

Total DVDs sold = 3 + 6 + 0 = 9 DVDs

Therefore, the answer is: 9
