To determine the total number of unoccupied units in Richard's apartment building, we need to follow these steps:

**Premise 1:** There are 15 floors in the apartment building.
- Premise Explanation: This establishes the base structure of the building.

**Premise 2:** Each floor has 8 units.
- Premise Explanation: This provides the unit count per floor.

**Premise 3:** 3/4 of the building is occupied.
- Premise Explanation: This indicates the proportion of the building that is not empty.

**Step-by-step Logical Reasoning:**

1. Calculate the total number of units in the entire building.
   - Total units = Floors × Units per floor
     \[
     \text{Total units} = 15 \times 8 = 120
     \]

2. Determine how many units are occupied.
   - Occupied units = Total units × (Fraction occupied)
     \[
     \text{Occupied units} = 120 \times \frac{3}{4} = 90
     \]

3. Find out how many units are unoccupied.
   - Unoccupied units = Total units − Occupied units
     \[
     \text{Unoccupied units} = 120 - 90 = 30
     \]

Therefore, the total number of unoccupied units in the building is **30**.

The answer is: 30
