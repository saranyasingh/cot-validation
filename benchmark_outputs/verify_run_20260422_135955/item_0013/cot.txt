EXPLANATION
To determine how many years it takes for Carlos to start earning money on the lemon tree, we need to calculate his total earnings over time after accounting for both his initial investment and ongoing expenses.

1. **Initial Investment:** Carlos spends $90 initially to plant the lemon tree.
2. **Annual Revenue:** For each year, the lemon tree grows 7 lemons at a price of $1.5 each. Therefore, annually, he earns \(7 \times \$1.5 = \$10.5\) from selling the lemons.
3. **Annual Expenses:** He also spends $3 per year to water and feed the tree.
4. **Net Annual Profit/Revenue:** To find out if Carlos starts making money, we subtract the annual expense from the annual revenue. Thus, his net profit per year is \( \$10.5 - \$3 = \$7.5 \).

Now let's set up an equation to determine when Carlos starts making money. Let \( n \) represent the number of years it takes for him to break even.

- Initial investment: $90
- Net profit per year: $7.5

For Carlos to break even (start making money), the total net profit must equal or exceed the initial investment:
\[ 7.5n \geq 90 \]

Solving this inequality for \( n \):
\[ n \geq \frac{90}{7.5} \]
\[ n \geq 12 \]

Therefore, Carlos needs to wait for 12 years before starting to earn money from the lemon tree.

The answer is: 12
