EXPLANATION

To determine how many hours John spends walking and caring for his dogs each week, we need to break down the problem into smaller steps.

First, let's calculate how many hours John spends on each dog per day:
- Each dog takes 0.5 hours a day.
- There are 10 dogs.

So, the total time spent per day on all dogs is \( 10 \times 0.5 = 5 \) hours.

Next, we need to find out how many days there are in a week:
- A week has 7 days.

Now, we calculate the total time spent on all dogs in a week:
- Total time per day = 5 hours
- Number of days in a week = 7 days

Thus, the total time spent per week is \( 5 \times 7 = 35 \) hours.

Therefore, John spends 35 hours a week taking care of his dogs.

The answer is: 35
