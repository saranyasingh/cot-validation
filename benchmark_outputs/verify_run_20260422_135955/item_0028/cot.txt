Explanation:
To determine how much Cynthia will spend on ice cream after 60 days, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total number of servings of ice cream Cynthia consumes over 60 days.**
   - Cynthia eats one serving of ice cream every night.
   - There are 30 nights in a month (assuming an average month has about 30 days).
   - Therefore, in 60 days, she will consume \( 60 \times 30 = 1800 \) servings of ice cream.

2. **Determine the number of cartons needed to meet her consumption.**
   - Each carton contains 15 servings.
   - To find out how many cartons are needed, divide the total number of servings by the number of servings per carton: \( \frac{1800}{15} = 120 \) cartons.

3. **Calculate the total cost based on the number of cartons purchased.**
   - Each carton costs $4.00.
   - Multiply the number of cartons by the cost per carton: \( 120 \times 4 = 480 \).

Therefore, after 60 days, Cynthia will spend $480 on ice cream.

The answer is: 480
