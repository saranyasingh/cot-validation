EXPLANATION
To determine how many boxes of pizza Marie ordered, we need to calculate the total amount spent on chicken meals, milk, and apples first, then subtract these amounts from the total payment to find out how much was spent on pizzas.

1. Calculate the total cost of chicken meals.
   - One chicken meal costs $12.
   - Therefore, the total cost of chicken meals is \(1 \times \$12 = \$12\).

2. Calculate the total cost of milk.
   - Five packs of milk cost $3 each.
   - Therefore, the total cost of milk is \(5 \times \$3 = \$15\).

3. Calculate the total cost of apples.
   - Four apples cost $1.50 each.
   - Therefore, the total cost of apples is \(4 \times \$1.50 = \$6\).

4. Add up all the expenses excluding the pizzas.
   - Total cost (excluding pizzas) = Cost of chicken meals + Cost of milk + Cost of apples
     = \$12 + \$15 + \$6 = \$33.

5. Subtract this total from the overall amount paid to find the cost of pizzas.
   - Total payment = \$50.
   - Cost of pizzas = Total payment - Total cost excluding pizzas
     = \$50 - \$33 = \$17.

6. Determine the number of boxes of pizza by dividing the total cost of pizzas by the price per box.
   - Each box of pizza costs \$8.50.
   - Number of boxes of pizza = Cost of pizzas / Price per box
     = \$17 / \$8.50 ≈ 2.

Therefore, Marie ordered approximately 2 boxes of pizza.

The answer is: 2
