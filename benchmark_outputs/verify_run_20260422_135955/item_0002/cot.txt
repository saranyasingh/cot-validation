EXPLANATION
Let's break down the information given in the question step-by-step.

1. **First Premise:** A robe requires 2 bolts of blue fiber.
   - This establishes the amount of blue fiber needed for one robe.

2. **Second Premise:** Half as much white fiber as blue fiber is required.
   - If we denote the amount of blue fiber as \( B \), then the amount of white fiber would be \( \frac{B}{2} \).
   - Since \( B = 2 \) (from the first premise), the amount of white fiber required is \( \frac{2}{2} = 1 \) bolt.

3. **Third Premise:** To find the total number of bolts needed, we sum the amounts of both types of fiber.
   - Total bolts = Blue fiber + White fiber
   - Total bolts = \( 2 + 1 = 3 \)

4. **Conclusion:** The total number of bolts required to make one robe is 3.

Therefore, the answer is: 3.
