Explanation:

1. Let's denote the total number of vacuum cleaners Melanie started with as \( x \).

2. According to the problem, Melanie sold a third of her vacuum cleaners at the green house.
   - This means she sold \( \frac{x}{3} \) vacuum cleaners at the green house.

3. After selling to the green house, the remaining vacuum cleaners are:
   \[ x - \frac{x}{3} = \frac{2x}{3} \]

4. Next, Melanie sold 2 more vacuum cleaners to the red house.
   - This leaves her with:
   \[ \frac{2x}{3} - 2 \]

5. Then, Melanie sold half of what was left at the orange house.
   - Half of what was left after the red house sale would be:
   \[ \left( \frac{2x}{3} - 2 \right) / 2 = \frac{x}{3} - 1 \]

6. We know that after these transactions, Melanie had 5 vacuum cleaners left.
   - Therefore, we set up the equation:
   \[ \frac{x}{3} - 1 = 5 \]

7. Solving this equation for \( x \):
   \[
   \begin{align*}
   \frac{x}{3} &= 5 + 1 \\
   \frac{x}{3} &= 6 \\
   x &= 6 \times 3 \\
   x &= 18
   \end{align*}
   \]

Therefore, Melanie started with 18 vacuum cleaners. The answer is: 18.
