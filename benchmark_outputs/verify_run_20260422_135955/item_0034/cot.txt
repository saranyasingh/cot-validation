To determine how many gold coins Gretchen has, we need to set up an equation based on the information given.

**Premise 1:** Gretchen has 110 coins in total.
- This tells us that if we add all the coins together, they equal 110.

- Let \( G \) represent the number of gold coins.
- Let \( S \) represent the number of silver coins.

\[ G = S + 30 \]

We also know the total number of coins is 110:
\[ G + S = 110 \]

Now, substitute the expression for \( G \) from Premise 2 into the second equation:
\[ (S + 30) + S = 110 \]

Combine like terms:
\[ 2S + 30 = 110 \]

Subtract 30 from both sides:
\[ 2S = 80 \]

Divide by 2:
\[ S = 40 \]

\[ G = S + 30 \]
\[ G = 40 + 30 \]
\[ G = 70 \]
