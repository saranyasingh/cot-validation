EXPLANATION

1. Mike played ping pong for a total of 40 minutes.
   Premise: Mike played for 40 minutes.

2. In the first 20 minutes, he scored 4 points.
   Premise: He scored 4 points in the first 20 minutes.

3. In the next 20 minutes, he scored 25% more than the first 20 minutes.
   Reasoning: To find out how much more points he scored, we need to calculate 25% of 4 points.
   
   Calculation: \( 4 \times 0.25 = 1 \) point
   Conclusion: He scored an additional 1 point in the second 20 minutes.

4. Therefore, the total points scored in the second 20 minutes would be:
   Points in the second 20 minutes + Additional points = \( 25 - 1 = 24 \) points.

5. Adding the points from both halves of his game:
   Total points = Points in the first half + Points in the second half = \( 4 + 24 = 28 \) points.

6. Finally, since the total time was 40 minutes and it was divided into two equal halves, there were no partial points.

7. Thus, the total points scored by Mike during the entire match is 28.

Therefore, the answer is: 28.
