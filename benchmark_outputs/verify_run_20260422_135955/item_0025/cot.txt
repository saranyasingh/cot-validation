EXPLANATION
Let's denote the original price of the book as \( P \). According to the problem, Kyle paid $19.50 after receiving a 25% discount on the original price.

A 25% discount means that Kyle only had to pay 75% of the original price (since 100% - 25% = 75%). Therefore, we can set up the following equation based on this information:
\[ 0.75P = \$19.50 \]

To find the original price \( P \), we need to solve for \( P \) by dividing both sides of the equation by 0.75:
\[ P = \frac{\$19.50}{0.75} \]

Now let's calculate this value.
\[ P = \frac{19.50}{0.75} \]
\[ P = 26 \]

So, the original price of the book was $26.

THE ANSWER IS: 26
