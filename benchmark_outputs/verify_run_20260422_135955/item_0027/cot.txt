EXPLANATION

To determine how much Mishka spent on all the clothing items, we need to calculate the cost of each type of item separately and then sum these amounts.

1. **Calculate the total cost of shorts:**
   - Mishka bought 3 pairs of shorts.
   - Each pair of shorts costs $16.50.
   - Total cost of shorts = \( 3 \times 16.50 \).

\[
3 \times 16.50 = 49.50 \text{ dollars}
\]

2. **Calculate the total cost of pants:**
   - Mishka bought 3 pairs of pants.
   - Each pair of pants costs $22.50.
   - Total cost of pants = \( 3 \times 22.50 \).

\[
3 \times 22.50 = 67.50 \text{ dollars}
\]

3. **Calculate the total cost of shoes:**
   - Mishka bought 3 pairs of shoes.
   - Each pair of shoes costs $42.
   - Total cost of shoes = \( 3 \times 42 \).

\[
3 \times 42 = 126 \text{ dollars}
\]

4. **Sum the total costs:**
   - Total cost of shorts + Total cost of pants + Total cost of shoes.

\[
49.50 + 67.50 + 126 = 243 \text{ dollars}
\]

Therefore, the total amount Mishka spent on all the clothing items is \( 243 \) dollars.

The answer is: 243
