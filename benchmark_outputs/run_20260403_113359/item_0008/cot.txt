To determine how long it will take Carla to download the entire 200 GB file after restarting and resuming, we need to break down the problem into manageable steps using logical reasoning.

### Step-by-Step Logical Reasoning

1. **Initial Download Speed**: Carla's initial download speed is 2 GB per minute.
2. **First Half of the File**: 
   - The first half of the file (100 GB) would normally take \( \frac{100 \text{GB}}{2 \text{GB/min}} = 50 \text{ minutes} \).
3. **Windows Restart**:
   - During this time, Carla's download speed drops to 1 GB per minute due to the system being forced to restart.
   - It takes \( \frac{20 \text{minutes}}{1 \text{GB/min}} = 20 \text{ minutes} \) to complete the second half of the file during the restart period.
4. **Total Time Before Restart**: 
   - Total time before the restart = Initial download + First half download during restart = \( 50 \text{ minutes} + 20 \text{ minutes} = 70 \text{ minutes} \).
5. **Restart and Resumption**:
   - After the restart, Carla resumes at her normal speed of 2 GB per minute.
6. **Remaining File Size**:
   - Remaining file size after the restart = 200 GB - 100 GB = 100 GB.
7. **Time to Complete Remaining File**:
   - Time required to download remaining 100 GB at 2 GB per minute = \( \frac{100 \text{GB}}{2 \text{GB/min}} = 50 \text{ minutes} \).

8. **Total Time Including Restart**:
   - Total time = Time before restart + Time to resume + Time to finish remaining file = \( 70 \text{ minutes} + 50 \text{ minutes} = 120 \text{ minutes} \).

### Conclusion

The total time it takes Carla to download the entire 200 GB file after restarting and resuming is **120 minutes**. 

Therefore, the answer is: 120 minutes.
