To calculate Josh's profit from flipping the house, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Determine the total cost of buying the house including repairs**:
   - The initial purchase price of the house is $80,000.
   - The amount spent on repairs is $50,000.
   - Total cost = Purchase price + Repair cost = $80,000 + $50,000 = $130,000.

2. **Calculate the new value of the house after the increase due to repairs**:
   - The house increased in value by 150%.
   - To find 150% of the original value ($80,000), we use the formula: (150/100) * $80,000 = 1.5 * $80,000 = $120,000.
   - New value of the house = Original value + Increase in value = $80,000 + $120,000 = $200,000.

3. **Calculate the profit**:
   - Profit = New value of the house - Total cost = $200,000 - $130,000 = $70,000.

Therefore, Josh made a profit of $70,000 from flipping the house. 

The answer is: $70,000.
