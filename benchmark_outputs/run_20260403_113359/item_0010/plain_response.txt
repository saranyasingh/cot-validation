To calculate Eliza's earnings for the week when she worked 45 hours, we need to consider both her regular and overtime hours.

1. **Regular Hours**: Eliza has already worked 40 hours at her regular rate of $10 per hour.
   \[
   \text{Earnings from Regular Hours} = 40 \times \$10 = \$400
   \]

2. **Overtime Hours**: The remaining hours after 40 regular hours would be considered as overtime. Since she worked a total of 45 hours:
   \[
   \text{Overtime Hours} = 45 - 40 = 5 \text{ hours}
   \]
   
3. **Overtime Rate**: Her overtime rate is 1.2 times her regular rate of $10.
   \[
   \text{Overtime Rate} = 1.2 \times \$10 = \$12 \text{ per hour}
   \]

4. **Earnings from Overtime**: Multiply the number of overtime hours by the overtime rate.
   \[
   \text{Earnings from Overtime} = 5 \times \$12 = \$60
   \]

5. **Total Earnings**:
   Add the earnings from regular hours and overtime hours together.
   \[
   \text{Total Earnings} = \$400 + \$60 = \$460
   \]

Therefore, Eliza's earnings for this week amount to $\boxed{\$460}$, which corresponds to option D.
