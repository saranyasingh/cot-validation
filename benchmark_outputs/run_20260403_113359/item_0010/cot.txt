**Logical Syllogism**

1. **Premise**: For the first 40 hours, Eliza earns $10 per hour.
   - Premise Explanation: This is given directly and establishes her base hourly wage before overtime.

2. **Premise**: Eliza has an additional 5 hours of work at her regular rate due to being paid time-and-a-half for overtime.
   - Premise Explanation: Overtime pay is calculated as 1.2 times the regular hourly rate, so if she works 5 hours at that rate, we need to consider both her normal and overtime pay separately.

3. **Conclusion**: We must calculate Eliza’s total earnings by adding her earnings from the first 40 hours and her earnings from the additional 5 hours of overtime.
   - Conclusion Explanation: By summing up her earnings from both periods (regular hours and overtime), we get her total weekly earnings.

**Calculation**:
- Earnings from the first 40 hours = \(40 \times \$10\)
- Earnings from the overtime = \(5 \times (\$10 \times 1.2)\)

Let's perform these calculations:

- Regular earnings: \(40 \times \$10 = \$400\)
- Overtime earnings: \(5 \times (\$10 \times 1.2) = 5 \times \$12 = \$60\)

Total earnings = Regular earnings + Overtime earnings

\[ Total Earnings = \$400 + \$60 = \$460 \]

**Answer**: Eliza's earnings for this week are $460.
