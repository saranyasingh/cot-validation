To determine how many cups of feed Wendi needs for her chickens in the final meal of the day, we can follow these steps:

### Premise 1:
Wendi feeds each chicken three cups of mixed chicken feed per day.

- **Premise Explanation:** This establishes that no matter what, each chicken gets three cups of feed daily.
  
### Premise 2:
She divides this feed into three separate meals throughout the day.

- **Premise Explanation:** Dividing the total amount into equal parts helps distribute the feed evenly across different times of the day.

### Premise 3:
The total feed given to all chickens over two days (morning and afternoon) equals the number of chickens multiplied by the total feed provided in those sessions.

- **Premise Explanation:** Summing up the amounts fed over both feeding times ensures we account for the entire quantity of feed distributed to all chickens.
  
### Premise 4:
Wendi has 20 chickens.

- **Premise Explanation:** This fact informs us about the total number of chickens in her flock.

### Premise 5:
In the morning, she gives her flock of chickens 15 cups of feed.

- **Premise Explanation:** Given information directly states the amount of feed provided during the first feeding session.

### Premise 6:
In the afternoon, she gives her chickens another 25 cups of feed.

- **Premise Explanation:** Another piece of given information stating the amount of feed provided during the second feeding session.

### Step-by-step Calculation:

#### Total Feed Provided Over Two Days:
First, calculate the total amount of feed provided over the two days by summing the amounts given in the morning and afternoon:
\[ \text{Total Feed} = \text{Morning Feeding} + \text{Afternoon Feeding} \]
\[ \text{Total Feed} = 15 \text{ cups} + 25 \text{ cups} \]
\[ \text{Total Feed} = 40 \text{ cups} \]

#### Calculate the Amount Needed for One Meal:
Since there are three meals per day and the total feed is divided equally among these three meals, we divide the total feed by the number of meals:
\[ \text{Feed per Meal} = \frac{\text{Total Feed}}{\text{Number of Meals}} \]
\[ \text{Feed per Meal} = \frac{40 \text{ cups}}{3} \]
\[ \text{Feed per Meal} = 13.\overline{3} \text{ cups} \]

#### Conclusion:
Therefore, Wendi needs approximately 13.33 cups of feed for each of her chickens in the final meal of the day.

**Answer:** The answer is: 13.33 cups.
