EXPLANATION
To determine how much Cynthia will spend on ice cream after 60 days, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the total number of servings of ice cream Cynthia consumes in 60 days.
2. Determine how many cartons of ice cream she needs based on the number of servings.
3. Find out the total cost of the cartons.

Step 1: Calculate the total number of servings of ice cream consumed in 60 days.
- Cynthia eats 1 serving of ice cream every night.
- There are 60 nights in 60 days.
- Therefore, the total number of servings is \(1 \times 60 = 60\) servings.

Step 2: Determine how many cartons of ice cream are needed.
- Each carton contains 15 servings.
- To find out how many cartons are needed, divide the total number of servings by the number of servings per carton: \(60 \div 15 = 4\).

Step 3: Find out the total cost of the cartons.
- Each carton costs $4.00.
- Since she needs 4 cartons, the total cost is \(4 \times 4 = 16\) dollars.

Therefore, Cynthia will spend $16.00 on ice cream after 60 days. The answer is: 16
