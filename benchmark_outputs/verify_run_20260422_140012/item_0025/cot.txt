EXPLANATION
Let's denote the original price of the book as P.

1. Kyle bought the book for $19.50 after receiving a 25% discount.
   - A 25% discount means that Kyle paid only 75% (or 0.75 times) of the original price.

2. We can set up an equation to represent this situation:
   \( \text{Discounted Price} = \text{Original Price} \times \left(1 - \frac{\text{Discount}}{100}\right) \)

3. Substituting the given values into the equation:
   \( \$19.50 = P \times \left(1 - \frac{25}{100}\right) \)
   
4. Simplify the expression inside the parentheses:
   \( \frac{25}{100} = 0.25 \), so the equation becomes:
   \( \$19.50 = P \times 0.75 \)

5. To find the original price P, we need to solve for P by dividing both sides of the equation by 0.75:
   \( P = \frac{\$19.50}{0.75} \)

6. Perform the division:
   \( P = \$26.00 \)

Therefore, the original price of the book was $26.00.
