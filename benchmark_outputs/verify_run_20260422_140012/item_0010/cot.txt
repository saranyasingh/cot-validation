EXPLANATION
Let's break down the problem into smaller parts to find out Eliza's earnings for the week.

Step 1: Determine Eliza's regular hourly rate.
- Eliza's regular rate is $10 per hour.

Step 2: Calculate Eliza's earnings for the first 40 hours.
- Regular hours = 40 hours
- Earnings for regular hours = 40 hours * $10/hour = $400

Step 3: Calculate the overtime hours.
- Total hours worked = 45 hours
- Overtime hours = Total hours - Regular hours = 45 - 40 = 5 hours

Step 4: Calculate Eliza's overtime pay.
- Overtime rate = 1.2 times the regular rate = 1.2 * $10 = $12/hour
- Earnings for overtime = 5 hours * $12/hour = $60

Step 5: Add the earnings from regular hours and overtime to get total earnings.
- Total earnings = Earnings for regular hours + Earnings for overtime
- Total earnings = $400 + $60 = $460

Therefore, Eliza's earnings for this week amount to $460.

The answer is: 460
