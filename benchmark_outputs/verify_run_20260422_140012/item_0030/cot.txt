EXPLANATION
Let's break down the information given and form our logical arguments.

1. **Identify variables**:
   - Let \( B \) represent the price of the boots.
   - One pair of heels costs $33, so the second pair of heels also costs $33 (since it costs twice as much).
   - Together, the two pairs of heels cost $5 less than the boots. Therefore, the combined cost of the two pairs of heels is \( 2 \times \$33 = \$66 \).

2. **Formulate the equation**:
   - The total cost of the two pairs of heels is $66.
   - Since the boots and the two pairs of heels together cost $5 less than the boots, we can write the equation:
     \[
     B + \$66 = B - \$5
     \]

3. **Solve the equation**:
   - Subtracting \( B \) from both sides of the equation gives us:
     \[
     \$66 = -\$5
     \]
   - This simplifies to:
     \[
     \$71 = B
     \]

4. **Conclusion**:
   - Therefore, the price of the boots is $71.
