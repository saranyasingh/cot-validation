EXPLANATION
Let's break down the problem into smaller steps.

Step 1: Calculate the total amount of feed given to all chickens throughout the day.
- In the morning, she gives 15 cups of feed.
- In the afternoon, she gives 25 cups of feed.
Total feed given = 15 + 25 = 40 cups

Step 2: Determine how much feed each chicken gets per day.
Wendi has 20 chickens, so we divide the total daily feed by the number of chickens.
Feed per chicken = Total daily feed / Number of chickens
= 40 / 20 = 2 cups per chicken

Step 3: Find out how much feed is needed for the last meal (even though it isn't explicitly mentioned).
Since Wendi gives each chicken 2 cups of feed every day, and there are 20 chickens, the total feed required for all chickens would be:
Total feed needed for all chickens = Feed per chicken * Number of chickens
= 2 * 20 = 40 cups

Therefore, Wendi needs to give 40 cups of feed in the final meal of the day.
