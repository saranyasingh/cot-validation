EXPLANATION
To determine the total cost Toula spent at the bakery, we need to calculate the cost for each type of pastry she purchased separately and then sum these costs.

1. **Cost calculation for donuts**:  
   - Toula bought 3 dozen donuts.
   - Each dozen donuts cost $68.
   - Total cost for donuts = Number of dozens × Cost per dozen = 3 × $68 = $204.

2. **Cost calculation for mini cupcakes**:  
   - Toula bought 2 dozen mini cupcakes.
   - Each dozen mini cupcakes cost $80.
   - Total cost for mini cupcakes = Number of dozens × Cost per dozen = 2 × $80 = $160.

3. **Cost calculation for mini cheesecakes**:  
   - Toula bought 6 dozen mini cheesecakes.
   - Each dozen mini cheesecakes cost $55.
   - Total cost for mini cheesecakes = Number of dozens × Cost per dozen = 6 × $55 = $330.

Now, let's add up all the individual costs to find the total amount spent:

Total cost = Cost for donuts + Cost for mini cupcakes + Cost for mini cheesecakes
           = $204 + $160 + $330
           = $794.

Therefore, the total cost Toula paid at the bakery is $794.
