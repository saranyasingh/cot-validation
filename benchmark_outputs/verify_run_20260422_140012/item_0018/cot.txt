EXPLANATION
To calculate Jill's annual salary, we need to determine how much she earns from teaching and coaching separately, then sum these amounts.

First, let's find out how much she earns from being a teacher:
- She teaches for 35 hours a week.
- Her hourly rate for teaching is $20.
- Therefore, her weekly earnings from teaching = 35 hours * $20/hour = $700.

Next, let's calculate her earnings from being a cheerleader:
- She coaches for 15 hours a week.
- Her hourly rate for coaching is $30.
- Therefore, her weekly earnings from coaching = 15 hours * $30/hour = $450.

Now that we have both her weekly earnings from teaching and coaching, we can add them together to find her total weekly earnings:
Total weekly earnings = Weekly earnings from teaching + Weekly earnings from coaching
                      = $700 + $450
                      = $1,150.

Finally, to get her annual salary, we multiply her total weekly earnings by the number of weeks she works in a year (50):
Annual salary = Total weekly earnings * Number of weeks worked per year
              = $1,150 * 50
              = $57,500.

Therefore, Jill's annual salary is $57,500.

The answer is: 57500.
