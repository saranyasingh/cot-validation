To determine Josh's profit, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total cost of buying the house including repairs**:
   - Cost of the house: $80,000
   - Amount spent on repairs: $50,000
   - Total initial investment = $80,000 + $50,000 = $130,000

2. **Determine the increase in the value of the house due to renovations**:
   - Increase in value: 150% of original price
   - Value after repairs = Original price * (1 + 150/100) = $80,000 * 2.5 = $200,000

3. **Calculate the profit**:
   - Profit = Final value - Initial investment
   - Profit = $200,000 - $130,000 = $70,000

- First, we summed up the initial costs to get the total amount invested.
- Then, we calculated the new value of the house after the renovations using the percentage increase provided.
- Finally, we subtracted the total initial investment from this new value to find the profit.
