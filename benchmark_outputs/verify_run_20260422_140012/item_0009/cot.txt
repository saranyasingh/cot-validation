EXPLANATION
To determine how far John is from home after 4 hours, we need to calculate his total distance traveled.

1. **First Leg:** John drives for 3 hours at a speed of 60 mph.
   - Distance = Speed × Time = 60 mph × 3 hours = 180 miles.

2. **Second Leg (Turnaround):** After realizing he needs to return home, he stops for 2 hours in traffic.
   - No additional distance covered during this stop.

3. **Third Leg:** John drives for the next half hour at a speed of 30 mph.
   - Distance = Speed × Time = 30 mph × 0.5 hours = 15 miles.

4. **Fourth Leg:** John drives for the remaining part of the 4 hours at a speed of 80 mph.
   - Total time spent driving = 4 hours - 2 hours (traffic) - 0.5 hours (half an hour) = 1.5 hours.
   - Distance = Speed × Time = 80 mph × 1.5 hours = 120 miles.

Now, let's sum up all the distances:
- First leg: 180 miles
- Second leg: 0 miles
- Third leg: 15 miles
- Fourth leg: 120 miles

Total distance traveled = 180 + 0 + 15 + 120 = 315 miles.

Since John started at home and drove away until he was 315 miles away, he must have been 315 miles from home when he turned around.

Therefore, the answer is: 315.
