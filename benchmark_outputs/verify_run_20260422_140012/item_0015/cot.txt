To determine what percentage of the entire students enrolled in hip-hop dance, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the number of students enrolled in contemporary dance:**
   - Total students = 20
   - Percentage enrolled in contemporary dance = 20%
   - Number of students in contemporary dance = \( 20 \times \frac{20}{100} = 4 \)

2. **Determine the number of students remaining after those enrolled in contemporary dance:**
   - Remaining students = Total students - Students in contemporary dance
   - Remaining students = \( 20 - 4 = 16 \)

3. **Calculate the number of students enrolled in jazz dance:**
   - Percentage enrolled in jazz dance = 25%
   - Number of students in jazz dance = \( 16 \times \frac{25}{100} = 4 \)

4. **Determine the number of students enrolled in hip-hop dance:**
   - Remaining students after contemporary and jazz = Initial remaining students - Students in jazz
   - Remaining students = \( 16 - 4 = 12 \)
   - Percentage enrolled in hip-hop dance = \( \frac{\text{Number of students in hip-hop}}{\text{Total students}} \times 100\% \)
   - Percentage enrolled in hip-hop dance = \( \frac{12}{20} \times 100\% = 60\% \)

Therefore, the percentage of the entire students who enrolled in hip-hop dance is \( 60\% \).
