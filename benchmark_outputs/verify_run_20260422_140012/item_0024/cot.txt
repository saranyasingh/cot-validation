EXPLANATION

To determine how many centimeters shorter a candle will be after burning from 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM, we need to calculate the total time during which the candle burns.

1. Identify the start and end times: From 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM.
   - Start time = 1:00 PM (or 13:00)
   - End time = 5:00 PM (or 17:00)

2. Calculate the duration of the burn:
   - Duration = End time - Start time
   - Duration = 17:00 - 13:00
   - Duration = 4 hours

3. Determine the rate at which the candle melts:
   - Rate of melting = 2 cm/hour

4. Calculate the total amount melted over the given period:
   - Total melted = Rate × Time
   - Total melted = 2 cm/hour × 4 hours
   - Total melted = 8 cm

Therefore, the candle will be 8 centimeters shorter after burning from 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM.

The answer is: 8
