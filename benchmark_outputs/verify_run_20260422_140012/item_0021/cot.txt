EXPLANATION

Let's break down the problem into steps using a logical syllogism.

**Premise 1:** I start with 10 liters of orange drink that is two-thirds water.
- This means there are \( \frac{2}{3} \times 10 = \frac{20}{3} \) liters of water and \( 10 - \frac{20}{3} = \frac{10}{3} \) liters of orange concentrate left.

**Premise 2:** I then add 15 liters of pineapple drink that is three-fifths water.
- This means there are \( \frac{3}{5} \times 15 = 9 \) liters of water and \( 15 - \frac{3}{5} \times 15 = 0 \) liters of pineapple concentrate left.

**Premise 3:** However, I spill one liter of the orange drink.
- After spilling, I am left with \( 10 - 1 = 9 \) liters of orange drink (which contains \( \frac{20}{3} \) liters of water).

**Premise 4:** Now, I combine the remaining orange drink with the pineapple drink.
- There are still \( 9 + 15 = 24 \) liters of liquid total.

**Conclusion:** Since all the orange drink was originally made up of water, when we mix it back with the pineapple drink, which has only water, the entire mixture remains pure water.

Therefore, the amount of water in the remaining 24 liters is **\( \frac{20}{3} \) liters or approximately 6.67 liters.**

The answer is: 6.67
