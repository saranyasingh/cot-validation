Explanation:

1. Henry's total distance for the bike trip is 60 miles.
2. He first stopped after traveling 20 miles.
3. His second stop was 15 miles before the end of the trip.

To find out how many miles he traveled between his first and second stops, we need to calculate the difference between the distance at which he stopped for the second time (after 45 miles) and the distance at which he stopped for the first time (after 20 miles).

Calculation:
Distance traveled between stops = Distance at second stop - Distance at first stop
= 45 miles - 20 miles
= 25 miles

Therefore, Henry traveled 25 miles between his first and second stops.

The answer is: 25
