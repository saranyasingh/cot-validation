To solve this problem, we need to break down the information given and use it to find out how much was spent on pizzas. Let's go through the steps systematically.

### Step 1: Calculate the total cost of known items.
- Chicken meal: \( \$12 \)
- Packs of milk: \( 5 \) packs at \( \$3 \) each
- Apples: \( 4 \) apples at \( \$1.50 \) each

First, let's calculate the total cost of these items:

\[
\text{Total cost of chicken meal} = \$12
\]

\[
\text{Total cost of milk packs} = 5 \times \$3 = \$15
\]

\[
\text{Total cost of apples} = 4 \times \$1.50 = \$6
\]

Now, sum up all these costs:

\[
\text{Total cost of chicken meal + milk packs + apples} = \$12 + \$15 + \$6 = \$33
\]

### Step 2: Determine the remaining amount spent on pizzas.
Marie paid a total of \( \$50 \). We subtract the amount already accounted for (chicken meal, milk, and apples):

\[
\text{Remaining amount} = \$50 - \$33 = \$17
\]

### Step 3: Calculate the number of pizza boxes ordered.
Each box of pizza costs \( \$8.50 \). To find out how many boxes were ordered, divide the remaining amount by the price per box:

\[
\text{Number of pizza boxes} = \frac{\$17}{\$8.50}
\]

Performing the division:

\[
\frac{\$17}{\$8.50} = 2
\]

So, Marie ordered **2** boxes of pizza.

### Conclusion:
The answer is: 2
