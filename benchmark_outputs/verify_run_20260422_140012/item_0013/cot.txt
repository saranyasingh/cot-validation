EXPLANATION
To determine how many years it will take for Carlos to start earning money on the lemon tree, we need to calculate his net profit per year after accounting for both the initial investment and ongoing expenses.

First, let's identify the key components:
- Initial cost to plant the tree: $90
- Annual revenue from selling lemons: 7 lemons * $1.5/lemon = $10.50
- Annual cost to water and feed the tree: $3

Next, we calculate the annual profit by subtracting the annual expenses from the annual revenue:
Annual profit = Annual revenue - Annual expenses
Annual profit = $10.50 - $3 = $7.50

This means that each year, Carlos earns an additional $7.50 from selling lemons after covering the water and feeding costs.

Now, we need to find out how many years it takes for this $7.50 profit to cover the initial $90 investment.
Number of years needed = Initial investment / Annual profit
Number of years needed = $90 / $7.50 = 12

Therefore, it will take Carlos 12 years before he starts earning money on the lemon tree.

The answer is: 12
