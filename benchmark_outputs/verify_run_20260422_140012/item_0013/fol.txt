## FACTS
FACT-1: Initial cost to plant the tree is $90 :: InitialCost(n_90)
FACT-2: Annual revenue from selling lemons is $10.50 :: AnnualRevenue(n_10_50)
FACT-3: Annual cost to water and feed the tree is $3 :: AnnualExpense(n_3)

## RULES
RULE-1: Profit equals revenue minus expenses :: ∀R∀E∀P((AnnualRevenue(R) ∧ AnnualExpense(E) ∧ P = R - E) → Profit(P))
RULE-2: Years to recoup equals initial cost divided by profit :: ∀I∀N∀Y((InitialCost(I) ∧ Profit(N) ∧ Y = I / N) → YearsToRecoup(Y))

## INFERENCES
INF-1: Annual profit computed as 10.50 minus 3 equals 7.50 from FACT-2 and FACT-3 by Arithmetic Computation :: Profit(n_7_50)
INF-2: Number of years needed to recoup = 90 / 7.50 = 12 from FACT-1 and INF-1 by Modus Ponens using RULE-2 :: YearsToRecoup(n_12)
