To determine how many bolts of fiber are needed to make a robe, we need to calculate the amount of each color of fiber required.

1. **Step 1:** Determine the amount of blue fiber.
   - A robe requires 2 bolts of blue fiber.

2. **Step 2:** Calculate the amount of white fiber.
   - White fiber is half the amount of blue fiber.
   - Therefore, the amount of white fiber = 0.5 * (amount of blue fiber).
   - Amount of white fiber = 0.5 * 2 bolts = 1 bolt.

3. **Step 3:** Add the amounts together to find the total number of bolts.
   - Total bolts = Blue fiber + White fiber.
   - Total bolts = 2 bolts + 1 bolt = 3 bolts.

**Explanation:**
- We started by identifying the requirement for blue fiber as stated directly.
- Then, we used the given ratio (half the amount of blue fiber) to find out how much white fiber was needed.
- Finally, we added both quantities to get the total number of bolts required.

Therefore, the answer is: 3.
