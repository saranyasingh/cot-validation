EXPLANATION

To determine how much Mishka spent on all the clothing items, we need to calculate the cost of each type of item she purchased and then sum these amounts.

First, let's find the total cost of the shorts. Mishka bought 3 pairs of shorts at $16.50 per pair.
\[
3 \times \$16.50 = \$49.50
\]

Next, we calculate the total cost of the pants. Mishka also bought 3 pairs of pants at $22.50 per pair.
\[
3 \times \$22.50 = \$67.50
\]

Now, we need to find the total cost of the shoes. Mishka bought 3 pairs of shoes at $42.00 per pair.
\[
3 \times \$42.00 = \$126.00
\]

Finally, we add up the totals for all three types of clothing items to find the overall amount Mishka spent.
\[
\$49.50 + \$67.50 + \$126.00 = \$243.00
\]

Therefore, the total amount Mishka spent on all the clothing items is \( \$243.00 \). The answer is: 243.
