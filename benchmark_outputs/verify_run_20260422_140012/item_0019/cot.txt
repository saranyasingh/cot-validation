To determine how many dozen eggs Claire eats in 4 weeks, we need to break down the problem into smaller steps.

**Premise 1:** Claire makes a 3-egg omelet every morning.
This tells us that each day, Claire uses 3 eggs.

**Premise 2:** There are 7 days in a week.
This gives us the total number of eggs used per week based on Premise 1.

**Step-by-step calculation:**

1. **Eggs used per week:**
   \[
   \text{Eggs/day} \times \text{Days/week} = 3 \times 7 = 21 \text{ eggs}
   \]

2. **Eggs used in 4 weeks:**
   \[
   \text{Eggs/week} \times \text{Weeks} = 21 \times 4 = 84 \text{ eggs}
   \]

3. **Convert eggs to dozens:**
   - Since there are 12 eggs in a dozen,
   \[
   \frac{\text{Total eggs}}{12} = \frac{84}{12} = 7 \text{ dozens}
   \]

**Explanation:**
- We multiplied the daily egg count by the number of days in a week because this gives us the total number of eggs consumed in one week.
- Then, since there are 7 days in a week, we calculated the total number of eggs consumed over four weeks.
- Finally, we converted the total number of eggs (in individual counts) to dozens by dividing by 12, as there are 12 eggs in a dozen.

**Conclusion:**
The answer is: 7.
