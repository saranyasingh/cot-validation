EXPLANATION
1. A robe requires 2 bolts of blue fiber.
2. The amount of white fiber required is half the amount of blue fiber.
3. Therefore, the amount of white fiber required is 2 / 2 = 1 bolt.
4. To find the total amount of fiber required, we add the bolts of blue fiber to the bolts of white fiber.
5. Thus, the total amount of fiber required is 2 (blue) + 1 (white) = 3 bolts.

The answer is: 3
