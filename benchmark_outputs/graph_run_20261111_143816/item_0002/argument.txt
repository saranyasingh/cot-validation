S1 [apply]: Because robe requires 2 bolts of blue fiber; and for robe, the amount of white fiber required is half the amount of blue fiber; and since for any garment r, any fibers f1, f2, any numbers a, c: if r requires a bolts of f1 and for r, the amount of f2 required is half the amount of f1 and c = a ÷ 2, then r requires c bolts of f2: robe requires 1 bolts of white fiber (2 / 2 = 1).
S2 [aggregate]: Because robe requires 2 bolts of blue fiber; and robe requires 1 bolts of white fiber; and since for any garment r, any numbers a, b, c: if r requires a bolts of blue fiber and r requires b bolts of white fiber and c = a + b, then r requires c bolts of fiber in total: robe requires 3 bolts of fiber in total (2 + 1 = 3).
