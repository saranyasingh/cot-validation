Let's break down the problem step by step using logical syllogism.

1. Each chicken gets 3 cups of feed per day.
2. Wendi has 20 chickens.
3. Therefore, the total daily feed requirement for all chickens is \(20 \text{ chickens} \times 3 \text{ cups/chicken} = 60 \text{ cups}\).
4. In the morning, Wendi gives 15 cups of feed.
5. In the afternoon, Wendi gives 25 cups of feed.
6. The total feed given in the morning and afternoon is \(15 \text{ cups} + 25 \text{ cups} = 40 \text{ cups}\).
7. To meet the total daily requirement of 60 cups, the remaining amount of feed needed is \(60 \text{ cups} - 40 \text{ cups} = 20 \text{ cups}\).

The answer is: 20
