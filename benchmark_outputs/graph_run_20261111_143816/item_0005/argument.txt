S1 [apply]: Because each of Wendi's chickens gets 3 cups of feed per day; and Wendi has 20 chickens; and since for any person x, any numbers a, n, c: if each of x's chickens gets a cups of feed per day and x has n chickens and c = n × a, then the total daily feed requirement for all of x's chickens is c cups: the total daily feed requirement for all of Wendi's chickens is 60 cups (20 x 3 = 60).
S2 [aggregate]: Because the Morning feeding gives 15 cups of feed; and the Afternoon feeding gives 25 cups of feed; and since the only X such that "Feedings given so far that day includes X" are Morning feeding and Afternoon feeding; there are no others: the total feed given in Feedings given so far that day is 40 cups (15 + 25 = 40).
S3 [apply]: Because the total daily feed requirement for all of Wendi's chickens is 60 cups; and the total feed given in Feedings given so far that day is 40 cups; and Wendi's full daily requirement must be met by the end of the day, with the final meal supplying whatever was not given in Feedings given so far that day; and since for any person x, any feeding_set s, any numbers a, b, c: if the total daily feed requirement for all of x's chickens is a cups and the total feed given in s is b cups and x's full daily requirement must be met by the end of the day, with the final meal supplying whatever was not given in s and c = a - b, then the remaining amount of feed x needs to give to meet the daily requirement is c cups: the remaining amount of feed Wendi needs to give to meet the daily requirement is 20 cups (60 - 40 = 20).
