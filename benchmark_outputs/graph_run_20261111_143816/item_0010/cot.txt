EXPLANATION
1. Eliza's regular hourly rate is $10.
2. For the first 40 hours, Eliza earns at her regular rate of $10 per hour.
3. Therefore, for the first 40 hours, Eliza earns 40 * $10 = $400.
4. Eliza worked for 45 hours this week, which means she worked 5 hours of overtime.
5. Overtime pay is 1.2 times her regular hourly rate, so her overtime rate is 1.2 * $10 = $12 per hour.
6. For the 5 hours of overtime, Eliza earns 5 * $12 = $60.
7. Therefore, her total earnings for the week are the sum of her regular earnings and her overtime earnings, which is $400 + $60 = $460.

The answer is: 460
