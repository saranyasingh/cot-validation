EXPLANATION
1. James runs 3 sprints each time he exercises.
2. He exercises 3 times a week.
3. Therefore, the total number of sprints he runs in a week is 3 sprints/time * 3 times/week = 9 sprints/week.
4. Each sprint is 60 meters long.
5. Thus, the total distance he runs in a week is 9 sprints/week * 60 meters/sprint = 540 meters/week.

The answer is: 540
