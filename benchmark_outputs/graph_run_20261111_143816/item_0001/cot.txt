EXPLANATION
Step 1: Determine the total number of eggs laid per day.
Janet's ducks lay 16 eggs per day.

Step 2: Calculate the number of eggs Janet uses for her personal consumption.
Janet eats 3 eggs for breakfast every morning.

Step 3: Calculate the number of eggs used for baking muffins.
Janet bakes muffins with 4 eggs every day.

Step 4: Compute the total number of eggs used (both for eating and baking).
Total eggs used = Eggs for breakfast + Eggs for baking muffins
Total eggs used = 3 + 4 = 7

Step 5: Calculate the number of eggs remaining after Janet's personal use and baking.
Remaining eggs = Total eggs laid - Total eggs used
Remaining eggs = 16 - 7 = 9

Step 6: Calculate the revenue from selling the remaining eggs at the farmers' market.
Revenue = Remaining eggs * Price per egg
Revenue = 9 * $2 = $18

The answer is: 18
