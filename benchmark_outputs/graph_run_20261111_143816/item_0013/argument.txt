S1 [apply]: Because the tree produces 7 lemons each year; and Carlos sells lemons at $1.5 per lemon; and since for any tree t, any person s, any numbers n, r, v: if t produces n lemons each year and s sells lemons at $r per lemon and v = n × r, then t generates $v in revenue annually: the tree generates $10.5 in revenue annually.
S2 [apply]: Because the tree generates $10.5 in revenue annually; and watering and feeding the tree costs $3 each year; and since for any tree t, any numbers r, c, p: if t generates $r in revenue annually and watering and feeding t costs $c each year and p = r - c, then the net profit from t is $p per year: the net profit from the tree is $7.5 per year ($10.5 - $3 = $7.5).
S3 [apply]: Because the tree costs $90 to plant; and the net profit from the tree is $7.5 per year; and the net profit from the tree is the same $7.5 every year, so cumulative profit grows linearly with years; and since for any tree t, any numbers i, p, y: if t costs $i to plant and the net profit from t is $p per year and the net profit from t is the same $p every year, so cumulative profit grows linearly with years and y = i ÷ p, then it takes y years for t to break even: it takes 12 years for the tree to break even ($90 / $7.5 = 12).
