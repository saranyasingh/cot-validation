To solve this problem, let's break down the costs and revenues over time.

1. **Initial Cost**: The tree costs $90 to plant.
2. **Annual Costs**: Each year, Carlos incurs an additional cost of $3 for watering and feeding the tree.
3. **Annual Revenue**: Each year, the tree produces 7 lemons, which he sells at $1.5 per lemon, generating $10.5 in revenue annually.
4. **Net Annual Profit**: To find out when Carlos starts earning money, we need to calculate the net profit each year after subtracting the annual costs from the annual revenue. This gives us a net profit of $10.5 - $3 = $7.5 per year.
5. **Time to Break Even**: We need to determine how many years it takes for the net profit to cover the initial cost of $90. This can be calculated by dividing the initial cost by the net annual profit: $90 / $7.5 = 12 years.

The answer is: 12
