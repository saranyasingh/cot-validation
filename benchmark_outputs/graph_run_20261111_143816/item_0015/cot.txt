EXPLANATION
1. Let's start by calculating the number of students who enrolled in contemporary dance.
   - Total students = 20
   - Percentage enrolled in contemporary dance = 20%
   - Number of students in contemporary dance = 20 * 20% = 4 students

2. Now we calculate the number of students remaining after those who enrolled in contemporary dance.
   - Remaining students = Total students - Students in contemporary dance
   - Remaining students = 20 - 4 = 16 students

3. Next, we determine how many of the remaining students enrolled in jazz dance.
   - Percentage of remaining students who enrolled in jazz dance = 25%
   - Number of students in jazz dance = 16 * 25% = 4 students

4. We now calculate the number of students who enrolled in hip-hop dance.
   - Students in hip-hop dance = Remaining students - Students in jazz dance
   - Students in hip-hop dance = 16 - 4 = 12 students

5. Finally, we find the percentage of the entire class that enrolled in hip-hop dance.
   - Percentage of students in hip-hop dance = (Students in hip-hop dance / Total students) * 100
   - Percentage of students in hip-hop dance = (12 / 20) * 100 = 60%

The answer is: 60
