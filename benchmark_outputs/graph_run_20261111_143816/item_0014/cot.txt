2. She then sold 2 more vacuum cleaners to the red house, leaving her with \( \frac{2x}{3} - 2 \) vacuum cleaners.
3. After selling to the red house, she sold half of what was left at the orange house. This means she sold \( \frac{1}{2} \left( \frac{2x}{3} - 2 \right) \) vacuum cleaners at the orange house, and thus had \( \frac{1}{2} \left( \frac{2x}{3} - 2 \right) \) vacuum cleaners left.
4. According to the problem, after all these sales, she has 5 vacuum cleaners left. Therefore, we set up the equation:
   \[
   \frac{1}{2} \left( \frac{2x}{3} - 2 \right) = 5
   \]

   \[
   \frac{2x}{3} - 2 = 10
   \]
6. Then add 2 to both sides:
   \[
   \frac{2x}{3} = 12
   \]

   \]
