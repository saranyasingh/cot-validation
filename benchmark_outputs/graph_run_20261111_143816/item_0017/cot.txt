EXPLANATION
Train A travels westward for 80 miles and then northward for 150 miles.
Train B travels westward for 80 miles and then northward for 150 miles.

Premise 1: Both trains travel the same distances in the same directions.
Premise 2: Therefore, the distance covered by Train A in the two days is the sum of its westward and northward distances.
Premise 3: The distance covered by Train A is 80 miles (west) + 150 miles (north).
Premise 4: Therefore, the distance covered by Train A is 230 miles.

Premise 5: Similarly, the distance covered by Train B in the two days is the sum of its westward and northward distances.
Premise 6: The distance covered by Train B is 80 miles (west) + 150 miles (north).
Premise 7: Therefore, the distance covered by Train B is 230 miles.

The answer is: 230
