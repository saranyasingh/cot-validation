EXPLANATION

1. Janet's ducks lay 16 eggs per day.
   Premise: This establishes the total number of eggs laid.

2. Janet eats 3 eggs for breakfast every morning.
   Premise: This determines how many eggs are used for consumption.

3. Janet bakes muffins using 4 eggs for her friends.
   Premise: This indicates another source of egg usage.

4. To find out how many eggs are left after eating and baking,
   we subtract the consumed eggs from the total.
   Remaining eggs = Total eggs - Eggs eaten + Eggs baked
   Remaining eggs = 16 - 3 + 4 = 17

5. Janet sells the remaining eggs at the farmers' market for $2 per egg.
   Premise: This provides the price per egg for sale.

6. To calculate her earnings, multiply the number of remaining eggs by the selling price per egg.
   Earnings = Remaining eggs * Price per egg
   Earnings = 17 * $2 = $34

Therefore, Janet makes $34 every day at the farmers' market.

The answer is: 34
