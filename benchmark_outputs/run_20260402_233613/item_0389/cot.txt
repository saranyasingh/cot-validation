To determine how many rows Faye could make with 30 pencils arranged in rows of 5 pencils each, we need to perform a division operation. Here's the logical reasoning:

1. **Premise 1:** There are 30 pencils.
2. **Premise 2:** Each row contains 5 pencils.
3. **Premise 3:** To find out how many rows can be made, divide the total number of pencils by the number of pencils per row.

Let's apply this to our specific scenario:
4. **Logical Operation:** Divide 30 by 5 (since there are 30 pencils and each row has 5 pencils).
   \[
   \frac{30}{5} = 6
   \]

Therefore, Faye could make 6 rows.

The answer is: 6
