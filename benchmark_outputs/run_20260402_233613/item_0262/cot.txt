Let's break down the problem into clear steps using a logical syllogism:

**Premise 1:** Danny initially had 15 bottle caps.
- This tells us the starting number of bottle caps.

**Premise 2:** Danny initially had 18 wrappers.
- This tells us the starting number of wrappers.

**Premise 3:** After finding additional items:
   - He now has 67 wrappers.
   - He now has 35 bottle caps.
- These are the current numbers we know after collecting more items.

**Step-by-step Logical Reasoning:**

1. **Determine Initial Collection:**
   - Let \( B \) represent the initial number of bottle caps.
   - Let \( W \) represent the initial number of wrappers.
   
   Given:
   \[
   B = 15
   \]
   \[
   W = 18
   \]

2. **Current Collection:**
   - Current number of wrappers (\( C_W \)) = 67
   - Current number of bottle caps (\( C_B \)) = 35

3. **Calculate Total Items Collected:**
   - Total wrappers collected = \( C_W - W \)
   \[
   \text{Total wrappers collected} = 67 - 18 = 49
   \]

4. **Calculate Total Bottle Caps Collected:**
   - Total bottle caps collected = \( C_B - B \)
   \[
   \text{Total bottle caps collected} = 35 - 15 = 20
   \]

5. **Compare Wrappers to Bottle Caps:**
   - Difference between total wrappers and total bottle caps = 
   \[
   49 - 20 = 29
   \]

**Conclusion:**
Danny now has 29 more wrappers than bottle caps in his collection.

The answer is: Danny has 29 more wrappers than bottle caps.
