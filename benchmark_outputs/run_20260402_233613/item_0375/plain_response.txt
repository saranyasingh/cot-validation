To determine how much more money Gwen received from her mom compared to her dad, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the total amount of money Gwen received:
   - From her dad: $5
   - From her mom: $7

2. Add the amounts received from both parents:
   \[
   5 + 7 = 12
   \]

3. Subtract the amount spent from the total amount received:
   \[
   12 - 4 = 8
   \]

So, Gwen received $8 more from her mom than she did from her dad.

The answer is: 8
