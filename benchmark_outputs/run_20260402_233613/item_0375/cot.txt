To determine how much more money Gwen received from her mom compared to her dad, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total amount of money Gwen received from both parents**:
   - Gwen received $5 from her dad.
   - She also received $7 from her mom.
   - Therefore, the total amount of money Gwen received from her parents is:
     \[
     5 + 7 = 12 \text{ dollars}
     \]

2. **Determine the difference between the amounts received from her mom and dad**:
   - Gwen spent $4 on something.
   - To find out how much more money she received from her mom than from her dad, subtract the amount she spent from the total amount received from her parents:
     \[
     12 - 4 = 8 \text{ dollars}
     \]

3. **Verify that this calculation matches the problem statement**:
   - We know Gwen received $5 from her dad.
   - She received $7 from her mom.
   - After spending $4, the remaining amount is indeed $8 (since $5 + $7 - $4 = $8).

Therefore, based on our calculations, the answer is: 
The answer is: 8
