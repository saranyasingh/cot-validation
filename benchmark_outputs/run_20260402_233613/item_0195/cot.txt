Premise 1: Frank's favorite book has 612 pages.
Premise 2: These pages are equally distributed over 24 chapters.
Premise 3: Frank finished the entire book in 6 days.

To find out how many pages Frank read per day, we need to divide the total number of pages by the number of days it took him to finish the book.

Step 1: Calculate the average number of pages per chapter.
Total number of pages = 612
Number of chapters = 24

Average pages per chapter = Total number of pages / Number of chapters
= 612 / 24
= 25.5

Step 2: Determine the number of pages Frank read per day.
It took Frank 6 days to finish the book.

Pages per day = Average pages per chapter / Number of days
= 25.5 / 6
= 4.25

Therefore, Frank read approximately 4.25 pages per day.

The answer is: 4.25
