To determine how many pages Frank read per day, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the number of pages in each chapter**:
   Since the book has 612 pages and it's divided into 24 equal parts (chapters), we divide the total number of pages by the number of chapters.
   \[
   \text{Pages per chapter} = \frac{612}{24}
   \]

2. **Perform the division**:
   Let's do this step-by-step:
   - First, find the largest multiple of 24 that is less than or equal to 612.
   - Then, subtract this multiple from 612.
   \[
   612 ÷ 24 = 25 \quad \text{(since } 24 × 25 = 600\text{)}
   \]
   So, there are 25 pages in each chapter.

3. **Calculate the total number of pages Frank read in 6 days**:
   If Frank finished the entire book in 6 days, then he read all 612 pages in those 6 days.

4. **Determine the number of pages read per day**:
   To find out how many pages Frank read per day, we divide the total number of pages by the number of days.
   \[
   \text{Pages per day} = \frac{612}{6}
   \]

5. **Perform the division again**:
   \[
   612 ÷ 6 = 102
   \]

Therefore, Frank read 102 pages per day. The answer is: 102.
