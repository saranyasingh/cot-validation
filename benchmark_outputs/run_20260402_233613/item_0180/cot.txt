Let's break down the problem into a logical syllogism:

**Premise 1:** In a school there are 308 girls.
- This establishes the number of female students (girls) in the school.

**Premise 2:** In a school there are 318 boys.
- This establishes the number of male students (boys) in the school.

**Premise 3:** There are also 36 teachers in the school.
- This introduces another group to consider within the total student population.

To find the total number of pupils in the school, we need to sum up all the students (both girls and boys).

**Step-by-step Logical Reasoning:**

1. **Total Number of Students:**
   - We know there are 308 girls and 318 boys.
   - Therefore, the total number of students = 308 girls + 318 boys.

2. **Calculate Total Students:**
   \[
   308 + 318 = 626
   \]

Thus, the total number of pupils in the school is 626.

**Conclusion:**
The answer is: 626 pupils.
