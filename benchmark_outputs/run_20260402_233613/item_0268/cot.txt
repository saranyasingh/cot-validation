**Logical Syllogism**

1. **Premise 1**: During summer break, 34,044 kids from Lawrence County went to camp.
   - This tells us about the number of kids who went to camp within the specified area (Lawrence County).

2. **Premise 2**: Additionally, an extra 424,944 kids from outside the county attended the camp.
   - This provides information about the number of kids attending the camp but not from within the specified area (Lawrence County).

3. **Conclusion**: To find out how many kids in total attended the camp, we need to add the number of kids from Lawrence County to the number of kids from outside the county.
   - Total attendance = Number of kids from Lawrence County + Number of kids from outside the county

Therefore, the total number of kids who attended the camp is:
\[ \text{Total attendance} = 34,044 + 424,944 = 459,000 \]

The answer is: 459,000 kids attended the camp.
