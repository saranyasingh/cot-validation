Premise 1: The total number of sacks of unripe oranges harvested per day is 71.
Premise 2: The total number of sacks of ripe oranges harvested per day is 60.
Conclusion: The difference between the number of sacks of unripe oranges and ripe oranges harvested per day is 71 - 60 = 11 sacks.

The answer is: 11.
