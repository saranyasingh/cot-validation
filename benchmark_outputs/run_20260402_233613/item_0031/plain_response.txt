To determine how many more salty cookies than sweet cookies Paco ate, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the number of sweet cookies Paco ate.
   - Paco originally had 8 sweet cookies.
   - He ate 20 sweet cookies.
   - Therefore, the total number of sweet cookies eaten is \(8 + 20 = 28\).

2. Calculate the number of salty cookies Paco ate.
   - Paco originally had 6 salty cookies.
   - He ate 34 salty cookies.
   - Therefore, the total number of salty cookies eaten is \(6 + 34 = 40\).

3. Find out how many more salty cookies than sweet cookies Paco ate.
   - The difference between the number of salty cookies eaten and the number of sweet cookies eaten is \(40 - 28 = 12\).

So, Paco ate 12 more salty cookies than sweet cookies.

The answer is: 12
