To determine how many apps Dave has left on his phone after deleting some, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Identify Initial Condition**: 
   - Dave initially had 19 apps on his phone.
   
2. **Identify Action Taken**:
   - Dave deleted 5 apps from his phone.
   
3. **Apply Deletion Process**:
   - When Dave deletes 5 apps, he subtracts this number from the initial count.
   - Therefore, the new total becomes \(19 - 5\).

4. **Perform Subtraction**:
   \[
   19 - 5 = 14
   \]

So, after deleting 5 apps, Dave has 14 apps remaining on his phone.

The answer is: The number of apps left on Dave's phone is 14.
