To determine how many snakes were not hiding, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Identify the total number of snakes:** 
   There are 95 snakes in total.

2. **Determine how many snakes are hiding:**
   It's given that 64 snakes are hiding.

3. **Calculate the number of snakes not hiding:**
   To find out how many snakes are not hiding, subtract the number of hiding snakes from the total number of snakes.
   
   \[
   \text{Number of snakes not hiding} = \text{Total number of snakes} - \text{Number of hiding snakes}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Number of snakes not hiding} = 95 - 64
   \]

4. **Perform the subtraction:**
   \[
   95 - 64 = 31
   \]

Therefore, there are 31 snakes that are not hiding.

The answer is: 31
