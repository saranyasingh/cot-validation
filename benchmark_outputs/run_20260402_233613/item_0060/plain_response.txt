To determine how many children got on the bus at the bus stop, we can follow these steps:

1. Initially, there were 21 children on the bus.
2. At the bus stop, 10 children got off the bus. So, the number of children left on the bus after they got off was:
   \[
   21 - 10 = 11
   \]
3. After that, let's say \( x \) children got on the bus. The total number of children on the bus became 16. Therefore, we can set up the equation:
   \[
   11 + x = 16
   \]
4. To find \( x \), we subtract 11 from both sides of the equation:
   \[
   x = 16 - 11
   \]
5. Simplifying the right side gives us:
   \[
   x = 5
   \]

So, 5 children got on the bus at the bus stop.

The answer is: 5
