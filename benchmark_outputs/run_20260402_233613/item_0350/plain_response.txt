To determine how many more books you still need to read, we can follow these steps:

1. **Identify the total number of books**: There are 22 books in the series.
2. **Determine the number of books already read**: You have read 12 books.
3. **Calculate the remaining books**:
   \[
   \text{Remaining books} = \text{Total books} - \text{Books read}
   \]
   Substituting the given values:
   \[
   \text{Remaining books} = 22 - 12 = 10
   \]
