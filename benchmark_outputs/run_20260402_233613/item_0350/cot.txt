Premise 1: There are 22 different books in the 'Crazy Silly School' series.
Premise 2: There are 10 different movies in the 'Crazy Silly School' series.
Premise 3: Out of these 22 books, 12 were read.
Premise 4: Out of these 10 movies, 56 were watched.

Given Information:
- Total number of books = 22
- Number of books read = 12

Question: How many more books do you still have to read?

To solve this problem logically:

Step 1: Determine how many books are left unread after reading some.
Since there are 22 books in total and 12 were read, we subtract the number of books read from the total number of books:
22 - 12 = 10 books remaining unread.

Therefore, based on the given information and premises, there are 10 more books that need to be read from the total collection of 22 books in the 'Crazy Silly School' series.

The answer is: 10.
