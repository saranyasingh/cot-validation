Let's denote the number of pages of reading homework Rachel had to complete as \( R \).

According to the problem, Rachel had to complete 7 pages of math homework and 4 more pages of math homework than reading homework. This can be expressed as:
\[ 7 = R + 4 \]

To find out how many pages of reading homework Rachel had to complete, we need to solve for \( R \):
\[ R = 7 - 4 \]
\[ R = 3 \]

So, Rachel had to complete 3 pages of reading homework. The answer is: 3.
