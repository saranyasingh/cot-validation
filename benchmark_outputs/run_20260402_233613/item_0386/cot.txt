Let's break down this problem using a logical syllogism:

1. **Premise 1**: Rachel had to complete 7 pages of math homework.
   - This establishes that there is a total amount of math homework assigned.

2. **Premise 2**: She had to complete 4 more pages of math homework than reading homework.
   - From Premise 1 and Premise 2, we know that the difference between the number of math pages and reading pages is 4.

3. **Conclusion**: Let \( R \) represent the number of pages of reading homework.
   
   Therefore:
   - The number of math pages (M) = 7
   - M = R + 4

4. **Substitute**:
   - Since \( M = 7 \), substitute into our equation:
     \[
     7 = R + 4
     \]

5. **Solve for \( R \)**:
   - Subtract 4 from both sides:
     \[
     7 - 4 = R
     \]
     \[
     3 = R
     \]

Thus, Rachel had to complete 3 pages of reading homework.

The answer is: The number of pages of reading homework Rachel had to complete is 3.
