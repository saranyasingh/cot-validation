To find out how many more kids Julia played with on Monday compared to Tuesday, we need to subtract the number of kids she played with on Tuesday from the number of kids she played with on Monday.

Number of kids on Monday = 18
Number of kids on Tuesday = 10

Difference = Number of kids on Monday - Number of kids on Tuesday
           = 18 - 10
           = 8

Therefore, Julia played with 8 more kids on Monday than on Tuesday. The answer is: 8.
