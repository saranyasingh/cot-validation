Let's break down this problem using a logical syllogism:

**Premise 1:** Julia played tag with 18 kids on Monday.
- This establishes the number of children involved on the first day.

**Premise 2:** Julia played tag with 10 kids on Tuesday.
- This establishes the number of children involved on the second day.

**Conclusion:** To find out how many more kids she played with on Monday than on Tuesday, we subtract the number of kids she played with on Tuesday from the number of kids she played with on Monday.

**Logical Syllogism:**
1. If A then B (Premise 1): Julia played tag with 18 kids on Monday.
   - This sets up our starting point and establishes that the number of kids was 18 on Monday.
   
2. If C then D (Premise 2): Julia played tag with 10 kids on Tuesday.
   - This confirms the number of kids was 10 on Tuesday.

3. Therefore, if E, then F (Consequence): Number of kids on Monday minus number of kids on Tuesday equals difference.
   - Mathematically, \(18 - 10 = 8\).

4. Hence, Julia played with 8 more kids on Monday than on Tuesday.

The answer is: 8
