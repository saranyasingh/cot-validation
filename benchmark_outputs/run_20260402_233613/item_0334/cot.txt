To determine how many more tickets Dave used to buy clothes than he did to buy toys, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Identify the total number of tickets Dave initially had**: 
   - Dave started with 13 tickets.
   
2. **Calculate the remaining tickets after buying toys**:
   - Dave spent 8 tickets on toys.
   - Remaining tickets = Initial tickets - Tickets spent on toys
   - Remaining tickets = 13 - 8 = 5 tickets

3. **Determine the number of tickets spent on clothes**:
   - After buying toys, Dave still has 5 tickets left.
   - He then spends an additional 18 tickets on clothes.
   - Total tickets spent on clothes = Tickets spent on toys + Additional tickets spent on clothes
   - Total tickets spent on clothes = 8 + 18 = 26 tickets
   
4. **Compare the tickets spent on clothes to those spent on toys**:
   - Tickets spent on clothes (26) > Tickets spent on toys (8)
   - Difference = Tickets spent on clothes - Tickets spent on toys
   - Difference = 26 - 8 = 18 tickets

Therefore, the answer is: 18.
