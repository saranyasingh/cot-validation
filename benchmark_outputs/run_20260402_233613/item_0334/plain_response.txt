To determine how many more tickets Dave used to buy clothes compared to buying toys, we need to subtract the number of tickets spent on toys from the number of tickets spent on clothes.

First, let's find out how many tickets Dave had left after buying toys:
Dave initially had 13 tickets.
He spent 8 tickets on toys.
So, the remaining tickets are \( 13 - 8 = 5 \) tickets.

Next, we know that Dave spent an additional 18 tickets on clothes.

Now, we calculate the difference between the tickets used for clothes and those used for toys:
\( 18 \) (tickets for clothes) - \( 5 \) (remaining tickets after toys) = \( 13 \)

Therefore, Dave used 13 more tickets to buy clothes than he did to buy toys.

The answer is: 13
