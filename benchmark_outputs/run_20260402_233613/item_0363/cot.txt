Let's break down the problem and solve it step-by-step using a logical syllogism:

**Premise 1:** Frank finished a book that has 41 chapters.
- This establishes the total number of chapters in the book.

**Premise 2:** Each chapter contains an equal number of pages.
- This implies uniform distribution of pages across all chapters.

**Premise 3:** The entire book consists of 450 pages.
- This gives us the total number of pages available in the book.

**Premise 4:** It took Frank 30 days to finish the book.
- This tells us how long it took him to complete the entire book.

**Premise 5:** We need to find out how many pages Frank read per day.
- This is what we're trying to determine.

To logically connect these premises, let’s use the following steps:

### Step 1: Determine the number of pages per chapter

Since there are 41 chapters and the total number of pages is 450, we divide the total number of pages by the number of chapters:
\[
\text{Pages per chapter} = \frac{\text{Total pages}}{\text{Number of chapters}} = \frac{450}{41}
\]

Performing this division:
\[
\frac{450}{41} \approx 10.98
\]
This means each chapter contains approximately 10.98 pages.

### Step 2: Calculate the average number of pages Frank read per day

Given that Frank spent 30 days finishing the book, we divide the total number of pages by the number of days:
\[
\text{Pages per day} = \frac{\text{Total pages}}{\text{Number of days}} = \frac{450}{30}
\]

Performing this division:
\[
\frac{450}{30} = 15
\]
So, Frank read approximately 15 pages per day on average.

### Conclusion

The answer is: **15**

This conclusion is derived directly from dividing the total number of pages (450) by the total number of days taken to finish the book (30), resulting in an average of 15 pages per day.
