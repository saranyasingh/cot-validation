1. **Premise 1:** Emily wants to make necklaces for her 44 friends.
   - Premise 2:** Each necklace requires 2 beads.

Let's use these premises to determine how many beads Emily has:

2. **Step 1:** Calculate the total number of beads needed for all the necklaces.
   \[
   \text{Total beads} = \text{Number of necklaces} \times \text{Beads per necklace}
   \]
   Substituting the given values:
   \[
   \text{Total beads} = 26 \times 2 = 52
   \]

3. **Conclusion:** Therefore, Emily had 52 beads.

The answer is: 52
