To determine how many orchids Jessica cut from her flower garden, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Initial Count of Flowers**: Initially, there were 12 roses and 2 orchids in the vase.
2. **Final Count of Flowers**: After cutting additional flowers, there are now 10 roses and 21 orchids in the vase.

We can set up an equation to represent the situation after Jessica added more flowers:
\[ \text{Number of roses} + \text{Number of orchids} = \text{Total number of flowers after adding} \]
\[ 10 + x = 21 \]

Where \( x \) represents the number of orchids Jessica cut from her garden.

3. **Solve for \( x \)**:
   \[ x = 21 - 10 \]
   \[ x = 11 \]

So, Jessica cut 11 orchids from her flower garden.

The answer is: 11
