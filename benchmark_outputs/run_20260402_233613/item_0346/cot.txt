Premise 1: Faye has 31 packs of pencils.
Premise 2: Each pack contains 6 pencils.
Conclusion: Therefore, Faye has \( 31 \times 6 = 186 \) pencils.

Premise 3: Faye wants to place these pencils into rows with 19 pencils per row.
Premise 4: To find out how many rows she can make, we divide the total number of pencils by the number of pencils per row.
Conclusion: So, \( 186 \div 19 = 9 \) complete rows and there would be some leftover pencils that don't form a full row.

The answer is: 186
