Premise 1: Paco initially had 39 sweet cookies.
Premise 2: Paco initially had 6 salty cookies.
Premise 3: Paco ate 23 salty cookies.
Premise 4: Paco ate 32 sweet cookies.

To determine how many more sweet cookies than salty cookies Paco ate, we need to calculate:
- The number of sweet cookies eaten.
- The number of salty cookies eaten.
- The difference between these two quantities.

Step 1: Calculate the number of salty cookies eaten.
Number of salty cookies eaten = 23

Step 2: Calculate the number of sweet cookies eaten.
Number of sweet cookies eaten = 32

Step 3: Find the difference between the number of sweet cookies eaten and the number of salty cookies eaten.
Difference = Number of sweet cookies eaten - Number of salty cookies eaten
          = 32 - 23
