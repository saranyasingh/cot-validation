To determine how many more sweet cookies than salty cookies Paco ate, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the number of salty cookies Paco ate.
2. Calculate the number of sweet cookies Paco ate.
3. Subtract the number of salty cookies eaten from the number of sweet cookies eaten.

First, let's calculate the number of salty cookies Paco ate:
\[ \text{Number of salty cookies eaten} = 23 \]

Next, let's calculate the number of sweet cookies Paco ate:
\[ \text{Number of sweet cookies eaten} = 32 \]

Now, subtract the number of salty cookies eaten from the number of sweet cookies eaten:
\[ \text{Difference} = 32 - 23 = 9 \]
