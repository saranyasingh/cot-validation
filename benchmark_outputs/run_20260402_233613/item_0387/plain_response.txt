To determine how much the ring toss game made per day in the first 20 days, we need to follow these steps:

1. Identify the total earnings for the first 20 days.
2. Divide the total earnings of the first 20 days by the number of days (which is 20) to find the daily average.

From the problem statement:
- The ring toss game made $120 in the first 20 days.
- We need to find out how much was made per day during this period.

Now, let's calculate the daily average:
\[ \text{Daily average} = \frac{\text{Total earnings}}{\text{Number of days}} = \frac{120}{20} = 6 \]

Therefore, the ring toss game made $6 per day on average in the first 20 days.
