To determine how much was made per day in the first 20 days, we need to follow these steps:

**Premise 1:** The total earnings over 20 days were $120.
   - This establishes our total earnings for the initial period.

**Premise 2:** The total earnings over the next 16 days were $66.
   - This establishes our total earnings for the subsequent period.

**Premise 3:** We want to find out the average daily earnings for the first 20 days.
   - To do this, we divide the total earnings of the first 20 days by the number of days (which is 20).

Now let's perform the calculation:

**Calculation:**
\[ \text{Average daily earnings} = \frac{\text{Total earnings for the first 20 days}}{\text{Number of days in the first 20 days}} \]
\[ \text{Average daily earnings} = \frac{120}{20} \]

**Step-by-step reasoning:**

1. **Total Earnings Calculation:**
   - Total earnings = $120

2. **Days Calculation:**
   - Number of days in the first 20 days = 20

3. **Average Daily Earnings:**
   - Average daily earnings = $\frac{120}{20}$
   - Average daily earnings = 6

Therefore, the answer is:
The ring toss game made an average of $6 per day in the first 20 days at the carnival.
