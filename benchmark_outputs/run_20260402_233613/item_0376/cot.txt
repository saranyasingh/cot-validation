Let's break down the problem using a logical syllogism:

Premise 1: Baker initially had 7 cakes.
Premise 2: Baker also had 148 pastries.
Premise 3: Baker sold 15 cakes.
Premise 4: Baker sold 103 pastries.

To determine how many pastries the baker has left after selling some, we need to follow these steps:

Step 1: Calculate the number of pastries remaining before any were sold.
- Initial number of pastries = 148
- Number of pastries sold = 103

Remaining pastries = Initial pastries - Sold pastries
= 148 - 103
= 45

So, after selling 103 pastries, the baker has 45 pastries left.

Therefore, the baker still has 45 pastries.

The answer is: 45
