Let's break down this problem using a logical syllogism:

**Premise 1:** Kelly currently has 22 Nintendo games.
- This is our starting point and initial condition.

**Premise 2:** Kelly wants to have 140 Nintendo games after making some purchases.
- We want to determine how many additional games Kelly needs to buy to reach her goal.

**Conclusion:** Let \( x \) be the number of games Kelly needs to buy to have 140 games in total.

**Step-by-step logical reasoning:**

1. **Current total games:** 
   - Kelly already has 22 games.
   
2. **Desired total games:**
   - Kelly wants to end up with 140 games.

3. **Calculate the difference between desired and current totals:**
   - To find out how many more games Kelly needs to reach her goal:
     \[
     140 - 22 = 118
     \]
   - Therefore, Kelly needs to acquire 118 more games.

**Final Answer:** The answer is: 118

Kelly needs to buy 118 more Nintendo games to have a total of 140 games.
