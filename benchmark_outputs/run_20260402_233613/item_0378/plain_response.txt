To determine how many Nintendo games Kelly needs to buy in order to have 140 games left after her current collection of 22 games, we can set up an equation:

Let \( x \) be the number of games Kelly needs to buy.

Currently, Kelly has:
\[ 22 \text{ games} \]

After buying \( x \) more games, she should have:
\[ 22 + x \text{ games} \]

We want this total to equal 140 games:
\[ 22 + x = 140 \]

Now, solve for \( x \):
\[ x = 140 - 22 \]
\[ x = 118 \]

Therefore, Kelly needs to buy **118** games to have a total of 140 games. The answer is: 118.
