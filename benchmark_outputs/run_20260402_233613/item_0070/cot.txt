1. **Premise**: Dan had $3 left with him after buying a candy bar.
   - This sets up the initial state and what we know about his remaining money before purchasing the candy bar.

2. **Premise**: He started with $4.
   - This gives us an idea of how much money Dan had initially.

3. **Conclusion**:
   - To find out how much the candy bar cost, we need to subtract the amount he has left ($3) from the amount he started with ($4).
   - Therefore, the cost of the candy bar = Initial amount - Remaining amount

The answer is: $1.
