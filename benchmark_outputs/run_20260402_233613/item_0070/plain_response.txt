To determine how much the candy bar cost, we need to consider the information given:

1. Dan started with $4.
2. After buying the candy bar and having $3 left, we can calculate the total amount spent on the candy bar.

Let's denote:
- The initial amount of money as \( I \)
- The amount spent on the candy bar as \( C \)

Given:
\( I = \$4 \)
\( I - C = \$3 \)

We can solve for \( C \) (the cost of the candy bar):

\( C = I - (\text{amount left}) \)
\( C = \$4 - \$3 \)
\( C = \$1 \)

Therefore, the answer is: The candy bar cost $1.
