To find out how many bottles of regular soda the grocery store had, we need to add the number of diet soda bottles to the additional amount mentioned in the problem statement.

Given:
- Number of diet soda bottles = 4
- Additional bottles of regular soda = 79

Calculation:
Number of regular soda bottles = Number of diet soda bottles + Additional bottles of regular soda
Number of regular soda bottles = 4 + 79
Number of regular soda bottles = 83

Therefore, the answer is: The grocery store had 83 bottles of regular soda.
