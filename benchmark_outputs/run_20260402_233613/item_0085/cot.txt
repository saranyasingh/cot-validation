Premise 1: The grocery store initially had 4 bottles of diet soda.
Premise 2: They had 79 more bottles of regular soda than diet soda.
Premise 3: We need to determine how many bottles of regular soda they had.

Logical Syllogism:
Step 1: Let D represent the number of diet soda bottles and R represent the number of regular soda bottles.
Step 2: According to Premise 1, D = 4.
Step 3: According to Premise 2, R = D + 79.
Step 4: Substitute the value of D into the equation for R.
   R = 4 + 79
Step 5: Perform the addition on the right side of the equation.
   R = 83

Therefore, the grocery store had 83 bottles of regular soda.

The answer is: 83
