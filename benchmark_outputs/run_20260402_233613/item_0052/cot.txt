To determine how many apples the chef used to make the pies, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Identify the initial number of apples**: The chef started with 40 apples.
2. **Determine the number of apples remaining after making pies**: After making pies, the chef has 39 apples left.
3. **Calculate the number of apples used**: Subtract the number of apples remaining from the initial number of apples.

Let's perform this calculation using a logical syllogism:

**Premise 1:** The chef started with 40 apples.
- This sets our starting point.

**Premise 2:** After making pies, the chef has 39 apples left.
- This indicates what remains after some action (making pies).

**Conclusion:** To find out how many apples were used, subtract the number of apples remaining from the initial amount:
\[ \text{Apples used} = \text{Initial apples} - \text{Apples remaining} \]
\[ \text{Apples used} = 40 - 39 \]
\[ \text{Apples used} = 1 \]

Therefore, the chef used 1 apple to make the pies.

The answer is: 1
