Let's break down the problem using a logical syllogism:

**Premise 1:** Dave initially had 19 apps and 18 files on his phone.
- This establishes our starting point with initial quantities.

**Premise 2:** After deleting some apps and files, Dave has 6 apps and 15 files remaining.
- This tells us what remains after certain actions were taken.

**Premise 3:** We need to find how many more files than apps Dave has left on the phone.
- This sets the goal we're trying to achieve.

**Step-by-step Reasoning:**

1. **Initial Quantities:**
   - Apps: 19
   - Files: 18

2. **Remaining Quantities:**
   - Apps: 6
   - Files: 15

3. **Difference Calculation:**
   - To determine how many more files than apps Dave has left:
     \[
     \text{Difference} = (\text{Files}) - (\text{Apps})
     \]
   
4. **Substitute the known values into the formula:**
   \[
   \text{Difference} = 15 - 6
   \]

5. **Perform the subtraction:**
   \[
   \text{Difference} = 9
   \]

**Conclusion:**
Dave has 9 more files than apps left on the phone.

Therefore, the answer is: 9.
