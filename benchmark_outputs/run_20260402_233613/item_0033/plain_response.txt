To determine how many houses are in a block given that the mailman gives out 2 junk mails per house and that he gives out 14 pieces of junk mail in total, we can follow these steps:

1. Let's denote the number of houses in the block as \( H \).
2. According to the problem, the mailman gives out 2 junk mails per house.
3. Therefore, the total number of junk mails distributed can be expressed as:
   \[
   2H = 14
   \]
4. To find \( H \), we need to solve for \( H \) by dividing both sides of the equation by 2:
   \[
   H = \frac{14}{2}
   \]
5. Simplifying the right side of the equation gives us:
   \[
   H = 7
   \]

So, the number of houses in a block is \( 7 \).

The answer is: 7
