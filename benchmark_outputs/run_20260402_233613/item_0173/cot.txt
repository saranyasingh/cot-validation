To determine how many houses are in a block given that the mailman gives 25 pieces of junk mail per block and 5 pieces of mail per house, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Identify the total number of pieces of junk mail per block**: This is given as 25.
2. **Identify the number of pieces of junk mail per house**: This is given as 5.
3. **Set up the relationship between the total number of pieces of junk mail and the number of houses**:
   - Let \( n \) be the number of houses in a block.
   - Each house receives 5 pieces of mail.
   - Therefore, the total number of pieces of mail distributed across all houses is \( 5n \).
4. **Relate this to the total number of pieces of mail given to the entire block**:
   - Since the mailman distributes 25 pieces of mail per block, we have \( 5n = 25 \).

5. **Solve for \( n \)**:
   - Divide both sides of the equation by 5: 
     \[
     n = \frac{25}{5} = 5
     \]

Thus, there are 5 houses in a block.

The answer is: 5
