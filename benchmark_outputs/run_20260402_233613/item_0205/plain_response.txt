To determine how many drums of grapes would be filled in 58 days, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the number of drums filled per day.
2. Multiply the daily production by the number of days (58).

Step 1: Number of drums filled per day
Given that each picker fills 108 drums per day and there are 252 pickers:
\[ \text{Total drums per day} = 108 \times 252 \]

Step 2: Calculate for 58 days
Now, multiply the total drums filled per day by 58:
\[ \text{Total drums in 58 days} = (\text{Total drums per day}) \times 58 \]

Let's perform the calculations:

First, calculate the total drums per day:
\[ 108 \times 252 = 27336 \]

Then, calculate the total drums in 58 days:
\[ 27336 \times 58 = 1595448 \]

So, the total number of drums of grapes that would be filled in 58 days is \( 1,595,448 \).

The answer is: 1,595,448
