To determine how many balloons Jake brought to the park, we can follow these steps:

1. We know that Allan and Jake together brought 3 balloons.
2. Allan brought 2 balloons.
3. To find out how many balloons Jake brought, we subtract the number of balloons Allan brought from the total number of balloons they both brought:
   \[
   \text{Number of balloons Jake brought} = \text{Total number of balloons} - \text{Number of balloons Allan brought}
   \]
   Substituting the known values:
   \[
   \text{Number of balloons Jake brought} = 3 - 2 = 1
   \]

Therefore, Jake brought 1 balloon to the park.
