**Logical Syllogism**

1. **Premise 1:** Sofia asked the students to suggest specific types of food.
   - This premise establishes the context of the situation and provides the initial condition for our calculation.

2. **Premise 2:** 144 students suggested adding mashed potatoes.
   - This premise introduces the number of suggestions related to mashed potatoes as part of the given data.

3. **Premise 3:** 467 students suggested adding bacon to the menu.
   - This premise adds another piece of information about other options suggested by students.

4. **Premise 4:** 79 students suggested adding tomatoes.
   - This premise gives us the third piece of information about tomato-related suggestions.

5. **Conclusion:** We need to find out how many more students suggested mashed potatoes than those that suggested tomatoes.
   - To do this, we subtract the number of students who suggested tomatoes from the number of students who suggested mashed potatoes:
     \[
     \text{Difference} = 144 (\text{suggestions for mashed potatoes}) - 79 (\text{suggestions for tomatoes})
     \]
     \[
     \text{Difference} = 65
     \]

Therefore, **The answer is: 65**.
