To find out how many more students suggested mashed potatoes than those that suggested tomatoes, we need to subtract the number of students who suggested tomatoes from the number of students who suggested mashed potatoes.

Number of students who suggested mashed potatoes = 144
Number of students who suggested tomatoes = 79

Difference = Number of students who suggested mashed potatoes - Number of students who suggested tomatoes
                = 144 - 79
                = 65

Therefore, 65 more students suggested mashed potatoes than those that suggested tomatoes.

The answer is: 65
