Let's break down this problem into a logical syllogism:

**Premise 1:** Jerry initially had 8 action figures.
- This establishes the starting point of our count.

**Premise 2:** Jerry also had 9 books.
- This introduces another set of items Jerry already possessed before adding anything else.

**Premise 3:** Jerry then added 10 more books to the shelf.
- This indicates an addition to the existing collection.

**Conclusion:** We need to determine the total number of books on the shelf after adding the new ones.

To find out how many books are on the shelf now, we add:
- The initial number of books (9) + the additional books (10)

So, let’s perform the calculation:
\[ \text{Total books} = 9 (\text{initial books}) + 10 (\text{additional books}) \]
\[ \text{Total books} = 19 \]

Therefore, the answer is: **The total number of books on his shelf is 19.**

This conclusion logically follows because it directly results from the addition of the extra books to the initial collection.
