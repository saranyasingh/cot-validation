To determine the total number of books Jerry has on his shelf after adding more, we can follow these steps:

1. Identify the initial number of books Jerry had.
   - Jerry initially had 9 books.

2. Determine how many additional books Jerry added later.
   - Jerry added 10 more books.

3. Add the initial number of books to the number of books added later.
   - Initial books + Additional books = Total books

Now let's perform the calculation:
9 (initial books) + 10 (additional books) = 19 books

Therefore, the total number of books Jerry has on his shelf is 19.

The answer is: 19
