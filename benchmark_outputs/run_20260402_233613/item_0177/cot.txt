To determine how many kids Julia played tag with altogether, we need to follow these steps:

### Step-by-Step Logical Reasoning (Logical Syllogism):

**Premise 1:** Julia played tag with 7 kids on Monday.
- **Premise 2:** Julia played tag with 13 kids on Tuesday.
- **Conclusion:** Therefore, Julia played tag with a total of \(7 + 13\) kids over both days.

**Explanation:**
1. We start with the information given about the number of kids Julia played tag with on two specific days.
2. To find out the total number of kids she played tag with, we add the numbers from both days together.

So, the calculation proceeds as follows:
\[ \text{Total number of kids} = 7 + 13 \]

Performing the addition:
\[ 7 + 13 = 20 \]

Thus, the answer is:
The answer is: 20

This conclusion is derived directly from adding the number of kids tagged on Monday and Tuesday, which gives us the total number of kids Julia played tag with over those two days.
