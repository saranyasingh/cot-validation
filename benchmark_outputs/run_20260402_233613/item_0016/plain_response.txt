To determine how many rows Faye can make with her pencils, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the total number of pencils Faye has.
2. Determine how many rows she can make with the total number of pencils, given that each row contains 2 pencils.

Step 1: Calculate the total number of pencils.
- Faye has 35 packs of pencils.
- Each pack contains 4 pencils.
- Therefore, the total number of pencils is \( 35 \times 4 = 140 \) pencils.

Step 2: Determine how many rows she can make.
- Each row contains 2 pencils.
- To find out how many rows she can make, divide the total number of pencils by the number of pencils per row:
  \[
  \frac{140}{2} = 70
  \]

So, Faye can make 70 rows with her pencils. The answer is: 70.
