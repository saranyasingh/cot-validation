To determine how many roses Jessica cut, we can set up an equation based on the information given:

Initially, there were:
- 15 roses
- 62 orchids

After cutting additional flowers, the number of flowers became:
- 17 roses
- 96 orchids

Let's denote the number of roses Jessica cut as \( x \) and the number of orchids she cut as \( y \).

We know that the total number of flowers after cutting remains constant, so we have two equations:
1. The initial number of roses plus the number of roses cut equals the new total number of roses:
\[ 15 + x = 17 \]

2. The initial number of orchids plus the number of orchids cut equals the new total number of orchids:
\[ 62 + y = 96 \]

First, solve for \( x \):
\[ 15 + x = 17 \]
Subtract 15 from both sides:
\[ x = 17 - 15 \]
\[ x = 2 \]

Next, solve for \( y \):
\[ 62 + y = 96 \]
Subtract 62 from both sides:
\[ y = 96 - 62 \]
\[ y = 34 \]

So, Jessica cut 2 roses and 34 orchids.

The answer is: 2 roses.
