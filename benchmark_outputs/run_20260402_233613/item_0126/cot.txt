Premise 1: Bryan has a total of 38 books.
Premise 2: Each bookshelf contains 2 books.
Conclusion: Therefore, to determine how many bookshelves Bryan has, we need to divide the total number of books by the number of books per bookshelf.

Logical Syllogism:
1. Total number of books = 38
2. Number of books per bookshelf = 2
3. To find the number of bookshelves, we perform the division: 38 ÷ 2

Calculation:
4. 38 ÷ 2 = 19

Therefore, Bryan has 19 bookshelves.

The answer is: 19 bookshelves.
