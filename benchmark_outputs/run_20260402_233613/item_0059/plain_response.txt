To find out how much money was made from selling t-shirts during the Texas Tech game, we can follow these steps:

1. Calculate the number of t-shirts sold during the Texas Tech game:
   Total t-shirts sold = T-shirts sold in Arkansas + T-shirts sold in Texas Tech
   Let \( x \) be the number of t-shirts sold during the Texas Tech game.
   Given that total t-shirts sold = 186 and T-shirts sold in Arkansas = 172,
   then \( 172 + x = 186 \).

2. Solve for \( x \):
   \( x = 186 - 172 \)
   \( x = 14 \)

So, 14 t-shirts were sold during the Texas Tech game.

3. Calculate the money made from selling those t-shirts:
   Money made per t-shirt = $78
   Therefore, money made from 14 t-shirts = \( 14 \times 78 \)

Let's do the multiplication to get the exact amount:

\( 14 \times 78 = 1092 \)

Therefore, the answer is: The Razorback t-shirt shop made $1092 from selling t-shirts during the Texas Tech game.
