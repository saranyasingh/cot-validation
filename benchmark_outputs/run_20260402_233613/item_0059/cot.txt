Let's break down this problem using a logical syllogism:

**Premise 1:** The Razorback t-shirt shop makes $78 off each t-shirt sold.
- This establishes the profit per t-shirt.
  
**Premise 2:** During the Arkansas game and the Texas Tech game they sold a total of 186 t-shirts.
- This gives us the total number of t-shirts sold over both games.

**Premise 3:** They sold 172 t-shirts during the Arkansas game.
- We need to find out how many were sold during the Texas Tech game.

**Logical Reasoning:**

From Premise 3:
- Total t-shirts sold = Arkansas game + Texas Tech game
- Let \( T \) represent the number of t-shirts sold during the Texas Tech game.
- Therefore, we have:
\[ 172 (Arkansas game) + T (Texas Tech game) = 186 \]

Solving for \( T \):
\[ T = 186 - 172 \]
\[ T = 14 \]

So, they sold 14 t-shirts during the Texas Tech game.

**Conclusion:**
To find out how much money they made from selling these t-shirts during the Texas Tech game, multiply the number of t-shirts sold by the profit per t-shirt:
\[ \text{Money made} = 14 \times 78 \]

Calculating:
\[ 14 \times 78 = 1092 \]

Therefore, the Razorback t-shirt shop made $1092 from selling the t-shirts during the Texas Tech game.

**Answer:** The answer is: 1092
