Let's break down this problem into a series of logical steps:

**Premise 1:** Kelly currently has 20 Nintendo games.
- This establishes the starting point of our calculation.

**Premise 2:** Kelly wants to end up with 12 Nintendo games.
- This sets her desired outcome, which we aim to reach through giving away some games.

**Premise 3:** She currently has 70 Sony games.
- This gives us an idea of how many games she has overall but doesn't affect her desire to reduce the number of Nintendo games directly.

**Step-by-step Logical Reasoning:**

1. **Initial State Calculation:**
   - Let \( N \) represent the initial number of Nintendo games Kelly has.
   - Given: \( N = 20 \).

2. **Desired Final State:**
   - Let \( F \) represent the final number of Nintendo games Kelly wants.
   - Given: \( F = 12 \).

3. **Number of Nintendo Games to Give Away:**
   - To find out how many Nintendo games Kelly needs to give away, subtract the desired final state from the current total:
     \[
     \text{Games to give away} = N - F
     \]
   - Plugging in the values:
     \[
     \text{Games to give away} = 20 - 12 = 8
     \]

4. **Verification:**
   - If Kelly gives away 8 Nintendo games, she will have:
     \[
     20 - 8 = 12 \text{ Nintendo games}
     \]
   - This matches her goal of having exactly 12 Nintendo games left.

Therefore, the answer is: The number of Nintendo games Kelly needs to give away is 8.
