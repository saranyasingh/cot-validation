To determine how many shirts the machine made today, we need to break down the problem into smaller steps and use logical reasoning based on the given information.

### Step-by-Step Logical Reasoning:

#### Premise 1:
The industrial machine makes 6 shirts per minute.

**Explanation:** This establishes the rate at which the machine operates.

#### Premise 2:
The machine worked for 12 minutes today.

**Explanation:** This gives us the duration for which the machine was operational today.

#### Step 3:
Calculate the number of shirts made during today's work period using the rate established in Premise 1.

**Calculation:**
\[ \text{Number of shirts made today} = (\text{Rate}) \times (\text{Time}) \]
\[ \text{Number of shirts made today} = 6 \text{ shirts/minute} \times 12 \text{ minutes} \]

#### Step 4:
Perform the multiplication to find the total number of shirts made today.

**Calculation:**
\[ \text{Number of shirts made today} = 72 \]

Therefore, the machine made **72 shirts** today.
