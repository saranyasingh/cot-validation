To determine how many shirts the industrial machine made today, we need to follow these steps:

1. Identify the rate at which the machine makes shirts per minute.
2. Calculate the number of shirts made in one day based on this rate.

From the problem statement:
- The machine makes 6 shirts per minute.
- Today, it worked for 12 minutes.

Now, let's calculate the total number of shirts made today:
\[ \text{Shirts made today} = \text{Rate} \times \text{Time (minutes)} \]
\[ \text{Shirts made today} = 6 \text{ shirts/minute} \times 12 \text{ minutes} \]

Performing the multiplication:
\[ \text{Shirts made today} = 72 \text{ shirts} \]

Therefore, the answer is: 72.
