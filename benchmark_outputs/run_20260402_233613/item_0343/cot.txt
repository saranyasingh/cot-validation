To determine how much money Fred and Jason have together now, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the total amount of money Fred has after washing cars.
2. Determine the total amount of money Jason has since he didn't work.
3. Add the amounts of money Fred and Jason have to find their combined total.

**Step 1:** Fred had $86 initially but ended up with $9 after washing cars. So, Fred now has:
\[ 86 - (86 - 9) = 86 - 77 = 9 \text{ dollars} \]

**Step 2:** Jason had $5 initially but didn't work over the weekend, so Jason still has:
\[ 5 \text{ dollars} \]

\[ 9 + 5 = 14 \text{ dollars} \]
