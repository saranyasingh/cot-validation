To determine how much each candy bar costs, we can follow these steps:

1. Dan started with $3.
2. He spent $4 on 2 candy bars.
3. To find out how much he spent per candy bar, we divide the total amount spent by the number of candy bars:
   \[
   \text{Cost per candy bar} = \frac{\$4}{2} = \$2
   \]

So, each candy bar cost $2.

The answer is: $2
