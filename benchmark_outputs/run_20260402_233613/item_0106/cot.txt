To determine how many drums of grapes would be filled per day based on the information provided, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Identify the total number of drums filled and the total time period**:
   - Total drums filled = 90 drums
   - Time period (days) = 6 days

2. **Calculate the daily average**:
   - To find out how many drums are filled per day, divide the total number of drums by the total number of days.
   \[
   \text{Drums per day} = \frac{\text{Total drums}}{\text{Total days}}
   \]
   Substituting the given values:
   \[
   \text{Drums per day} = \frac{90}{6}
   \]

3. **Perform the division**:
   \[
   \frac{90}{6} = 15
   \]

Therefore, the answer is: 15 drums of grapes would be filled per day.
