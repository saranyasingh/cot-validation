INF-1: Given the total number of drums filled (90) and the total time period (6 days),
the daily average can be calculated using Division Rule.
Division Rule states that if \( A / B = C \), then \( A = B * C \).
In our case,
\[
\text{Drums per day} = \frac{90}{6}
\] 
Performing the division gives us:
\[
\text{Drums per day} = 15
\]

Thus, the answer is: 15 drums of grapes would be filled per day.
