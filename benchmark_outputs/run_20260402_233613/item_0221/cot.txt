Premise 1: Ed had 5 more marbles than Doug.
Premise 2: Doug lost 3 of his marbles at the playground.
Premise 3: If Ed had 27 marbles.

To logically derive how many marbles Doug had initially:

Step 1: From Premise 1 and Step 1, we know that if Ed had 27 marbles, then Doug must have had 27 - 5 = 22 marbles before Doug lost any marbles. This is because Ed has 5 more marbles than Doug, so to find out how many marbles Doug had initially, we subtract 5 from the number of marbles Ed had.

Step 2: We also know from Premise 2 that after losing 3 marbles, Doug would have 22 - 3 = 19 marbles left.

The answer is: Doug had 19 marbles initially.
