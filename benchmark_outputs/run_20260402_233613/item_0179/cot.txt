1. **Premise**: Rebecca wants to split her collection of eggs into 4 groups.
   - This sets up the goal of dividing the eggs equally among 4 groups.

2. **Premise**: Rebecca has 8 eggs.
   - This gives us the total number of eggs available for distribution.

3. **Premise**: Rebecca also has 6 marbles.
   - This introduces another item (marbles) that we need to consider but does not affect the egg count directly.

4. **Logical Deduction**:
   - To find out how many eggs will be in each group, we need to divide the total number of eggs by the number of groups.
   - Therefore, \( \frac{8}{4} = 2 \).

5. **Conclusion**:
   - Each group will contain 2 eggs.

The answer is: Each group will have 2 eggs.
