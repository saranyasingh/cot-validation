Let's break down the problem step by step:

1. Initially, Jerry had 3 action figures.
2. He then added 4 more action figures to the shelf.
   - So after adding, he has \(3 + 4 = 7\) action figures.
3. Now, he removes some of these action figures.
4. After removing some, he ends up with 6 action figures left.

To find out how many action figures he removed, we need to subtract the number of action figures he ended up with (6) from the total he had before removing any (7).

So, the calculation would be:
\[ \text{Action figures removed} = 7 - 6 = 1 \]

Therefore, the answer is: 1.
