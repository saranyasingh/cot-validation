Let's convert the given chain-of-thought reasoning into structured First-Order Logic (FOL):

### Facts
These represent the information explicitly stated in the premise.

#### Fact-1: Initial Amount of Money
\[ \text{InitialMoney} ::= P = \$128 \]

### Rules
These represent logical deductions based on the facts provided.

#### Rule-1: Remaining Money After Purchase
\[ \text{RemainingMoney} ::= \forall x (\text{Purchased}(x) \rightarrow \text{RemainingMoney}(x)) \]
Where:
- \(\text{Purchased}(x)\) denotes that an item \(x\) was purchased.
- \(\text{RemainingMoney}(x)\) denotes the remaining money after purchasing item \(x\).

Using the above rules, we derive the conclusion step-by-step.

### Inferences
These represent the logical conclusions derived from the facts using appropriate inference rules.

#### Inf-1: Remaining Money After Spending $38
\[ \text{RemainingMoney} ::= \text{RemainingMoney} \left( \text{Purchased}(groceries) \right) \]
This infers that since groceries were purchased, the remaining money is calculated by subtracting the amount spent ($38) from the initial amount ($128). 

Substituting the values:
\[ \text{RemainingMoney} = \$128 - \$38 = \$90 \]

Thus, the final remaining amount of money Olivia has is:
\[ \boxed{\$90} \]
