To determine how much money Olivia has left after spending $38 on groceries, we need to follow these steps:

### Premise 1:
- Olivia started with $128 in her wallet.
- She then made a purchase of $38 at the supermarket.

We'll denote the initial amount as \( P \) and the amount spent as \( S \).

Premise 2:
\[ P = \$128 \]

Premise 3:
\[ S = \$38 \]

### Logical Reasoning:
**Step 1:** We know that Olivia's total amount of money before making any purchases was $128. This means her initial balance in her wallet is exactly what she had when she went into the store.

**Step 2:** When Olivia makes a purchase, it reduces her available funds. Therefore, if she spent $38, the remaining amount of money she has is calculated by subtracting the amount spent from the initial amount.

**Step 3:** Mathematically, this can be expressed as:
\[ \text{Remaining Money} = P - S \]

Substituting the values from Premises 1 and 2:
\[ \text{Remaining Money} = \$128 - \$38 \]

### Calculation:
\[ \text{Remaining Money} = \$90 \]

### Conclusion:
After accounting for the $38 spent at the supermarket, Olivia has $90 left in her wallet.

The answer is: **$90**
