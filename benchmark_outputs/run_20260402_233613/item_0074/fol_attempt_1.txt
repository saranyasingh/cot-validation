- Fact-2: Each house receives 32 pieces of junk mail :: MailPerHouse(32)

### Rules
- Rule-1: If there are \( n \) houses, then each house gets \( m \) pieces of mail :: Mail(n, m)

- INF-2: Total pieces of junk mail = Number of houses × Mail per house :: TotalJunkMail(Houses, MailPerHouse)
