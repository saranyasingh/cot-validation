To determine how many pieces of junk mail the mailman should give to each block, we need to multiply the number of houses in one block by the number of pieces of junk mail given per house.

Number of houses in a block = 20
Pieces of junk mail per house = 32

Total pieces of junk mail for one block = Number of houses × Pieces of junk mail per house
= 20 × 32
= 640

Therefore, the mailman should give 640 pieces of junk mail to each block. 

The answer is: 640
