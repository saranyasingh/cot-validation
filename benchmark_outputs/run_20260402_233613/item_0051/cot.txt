To determine how many pages are in each chapter, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total number of pages in the book:**  
   Since the book has 555 pages and they are equally distributed over 5 chapters, we divide the total number of pages by the number of chapters.
   
   \[
   \text{Total pages} = 555
   \]
   \[
   \text{Number of chapters} = 5
   \]

   \[
   \text{Pages per chapter} = \frac{\text{Total pages}}{\text{Number of chapters}} = \frac{555}{5}
   \]

2. **Perform the division:**  
   To find out how many pages are in each chapter, we perform the division:
   
   \[
   \frac{555}{5} = 111
   \]

Therefore, there are 111 pages in each chapter.

The answer is: 111
