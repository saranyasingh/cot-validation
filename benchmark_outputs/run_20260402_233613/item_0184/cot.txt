Let's break down this problem into a logical syllogism:

**Premise 1:** Paco initially had 12 cookies.
- This establishes our starting point with the total number of cookies Paco has before any actions occur.

**Premise 2:** Paco ate 16 cookies.
- This action changes the quantity of cookies from 12 to 16, indicating that some cookies were consumed.

**Premise 3:** Paco then gave 21 cookies to his friend.
- This action involves transferring 21 cookies from Paco’s possession to his friend’s possession.

**Premise 4:** We need to find out how many more cookies Paco gave to his friend than those he ate.
- This comparison requires us to subtract the number of cookies eaten (16) from the number given away (21).

**Conclusion:** By applying subtraction:
\[ \text{Cookies given} - \text{Cookies eaten} = 21 - 16 = 5 \]

Therefore, the answer is: 5.
