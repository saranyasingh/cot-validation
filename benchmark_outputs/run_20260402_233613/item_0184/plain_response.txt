Let's break down the problem step by step:

1. Paco initially had 12 cookies.
2. He ate 16 cookies.
3. He gave 21 cookies to his friend.

Now, let's calculate how many more cookies he gave to his friend compared to what he ate:
- Number of cookies given to friend = 21
- Number of cookies eaten = 16

Difference = Number of cookies given - Number of cookies eaten
= 21 - 16
= 5

Therefore, Paco gave 5 more cookies to his friend than those he ate.

The answer is: 5
