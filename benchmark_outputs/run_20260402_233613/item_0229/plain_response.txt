To determine how many marbles Haley had initially, we need to calculate the total number of marbles distributed to all the boys in her class.

Given:
- Number of boys = 11
- Marbles given to each boy = 9

We can find the total number of marbles by multiplying the number of boys by the number of marbles each boy received:

Total marbles = Number of boys × Marbles per boy
Total marbles = 11 × 9
Total marbles = 99

Therefore, Haley had 99 marbles initially. The answer is: 99.
