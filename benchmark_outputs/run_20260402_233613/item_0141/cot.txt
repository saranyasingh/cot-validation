To determine how many goldfish disappeared, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Initial Goldfish Count**: Paige originally had 8 goldfish in the pond.
2. **Goldfish Disappearance**: Stray cats ate some of the goldfish and left only 6 goldfish behind.
3. **Calculate Disappeared Goldfish**: Subtract the number of goldfish left after the strays from the initial count.

The logical reasoning can be represented as follows:

**Premise 1:** Paige initially had 8 goldfish in the pond.
- Premise 2: After the strays ate some fish, there were 6 goldfish remaining.
- Conclusion: Therefore, \( \text{Number of disappeared goldfish} = \text{Initial goldfish count} - \text{Remaining goldfish} \).

By substituting the values:
\[ \text{Number of disappeared goldfish} = 8 - 6 \]

Thus,
\[ \text{Number of disappeared goldfish} = 2 \]

Therefore, the answer is: 2 goldfish disappeared.
