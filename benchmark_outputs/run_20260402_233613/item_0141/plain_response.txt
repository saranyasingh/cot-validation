To determine how many goldfish disappeared, we need to consider both the initial number of goldfish and the additional goldfish that were eaten by stray cats.

1. Initially, there were 8 goldfish in the pond.
2. After the cats ate some goldfish, only 6 goldfish remained.

To find out how many goldfish disappeared, subtract the remaining number of goldfish from the initial number:

\[ \text{Goldfish that disappeared} = \text{Initial goldfish} - \text{Remaining goldfish} \]
\[ \text{Goldfish that disappeared} = 8 - 6 \]
\[ \text{Goldfish that disappeared} = 2 \]

Therefore, the answer is: 2
