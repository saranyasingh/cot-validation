To determine how many more hours Ryan spends on learning English compared to Chinese, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate total study time for each language:**
   - English:
     \[
     6 \text{ hours/day}
     \]
   
   - Chinese:
     \[
     3 \text{ hours/day}
     \]

2. **Find the difference in study times between English and Chinese:**
   \[
   \text{Difference} = \text{English Study Time} - \text{Chinese Study Time}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Difference} = 6 \text{ hours/day} - 3 \text{ hours/day}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Difference} = 3 \text{ hours/day}
   \]

Thus, Ryan spends \(3\) more hours studying English than he does studying Chinese daily. The answer is: 3.
