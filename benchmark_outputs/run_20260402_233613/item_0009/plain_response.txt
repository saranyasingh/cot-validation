To solve this problem, let's break it down step by step:

1. We know that Dave initially had some tickets.
2. He spent 12 tickets on toys.
3. After spending these 12 tickets, he had 14 tickets left.

Let's denote the initial number of tickets Dave won as \( x \).

According to the information given:
\[ x - 12 = 14 \]

Now, we need to solve for \( x \):
\[ x = 14 + 12 \]
\[ x = 26 \]

So, Dave originally won 26 tickets at the arcade.
