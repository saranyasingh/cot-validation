Premise 1: Dave had won some tickets.
Premise 2: Dave used 12 tickets to buy some toys.
Premise 3: After buying the toys, Dave has 14 tickets left.

The logical syllogism:

Step 1: From Premise 1 and Premise 2, we know that Dave initially had more than 12 tickets because he bought toys with those tickets.
Step 2: We also know from Premise 3 that after spending 12 tickets, Dave has 14 tickets remaining.
Step 3: Therefore, if we add the number of tickets he spent (12) to the number of tickets he has left (14), we get his initial total number of tickets before buying the toys.
Step 4: Calculating this sum gives us: 12 + 14 = 26

Conclusion:
Therefore, Dave won 26 tickets at the arcade.
