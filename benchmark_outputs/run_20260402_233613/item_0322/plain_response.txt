To determine how many books Paul sold at the garage sale, we can set up an equation based on the information given:

Let \( x \) represent the number of books Paul sold.

Initially, Paul had:
\[ 2 \text{ books} \]

After selling \( x \) books and buying 150 new ones, he had:
\[ 2 - x + 150 = 58 \text{ books} \]

Now, solve for \( x \):

\[
2 - x + 150 = 58
\]

Combine like terms:

\[
152 - x = 58
\]

Subtract 152 from both sides to isolate \( x \):

\[
-x = 58 - 152
\]

Simplify the right side:

\[
-x = -94
\]

Multiply both sides by -1 to solve for \( x \):

\[
x = 94
\]

Therefore, Paul sold 94 books. 

The answer is: 94
