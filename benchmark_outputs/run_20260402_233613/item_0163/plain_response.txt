Let's denote the number of campers who went rowing in the morning as \( x \).

According to the problem, the number of campers who went rowing in the morning plus the number of campers who went rowing in the afternoon equals the total number of campers who went rowing that day. This can be expressed as:
\[ x + 7 = 60 \]

To find \( x \), we need to isolate it on one side of the equation. We do this by subtracting 7 from both sides of the equation:
\[ x + 7 - 7 = 60 - 7 \]
\[ x = 53 \]

Therefore, the number of campers who went rowing in the morning is \( 53 \).

The answer is: 53
