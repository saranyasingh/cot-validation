Let's break down the problem using a logical syllogism:

1. **Premise 1:** Some campers went rowing in the morning.
   - This means there exists at least one camper who went rowing in the morning.

2. **Premise 2:** 7 campers went rowing in the afternoon.
   - This confirms that there were 7 specific campers who went rowing after noon.

3. **Premise 3:** A total of 60 campers went rowing that day.
   - This gives us the total number of campers involved in rowing activities on that particular day.

4. **Premise 4:** Let \( M \) be the number of campers who went rowing in the morning.
   - We need to find the value of \( M \).

5. **Premise 5:** Therefore, the number of campers who went rowing in the morning plus the number of campers who went rowing in the afternoon equals the total number of campers who went rowing:
   \[
   M + 7 = 60
   \]

6. **Step-by-step Reasoning:**
   - From Premise 5, we know that the sum of the morning and afternoon participants equals 60.
   - To isolate \( M \), we subtract 7 (the number of campers who went rowing in the afternoon) from both sides of the equation:
     \[
     M = 60 - 7
     \]
   - Simplifying this, we get:
     \[
     M = 53
     \]

**Conclusion:**
The number of campers who went rowing in the morning is 53.

**Answer:**
The answer is: 53.
