Premise 1: Julia played tag with 5 kids on Monday.
Premise 2: Julia played tag with 9 kids on Tuesday.
Premise 3: Julia played tag with 15 kids on Wednesday.
Premise 4: We need to find out how many kids Julia played with on Monday and Wednesday combined.

Logical Syllogism:
Step 1: From Premise 1, we know that Julia played with 5 kids on Monday.
Step 2: From Premise 3, we know that Julia played with 15 kids on Wednesday.
Step 3: To find the total number of kids Julia played with on both days, we add the number of kids from Monday and Wednesday together.
Step 4: Therefore, the total number of kids Julia played with on Monday and Wednesday is 5 + 15 = 20.

The answer is: 20.
