To determine how many books are in each bookshelf when Bryan distributes 34 books equally among 2 shelves, you can use simple division:

Total number of books = 34
Number of bookshelves = 2

Books per shelf = Total number of books ÷ Number of bookshelves
                   = 34 ÷ 2
                   = 17

Therefore, the answer is: There are 17 books in each bookshelf.
