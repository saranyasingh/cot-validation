To determine how many more crayons Paul gave to his friends than those he lost, we need to subtract the number of crayons he lost from the number of crayons he gave away.

Step-by-step calculation:
1. Number of crayons given away = 308
2. Number of crayons lost = 231

Now, calculate the difference:
308 - 231 = 77

Therefore, Paul gave away 77 more crayons than he lost. The answer is: 77.
