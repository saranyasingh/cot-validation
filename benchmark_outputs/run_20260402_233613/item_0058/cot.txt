Let's break down the problem using a logical syllogism:

**Premise 1:** Paul started with 267 crayons.
- This establishes the initial quantity of crayons Paul had before any losses or giveaways occurred.

**Premise 2:** Paul lost 231 crayons during the school year.
- This introduces an event that affected the number of crayons Paul had left after losing some.

**Premise 3:** Paul gave away 308 crayons to his friends.
- This indicates another action taken by Paul regarding his crayon supply.

To find out how many more crayons he gave to his friends than those he lost, we need to calculate:
- Total crayons given away = 308
- Total crayons lost = 231

**Calculation:**
Total crayons given away - Total crayons lost = \( 308 - 231 \)

Performing the subtraction gives us:
\( 308 - 231 = 77 \)

Therefore, Paul gave away 77 more crayons than he lost.

The answer is: 77
