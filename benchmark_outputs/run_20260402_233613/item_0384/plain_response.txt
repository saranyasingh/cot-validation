To determine how many games Melissa played, we can use the information given:

1. Melissa scores 12 points per game.
2. She scored a total of 36 points.

We need to find out how many times 12 goes into 36. This can be calculated using division:

\[ \text{Number of games} = \frac{\text{Total points}}{\text{Points per game}} \]

Plugging in the values:

\[ \text{Number of games} = \frac{36}{12} = 3 \]

Therefore, the answer is: 3.
