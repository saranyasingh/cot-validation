To determine how many games Melissa played given her scoring pattern and total points, we can use basic arithmetic to form a logical syllogism:

1. **Premise 1**: Melissa scores 12 points per game.
   - This establishes a consistent rate at which she scores points.

2. **Premise 2**: Melissa has a total score of 36 points.
   - This gives us the sum of all her points over time.

3. **Conclusion**:
   - To find out how many games she played, divide the total points by the points per game.
   - Mathematically, this translates into dividing 36 (total points) by 12 (points per game).

4. **Calculation**:
   \[
   \text{Number of games} = \frac{\text{Total points}}{\text{Points per game}} = \frac{36}{12}
   \]

5. **Simplification**:
   \[
   \frac{36}{12} = 3
   \]

Therefore, the answer is: 
The answer is: 3

This conclusion logically follows because if Melissa scores 12 points per game and has a total of 36 points, then she must have played 3 games to reach that total.
