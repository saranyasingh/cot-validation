**Premise 1:** Last week Fred had 49 dollars.

**Premise 2:** Fred washed cars over the weekend and now has 112 dollars.
- This tells us that Fred earned some additional money through his car washing activities during the weekend.

**Premise 3:** Jason delivered newspapers and now has 63 dollars.
- This provides information about Jason’s current earnings from delivering newspapers.

**Conclusion to find out how much money Jason earned by delivering newspapers:**

To determine how much money Jason earned by delivering newspapers, let's subtract the amount he originally had (which we know was 3 dollars) from the total amount he currently has (which is 63 dollars).

Here's the logical deduction:
- Let \( J \) be the amount Jason earned by delivering newspapers.
- According to Premise 3, Jason's current amount (\( 63 \)) is equal to his original amount plus what he earned from delivering newspapers (\( J \)).
- Therefore, we can write the equation: \( 63 = 3 + J \).

Now, solve for \( J \):
\[ 63 - 3 = J \]
\[ J = 60 \]

So, Jason earned $60 by delivering newspapers.
