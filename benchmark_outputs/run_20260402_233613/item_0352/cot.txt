Let's break down the problem into a series of logical steps:

1. **Initial Condition**: Rachel’s tree initially has 4 apples.
   - Premise 1: Rachel's tree starts with 4 apples.

2. **Action Taken**: Rachel picked 2 apples from her tree.
   - Premise 2: After picking, she removes 2 apples from the tree.

3. **Result of Action**: The number of apples left after picking would be:
   \[
   4 - 2 = 2 \text{ apples}
   \]

4. **New Growth**: Subsequently, 3 new apples grew on the tree.
   - Premise 3: New growth adds to the existing count.

5. **Final Count**: To find out how many apples are on the tree now, we add the newly grown apples to the remaining apples:
   \[
   2 + 3 = 5 \text{ apples}
   \]

**Conclusion**: Therefore, the total number of apples on the tree now is 5.

The answer is: 5
