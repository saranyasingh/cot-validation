Let's break down the problem step by step:

1. Olivia initially had $3 in her wallet.
2. She collected $49 from an ATM.
   - Total amount after collecting from ATM: \(3 + 49 = 52\) dollars.
3. After visiting the supermarket, she had $49 left.
   - Amount spent at the supermarket: \(52 - 49 = 3\) dollars.

Now, we need to find out how much more money she collected at the ATM compared to what she spent at the supermarket:
- Money collected at the ATM: $49.
- Money spent at the supermarket: $3.
- Difference: \(49 - 3 = 46\) dollars.

Therefore, the answer is: The difference between the amount of money collected at the ATM and the amount spent at the supermarket is 46 dollars.
