**Logical Syllogism**

1. **Premise 1:** Jake has 6 fewer peaches than Steven.
   - Premise 2: Jake has 8 more apples than Steven.
   - Conclusion: Therefore, Jake must also have 6 fewer peaches than Steven because he already knows he has 8 more apples.

2. **Premise 3:** Steven has 17 peaches.
   - From Premise 1 and Premise 2, we know that Jake has 6 fewer peaches than Steven.
   - Therefore, if Steven has 17 peaches, then Jake must have \(17 - 6 = 11\) peaches.

3. **Premise 4:** Steven has 16 apples.
   - Since Jake has 8 more apples than Steven, and we found out Jake has 16 apples (from his 16 apples), this aligns with our conclusion that Jake's total number of apples matches Steven’s.

4. **Conclusion:** Based on all premises, we determine that Steven has 16 apples.
   
5. **Comparison:** To find how many more peaches than apples Steven has:
   - Number of peaches Steven has: 16
   - Number of apples Steven has: 16
   
6. **Calculation:** 
   \[
   \text{Difference} = \text{Number of peaches} - \text{Number of apples}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Difference} = 16 - 16 = 0
   \]

**The answer is:** 0

Steven has the same number of peaches as apples.
