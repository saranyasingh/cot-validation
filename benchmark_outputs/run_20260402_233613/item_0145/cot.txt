**Logical Syllogism**

1. **Premise 1**: There were initially 13 roses in the vase.
   - This establishes the starting point and the number of roses before any additional cutting occurred.

2. **Premise 2**: Jessica added some roses to the vase, bringing the total to 21 roses.
   - This introduces an action (adding roses) that changed the quantity in the vase.

3. **Conclusion**: To find out how many roses are left in the garden after Jessica's addition, we need to determine what happened to the roses originally present in the vase.
   
4. **Step-by-Step Reasoning**:
   - Let \( x \) be the number of roses Jessica cut from her garden.
   - According to Premise 2, adding these \( x \) roses increased the total count in the vase to 21 roses.
   - Therefore, the equation representing this situation is:
     \[
     13 + x = 21
     \]
   - Solving for \( x \):
     \[
     x = 21 - 13
     \]
     \[
     x = 8
     \]

5. **Final Answer**: After Jessica added 8 roses to the vase, there are 8 roses left in the garden.

The answer is: 8 roses are left in the garden.
