1. Initially, there were 13 roses in the vase.
2. Jessica added some roses to the vase, and now there are 21 roses in total.
3. To find out how many roses Jessica added, we subtract the initial number of roses (13) from the current total (21):
   \(21 - 13 = 8\)
4. So, Jessica added 8 roses to the vase.
5. The original number of roses in the garden was 12.
6. Adding the 8 new roses to the original 12 gives us:
   \(12 + 8 = 20\)
