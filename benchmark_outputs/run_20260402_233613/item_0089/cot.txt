Let's break down this problem into a logical syllogism:

**Premise 1:** Josh had some marbles in his collection initially.
- Let's denote the number of marbles Josh originally had as \( M \).

**Premise 2:** Josh lost 21 marbles.
- This means that after losing these marbles, Josh still has some marbles left.

**Premise 3:** After losing 21 marbles, Josh has 12 marbles remaining.
- We know that \( M - 21 = 12 \).

**Conclusion:** Therefore, to find out how many marbles Josh originally had (\( M \)), we need to solve the equation derived from Premises 2 and 3:
\[ M - 21 = 12 \]

To solve for \( M \), we add 21 to both sides of the equation:
\[ M - 21 + 21 = 12 + 21 \]
\[ M = 33 \]

So, **the answer is: 33**

Therefore, Josh originally had 33 marbles in his collection.
