**Logical Syllogism**

1. **Premise 1:** A book has 2 chapters.
   - This establishes that we are dealing with two distinct sections within the same book.

2. **Premise 2:** The first chapter is 48 pages long.
   - We know the exact length of the first chapter and need to compare it to the second chapter.

3. **Premise 3:** The second chapter is 11 pages long.
   - Here, we provide information about the number of pages in the second chapter, which is needed to determine how much longer or shorter the first chapter is compared to the second.

4. **Conclusion:** To find out how many more pages the first chapter has than the second chapter, we subtract the number of pages in the second chapter from the number of pages in the first chapter.
   - Calculation: \( 48 \text{ pages} - 11 \text{ pages} = 37 \text{ pages} \).

Therefore, the first chapter has 37 more pages than the second chapter.

The answer is: 37.
