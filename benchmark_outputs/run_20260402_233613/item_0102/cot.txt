To determine how many campers went rowing in total, we need to consider the information given about their activities in the morning and the afternoon separately.

**Premise 1:** 
- 13 campers went rowing in the morning.
  
**Premise 2:** 
- 21 campers went rowing in the afternoon.

**Conclusion:**
The total number of campers who went rowing is the sum of those who went rowing in the morning and those who went rowing in the afternoon.

Let's denote:
- \( R_m \) as the number of campers who went rowing in the morning.
- \( R_a \) as the number of campers who went rowing in the afternoon.

From Premise 1, we have:
\[ R_m = 13 \]

From Premise 2, we have:
\[ R_a = 21 \]

Now, adding these together gives us the total number of campers who went rowing:

\[ \text{Total} = R_m + R_a \]
\[ \text{Total} = 13 + 21 \]
\[ \text{Total} = 34 \]

Therefore, the total number of campers who went rowing is **34**.
