**Premise 1:** Robin has 28 packages of gum.
- This establishes the number of packages of gum Robin owns.

**Premise 2:** Each package contains 4 pieces of gum.
- This provides the quantity of items per package.

**Conclusion:** Therefore, to find the total number of pieces of gum, we multiply the number of packages by the quantity per package.
- Calculation: \(28 \text{ packages} \times 4 \text{ pieces/package} = 112 \text{ pieces}\).

**Answer:** Robin has 112 pieces of gum.
