To determine how many more bottle caps Danny has compared to wrappers, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Identify initial quantities:**
   - Initial number of bottle caps: 71
   - Initial number of wrappers: 24

2. **Determine current quantities:**
   - Current number of bottle caps: 28
   - Current number of wrappers: 16

3. **Calculate the difference between the current and initial numbers of bottle caps:**
   \[
   \text{Difference in bottle caps} = \text{Current bottle caps} - \text{Initial bottle caps}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Difference in bottle caps} = 28 - 71
   \]

4. **Calculate the difference between the current and initial numbers of wrappers:**
   \[
   \text{Difference in wrappers} = \text{Current wrappers} - \text{Initial wrappers}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Difference in wrappers} = 16 - 24
   \]

5. **Find out how many more bottle caps Danny has compared to wrappers:**
   - Since we want to find the difference between bottle caps and wrappers, we subtract the smaller quantity (wrappers) from the larger quantity (bottle caps):
   \[
   \text{More bottle caps} = (\text{Current bottle caps}) - (\text{Current wrappers})
   \]
   \[
   \text{More bottle caps} = 28 - 16
   \]

6. **Perform the subtraction:**
   \[
   28 - 16 = 12
   \]

Therefore, Danny has 12 more bottle caps than wrappers currently. 

The answer is: 12
