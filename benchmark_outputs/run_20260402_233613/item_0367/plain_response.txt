To find out how much Marco's strawberries weighed, we need to subtract the weight of his dad's strawberries from the total weight of all strawberries. 

Given:
- Total weight of all strawberries = 20 pounds
- Weight of Marco's dad's strawberries = 17 pounds

We can calculate Marco's strawberries as follows:

\[ \text{Weight of Marco's strawberries} = \text{Total weight} - \text{Weight of Dad's strawberries} \]

Substituting the given values:

\[ \text{Weight of Marco's strawberries} = 20 \text{ pounds} - 17 \text{ pounds} \]
\[ \text{Weight of Marco's strawberries} = 3 \text{ pounds} \]

Therefore, the answer is: 3.
