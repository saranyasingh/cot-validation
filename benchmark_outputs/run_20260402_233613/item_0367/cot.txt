Premise 1: Marco and his dad together had 20 pounds of strawberries.
Premise 2: His dad's strawberries weighed 17 pounds.
Conclusion: Therefore, Marco's strawberries weighed 3 pounds.

Here’s the logical syllogism:

1. (Premise) Marco and his dad together had 20 pounds of strawberries.
   - This establishes that there was a total weight of strawberries between them.

2. (Premise) His dad's strawberries weighed 17 pounds.
   - This tells us how much his father contributed to the total weight.

3. To find out how much Marco's strawberries weighed:
   - We need to subtract the amount his father contributed from the total amount.
   - Therefore, Marco's strawberries = Total weight - Father's weight

4. Calculation: 
   - Marco's strawberries = 20 pounds - 17 pounds
   - Marco's strawberries = 3 pounds

The answer is: Marco's strawberries weighed 3 pounds.
