To determine how many more emails Jack received in the morning than in the afternoon, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Identify the total number of emails received in the morning:** 
   - Jack received 6 emails in the morning.
   
2. **Identify the total number of emails received in the afternoon:** 
   - Jack received 2 emails in the afternoon.
   
3. **Calculate the difference between the number of emails received in the morning and the afternoon:**
   - Subtract the number of emails received in the afternoon from the number of emails received in the morning:
     \[
     6 - 2 = 4
     \]

Therefore, Jack received 4 more emails in the morning than in the afternoon.

The answer is: The answer is: 4.
