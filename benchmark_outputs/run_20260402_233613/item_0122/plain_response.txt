To determine how many balls Allan and Jake had together in the park, we need to add up their individual amounts of balls.

First, let's calculate the total number of balls that Allan brought:
- Allan brought 5 balls.

Next, let's calculate the total number of balls that Jake brought:
- Jake brought 4 balls.

Now, we sum these quantities to find the total number of balls both boys brought to the park:
\[ \text{Total number of balls} = \text{Allan's balls} + \text{Jake's balls} \]
\[ \text{Total number of balls} = 5 + 4 \]
\[ \text{Total number of balls} = 9 \]

Therefore, the total number of balls that Allan and Jake had in the park is 9. The answer is: 9.
