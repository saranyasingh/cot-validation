Premise 1: Allan brought 7 balloons.
Premise 2: Jake brought 6 balloons.
Premise 3: All balloons belong to Allan.
Premise 4: All balloons belong to Jake.
Premise 5: Allans' balloons do not belong to Jake's collection.
Premise 6: Jake has more balloons than Allan.

Premise 7: Allan brought 5 balls.
Premise 8: Jake brought 4 balls.
Premise 9: All balls belong to Allan.
Premise 10: All balls belong to Jake.
Premise 11: Allans' balls do not belong to Jake's collection.
Premise 12: Jake has more balls than Allan.

The conclusion:
Allan and Jake together had 5 + 4 = 9 balls in the park.

Explanation:

1. From Premises 1-3 and 7-9, we know that all balloons belong to Allan and none to Jake.
2. From Premises 5-6 and 11-12, we know that all balls belong to Allan and none to Jake.
3. Therefore, when they go to the park, both Allan and Jake bring their respective collections of balloons and balls.
4. We add the number of balloons (7+6=13) and the number of balls (5+4=9).
5. Thus, the total number of items (balls and balloons combined) is 13+9=22.

Therefore, the answer is: 22
