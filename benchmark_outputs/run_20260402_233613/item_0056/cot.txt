**Premise 1:** Jack received 5 emails and 6 letters in the morning.
- This establishes the total number of emails and letters he had at the start of the day.

**Premise 2:** Jack then received 9 emails and 7 letters in the afternoon.
- This describes his additional mail reception during the second part of the day.

**Premise 3:** We need to determine how many letters Jack received in total over the course of the day.
- To do this, we must consider both the morning and afternoon totals separately but sum them up because these represent different parts of the same day (morning + afternoon).

**Step-by-step Logical Reasoning:**

1. **Morning Total:**
   - Emails received in the morning: 5
   - Letters received in the morning: 6

2. **Afternoon Total:**
   - Emails received in the afternoon: 9
   - Letters received in the afternoon: 7

3. **Total Letters Received:**
   - Add the number of letters received in the morning to the number of letters received in the afternoon:
     \[
     \text{Total letters} = 6 (\text{morning}) + 7 (\text{afternoon})
     \]

4. **Calculation:**
   \[
   \text{Total letters} = 6 + 7 = 13
   \]

Therefore, the answer is: 13 letters.
