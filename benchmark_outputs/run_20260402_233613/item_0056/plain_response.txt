To determine how many letters Jack received in total for the day, we need to add up all the letters he received during both the morning and the afternoon.

First, let's calculate the number of letters received in the morning:
- Morning: 6 letters

Next, let's calculate the number of letters received in the afternoon:
- Afternoon: 7 letters

Now, we add these two amounts together to find the total number of letters received throughout the day:
\[ \text{Total letters} = \text{Morning letters} + \text{Afternoon letters} \]
\[ \text{Total letters} = 6 + 7 \]
\[ \text{Total letters} = 13 \]

Therefore, the total number of letters Jack received in the day is \(13\).

The answer is: 13
