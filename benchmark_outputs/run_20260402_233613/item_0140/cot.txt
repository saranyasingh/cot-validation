To determine how much more money Dan spent on the candy bar compared to the chocolate, we need to calculate the total amount spent on each item and then find the difference between these amounts.

**Step 1:** Calculate the total amount spent on the candy bar.
- Cost of the candy bar = $6

**Step 2:** Calculate the total amount spent on the chocolate.
- Cost of the chocolate = $3

**Step 3:** Find the difference between the cost of the candy bar and the cost of the chocolate.
- Difference = Cost of the candy bar - Cost of the chocolate
- Difference = $6 - $3 = $3

Therefore, Dan spent $3 more on the candy bar than he did on the chocolate.

The answer is: $3
