To determine how much more money Dan spent on the candy bar compared to the chocolate, we need to calculate the difference between the cost of the candy bar and the cost of the chocolate.

1. Cost of the candy bar = $6
2. Cost of the chocolate = $3

Now, subtract the cost of the chocolate from the cost of the candy bar:

$6 - $3 = $3

Therefore, Dan spent $3 more on the candy bar than he did on the chocolate.

The answer is: $3
