### Facts
FACT-1: The Razorback shop sells t-shirts at a price of $62 per shirt.
- Fact-1: \( \text{PricePerTshirt} = \$62 \)

FACT-2: They sold 183 t-shirts.
- Fact-2: \( \text{QuantitySold} = 183 \)

### Rules
RULE-1: If they sell t-shirts at a certain price and quantity, then their revenue can be calculated as the product of the price per t-shirt and the quantity sold.
- Rule-1: \( \text{Revenue} = \text{PricePerTshirt} \times \text{QuantitySold} \)

### Inferences
INF-1: From FACT-1 and RULE-1, we derive the total revenue from t-shirts.
- Inf-1: \( \text{TotalRevenueFromTshirts} = 62 \times 183 \)
- Calculating Inf-1: \( \text{TotalRevenueFromTshirts} = 11406 \)

Thus, the Razorback shop made **$11,406** from selling t-shirts.
