To determine how much money the Razorback shop made from selling t-shirts during the Arkansas and Texas Tech game, we need to follow these steps:

- This establishes the unit price of t-shirts.

**Premise 2:** They sold 183 t-shirts.
- This gives us the number of t-shirts sold.

**Premise 3:** To find the total revenue from t-shirts, we multiply the unit price by the quantity sold.
- Revenue = Unit Price × Quantity Sold

- Unit Price (t-shirt) = $62
- Quantity Sold = 183

Revenue from t-shirts = $62 × 183

Now let's calculate it:
\[ \text{Revenue} = 62 \times 183 = 11406 \]
