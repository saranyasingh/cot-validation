To determine how much more money Dan spent on the chocolate compared to the candy bar, we need to calculate the total amount spent on each item and then find the difference between these amounts.

1. Calculate the total amount spent on the chocolate:
   - Cost of chocolate = $7

2. Calculate the total amount spent on the candy bar:
   - Cost of candy bar = $2

3. Find the difference in spending:
   - Difference = Cost of chocolate - Cost of candy bar
   - Difference = $7 - $2
   - Difference = $5

Therefore, Dan spent $5 more on buying the chocolate than he did on buying the candy bar.

The answer is: $5
