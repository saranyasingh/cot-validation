To determine how many pieces of candy Bobby ate, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Identify the total amount of candy initially**: Bobby started with 32 pieces of candy.
   
   Premise 1: Bobby had 32 pieces of candy.

2. **Identify the remaining amount of candy after eating**: After eating some pieces, Bobby has 20 pieces left.

   Premise 2: Bobby has 20 pieces of candy left.

3. **Calculate the number of pieces eaten**: To find out how many pieces Bobby ate, subtract the remaining amount from the initial amount.

   Calculation:
   \[
   \text{Pieces eaten} = \text{Initial pieces} - \text{Remaining pieces}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Pieces eaten} = 32 - 20
   \]

4. **Perform the subtraction**:
   \[
   32 - 20 = 12
   \]

5. **Conclusion**: Bobby ate 12 pieces of candy.
