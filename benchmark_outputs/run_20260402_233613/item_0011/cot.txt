Let's break down the problem into logical steps:

**Premise 1:** Dave originally had 15 apps.
- This sets our starting point and establishes that we need to account for these initial apps before any changes occur.

**Premise 2:** Dave added 71 new apps.
- This introduces an increase in the number of apps on his phone.

**Premise 3:** After adding the new apps, Dave had a total of 86 apps (15 original + 71 new).
- Adding both premises together shows us how the count increased after the addition.

**Premise 4:** After deleting some apps, Dave ended up with only 14 apps remaining.
- This indicates that there was a reduction in the number of apps due to deletion.

**Premise 5:** We know that 71 - 14 = 57 apps were deleted.
- This calculates the total number of apps removed through deletion.

**Logical Reasoning:**

1. Start with 15 apps.
2. Add 71 new apps: 15 + 71 = 86 apps.
3. Subtract the number of apps deleted to find out how many were left:
   86 - 57 = 19 apps.
   
Therefore, Dave deleted 19 more apps than he added.

The answer is: 19
