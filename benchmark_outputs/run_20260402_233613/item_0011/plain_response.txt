To determine how many more apps Dave deleted than he added, we need to follow these steps:

- Initial number of apps = 15
- Apps added = 71

Total number of apps before deleting = Initial number + Apps added = 15 + 71 = 86

- Number of apps left = 14

Number of apps deleted = Total number of apps - Number of apps left = 86 - 14 = 72

- Apps deleted = 72
