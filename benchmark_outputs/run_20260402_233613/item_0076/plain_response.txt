To determine the total number of pieces of gum and candy that Robin has, we need to calculate the number of pieces for each type separately and then sum them up.

First, let's find out how many pieces of gum Robin has:
- Robin has 28 packages of gum.
- Each package contains 6 pieces of gum.
So, the total number of pieces of gum is \( 28 \times 6 = 168 \).

Next, let's find out how many pieces of candy Robin has:
- Robin has 14 packages of candy.
- Each package contains 6 pieces of candy.
So, the total number of pieces of candy is \( 14 \times 6 = 84 \).

Now, we add the total number of pieces of gum and candy together:
\[ 168 + 84 = 252 \]

Therefore, the total number of pieces of gum and candy that Robin has is \( 252 \). The answer is: 252.
