**Logical Syllogism**

1. **Premise 1:** Robin has 28 packages of gum.
   - This tells us that there are 28 units (packages) of gum.

2. **Premise 2:** Each package contains 6 pieces of gum.
   - This indicates that within each individual unit (package), there are 6 pieces.

3. **Conclusion:** Therefore, to find out how many pieces of gum Robin has in total, we multiply the number of packages by the number of pieces per package:
   
   \[
   28 \text{ packages} \times 6 \text{ pieces/package}
   \]

4. **Calculation:**
   \[
   28 \times 6 = 168
   \]

5. **Final Answer:** Robin has 168 pieces of gum.

The answer is: 168
