Let's break down the problem using a series of logical steps:

1. **Premise 1**: Zachary did 51 push-ups.
   - This establishes our starting point and gives us a known quantity to work with.

2. **Premise 2**: David did 22 more push-ups than Zachary.
   - From Premise 1, we know that Zachary did 51 push-ups.
   - Therefore, if David did 22 more than Zachary:
     \[
     \text{David's push-ups} = 51 + 22 = 73
     \]

3. **Premise 3**: John did 4 push-ups less than David.
   - We now have David’s number of push-ups (from Premise 2) which is 73.
   - If John did 4 fewer push-ups than David:
     \[
     \text{John's push-ups} = 73 - 4 = 69
     \]

The answer is: John did 69 push-ups.
