**Logical Syllogism**

1. **Premise 1**: Nell originally had 309 baseball cards and 356 Ace cards.
   - This establishes the initial quantities of both types of cards that Nell possessed before any were given away or received.

2. **Premise 2**: After giving some cards to Jeff, Nell has 415 baseball cards and Ace cards remaining.
   - This introduces the condition after the transaction involving Jeff.

3. **Premise 3**: Let's denote the number of baseball cards given to Jeff as \( B \) and the number of Ace cards given to Jeff as \( A \).
   - These variables represent the specific numbers of each type of card that Nell gave to Jeff.

4. **Conclusion**: We need to find how many more baseball cards than Ace cards Nell has after the transaction.

5. **Step-by-Step Reasoning**:

   - Initially, Nell had:
     - Baseball cards: 309
     - Ace cards: 356

   - After giving some cards to Jeff:
     - Baseball cards left: 415
     - Ace cards left: ?

   - From Premises 1 and 2, we know the total amount of cards given to Jeff must equal the difference between the initial quantity and the remaining quantity:
     \[
     (309 + 356) - (415 + x) = 0
     \]
     Here, \( x \) represents the number of cards given to Jeff.

6. **Solving for \( x \)**:
   - Simplify the equation:
     \[
     665 - 415 - x = 0
     \]
     \[
     250 - x = 0
     \]
     \[
     x = 250
     \]

7. **Revisiting the Remaining Cards**:
   - With \( x = 250 \), the number of Ace cards given to Jeff is also 250.
   - Therefore, the remaining number of Ace cards is:
     \[
     356 - 250 = 106
     \]

8. **Calculating the Difference**:
   - Now, compare the remaining baseball cards and Ace cards:
     - Baseball cards left: 415
     - Ace cards left: 106
     - Difference: \( 415 - 106 = 309 \)

**The answer is:**
\[
\boxed{309}
\]
