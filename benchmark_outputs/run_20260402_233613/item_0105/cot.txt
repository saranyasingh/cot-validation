Premise 1: Every day Ryan spends 4 hours on learning English.
Premise 2: Every day Ryan spends 6 hours on learning Chinese.
Premise 3: Ryan learns for 86 days.

To find out how many hours Ryan spends on learning both languages combined over 86 days:

Step 1: Calculate the total number of hours spent on learning English in 86 days.
Total hours on English = Hours per day × Number of days
= 4 hours/day × 86 days
= 344 hours

Step 2: Calculate the total number of hours spent on learning Chinese in 86 days.
Total hours on Chinese = Hours per day × Number of days
= 6 hours/day × 86 days
= 516 hours

Step 3: Add the total hours spent on English to the total hours spent on Chinese.
Total hours spent on both languages = Total hours on English + Total hours on Chinese
= 344 hours + 516 hours
= 860 hours

Therefore, the answer is:
The answer is: 860 hours. This is because Ryan spends a total of 860 hours learning both English and Chinese over 86 days.
