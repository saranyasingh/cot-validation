To determine the number of grades in the school, we need to divide the total number of students by the number of students per grade.

Given:
- Total number of students = 270
- Number of students per grade = 5

We can use the formula for division:

\[ \text{Number of grades} = \frac{\text{Total number of students}}{\text{Number of students per grade}} \]

Substituting the given values into the formula:

\[ \text{Number of grades} = \frac{270}{5} \]

Now, perform the division:

\[ \text{Number of grades} = 54 \]

Therefore, the number of grades in the school is 54.

The answer is: 54
