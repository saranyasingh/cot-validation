Let's break down this problem into a logical syllogism:

1. **Premise 1:** Josh lost 11 marbles.
   - This establishes that there was an initial state involving marbles and then a change occurred.

2. **Premise 2:** He had 19 marbles in his collection earlier.
   - This provides us with the quantity of marbles at the beginning before any loss occurred.

3. **Premise 3:** We need to determine how many marbles he has now after losing 11.
   - This sets up our goal based on the information provided.

4. **Conclusion:** By subtracting the number of marbles lost (11) from the total number of marbles he originally had (19), we can find out how many marbles he currently has.

Therefore, let’s perform the calculation:
\[ \text{Current number of marbles} = \text{Original number of marbles} - \text{Number of marbles lost} \]
\[ \text{Current number of marbles} = 19 - 11 \]
\[ \text{Current number of marbles} = 8 \]

So, the answer is: 8.
