To determine the total number of peaches in all the baskets, we need to follow these steps:

### Step-by-Step Logical Reasoning

**Premise 1:** Each basket contains 19 red peaches.
   - This establishes that within any single basket, there are always 19 red peaches regardless of how many other fruits or vegetables might be included.

**Premise 2:** Each basket also contains 4 green peaches.
   - Similar to Premise 1, this states that no matter what else is inside the basket, it must contain exactly 4 green peaches.

**Premise 3:** There are 15 such baskets.
   - This tells us the quantity of baskets available for consideration.

**Conclusion (Final Answer):**
The total number of peaches in all the baskets together is calculated by adding the number of red peaches to the number of green peaches per basket, multiplied by the total number of baskets.

\[
\text{Total Peaches} = (\text{Red Peaches per Basket} + \text{Green Peaches per Basket}) \times \text{Number of Baskets}
\]

Substituting the given values into our formula:

\[
\text{Total Peaches} = (19 + 4) \times 15
\]
\[
\text{Total Peaches} = 23 \times 15
\]
\[
\text{Total Peaches} = 345
\]

Therefore, the answer is:
The total number of peaches in the baskets altogether is **345**.
