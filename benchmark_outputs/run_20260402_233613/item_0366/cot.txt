1. **Premise**: All given information is about balloons and their quantities.
2. **Premise**: Allan brought 5 balloons to the park.
3. **Premise**: Jake brought 6 more balloons than the number of balloons that Allan brought.
4. **Step-by-Step Reasoning**:
   - Let's denote the number of balloons Allan brought as \( A \).
   - According to the problem, \( A = 5 \).
   - Jake brings \( A + 6 \) balloons.
   - Substituting the value of \( A \), we get: 
     \[
     J = 5 + 6
     \]
   - Performing the addition gives us:
     \[
     J = 11
     \]
5. **Conclusion**: Jake brought 11 balloons to the park.

The answer is: 11
