## FACTS
- **I**: Haley initially had 20 trees.
- **S**: After the typhoon, 4 trees remained.

## RULES
- **Rule for Subtraction**: If A and B are sets, then the difference between A and B gives the elements present only in A but not in B.

## INFERENCES
- **Inference 1**: Let \( I \) represent the initial state where Haley has 20 trees.
- **Inference 2**: Let \( S \) represent the state where 4 trees remain after the typhoon.
- **Inference 3**: By definition, the number of trees that died (\( D \)) is given by \( D = I - S \). Since \( I = 20 \) and \( S = 4 \), it follows that \( D = 20 - 4 \). Therefore, \( D = 16 \).
