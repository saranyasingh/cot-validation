LABEL: ## FACTS :: 
FACT-1: Haley originally planted 3 trees in her backyard.
FACT-2: After the typhoon, 13 trees died.

LABEL: ## RULES :: 
RULE-1: If Haley plants a tree, then she has 1 tree.
RULE-2: Trees die during the typhoon.

LABEL: ## INFERENCES :: 
INF-1: From Rule-1, if Haley plants a tree, then she has 1 tree.
INF-2: From Rule-2, if Haley plants a tree, then the tree dies during the typhoon.
INF-3: Since Haley originally planted 3 trees, the total number of trees before the typhoon is 3.
INF-4: After the typhoon, 13 trees died, which includes both the ones that died during the typhoon and the newly planted trees that also died.
INF-5: Since the problem does not specify any new trees being planted post-typhoon, all 13 deaths are accounted for by the existing trees.
INF-6: The difference between the number of trees that died and survived is calculated as follows:
\[ \text{Number of trees that died} - \text{Number of trees that survived} = 13 - (\text{number of trees that died}) \]

\[ \text{Difference} = 13 - 16 = -3 \]
