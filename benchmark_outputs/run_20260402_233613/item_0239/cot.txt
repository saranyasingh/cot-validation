To determine how many books Paul sold at the garage sale, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Initial number of books**: Paul started with 134 books.
2. **Books given away**: He gave 39 books to his friend.
   - This means after giving away 39 books, the number of books remaining is \(134 - 39\).

Let's calculate this first:
\[ 134 - 39 = 95 \]

So, after giving away 39 books, Paul has 95 books left.

3. **Final number of books**: We know that after the garage sale, Paul has 68 books left.
   - Let \( x \) be the number of books he sold during the garage sale.

We set up the equation based on the information provided:
\[ 95 - x = 68 \]

This equation represents the situation where the initial number of books minus the number of books sold equals the final number of books.

4. **Solve for \( x \)**: To find out how many books were sold, we solve the equation for \( x \).
   \[ 95 - x = 68 \]
   Subtract 68 from both sides:
   \[ 95 - 68 = x \]
   Simplify:
   \[ 27 = x \]

Therefore, Paul sold 27 books at the garage sale.

The answer is: 27
