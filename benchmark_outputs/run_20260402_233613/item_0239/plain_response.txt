To determine how many books Paul sold in the garage sale, we need to follow these steps:

1. Start with the initial number of books Paul had.
2. Subtract the number of books given away.
3. Compare the remaining number of books to what was expected after the sales event.

Let's go through this step-by-step:

1. Paul initially had 134 books.
2. He gave away 39 books:
   \[
   134 - 39 = 95
   \]
   So, after giving away the books, Paul had 95 books left.
3. We know that after the garage sale, he had 68 books left. To find out how many books he sold, we subtract the number of books left from the number of books he had after giving away the books:
   \[
   95 - 68 = 27
   \]

Therefore, Paul sold 27 books in the garage sale.

The answer is: 27
