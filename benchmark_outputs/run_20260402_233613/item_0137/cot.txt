1. **Premise**: Frank has 42 pieces of candy.
   - This establishes the total quantity of candies available to be divided.

2. **Premise**: He wants to put these candies equally into 2 bags.
   - This introduces the condition that the candies need to be distributed evenly among two groups.

3. **Logical Reasoning**:
   - To find out how many pieces of candy are in each bag, we need to divide the total number of candies (42) by the number of bags (2).
   - Therefore, \( \frac{42}{2} = 21 \).

The conclusion is that there are 21 pieces of candy in each bag.

**Answer**: The answer is: 21
