To find out how many pieces of candy are in each bag when Frank divides 42 pieces equally between 2 bags, you would divide the total number of candies by the number of bags:

\[ \text{Candies per bag} = \frac{\text{Total candies}}{\text{Number of bags}} \]

\[ \text{Candies per bag} = \frac{42}{2} \]

\[ \text{Candies per bag} = 21 \]

So, there are 21 pieces of candy in each bag. The answer is: 21.
