To solve this problem logically, we need to follow these steps:

### Step 1: Determine how many apps were deleted.
Initially, Dave had 24 apps on his phone. After deleting some apps, he was left with 12 apps. Therefore:
\[ \text{Number of apps deleted} = 24 - 12 = 12 \]

### Step 2: Determine how many files were deleted.
Initially, Dave had 9 files on his phone. After deleting some files, he was left with 5 files. Therefore:
\[ \text{Number of files deleted} = 9 - 5 = 4 \]

### Step 3: Calculate how many more apps than files Dave has left on the phone.
Now that we know how many apps and files Dave has left:
- Apps left: 12
- Files left: 5

The difference between the number of apps and files is:
\[ \text{Difference} = \text{Apps left} - \text{Files left} = 12 - 5 = 7 \]

Thus, Dave has 7 more apps than files left on his phone.

The answer is: 7
