To determine the number of green peaches in the basket, we start with the information given:

1. The total number of peaches in the basket is 16.
2. There are 13 red peaches in the basket.

We can use the equation:
\[ \text{Total number of peaches} = \text{Number of red peaches} + \text{Number of green peaches} \]

Let's denote the number of green peaches as \( G \). Therefore, the equation becomes:
\[ 16 = 13 + G \]

To find \( G \), we subtract 13 from both sides of the equation:
\[ G = 16 - 13 \]
\[ G = 3 \]

So, there are 3 green peaches in the basket.

The answer is: 3
