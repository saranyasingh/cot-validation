Let's break down this problem using a logical syllogism:

Premise 1: There are a total of 16 peaches in the basket.
Premise 2: There are 13 red peaches in the basket.
Conclusion: Therefore, the number of green peaches must be 16 - 13 = 3.

Explanation:
1. We start with Premise 1 which states that there are a total of 16 peaches in the basket.
2. Premise 2 tells us that there are 13 red peaches in the basket.
3. To find out how many green peaches there are, we subtract the number of red peaches (13) from the total number of peaches (16).
4. This subtraction gives us the number of green peaches: 16 - 13 = 3.
5. Thus, the conclusion logically follows from the premises provided.

The answer is: 3.
