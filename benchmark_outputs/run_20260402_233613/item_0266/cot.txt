Premise 1: Winter is almost here.
Premise 2: Most animals are migrating to warmer countries during winter.
Premise 3: There are 85 bird families living near the mountain.
Premise 4: 23 bird families flew away to Africa.
Premise 5: 37 bird families flew away to Asia.

Given information:
- Total number of bird families initially = 85
- Number of birds that flew away to Africa = 23
- Number of birds that flew away to Asia = 37

Logical Syllogism:

Step 1: Determine how many bird families flew away in total.
The total number of bird families that flew away can be calculated as follows:
\[ \text{Total birds flown away} = \text{Birds flying to Africa} + \text{Birds flying to Asia} \]
\[ \text{Total birds flown away} = 23 + 37 \]
\[ \text{Total birds flown away} = 60 \]

Step 2: Calculate the number of bird families remaining near the mountain.
To find out how many bird families were left near the mountain, we subtract the total number of birds that flew away from the initial number of bird families:
\[ \text{Birds remaining near the mountain} = \text{Initial bird families} - \text{Total birds flown away} \]
\[ \text{Birds remaining near the mountain} = 85 - 60 \]
\[ \text{Birds remaining near the mountain} = 25 \]

Conclusion: Therefore, there are 25 bird families left near the mountain.

Answer: The answer is: 25
