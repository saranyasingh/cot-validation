To find out how many bird families were left near the mountain, we need to subtract the number of birds that flew away from the total number of bird families initially living there.

Initial number of bird families = 85
Birds that flew away to Africa = 23
Birds that flew away to Asia = 37

Total number of birds that flew away = Birds flying to Africa + Birds flying to Asia
= 23 + 37
= 60

Number of bird families left near the mountain = Initial number of bird families - Total number of birds that flew away
= 85 - 60
= 25

Therefore, the answer is: 25.
