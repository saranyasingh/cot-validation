To determine how many pens Paul sold in the garage sale, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the total number of books and pens Paul originally had.
   - Books: 51
   - Pens: 106

2. Determine the number of books and pens left after the garage sale.
   - Books: 82
   - Pens: 14

3. Find out how many more books were sold than pens sold.
   - Difference between books and pens sold = (Books left) - (Pens left)
