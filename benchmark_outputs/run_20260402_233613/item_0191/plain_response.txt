To determine how many emails Jack received in the day, we need to add up the number of emails he received during each part of the day:

1. Morning: 6 emails
2. Afternoon: 3 emails
3. Evening: 5 emails

Now, let's sum these amounts:

\[ 6 + 3 + 5 = 14 \]

So, Jack received a total of 14 emails in the day.

The answer is: 14
