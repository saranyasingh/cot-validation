1. **Premise 1:** Jack received 6 emails in the morning.
   - This establishes the number of emails received during the first part of the day.

2. **Premise 2:** Jack received 3 emails in the afternoon.
   - This provides the number of emails received during the second part of the day.

3. **Premise 3:** Jack received 5 emails in the evening.
   - This gives the number of emails received during the third part of the day.

4. **Conclusion:** To find the total number of emails Jack received throughout the entire day, we need to add up all the emails he received at different times:
   
   \[
   \text{Total emails} = \text{Morning emails} + \text{Afternoon emails} + \text{Evening emails}
   \]

5. **Substitute the values into the equation:**
   
   \[
   \text{Total emails} = 6 + 3 + 5
   \]

6. **Perform the addition:**

   \[
   6 + 3 = 9
   \]
   \[
   9 + 5 = 14
   \]

7. **Final Conclusion:** Therefore, the total number of emails Jack received in the day is 14.

**The answer is: 14**
