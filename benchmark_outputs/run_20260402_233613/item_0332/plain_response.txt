Let's solve this step by step:

1) Initially, there were 22 children on the bus.
2) Some children got off the bus and then 40 more got on, making a total of 62 children (22 + 40).
3) After these changes, there were only 2 children left on the bus.

To find out how many children got off:
- Subtract the number of children left from the new total after getting on.
- So, we calculate: 62 - 2 = 60

Therefore, 60 children got off the bus at the bus stop.
