To determine how many more campers went rowing in the morning compared to the afternoon, we need to follow these steps:

1. Identify the number of campers who went rowing in the morning.
   - According to the problem, 62 campers went rowing in the morning.

2. Identify the number of campers who went rowing in the afternoon.
   - According to the problem, 39 campers went rowing in the afternoon.

3. Calculate the difference between the number of campers who went rowing in the morning and those who went in the afternoon.
   - This can be done by subtracting the number of campers who went rowing in the afternoon from the number who went rowing in the morning.

Let's perform this calculation:
\[ \text{Difference} = \text{Number of campers in the morning} - \text{Number of campers in the afternoon} \]
\[ \text{Difference} = 62 - 39 \]

4. Perform the subtraction:
\[ 62 - 39 = 23 \]

Therefore, there were 23 more campers who went rowing in the morning than in the afternoon.

The answer is: 23
