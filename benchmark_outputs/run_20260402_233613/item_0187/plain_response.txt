To find out how many more campers went rowing in the morning than in the afternoon, we need to subtract the number of campers who went rowing in the afternoon from the number of campers who went rowing in the morning.

Number of campers who went rowing in the morning = 62
Number of campers who went rowing in the afternoon = 39

Difference = Number of campers in the morning - Number of campers in the afternoon
          = 62 - 39
          = 23

Therefore, the answer is: The difference between the number of campers who went rowing in the morning and those who went rowing in the afternoon is 23.
