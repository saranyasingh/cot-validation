Let's denote the number of apples that the tree had initially as \( x \). According to the information provided:

1. Rachel picked 2 apples from one of her trees.
2. After picking these 2 apples, there were 7 apples left on the tree.

We can set up an equation based on this information:
\[ x - 2 = 7 \]

To find the initial number of apples (\( x \)), we add 2 to both sides of the equation:
\[ x - 2 + 2 = 7 + 2 \]
\[ x = 9 \]

Therefore, the tree originally had 9 apples. The answer is: 9
