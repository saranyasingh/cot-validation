To determine how many more emails Jack received in the afternoon compared to the evening, we need to follow these steps:

2. **Identify the number of emails received in the afternoon**: Jack received 5 emails.
3. **Identify the number of emails received in the evening**: Jack received 4 emails.

- Number of emails in the afternoon = 5
- Number of emails in the evening = 4

Difference = Number of emails in the afternoon - Number of emails in the evening

Substituting the values:
Difference = 5 - 4

Calculating the result:
Difference = 1
