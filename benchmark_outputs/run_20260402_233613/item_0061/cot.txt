Let's break down this problem using a logical syllogism:

1. **Premise 1:** Every day Ryan spends 5 hours on learning Chinese.
   - This establishes that there is a fixed amount of time spent on learning Chinese daily.

2. **Premise 2:** Ryan spends 2 hours more on learning English than on learning Chinese.
   - From Premise 1, we know he spends 5 hours on Chinese. Therefore, if he spends 2 hours more on English:
     \[
     \text{Time spent on English} = \text{Time spent on Chinese} + 2 \text{ hours}
     \]
     Substituting the known value:
     \[
     \text{Time spent on English} = 5 \text{ hours} + 2 \text{ hours} = 7 \text{ hours}
     \]

3. **Conclusion:** Ryan spends 7 hours on learning English.
   - We have directly calculated this based on the given information and the established premises.

The answer is: 7
