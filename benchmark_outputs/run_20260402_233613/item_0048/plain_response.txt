To determine the depth of the water, we need to follow these steps:

1. Find out how tall Dean is.
2. Calculate the depth of the water based on Dean's height.

Step 1: Determine Dean's height
Ron stands at 14 feet tall, and Dean is 8 feet shorter than Ron.
\[ \text{Dean's height} = \text{Ron's height} - 8 \]
\[ \text{Dean's height} = 14 - 8 \]
\[ \text{Dean's height} = 6 \text{ feet} \]

Step 2: Calculate the depth of the water
The depth of the water is 2 times Dean's height.
\[ \text{Depth of the water} = 2 \times \text{Dean's height} \]
\[ \text{Depth of the water} = 2 \times 6 \]
\[ \text{Depth of the water} = 12 \text{ feet} \]
