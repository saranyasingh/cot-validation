Let's break down this problem into a logical syllogism:

**Premise 1:** The depth of the water is 2 times Dean's height.
- This establishes a relationship between the depth of the water and Dean's height.

**Premise 2:** Dean is 8 feet shorter than Ron.
- This gives us another relationship between Dean and Ron's heights.

**Premise 3:** Ron stands at 14 feet tall.
- This provides us with Ron's exact height.

Now we need to find out how much taller Dean is compared to Ron and then use that information to determine the depth of the water.

Step-by-step logical reasoning:

1. **Determine Dean's Height:**
   - From Premise 2: Dean is 8 feet shorter than Ron.
   - Given Ron's height (from Premise 3): 14 feet.
   - Therefore, Dean's height = Ron's height - 8 feet.
   - So, Dean's height = 14 feet - 8 feet = 6 feet.

2. **Calculate the Depth of the Water:**
   - According to Premise 1: The depth of the water is 2 times Dean's height.
   - Now that we know Dean's height (from Step 1), calculate the depth:
     - Depth of the water = 2 × Dean's height.
     - Depth of the water = 2 × 6 feet = 12 feet.

Therefore, the answer is: The depth of the water was 12 feet.
