Let's break down this problem into a series of logical steps:

1. **Identify the quantities given:**
   - Regular soda: 49 bottles
   - Diet soda: 40 bottles

2. **Sum up the quantities:**
   - To find the total number of bottles of regular and diet soda combined, we need to add the two numbers together:
     \[
     49 + 40 = 89
     \]

3. **Conclusion:**
   - Therefore, the total number of bottles of regular soda and diet soda that the grocery store had is 89.

- Premise 1: There are 49 bottles of regular soda.
- Premise 2: There are 40 bottles of diet soda.
