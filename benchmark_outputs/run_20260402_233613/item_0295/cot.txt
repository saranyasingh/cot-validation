To determine how many students suggested bacon, we need to follow these steps:

1. Let's denote the number of students who suggested bacon as \( B \).
2. According to the problem, the number of students who suggested mashed potatoes is 457.
3. It is also stated that there were 63 more students who suggested mashed potatoes than those who suggested bacon. This relationship can be expressed mathematically as:
   \[
   457 = B + 63
   \]
4. To find \( B \), we subtract 63 from both sides of the equation:
   \[
   457 - 63 = B
   \]
5. Simplifying the left side gives us:
   \[
   394 = B
   \]

Therefore, the number of students who suggested bacon is 394.

The answer is: 394
