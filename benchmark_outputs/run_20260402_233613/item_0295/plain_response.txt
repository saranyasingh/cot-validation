Let's denote:
- \( B \) as the number of students who suggested bacon.
- \( M \) as the number of students who suggested mashed potatoes.

From the information given:
1. The total number of students suggesting mashed potatoes is 457.
2. There were 63 more students suggesting mashed potatoes than bacon.

We can set up two equations based on this information:

Equation 1 (total students suggesting mashed potatoes):
\[ M = 457 \]

Equation 2 (difference between students suggesting mashed potatoes and bacon):
\[ M = B + 63 \]

Now we substitute \( M \) from Equation 1 into Equation 2:
\[ 457 = B + 63 \]

To find \( B \), subtract 63 from both sides of the equation:
\[ 457 - 63 = B \]
\[ 394 = B \]

So, 394 students suggested bacon.

Therefore, the answer is: 394
