Premise 1: Haley has 20 marbles.
Premise 2: There are 2 boys who love to play marbles.
Premise 3: Haley distributes her marbles equally among the boys.

Conclusion: Each boy will receive 10 marbles.

\[ \text{Marbles per boy} = \frac{\text{Total Marbles}}{\text{Number of Boys}} = \frac{20}{2} = 10 \]

Therefore, if there were no restriction on distributing the marbles equally, each boy would receive 10 marbles. However, this does not take into account that the boys might have different preferences or requirements regarding how the marbles should be distributed.

Step 2: Given the information provided (that these are two specific boys who love to play marbles), it implies that there may be some additional constraints or conditions under which the marbles could be distributed. For example, perhaps one boy prefers more red marbles while the other prefers blue ones. Without knowing these specifics, we cannot distribute the marbles completely evenly based solely on the number of boys.

Step 3: To conclude what each boy receives, we must consider the most straightforward interpretation of "distributing her marbles equally," assuming the only constraint is dividing them between the two boys. Based on Premise 3, if there are no further restrictions, each boy would indeed receive 10 marbles.

Therefore, given the premises and our understanding of the problem statement, the conclusion is that each boy will receive 10 marbles.
