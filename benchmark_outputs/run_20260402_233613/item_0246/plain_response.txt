To determine how many marbles each boy will receive when Haley distributes them equally among herself and the two other students (the girls), we can follow these steps:

2. **Total number of marbles**:
   - Haley has 20 marbles

3. **Distribute marbles equally**:
   - To find out how many marbles each person gets, divide the total number of marbles by the total number of people.
   
4. **Calculation**:
   \[
   \text{Marbles per person} = \frac{\text{Total marbles}}{\text{Number of people}} = \frac{20}{3}
   \]

5. **Result**:
   - Each person will get approximately 6.67 marbles.
