To determine how many points Melissa scored in each game, we need to divide her total points by the number of games played.

Given:
- Total points = 91
- Number of games = 13

We use the formula:
\[ \text{Points per game} = \frac{\text{Total Points}}{\text{Number of Games}} \]

Substituting the given values:
\[ \text{Points per game} = \frac{91}{13} \]

Performing the division:
\[ \text{Points per game} = 7 \]

Therefore, Melissa scored \( \boxed{7} \) points in each game.
