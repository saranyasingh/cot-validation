To determine how many more marbles Josh found than those he lost, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the total number of marbles Josh has after finding and losing some.
2. Subtract the number of marbles he lost from the number he found to get the difference.

Let's break it down step-by-step:

1. **Initial Marbles**: Josh starts with 3 marbles.
2. **Marbles Found**: Josh finds 6 more marbles.
   - Total marbles now = Initial marbles + Marbles found = \(3 + 6\).

\[3 + 6 = 9\]

So, Josh now has 9 marbles.

3. **Marbles Lost**: Josh loses 5 marbles.
4. **Difference**: To find out how many more marbles he found than those he lost, subtract the number of marbles lost from the total number of marbles found.

\[ \text{Difference} = \text{Total marbles found} - \text{Marbles lost} \]
\[ \text{Difference} = 9 - 5 \]
\[ \text{Difference} = 4 \]

Therefore, Josh found 4 more marbles than those he lost.

The answer is: 4
