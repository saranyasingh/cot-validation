To find out how many more bottles of regular soda there were compared to diet soda, we need to subtract the number of diet soda bottles from the number of regular soda bottles:

Regular soda bottles = 81
Diet soda bottles = 60

Difference = Regular soda bottles - Diet soda bottles
          = 81 - 60
          = 21

So, the grocery store had 21 more bottles of regular soda than diet soda. The answer is: 21.
