To determine how many more bottles of regular soda the grocery store had compared to diet soda, we need to follow these steps:

### Premise 1:
The total number of bottles of regular soda is 81.
- This establishes that there are 81 bottles of regular soda available.

### Premise 2:
The total number of bottles of diet soda is 60.
- This establishes that there are 60 bottles of diet soda available.

### Conclusion:
We need to find out how many more bottles of regular soda (81) there are than diet soda (60).
- To do this, we subtract the number of diet soda bottles from the number of regular soda bottles.

### Logical Reasoning Step-by-Step:
1. Identify the number of regular soda bottles: 81
2. Identify the number of diet soda bottles: 60
3. Subtract the number of diet soda bottles from the number of regular soda bottles:
   \[
   81 - 60 = 21
   \]

Therefore, the grocery store had 21 more bottles of regular soda than diet soda.

### Final Answer:
The answer is: 21
