To determine how many sacks of oranges they will have after 26 days of harvest, we need to calculate the total number of sacks harvested each day and then multiply it by the number of days.

First, let's find out how many sacks of oranges are harvested in one day:
- Ripe oranges: 28 sacks/day
- Unripe oranges: 52 sacks/day

Total sacks harvested per day = 28 + 52 = 80 sacks/day

Next, we need to find out how many sacks of oranges are harvested over 26 days:
Total sacks after 26 days = Total sacks per day * Number of days
Total sacks after 26 days = 80 sacks/day * 26 days

Now, let's perform the multiplication:

80 * 26 = 2080

Therefore, the answer is: 2080 sacks of oranges.
