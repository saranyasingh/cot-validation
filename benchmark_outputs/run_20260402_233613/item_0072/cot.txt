Let's break down the problem into two parts:

**Premise 1:** Helen baked 197 chocolate chip cookies and 46 raisin cookies yesterday.
- This establishes the number of chocolate chip cookies baked yesterday.

**Premise 2:** Helen baked 75 raisin cookies and 66 chocolate chip cookies this morning.
- This establishes the number of raisin cookies baked this morning.

Now, let's compare these numbers:

**Step 1:** Calculate the total number of chocolate chip cookies baked yesterday.
\[ \text{Chocolate chip cookies baked yesterday} = 197 \]

**Step 2:** Calculate the total number of raisin cookies baked today.
\[ \text{Raisin cookies baked today} = 66 \]

**Step 3:** Find out how many more chocolate chip cookies were baked yesterday than today.
\[ \text{Difference} = \text{Chocolate chip cookies baked yesterday} - \text{Raisin cookies baked today} \]
\[ \text{Difference} = 197 - 66 \]
\[ \text{Difference} = 131 \]

Therefore, Helen baked 131 more chocolate chip cookies yesterday compared to today.

The answer is: 131
