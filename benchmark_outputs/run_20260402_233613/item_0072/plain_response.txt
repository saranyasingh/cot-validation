To determine how many more chocolate chip cookies Helen baked yesterday compared to today, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the total number of chocolate chip cookies baked in both days.
2. Calculate the total number of raisin cookies baked in both days.
3. Find the difference between the total number of chocolate chip cookies and the total number of raisin cookies.

First, let's calculate the total number of chocolate chip cookies:
- Yesterday: 197 chocolate chip cookies
- Today: 66 chocolate chip cookies

Total chocolate chip cookies = 197 + 66 = 263

Next, let's calculate the total number of raisin cookies:
- Yesterday: 46 raisin cookies
- Today: 75 raisin cookies

Total raisin cookies = 46 + 75 = 121

Now, we find the difference between the total number of chocolate chip cookies and the total number of raisin cookies:
Difference = Total chocolate chip cookies - Total raisin cookies
Difference = 263 - 121 = 142

Therefore, Helen baked 142 more chocolate chip cookies yesterday compared to today. The answer is: 142
