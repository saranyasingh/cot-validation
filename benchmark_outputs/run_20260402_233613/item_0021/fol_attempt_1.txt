Let's convert the given chain-of-thought reasoning into structured First-Order Logic (FOL):

### Facts
1. Let \( t_{\text{harvest}} \) be the time when the oranges were harvested.
2. Let \( n_sacks \) be the total number of sacks of oranges harvested.
3. Let \( s\_sacks \) be the number of sacks harvested per day.
4. Let \( d\_days \) be the total number of days required to harvest all the sacks.

**Facts:**

1. \( n_sacks = 54 \)
2. \( s\_sacks = 18 \)
3. \( d\_days = \frac{n_sacks}{s\_sacks} \)

### Rules
1. \( \forall t (n_sacks = 54) \)
2. \( \forall t (s\_sacks = 18) \)
3. \( \forall t (d\_days = \frac{n_sacks}{s\_sacks}) \)

### Inferences
1. \( \text{From Fact 1, Rule 1 by Modus Ponens: } \text{d\_days = } \frac{54}{18} \)
2. \( \text{d\_days = 3} \)

Thus, the answer is: 3 days
