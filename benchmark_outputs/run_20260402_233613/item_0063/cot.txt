**Logical Syllogism**

1. **Premise 1:** Jack received 4 emails and sent 2 letters in the morning.
   - Context: This establishes the initial state of emails and letters Jack had before sending any during the morning.

2. **Premise 2:** Jack received 6 emails and sent 8 letters in the afternoon.
   - Context: This provides information about his actions after he had already received more emails than initially stated (since it's mentioned that he received 6 emails).

3. **Conclusion:** We need to find out how many emails Jack sent in total for the entire day.

**Step-by-Step Reasoning:**
- From Premise 1, we know:
  - Morning: Received = 4 emails, Sent = 2 letters.
  
- From Premise 2, we know:
  - Afternoon: Received = 6 emails, Sent = 8 letters.

4. **Total Emails Received During the Day:**
   - Total received in the morning + Total received in the afternoon
     \(= 4 \text{ (morning)} + 6 \text{ (afternoon)}\)
     \(= 10 \text{ emails}\)

5. **Total Letters Sent During the Day:**
   - Total sent in the morning + Total sent in the afternoon
     \(= 2 \text{ (morning)} + 8 \text{ (afternoon)}\)
     \(= 10 \text{ letters}\)

6. **Emails Sent in the Day:**
   - Since the problem asks specifically about emails sent, we only consider the total emails received.
   
Therefore, the answer is:

The answer is: 10 emails.
