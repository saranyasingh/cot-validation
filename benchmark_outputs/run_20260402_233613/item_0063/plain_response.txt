To determine how many emails Jack sent in the day, we need to add up all the emails he sent throughout the entire day.

1. In the morning, Jack sent 2 emails.
2. In the afternoon, Jack sent 8 more emails.

We can calculate the total number of emails sent as follows:

\[ \text{Total emails} = \text{Emails in the morning} + \text{Emails in the afternoon} \]
\[ \text{Total emails} = 2 + 8 \]
\[ \text{Total emails} = 10 \]

Therefore, the total number of emails Jack sent in the day is 10. The answer is: 10.
