To solve this problem, we need to calculate how many crayons Paul gave away and then compare that number to the number of crayons he lost. Let's break it down step-by-step:

### Step 1: Calculate the total number of crayons Paul had initially.
Paul started with 589 crayons.

### Step 2: Determine how many crayons Paul gave away during the school year.
Paul gave away 571 crayons to his friends.

### Step 3: Calculate how many crayons Paul lost.
Paul lost 161 crayons.

### Step 4: Find out how many more crayons Paul gave away compared to what he lost.
We subtract the number of crayons he lost from the number of crayons he gave away:
\[ \text{Difference} = \text{Crayons given away} - \text{Crayons lost} \]
\[ \text{Difference} = 571 - 161 \]

### Calculation:
\[ 571 - 161 = 410 \]

So, Paul gave away 410 more crayons than he lost.

The answer is: 410
