To determine how many more crayons Paul gave to his friends than those he lost, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the total number of crayons Paul had after giving some away and losing some.
2. Subtract the number of crayons he lost from the number of crayons he gave away.

First, let's find out how many crayons Paul had left:
\[ \text{Total crayons} = \text{Initial crayons} - \text{Crayons given away} - \text{Crayons lost} \]
\[ \text{Total crayons} = 589 - 571 - 161 \]

Now, perform the subtraction step-by-step:
\[ 589 - 571 = 18 \]
\[ 18 - 161 = -143 \]

So, Paul ended up with -143 crayons (which means he owes 143 crayons).

Next, we calculate how many more crayons he gave to his friends than those he lost:
\[ \text{Difference} = \text{Crayons given away} - \text{Crayons lost} \]
\[ \text{Difference} = 571 - 161 \]

Perform the subtraction:
\[ 571 - 161 = 410 \]

Therefore, Paul gave 410 more crayons to his friends than those he lost. The answer is: 410.
