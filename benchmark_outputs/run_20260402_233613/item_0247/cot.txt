To solve this problem, let's break it down into clear steps using a logical syllogism:

1. **Initial Condition**: There were 28 children initially on the bus.
   - Premise (P1): Initially, \( \text{number of children} = 28 \).

2. **Event at Bus Stop**: Some number of children got on the bus and others got off.
   - Let’s denote the number of children who got on as \( x \) and the number who got off as \( y \).
   - Therefore, after these events, the total number of children on the bus becomes \( 28 + x - y \).

3. **Final Condition**: After all these changes, there were 30 children on the bus.
   - Premise (P2): Final condition, \( 28 + x - y = 30 \).

4. **Solving for \( x \)**:
   - From P2, we can rearrange to find \( x \):
     \[
     28 + x - y = 30 \\
     x - y = 30 - 28 \\
     x - y = 2
     \]
   - So, \( x = y + 2 \).

5. **Understanding \( x \) and \( y \)**:
   - We know \( x \) represents the number of children who got on the bus, which means they must be positive integers.
   - Since \( x = y + 2 \), \( y \) must be an integer such that \( x \) remains a positive integer.

6. **Total Children Calculation**:
   - The total number of children on the bus before any got on or off was 28.
   - Adding the number of children who got on (\( x \)) minus those who got off (\( y \)), gives us 30 children.
   - This implies \( x - y = 2 \).

7. **Conclusion**:
   - To determine how many more children got on compared to those who got off, we need to consider the possible values for \( x \) and \( y \). Given \( x - y = 2 \), if \( y = 0 \), then \( x = 2 \). However, since \( y \) cannot be zero because it represents people getting off the bus, \( y \) must be greater than zero.
   - If \( y = 1 \), then \( x = 3 \). In this case, \( x - y = 3 - 1 = 2 \), so 3 children got on and 1 child got off.
   - If \( y = 2 \), then \( x = 4 \). Here, \( x - y = 4 - 2 = 2 \), meaning 4 children got on and 2 got off.
   - Generally, for any positive value of \( y \), \( x \) would always be two more than \( y \).

Therefore, the conclusion is:
The answer is: 2.
