Premise 1: There are 5 red peaches in the basket.
Premise 2: There are 14 yellow peaches in the basket.
Premise 3: There are 6 green peaches in the basket.

The conclusion we want to draw is that there are 14 yellow peaches and 6 green peaches in total in the basket.

Logical Syllogism:

P1 → R (There are 5 red peaches)
P2 → Y (There are 14 yellow peaches)
P3 → G (There are 6 green peaches)

From P1 and P2, we know:
R + Y = Total number of peaches

Substituting known values:
5 + 14 = Total number of peaches

Total number of peaches = 19

Now, let's find out how many green and yellow peaches together make up this total:
G + Y = Total number of peaches

Substitute the value of total peaches:
G + 14 = 19

To solve for G, subtract 14 from both sides:
G = 19 - 14
G = 5

So, there are 5 green peaches in the basket.

Therefore, the answer is: Green and yellow peaches combined make up 14 peaches, which means there are 5 green peaches. The remaining peaches must be yellow, making it 14 yellow peaches.
