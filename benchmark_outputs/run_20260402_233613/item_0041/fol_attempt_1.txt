### FACTS
FACT-1: There are 5 red peaches in the basket :: R(5)
FACT-2: There are 14 yellow peaches in the basket :: Y(14)
FACT-3: There are 6 green peaches in the basket :: G(6)

### RULES
RULE-1: Given a set of quantities, the sum equals the total count :: SUM(P1, P2, P3) = T(TotalPeaches)

### INFERENCES
INF-1: Summing the quantities gives us the total number of peaches :: SUM(R(5), Y(14), G(6)) = T(TotalPeaches)
INF-2: Substitute the known quantities into the equation :: (SUM(R(5), Y(14), G(6))) = T(19)
INF-3: The remaining quantity after accounting for red and yellow peaches is green peaches :: G(6) = T(19) - SUM(R(5), Y(14))
INF-4: Therefore, there are 5 green peaches in the basket :: G(5)

So, the answer is: Green and yellow peaches combined make up 14 peaches, which means there are 5 green peaches.
