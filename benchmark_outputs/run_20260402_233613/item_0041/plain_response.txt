To find out how many green and yellow peaches are in the basket, we need to add the number of green peaches to the number of yellow peaches.

Number of green peaches = 6
Number of yellow peaches = 14

Total number of green and yellow peaches = Number of green peaches + Number of yellow peaches
= 6 + 14
= 20

Therefore, there are 20 green and yellow peaches in the basket.

The answer is: 20
