To determine how many sacks of unripe oranges will be harvested after 6 days, we need to follow these steps:

1. Identify the number of unripe oranges harvested per day.
2. Multiply this daily amount by the number of days (which is 6 in this case).

From the problem statement:
- The number of unripe oranges harvested per day is 65 sacks.
- The number of days for the harvest is 6.

Now, let's calculate the total number of unripe oranges harvested over 6 days:

\[ \text{Total unripe oranges} = \text{Number of unripe oranges per day} \times \text{Number of days} \]

Substituting the given values:

\[ \text{Total unripe oranges} = 65 \times 6 \]

Performing the multiplication:

\[ 65 \times 6 = 390 \]

So, the total number of sacks of unripe oranges harvested after 6 days is 390.

Therefore, the answer is: 390.
