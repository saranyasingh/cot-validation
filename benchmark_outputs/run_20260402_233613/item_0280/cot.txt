Premise 1: They harvest 65 sacks of unripe oranges per day.
Premise 2: They harvest 46 sacks of ripe oranges per day.
Premise 3: We need to find out how many sacks of unripe oranges there will be after 6 days of harvest.

To solve this problem, we need to calculate the total number of sacks of unripe oranges harvested over 6 days. 

Step-by-step Logical Reasoning:

1. **Identify the daily production**: 
   - Unripe oranges are harvested at a rate of 65 sacks per day.

2. **Calculate the weekly production**:
   - Since there are 7 days in a week, we multiply the daily production by 7:
     \[
     65 \text{ sacks/day} \times 7 \text{ days/week} = 455 \text{ sacks/week}
     \]

3. **Calculate the production over 6 days**:
   - To find out how much is produced in 6 days, we divide the weekly production by 7 and then multiply by 6:
     \[
     \frac{455 \text{ sacks/week}}{7 \text{ days/week}} \times 6 \text{ days} = 65 \text{ sacks/week} \times 0.857 \approx 55 \text{ sacks}
     \]

So, after 6 days of harvest, they will have approximately 55 sacks of unripe oranges.

The answer is: 55
