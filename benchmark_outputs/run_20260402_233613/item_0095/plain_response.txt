To determine how many emails Jack received in the afternoon, we can follow these steps:

1. Let's denote the number of emails Jack received in the evening as \( E \).
2. According to the problem, Jack received 6 emails in the morning and 3 emails in the afternoon.
3. The total number of emails received throughout the day is given as 10.

We can set up an equation based on this information:
\[ \text{Total emails} = \text{Morning emails} + \text{Afternoon emails} + \text{Evening emails} \]
\[ 10 = 6 + 3 + E \]

Now, solve for \( E \):
\[ 10 = 9 + E \]
Subtract 9 from both sides:
\[ 10 - 9 = E \]
\[ E = 1 \]

Therefore, Jack received 1 email in the evening.

The answer is: 1
