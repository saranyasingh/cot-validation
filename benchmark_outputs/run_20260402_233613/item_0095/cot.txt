To determine how many emails Jack received in the afternoon, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Identify the given information:**
   - Jack received 6 emails in the morning.
   - Jack received 3 emails in the afternoon.
   - The total number of emails received in the day is 10.

2. **Set up an equation based on the total number of emails:**
   Let \( x \) represent the number of emails Jack received in the evening.

3. **Formulate the equation:**
   According to the problem, the sum of emails received in the morning, afternoon, and evening equals the total number of emails received in the day:
   \[
   6 + 3 + x = 10
   \]

4. **Solve the equation for \( x \):**
   First, combine the known quantities:
   \[
   9 + x = 10
   \]
   Next, isolate \( x \) by subtracting 9 from both sides:
   \[
   x = 10 - 9
   \]
   Simplify the right side:
   \[
   x = 1
   \]

5. **Conclusion:**
   Jack received 1 email in the evening.

Therefore, the answer is: Jack received 1 email in the afternoon.
