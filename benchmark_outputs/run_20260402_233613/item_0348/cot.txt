Premise 1: Matthew has some crackers.
Premise 2: Matthew gives 2 crackers to each friend.
Premise 3: Matthew has 11 friends.
Premise 4: We need to find out how many crackers Matthew originally had.

Step-by-step logical reasoning:

1. **Identify the total number of crackers given**: 
   - Since Matthew gives 2 crackers to each of his 11 friends, we calculate the total number of crackers given as:
     \[
     2 \text{ crackers/friend} \times 11 \text{ friends} = 22 \text{ crackers}
     \]

2. **Determine if any crackers were left over**:
   - In this scenario, no information suggests that Matthew didn't give away all his crackers or that he kept any back. Therefore, we assume he gave away all his crackers.

3. **Conclusion**:
   - Since Matthew gave away 22 crackers and there's no indication that he had leftover crackers, it means Matthew originally had exactly 22 crackers.

Therefore, the answer is: Matthew had 22 crackers initially.
