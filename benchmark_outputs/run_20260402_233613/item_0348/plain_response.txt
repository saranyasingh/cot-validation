To determine how many crackers Matthew originally had, we need to calculate the total number of crackers he distributed to his friends and then add that amount back to himself.

Given:
- Matthew has 11 friends.
- He gives 2 crackers to each friend.

First, let's find out how many crackers were given away in total:

\[ \text{Total crackers given} = \text{Number of friends} \times \text{Crackers per friend} \]
\[ \text{Total crackers given} = 11 \times 2 \]
\[ \text{Total crackers given} = 22 \]

Since this represents the total number of crackers Matthew gave away, it also equals the total number of crackers he originally had before giving any away.

Therefore, Matthew had 22 crackers initially.

The answer is: 22
