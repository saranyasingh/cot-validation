To determine how many books Frank read in 492 days, we can follow these steps:

1. Calculate the total number of pages Frank reads per day.
   - Since each book has 66 pages and it takes him 12 days to finish one book:
     \[
     \text{Pages per day} = \frac{66 \text{ pages}}{12 \text{ days}}
     \]
     Simplifying this gives:
     \[
     \text{Pages per day} = 5.5 \text{ pages/day}
     \]

2. Determine the total number of pages Frank read in 492 days.
   - Multiply the number of pages he reads per day by the total number of days:
     \[
     \text{Total pages} = 5.5 \text{ pages/day} \times 492 \text{ days}
     \]
     Calculating this product:
     \[
     \text{Total pages} = 2736 \text{ pages}
     \]

3. Find out how many books he read by dividing the total number of pages by the number of pages per book:
   - Divide the total number of pages by the number of pages per book:
     \[
     \text{Number of books} = \frac{2736 \text{ pages}}{66 \text{ pages/book}}
     \]
     Simplifying this division:
     \[
     \text{Number of books} = 42
     \]

Therefore, Frank read 42 books in 492 days. The answer is: 42.
