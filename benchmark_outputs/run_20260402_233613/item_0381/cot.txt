**Premise 1:** Each book has 66 pages.
- Premise 1 directly states that each book contains 66 pages.

**Premise 2:** It took Frank 12 days to finish each book.
- Premise 2 indicates that Frank spends 12 days on each book.

**Premise 3:** Frank takes 492 days to finish all books.
- Premise 3 states that the total time taken by Frank to finish all books is 492 days.

**Premise 4:** We need to find out how many books Frank read.
- Premise 4 sets up our goal to determine the number of books.

**Logical Reasoning:**

1. **Total Days Calculation**: 
   - Since it took Frank 12 days to finish each book, we calculate the total number of books he reads by dividing the total days spent by the days per book:
   \[
   \text{Number of Books} = \frac{\text{Total Days}}{\text{Days per Book}}
   \]
   Substituting the given values:
   \[
   \text{Number of Books} = \frac{492}{12}
   \]

2. **Perform the Division**:
   - Dividing 492 by 12 gives us:
   \[
   \frac{492}{12} = 41
   \]

Thus, Frank read 41 books.

**Conclusion:**
The answer is: The number of books Frank read is \(41\).
