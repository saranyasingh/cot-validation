Let's break down the problem using a logical syllogism:

**Premise 1:** Fred started last week with $19.
- This sets up our initial amount for Fred.

**Premise 2:** Fred ended last week with $40.
- This tells us how much Fred accumulated during the weekend.

**Premise 3:** Jason started last week with $16.
- This establishes his starting point.

**Premise 4:** Jason ended last week with $69.
- This gives us his ending amount.

**Premise 5:** The total earnings of both Fred and Jason over the weekend equals their combined earnings at the end minus their combined starting amounts.
- We need to find out how much Fred earned specifically.

**Conclusion:** Let’s denote Fred's earning as F and Jason's earning as J. According to Premises 2 and 4:
\[ F + J = 40 \]
\[ 16 + 69 = 85 \]

Since \(F\) (Fred's earnings) and \(J\) (Jason's earnings) together equal their combined earnings at the end, we subtract their combined starting amounts to find Fred's specific earnings:
\[ F = 40 - 16 \]
\[ F = 24 \]

Therefore, Fred earned $24 over the weekend.

The answer is: 24
