To determine how much money Fred earned over the weekend, we need to calculate the difference between the amount of money Fred has after washing cars and the amount he initially had.

Fred's initial amount of money:
\[ \text{Initial amount} = \$19 \]

Fred's final amount of money:
\[ \text{Final amount} = \$40 \]

The amount of money Fred earned over the weekend can be found by subtracting his initial amount from his final amount:
\[ \text{Amount earned} = \text{Final amount} - \text{Initial amount} \]
\[ \text{Amount earned} = \$40 - \$19 \]
\[ \text{Amount earned} = \$21 \]

Therefore, the answer is: 21
