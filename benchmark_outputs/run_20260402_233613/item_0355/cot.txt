1. **Premise 1:** Robin has 43 packages of gum.
   - This establishes that we're dealing with a quantity of items (packages) and it's given as 43.

2. **Premise 2:** Each package contains 23 pieces of gum.
   - This provides the size or content of each individual unit (piece of gum).

3. **Premise 3:** Robin has 8 extra pieces of gum.
   - This introduces an additional count of gum beyond what was originally stated.

4. **Logical Deduction 1:** To find out how much gum Robin has in total, we need to add up all the pieces of gum he has.
   - We start with the total number of packages: \(43\).
   - Then add the extra pieces of gum: \(8\).

5. **Calculation 1:** Total pieces of gum = Packages + Extra pieces.
   \[
   \text{Total pieces} = 43 + 8 = 51
   \]

6. **Conclusion:** Therefore, Robin has 51 pieces of gum in total.

The answer is: 51.
