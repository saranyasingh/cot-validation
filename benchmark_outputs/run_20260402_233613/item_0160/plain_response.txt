To determine how many aquariums the person had, we need to divide the total number of saltwater animals by the number of animals per aquarium.

Given:
- Total number of saltwater animals = 40
- Number of animals per aquarium = 2

We can calculate the number of aquariums as follows:

\[
\text{Number of aquariums} = \frac{\text{Total number of saltwater animals}}{\text{Number of animals per aquarium}}
\]

Substitute the given values into the formula:

\[
\text{Number of aquariums} = \frac{40}{2}
\]

Perform the division:

\[
\text{Number of aquariums} = 20
\]

Therefore, the person had 20 aquariums. The answer is: 20.
