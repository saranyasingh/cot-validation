To determine how many birds were initially sitting on the fence, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Identify the given information:**
   - Initially, there were \( x \) birds on the fence.
   - Then, 4 more birds joined them.
   - After this, the total number of birds on the fence is 5.

2. **Set up an equation based on the given information:**
   - Let's denote the initial number of birds as \( x \).
   - When 4 more birds joined, the new total became \( x + 4 \).

3. **Formulate the equation:**
   According to the problem statement:
   \[
   x + 4 = 5
   \]

4. **Solve the equation for \( x \):**
   Subtract 4 from both sides of the equation:
   \[
   x + 4 - 4 = 5 - 4
   \]
   Simplifying this gives us:
   \[
   x = 1
   \]

Therefore, the answer is: 
The answer is: 1

This means that initially, there was 1 bird sitting on the fence before 4 more birds arrived and they formed a total of 5 birds on the fence.
