Let's break down the problem step by step:

1. We know that after 4 more birds joined, the total number of birds became 5.
2. To find out how many birds were originally sitting on the fence, we need to subtract the 4 new birds from the total number of birds.

Calculation:
Total birds = 5
New birds = 4

Original birds = Total birds - New birds
Original birds = 5 - 4
Original birds = 1
