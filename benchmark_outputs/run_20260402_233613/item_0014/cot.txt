1. **Premise 1**: Jerry originally had 7 books.
   - This establishes the initial quantity of books.

2. **Premise 2**: Jerry also had 3 action figures initially.
   - This establishes the initial quantity of action figures.

3. **Premise 3**: Jerry then added 2 more action figures to the shelf.
   - This introduces an additional quantity that has been added to the collection.

4. **Conclusion from Premises 2 and 3**:
   - After adding 2 more action figures, Jerry now has \(3 + 2 = 5\) action figures.
   - Adding this new total (5 action figures) to the original number of books (7), we get:
     \[
     7 + 5 = 12 \text{ items}
     \]

5. **Comparison between books and action figures**:
   - We need to find out how many more books than action figures are on the shelf.
   - From the above calculation, there are 12 items in total, with 7 being books and 5 being action figures.
   - Therefore, the difference between the number of books and action figures is:
     \[
     7 - 5 = 2
     \]

6. **Final Answer**:
   - There are 2 more books than action figures on Jerry's shelf.

The answer is: 2
