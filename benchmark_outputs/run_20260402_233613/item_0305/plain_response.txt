To find out how many more green peaches there are than yellow peaches, we need to subtract the number of yellow peaches from the number of green peaches:

Green peaches - Yellow peaches = Difference

Given:
- Green peaches = 14
- Yellow peaches = 6

So,
Difference = 14 - 6 = 8

Therefore, the answer is: The difference between the number of green and yellow peaches is 8.
