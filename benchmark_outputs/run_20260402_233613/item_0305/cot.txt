To determine how many more green peaches than yellow peaches are in the basket, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Identify the quantities of each type of peach:**
   - Red peaches: 2
   - Yellow peaches: 6
   - Green peaches: 14

2. **Determine the difference between the number of green peaches and yellow peaches:**
   - We subtract the quantity of yellow peaches from the quantity of green peaches:
     \[
     14 - 6 = 8
     \]

3. **Conclusion:**
   - There are 8 more green peaches than yellow peaches in the basket.
