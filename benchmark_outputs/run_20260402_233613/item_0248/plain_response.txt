To determine how many peaches Jill has, we can follow these steps:

1. First, let's find out how many peaches Jake has. Since Jake has 7 fewer peaches than Steven and Steven has 17 peaches:
   \[
   \text{Jake's peaches} = \text{Steven's peaches} - 7 = 17 - 7 = 10
   \]

2. Next, we know that Jake has 8 more peaches than Jill. So, to find out how many peaches Jill has, we subtract 8 from Jake's number of peaches:
   \[
   \text{Jill's peaches} = \text{Jake's peaches} - 8 = 10 - 8 = 2
   \]

Therefore, Jill has 2 peaches. The answer is: 2.
