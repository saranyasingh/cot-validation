Let's break down the problem using a logical syllogism:

**Premise 1:** Julia played tag with 17 kids on Monday.
- This is our starting point.

**Premise 2:** Julia played tag with 15 kids on Tuesday.
- We need to add this information to the first one.

**Premise 3:** Julia played tag with 2 kids on Wednesday.
- We also have this additional piece of information.

To find out how many kids Julia played with altogether, we need to sum up all the kids from each day:
- Total = Kids on Monday + Kids on Tuesday + Kids on Wednesday

Now let's do the calculation:
- Total = 17 (Monday) + 15 (Tuesday) + 2 (Wednesday)
- Total = 34

Therefore, the total number of kids Julia played with altogether is **34**.
