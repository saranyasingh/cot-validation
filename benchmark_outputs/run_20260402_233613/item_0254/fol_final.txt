### ## FACTS
- Initially, there were 2 roses in the vase.
- Jessica threw away 4 roses from the vase.
- After throwing away 4 roses, there should have been -2 roses remaining, which is impossible.

### ## RULES
- Starting with 0 roses and adding more increases the total.
- Throwing away 4 roses leaves at least one rose behind.

### ## INFERENCES
- Jessica did not throw away any roses. Therefore, she must have started with 0 roses. Adding 23 new roses results in a total of 23 roses. Hence, Jessica added 23 roses to the vase.
