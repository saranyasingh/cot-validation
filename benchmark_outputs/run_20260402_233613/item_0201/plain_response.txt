To find out how much more money Olivia spent at the supermarket than she collected at the ATM, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the total amount of money Olivia had after collecting from the ATM.
2. Subtract this total from the initial amount in her wallet to determine how much was left after shopping at the supermarket.
3. The difference between what was left and the initial amount will give us the additional money spent on groceries.

Let's break it down step-by-step:

1. Initial amount in Olivia's wallet = $53
   Amount collected from ATM = $91

   Total amount after collecting from ATM = $53 + $91 = $144

2. Amount left after visiting the supermarket = $14

3. Additional money spent at the supermarket = Total amount - Amount left
   = $144 - $14 = $130

Therefore, Olivia spent $130 more at the supermarket than she collected from the ATM.
