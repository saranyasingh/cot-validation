Let's break down this problem using a logical syllogism:

Premise 1: Olivia had $53 initially.
Premise 2: She collected $91 from an ATM.
Premise 3: After visiting the supermarket, she had $14 left.

From these premises, we need to determine how much more money she spent at the supermarket compared to what she collected from the ATM.

Step 1: Calculate the total amount of money before visiting the supermarket.
- Initial amount: $53
- Amount collected from ATM: $91

Total amount = $53 + $91 = $144

Step 2: Determine the amount of money left after visiting the supermarket.
- Total amount before supermarket visit: $144
- Amount left after visit: $14

Amount spent at supermarket = Total amount - Amount left = $144 - $14 = $130

Step 3: Compare the amount spent at the supermarket to the amount collected from the ATM.
- Amount spent at supermarket: $130
- Amount collected from ATM: $91

Difference = Amount spent at supermarket - Amount collected from ATM = $130 - $91 = $39

The answer is: The amount more money that Olivia spent at the supermarket than she collected from the ATM is $39.
