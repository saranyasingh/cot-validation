To determine how long the soda bottles would last, we need to calculate the total number of days based on the consumption rate of both soda and water. We'll break this down into steps:

**Step 1:** Calculate the total amount of soda consumed.
- Debby buys 360 soda bottles initially.
- She drinks 9 soda bottles per day.
- Total soda consumed = Initial soda bottles * Daily consumption rate

\[ \text{Total soda consumed} = 360 \times 9 = 3240 \text{ bottles/day} \]

**Step 2:** Calculate the total amount of water consumed.
- Debby buys 162 water bottles initially.
- She drinks 122 water bottles per day.
- Total water consumed = Initial water bottles * Daily consumption rate

\[ \text{Total water consumed} = 162 \times 122 = 19584 \text{ bottles/day} \]

**Step 3:** Determine the duration the soda bottles would last given their consumption rates.
- We know that 3240 soda bottles would be consumed over the course of 360 days (since there are 360 soda bottles).
- To find out how many days the soda bottles would last, divide the total number of soda bottles available by the daily consumption rate.

\[ \text{Days the soda bottles would last} = \frac{\text{Total soda bottles}}{\text{Daily consumption rate}} \]
\[ \text{Days the soda bottles would last} = \frac{360}{9} = 40 \text{ days} \]

Therefore, the answer is: The soda bottles would last 40 days.
