To determine how many days the soda bottles would last, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the total number of soda bottles Debby has.
2. Determine how many days the soda bottles will last based on her daily consumption rate.

Step 1: Total number of soda bottles = 360

Step 2: Daily consumption of soda bottles = 9 per day

Now, divide the total number of soda bottles by the daily consumption:
\[ \text{Number of days} = \frac{\text{Total number of soda bottles}}{\text{Daily consumption}} = \frac{360}{9} = 40 \]

Therefore, the soda bottles would last for 40 days.

The answer is: 40
