Let's break down the problem using a logical syllogism:

**Premise 1:** Julia played tag with 5 kids on Monday.
- This establishes that there were 5 kids involved on Monday.

**Premise 2:** Julia played tag with some more kids on Tuesday.
- We need to determine how many kids she played with on Tuesday.

**Premise 3:** Julia played with a total of 15 kids over both days.
- This gives us the combined total number of kids she played with on both days.

To find out how many kids she played with on Tuesday, we can set up an equation based on the information provided:

**Step 1:** Let \( x \) represent the number of kids Julia played with on Tuesday.
- From Premise 2 and Premise 3, we know:
  - Total kids = Kids on Monday + Kids on Tuesday

**Step 2:** Substitute the known values into the equation:
  - 15 = 5 (kids on Monday) + x (kids on Tuesday)

**Step 3:** Solve for \( x \):
  - 15 = 5 + x
  - Subtract 5 from both sides:
    - 10 = x

Therefore, Julia played with 10 kids on Tuesday.

The answer is: The answer is: 10.
