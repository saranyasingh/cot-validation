To find out how many kids Julia played with on Tuesday, we can use the information given and perform some simple arithmetic operations.

We know:
- Julia played tag with 5 kids on Monday.
- The total number of kids she played with over both days was 15.

Let's denote the number of kids she played with on Tuesday as \( x \).

According to the problem, the sum of the kids from Monday and Tuesday equals the total number of kids:

\[ 5 + x = 15 \]

Now, solve for \( x \):

Subtract 5 from both sides of the equation:

\[ x = 15 - 5 \]
\[ x = 10 \]

So, Julia played with 10 kids on Tuesday.

Therefore, the answer is: 10
