To determine how much more money Gwen received from her dad than from her mom, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the total amount of money Gwen received.
   - From her dad: \(5\) dollars
   - From her mom: \(2\) dollars
   - Total received: \(5 + 2 = 7\) dollars

2. Determine how much money Gwen spent.
   - Amount spent: \(6\) dollars

3. Subtract the amount spent from the total amount received to find out how much money Gwen has left.
   - Money remaining: \(7 - 6 = 1\) dollar

4. Compare the amount of money Gwen received from her dad (which is \(5\) dollars) with the amount she received from her mom (\(2\) dollars).

5. Calculate the difference between the two amounts:
   - Difference: \(5 - 2 = 3\) dollars

Therefore, Gwen received \(\boxed{3}\) dollars more from her dad than from her mom.
