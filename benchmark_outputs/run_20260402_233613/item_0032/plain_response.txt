To find out how many seats the Ferris wheel has, we need to divide the total number of people that can ride the wheel at once (which is given as 18) by the capacity of each seat (which is given as 9).

Let's calculate this:

\[ \text{Number of seats} = \frac{\text{Total number of people}}{\text{Capacity per seat}} \]

\[ \text{Number of seats} = \frac{18}{9} \]

\[ \text{Number of seats} = 2 \]

So, the Ferris wheel has 2 seats. 

Therefore, the answer is: 2
