To determine how many seats the Ferris wheel has, we need to follow these steps:

**Premise 1:** There are 18 people who can ride the Ferris wheel simultaneously.
- This establishes that the number of riders equals the total capacity of the Ferris wheel.

**Premise 2:** Each seat on the Ferris wheel can hold 9 people.
- This defines the capacity per seat.

**Conclusion:** Therefore, the Ferris wheel must have \( \frac{18}{9} = 2 \) seats.

**Explanation of Logical Reasoning:**

1. We start with Premise 1 which tells us there are 18 people riding the Ferris wheel at once.
2. We then use Premise 2, which states that each seat holds exactly 9 people.
3. To find out how many seats are needed to accommodate all 18 people, we divide the total number of people (18) by the capacity per seat (9).
4. Performing this division gives us \( \frac{18}{9} = 2 \).

Thus, logically deduced, the Ferris wheel has **2 seats**.
