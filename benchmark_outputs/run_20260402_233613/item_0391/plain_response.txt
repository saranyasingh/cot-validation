To find out how much Edward had before spending his money, we need to add up all the expenses he made:

Expenses:
- Books: $6
- Pens: $16

Total Expenses = $6 + $16 = $22

Now, subtract the total expenses from the amount of money Edward has left after making these purchases:

Money Left: $19
Total Expenses: $22

Money Before Purchases = Money Left - Total Expenses
Money Before Purchases = $19 - $22 = -$3

Since the result is negative, it means that Edward does not have enough money to cover his expenses; he actually owes $3. Therefore, the correct calculation should be:

Money Before Purchases = Money Left + Total Expenses
Money Before Purchases = $19 + $22 = $41

So, Edward had $41 before he spent any of his money on books and pens. The answer is: 41
