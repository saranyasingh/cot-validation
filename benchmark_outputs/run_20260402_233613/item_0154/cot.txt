1. **Premise:** Frank started with 33 pieces of candy.
   - Premise Explanation: This establishes the initial quantity of candies available to be manipulated or altered according to other premises.

2. **Premise:** Frank lost 24 pieces of candy.
   - Premise Explanation: This introduces an action (losing) that reduces the total number of candies, setting up a subtraction operation later.

3. **Conclusion:** We need to determine how many bags can hold 9 pieces each after losing some.
   
   - Let's calculate the remaining candies:
     \[
     33 - 24 = 9
     \]
   - So, Frank has 9 pieces of candy left.

4. **Premise:** Each bag should contain exactly 9 pieces of candy.
   - Premise Explanation: This provides a specific requirement on how the remaining candies should be distributed.

5. **Calculation:** To find out how many bags can be filled with 9 pieces each,
   \[
   \frac{9}{9} = 1
   \]

**Logical Syllogism Summary:**

- Starting with 33 pieces of candy and losing 24 leaves us with 9 pieces.
- Since we want to fill these 9 pieces into bags of 9, we divide 9 by 9, resulting in 1 bag.

**Answer:** The answer is: 1 bag.
