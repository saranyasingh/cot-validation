To determine how many bags Frank would have, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the number of candies left after losing some.
2. Determine how many bags can be filled with the remaining candies.

Step 1: Calculate the number of candies left.
Frank originally had 33 pieces of candy and lost 24 of them. So, the number of candies left is:
\[ 33 - 24 = 9 \]

Step 2: Determine how many bags can be filled with the remaining candies.
Each bag contains 9 pieces of candy. To find out how many full bags can be made from 9 pieces, we divide the remaining candies by the number of pieces per bag:
\[ 9 \div 9 = 1 \]

Therefore, Frank would have 1 bag of candy. The answer is: 1.
