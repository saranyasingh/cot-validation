To determine how many more customers stayed behind than those that left, we can follow these steps:

1. Identify the number of customers who initially came to the table.
2. Identify the number of customers remaining after some have left.
3. Calculate the difference between the initial number of customers and the number of customers remaining.

Given:
- Initially, there were 3 customers.
- After some customers left, there were 4 customers remaining.

Now, calculate the difference:
\[ \text{Number of customers who stayed} - \text{Number of customers who left} = 4 - 3 = 1 \]

Therefore, 1 more customer stayed behind than those that left.

The answer is: 1
