To determine how many more emails Jack received in the morning than in the afternoon, we need to follow these steps:

**Premise 1:** Jack received 10 emails in the morning.
- This establishes the number of emails he received during that specific time period.

**Premise 2:** Jack received 7 emails in the afternoon.
- This provides another count of the emails he received at a different time.

**Premise 3:** We want to find out how many more emails Jack received in the morning than in the afternoon.
- This is our goal, which connects the two pieces of information provided in Premises 1 and 2.

**Step-by-step Logical Reasoning:**

1. **Identify the difference between the morning and afternoon counts:**
   - Morning emails = 10
   - Afternoon emails = 7

2. **Calculate the difference:**
   - Difference = Morning emails - Afternoon emails
   - Difference = 10 - 7

3. **Perform the subtraction:**
   - Difference = 3

Therefore, Jack received 3 more emails in the morning than in the afternoon.

The answer is: The answer is: 3
