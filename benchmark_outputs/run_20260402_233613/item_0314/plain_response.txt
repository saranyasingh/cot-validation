To determine how many emails Jack received in the afternoon, we can use the information given:

1. Total emails received = 9
2. Emails received in the morning = 7

We need to find out how many emails were received in the afternoon. We can do this by subtracting the number of emails received in the morning from the total number of emails:

\[ \text{Emails received in the afternoon} = \text{Total emails} - \text{Emails received in the morning} \]
\[ \text{Emails received in the afternoon} = 9 - 7 \]
\[ \text{Emails received in the afternoon} = 2 \]

Therefore, Jack received 2 emails in the afternoon. The answer is: 2.
