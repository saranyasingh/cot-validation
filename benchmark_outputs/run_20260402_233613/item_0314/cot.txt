1. **Premise 1:** Jack received a total of 9 emails in the day.
   - This is our starting point.

2. **Premise 2:** He received 7 emails in the morning.
   - We know how many emails he got during the first part of the day (morning).

3. **Conclusion:** To find out how many emails Jack received in the afternoon, we need to subtract the number of emails he received in the morning from the total number of emails he received that day.
   
4. **Calculation:**  
   \[
   \text{Emails in the afternoon} = \text{Total emails} - \text{Morning emails}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Emails in the afternoon} = 9 - 7
   \]
   \[
   \text{Emails in the afternoon} = 2
   \]

Therefore, the answer is: Jack received 2 emails in the afternoon.
