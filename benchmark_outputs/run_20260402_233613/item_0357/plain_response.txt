To find out how many bottles of soda the grocery store had in total, we need to add up all the different types of sodas:

Regular soda: 57 bottles
Diet soda: 26 bottles
Lite soda: 27 bottles

Total = Regular soda + Diet soda + Lite soda
Total = 57 + 26 + 27
Total = 108
