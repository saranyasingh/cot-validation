To determine how much money the Razorback t-shirt shop made from selling the t-shirts during the Arkansas and Texas Tech game, we need to follow these steps:

**Premise 1:** Each t-shirt is normally priced at $51.
**Premise 2:** A discount of $8 was applied to each t-shirt during the event.
**Premise 3:** They sold 130 t-shirts during this event.

We want to find out how much total revenue was generated from these sales.

**Step-by-Step Logical Reasoning:**

1. **Calculate the discounted price of each t-shirt:**
   - Original price = $51
   - Discount = $8
   - Discounted price = $51 - $8 = $43

2. **Determine the number of t-shirts sold at the discounted price:**
   - Number of t-shirts sold = 130

3. **Calculate the total revenue from the sale of the discounted t-shirts:**
   - Total revenue = Discounted price per t-shirt × Number of t-shirts sold
   - Total revenue = $43 × 130

Now let's calculate the total revenue:

\[ \text{Total Revenue} = 43 \times 130 = 5590 \]

So, the Razorback t-shirt shop made $\boxed{5590}$ dollars from selling the t-shirts during the Arkansas and Texas Tech game.
