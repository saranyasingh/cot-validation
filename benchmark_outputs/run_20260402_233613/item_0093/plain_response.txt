To calculate the total revenue made from selling the t-shirts after the discount, we need to follow these steps:

1. Determine the discounted price of each t-shirt.
2. Calculate the total number of t-shirts sold at the discounted price.
3. Multiply the discounted price by the number of t-shirts sold to get the total revenue.

Step 1: The original price of each t-shirt is $51, but there's a $8 discount per t-shirt.
Discounted price = Original price - Discount
Discounted price = $51 - $8
Discounted price = $43

Step 2: They sold 130 t-shirts during the game.
Total number of t-shirts sold = Number of t-shirts sold * Price per t-shirt (discounted)
Total number of t-shirts sold = 130 * $43
Total number of t-shirts sold = $5590

Step 3: Now, multiply the discounted price by the number of t-shirts sold to find the total revenue.
Total revenue = Discounted price * Total number of t-shirts sold
Total revenue = $43 * $5590
Total revenue = $24,177

Therefore, the Razorback t-shirt shop made $24,177 from selling the t-shirts during the Arkansas and Texas Tech game.
