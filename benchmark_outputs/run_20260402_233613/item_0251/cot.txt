To determine how many cakes the baker would still have after selling some, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Initial number of cakes**: Baker initially made 149 cakes.
2. **Cakes sold**: Baker sold 10 cakes.
3. **Calculate remaining cakes**:
   - Subtract the number of cakes sold from the initial number of cakes:
     \[
     149 - 10 = 139
     \]
   
So, the baker would still have 139 cakes after selling 10 cakes.

The answer is: 139
