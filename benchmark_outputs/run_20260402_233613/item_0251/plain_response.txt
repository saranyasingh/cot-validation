To determine how many cakes the baker would still have after selling some, we need to subtract the number of cakes sold from the total number of cakes baked:

Total cakes baked = 149
Cakes sold = 10

Remaining cakes = Total cakes baked - Cakes sold
Remaining cakes = 149 - 10
Remaining cakes = 139

So, the baker would still have 139 cakes. 

The answer is: 139
