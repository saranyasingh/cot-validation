1. **Identify the quantities given:**
   - Regular soda: 79 bottles
   - Diet soda: 53 bottles

2. **Set up the comparison:**
   We need to find how many more bottles of regular soda there are compared to diet soda.

3. **Apply subtraction:**
   To determine this difference, subtract the number of diet soda bottles from the number of regular soda bottles.
   
   \[
   \text{Difference} = \text{Number of regular soda bottles} - \text{Number of diet soda bottles}
   \]
   Substituting the values:
   \[
   \text{Difference} = 79 - 53
   \]

4. **Perform the subtraction:**
   \[
   79 - 53 = 26
   \]

5. **Conclusion:**
   The grocery store has 26 more bottles of regular soda than diet soda.

The answer is: 26
