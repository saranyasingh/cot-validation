To find out how many more bottles of regular soda than diet soda the grocery store had, we need to subtract the number of diet sodas from the number of regular sodas.

Number of regular sodas = 79
Number of diet sodas = 53

Difference = Number of regular sodas - Number of diet sodas
           = 79 - 53
           = 26

So, the grocery store had 26 more bottles of regular soda than diet soda. The answer is: 26.
