To determine how many pencils Faye has, we need to calculate the total number of pencils based on the information given about the arrangement.

**Premise 1:** Faye places her pencils and crayons into 19 rows.
- Premise Explanation: This establishes that there are 19 rows, which will be used to distribute both pencils and crayons.

**Premise 2:** Each row contains 4 pencils.
- Premise Explanation: This indicates that within each of the 19 rows, there are exactly 4 pencils placed.

**Step-by-step Logical Reasoning:**
1. **Calculate Total Number of Pencils:**
   - Since there are 19 rows and each row contains 4 pencils,
   \[
   \text{Total number of pencils} = \text{Number of rows} \times \text{Pencils per row}
   \]
   Substituting the values:
   \[
   \text{Total number of pencils} = 19 \times 4
   \]

2. **Perform the Multiplication:**
   \[
   19 \times 4 = 76
   \]

Therefore, the total number of pencils Faye has is:

The answer is: 76
