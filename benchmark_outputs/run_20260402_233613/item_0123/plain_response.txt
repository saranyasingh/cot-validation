\[ \text{Number of pencils per row} = 4 \]

\[ \text{Total number of pencils} = 4 \times 19 \]

\[ 4 \times 19 = 4 \times (20 - 1) = 4 \times 20 - 4 \times 1 = 80 - 4 = 76 \]
