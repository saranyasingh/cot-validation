1. **Premise 1:** Sofia requested that students suggest specific types of food.
   - This is our starting point as it sets up the scenario where students are being asked to contribute suggestions.

2. **Premise 2:** Out of 450 students, 38 suggested adding bacon to the menu.
   - We know this because the problem directly states this fact.

3. **Premise 3:** Of the remaining students (after accounting for those who added bacon), the rest did not participate in suggesting any new foods.
   - This means we need to subtract the number of students who added bacon from the total number of students to find out how many didn't add anything.

4. **Calculation Step:** 
   - Total students = 450
   - Students who added bacon = 38
   - Students who did not add anything = Total students - Students who added bacon
     \[
     450 - 38 = 412
     \]

5. **Conclusion:** Therefore, 412 students participated in the suggestion of new food items.

The answer is: The answer is: 412
