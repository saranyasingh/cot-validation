Premise 1: Zachary did 44 push-ups and 17 crunches.
Premise 2: David did 29 more push-ups than Zachary.
Premise 3: David did 13 fewer crunches than Zachary.

To find out how many crunches David did:

Step 1: Calculate the number of push-ups David did:
- Given: Zachary did 44 push-ups.
- Additional: David did 29 more push-ups than Zachary.
- Calculation: \( 44 + 29 = 73 \) push-ups

Step 2: Calculate the number of crunches David did:
- Given: Zachary did 17 crunches.
- Less: David did 13 fewer crunches than Zachary.
- Calculation: \( 17 - 13 = 4 \) crunches

Therefore, David did 4 crunches.

The answer is: 4
