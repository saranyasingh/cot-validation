To determine how many crunches David did, we start by identifying the number of crunches Zachary did. According to the problem, Zachary did 17 crunches.

Next, we need to find out how many crunches David did. The problem states that David did 13 fewer crunches than Zachary. Therefore, we subtract 13 from the number of crunches Zachary did:

\[ \text{Crunches David did} = \text{Crunches Zachary did} - 13 \]
\[ \text{Crunches David did} = 17 - 13 \]
\[ \text{Crunches David did} = 4 \]

Thus, David did 4 crunches. The answer is: 4.
