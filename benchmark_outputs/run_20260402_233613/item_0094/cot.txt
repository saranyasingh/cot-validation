**Logical Syllogism**

Premise 1: Jack received 3 emails in the afternoon.
Premise 2: Jack received 6 emails in the morning.
Premise 3: Let x be the number of emails Jack received in the evening.
Premise 4: The total number of emails Jack received in the day is 10.

**Conclusion:** Therefore, Jack must have received \(x\) emails in the evening such that:

\[ \text{Total emails} = \text{Emails in the afternoon} + \text{Emails in the morning} + \text{Emails in the evening} \]

Given:
- Total emails (T) = 10
- Emails in the afternoon (A) = 3
- Emails in the morning (M) = 6

We substitute these values into our equation:

\[ T = A + M + x \]
\[ 10 = 3 + 6 + x \]

Solving for \(x\):

\[ 10 = 9 + x \]
\[ x = 10 - 9 \]
\[ x = 1 \]

**Answer:** The number of emails Jack received in the evening is **1**.
