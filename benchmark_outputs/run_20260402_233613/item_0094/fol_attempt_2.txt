### FACTS
FACT-1: Jack received 3 emails in the afternoon :: Received(3, 'afternoon')
FACT-2: Jack received 6 emails in the morning :: Received(6, 'morning')
FACT-3: Jack received \(x\) emails in the evening :: Received(x, 'evening')

### RULES
RULE-1: If a person receives emails in a specific time slot, then they receive those emails at that time :: ∀time (Received(time, slot) → Received(time, slot))

### INFERENCES
INF-1: Since Jack received emails in both the morning and the afternoon, he must have also received them in the evening :: Received(3, 'afternoon') ∧ Received(6, 'morning') → Received(x, 'evening')

INF-2: Given the total number of emails received throughout the day is 10, we can set up an equation to find \(x\) :: TotalEmails(T) = Received(3, 'afternoon') + Received(6, 'morning') + Received(x, 'evening')

INF-3: Solving for \(x\):
\[ 10 = 3 + 6 + x \]
\[ x = 10 - 9 \]
\[ x = 1 \]
