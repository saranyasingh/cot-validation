To determine how much farther the frog jumped than the grasshopper, we need to subtract the distance the grasshopper jumped from the distance the frog jumped. This problem can be represented as a simple subtraction operation:

Premise 1: The frog jumped 39 inches.
Premise 2: The grasshopper jumped 17 inches.
Premise 3: To find out how much farther the frog jumped, we need to calculate 39 - 17.

Therefore, following the rules of arithmetic:
Step 1: Subtract the number of inches the grasshopper jumped (17) from the number of inches the frog jumped (39).
39 - 17 = 22

Conclusion: The frog jumped 22 inches farther than the grasshopper.

The answer is: 22
