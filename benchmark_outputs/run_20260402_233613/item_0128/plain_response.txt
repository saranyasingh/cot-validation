To find out how much farther the frog jumped than the grasshopper, we need to subtract the distance the grasshopper jumped from the distance the frog jumped:

Frog's jump - Grasshopper's jump = Difference

Given:
- Frog's jump = 39 inches
- Grasshopper's jump = 17 inches

Now, let's perform the subtraction:

39 inches - 17 inches = 22 inches

Therefore, the frog jumped 22 inches farther than the grasshopper.
