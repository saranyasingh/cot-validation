To determine how many birds are currently sitting on the fence, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Initial Count**: There were initially 4 birds and 46 storks on the fence.
2. **Additional Birds Joining**: 6 more birds joined them.

Now let's break this down into a logical syllogism:

### Premise 1:
There were initially 4 birds on the fence.

Premise 2:
There were also 46 storks on the fence.

Premise 3:
6 more birds came to join the initial group.

### Step-by-Step Reasoning:
1. **Total Initial Animals**:
   - We start with 4 birds + 46 storks = 50 animals in total.
   
2. **Birds After Addition**:
   - Since 6 more birds came to join the existing group of 4 birds,
   - The new count of birds becomes \( 4 \) (initially there) + \( 6 \) (added) = 10 birds.

Therefore, after adding the additional 6 birds, the number of birds sitting on the fence is 10.

### Conclusion:
The answer is: 10 birds.
