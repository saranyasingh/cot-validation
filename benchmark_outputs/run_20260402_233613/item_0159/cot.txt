To determine how many more trees died in the typhoon compared to those that survived, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Identify the total number of trees Haley initially planted:**  
   Haley planted 14 trees.

2. **Calculate the number of trees that survived after the typhoon:**  
   After the typhoon, 9 trees died out of the initial 14 trees. Therefore, the number of trees that survived is:
   \[
   14 - 9 = 5
   \]

3. **Determine the number of trees that died in the typhoon:**  
   We know that 9 trees died during the typhoon.

4. **Compare the number of trees that died to the number of trees that survived:**  
   To find how many more trees died than those that survived, subtract the number of surviving trees from the number of dead trees:
   \[
   9 - 5 = 4
   \]

**Conclusion:**  
The answer is: The number of trees that died in the typhoon (9) is 4 more than the number of trees that survived (5).

The final answer is: 4.
