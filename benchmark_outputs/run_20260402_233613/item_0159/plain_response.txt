To determine how many more trees died in the typhoon compared to those that survived, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the number of trees that survived after the typhoon.
2. Subtract the number of trees that survived from the number of trees that died.

First, let's find out how many trees survived:
\[ \text{Number of surviving trees} = \text{Total number of trees} - \text{Number of dead trees} \]
\[ \text{Number of surviving trees} = 14 - 9 \]
\[ \text{Number of surviving trees} = 5 \]

Next, we calculate how many more trees died than those that survived:
\[ \text{Difference} = \text{Number of dead trees} - \text{Number of surviving trees} \]
\[ \text{Difference} = 9 - 5 \]
\[ \text{Difference} = 4 \]

Therefore, 4 more trees died in the typhoon than those that survived. 

The answer is: 4
