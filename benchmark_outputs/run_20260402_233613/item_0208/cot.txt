Let's break down the problem into logical steps using a syllogistic approach:

**Premise 1:** The Razorback shop sells t-shirts for $215 each.
- This establishes the price per unit (t-shirt).

**Premise 2:** The Razorback shop sold 20 t-shirts during the game.
- This specifies the quantity sold (t-shirts).

**Premise 3:** Each t-shirt sold brings in $215 revenue.
- This states the profit made on each t-shirt sale.

**Conclusion:** The total revenue from selling 20 t-shirts is calculated as:
\[ \text{Total Revenue} = \text{Price per T-Shirt} \times \text{Number Sold} \]
\[ \text{Total Revenue} = \$215 \times 20 \]

Now let’s perform the multiplication to find the total revenue:

\[ \$215 \times 20 = \$4300 \]

Therefore, the Razorback shop made **$4300** from selling the t-shirts.
