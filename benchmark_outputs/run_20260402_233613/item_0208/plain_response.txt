To calculate how much money the Razorback shop made from selling the t-shirts during the Arkansas and Texas Tech game, we need to follow these steps:

1. Determine the price of one t-shirt.
2. Multiply the number of t-shirts sold by the price per t-shirt.

Given:
- Price per t-shirt = $215
- Number of t-shirts sold = 20

Now, let's perform the calculation:

\[ \text{Total revenue from t-shirts} = \text{Price per t-shirt} \times \text{Number of t-shirts sold} \]
\[ \text{Total revenue from t-shirts} = \$215 \times 20 \]

Let's do the multiplication:

\[ \$215 \times 20 = \$4300 \]

So, the Razorback shop made $4300 from selling the t-shirts.

The answer is: $4300
