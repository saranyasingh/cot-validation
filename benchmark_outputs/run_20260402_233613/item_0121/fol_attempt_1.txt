FACT-1: Initially there were 4 birds.
FACT-2: Initially there were 3 storks.
RULE-1: Adding 6 more storks increases the count of storks by 6.
INF-1: After adding 6 more storks, there are now 9 storks.
INF-2: Subtracting the initial number of birds from the current total number of storks gives the difference.
INF-3: Applying INF-2 results in \(9 - 4 = 5\).
