To determine how many more storks than birds are sitting on the fence, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Initial Count**: 
   - There are 4 birds.
   - There are initially 3 storks.

2. **Additional Storks**:
   - 6 more storks come to join them.

3. **Total Number of Birds**:
   - Initial number of birds + additional birds = \(4 + 0 = 4\) (since no new birds joined).

4. **Total Number of Storks**:
   - Initial number of storks + additional storks = \(3 + 6 = 9\).

5. **Difference Between Storks and Birds**:
   - To find out how many more storks there are compared to birds, subtract the total number of birds from the total number of storks:
     \[
     9 - 4 = 5
     \]
