Let's break down the problem step by step:

1. Initially, there are 4 birds and 3 storks sitting on the fence.
2. Then, 6 more storks come to join them.
3. Now we need to calculate how many more storks there are compared to the number of birds.

First, let's find out how many storks are now on the fence:
- Initial storks: 3
- Storks that joined: 6
- Total storks: 3 + 6 = 9

Next, we compare the total number of storks to the number of birds:
- Birds: 4
- Storks: 9

Now, we subtract the number of birds from the number of storks to find out how many more storks there are:
- Difference: 9 - 4 = 5

Therefore, there are 5 more storks than birds sitting on the fence.

The answer is: 5
