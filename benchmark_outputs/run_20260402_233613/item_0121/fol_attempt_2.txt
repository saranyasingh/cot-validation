## FACTS
- Initially there were 4 birds.
- Initially there were 3 storks.

## RULES
- Rule-1: Adding 6 more storks increases the count of storks by 6.

## INFERENCES
- After adding 6 more storks, there are now 9 storks.
- Subtracting the initial number of birds from the current total number of storks gives the difference.
- Applying INF-2 results in \(9 - 4 = 5\).
