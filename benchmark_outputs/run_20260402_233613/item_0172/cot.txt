Premise 1: After visiting the supermarket, Olivia had 29 dollars left.
Premise 2: Olivia started with 54 dollars in her wallet initially.
Premise 3: We need to determine how much Olivia spent at the supermarket.

**Logical Syllogism**

1. **Starting amount:** Olivia started with 54 dollars (P1).
2. **Final amount after spending:** She had 29 dollars left after shopping (P2).
3. **Total spent calculation:** To find out how much she spent, we subtract the final amount from the initial amount (Q).

**Calculation**
- Initial amount = 54 dollars
- Final amount = 29 dollars

Spent amount = Initial amount - Final amount
= 54 - 29
= 25 dollars

**Conclusion**: Olivia spent 25 dollars at the supermarket.

The answer is: Olivia spent $25 at the supermarket.
