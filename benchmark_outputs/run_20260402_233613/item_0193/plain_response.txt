Let's denote the number of red peaches as R and the number of green peaches as G.

According to the problem:
1. There are 3 red peaches (R = 3).
2. There are 10 more green peaches than red peaches (G = R + 10).

Now we can substitute the value of R into the equation for G:

G = 3 + 10
G = 13

So, there are 13 green peaches in the basket.

Therefore, the answer is: The answer is: 13.
