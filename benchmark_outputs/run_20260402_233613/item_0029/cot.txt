Let's break down this problem into a logical syllogism:

1. **Premise 1:** Zachary did 44 push-ups.
   - This establishes the number of push-ups Zachary performed as 44.

2. **Premise 2:** David did 58 more push-ups than Zachary.
   - From Premise 1, we know that Zachary did 44 push-ups.
   - Adding 58 to Zachary’s total gives us David's total:
     \[
     44 + 58 = 102
     \]
   - Therefore, David did 102 push-ups.

3. **Conclusion:** To find out how many push-ups Zachary and David did together, we add their individual totals:
   \[
   44 + 102 = 146
   \]

Thus, the answer is: The combined total of push-ups done by Zachary and David is 146.
