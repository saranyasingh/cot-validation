To determine the total number of green peaches in all seven baskets, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Identify the number of green peaches per basket**: 
   - Each basket contains 2 green peaches.
   
2. **Determine the number of baskets**:
   - There are 7 baskets in total.
   
3. **Calculate the total number of green peaches**:
   - Multiply the number of green peaches per basket (2) by the number of baskets (7).
     \[
     2 \text{ green peaches/basket} \times 7 \text{ baskets} = 14 \text{ green peaches}
     \]

Therefore, there are 14 green peaches in the baskets altogether.

The answer is: 14
