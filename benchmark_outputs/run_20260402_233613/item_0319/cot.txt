Premise 1: Jessica cut some roses from her flower garden.
Premise 2: After cutting, there are now 19 roses in the vase.
Premise 3: Initially, there were 3 roses in the vase.
Conclusion: Therefore, Jessica cut 16 roses.

Here's the logical reasoning:

1. Let x be the number of roses Jessica cut.
2. Initially, there were 3 roses in the vase.
3. After cutting, there are 19 roses in the vase.
4. To find out how many roses Jessica cut, we need to subtract the initial number of roses from the total number after cutting:
   \( x = 19 - 3 \)
5. Simplify the equation:
   \( x = 16 \)

Therefore, the answer is: The answer is: 16.
