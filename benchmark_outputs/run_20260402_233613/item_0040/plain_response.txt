To determine how many cakes each person ate, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Identify the total number of cakes Matthew has:** Matthew has 30 cakes.
2. **Determine how many people are sharing the cakes:** Matthew has 2 friends, making a total of 3 people (Matthew + 2 friends).
3. **Divide the total number of cakes by the number of people:** We divide 30 cakes by 3 people.

\[ \text{Number of cakes per person} = \frac{30}{3} = 10 \]

So, each person eats 10 cakes.

The answer is: 10
