To determine how many boxes are in each case when Shirley delivers 5 cases and sells a total of 10 boxes, we can follow these steps:

1. **Identify the total number of boxes sold**: Shirley sold a total of 10 boxes.
2. **Determine the number of cases delivered**: She needs to deliver 5 cases.
3. **Calculate the number of boxes per case**:
   - Since she sold 10 boxes over 5 cases, we divide the total number of boxes by the number of cases.
   \[
   \text{Number of boxes per case} = \frac{\text{Total number of boxes}}{\text{Number of cases}}
   \]
   - Substituting the known values:
   \[
   \text{Number of boxes per case} = \frac{10}{5}
   \]
   - Simplifying this fraction:
   \[
   \text{Number of boxes per case} = 2
   \]

Thus, there are 2 boxes in each case. The answer is: 2.
