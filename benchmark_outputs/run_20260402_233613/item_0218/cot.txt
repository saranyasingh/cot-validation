Let's break down this problem into a logical syllogism:

**Premise 1:** Marco's strawberries weighed 15 pounds.
- This is our starting point or given information about Marco's strawberries.

**Premise 2:** Together, Marco's and his dad's strawberries weighed 37 pounds.
- This tells us that if we add Marco's strawberries to his dad's strawberries, they equal 37 pounds.

**Conclusion:** We need to find out how much his dad's strawberries weigh.
- To solve this, we subtract the weight of Marco's strawberries from the total weight (37 pounds).

**Logical Syllogism:**
1. **Premise 1:** Marco's strawberries = 15 pounds.
   - Marco's strawberries are known to be 15 pounds.
   
2. **Premise 2:** Marco's strawberries + Dad's strawberries = Total weight.
   - Marco's strawberries plus his dad's strawberries equals 37 pounds.
   
3. **Step:** Subtract Marco's strawberries from the total weight.
   - Since Marco's strawberries weigh 15 pounds, we subtract this amount from the total weight:
     \[
     37 \text{ pounds} - 15 \text{ pounds} = 22 \text{ pounds}
     \]
   - Therefore, his dad's strawberries must weigh 22 pounds.

**The answer is:** 22 pounds.
