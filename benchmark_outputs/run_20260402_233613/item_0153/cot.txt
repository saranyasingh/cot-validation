To determine how many more cups of flour than cups of salt Mary needs to add, we can break this down into several steps:

### Step 1: Determine the total amount of flour needed.
The recipe calls for 12 cups of flour. Mary has already added 2 cups of flour.

\[ \text{Total flour needed} = 12 \text{ cups} \]
\[ \text{Flour already added} = 2 \text{ cups} \]

Subtract the flour already added from the total flour needed:
\[ \text{Remaining flour} = 12 - 2 = 10 \text{ cups} \]

### Step 2: Calculate the difference between remaining flour and salt.
The recipe requires 7 cups of salt. We know that Mary still needs to add flour but not salt yet.

\[ \text{Remaining salt needed} = 7 \text{ cups} \]

Now subtract the required salt from the remaining flour:
\[ \text{Difference (flour vs. salt)} = 10 \text{ cups (remaining flour)} - 7 \text{ cups (salt)} \]

Performing the subtraction gives us:
\[ \text{Difference (flour vs. salt)} = 3 \text{ cups} \]

### Conclusion
Mary needs to add 3 more cups of flour than cups of salt to complete her cake baking according to the recipe. 

The answer is: 3
