To determine how many more chairs they need to buy, we first calculate the total number of chairs available from the existing tables:

Each set of tables has 3 chairs, and there are 4 sets:
\[ 3 \text{ chairs/set} \times 4 \text{ sets} = 12 \text{ chairs} \]

Next, we know that 14 people will attend the party, so we need to find out how many additional chairs are required:
\[ 14 \text{ people} - 12 \text{ chairs} = 2 \text{ chairs} \]

Therefore, they will have to buy 2 more chairs. The answer is: 2.
