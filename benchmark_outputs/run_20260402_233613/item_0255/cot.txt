To determine how many more chairs they need to buy, we start with the information given:

- They decided to hold the party in their backyard.
- They have 4 sets of tables, and each set contains 3 chairs.
- A total of 14 people will attend the party.

First, let's calculate the number of chairs currently available:
\[
4 \text{ sets} \times 3 \text{ chairs per set} = 12 \text{ chairs}
\]

Next, we compare this number to the number of chairs needed for the event:
\[
14 \text{ attendees} - 12 \text{ existing chairs} = 2 \text{ additional chairs needed}
\]

Therefore, the answer is: 
The answer is: 2.
