1. **Premise 1:** Rachel picked 7 ripe apples from her tree.
   - This means that there were originally more than 7 ripe apples on the tree before she picked them.

2. **Premise 2:** After picking, the total number of apples remaining on the tree is 5.
   - This implies that the original number of apples was at least 7 (since Rachel picked 7).

3. **Premise 3:** Among the 5 remaining apples, 3 are ripe.
   - Therefore, the number of unripe apples among these 5 must be \(5 - 3 = 2\).

4. **Conclusion:** Since we know there are 2 unripe apples out of the 5 remaining on the tree after Rachel's pick, this directly answers our question about how many unripe apples the tree has now.

**The answer is:** 2
