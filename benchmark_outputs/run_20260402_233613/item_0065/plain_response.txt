To determine how many unripe apples are left on Rachel's tree after picking some, we can follow these steps:

1. **Identify the total number of apples initially**: The tree originally had 7 ripe apples.
2. **Determine the current number of apples on the tree**: After Rachel picked 7 apples, there are 5 apples remaining on the tree.
3. **Calculate the number of apples that were not picked**: Since there are 5 apples left and 7 were picked, this means \(5 + 7 = 12\) apples were picked in total (assuming all picked apples were ripe).
4. **Find out how many apples were picked specifically to get unripe**: Out of the 12 picked apples, since 3 are already identified as ripe, the remaining unpicked ones must be unripe.
   \[
   12 - 3 = 9
   \]
5. **Add the number of ripe apples to the number of unripe apples**:
   \[
   9 \text{ (unripe)} + 3 \text{ (ripe)} = 12
   \]

Therefore, the total number of unripe apples on the tree now is 9.

The answer is: 9
