To determine how many rows Faye could make, we need to follow these steps:

**Premise 1:** Faye has 28 packs of pencils.
- Premise 2 (derived): Each pack contains 24 pencils.
- Conclusion (from Premises 1 and 2): Total number of pencils = Number of packs × Pencils per pack.

Total number of pencils = \( 28 \times 24 \)

Let's calculate this:
\[ 28 \times 24 = 672 \]

So, Faye has 672 pencils in total.

**Premise 3:** Faye wants to place her pencils into rows with 16 pencils in each row.
- Conclusion (based on Premise 3): Rows = Total number of pencils ÷ Pencils per row.

Rows = \( \frac{672}{16} \)

Now let's perform the division:
\[ \frac{672}{16} = 42 \]

Therefore, Faye could make **42 rows** of pencils.

The answer is: 42
