To determine how many rows Faye can make, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the total number of pencils Faye has.
2. Determine how many rows she can form given that each row contains 16 pencils.

First, let's find out how many pencils Faye has in total:
- Each pack contains 24 pencils.
- She has 28 packs.

So, the total number of pencils is:
\[ \text{Total Pencils} = 28 \times 24 \]

Now, calculate the total number of pencils:
\[ \text{Total Pencils} = 28 \times 24 = 672 \]

Next, we need to figure out how many rows of 16 pencils she can make from these 672 pencils:
\[ \text{Number of Rows} = \frac{\text{Total Pencils}}{\text{Pencils per Row}} \]
\[ \text{Number of Rows} = \frac{672}{16} \]

Perform the division:
\[ \text{Number of Rows} = 42 \]

Therefore, the answer is: 42.
