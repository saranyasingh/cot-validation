To determine the number of students in the school, we need to calculate the total number of students based on the information provided:

1. First, we know that there are 95 school buses.
2. Each school bus is fully filled with 118 seats.
3. Therefore, the total number of seats available across all the buses is \( 95 \times 118 \).

Let's perform this calculation:
\[ 95 \times 118 = 11,270 \]

So, the total number of students in the school is 11,270.

The answer is: 11,270
