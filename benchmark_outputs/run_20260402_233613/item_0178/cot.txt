Premise 1: Mary needs a total of 14 cups of flour for the cake.
Premise 2: Mary has already added 7 cups of flour.
Premise 3: Subtracting the amount of flour Mary has already added from the total required gives us how much more flour Mary needs to add.

Conclusion:
Therefore, Mary needs to add \(14 - 7 = 7\) more cups of flour to the mixture.

The answer is: 7.
