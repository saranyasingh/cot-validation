Let's solve this step-by-step:

1) Initially, there were 23 children on the bus.
2) At the bus stop, 24 children got on the bus.
   Now there are 23 + 24 = 47 children on the bus.

3) After some children got off, there were 8 children left on the bus.
   Let x be the number of children who got off the bus.
   So, 47 - x = 8

4) Solve for x:
   47 - x = 8
   x = 47 - 8
   x = 39

5) We need to find how many more children got off the bus than those that got on:
   39 (children who got off) - 24 (children who got on) = 15

Therefore, 15 more children got off the bus than those that got on.

The answer is: 15
