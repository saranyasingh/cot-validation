To determine the total weight of Marco and his dad's strawberries, we need to add the weights together. Let's break this down into steps:

1. **Identify the given information:**
   - Marco’s strawberries weigh 8 pounds.
   - His dad’s strawberries weigh 32 pounds.

2. **Set up the addition problem:**
   We need to add these two quantities (8 + 32).

3. **Perform the addition:**
   \[
   8 + 32 = 40
   \]

4. **Conclusion:**
   The total weight of their strawberries combined is 40 pounds.

**The answer is: 40**
