To find out how much Marco and his dad's strawberries weighed together, we need to add the weight of Marco's strawberries to the weight of his dad's strawberries.

Step 1: Identify the weights.
- Marco's strawberries = 8 pounds
- Dad's strawberries = 32 pounds

Step 2: Add the two weights together.
