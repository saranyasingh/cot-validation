To determine how many more boys than girls the school has, we need to follow these steps:

**Premise 1:** There are 34 girls in the school.
**Premise 2:** There are 841 boys in the school.

We want to find out how many more boys there are compared to girls. To do this, we subtract the number of girls from the number of boys.

**Step-by-step Logical Reasoning:**

1. Identify the total number of girls: \( \text{Number of girls} = 34 \)
2. Identify the total number of boys: \( \text{Number of boys} = 841 \)
3. Calculate the difference between the number of boys and the number of girls:
   \[
   \text{Difference} = \text{Number of boys} - \text{Number of girls}
   \]
4. Substitute the given values into the equation:
   \[
   \text{Difference} = 841 - 34
   \]

5. Perform the subtraction:
   \[
   841 - 34 = 807
   \]

Thus, the school has 807 more boys than girls.

The answer is: The school has 807 more boys than girls.
