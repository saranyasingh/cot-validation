Let's break down this problem into a logical syllogism:

**Premise 1:** There were 44 campers who went rowing in the morning.
- This is our starting point and establishes the total number of campers who participated in the morning activities.

**Premise 2:** 39 campers went rowing in the afternoon.
- This gives us the number of campers involved in the afternoon activities.

**Premise 3:** 31 campers went rowing in the evening.
- This provides the number of campers participating in the evening activities.

Now, we need to determine how many more campers went rowing in the morning compared to the afternoon.

**Conclusion:** To find out how many more campers went rowing in the morning than in the afternoon, we subtract the number of campers who went rowing in the afternoon from the number of campers who went rowing in the morning.

The calculation is as follows:
\[ \text{Difference} = \text{Morning participants} - \text{Afternoon participants} \]
\[ \text{Difference} = 44 - 39 \]

Therefore, **the answer is: 5**.
