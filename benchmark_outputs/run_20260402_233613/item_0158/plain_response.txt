To determine how many packs of groceries Lucy bought in total, we need to consider her purchases:

1. Lucy bought 2 packs of cookies.
2. She also bought 12 packs of cakes.

Adding these together gives us the total number of packs of groceries:
\[ 2 \text{ (cookies)} + 12 \text{ (cakes)} = 14 \text{ packs} \]

So, Lucy bought 14 packs of groceries in all.

The answer is: 14
