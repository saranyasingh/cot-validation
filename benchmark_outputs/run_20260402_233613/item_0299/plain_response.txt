To determine how many more birds than storks are sitting on the fence, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the total number of birds initially on the fence.
2. Add the additional birds that joined them.
3. Subtract the number of storks from the new total number of birds.

Let's break it down:

1. Initially, there are 5 storks and 3 birds on the fence.
   Total initial birds = 5 (storks) + 3 (birds) = 8 birds

2. Then, 4 more birds come to join them:
   New total birds = Initial total birds + Additional birds = 8 + 4 = 12 birds

3. Now, subtract the number of storks from the new total number of birds:
   More birds than storks = New total birds - Number of storks = 12 - 5 = 7

Therefore, there are 7 more birds than storks sitting on the fence.

The answer is: 7
