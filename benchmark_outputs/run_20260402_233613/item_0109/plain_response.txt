To determine how many more emails Jack received in the morning than in the evening, we need to follow these steps:

1. Identify the number of emails received in the morning.
2. Identify the number of emails received in the evening.
3. Subtract the number of emails received in the evening from the number of emails received in the morning.

Given:
- Morning emails = 9
- Evening emails = 7

Calculation:
\( \text{Difference} = \text{Morning emails} - \text{Evening emails} \)
\( \text{Difference} = 9 - 7 \)
\( \text{Difference} = 2 \)

Therefore, Jack received 2 more emails in the morning than in the evening. The answer is: 2.
