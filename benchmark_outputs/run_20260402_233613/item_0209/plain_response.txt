To find out how many oranges are harvested in total when they harvest 35 sacks of oranges, we can follow these steps:

1. First, determine the number of oranges in one sack.
   - Each sack contains 12 oranges.

2. Next, calculate the total number of oranges in 35 sacks.
   - Since there are 12 oranges in each sack, we multiply 12 by 35:
     \[
     12 \times 35 = 420
     \]

So, the total number of oranges harvested if they harvest 35 sacks of oranges is 420 oranges.

The answer is: 420
