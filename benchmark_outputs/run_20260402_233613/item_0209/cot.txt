**Logical Syllogism**

1. **Premise**: He saw the oranges being harvested.
   - Context: This establishes the scenario where we need to calculate the number of oranges harvested.

2. **Premise**: They harvest 67 sacks per day.
   - Context: This provides the rate at which oranges are harvested daily.

3. **Premise**: Each sack contains 12 oranges.
   - Context: This gives us the number of oranges contained within each sack.

4. **Conclusion (The Final Step)**:
   - To find the total number of oranges harvested when they harvest a total of 35 sacks of oranges,
     - We multiply the number of sacks by the number of oranges per sack.

5. **Calculation**:
   \[
   \text{Total Oranges} = (\text{Number of Sacks}) \times (\text{Oranges Per Sack})
   \]
   Substituting the given values:
   \[
   \text{Total Oranges} = 35 \times 12
   \]

6. **Final Calculation**:
   \[
   \text{Total Oranges} = 35 \times 12 = 420
   \]

**Answer**: The answer is: 420 oranges.
