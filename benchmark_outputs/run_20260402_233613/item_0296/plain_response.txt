To find out how many sacks of oranges they harvest per day, we need to divide the total number of sacks harvested by the number of days it takes to harvest them.

Given:
- Total number of sacks harvested = 56
- Number of days = 4

Calculation:
\[ \text{Sacks per day} = \frac{\text{Total sacks}}{\text{Number of days}} = \frac{56}{4} = 14 \]

So, the answer is: 14.
