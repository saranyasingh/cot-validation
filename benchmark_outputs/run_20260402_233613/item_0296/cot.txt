To determine how many sacks of oranges they harvest per day, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Identify the total number of sacks harvested**: The problem states that they harvested a total of 56 sacks of oranges over the course of 4 days.
2. **Determine the time period for harvesting**: We know that the harvest will continue for 4 days.
3. **Calculate the number of sacks harvested per day**:
   - To find this, we divide the total number of sacks by the number of days the harvest lasts.
   
So, let's perform the calculation:
\[ \text{Number of sacks harvested per day} = \frac{\text{Total number of sacks}}{\text{Number of days}} \]
\[ \text{Number of sacks harvested per day} = \frac{56}{4} \]

Now, performing the division:
\[ \frac{56}{4} = 14 \]

Therefore, they harvest **14 sacks of oranges per day**.

The answer is: 14.
