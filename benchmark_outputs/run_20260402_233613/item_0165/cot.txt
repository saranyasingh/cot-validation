To determine the width of Jesse's room given that she needs a carpet of 10 square feet and the length is 5 feet, we can use basic area calculations.

### Premise 1:
The formula for calculating the area of a rectangle (which fits our scenario) is:
\[ \text{Area} = \text{Length} \times \text{Width} \]

### Premise 2:
We know the total area required is 10 square feet.
\[ \text{Area} = 10 \, \text{square feet} \]

### Premise 3:
We also know the length of the room is 5 feet.
\[ \text{Length} = 5 \, \text{feet} \]

### Conclusion:
Using the formula for area, we substitute the known values into it:
\[ 10 = 5 \times \text{Width} \]

To find the width, we divide both sides of the equation by 5:
\[ \text{Width} = \frac{10}{5} = 2 \, \text{feet} \]

Therefore, the width of Jesse's room is **2 feet**.

The answer is: 2
