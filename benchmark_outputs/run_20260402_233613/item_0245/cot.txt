Let's break down this problem into a logical syllogism:

**Premise 1:** Matthew gave equal numbers of crackers and cakes to his 4 friends.
- This tells us that if we divide both the number of crackers and cakes equally among his friends, they would get an equal share.

**Premise 2:** Matthew initially had 10 crackers and 8 cakes.
- We know the total quantities of crackers and cakes Matthew started with.

**Premise 3:** Each friend gets an equal amount of crackers and cakes.
- Since there are 4 friends, we need to determine how these amounts translate to individual servings.

To find out how many cakes each person ate:
1. **Step 1:** Calculate the total number of cakes per person.
   - Total cakes = 8 cakes
   - Number of people = 4

   \[
   \text{Cakes per person} = \frac{\text{Total cakes}}{\text{Number of people}} = \frac{8}{4} = 2 \text{ cakes}
   \]

Therefore, each person ate 2 cakes.

The answer is: 2
