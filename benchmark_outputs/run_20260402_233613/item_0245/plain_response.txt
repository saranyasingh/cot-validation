To determine how many cakes each person ate, we can follow these steps:

1. **Identify the total number of crackers and cakes Matthew has:** 
   - Crackers: 10
   - Cakes: 8

2. **Determine how many people received crackers and cakes:** 
   - Matthew has 4 friends, so there are 5 people in total (including Matthew).

3. **Calculate the number of crackers each person receives:** 
   \[
   \text{Crackers per person} = \frac{\text{Total crackers}}{\text{Number of people}} = \frac{10}{5} = 2
   \]

4. **Since Matthew distributed crackers equally among his friends, it means he also distributes cakes equally.** 
   - Therefore, each person gets:
     \[
     \text{Cakes per person} = \frac{\text{Total cakes}}{\text{Number of people}} = \frac{8}{5}
     \]
   - Simplifying this fraction:
     \[
     \frac{8}{5} = 1 \text{ cake and } 3/5 \text{ of a cake}
     \]

Thus, each person ate 1 whole cake and an additional 3/5 of a cake. 

Therefore, the answer is: Each person ate 1 whole cake and 3/5 of another cake.
