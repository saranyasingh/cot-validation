To determine how many weeks the harvest lasted, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Identify the given information:**
   - Lewis earns $2 per week.
   - He earned a total of $178 over the course of the harvest.

2. **Set up the relationship between earnings and time:**
   Since Lewis earns $2 per week, we can use this rate to find out how many weeks it took him to earn his total amount.

3. **Formulate an equation based on the given information:**
   Let \( w \) represent the number of weeks the harvest lasted. Given that Lewis earns $2 per week, we can write:
   \[
   2w = 178
   \]

4. **Solve the equation for \( w \):**
   To find \( w \), divide both sides of the equation by 2:
   \[
   w = \frac{178}{2}
   \]
   Simplifying this division gives:
   \[
   w = 89
   \]

5. **Conclusion:**
   The harvest lasted for 89 weeks.

**The answer is:** 89
