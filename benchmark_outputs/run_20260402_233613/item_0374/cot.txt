Premise 1: The school has 66 classrooms.
Premise 2: Each classroom has an equal number of students.
Premise 3: There are 102 students in total at the school.
Premise 4: 22 students do not want to go on the field trip.
Premise 5: Each school bus has 8 seats.
Premise 6: We need to find out how many buses are needed to transport all the students who want to go on the field trip.

To determine how many buses are needed:

Step 1: Calculate the number of students who want to go on the field trip.
Total students - Students who don't want to go = Students who want to go

102 (total students) - 22 (students who don't want to go) = 80 students want to go

Step 2: Determine how many buses are required to transport these students.
Number of buses = Number of students who want to go / Seats per bus

80 (students who want to go) ÷ 8 (seats per bus) = 10 buses

Therefore, the answer is: X 10 buses are needed to transport all the students who want to go on the field trip.
