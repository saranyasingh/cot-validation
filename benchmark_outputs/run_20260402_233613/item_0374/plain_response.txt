To determine how many buses are needed for the field trip, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the total number of students who want to go on the trip.
2. Determine how many buses are required based on the number of seats available per bus.

First, let's find out how many students want to go on the trip:
- Total number of students = 102
- Number of students who don't want to go = 22

So, the number of students who want to go on the trip is:
\[ 102 - 22 = 80 \]

Next, we need to calculate how many buses are required to transport 80 students when there are 8 seats per bus:
\[ \text{Number of buses} = \frac{\text{Total number of students}}{\text{Seats per bus}} \]
\[ \text{Number of buses} = \frac{80}{8} = 10 \]

Therefore, the number of buses needed to take the trip is 10.

The answer is: 10
