To determine how many more cakes the baker sold compared to those he bought, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the number of cakes sold:
   Baker initially had 170 cakes and sold 78 of them.
   \[
   170 - 78 = 92
   \]
   So, the baker sold 92 cakes.

2. Determine the difference between the number of cakes sold and bought:
   The baker bought 31 new cakes.
   \[
   92 - 31 = 61
   \]

Therefore, the baker sold 61 more cakes than he bought.

The answer is: 61
