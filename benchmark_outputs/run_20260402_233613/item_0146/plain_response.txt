To determine how many nails John needs for the house wall, we can follow these steps:

1. **Identify the number of nails per plank**: According to the problem, each plank requires 2 pieces of nails.
   
2. **Determine the total number of planks**: The problem states that John will use 16 planks.

3. **Calculate the total number of nails needed**:
   - Multiply the number of nails per plank (2) by the total number of planks (16).

Let's perform the calculation:
\[ \text{Total nails} = (\text{Nails per plank}) \times (\text{Number of planks}) \]
\[ \text{Total nails} = 2 \times 16 \]
\[ \text{Total nails} = 32 \]

Therefore, John needs 32 nails for the house wall. 

The answer is: 32
