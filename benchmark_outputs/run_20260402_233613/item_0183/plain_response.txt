To determine how much money Fred earned by washing cars, we need to calculate the difference between the amount of money he had after washing cars and the amount he initially had before washing cars.

Fred's initial amount of money:
\[ \text{Initial amount} = \$111 \]

Fred's current amount of money:
\[ \text{Current amount} = \$115 \]

Amount earned by Fred from washing cars:
\[ \text{Earnings} = \text{Current amount} - \text{Initial amount} \]
\[ \text{Earnings} = \$115 - \$111 \]
\[ \text{Earnings} = \$4 \]

Therefore, Fred earned $4 by washing cars. The answer is: 4.
