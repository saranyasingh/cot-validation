**Logical Syllogism**

1. **Premise**: Fred had 111 dollars last week.
   - This establishes Fred's initial amount of money before any transactions occurred.

2. **Premise**: Fred now has 115 dollars after washing cars.
   - This shows that Fred earned some money by washing cars during the weekend.

3. **Conclusion**: Fred earned \(115 - 111 = 4\) dollars by washing cars.

Therefore, the answer is:
The answer is: 4 dollars.
