To determine how many books Paul bought, we can use the basic arithmetic operation of subtraction. 

Initially, Paul had 50 books. After buying some books at a garage sale, he ended up with 151 books. The number of books he bought can be calculated by subtracting the initial number of books from the total number of books after the purchase:

\[ \text{Number of books bought} = \text{Total books after purchase} - \text{Initial number of books} \]

Plugging in the numbers:

\[ \text{Number of books bought} = 151 - 50 \]
\[ \text{Number of books bought} = 101 \]

Therefore, Paul bought 101 books.
