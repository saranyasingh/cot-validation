Let's break down this problem into a series of logical steps using a syllogistic approach:

1. **Premise 1:** Paul initially had 50 books.
   - Let P be the number of books Paul started with.
   - Therefore, \( P = 50 \).

2. **Premise 2:** After purchasing some books at a garage sale, Paul ended up with 151 books.
   - Let B be the number of books Paul bought at the garage sale.
   - Therefore, after buying B books, his total became \( P + B \).
   - Given that \( P + B = 151 \).

3. **Substitution Step:** We know \( P = 50 \) from Premise 1.
   - Substituting \( P \) in the equation from Premise 2:
     \[
     50 + B = 151
     \]

4. **Solving for B:** To find out how many books Paul bought (B), we need to isolate B on one side of the equation.
   - Subtract 50 from both sides of the equation:
     \[
     B = 151 - 50
     \]
   - Simplify the right-hand side:
     \[
     B = 101
     \]

**Conclusion:** Paul bought 101 books at the garage sale.

The answer is: Paul bought 101 books.
