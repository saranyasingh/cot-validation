To determine how much money Jason made washing cars, we need to find the difference between the amount of money he has after washing cars and the amount he had initially.

1. Identify the initial amount of money Jason had:
   - Initial amount = $19

2. Identify the current amount of money Jason has:
   - Current amount = $33

3. Calculate the increase in Jason's money due to washing cars:
   - Increase = Current amount - Initial amount
   - Increase = $33 - $19

4. Perform the subtraction:
   - Increase = $14

Therefore, Jason made $14 washing cars.

The answer is: $14
