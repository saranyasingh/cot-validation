To determine how much money Jason made washing cars, we need to analyze the given information and apply basic arithmetic operations.

**Premise 1:** Last week Fred had 60 dollars and Jason had 19 dollars.
- This establishes the initial amounts of money that both individuals had before any transactions occurred.

**Premise 2:** Jason washed cars over the weekend and now has 33 dollars.
- This provides us with the current amount of money Jason has after his car-washing activity.

**Step-by-step Logical Reasoning:**

1. **Determine the total increase in Jason's money:**
   - Initially, Jason had 19 dollars.
   - After washing cars, he has 33 dollars.
   - Therefore, the increase in Jason's money = Final amount - Initial amount
     \[
     \text{Increase} = 33 - 19 = 14 \text{ dollars}
     \]

2. **Conclude the amount Jason made:**
   - Since the increase in Jason's money is due to him washing cars, this increase must be equal to the amount he earned from washing cars.
   - Thus, Jason made 14 dollars washing cars.

Therefore, the answer is: Jason made 14 dollars washing cars.
