To determine how much money Gwen received for her birthday, we need to consider the information given:

1. Gwen spent $8.
2. After spending $8, she had $6 left.

We can set up an equation to find out how much money she originally had before spending any of it:
\[ \text{Money received} - \$8 = \$6 \]

Let \( x \) represent the amount of money Gwen received for her birthday. The equation becomes:
\[ x - 8 = 6 \]

Next, solve for \( x \):
\[ x = 6 + 8 \]
\[ x = 14 \]

Therefore, Gwen received $14 for her birthday. 

The answer is: 14
