1. **Premise 1**: Gwen had an initial amount of money.
2. **Premise 2**: After spending $8, Gwen was left with $6.
3. **Conclusion**: Therefore, before spending, Gwen must have had more than $8 but less than or equal to $14 because subtracting $8 from any amount between these values would leave her with at least $6.

- If A - B = C (Gwen spent $8 leaving $6)
- Then A > B + C (Before spending, Gwen had more than $8)
