To determine how many bags of cookies you have, we need to divide the total number of cookies by the number of cookies per bag.

Given:
- Total cookies = 21
- Cookies per bag = 3

We can calculate the number of bags as follows:

Number of bags = Total cookies ÷ Cookies per bag
Number of bags = 21 ÷ 3
Number of bags = 7

Therefore, the answer is: The answer is: 7.
