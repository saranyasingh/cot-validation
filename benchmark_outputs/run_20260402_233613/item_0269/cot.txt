Let's break down this problem into a series of logical steps:

**Premise 1:** Each bag contains 3 cookies.
- This establishes the quantity per bag which we'll use to calculate the number of bags needed.

**Premise 2:** We have a total of 21 cookies.
- This sets up our goal, indicating how many cookies we need to distribute among the bags.

**Premise 3:** To find out how many bags we have, we divide the total number of cookies by the number of cookies per bag.
- This is the core calculation that directly relates the total amount of cookies to the number of bags.

**Logical Reasoning Step 1:** Divide the total number of cookies (21) by the number of cookies per bag (3).
- \( \frac{21}{3} = 7 \)

**Conclusion:** Therefore, we have 7 bags of cookies.

The answer is: 7
