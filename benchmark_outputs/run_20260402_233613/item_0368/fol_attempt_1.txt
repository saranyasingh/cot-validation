This problem involves translating a simple arithmetic operation into first-order logic (FOL). Here's the conversion process broken down step-by-step:

### Facts
These are the given facts about the situation.

**Fact-1:**
Mary needs 9 cups of flour to make the cake.

**Fact-2:**
Mary has already added 3 cups of flour to the mixture.

### Rules
These are derived rules based on the given information.

**Rule-1:**
If someone adds x cups of flour, then they need to add y cups to reach the total.

Since we don't know what 'y' represents, let's assume it's the unknown amount of flour left to be added after adding 3 cups. We can express this as:

**Rule-2:**
Adding 3 cups leaves 6 cups to be added.

### Inferences
Using these rules, we derive the conclusion.

**Inference-1:**
From Fact-1 and Rule-1, if Mary adds 3 cups, she needs 6 more cups to reach the total.

So, the final result is:

**Inference-1:**
6

### Final Answer
**Inference-1:**
6 :: 9 - 3 = 6
