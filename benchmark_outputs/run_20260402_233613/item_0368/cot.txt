1. **Premise 1:** Mary needs to make a cake that requires 9 cups of flour.
   - Premise Explanation: This establishes the total amount of flour needed for the cake.

   - Premise Explanation: This indicates how much flour has been used so far.

3. **Premise 3:** The remaining amount of flour needed is calculated by subtracting the flour already added from the total required.
   - Calculation: \(9 \text{ cups (total)} - 3 \text{ cups (added)} = 6 \text{ cups} \)

4. **Conclusion:** Mary still needs to add 6 more cups of flour to complete her cake recipe.
