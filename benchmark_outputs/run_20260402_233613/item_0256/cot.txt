To determine how much more a t-shirt costs than a jersey, we need to calculate the total revenue from selling both items and then find the difference between the prices of these two items.

### Step-by-Step Logical Reasoning

**Premise 1:** A Razorback shop sells jerseys at $210 each.
   - This is given directly as part of the problem statement.

**Premise 2:** A Razorback shop sells t-shirts at $240 each.
   - This is also given directly as part of the problem statement.

**Premise 3:** During the Arkansas and Texas Tech game, they sold 177 t-shirts.
   - This information about the number of t-shirts sold is provided.

**Premise 4:** They sold 23 jerseys.
   - This information about the number of jerseys sold is provided.

**Premise 5:** To find out how much more a t-shirt costs than a jersey, we first need to calculate the price difference per item.
   - We subtract the price of a jersey from the price of a t-shirt:
     \[
     \text{Price difference} = \$240 - \$210
     \]
   - Simplifying this, we get:
     \[
     \text{Price difference} = \$30
     \]

**Conclusion:**
A t-shirt costs $30 more than a jersey.

Therefore, the answer is: 
\[
\boxed{30}
\]
