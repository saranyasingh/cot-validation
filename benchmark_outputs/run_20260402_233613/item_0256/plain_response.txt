To determine how much more a t-shirt costs than a jersey, we need to calculate the total revenue from selling both items and then find the difference between their prices.

First, let's calculate the revenue from selling t-shirts:
- Price of one t-shirt = $240
- Number of t-shirts sold = 177

Revenue from t-shirts = \( 240 \times 177 \)

Next, let's calculate the revenue from selling jerseys:
- Price of one jersey = $210
- Number of jerseys sold = 23

Revenue from jerseys = \( 210 \times 23 \)

Now, let's compute these values:

Revenue from t-shirts:
\[ 240 \times 177 = 42480 \]

Revenue from jerseys:
\[ 210 \times 23 = 4830 \]

Finally, to find out how much more a t-shirt costs than a jersey:
\[ \text{Difference} = \text{Revenue from t-shirts} - \text{Revenue from jerseys} \]
\[ \text{Difference} = 42480 - 4830 \]
\[ \text{Difference} = 37650 \]

So, a t-shirt costs $37650 more than a jersey. The answer is: 37650
