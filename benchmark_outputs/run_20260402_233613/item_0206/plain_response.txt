To determine how many days Frank took to finish the book, we can use the formula:

\[ \text{Number of days} = \frac{\text{Total number of pages}}{\text{Pages read per day}} \]

Given that Frank reads 8 pages per day and the book has 576 pages, we can calculate as follows:

\[ \text{Number of days} = \frac{576}{8} \]

Performing the division:

\[ \text{Number of days} = 72 \]

Therefore, Frank took 72 days to finish the book.

The answer is: 72
