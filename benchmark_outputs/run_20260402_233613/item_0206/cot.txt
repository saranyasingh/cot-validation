Let's break down Frank's reading journey into a logical syllogism:

1. **Premise 1:** Frank reads 8 pages per day.
   - This establishes the daily reading rate and how much content is consumed each day.

2. **Premise 2:** The book has 576 pages.
   - This gives us the total amount of material available for reading.

3. **Conclusion:** To find out how many days it took Frank to finish the book, we need to divide the total number of pages by the number of pages he reads per day.
   
4. **Calculation:** 
   \[
   \text{Number of days} = \frac{\text{Total pages}}{\text{Pages per day}} = \frac{576}{8}
   \]
   
5. **Simplification:**
   \[
   \frac{576}{8} = 72
   \]

Therefore, Frank took 72 days to finish the book.

The answer is: 72 days.
