Let's break down this problem using a logical syllogism:

Premise 1:
- Julia played tag with some kids on Monday.
- She played tag with 14 kids on Tuesday.
- We know that if she played with a total of 16 kids over these two days, then we need to find out how many kids she played with on Monday.

Premise 2:
- Let's denote the number of kids Julia played with on Monday as M.
- According to Premise 1, M + 14 = Total number of kids (since she played with 14 kids on Tuesday).

Premise 3:
- From Premise 2, we have M + 14 = 16.
- To find M, we subtract 14 from both sides of the equation.

Conclusion:
M = 16 - 14

Therefore, M = 2.

The answer is: 2.
