Let's denote the number of kids Julia played with on Monday as M. According to the problem statement:

- On Monday, Julia played with M kids.
- On Tuesday, she played with 14 kids.
- The total number of kids she played with over these two days was 16.

We can set up an equation based on this information:
\[ M + 14 = 16 \]

To find M, we subtract 14 from both sides of the equation:
\[ M = 16 - 14 \]
\[ M = 2 \]

So, Julia played with 2 kids on Monday.

The answer is: 2
