To determine how many cakes the baker sold, we need to follow these steps:

**Premise 1:** Baker made 54 cakes initially.
- This establishes the total number of cakes available at the start.

**Premise 2:** After selling some cakes, the baker now has 13 cakes remaining.
- This tells us that after selling, the quantity of cakes is reduced to 13.

**Premise 3:** We need to find out how many cakes were sold.
- This is our goal to solve the problem.

**Step-by-step Logical Reasoning:**

1. **Identify Initial and Final Quantities:**
   - Initial quantity: 54 cakes (before any sales)
   - Final quantity: 13 cakes (after all sales)

2. **Calculate the Difference Between Initial and Final Quantities:**
   - To find out how many cakes were sold, we subtract the final quantity from the initial quantity:
     \[
     54 - 13 = 41
     \]

Therefore, the baker sold 41 cakes.

The answer is: The baker sold 41 cakes.
