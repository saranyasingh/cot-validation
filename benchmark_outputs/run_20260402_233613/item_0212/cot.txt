To determine how many guests Brenda's mother prepared cookies for, we need to follow these steps:

**Premise 1:** Brenda's mother prepared 38 cookies.
- This establishes the total number of cookies available.

**Premise 2:** Each guest received 19 cookies.
- This indicates the quantity of cookies each person ate.

**Premise 3:** We need to find out how many guests there were.
- This requires us to divide the total number of cookies by the number of cookies per guest.

**Logical Syllogism:**
1. **Premise 1:** There are 38 cookies (total).
2. **Premise 2:** Each guest receives 19 cookies.
3. **Conclusion:** To find the number of guests, divide the total number of cookies by the number of cookies per guest:
   \[
   \text{Number of guests} = \frac{\text{Total number of cookies}}{\text{Cookies per guest}} = \frac{38}{19}
   \]

4. **Calculation:**
   - Performing the division:
     \[
     \frac{38}{19} = 2
     \]
   - Therefore, the number of guests is 2.

**Answer:** The number of guests Brenda's mother prepared cookies for is \( \boxed{2} \).
