To determine how many bottles of diet soda the grocery store had, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Identify the given information:**
   - Total number of bottles: 17
   - Number of regular soda bottles: 9

2. **Calculate the number of diet soda bottles:**
   - We know that the total number of bottles is made up of both regular soda bottles and diet soda bottles.
   - Let \( D \) represent the number of diet soda bottles.
   - According to the problem, the sum of the number of regular soda bottles and diet soda bottles equals the total number of bottles:
     \[
     9 + D = 17
     \]

3. **Solve for \( D \):**
   - To find \( D \), subtract the number of regular soda bottles from the total number of bottles:
     \[
     D = 17 - 9
     \]
   - Perform the subtraction:
     \[
     D = 8
     \]

4. **Conclusion:**
   - The grocery store had 8 bottles of diet soda.

The answer is: 8.
