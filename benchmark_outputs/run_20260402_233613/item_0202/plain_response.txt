To determine how many bottles of diet soda the grocery store had, we need to follow these steps:

1. Identify the total number of bottles.
2. Subtract the number of bottles of regular soda from the total number of bottles to find the number of bottles of diet soda.

Given:
- Total number of bottles = 17
- Number of bottles of regular soda = 9

We can calculate the number of bottles of diet soda as follows:

Number of bottles of diet soda = Total number of bottles - Number of bottles of regular soda
Number of bottles of diet soda = 17 - 9
Number of bottles of diet soda = 8

So, the grocery store had 8 bottles of diet soda.
