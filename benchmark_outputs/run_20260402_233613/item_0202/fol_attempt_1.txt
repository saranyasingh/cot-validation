Let's convert the chain-of-thought reasoning into structured First-Order Logic (FOL):

### FACTS
- There are 17 bottles in total.
- There are 9 regular soda bottles.
- Let \( D \) be the number of diet soda bottles.

**Fact-1:** There are 17 bottles in total.
\[
Bottles(17)
\]

**Fact-2:** There are 9 regular soda bottles.
\[
RegularSodaBottles(9)
\]

**Fact-3:** Diet soda bottles make up the rest of the bottles.
\[
DietSodaBottles(D)
\]

### RULES
No rules needed here since all facts are basic statements about the quantities of bottles.

### INFERENCES
We need to solve for \( D \) using the given facts.

**Inference-1:** Diet soda bottles make up the remaining bottles after accounting for regular soda bottles.
\[
D = Bottles - RegularSodaBottles
\]

Since there are no other conditions provided that would modify \( D \), we can conclude:
\[
D = 17 - 9
\]

Performing the subtraction:
\[
D = 8
\]

Therefore, the number of diet soda bottles is:
\[
\boxed{8}
\]
