To determine how many more students suggested mashed potatoes than those that suggested bacon, we need to subtract the number of students who suggested bacon from the number of students who suggested mashed potatoes.

Number of students who suggested mashed potatoes = 257
Number of students who suggested bacon = 120

Difference = Number of students who suggested mashed potatoes - Number of students who suggested bacon
          = 257 - 120
          = 137

Therefore, 137 more students suggested mashed potatoes than those that suggested bacon.

The answer is: 137
