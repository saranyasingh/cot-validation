To determine how much money Lewis earns each week, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Identify the total earnings and time period**: 
   - Total earnings = $133
   - Time period (weeks) = 19

2. **Set up the division problem**:
   To find out how much money Lewis earns per week, we divide his total earnings by the number of weeks.
   
   \[
   \text{Earnings per week} = \frac{\text{Total Earnings}}{\text{Number of Weeks}}
   \]

3. **Perform the calculation**:
   \[
   \text{Earnings per week} = \frac{133}{19}
   \]

4. **Simplify the fraction**:
   We simplify \( \frac{133}{19} \) to get the exact amount.

   Using long division or a calculator, we find:
   \[
   133 \div 19 = 7
   \]
   This means that each week, Lewis earns $7.

5. **Conclusion**:
   Therefore, Lewis earns $7 each week.

The answer is: X $7.
