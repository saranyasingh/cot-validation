To find out how much Olivia spent, we need to follow these steps:

1. First, calculate the total amount of money Olivia had after collecting money from the ATM.
2. Then, subtract this total from the amount left in her wallet after visiting the supermarket to find out how much she spent.

Step 1:
Olivia initially had $100 and then collected $148 from the ATM. So, the total amount after collection is:
$100 + $148 = $248

Step 2:
After spending some money at the supermarket, Olivia was left with $159. To find out how much she spent, we subtract the remaining amount from the total amount before shopping:
$248 - $159 = $89

Therefore, Olivia spent $89 at the supermarket. 

The answer is: $89
