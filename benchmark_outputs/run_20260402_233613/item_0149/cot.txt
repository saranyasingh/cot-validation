To determine how much Olivia spent, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Initial amount**: Olivia started with $100.
2. **Amount collected at ATM**: She added $148 to her initial amount.
3. **Final amount after visiting the supermarket**: This was $159.

We can set up the problem using addition and subtraction as follows:

- First, calculate the total amount before spending:
  \[
  100 + 148 = 248
  \]
  So, before visiting the supermarket, Olivia had $248.

- Next, subtract the amount spent from this total to find out how much she actually spent:
  \[
  248 - 159 = 89
  \]

Therefore, Olivia spent $89.
