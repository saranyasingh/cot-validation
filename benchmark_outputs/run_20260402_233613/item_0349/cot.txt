**Logical Syllogism**

1. **Premise 1**: Matthew initially had 23 crackers.
   - This establishes the starting point of the problem and provides the total quantity of crackers Matthew possessed before any were given away or eaten.

2. **Premise 2**: After giving some crackers to his two friends, Matthew has 11 crackers left.
   - This tells us that the total number of crackers given to the friends equals the difference between the initial amount and the remaining amount after sharing.

3. **Premise 3**: Matthew shared crackers equally among his two friends.
   - This assumption implies that both friends received an equal share of the crackers given away.

4. **Conclusion**: Each friend ate \( \frac{11}{2} = 5.5 \) crackers.
   - By dividing the total number of crackers given (which we calculated as 11) by the number of friends (which is 2), we find out how many crackers each friend consumed.

**The Answer is: 5.5**

This conclusion is reached through direct division of the total number of crackers given away by the number of friends, assuming they received them equally.
