Let's break down the problem and construct a logical argument step-by-step:

1. **Initial number of crackers**: Matthew starts with 31 crackers.
2. **Crackers left after giving away**: After distributing crackers among his friends, he has 23 crackers remaining.
3. **Number of friends**: Matthew has 5 friends.
4. **Calculation**:
   - To find out how many crackers Matthew gave away initially, we subtract the number of crackers he has left from the initial amount:
     \[
     \text{Crackers given away} = \text{Initial crackers} - \text{Crackers left}
     \]
     Substituting the values:
     \[
     \text{Crackers given away} = 31 - 23
     \]

Now let's perform the subtraction:
\[
31 - 23 = 8
\]

Therefore, Matthew gave away 8 crackers to his friends.

The answer is: 8
