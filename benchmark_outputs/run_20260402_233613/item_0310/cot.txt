To determine how many more kids Julia played with on Tuesday compared to Wednesday, we need to follow these steps:

**Premise 1:** Julia played tag with 8 kids on Monday.
- Premise 2:** Julia played tag with 11 kids on Tuesday.
- Premise 3:** Julia played tag with 9 kids on Wednesday.

**Step-by-step logical reasoning:**

1. **Identify the number of kids on each day:**
   - Number of kids on Monday = 8
   - Number of kids on Tuesday = 11
   - Number of kids on Wednesday = 9

2. **Calculate the difference between the number of kids on Tuesday and Wednesday:**
   \[
   \text{Difference} = \text{Number of kids on Tuesday} - \text{Number of kids on Wednesday}
   \]
   Substituting the values:
   \[
   \text{Difference} = 11 - 9 = 2
   \]

Therefore, Julia played with 2 more kids on Tuesday than on Wednesday.

The answer is: 2
