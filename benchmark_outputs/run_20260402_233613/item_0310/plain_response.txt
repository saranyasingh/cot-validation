To determine how many more kids Julia played with on Tuesday than on Wednesday, we need to subtract the number of kids she played with on Wednesday from the number of kids she played with on Tuesday.

Number of kids played with on Tuesday = 11
Number of kids played with on Wednesday = 9

Difference = Number of kids on Tuesday - Number of kids on Wednesday
           = 11 - 9
           = 2

Therefore, Julia played with 2 more kids on Tuesday than on Wednesday. The answer is: 2.
