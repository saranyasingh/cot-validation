Premise 1: After a typhoon, two trees in Haley's backyard died.
Premise 2: She had grown 11 trees initially.
Conclusion: Therefore, the number of trees that survived the typhoon minus the number that died equals 9.

Logical Syllogism:
1. Initial number of trees = 11 (given)
2. Number of trees that died = 2 (given after typhoon)
3. Number of trees that survived = Initial number - Number that died = 11 - 2 = 9

Therefore, the answer is: The number of trees that survived the typhoon is 9 more than those that died.
