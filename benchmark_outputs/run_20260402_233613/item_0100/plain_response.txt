To determine how many more trees survived the typhoon than those that died, we need to follow these steps:

1. Identify the number of trees that survived.
   - Haley originally had 11 trees.
   - After the typhoon, 2 trees died.
   - Therefore, the number of trees that survived is \( 11 - 2 = 9 \).

2. Calculate the difference between the number of trees that survived and those that died.
   - The number of trees that survived is 9.
   - The number of trees that died is 2.
   - The difference is \( 9 - 2 = 7 \).

So, the number of trees that survived the typhoon is 7 more than those that died. 

The answer is: 7
