**Premise 1:** Debby bought 95 soda bottles.
- This establishes the total number of soda bottles available.

**Premise 2:** Debby bought 180 water bottles.
- This establishes the total number of water bottles available.

**Premise 3:** Debby drinks 15 water bottles per day.

**Premise 4:** Debby drinks 54 soda bottles per day.
- This sets the daily consumption rate for soda bottles.

**Premise 5:** We need to determine how long the water bottles will last based on the daily consumption rates.
- This connects the information about bottle types and consumption rates directly to our goal.

**Conclusion:** To find out how many days the water bottles will last, we divide the total number of water bottles by the daily consumption rate of water bottles.
- \( \frac{180}{15} = 12 \) days.

The answer is: **12**

- We have established that there are 180 water bottles (Premise 2).

- By dividing the total number of water bottles by the daily consumption rate, we calculate the number of days the water bottles will last (Conclusion).
