To determine how long the water bottles will last Debby, we need to calculate the total number of days each type of bottle can be consumed based on her daily consumption rates and then compare them.

First, let's calculate the number of days the water bottles will last:
- Debby drinks 15 water bottles per day.
- She has 180 water bottles in total.

\[
\text{Number of days the water bottles will last} = \frac{\text{Total number of water bottles}}{\text{Water bottles consumed per day}}
\]

\[
= \frac{180}{15}
\]

\[
= 12 \text{ days}
\]

Therefore, the water bottles will last Debby for 12 days.
