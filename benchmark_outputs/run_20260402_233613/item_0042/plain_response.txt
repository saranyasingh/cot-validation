To determine how many more hours Ryan spends on learning English compared to Spanish, we need to subtract the number of hours spent on Spanish from the number of hours spent on English.

Ryan spends:
- 7 hours on English
- 4 hours on Spanish

The calculation is as follows:

\[ \text{Difference} = \text{Hours on English} - \text{Hours on Spanish} \]

Substituting the given values:

\[ \text{Difference} = 7 - 4 = 3 \]

So, Ryan spends 3 more hours studying English than he does on Spanish. 

The answer is: 3
