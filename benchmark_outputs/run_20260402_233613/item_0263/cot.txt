**Logical Syllogism**

1. **Premise 1**: Baker made 19 cakes.
   - This establishes the initial quantity of cakes available.

2. **Premise 2**: Baker made 131 pastries.
   - This establishes the initial quantity of pastries available.

3. **Premise 3**: Baker sold 70 cakes.
   - This provides information about what happened to the cakes after they were made.

4. **Premise 4**: Baker sold 88 pastries.
   - This provides information about what happened to the pastries after they were made.

5. **Conclusion 1**: Calculate the remaining number of cakes:
   \[
   \text{Remaining cakes} = \text{Initial cakes} - \text{Sold cakes}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Remaining cakes} = 19 - 70 = -51
   \]

6. **Conclusion 2**: Calculate the remaining number of pastries:
   \[
   \text{Remaining pastries} = \text{Initial pastries} - \text{Sold pastries}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Remaining pastries} = 131 - 88 = 43
   \]

7. **Comparison**: Determine how many more pastries than cakes were made.
   \[
   \text{Difference} = \text{Remaining pastries} - \text{Remaining cakes}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Difference} = 43 - (-51) = 43 + 51 = 94
   \]

**The answer is: 94**

This conclusion shows that despite selling some cakes, the baker still managed to produce significantly more pastries than cakes before any sales occurred.
