**Logical Syllogism**

Premise 1:
- The Razorback T-shirt shop made $60 by selling t-shirts.
- They made $10 dollars off each t-shirt sold.

Premise 2:
- To find out how many t-shirts were sold, we need to divide the total revenue ($60) by the price per t-shirt ($10).

Conclusion:
- Therefore, the number of t-shirts sold = Total Revenue / Price per t-shirt = $60 / $10 = 6 t-shirts.

The answer is: 6 t-shirts.
