To determine how many nails John needs for the house wall, we'll break down the problem into two parts:

**Premise 1:** John uses 13 large planks of wood.
- This tells us that there are 13 large planks available for securing with nails.

**Premise 2:** Each large plank requires 17 pieces of nails.
- This means for all the large planks combined:
\[ \text{Total nails for large planks} = 13 \times 17 \]

Calculating this gives:
\[ 13 \times 17 = 221 \]
So, John needs 221 nails for the large planks.

**Premise 3:** In addition, John needs 8 nails for some smaller planks.
- This indicates an extra requirement specific to smaller planks, which do not affect the calculation for large planks but add to the total number of nails required.

**Conclusion:** Adding the nails for both types of planks together:
\[ \text{Total nails needed} = 221 + 8 \]

Performing the addition:
\[ 221 + 8 = 229 \]

Therefore, John needs **229 nails** for the house wall.

The answer is: 229
