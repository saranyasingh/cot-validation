Let's break down this problem using a logical syllogism:

1. **Premise**: Danny collected bottle caps and wrappers.
   - This sets up our initial conditions and establishes that we're dealing with two types of items (bottle caps and wrappers) that Danny gathered.

2. **Premise**: At the park, Danny found 11 bottle caps and 28 wrappers.
   - We know these specific quantities when he was there. This gives us an exact count of how much Danny had initially.

3. **Conclusion**: After collecting, Danny now has 68 bottle caps and 51 wrappers in his collection.
   - We need to determine how many more wrappers than bottle caps Danny found at the park.

4. **Step-by-Step Logical Reasoning**:
   - Let \( B \) represent the number of bottle caps Danny originally had.
     \[
     B = 11
     \]
   - Let \( W \) represent the number of wrappers Danny originally had.
     \[
     W = 28
     \]

5. **Determine Initial Total Items Collected**:
   - The total number of items Danny collected before leaving the park would be:
     \[
     B + W = 11 + 28 = 39
     \]

6. **Current Collection Details**:
   - After collecting, Danny has 68 bottle caps and 51 wrappers.
   - The current total number of items is:
     \[
     68 + 51 = 119
     \]

7. **Calculate Additional Items Found**:
   - To find out how many additional items Danny found, subtract the original amount from the current total:
     \[
     119 - 39 = 80
     \]

8. **Relate Additional Items to Wrappers and Bottle Caps**:
   - We need to see if the extra items were primarily wrappers or bottles caps.
   - Since Danny originally had fewer wrappers (\(W = 28\)) compared to bottle caps (\(B = 11\)), it’s likely he found more wrappers than bottle caps.

9. **Verify Calculation**:
   - If we assume all additional items were wrappers:
     \[
     80 = 28 + \text{(additional bottle caps)}
     \]
   - Solving for additional bottle caps:
     \[
     \text{Additional bottle caps} = 80 - 28 = 52
     \]

Since the calculation shows 80 items were added overall, and assuming most of them were wrappers (as they outnumbered bottle caps), the difference between the number of wrappers and bottle caps should reflect this imbalance. Thus, the number of additional wrappers over bottle caps is indeed higher.

Therefore, Danny found more wrappers than bottle caps at the park.

The answer is: 51
