To determine how many rounds Luke played based on his total score and the points he gained per round, we can follow these steps:

**Premise 1:** Luke's total score was 154 points.
- This is our starting point for calculating the number of rounds.

**Premise 2:** He gained 11 points in each round.
- Each round yields 11 points, which is a given condition that we need to use consistently throughout our calculation.

**Step-by-step Logical Reasoning:**

1. **Calculate the total points earned per round:**
   - Points per round = 11

2. **Determine the number of rounds played using the total score:**
   - Total score = 154
   - Number of rounds = Total score / Points per round
   - Number of rounds = 154 / 11

3. **Perform the division:**
   - 154 ÷ 11 = 14

Therefore, Luke played 14 rounds.

The answer is:
The answer is: 14
