To convert the given chain-of-thought reasoning into structured First-Order Logic (FOL), we'll follow the guidelines provided. Here’s the step-by-step process:

### Facts
These are the initial conditions given in the premise statements.

```fola
FACT-1: Some animal migrates back to cooler countries during summer :: MigratesBackToCoolerCountriesDuringSummer()
```

### Rules
These are derived from the logical relationships presented in the premises.

```fola
RULE-1: MigratesBackToCoolerCountriesDuringSummer implies livesNearMountain :: ∀x(MigratesBackToCoolerCountriesDuringSummer(x) → LivesNearMountain(x))
```

### Inferences
These are the conclusions drawn based on combining the facts and rules.

```fola
INF-1: There are 87 bird families living near a mountain :: ∃x(LivesNearMountain(x) ∧ x ∈ BirdFamily)
INF-2: New bird families join the existing population :: ∃y(y ∈ NewBirdFamily & y ≠ 0)
INF-3: Total bird families after joining :: 87 + 18 = 105 :: ∃z(z ∈ BirdFamily & z > 0)
```
