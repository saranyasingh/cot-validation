### Facts
These are the direct statements from the premise without any quantifiers.

```plaintext
FACT-1: There were 12 customers at the start :: CustomerCount(S) = 12
```

### Inferences
These are derived conclusions based on the facts and rules provided.

```plaintext
INF-1: The number of customers reaches zero :: CustomerCount(t_final) = 0
```
