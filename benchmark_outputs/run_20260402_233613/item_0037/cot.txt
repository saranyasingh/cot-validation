To determine the length of Jesse's room given its width and the required area of the carpet, we can use the formula for the area of a rectangle:

Area = Length × Width

We know:
- Area (A) = 14 square feet
- Width (W) = 7 feet

Let L be the length of the room. We need to find L.

Step 1: Set up the equation using the known values.
\[ A = L \times W \]
\[ 14 = L \times 7 \]

Step 2: Solve for L by dividing both sides of the equation by 7.
\[ L = \frac{14}{7} \]

Step 3: Perform the division.
\[ L = 2 \]

Therefore, the length of Jesse's room is 2 feet.

The answer is: 2
