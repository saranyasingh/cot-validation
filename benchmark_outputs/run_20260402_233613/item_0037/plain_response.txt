To find the length of Jesse's room, we can use the formula for the area of a rectangle:

\[ \text{Area} = \text{Length} \times \text{Width} \]

Given:
- Area = 14 square feet
- Width = 7 feet

We need to solve for Length. Rearranging the formula gives us:

\[ \text{Length} = \frac{\text{Area}}{\text{Width}} \]

Substituting the given values:

\[ \text{Length} = \frac{14}{7} = 2 \text{ feet} \]

So, the length of Jesse's room is 2 feet. The answer is: 2.
