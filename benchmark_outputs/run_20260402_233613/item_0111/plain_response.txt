To determine how many crackers each friend ate, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total number of crackers given out:** Matthew has 32 crackers and he gives an equal number of crackers to each of his 4 friends.
   \[
   \text{Number of crackers per friend} = \frac{\text{Total crackers}}{\text{Number of friends}} = \frac{32}{4}
   \]

2. **Perform the division:**
   \[
   \frac{32}{4} = 8
   \]
   So, each friend receives 8 crackers.

Therefore, the answer is:
The answer is: 8
