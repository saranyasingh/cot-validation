Let's break down this problem into a logical syllogism:

1. **Premise 1**: Two birds were initially sitting on the fence.
   - This establishes our starting point and gives us the initial number of birds (2).

2. **Premise 2**: More birds came to join them.
   - This introduces an additional unknown number of birds that joined the initial two.

3. **Premise 3**: After these new birds arrived, the total count of birds on the fence became 6.
   - This provides us with the final number of birds (6) after all the arrivals.

4. **Conclusion**: We need to determine how many more birds came to sit on the fence.
   - Let’s denote the number of new birds as \( x \).
   - According to Premise 3, the sum of the initial two birds plus the \( x \) new birds equals 6:
     \[
     2 + x = 6
     \]

5. **Solving for \( x \)**:
   - To find \( x \), we subtract 2 from both sides of the equation:
     \[
     x = 6 - 2
     \]
   - Simplifying this gives us:
     \[
     x = 4
     \]

Therefore, the answer is: 4

The logic here is straightforward: we started with 2 birds and ended up with 6 birds after some new ones joined. By isolating the variable representing the new birds (\( x \)), we found out exactly how many new birds came to sit on the fence.
