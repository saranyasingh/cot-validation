Let's break down this problem using a logical syllogism:

1. **Premise 1**: Gwen received 2 dollars from her mom.
   - This establishes the initial amount of money Gwen has before any spending or receiving additional funds.

2. **Premise 2**: Gwen's dad gave her 4 more dollars.
   - This introduces an additional source of money to Gwen’s total.

3. **Conclusion based on Premises 1 and 2**:
   - Total money Gwen had = Initial amount + Additional amount given by parents
     \[
     \text{Total money} = \$2 + \$4 = \$6
     \]

4. **Step 3**: Gwen then spent 3 dollars.
   - Spending reduces the total amount of money Gwen has.

5. **Calculation after spending**:
   - Remaining money = Total money - Amount spent
     \[
     \text{Remaining money} = \$6 - \$3 = \$3
     \]

Therefore, the answer is: The amount of money Gwen still has is $3.
