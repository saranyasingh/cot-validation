Let's break down the information given in the problem:

1. Rachel picked 7 apples.
2. Then, 2 new apples grew on the tree.
3. After these events, there were 6 apples left on the tree.

To find out how many apples the tree had initially, we need to reverse the actions that happened after Rachel picked the apples.

First, let's add back the 2 apples that grew on the tree:
\[ 6 \text{ (apples remaining)} + 2 \text{ (new apples)} = 8 \text{ (total apples before Rachel picked)} \]

Next, since Rachel picked 7 apples, we subtract those from the total number of apples before she picked them:
\[ 8 \text{ (total apples before Rachel picked)} - 7 \text{ (apples picked by Rachel)} = 1 \text{ (initial apples on the tree)} \]

So, the tree had 1 apple to begin with.

Therefore, the answer is: 1
