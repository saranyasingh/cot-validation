To determine how far the mouse jumped, we need to follow these steps:

1. Determine how far the frog jumped.
2. Use that information to find out how far the mouse jumped.

Let's start with the first step:

Step 1: Calculate the distance the frog jumped.
- We know the grasshopper jumped 25 inches.
- The frog jumped 32 inches farther than the grasshopper.
- Therefore, the frog jumped \( 25 + 32 = 57 \) inches.

Now let's move on to the second step:

Step 2: Calculate the distance the mouse jumped.
- We know the frog jumped 57 inches.
- The mouse jumped 26 inches less than the frog.
- Therefore, the mouse jumped \( 57 - 26 = 31 \) inches.

So, the mouse jumped 31 inches.

The answer is: The mouse jumped 31 inches.
