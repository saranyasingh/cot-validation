To determine how far the mouse jumped, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the distance the frog jumped**:
   - The grasshopper jumped 25 inches.
   - The frog jumped 32 inches farther than the grasshopper.
   \[
   \text{Distance frog jumped} = 25 + 32 = 57 \text{ inches}
   \]

2. **Calculate the distance the mouse jumped**:
   - The frog jumped 57 inches.
   - The mouse jumped 26 inches less than the frog.
   \[
   \text{Distance mouse jumped} = 57 - 26 = 31 \text{ inches}
   \]

Therefore, the mouse jumped 31 inches. 

The answer is: 31
