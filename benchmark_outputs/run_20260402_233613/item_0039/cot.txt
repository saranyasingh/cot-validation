Premise 1: Frank puts 11 pieces of candy in each bag.
Premise 2: He has 22 pieces of candy.
Conclusion: Therefore, he would have 2 bags of candy.

- From Premise 1, we know that each bag contains 11 pieces of candy.
- From Premise 2, we know that Frank has 22 pieces of candy.
- To find out how many bags he has, we need to divide the total number of candies (22) by the number of candies per bag (11).

Therefore, the answer is:
The answer is: 2.
