This seems to be a simple arithmetic problem rather than a logical deduction involving FOL. Let's break down the steps:

### Given Information:
1. **Premise 1:** Frank puts 11 pieces of candy in each bag.
2. **Premise 2:** He has 22 pieces of candy.
3. **Conclusion:** Therefore, he would have 2 bags of candy.

### Reasoning:
To determine how many bags Frank can make with 22 pieces of candy if each bag holds 11 pieces, we perform the division \( \frac{22}{11} \). This calculation will give us the number of bags.

\[ \text{Number of Bags} = \frac{\text{Total Pieces}}{\text{Pieces per Bag}} = \frac{22}{11} = 2 \]

### Conclusion:
Frank would have 2 bags of candy.

- Fact-2: CandyPerBag(11) :: 11

No specific rules needed since it's just an arithmetic operation.
