To determine how many bags Frank can fill with 11 pieces of candy per bag and 22 pieces of candy total, we need to perform division:

Total number of candies = 22
Candies per bag = 11

Number of bags = Total number of candies ÷ Candies per bag

Number of bags = 22 ÷ 11 = 2

Therefore, Frank would have 2 bags. The answer is: 2.
