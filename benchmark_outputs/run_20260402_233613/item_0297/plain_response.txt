Let's break down the problem step by step:

1. Initially, there are 3 birds on the fence.
2. Then, 6 more storks come to join them.
3. After that, 2 more birds also arrive.

Now let's calculate:
- Total number of storks = Initial storks + Additional storks = 0 + 6 = 6 storks
- Total number of birds = Initial birds + Additional birds = 3 + 2 = 5 birds

To find out how many more storks than birds are sitting on the fence, we subtract the total number of birds from the total number of storks:
- Difference = Number of storks - Number of birds = 6 - 5 = 1

Therefore, there is 1 more stork than birds sitting on the fence.

The answer is: 1
