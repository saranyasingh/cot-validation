To determine how many people each seat on the Ferris wheel can hold, we need to follow these steps:

### Premise 1:
There are 16 people riding the Ferris wheel simultaneously.

Premise 2:
The Ferris wheel has 4 seats.

### Logical Reasoning (Syllogism):
**Major Premise:** There are 16 people.
**Minor Premise:** The Ferris wheel has 4 seats.
**Conclusion:** Each seat can hold 4 people.

This conclusion logically follows because if there are 16 people and only 4 seats available, then each seat must be occupied by exactly 4 people. This ensures that all passengers fit within the given number of seats.

Therefore, **each seat holds 4 people**.

The answer is: Each seat holds 4 people.
