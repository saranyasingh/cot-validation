To determine how much more money Gwen received from her mom compared to her dad, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Identify the total amount of money Gwen received from her parents:** 
   - Gwen received $8 from her mom.
   - Gwen received $5 from her dad.
   
2. **Calculate the difference between what Gwen received from her mom and her dad:**
   - Total from mom = $8
   - Total from dad = $5
   
3. **Subtract the amount received from her dad from the amount received from her mom:**
   \[
   \text{Difference} = \text{Total from mom} - \text{Total from dad}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Difference} = \$8 - \$5 = \$3
   \]

Therefore, Gwen received $3 more from her mom than she did from her dad.

The answer is: \(3\)
