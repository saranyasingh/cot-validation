Let's break down the information given:

1. Gwen received $8 from her mom.
2. She received an additional $5 from her dad.
3. She spent $4.

Now, let's calculate how much money Gwen has in total after receiving and spending some of it:
- Total money Gwen had initially = Money from mom + Money from dad = $8 + $5 = $13
- Money spent = $4

So, the remaining amount Gwen has = Initial total - Spent amount = $13 - $4 = $9

To find out how much more money she received from her mom compared to her dad:
- Difference = Money from mom - Money from dad = $8 - $5 = $3

Therefore, the answer is: The difference is $3.
