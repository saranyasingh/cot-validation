Let's break down this problem into a logical syllogism:

**Premise 1:** Julia played tag with a total of 18 kids.
- This establishes the total number of kids involved.

- This provides information about her first day of playing.

**Premise 3:** Let x be the number of kids Julia played with on Tuesday.
- We need to find the value of x.

  - Kids on Monday = 4
  - Kids on Tuesday = Total kids - Kids on Monday
