To find out how many cookies are in each bag, we need to divide the total number of cookies by the number of bags. 

Given:
- Total number of cookies = 36
- Number of bags = 4

So, the number of cookies per bag would be:

\[ \text{Cookies per bag} = \frac{\text{Total number of cookies}}{\text{Number of bags}} \]

\[ \text{Cookies per bag} = \frac{36}{4} \]

\[ \text{Cookies per bag} = 9 \]

Therefore, each bag has 9 cookies. The answer is: 9.
