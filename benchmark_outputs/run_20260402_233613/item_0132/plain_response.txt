To determine how many students suggested mashed potatoes, we need to follow these steps:

1. Identify the total number of students who suggested suggestions.
2. Subtract the number of students who suggested adding bacon from the total number of suggestions.

Given:
- Total number of students = 288
- Number of students suggesting bacon = 264

Now, calculate the number of students suggesting mashed potatoes:

Number of students suggesting mashed potatoes = Total number of students - Number of students suggesting bacon
= 288 - 264
= 24

Therefore, the answer is: The number of students who suggested mashed potatoes is 24.
