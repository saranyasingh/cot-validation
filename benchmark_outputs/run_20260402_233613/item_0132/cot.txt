To determine how many students suggested mashed potatoes, we need to analyze the information provided and apply basic arithmetic principles:

1. **Total number of students surveyed**: 
   - Total = 288

2. **Number of students who suggested adding bacon**:
   - Bacon suggestions = 264

3. **Calculation for students suggesting mashed potatoes**:
   - Let's denote the total number of students as \( T \).
   - Number of students suggesting bacon = 264.
   - Let \( M \) represent the number of students suggesting mashed potatoes.

Since all other students suggested bacon, it implies that those remaining must have suggested mashed potatoes. Therefore, we can set up an equation based on these observations:

\[ T = (B + M) \]

Where B represents the number of students who suggested bacon.

Given:
- Total students (\(T\)) = 288
- Students who suggested bacon (\(B\)) = 264

We substitute these values into our equation:

\[ 288 = (264 + M) \]

Now solve for \(M\) (the number of students who suggested mashed potatoes):

Subtract 264 from both sides:

\[ 288 - 264 = M \]
\[ 24 = M \]

Therefore, the number of students who suggested mashed potatoes is 24.

The answer is: 24
