Premise 1: Winter is almost here.
Premise 2: Most animals migrate to warmer countries when winter approaches.
Premise 3: There are 8 bird families living near the mountain.
Premise 4: 42 bird families flew away to Africa.
Premise 5: 31 bird families flew away to Asia.

Conclusion: There were 11 more bird families that flew away to Africa than those that flew away to Asia.

Logical Syllogism:

Step 1: All animals migrate to warmer countries during winter (from Premise 2).
Step 2: Therefore, all birds in this region also migrate to warmer countries during winter (conclusion based on Step 1).

Step 3: Birds near the mountain migrated to either Africa or Asia due to the migration trend (from Premises 3 & 4).
Step 4: From Step 3, we know that there were two groups of birds that migrated - to Africa and to Asia (as stated in Step 4).

Step 5: We need to compare the number of birds that flew to Africa versus Asia.
Step 6: It's given that 42 birds flew to Africa and 31 birds flew to Asia.
Step 7: To find out how many more birds flew to Africa, subtract the number of birds that flew to Asia from the number of birds that flew to Africa:
   Number of birds that flew to Africa - Number of birds that flew to Asia = Difference

Substitute the numbers into Step 7:
42 - 31 = 11

Therefore, there were 11 more bird families that flew away to Africa than those that flew away to Asia.

The answer is: 11
