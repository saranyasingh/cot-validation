To determine how many sacks of ripe oranges will be available after 25 days of harvest, we need to follow these steps:

### Step-by-Step Logical Reasoning

**Premise 1:** 
The total number of sacks of ripe oranges harvested daily is 82.
This means:
\[ \text{Daily yield} = 82 \text{sacks/day} \]

**Premise 2:** 
The total number of sacks of unripe oranges harvested daily is 61.
This means:
\[ \text{Daily yield of unripe oranges} = 61 \text{sacks/day} \]

**Premise 3:** 
We need to find the total number of sacks of ripe oranges harvested over 25 days.
To do this, we multiply the daily yield of ripe oranges by the number of days (25):
\[ \text{Total sacks of ripe oranges after 25 days} = \text{Daily yield of ripe oranges} \times \text{Number of days} \]
\[ \text{Total sacks of ripe oranges after 25 days} = 82 \text{sacks/day} \times 25 \text{days} \]

**Logical Conclusion:**
\[ \text{Total sacks of ripe oranges after 25 days} = 2050 \text{sacks} \]

Therefore, the answer is: 2050 sacks of ripe oranges.
