To determine how many sacks of ripe oranges will be harvested after 25 days, we need to follow these steps:

1. Identify the daily production rate of ripe oranges.
2. Multiply the daily production rate by the number of days.

Given:
- Daily production rate of ripe oranges = 82 sacks/day

We want to find out the total production over 25 days:
\[ \text{Total sacks of ripe oranges} = \text{Daily production rate} \times \text{Number of days} \]
\[ \text{Total sacks of ripe oranges} = 82 \times 25 \]

Now, perform the multiplication:
\[ 82 \times 25 = (80 + 2) \times 25 = 80 \times 25 + 2 \times 25 \]
\[ 80 \times 25 = 2000 \]
\[ 2 \times 25 = 50 \]
\[ 2000 + 50 = 2050 \]

Therefore, the total number of sacks of ripe oranges after 25 days will be 2050.

The answer is: 2050
