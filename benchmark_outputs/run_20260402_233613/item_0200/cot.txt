To determine how much longer Jesse's room is compared to its width, we need to calculate the difference between the length of the room and its width. Let's break this down into steps:

1. **Identify the dimensions of the room**: 
   - Length = 20 feet
   - Width = 19 feet

2. **Determine what we're comparing**:
   - We want to find out how much longer the room's length is than its width.

3. **Set up the subtraction**:
   - Subtract the width from the length:
     \[
     20 \text{ feet} - 19 \text{ feet}
     \]

4. **Perform the subtraction**:
   - \( 20 - 19 = 1 \)

5. **Conclusion**:
   - The result of the subtraction tells us that Jesse's room is 1 foot longer than its width.

Therefore, the answer is: The room is 1 foot longer than it is wide.
