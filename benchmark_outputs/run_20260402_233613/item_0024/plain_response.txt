To find out how many pages are in the first chapter, we can use the information given about the total number of pages and the length of the second chapter.

1. We know that the total number of pages in the book is 67.
2. The second chapter is 36 pages long.
3. To find the number of pages in the first chapter, we subtract the number of pages in the second chapter from the total number of pages:

\[ \text{Pages in first chapter} = \text{Total pages} - \text{Pages in second chapter} \]

\[ \text{Pages in first chapter} = 67 - 36 \]

\[ \text{Pages in first chapter} = 31 \]

Therefore, the answer is: 31.
