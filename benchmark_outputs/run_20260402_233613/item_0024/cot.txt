1. Premise 1: There are two chapters in the book.
   - Let's denote these chapters as Chapter 1 and Chapter 2.
   
2. Premise 2: Chapter 2 contains 36 pages.
   - This directly states that Chapter 2 has 36 pages.
   
3. Premise 3: The total number of pages in the book is 67.
   - We know this information to be true based on the given statement.
   
4. Conclusion: To find out how many pages are in Chapter 1, we need to subtract the number of pages in Chapter 2 from the total number of pages in the book.
   - Mathematically, this would be \( \text{Total Pages} - \text{Pages in Chapter 2} = \text{Pages in Chapter 1} \).
   - Substituting the known values: \( 67 - 36 = 31 \).

Therefore, the answer is: The first chapter has 31 pages.
