To determine how many necklaces Emily can make, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total number of beads needed per necklace**: Each necklace requires 3 beads.
   
   \[
   \text{Total beads per necklace} = 3
   \]

2. **Determine the total number of beads Emily has**: Emily has 6 beads in total.

3. **Calculate the maximum number of necklaces she can make**:
   - We divide the total number of beads by the number of beads required per necklace.

   \[
   \text{Number of necklaces} = \frac{\text{Total beads}}{\text{Beads per necklace}} = \frac{6}{3} = 2
   \]

Therefore, Emily can make 2 necklaces. 

The answer is: 2
