1. **Premise**: Emily has 6 beads.
   - This sets the initial quantity available for crafting necklaces.

2. **Premise**: Each necklace requires 3 beads.
   - This establishes the required number of beads per necklace to be made.

3. **Premise**: Emily wants to make necklaces for her 72 friends.
   - This specifies the total number of necklaces needed based on the number of people she plans to share them with.

4. **Logical Reasoning**:
   - To find out how many necklaces Emily can make, we need to divide the total number of beads (which she has) by the number of beads required per necklace.
   - Mathematically, this would be \( \frac{6}{3} = 2 \).

5. **Conclusion**:
   - Therefore, Emily can make 2 necklaces given that she has 6 beads and each necklace needs 3 beads.
