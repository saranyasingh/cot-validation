To determine how many friends Matthew gave crackers to, we can use division. We know that Matthew has 8 crackers and gives 2 crackers to each friend. So, we divide the total number of crackers by the number of crackers given per friend:

\[ \text{Number of friends} = \frac{\text{Total number of crackers}}{\text{Crackers per friend}} \]

Substituting the known values:

\[ \text{Number of friends} = \frac{8}{2} \]

Performing the division:

\[ \text{Number of friends} = 4 \]

Therefore, Matthew gave crackers to 4 friends. The answer is: 4.
