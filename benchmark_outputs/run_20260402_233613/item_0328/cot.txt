Premise 1: Matthew has 8 crackers.
Premise 2: Matthew gives 2 crackers to each friend.
Premise 3: We want to find out how many friends Matthew gave crackers to.

Conclusion: Matthew gave crackers to 4 friends.

Here's the logical syllogism:

1. Matthew has 8 crackers (Premise 1).
2. Matthew gives 2 crackers to each friend (Premise 2).
3. To find out how many friends received crackers, we need to divide the total number of crackers by the number of crackers per friend.
4. Therefore, we perform the division: \( \frac{8}{2} = 4 \).

The answer is: The answer is: 4.
