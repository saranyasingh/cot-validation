To determine how far the frog jumped, we need to follow these steps:

### Step 1: Determine the distance the frog jumped.
- The problem states that the frog jumped 17 inches farther than the grasshopper.
- We know the grasshopper jumped 36 inches.
  
   Therefore:
\[ \text{Distance frog jumped} = \text{Distance grasshopper jumped} + 17 \]
\[ \text{Distance frog jumped} = 36 + 17 \]

### Step 2: Calculate the total distance the frog jumped.
\[ \text{Distance frog jumped} = 36 + 17 \]
\[ \text{Distance frog jumped} = 53 \]

### Conclusion:
Therefore, the frog jumped 53 inches.

The answer is: **53**
