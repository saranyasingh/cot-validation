Let's convert the given chain of thought reasoning into structured First-Order Logic (FOL). 

### Facts
1. The grasshopper jumped 36 inches.
   - Let \( G_h \) represent the distance the grasshopper jumped.
   - Fact: \( G_h = 36 \)

2. The frog jumped 17 inches farther than the grasshopper.
   - Let \( F_f \) represent the distance the frog jumped.
   - Fact: \( F_f = G_h + 17 \)

### Rules
There are no additional rules provided beyond what is needed to derive the conclusion.

### Inferences
From the facts derived, we can calculate the distance the frog jumped:
\[ F_f = 36 + 17 \]
\[ F_f = 53 \]

### Final Answer
Therefore, the frog jumped 53 inches.
