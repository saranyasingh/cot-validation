Premise 1: Every day Ryan spends 6 hours on learning English.
Premise 2: Every day Ryan spends 7 hours on learning Chinese.
Premise 3: Every day Ryan spends 3 hours on learning Spanish.

To find out how many total hours Ryan spends on learning English, Chinese, and Spanish in a single day, we need to add up the time spent on each language:

Step 1: Add the time spent on English (6 hours) + Time spent on Chinese (7 hours)
6 + 7 = 13

Step 2: Add the time spent on Spanish (3 hours) to the result from Step 1:
13 + 3 = 16

Therefore, the conclusion is that Ryan spends a total of 16 hours per day studying English, Chinese, and Spanish combined. 

The answer is: 16
