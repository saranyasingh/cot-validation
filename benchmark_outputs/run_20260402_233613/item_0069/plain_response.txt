To find out how many pages are in the book, we can use the formula:

\[
\text{Total Pages} = \text{Pages Per Day} \times \text{Days}
\]

Given:
- Pages per day = 22
- Days = 569

Plugging these values into the formula:

\[
\text{Total Pages} = 22 \times 569 = 12438
\]

Therefore, the total number of pages in the book is **12438**.
