Let's break down the problem using a logical syllogism:

1. **Premise 1:** Frank reads 22 pages per day.
   - This establishes the rate at which Frank consumes information from the book.

2. **Premise 2:** It took Frank 569 days to finish the book.
   - This tells us how long the entire book takes to be completed.

3. **Conclusion:** We need to determine the total number of pages in the book.
   - To find this, we multiply the daily reading rate (pages per day) by the total number of days it took to complete the book.

4. **Calculation:** 
   \[
   \text{Total Pages} = \text{Pages per Day} \times \text{Number of Days}
   \]
   Substituting the values:
   \[
   \text{Total Pages} = 22 \times 569
   \]

5. **Performing the multiplication:**
   \[
   22 \times 569 = 22 \times (500 + 60 + 9)
   \]
   Breaking it down further:
   \[
   22 \times 500 = 11000
   \]
   \[
   22 \times 60 = 1320
   \]
   \[
   22 \times 9 = 198
   \]
   Adding these results together:
   \[
   11000 + 1320 + 198 = 12518
   \]

Therefore, the answer is: The book has 12,518 pages.
