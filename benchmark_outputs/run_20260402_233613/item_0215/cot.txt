Premise 1: Robin's initial hair length was 14 inches.
Premise 2: His hair grew by 8 inches.
Premise 3: After growing, he then cut off 20 inches.

To derive the conclusion:
Step 1: Start with the initial length of Robin's hair.
Conclusion (from Premise 1): Robin’s hair initially measured 14 inches.

Step 2: Add the growth to the initial length.
Calculation: Initial length + Growth = 14 inches + 8 inches = 22 inches.
Conclusion (after adding growth): Robin's hair has grown to be 22 inches long.

Step 3: Subtract the amount cut off from the current total.
Calculation: Current length - Cut off = 22 inches - 20 inches = 2 inches.
Conclusion (after cutting off): Robin's hair is now 2 inches long.

The answer is: 2
