To determine how many apples He has, we can follow these steps:

1. Let's denote the number of apples Adam has as \( A \).
2. Let's denote the number of apples Jackie has as \( J \).
3. According to the problem, Adam and Jackie together have 7 apples:
   \[
   A + J = 7
   \]
4. The problem states that He has 9 apples more than what Adam and Jackie together have:
   \[
   H = (A + J) + 9
   \]

Now, substituting the value of \( A + J \) from step 3 into step 4:
\[
H = 7 + 9 = 16
\]

Therefore, He has 16 apples.

The answer is: 16
