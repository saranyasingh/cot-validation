To determine how many minutes the machine worked today, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Identify the total number of shirts made today**: 
   - Yesterday, the machine made 9 shirts.
   - Today, it made an additional 8 shirts.
   - Therefore, the total number of shirts made today is:
     \[
     9 + 8 = 17 \text{ shirts}
     \]

2. **Determine the rate at which the machine makes shirts**:
   - The machine can make 2 shirts per minute.

3. **Calculate the time taken to produce 17 shirts**:
   - If the machine produces 2 shirts per minute, then to find out how long it takes to produce 17 shirts, we use the formula:
     \[
     \text{Time} = \frac{\text{Total number of shirts}}{\text{Rate}}
     \]
   - Substituting the values:
     \[
     \text{Time} = \frac{17}{2} \text{ minutes}
     \]

4. **Perform the division**:
   - Dividing 17 by 2 gives us:
     \[
     \frac{17}{2} = 8.5 \text{ minutes}
     \]

Thus, the machine worked for 8.5 minutes today.

The answer is: 8.5 minutes
