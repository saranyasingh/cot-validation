To determine how many minutes the machine worked today, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total number of shirts made today**:
   - The machine made 8 shirts today.
   
2. **Determine the rate at which the machine makes shirts**:
   - The machine makes 2 shirts per minute.

3. **Use the formula to find the time spent making shirts**:
   \[
   \text{Time} = \frac{\text{Total number of shirts}}{\text{Rate}}
   \]
   Plugging in the values:
   \[
   \text{Time} = \frac{8}{2}
   \]

4. **Perform the division**:
   \[
   \text{Time} = 4 \text{ minutes}
   \]

Therefore, the machine worked for 4 minutes today. 

The answer is: 4
