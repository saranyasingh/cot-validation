To determine how many crackers Matthew gave to his friends, we can follow these steps:

1. First, calculate the total number of crackers given away:
   - Matthew started with 24 crackers.
   - He ended up with 17 crackers after giving some to his friends.
   - Therefore, the number of crackers given away is \( 24 - 17 = 7 \).

2. Since Matthew gave equal numbers of crackers to each of his 3 friends, we divide the total number of crackers given away by the number of friends:
   - The number of crackers given to each friend is \( 7 \div 3 \).
   - This division results in a quotient of 2 and a remainder of 1 (since \( 7 = 3 \times 2 + 1 \)).

So, Matthew gave 2 crackers to each of his friends, which totals to \( 2 \times 3 = 6 \) crackers.

Therefore, the answer is: 6
