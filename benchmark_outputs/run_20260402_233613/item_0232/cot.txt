Let's break down this problem into a logical syllogism:

**Premise 1:** Matthew had 24 crackers initially.
- This is our starting point, giving us the total amount of crackers Matthew started with.

**Premise 2:** Matthew gave equal numbers of crackers to his 3 friends.
- We know that Matthew distributed crackers equally among his friends, which means he divided them into three groups.

**Premise 3:** After distributing the crackers, Matthew still had 17 crackers left.
- This tells us what was left after distribution, helping us determine how much was given away.

Now, let's combine these premises to form a logical syllogism:

1. **Given Premises:** 
   - Matthew started with 24 crackers.
   - He gave out an equal number of crackers to 3 friends (let’s call this number \(x\)).
   - After giving out \(x\) crackers to each friend, he has 17 crackers left.

2. **Conclusion:**
   - To find out how many crackers were given to each friend (\(x\)), we need to subtract the remaining crackers from the initial amount:
     \[
     x = 24 - 17
     \]

3. **Calculation:**
   \[
   x = 6
   \]

Therefore, Matthew gave 6 crackers to each of his friends.

The answer is: 6
