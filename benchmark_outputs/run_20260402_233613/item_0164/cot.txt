To determine how many bottle caps Danny had initially, we need to consider the information given about his current total and the changes that occurred.

**Premise 1:** Danny found 82 wrappers and 29 bottle caps at the park.
- This tells us the number of items he collected there.

**Premise 2:** After collecting these items, Danny now has 42 bottle caps and 61 wrappers in his collection.
- This gives us his current totals after finding the new items.

**Step-by-step Logical Reasoning:**

1. **Identify Initial Items Collected:**
   - From Premise 1, we know Danny collected 82 wrappers and 29 bottle caps at the park.

2. **Calculate Current Totals:**
   - According to Premise 2, after collecting the items, Danny has:
     - 42 bottle caps
     - 61 wrappers

3. **Determine Initial Bottle Caps:**
   - Since the problem states that Danny collected 29 bottle caps and ended up with 42 bottle caps, we need to find out what he had before this collection.
   - Let \( x \) be the initial number of bottle caps Danny had.

4. **Set Up Equation:**
   - Before collecting the additional 29 bottle caps, Danny had \( x \) bottle caps.
   - After adding 29 more bottle caps, he got 42 bottle caps in total.

5. **Formulate the Equation:**
   - We know:
     \[
     x + 29 = 42
     \]

6. **Solve for \( x \):**
   - To find \( x \), subtract 29 from both sides of the equation:
     \[
     x = 42 - 29
     \]
     \[
     x = 13
     \]

Therefore, Danny had 13 bottle caps at first.

The answer is: 13
