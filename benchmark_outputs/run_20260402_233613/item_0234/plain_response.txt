Let's denote the number of hours Ryan spends on learning Chinese as \( C \). According to the information given:

1. Ryan spends 7 hours on learning English.
2. He spends \( C + 2 \) hours on learning Chinese (since he spends 2 hours more on English than on Chinese).

Since these two activities take up all his study time each day, we can set up an equation:
\[ 7 + (C + 2) = 24 \]

Here, 24 represents the total number of hours in a day. Now let's solve for \( C \):
\[ 7 + C + 2 = 24 \]
\[ C + 9 = 24 \]
\[ C = 24 - 9 \]
\[ C = 15 \]

So, Ryan spends 15 hours on learning Chinese each day.
