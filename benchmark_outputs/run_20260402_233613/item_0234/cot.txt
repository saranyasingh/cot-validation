To determine how many hours Ryan spends on learning Chinese, let's break down the problem using a logical syllogism:

1. **Premise 1**: Every day Ryan spends 7 hours on learning English.
   - This establishes the base amount of time spent on English.

2. **Premise 2**: Ryan spends 2 hours more on learning English than on learning Chinese.
   - Let \( C \) represent the number of hours Ryan spends on learning Chinese.
   - According to Premise 2, \( E = C + 2 \), where \( E \) represents the number of hours spent on English (which we know is 7).

3. **Step 1**: Substitute the value of \( E \) into the equation \( E = C + 2 \).
   - Since \( E = 7 \), we have \( 7 = C + 2 \).

4. **Step 2**: Solve for \( C \).
   - Subtract 2 from both sides of the equation:
     \[
     7 - 2 = C
     \]
   - Simplify:
     \[
     C = 5
     \]

Therefore, the conclusion drawn from these premises is that Ryan spends 5 hours per day on learning Chinese.

The answer is: Ryan spends 5 hours on learning Chinese per day.
