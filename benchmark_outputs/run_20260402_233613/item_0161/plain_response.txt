To determine how many more cakes the baker made than those he sold, we need to subtract the number of cakes sold from the total number of cakes made:

Total cakes made = 133
Cakes sold = 51

Difference = Total cakes made - Cakes sold
           = 133 - 51
           = 82

Therefore, the baker made 82 more cakes than those he sold.

The answer is: 82
