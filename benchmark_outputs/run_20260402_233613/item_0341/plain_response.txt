To determine how many baseball cards Nell had initially, we can set up an equation based on the information given:

Let \( x \) represent the initial number of cards Nell had.

According to the problem:
- Nell gave away 301 cards to Jeff.
- After giving away these cards, she had 154 cards left.

We can express this situation mathematically as:
\[ x - 301 = 154 \]

To find \( x \), we need to isolate it on one side of the equation. We do this by adding 301 to both sides of the equation:
\[ x - 301 + 301 = 154 + 301 \]
\[ x = 455 \]

Thus, Nell initially had 455 cards.

The answer is: 455
