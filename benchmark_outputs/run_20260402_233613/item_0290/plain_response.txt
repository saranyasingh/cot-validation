To determine how many more sweet cookies than salty cookies Paco ate, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the number of sweet cookies Paco ate.
   - Paco originally had 37 sweet cookies.
   - He ate 5 sweet cookies.
   - Therefore, the number of sweet cookies eaten is \( 37 - 5 = 32 \).

2. Calculate the number of salty cookies Paco ate.
   - Paco originally had 11 salty cookies.
   - He ate 2 salty cookies.
   - Therefore, the number of salty cookies eaten is \( 11 - 2 = 9 \).

3. Determine how many more sweet cookies than salty cookies were eaten.
   - The difference between the number of sweet cookies eaten and the number of salty cookies eaten is \( 32 - 9 = 23 \).

So, Paco ate 23 more sweet cookies than salty cookies. 

Therefore, the answer is: 23.
