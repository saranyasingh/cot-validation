To find out how many oranges they harvest per day, you need to multiply the number of sacks harvested per day by the number of oranges in each sack.

Number of sacks per day = 8
Number of oranges per sack = 35

Total oranges harvested per day = Number of sacks per day × Number of oranges per sack
= 8 × 35
= 280
