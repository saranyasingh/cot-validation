To determine how many cookies Paco has left after eating some and giving others away, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Initial number of cookies**: Paco starts with 40 cookies.
2. **Cookies eaten by Paco**: Paco eats 5 cookies.
   - After eating 5 cookies:
     \[
     40 - 5 = 35
     \]
   So, Paco now has 35 cookies left.
3. **Cookies given to his friend**: Paco gives 13 cookies to his friend.
   - After giving 13 cookies to his friend:
     \[
     35 - 13 = 22
     \]
   Therefore, Paco has 22 cookies remaining.

The answer is: 22

In this process, we used simple arithmetic operations (subtraction) based on the information provided about the initial amount of cookies, the number eaten by Paco, and the number given to his friend. Each step logically followed from the previous one, resulting in the final count of cookies Paco has left.
