To determine how many boxes Shirley needs to deliver, we can use a simple multiplication principle based on the information given:

1. **Premise 1:** Shirley sold 25 cases of boxes of trefoils.
   - This tells us that there are a total of 25 groups (cases) of trefoils.

2. **Premise 2:** Each case contains 14 boxes of trefoils.
   - This gives us the number of items per group (case), which is crucial for calculating the total quantity.

3. **Logical Reasoning Step:** To find out the total number of boxes, multiply the number of cases by the number of boxes per case.
   
   \[
   \text{Total Boxes} = \text{Number of Cases} \times \text{Boxes per Case}
   \]

   Substituting the values:
   \[
   \text{Total Boxes} = 25 \times 14
   \]

4. **Calculation Step:** Perform the multiplication to get the result.
   \[
   25 \times 14 = 350
   \]

Therefore, the total number of boxes Shirley needs to deliver is 350.

The answer is: 350
