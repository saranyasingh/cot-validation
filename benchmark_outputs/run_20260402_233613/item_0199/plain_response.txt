To determine how many boxes of trefoils Shirley needs to deliver, we can follow these steps:

1. Identify the number of cases Shirley sold.
   - Shirley sold 25 cases.

2. Determine the number of boxes in each case.
   - Each case contains 14 boxes.

3. Calculate the total number of boxes by multiplying the number of cases by the number of boxes per case.
   \[
   25 \text{ cases} \times 14 \text{ boxes per case} = 350 \text{ boxes}
   \]

Therefore, Shirley needs to deliver 350 boxes of trefoils. The answer is: 350.
