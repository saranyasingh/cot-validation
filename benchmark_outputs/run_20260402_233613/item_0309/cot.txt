To determine how many more red peaches there are than yellow peaches in the basket, we need to compare the quantities of these two types of peaches.

**Premise 1:** There are 19 red peaches.
**Premise 2:** There are 11 yellow peaches.
**Premise 3:** We want to find out how many more red peaches there are than yellow peaches.

We start by subtracting the number of yellow peaches from the number of red peaches:

\[ \text{Number of more red peaches} = \text{Number of red peaches} - \text{Number of yellow peaches} \]

Substituting the given values:

\[ \text{Number of more red peaches} = 19 - 11 \]

Now perform the subtraction:

\[ 19 - 11 = 8 \]

Therefore, there are 8 more red peaches than yellow peaches in the basket.

The answer is: 8
