To determine how many hours Ryan spends on learning English and Chinese over 5 days, we can follow these steps:

1. Calculate the total number of hours spent learning English per day:
   - Ryan spends 6 hours each day on learning English.
   - Over 5 days, this amounts to \(6 \text{ hours/day} \times 5 \text{ days} = 30 \text{ hours}\).

2. Calculate the total number of hours spent learning Chinese per day:
   - Ryan spends 7 hours each day on learning Chinese.
   - Over 5 days, this amounts to \(7 \text{ hours/day} \times 5 \text{ days} = 35 \text{ hours}\).

3. Add the total hours spent on both subjects:
   - Total hours spent on learning English and Chinese = \(30 \text{ hours} + 35 \text{ hours} = 65 \text{ hours}\).

Therefore, the answer is: The answer is: 65
