Let's break down this problem using a logical syllogism:

1. **Premise 1**: Every day Ryan spends 6 hours on learning English.
   - This establishes a consistent daily routine for learning English.

2. **Premise 2**: Every day Ryan spends 7 hours on learning Chinese.
   - This establishes a consistent daily routine for learning Chinese.

3. **Premise 3**: He learns for 5 days.
   - This specifies the duration over which we need to calculate his total time spent learning both languages.

4. **Conclusion**:
   - To find out how many hours he spends on learning English and Chinese in all over these 5 days,
   - We multiply the number of hours he spends on each language per day (6 + 7) by the number of days (5).

Therefore, the calculation steps are as follows:
- Total hours spent on English = 6 hours/day * 5 days = 30 hours
- Total hours spent on Chinese = 7 hours/day * 5 days = 35 hours

Combining both:
Total hours spent on both languages = 30 hours (English) + 35 hours (Chinese) = 65 hours

The answer is: 65 hours.
