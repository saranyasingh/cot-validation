To determine how far the mouse jumped, we need to follow these steps:

**Premise 1:** The grasshopper jumped 25 inches.
- This establishes the baseline distance that the grasshopper reached.

**Premise 2:** The frog jumped 18 inches farther than the grasshopper.
- By adding this extra distance to the grasshopper's jump:
  - Frog's jump = Grasshopper's jump + Extra distance
  - Frog's jump = 25 inches + 18 inches

**Calculation for Premise 2 Result:**
\[ \text{Frog's jump} = 25\text{ inches} + 18\text{ inches} = 43\text{ inches} \]

**Premise 3:** The mouse jumped 2 inches farther than the frog.
- Adding this additional distance to the frog's jump:
  - Mouse's jump = Frog's jump + Extra distance
  - Mouse's jump = 43 inches + 2 inches

**Calculation for Premise 3 Result:**
\[ \text{Mouse's jump} = 43\text{ inches} + 2\text{ inches} = 45\text{ inches} \]

**Conclusion:** The mouse jumped 45 inches.

Therefore, the answer is: The mouse jumped 45 inches.
