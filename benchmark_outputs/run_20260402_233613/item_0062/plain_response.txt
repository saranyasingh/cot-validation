To determine how far the mouse jumped, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the distance the frog jumped.
2. Use that information to find out how far the mouse jumped.

Step 1: Calculate the distance the frog jumped.
The grasshopper jumped 25 inches. The frog jumped 18 inches farther than the grasshopper.
\[ \text{Distance frog jumped} = 25 + 18 = 43 \text{ inches} \]

Step 2: Calculate the distance the mouse jumped.
The mouse jumped 2 inches farther than the frog.
\[ \text{Distance mouse jumped} = 43 + 2 = 45 \text{ inches} \]

Therefore, the mouse jumped 45 inches.
