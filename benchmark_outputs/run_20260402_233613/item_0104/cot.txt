Let's break down this problem into a logical syllogism:

1. **Premise 1**: With 28 dollars, two packs of DVDs can be bought.
   - This tells us that there is an available budget of 28 dollars and we can purchase exactly two packs of DVDs within that budget.

2. **Premise 2**: We need to determine how much each pack costs.
   - To find out the cost per DVD, we must divide the total amount spent (the budget) by the number of items purchased.

3. **Conclusion**:
   - Therefore, if we want to know the cost of each DVD pack, we should calculate \( \frac{28}{2} \).

4. **Calculation**:
   - \( \frac{28}{2} = 14 \)

So, the cost of each pack of DVDs is 14 dollars.

The answer is: 14
