To find out how much each pack of DVDs costs, we need to divide the total amount spent by the number of packs purchased. 

Given:
- Total amount spent = $28
- Number of packs bought = 2

We use the formula:

\[ \text{Cost per pack} = \frac{\text{Total amount spent}}{\text{Number of packs}} \]

Plugging in the values:

\[ \text{Cost per pack} = \frac{28}{2} = 14 \]

So, each pack of DVDs costs $14. The answer is: 14.
