To determine how many emails Jack received in the morning and afternoon, we need to add the number of emails he received in those two periods together.

1. **Premise 1:** Jack received 5 emails in the morning.
   - This establishes the quantity of emails received during the morning period.

2. **Premise 2:** Jack received 8 emails in the afternoon.
   - This provides information about the number of emails received during the afternoon period.

3. **Logical Reasoning Step:** To find the total number of emails Jack received in the morning and afternoon, we must sum the quantities given for these two periods.
   
4. **Conclusion:** 
   - Total emails = Emails in the morning + Emails in the afternoon
   
Therefore, the total number of emails Jack received in the morning and afternoon is:
\[ \text{Total} = 5 + 8 = 13 \]

The answer is: 13
