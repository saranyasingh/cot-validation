Premise 1: Jesse needs a carpet of size 10 square feet.
Premise 2: Her room is 2 feet wide.
Premise 3: To find the area of a rectangle (which we assume her room is), we use the formula Area = Length × Width.
Premise 4: We know the width and need to find the length.
Premise 5: Substitute the given values into the formula: 10 = Length × 2.
Premise 6: Solve for the length by dividing both sides by 2.
Premise 7: Therefore, the length of her room is 5 feet.

The answer is: 5 feet.
