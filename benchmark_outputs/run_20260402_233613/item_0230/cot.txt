**Logical Syllogism**

1. **Premise 1**: Paco initially had 97 salty cookies.
   - Premise Explanation: This establishes the total number of salty cookies Paco started with.

2. **Premise 2**: Paco also had 34 sweet cookies at the beginning.
   - Premise Explanation: This gives us the initial count of sweet cookies Paco possessed.

3. **Premise 3**: Paco ate 15 sweet cookies.
   - Premise Explanation: This tells us how many sweet cookies were removed from his collection.

4. **Premise 4**: Paco ate 56 salty cookies.
   - Premise Explanation: This indicates how many salty cookies he consumed.

5. **Conclusion**: We need to determine how many sweet cookies Paco has left after eating some.
   
   - Step-by-Step Reasoning:
     - Initially, Paco had 34 sweet cookies.
     - After eating 15 sweet cookies, we subtract these from the initial amount:
       \[
       34 - 15 = 19
       \]
     - Therefore, Paco now has 19 sweet cookies remaining.

**Final Answer:** The number of sweet cookies Paco has left is \( 19 \).
