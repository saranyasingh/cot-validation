To determine how many pieces of junk mail the mailman should give in each block, we need to break down the problem into smaller steps and apply logical reasoning.

**Premise 1:** The mailman needs to give 4 pieces of junk mail to each house in each block.
- This is our starting point, which tells us that regardless of the number of houses or blocks, each house within each block receives exactly 4 pieces of junk mail.

**Premise 2:** There are 12 houses in each block.
- This gives us the number of houses per block, but it doesn't directly affect the calculation of the total amount of junk mail needed because it's already factored into the given number of pieces per house.

**Premise 3:** The mailman has to distribute these pieces among all the blocks.
- Since we know there are 81 blocks, we must calculate how many pieces of junk mail go to each block based on the total number of pieces distributed (which is \(4 \times 12\)) divided by the number of blocks.

**Logical Syllogism:**
1. **Given:** Each house gets 4 pieces of junk mail.
2. **Each block has 12 houses.**
3. Therefore, each block gets \(4 \times 12\) pieces of junk mail.
4. To find out how many pieces of junk mail are distributed in total, multiply this by the number of blocks:
   - Total pieces of junk mail = \(4 \times 12 \times 81\).

Now let's perform the multiplication:

\[ 4 \times 12 = 48 \]
\[ 48 \times 81 = 3888 \]

So, the mailman should give 3888 pieces of junk mail in each block.

**Answer:** The answer is: 3888
