To determine how many cakes the baker still has after his friend purchased some, we need to subtract the number of cakes sold from the initial amount.

Initial cakes = 169
Cakes sold = 137

Cakes remaining = Initial cakes - Cakes sold
Cakes remaining = 169 - 137
Cakes remaining = 32

Therefore, the baker still has 32 cakes.

The answer is: 32
