Let's break down this problem into a logical syllogism:

**Premise 1:** Robin originally had 17 inches of hair.
- This is our starting point and initial information about Robin's original hair length.

**Premise 2:** After cutting some inches, Robin has 13 inches left.
- We know that after cutting, Robin's hair length is reduced to 13 inches.

**Conclusion:** Therefore, the amount of hair Robin cut must be:
\[ \text{Cut hair} = \text{Original hair length} - \text{Hair remaining} \]
\[ \text{Cut hair} = 17\, \text{inches} - 13\, \text{inches} \]
\[ \text{Cut hair} = 4\, \text{inches} \]

The answer is: **4 inches**
