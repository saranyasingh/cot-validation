Let's break down the problem using a logical syllogism:

Premise 1:
Paul started with a total of 531 crayons and 38 erasers.
Premise 2:
At the end of the school year, Paul had 391 crayons remaining.
Premise 3:
Paul did not lose any erasers during this period.

Step-by-step reasoning:

1. **Initial Total** (Premise 1):
   - Crayons: 531
   - Erasers: 38

2. **Remaining Crayons** (Premise 2):
   - After some time, Paul has 391 crayons left.

3. **Calculating Remaining Erasers**:
   - Since no erasers were lost, all 38 erasers must be still available at the end of the school year.
   
4. **Comparing Remaining Crayons and Erasers**:
   - Number of crayons left: 391
   - Number of erasers available: 38
   
5. **Difference Calculation**:
   - To find out how many more crayons than erasers Paul has left,
     we subtract the number of erasers from the number of crayons:
     \( 391 - 38 = 353 \)

Therefore, Paul has 353 more crayons than erasers left.

The answer is: 353
