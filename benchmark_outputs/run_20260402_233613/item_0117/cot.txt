Premise 1: Julia played tag with 5 kids on Tuesday.
Premise 2: Julia played tag with 6 kids on Monday.
Premise 3: To find out how many more kids Julia played with on Monday than on Tuesday, we need to subtract the number of kids she played with on Tuesday from the number of kids she played with on Monday.

Logical Reasoning:
- Let's denote the number of kids Julia played with on Tuesday as T and on Monday as M.
- From Premise 1, we know that T = 5.
- From Premise 2, we know that M = 6.
- We want to find M - T.

Calculation:
M - T = 6 - 5

The answer is: The difference is 1 kid. Therefore, the answer is: 1.
