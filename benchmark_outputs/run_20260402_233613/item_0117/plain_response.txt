To determine how many more kids Julia played with on Monday than on Tuesday, we need to follow these steps:

1. Identify the number of kids Julia played with on each day.
   - On Monday, she played with 6 kids.
   - On Tuesday, she played with 5 kids.

2. Subtract the number of kids from Tuesday from the number of kids from Monday.
   \[
   6 - 5 = 1
   \]

Therefore, Julia played with 1 more kid on Monday than on Tuesday.
