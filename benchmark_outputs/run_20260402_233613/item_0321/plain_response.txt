To determine how many cookies Paco ate, we can use simple subtraction. 

1. Paco started with 28 cookies.
2. After eating some cookies, he was left with 7 cookies.

We need to find out how many cookies were eaten:

\[ \text{Cookies eaten} = \text{Initial number of cookies} - \text{Number of cookies left} \]

Substitute the given values into the equation:

\[ \text{Cookies eaten} = 28 - 7 \]

Perform the subtraction:

\[ 28 - 7 = 21 \]

So, Paco ate 21 cookies.

The answer is: 21
