1. **Premise 1**: Paco initially had 28 cookies.
   - This establishes the starting point of our problem.

2. **Premise 2**: After eating some cookies, Paco has 7 cookies remaining.
   - This tells us what Paco's state was after he ate something.

3. **Premise 3**: We need to find out how many cookies Paco ate.
   - This is the goal we want to determine.

4. **Logical Reasoning**:
   - Let \( x \) represent the number of cookies Paco ate.
   - According to Premise 2, after eating \( x \) cookies, Paco has \( 28 - x = 7 \) cookies left.

5. **Solving for \( x \)**:
   - Rearrange the equation \( 28 - x = 7 \):
     \[
     28 - x = 7 \\
     28 - 7 = x \\
     21 = x
     \]

6. **Conclusion**:
   - Therefore, Paco ate 21 cookies.

The answer is: The answer is: 21
