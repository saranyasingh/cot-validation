To determine how many cards Nell gave to Jeff, we can follow these steps:

1. Calculate the number of cards Nell gave away initially:
   - Nell originally had 528 cards.
   - After giving some to Jeff, she has 252 cards left.
   - Therefore, the number of cards given away = original number of cards - remaining number of cards = 528 - 252 = 276 cards.

2. Verify the information about Jeff's initial number of cards:
   - Jeff originally had 11 cards.
   - The total number of cards after receiving from Nell should be equal to Jeff's initial number plus the number of cards he received from Nell.
   - Let \( x \) be the number of cards Jeff received from Nell.
   - Then, \( 11 + x = 276 \).

3. Solve for \( x \):
   - Rearrange the equation to solve for \( x \): \( x = 276 - 11 \).
   - Simplify: \( x = 265 \).

Therefore, Nell gave Jeff 265 cards. The answer is: 265.
