To solve this problem logically:

Premise 1: Nell initially had 528 cards.
Premise 2: Jeff had 11 cards initially.
Premise 3: After giving away some cards, Nell ends up with 252 cards.

We need to find out how many cards Nell gave to Jeff.

Let's denote:
- \( x \) as the number of cards Nell gave to Jeff.
- \( y \) as the number of cards Jeff received after receiving from Nell.

From Premise 3, we know that after giving away \( x \) cards, Nell ends up with 252 cards. This means before she gave away any cards, she must have had more than 252 cards (since she ended up with fewer).

Therefore, we can set up the following equation based on the initial total number of cards:

\[ 528 - x = 252 \]

Now, let's solve for \( x \):

1. Subtract 252 from both sides of the equation to isolate \( x \):
   \[ 528 - 252 = x \]
   \[ 276 = x \]

So, Nell gave Jeff 276 cards.

The answer is: Nell gave 276 cards to Jeff.
