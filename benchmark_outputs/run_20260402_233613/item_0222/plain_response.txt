To find out how many more bottle caps Danny threw away than those he found at the park, we need to follow these steps:

1. Determine how many bottle caps Danny had before throwing any away.
2. Subtract the number of bottle caps he threw away from the total number he had initially.
3. Compare this result with the number of bottle caps he found.

Step 2: After throwing away 54 bottle caps and finding 48 new ones, he now has 52 bottle caps. This can be expressed as:
\[ I - 54 + 48 = 52 \]

Simplifying the equation:
\[ I - 6 = 52 \]
\[ I = 52 + 6 \]
\[ I = 58 \]

\[ 54 - 48 = 6 \]
