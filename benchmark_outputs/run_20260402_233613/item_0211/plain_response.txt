To determine how many flower beds Paige and her mom had, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Total Seeds Planted**: Paige and her mom planted 36 seeds.
2. **Seeds per Flower Bed**: Each flower bed received 12 seeds.

First, calculate the number of flower beds by dividing the total number of seeds by the number of seeds per flower bed:
\[
\text{Number of flower beds} = \frac{\text{Total seeds}}{\text{Seeds per flower bed}} = \frac{36}{12}
\]

Perform the division:
\[
\frac{36}{12} = 3
\]

Therefore, they had 3 flower beds.

**The answer is: 3**
