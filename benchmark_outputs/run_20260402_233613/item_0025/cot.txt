Premise 1: During the summer break, 610769 kids from Lawrence County went to camp.
Premise 2: The remaining number of kids who stayed at home was 590796.
Premise 3: In addition, there were 22 kids from outside the county who also attended the camp.

To find out about the total population of Lawrence County, we need to add up all these groups:

First, let's determine how many kids are in Lawrence County:
- Number of kids from Lawrence County who went to camp = 610769
- Number of kids from Lawrence County who stayed home = 590796

Total number of kids in Lawrence County (who either went to camp or stayed home) = 610769 + 590796 = 1201565

Now, we know that 22 more kids from outside the county joined them as well. So, we add this group to our count:

Total population of Lawrence County = 1201565 (Lawrence County residents) + 22 (from outside)

The total population of Lawrence County ≈ 1201587

Therefore, the answer is: The population of Lawrence County is approximately 1201587 people.
