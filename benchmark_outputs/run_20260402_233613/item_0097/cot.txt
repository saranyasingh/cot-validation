**Logical Syllogism**

1. **Premise 1:** Marco and his dad together have strawberries that weigh 35 pounds.
   - This establishes the total weight of the strawberries between them.

2. **Premise 2:** Marco’s strawberries weigh 13 pounds.
   - This gives us Marco’s individual contribution to the total weight.

3. **Conclusion:** To find out how much more Marco's dad's strawberries weigh compared to Marco's, we need to subtract Marco's strawberries' weight from the total weight.
   
4. **Calculation:** Total weight = Marco's strawberries + Dad's strawberries
   - 35 pounds = 13 pounds + Dad's strawberries

5. **Subtraction:** 
   - Dad's strawberries = 35 pounds - 13 pounds
   - Dad's strawberries = 22 pounds

6. **Final Calculation:** Now, to find how much more Marco's strawberries weigh than his dad's:
   - Difference = Marco's strawberries - Dad's strawberries
   - Difference = 13 pounds - 22 pounds
   - Difference = -9 pounds

7. **Interpretation:** Since the difference is negative, it means Marco's strawberries actually weigh less than his dad's. 

**The answer is: 9 pounds**

This conclusion comes directly from the subtraction of Marco's strawberries' weight (13 pounds) from the total weight (35 pounds), which equals 9 pounds. Thus, Marco's strawberries weigh 9 pounds less than his dad's.
