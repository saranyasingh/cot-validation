To determine how many invitations are in each pack, we need to follow these steps:

1. Identify the total number of invitations Carol needs to send out.
   - Carol has 12 friends and she wants to send out birthday invitations to all of them.

2. Determine how many packs of invitations she bought.
   - Carol bought 3 packs of invitations.

3. Calculate the number of invitations per pack.
   - Since each pack contains the same number of invitations, we divide the total number of invitations by the number of packs.

Let's perform the division:
\[
\text{Number of invitations per pack} = \frac{\text{Total number of invitations}}{\text{Number of packs}}
\]
\[
\text{Number of invitations per pack} = \frac{12}{3}
\]

4. Simplify the fraction:
\[
\frac{12}{3} = 4
\]

Therefore, each pack contains 4 invitations.

The answer is: 4
