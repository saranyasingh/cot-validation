To determine how much money Jason earned by delivering newspapers, we need to follow these steps:

1. Identify the initial amount of money Jason had.
2. Identify the current amount of money Jason has after delivering newspapers.
3. Subtract the initial amount from the current amount to find out how much he earned.

Step 1: The initial amount of money Jason had is $31.

Step 2: The current amount of money Jason has after delivering newspapers is $57.

Step 3: Calculate the earnings:
\[ \text{Earnings} = \text{Current Amount} - \text{Initial Amount} \]
\[ \text{Earnings} = \$57 - \$31 \]
\[ \text{Earnings} = \$26 \]

Therefore, Jason earned $26 by delivering newspapers.
