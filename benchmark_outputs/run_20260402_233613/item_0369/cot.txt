**Logical Syllogism**

1. **Premise 1:** Jason delivered newspapers.
2. **Premise 2:** Now Jason has $57.
3. **Premise 3:** Before delivering newspapers, Jason had $31.
4. **Premise 4:** To find out how much Jason earned, we need to subtract his original amount from his current amount.
   
   - Current amount (after delivery): $57
   - Original amount before delivery: $31
   
   **Calculation:**
   \[
   \text{Earnings} = \text{Current Amount} - \text{Original Amount}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Earnings} = 57 - 31 = 26
   \]

Therefore, the answer is: Jason earned $26 by delivering newspapers.
