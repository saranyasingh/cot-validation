Let's break down this problem into a logical syllogism:

**Premise 1:** Dave initially had 17 apps and 21 files on his phone.
- This establishes the initial state before any deletions occur.

**Premise 2:** After deleting some apps and files, Dave has 3 apps and 7 files remaining.
- This shows us what the situation looks like after the deletions have been made.

**Premise 3:** To find out how many files were deleted, we need to subtract the number of files left from the initial number of files.
- This directly leads to our calculation needed to determine the amount of deletion.

**Conclusion:** Let \( F \) be the number of files that Dave deleted.
- Therefore, \( F = (Initial\ Files - Remaining\ Files) \)
- Substituting the known values:
  \( F = (21 - 7) \)
- Calculating the difference:
  \( F = 14 \)

**The answer is: 14**

So, Dave deleted 14 files from his phone.
