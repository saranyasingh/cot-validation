To determine how many files Dave deleted, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Identify Initial Total Items**: 
   - Apps initially: 17
   - Files initially: 21

2. **Identify Remaining Items**:
   - Apps remaining: 3
   - Files remaining: 7

3. **Calculate Deleted Items for Apps**:
   - Initially total apps: 17
   - Remaining apps: 3
   - Deleted apps = Initial apps - Remaining apps
   \[
   \text{Deleted apps} = 17 - 3 = 14
   \]

4. **Calculate Deleted Items for Files**:
   - Initially total files: 21
   - Remaining files: 7
   - Deleted files = Initial files - Remaining files
   \[
   \text{Deleted files} = 21 - 7 = 14
   \]

5. **Verify the Calculation**:
   - We have calculated that Dave deleted 14 apps and 14 files.
   - Adding back these values should give us the original counts:
     \[
     \text{Original apps} = \text{Deleted apps} + \text{Remaining apps} = 14 + 3 = 17
     \]
     \[
     \text{Original files} = \text{Deleted files} + \text{Remaining files} = 14 + 7 = 21
     \]

Since both calculations match the initial counts, our solution is correct.
