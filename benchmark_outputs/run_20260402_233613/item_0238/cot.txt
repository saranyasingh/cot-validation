**Logical Syllogism**

1. **Premise 1:** Edward spent $16 on 92 books.
   - This establishes the total expenditure and quantity of items purchased.

2. **Premise 2:** Each book cost the same amount of money.
   - This implies that we need to find out how much one book costs.

3. **Conclusion derived from Premises 1 and 2:**
   - To determine the price per book, divide the total expenditure by the number of books:
     \[
     \text{Price per book} = \frac{\text{Total Expenditure}}{\text{Number of Books}} = \frac{16}{92}
     \]

4. **Simplifying the fraction (if necessary):**
   - Simplify $\frac{16}{92}$ by dividing both numerator and denominator by their greatest common divisor, which is 8:
     \[
     \frac{16 \div 8}{92 \div 8} = \frac{2}{11.5}
     \]
   - Further simplification gives us approximately $\frac{2}{11.5} \approx 0.1765$ dollars per book.

5. **Given information about remaining money:**
   - Edward now has $6 after spending on books.

6. **Calculating initial amount of money:**
   - Add the remaining money to the total expenditure to find the original amount:
     \[
     \text{Initial Amount} = \text{Total Expenditure} + \text{Remaining Money} = 16 + 6 = 22
     \]

**The answer is: 22**

This conclusion follows directly from breaking down the problem into manageable steps using basic arithmetic operations based on the given premises.
