## FACTS
FACT-1: Edward spent $16 on 92 books :: Spent(Edward, 16, 92)

## RULES
RULE-1: Total Expenditure = Number of Items × Price per Item :: ∀n p (Spent(n, p, m) → n * p = m)

## INFERENCES
INF-1: From Fact-1, Rule-1 by Modus Ponens: The price per book is calculated as follows: 
\[
\text{Price per book} = \frac{16}{92}
\]

INF-2: Simplifying the fraction,
\[
\frac{16}{92} = \frac{2}{11.5} \approx 0.1765 \text{ dollars per book}
\]

INF-3: Initial Amount = Total Expenditure + Remaining Money :: InitialAmount = TotalExpenditure + RemainingMoney

INF-4: Given Information,
\[
\text{Remaining Money} = 6
\]

INF-5: Therefore,
\[
\text{Initial Amount} = 16 + 6 = 22
