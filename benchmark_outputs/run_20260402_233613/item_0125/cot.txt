Premise 1: Julia played tag with 2 kids on Monday.
Premise 2: Julia played tag with 14 kids on Tuesday.
Premise 3: Julia played tag with 16 kids on Wednesday.

The conclusion we aim to reach is:
Conclusion: Julia played with 14 + 16 = 30 kids on Tuesday and Wednesday combined.

Logical Syllogism:

1. If it's true that Julia played with 2 kids on Monday (Premise 1), then she must have played with at least 2 kids.
   - This establishes a minimum number of children involved on Monday.

2. Given that Julia played with 14 kids on Tuesday (Premise 2), this directly provides information about her interaction on that day.

3. Similarly, if it’s correct that Julia played with 16 kids on Wednesday (Premise 3), this also offers specific data regarding her interactions on that particular day.

4. To find out how many kids Julia interacted with over both days (Tuesday and Wednesday), we add up all the instances given.

5. Therefore, adding the number of kids from Monday (2) to the number of kids from Tuesday (14) gives us:
   - Total kids on Tuesday and Wednesday = Kids on Monday + Kids on Tuesday
   - Total kids on Tuesday and Wednesday = 2 + 14

6. Calculating this sum:
   - Total kids on Tuesday and Wednesday = 2 + 14 = 16

Thus, the total number of kids Julia played with on Tuesday and Wednesday is 16.

The answer is: 16
