**Logical Syllogism**

1. **Premise 1:** Baker made 52 pastries.
   - This establishes the quantity of pastries baked.

2. **Premise 2:** Baker made 84 cakes.
   - This establishes the quantity of cakes baked.

3. **Premise 3:** Baker sold 102 cakes.
   - This introduces the fact that some cakes were sold, reducing the total number available to sell or consume.

4. **Premise 4:** Baker sold 94 pastries.
   - This introduces the fact that some pastries were sold, reducing the total number available to eat or trade.

5. **Conclusion:** To find how many more cakes than pastries the baker made,
   - We need to compare the initial quantities (before any sales) with the quantities after sales.

6. **Step-by-Step Calculation:**
   
   - Initial number of cakes = 84
     \[
     \text{Cakes remaining} = 84 - 102 + 102 = 84
     \]
     (Note: Since we cannot have negative cakes, it simplifies back to 84.)

   - Initial number of pastries = 52
     \[
     \text{Pastries remaining} = 52 - 94 + 94 = 52
     \]

7. **Final Comparison:**
   - Number of cakes remaining = 84
   - Number of pastries remaining = 52

8. **Answer:** 
   - More cakes than pastries:
     \[
     84 - 52 = 32
     \]

Therefore, the baker made 32 more cakes than pastries. The answer is: 32.
