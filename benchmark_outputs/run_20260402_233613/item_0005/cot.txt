To determine how much money Olivia has left after spending some at the supermarket, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Initial amount**: Olivia starts with $78.
2. **Amount spent**: Olivia spends $15 at the supermarket.
3. **Subtraction**: To find out how much money Olivia has left, we subtract the amount spent from her initial amount.

We can express this as a simple subtraction problem:
\[ \text{Remaining amount} = \text{Initial amount} - \text{Amount spent} \]

Plugging in the numbers:
\[ \text{Remaining amount} = 78 - 15 \]

Now, let's perform the subtraction:
\[ 78 - 15 = 63 \]

Therefore, Olivia has $63 left.

**Conclusion**: Olivia has $63 left in her wallet after spending $15 at the supermarket. 

The answer is: Olivia has $63 left.
