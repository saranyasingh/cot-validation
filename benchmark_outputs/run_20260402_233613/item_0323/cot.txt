1. **Premise**: A book has 2 chapters.
   - This establishes the number of chapters and sets up our problem within the framework of having two parts to solve.

2. **Premise**: The first chapter is 60 pages long.
   - We know the length of one part (chapter) of the book.

3. **Premise**: There are a total of 93 pages in the book.
   - This provides us with the sum of all the pages across both chapters, giving us a way to calculate the other chapter's page count.

4. **Logical Deduction**:
   - Let \( P \) represent the number of pages in the first chapter.
   - According to Premise 2, \( P = 60 \).
   
5. **Logical Calculation**:
   - Total number of pages in the book (\( T \)) is given as 93.
   - Since we know \( P \), we can find the number of pages in the second chapter (\( S \)) by subtracting the pages in the first chapter from the total number of pages:
     \[
     S = T - P
     \]
   - Substituting the known values:
     \[
     S = 93 - 60
     \]

6. **Final Calculation**:
   - Performing the subtraction gives:
     \[
     S = 33
     \]

7. **Conclusion**:
   - Therefore, the second chapter has 33 pages.

The answer is: 33
