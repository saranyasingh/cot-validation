To find out how many slices of pizza there are altogether, we need to multiply the number of pizzas by the number of slices per pizza and then divide it by the number of people eating them. 

Here's the calculation:

Number of slices = Number of pizzas × Slices per pizza ÷ Number of people

Plugging in our values:

Number of slices = 17 × 4 ÷ 25

Now let's do the math:

Number of slices = 68 ÷ 25

Finally, we get:

Number of slices = 2.72

Since you can't have a fraction of a slice, we'll round down to the nearest whole number because even though we're left with a little bit over two full pizzas worth of slices for each person, we still only count the complete slices they ate.

Therefore, the answer is: 2 slices per person.
