Premise 1: We ordered 17 pizzas.
Premise 2: Each pizza has 4 slices.
Premise 3: There are 25 people (us).

Logical Reasoning:
Step 1: From Premise 1 and Premise 2, we know that the total number of slices of pizza is calculated as follows:

Total Slices = Number of Pizzas * Slices per Pizza

Step 2: Substituting the known values into Step 1:

Total Slices = 17 * 4

Step 3: Performing the multiplication to find the total number of slices:

Total Slices = 68

Therefore, the conclusion is:

The answer is: 68 slices of pizza.
