To determine how many points Luke scored per round, we can follow these steps:

1. **Identify the total points scored**: Luke scored 84 points in total.
2. **Determine the number of rounds played**: Luke played 2 rounds.
3. **Calculate the points per round**: Divide the total points by the number of rounds to find out how many points he scored per round.

Let's perform the calculation:
\[ \text{Points per round} = \frac{\text{Total points}}{\text{Number of rounds}} \]
\[ \text{Points per round} = \frac{84}{2} \]

Now, let's do the division:
\[ \text{Points per round} = 42 \]

Therefore, the answer is: 42.
