1. **Premise**: Luke scored 84 points after playing 2 rounds of a trivia game.
   - This establishes that there was an aggregate total of 84 points across two rounds.

2. **Premise**: He gained the same number of points each round.
   - This indicates uniform performance throughout both rounds.

3. **Conclusion**: Therefore, to find out how many points he scored per round, we divide the total points (84) by the number of rounds (2).
   
   \[
   \text{Points per round} = \frac{\text{Total Points}}{\text{Number of Rounds}} = \frac{84}{2}
   \]

4. **Calculation**:
   \[
   \frac{84}{2} = 42
   \]

5. **Final Answer**: Luke scored 42 points per round.

Therefore, the answer is: 42
