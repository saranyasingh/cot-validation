To determine how many people can ride the Ferris wheel on small seats, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Identify the number of small seats:** 
   - There are 2 small seats available.

2. **Determine the capacity of each small seat:**
   - Each small seat can hold 14 people.

3. **Calculate the total capacity for all small seats:**
   \[
   \text{Total capacity} = \text{Number of small seats} \times \text{Capacity per small seat}
   \]
   Substituting the given values:
   \[
   \text{Total capacity} = 2 \times 14 = 28
   \]

Thus, the total number of people that can ride the Ferris wheel on small seats is **28**.
