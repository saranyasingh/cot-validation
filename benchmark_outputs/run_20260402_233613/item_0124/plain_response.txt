To determine how many people can ride the Ferris wheel on small seats, we need to calculate the total capacity of the small seats.

First, identify the number of small seats:
\[ \text{Number of small seats} = 2 \]

Next, find out how many people each small seat can hold:
\[ \text{Capacity per small seat} = 14 \]

Now, multiply the number of small seats by the capacity of each small seat to get the total capacity for all small seats:
\[ \text{Total capacity for small seats} = 2 \times 14 = 28 \]

Therefore, the total number of people that can ride the Ferris wheel on small seats is \( 28 \).

The answer is: 28
