Let's break down this problem using a logical syllogism:

Premise 1:
- Jack received 10 emails in the morning.
- Jack received 3 emails in the afternoon.

Premise 2:
- We need to find out how many more emails Jack received in the morning compared to the afternoon.

Step-by-step reasoning:
1. **Identify the total number of emails received in the morning:** 
   - Jack received 10 emails in the morning.
   
2. **Identify the total number of emails received in the afternoon:**
   - Jack received 3 emails in the afternoon.
   
3. **Calculate the difference between the number of emails received in the morning and the afternoon:**
   - Difference = Total emails in the morning - Total emails in the afternoon
   - Difference = 10 emails - 3 emails
   - Difference = 7 emails

Therefore, the answer is: 7. Jack received 7 more emails in the morning than in the afternoon.
