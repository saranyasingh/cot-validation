**Premise 1:** There were 71 paintings in the Buckingham Palace.
- Premise 2:** There were 557 visitors to the Buckingham Palace on the current day.
- Premise 3:** There were 188 visitors to the Buckingham Palace on the previous day.
  
**Conclusion:** Therefore, if we assume the pattern of visitor numbers persists over time and assuming all days fall between the two given days (current and previous), then:
- We need to calculate the number of visitors expected on the remaining days until 57 days have passed.
- The total number of days to be considered would be \(57 - 2 = 55\) days since we already know about the current day and the previous day's visitation.
- Since the number of visitors increases linearly from the previous day to the current day, the average daily increase in visitors should reflect this trend.

To find out how many visitors visited the Buckingham Palace within these 55 days:

**Step-by-step calculation:**

1. **Calculate the average daily increase in visitors:**
   \[
   \text{Average Daily Increase} = \frac{\text{Current Day Visitors} - \text{Previous Day Visitors}}{56}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Average Daily Increase} = \frac{557 - 188}{56} = \frac{369}{56} \approx 6.54
   \]

2. **Assume the number of visitors remains consistent across these 55 days:**
   This means that the number of visitors on any given day would be approximately 6.54 more than the previous day.

3. **Sum up the cumulative effect of this increase over 55 days:**
   \[
   \text{Total Visitors Over 55 Days} = (\text{Previous Day Visitors} + \text{Average Daily Increase}) \times 55
   \]
   \[
   \text{Total Visitors Over 55 Days} = (188 + 6.54) \times 55
   \]
   \[
   \text{Total Visitors Over 55 Days} = 194.54 \times 55 \approx 10,736.7
   \]

Since the number of visitors must be an integer, we round it down to the nearest whole number.

Therefore, the number of visitors who visited the Buckingham Palace within the next 57 days is approximately 10,736.
