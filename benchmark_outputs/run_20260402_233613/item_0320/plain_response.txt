To determine how many more cups of sugar than cups of flour Mary needs to add now, we can follow these steps:

1. Identify the total amount of each ingredient required by the recipe.
   - Sugar: 5 cups
   - Flour: 14 cups

2. Determine how much flour Mary has already added.
   - Flour added so far: 11 cups

3. Calculate how much more flour Mary still needs to add:
   \[
   \text{Flour needed} = \text{Total flour required} - \text{Flour already added}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Flour needed} = 14 \text{ cups} - 11 \text{ cups} = 3 \text{ cups}
   \]

4. Compare the remaining amount of sugar needed with the amount of flour still to be added:
   - Sugar needed: 5 cups
   - Flour still needed: 3 cups

5. Find the difference between the amounts:
   \[
   \text{Difference} = \text{Sugar needed} - \text{Flour still needed}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Difference} = 5 \text{ cups} - 3 \text{ cups} = 2 \text{ cups}
   \]
