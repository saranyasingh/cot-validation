**Logical Syllogism**

1. **Premise 1:** Mary needs to add sugar to her cake.
   - Premise 2 (from the recipe): The recipe requires 5 cups of sugar.
   - Conclusion (derived from both premises): To bake the cake, Mary must add at least 5 cups of sugar.

2. **Premise 3:** Mary has already added 11 cups of flour to her cake.
   - Premise 4 (from the recipe): The recipe requires 14 cups of flour.
   - Conclusion (derived from both premises): To bake the cake, Mary must add at least 14 cups of flour.

3. **Conclusion:** Now, we need to determine how many more cups of sugar than cups of flour Mary needs to add.
   - From Premise 1 and Premise 2, we know:
     - Mary needs at least 5 cups of sugar.
     - Mary has already added 11 cups of flour.
   - Therefore, to compare the amount of sugar needed versus the amount of flour already added:
     - Sugar needed = 5 cups
     - Flour already added = 11 cups

4. **Calculation:** Subtract the amount of flour from the amount of sugar:
   \[
   \text{Sugar needed} - \text{Flour already added} = 5 - 11 = -6
   \]
   - Since this result is negative, it indicates that Mary actually needs less sugar than flour. Specifically, she needs to subtract 6 cups of sugar from what she has already added.

**The answer is:**
- The answer is: 6 cups of sugar less than cups of flour.
