Premise 1: The farmer initially had 105 green tomatoes and 71 red tomatoes.
Premise 2: He then picked 137 tomatoes.
Premise 3: We need to determine how many tomatoes are left after picking 137 tomatoes.

To find out how many tomatoes are left, we can use subtraction as it's straightforward:

Step 1: Start with the total number of tomatoes before picking any.
Total initial tomatoes = Green tomatoes + Red tomatoes
= 105 + 71
= 176

Step 2: Subtract the number of tomatoes picked from the total initial number of tomatoes.
Remaining tomatoes = Total initial tomatoes - Picked tomatoes
= 176 - 137
= 39

Therefore, the answer is: There are 39 tomatoes left in the garden after picking 137 tomatoes.
