To find out how many tomatoes are left after picking some, we need to subtract the number of tomatoes picked from the total number of tomatoes initially present. 

Here's the calculation:

Total initial tomatoes = Green tomatoes + Red tomatoes
= 105 + 71
= 176

Tomatoes picked = 137

Remaining tomatoes = Total initial tomatoes - Tomatoes picked
= 176 - 137
= 39

So, the answer is: 39
