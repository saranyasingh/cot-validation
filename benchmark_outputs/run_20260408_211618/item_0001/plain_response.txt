To determine how much Janet makes every day at the farmers' market, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the total number of eggs laid each day.
2. Subtract the number of eggs eaten for breakfast.
3. Subtract the number of eggs used to bake muffins for friends.
4. Determine the remaining number of eggs that can be sold at the market.
5. Calculate the revenue from selling these eggs.

Let's go through each step in detail:

1. Total eggs laid per day = 16

2. Eggs eaten for breakfast:
   - Breakfast = 3 eggs/day
   - Remaining after breakfast = 16 - 3 = 13 eggs

3. Eggs used to bake muffins:
   - Muffin baking = 4 eggs/day
   - Remaining after muffin baking = 13 - 4 = 9 eggs

4. Eggs available for sale at the market:
   - Market sales = 9 eggs/day

5. Revenue from selling eggs:
   - Selling price per egg = $2
   - Daily revenue = 9 eggs * $2/egg = $18

Therefore, the amount Janet makes every day at the farmers' market is $18.

The answer is: 18
