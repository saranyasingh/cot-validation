EXPLANATION
To determine how much Janet makes every day at the farmers' market, we need to calculate the total number of eggs laid per day, subtract the number eaten for breakfast and baked into muffins, and then multiply the remaining number of eggs by the price per egg.

Step 1: Calculate the total number of eggs laid per day.
Janet's ducks lay 16 eggs per day.

Step 2: Subtract the number of eggs eaten for breakfast.
Janet eats 3 eggs for breakfast every morning.
Total eggs eaten for breakfast = 3 eggs.

Step 3: Subtract the number of eggs used to bake muffins.
Janet uses 4 eggs to bake muffins every day.
Total eggs used for baking = 4 eggs.

Step 4: Calculate the number of eggs left after accounting for breakfast and muffin usage.
Remaining eggs = Total eggs laid - Eggs eaten for breakfast - Eggs used for baking
Remaining eggs = 16 eggs - 3 eggs - 4 eggs
Remaining eggs = 9 eggs.

Step 5: Calculate the amount made from selling the remaining eggs.
Each egg is sold for $2.
Amount made = Remaining eggs × Price per egg
Amount made = 9 eggs × $2/egg
Amount made = $18.

Therefore, Janet makes $18 every day at the farmers' market.
