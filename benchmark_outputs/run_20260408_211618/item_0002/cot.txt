Explanation:

1. Let's start by defining what we know about the robes.
   Premise 1: A robe requires 2 bolts of blue fiber.

2. Next, let's determine how much white fiber is needed for the same robe.
   Premise 2: For every 2 bolts of blue fiber, there needs to be half that amount of white fiber.
   Therefore, if we have 2 bolts of blue fiber, then we need \( \frac{2}{2} = 1 \) bolt of white fiber.

3. Now, we add up the total number of bolts required for both colors.
   Total bolts = Blue fiber + White fiber
                  = 2 (blue) + 1 (white)
                  = 3 bolts

4. Thus, the total number of bolts needed to make one robe is 3.
