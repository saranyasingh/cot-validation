EXPLANATION
To determine how much shorter a candle will be after burning from 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM, we need to calculate the total time the candle has been burning during this period.

First, let's identify the start and end times:
- Start time: 1:00 PM
- End time: 5:00 PM

Next, we calculate the duration of the burn:
- Duration = End time - Start time = 5:00 PM - 1:00 PM = 4 hours

Now, we know that the candle melts at a rate of 2 centimeters per hour. Therefore, over 4 hours, the total melting length will be:
- Total melting length = Melting rate × Time = 2 cm/hour × 4 hours = 8 cm

Thus, after burning from 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM, the candle will have melted down by 8 centimeters.

The answer is: 8
