Explanation:

1. First, we calculate the cost of one glass.
   - One glass costs $5.

2. Next, we determine how many glasses cost at 60% of their original price.
   - Since it's every second glass, there are 8 glasses that cost 60% (half) of their original price.

3. We find out the price of these glasses.
   - At 60%, the price of one glass is \(5 \times 0.6 = \$3\).

4. Now, we calculate the total cost for these 8 glasses.
   - Total cost for 8 glasses at $3 each is \(8 \times 3 = \$24\).

5. Finally, we add the cost of the other 8 glasses that cost the full price ($5 each).
   - Total cost for the remaining 8 glasses is \(8 \times 5 = \$40\).

6. To get the total amount Kylar needs to pay, we sum up both amounts.
   - Total cost is \(24 + 40 = \$64\).

Therefore, the answer is: 64.
