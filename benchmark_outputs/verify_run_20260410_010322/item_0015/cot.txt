To determine what percentage of the entire students enrolled in hip-hop dance, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the number of students enrolled in contemporary dance**:
   - Total number of students = 20
   - Percentage enrolled in contemporary dance = 20%
   \[
   \text{Number of students in contemporary dance} = 20 \times \frac{20}{100} = 4
   \]

2. **Determine the number of students remaining after those enrolled in contemporary dance**:
   \[
   \text{Remaining students} = 20 - 4 = 16
   \]

3. **Calculate the number of students enrolled in jazz dance**:
   - Percentage enrolled in jazz dance = 25%
   \[
   \text{Number of students in jazz dance} = 16 \times \frac{25}{100} = 4
   \]

4. **Determine the number of students remaining after those enrolled in jazz dance**:
   \[
   \text{Remaining students} = 16 - 4 = 12
   \]

5. **Identify that all remaining students are enrolled in hip-hop dance**:
   - All remaining students after both contemporary and jazz dancers have been accounted for.
   \[
   \text{Number of students in hip-hop dance} = 12
   \]

6. **Calculate the percentage of the total students enrolled in hip-hop dance**:
   \[
   \text{Percentage of hip-hop dancers} = \left(\frac{\text{Number of students in hip-hop dance}}{\text{Total number of students}}\right) \times 100 = \left(\frac{12}{20}\right) \times 100 = 60\%
   \]

Therefore, the percentage of the entire students who enrolled in hip-hop dance is \( 60\% \).

The answer is: 60
