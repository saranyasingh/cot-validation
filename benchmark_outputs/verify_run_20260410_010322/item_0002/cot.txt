To determine the total number of bolts of fiber required to make a robe, we need to calculate the amount of each type of fiber separately and then sum them up.

1. **Calculate the amount of blue fiber needed**:
   - A robe requires 2 bolts of blue fiber.
   
2. **Calculate the amount of white fiber needed**:
   - A robe requires half as much white fiber as blue fiber.
   - Therefore, the amount of white fiber needed = \( \frac{1}{2} \times 2 \) bolts.
   - Simplifying this gives us: \( 1 \) bolt of white fiber.

3. **Sum the amounts of both types of fiber**:
   - Total bolts of blue fiber = 2 bolts.
   - Total bolts of white fiber = 1 bolt.
   - Total bolts of fiber = Blue + White = 2 + 1 = 3 bolts.

Thus, the total number of bolts of fiber required to make a robe is 3.

The answer is: 3
