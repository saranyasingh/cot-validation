EXPLANATION

To calculate Jill's annual salary, we need to determine her earnings from both teaching and coaching separately, then sum them up.

First, let's find out how much she earns from teaching. She teaches for 50 weeks a year, working 35 hours each week at a rate of $20 per hour.

\[
\text{Earnings from teaching} = \text{Number of weeks} \times \text{Hours per week} \times \text{Hourly wage}
\]
\[
= 50 \times 35 \times 20
\]

Calculating this gives us:

\[
= 1750 \times 20
\]
\[
= 35000
\]

So, Jill earns $35,000 from teaching over the course of a year.

Next, let's find out how much she earns from being a cheerleading coach. She coaches for 50 weeks a year, working 15 hours each week at a rate of $30 per hour.

\[
\text{Earnings from coaching} = \text{Number of weeks} \times \text{Hours per week} \times \text{Hourly wage}
\]
\[
= 50 \times 15 \times 30
\]

Calculating this gives us:

\[
= 750 \times 30
\]
\[
= 22500
\]

So, Jill earns $22,500 from coaching over the course of a year.

Finally, to find her total annual salary, we add the earnings from teaching and coaching together:

\[
\text{Total annual salary} = \text{Earnings from teaching} + \text{Earnings from coaching}
\]
\[
= 35000 + 22500
\]
\[
= 57500
\]

Therefore, Jill's annual salary is $57,500. The answer is: 57500
