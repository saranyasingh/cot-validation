Explanation:

1. Claire makes a 3-egg omelet every morning.
2. There are 7 days in a week.
3. Therefore, Claire eats \(3 \times 7 = 21\) eggs per week.
4. In 4 weeks, Claire would consume \(21 \times 4 = 84\) eggs.

Now we need to convert the total number of eggs into dozens. One dozen equals 12 items. Thus,

5. To find out how many dozens of eggs Claire consumes in 4 weeks, divide the total number of eggs by 12.

6. Therefore,
\[ \frac{84}{12} = 7 \]

So, the answer is: 7.
