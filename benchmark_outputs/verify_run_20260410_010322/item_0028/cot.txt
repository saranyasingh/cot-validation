EXPLANATION

To determine how much Cynthia will spend on ice cream after 60 days, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the total number of servings of ice cream Cynthia consumes in 60 days.
2. Determine how many cartons of ice cream she needs based on the total servings consumed.
3. Calculate the total cost based on the number of cartons needed.

Step 1: Calculate the total number of servings of ice cream Cynthia consumes in 60 days.
- Since she eats one serving of ice cream every night and there are 60 days,
\[ \text{Total servings} = 1 \times 60 = 60 \]

Step 2: Determine how many cartons of ice cream she needs.
- Each carton contains 15 servings.
\[ \text{Number of cartons} = \frac{\text{Total servings}}{\text{Servings per carton}} = \frac{60}{15} = 4 \]

Step 3: Calculate the total cost.
- Each carton costs $4.00.
\[ \text{Total cost} = \text{Number of cartons} \times \text{Cost per carton} = 4 \times 4.00 = 16.00 \]

Therefore, Cynthia will spend $16.00 on ice cream after 60 days.

The answer is: 16
