To determine the total cost of Toula's purchases at the bakery, we need to calculate the cost of each type of pastry separately and then sum these costs.

1. **Cost of Donuts**:  
   - Toula bought 3 dozen donuts.
   - Each dozen donuts costs $68.
   - Total cost for donuts = Number of dozens × Cost per dozen = 3 × $68 = $204.

2. **Cost of Mini Cupcakes**:
   - Toula bought 2 dozen mini cupcakes.
   - Each dozen mini cupcakes costs $80.
   - Total cost for mini cupcakes = Number of dozens × Cost per dozen = 2 × $80 = $160.

3. **Cost of Mini Cheesecakes**:
   - Toula bought 6 dozen mini cheesecakes.
   - Each dozen mini cheesecakes costs $55.
   - Total cost for mini cheesecakes = Number of dozens × Cost per dozen = 6 × $55 = $330.

Now, let's add up all the individual costs to find the total amount spent:

- Total cost of donuts: $204
- Total cost of mini cupcakes: $160
- Total cost of mini cheesecakes: $330

Total cost = $204 + $160 + $330 = $794.

Therefore, the total cost of Toula’s purchases at the bakery is **$794**.
