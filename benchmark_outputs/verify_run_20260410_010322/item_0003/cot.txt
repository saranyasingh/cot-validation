EXPLANATION
To determine Josh's profit from flipping the house, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total cost of purchasing the house**: 
   - Cost of buying the house = $80,000

2. **Add the amount spent on repairs**:
   - Total expenses = Cost of buying the house + Amount spent on repairs
   - Total expenses = $80,000 + $50,000 = $130,000

3. **Determine the increase in value due to renovations**:
   - Value increase = Original purchase price * Percentage increase
   - Value increase = $80,000 * 150% (or 1.5)
   - Value increase = $80,000 * 1.5 = $120,000

4. **Calculate the net profit by subtracting the total expenses from the total increase in value**:
   - Net profit = Increase in value - Total expenses
   - Net profit = $120,000 - $130,000 = -$10,000

Therefore, Josh made a loss of $10,000.
