EXPLANATION
To find the original price of the book before any discount, we need to work backwards using the information given about the discounted price.

Step 1: Understand the relationship between the sale price, the discount rate, and the original price.
- Kyle paid $19.50 after a 25% discount on the original price.
- A 25% discount means that Kyle paid 75% (or 3/4) of the original price.

Step 2: Set up an equation to represent this relationship.
Let \( P \) be the original price of the book.
\[ 0.75P = 19.50 \]

Step 3: Solve for \( P \).
\[ P = \frac{19.50}{0.75} \]
\[ P = 26 \]

Therefore, the original price of the book was $26.

The answer is: 26
