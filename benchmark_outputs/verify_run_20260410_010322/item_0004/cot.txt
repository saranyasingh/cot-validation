To determine how many total meters James runs a week, we need to break down the problem into smaller steps.

1. **Calculate the distance for one complete session (one time through all three sprints):**
   - Each sprint is 60 meters.
   - James completes 3 sprints per session.
   \[
   \text{Distance per session} = 3 \times 60 \text{ meters}
   \]

2. **Perform the multiplication:**
   \[
   3 \times 60 = 180 \text{ meters}
   \]
   Therefore, James runs 180 meters in one complete session.

3. **Determine the frequency of his sessions:**
   - James runs this session 3 times a week.

4. **Calculate the total weekly distance:**
   - Multiply the distance per session by the number of sessions per week.
   \[
   \text{Total weekly distance} = 180 \text{ meters/session} \times 3 \text{ sessions/week}
   \]

5. **Perform the multiplication:**
   \[
   180 \times 3 = 540 \text{ meters}
   \]
   Therefore, James runs a total of 540 meters a week.

**Explanation:**  
- We first calculated the distance covered in one session (sprint) by multiplying the distance of one sprint by the number of sprints.
- Then, since James repeats these sessions 3 times a week, we multiplied the distance of one session by 3 to get the total weekly distance.

The answer is: 540
