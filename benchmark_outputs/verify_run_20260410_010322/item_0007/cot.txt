EXPLANATION
Let's break down the problem step-by-step using logical syllogisms.

1. **Premise 1:** "Seattle has 20 sheep."
   - This is our starting point.
   
2. **Premise 2:** "Charleston has 4 times as many sheep as Seattle."
   - From Premise 1, we know Seattle has 20 sheep.
   - Therefore, \( \text{Charleston} = 4 \times \text{Seattle} = 4 \times 20 \).
   - Calculation: \( \text{Charleston} = 80 \) sheep.

3. **Premise 3:** "Toulouse has twice as many sheep as Charleston."
   - From Premise 2, we already know Charleston has 80 sheep.
   - Therefore, \( \text{Toulouse} = 2 \times \text{Charleston} = 2 \times 80 \).
   - Calculation: \( \text{Toulouse} = 160 \) sheep.

4. **Premise 4:** "Together, they have 20 + 80 + 160 = 260 sheep."
   - We now know the number of sheep each city has individually.
   - Summing these values gives us the total number of sheep across all three cities.

5. **Conclusion:** The total number of sheep is 260.

Therefore, the answer is: 260.
