To determine the price of the boots, we need to set up an equation based on the given information.

1. Let \( B \) represent the price of the boots.
2. We know the prices of the two pairs of high heels:
   - One pair costs $33.
   - The other pair costs twice as much, so it costs \( 2 \times 33 = 66 \) dollars.
3. According to the problem, the combined cost of these two pairs of high heels is $5 less than the price of the boots. Therefore, we can write the following equation:
   \[
   33 + 66 = B - 5
   \]

4. Simplify the left side of the equation:
   \[
   99 = B - 5
   \]

5. To solve for \( B \), add 5 to both sides of the equation:
   \[
   99 + 5 = B
   \]
   \[
   104 = B
   \]

Therefore, the boots cost $104.

The answer is: 104
