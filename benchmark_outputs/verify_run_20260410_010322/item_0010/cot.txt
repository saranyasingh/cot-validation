EXPLANATION

To determine Eliza's total earnings for the week, we need to calculate both her regular earnings and her overtime earnings separately.

1. **Calculate Regular Earnings**:
   - Eliza earns $10 per hour for the first 40 hours.
   - Total regular earnings = Hourly rate × Number of hours worked
     \[
     \text{Regular earnings} = \$10/hour \times 40 \text{ hours}
     \]
   - Therefore,
     \[
     \text{Regular earnings} = \$400
     \]

2. **Calculate Overtime Earnings**:
   - For any hours worked beyond the first 40 hours, Eliza earns at a rate of \(1.2\) times her regular hourly rate.
   - First 40 hours: \$400 (already calculated)
   - Overtime hours = Total hours worked - Regular hours
     \[
     \text{Overtime hours} = 45 \text{ hours} - 40 \text{ hours} = 5 \text{ hours}
     \]
   - Overtime earnings = Hourly rate × Overtime hours
     \[
     \text{Overtime earnings} = \$10/hour \times 5 \text{ hours}
     \]
   - Therefore,
     \[
     \text{Overtime earnings} = \$50
     \]

3. **Total Earnings**:
   - Total earnings = Regular earnings + Overtime earnings
     \[
     \text{Total earnings} = \$400 + \$50
     \]
   - Therefore,
     \[
     \text{Total earnings} = \$450
     \]

The answer is: 450
