To determine how much Mishka spent on all the clothing items, we need to calculate the cost of each type of clothing separately and then sum them up.

**Premise 1:** Mishka bought 3 pairs of shorts.
- Cost per pair of shorts = $16.50
- Total cost for shorts = 3 * $16.50 = $49.50

**Premise 2:** Mishka bought 3 pairs of pants.
- Cost per pair of pants = $22.50
- Total cost for pants = 3 * $22.50 = $67.50

**Premise 3:** Mishka bought 3 pairs of shoes.
- Cost per pair of shoes = $42
- Total cost for shoes = 3 * $42 = $126

**Conclusion:** To find the total amount Mishka spent on all the clothing items, we add the total costs of the shorts, pants, and shoes together.
- Total expenditure = $49.50 (shorts) + $67.50 (pants) + $126 (shoes)
- Total expenditure = $243

Therefore, the answer is: 243
