EXPLANATION
To determine how many boxes of pizza Marie ordered, we need to first calculate the total amount spent on the other items she purchased.

1. **Calculate the total cost of the chicken meal:**  
   - Cost per chicken meal = $12
   - Number of chicken meals = 1
   - Total cost of chicken meal = $12 * 1 = $12

2. **Calculate the total cost of the milk packs:**  
   - Cost per pack of milk = $3
   - Number of packs of milk = 5
   - Total cost of milk = $3 * 5 = $15

3. **Calculate the total cost of the apples:**  
   - Cost per apple = $1.50
   - Number of apples = 4
   - Total cost of apples = $1.50 * 4 = $6

Now, let's add up these amounts to find out how much money was spent so far before buying the pizzas.
- Total cost before purchasing pizzas = $12 (chicken meal) + $15 (milk) + $6 (apples)
- Total cost before purchasing pizzas = $33

Next, we subtract this amount from the total payment to find out how much was spent on the pizzas.
- Total payment = $50
- Amount spent on pizzas = $50 - $33 = $17

Finally, we divide the amount spent on pizzas by the price per box of pizza to find out how many boxes were bought.
- Price per box of pizza = $8.50
- Number of boxes of pizza = $\frac{17}{8.50} = 2$

Therefore, Marie ordered 2 boxes of pizza. The answer is: 2.
