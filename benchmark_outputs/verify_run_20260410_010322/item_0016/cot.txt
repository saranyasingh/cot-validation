To determine how much profit the merchant would gain by choosing the jewelry plan over the electronic gadgets plan, we need to calculate the future value of both investments based on their respective growth rates.

**Premise 1:** The current value of the jewelry is $5,000.
- **Premise 2:** The jewelry market will increase by 2.5%.
- **Premise 3:** The current value of the electronic gadgets is $8,000.
- **Premise 4:** The electronic gadgets market will increase by 1.2%.

First, let's calculate the future value of the jewelry after a 2.5% increase:
\[ \text{Future Value of Jewelry} = \$5,000 \times (1 + 0.025) = \$5,000 \times 1.025 = \$5,125 \]

Next, let's calculate the future value of the electronic gadgets after a 1.2% increase:
\[ \text{Future Value of Electronic Gadgets} = \$8,000 \times (1 + 0.012) = \$8,000 \times 1.012 = \$8,096 \]

Now, we find the difference between the future values of the two investments to determine the profit:
\[ \text{Profit} = \text{Future Value of Jewelry} - \text{Future Value of Electronic Gadgets} = \$5,125 - \$8,096 = -\$2,971 \]

Thus, if the merchant makes the choice to buy the jewelry instead of the electronic gadgets, he would incur a loss of $2,971 at the end of the month.
