To solve this problem logically, let's break it down into steps using a syllogistic approach.

### Step 1: Define Variables
Let \( x \) represent the total number of vacuum cleaners Melanie started with.

### Step 2: Analyze Sales at Green House
- Melanie sold a third of her vacuum cleaners at the green house.
- This means she sold \( \frac{x}{3} \).

### Step 3: Calculate Remaining Vacuum Cleaners After Green House Sale
After selling at the green house, the remaining number of vacuum cleaners is:
\[ x - \frac{x}{3} = \frac{2x}{3} \]

### Step 4: Analyze Sales at Red House
- Melanie sold 2 more vacuum cleaners at the red house.
- Therefore, after selling at the red house, the remaining number of vacuum cleaners is:
\[ \frac{2x}{3} - 2 \]

### Step 5: Analyze Sales at Orange House
- At the orange house, she sold half of the remaining vacuum cleaners.
- Thus, she sold \( \frac{1}{2} \left( \frac{2x}{3} - 2 \right) \).

### Step 6: Set Up Equation Based on Given Information
According to the problem, after all these sales, Melanie had 5 vacuum cleaners left. Hence, we set up the equation:
\[ \frac{1}{2} \left( \frac{2x}{3} - 2 \right) + 5 = \frac{2x}{3} \]

### Step 7: Solve the Equation
First, simplify inside the parentheses:
\[ \frac{1}{2} \left( \frac{2x}{3} - 2 \right) + 5 = \frac{2x}{3} \]
\[ \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{2x}{3} - \frac{1}{2} \cdot 2 + 5 = \frac{2x}{3} \]
\[ \frac{x}{3} - 1 + 5 = \frac{2x}{3} \]
\[ \frac{x}{3} + 4 = \frac{2x}{3} \]

Next, subtract \( \frac{x}{3} \) from both sides:
\[ 4 = \frac{x}{3} \]

Finally, multiply both sides by 3:
\[ x = 12 \]

Therefore, Melanie started with **12** vacuum cleaners.
