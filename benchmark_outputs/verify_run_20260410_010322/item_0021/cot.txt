To determine how much water is left after spilling one liter of orange drink, we need to follow these steps:

**Premise 1:** Initially, there are 10 liters of orange drink.
- **Explanation:** This sets up our starting point.

**Premise 2:** Two-thirds of this orange drink is water.
- **Explanation:** We know the composition of the orange drink before any water is added or spilled.

**Premise 3:** There are 15 liters of pineapple drink.
- **Explanation:** This establishes the quantity of pineapple drink available.

**Premise 4:** Three-fifths of this pineapple drink is water.
- **Explanation:** This gives us the amount of water in the pineapple drink.

**Premise 5:** After pouring the orange drink into the pineapple drink, one liter is spilled.
- **Explanation:** This introduces an event (spillage) that affects the total volume but not the proportions of water within the mixtures.

**Premise 6:** The remaining mixture has a total volume of 24 liters.
- **Explanation:** After accounting for the spillage, we calculate the new total volume of the mixed drinks.

**Premise 7:** To find out how much water is in the remaining 24 liters, we need to consider both the initial amounts of water in the orange and pineapple drinks.
- **Explanation:** This leads us to the calculation needed to find the total amount of water.

Now, let's calculate the amount of water initially present in each drink and then sum them up to get the total water content in the remaining mixture.

**Calculation:**

1. **Water in Orange Drink:**
   - Initial volume of orange drink = 10 liters
   - Fraction of orange drink that is water = \(\frac{2}{3}\)
   - Water in orange drink = \(10 \times \frac{2}{3} = \frac{20}{3} \approx 6.67\) liters

2. **Water in Pineapple Drink:**
   - Initial volume of pineapple drink = 15 liters
   - Fraction of pineapple drink that is water = \(\frac{3}{5}\)
   - Water in pineapple drink = \(15 \times \frac{3}{5} = 9\) liters

3. **Total Water Content:**
   - Total water in the remaining 24 liters = Water in orange drink + Water in pineapple drink
   - Total water content = \(6.67 + 9 = 15.67\) liters

Thus, the total amount of water in the remaining 24 liters is approximately 15.67 liters.

**Answer:** The answer is: 15.67
