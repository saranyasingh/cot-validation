EXPLANATION
To determine how many total meters James runs a week, we need to consider both the frequency of his sprints (3 times a week) and the distance he covers per sprint (60 meters).

1. **Frequency of Sprints**: James runs 3 sprints each time.
2. **Distance Per Sprint**: Each sprint is 60 meters.

Since he runs this pattern 3 times a week, we multiply the frequency of sprints by the distance covered per sprint:

\[ \text{Total Meters} = (\text{Number of Sprints}) \times (\text{Distance per Sprint}) \]
\[ \text{Total Meters} = 3 \times 60 \]

Calculating this gives us:

\[ \text{Total Meters} = 180 \]

Therefore, James runs a total of 180 meters a week.
