EXPLANATION
1. First, we need to calculate how much time Carla spends downloading before the restart.
   - She normally downloads at 2 GB per minute.
   - There are 200 GB in total.
   - At 2 GB per minute, the time to download half of the file (100 GB) would be \( \frac{100}{2} = 50 \) minutes.

2. Next, we add the time taken during the restart period.
   - The restart takes 20 minutes.
   
3. Now, we sum up the time spent downloading and restarting.
   - Total time = Downloading time + Restart time
   - Total time = 50 minutes + 20 minutes = 70 minutes
   
4. Finally, we convert this total time into hours since the problem asks for the rate in GB/hour.
   - Time in hours = \(\frac{70}{60}\)
   - Time in hours = 1.1667 hours

Therefore, the answer is: 1.1667
