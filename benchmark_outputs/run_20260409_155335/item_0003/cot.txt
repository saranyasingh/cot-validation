To determine Josh's profit from flipping the house, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total cost of the house:**  
   - Initial purchase price: $80,000
   - Amount spent on repairs: $50,000
   - Total cost = $80,000 + $50,000 = $130,000

2. **Determine the increase in value due to the repairs:**  
   - Value increase percentage: 150%
   - Increase in value = 150% of initial purchase price
   - Increase in value = \( \frac{150}{100} \times \$80,000 = 1.5 \times \$80,000 = \$120,000 \)

3. **Calculate the net profit after adding the repair costs:**  
   - Net profit = Increase in value - Total cost
   - Net profit = \$120,000 - \$130,000 = -$10,000

4. **Interpret the result:**  
   - Since the net profit is negative, it means Josh actually lost money.

**Conclusion:**  
Josh made a loss of $10,000 from flipping the house.

Answer: -10
