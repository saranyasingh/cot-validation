Explanation:
1. John drives for 3 hours at 60 mph.
   - Distance = Speed × Time = 60 mph × 3 hours = 180 miles.

2. John turns around due to forgetting something at home.
   - He has already driven 180 miles away from home.

3. John wants to return within 4 hours total, spending the first 2 hours in traffic.
   - Remaining time available for driving = 4 hours - 2 hours = 2 hours.

4. In the first half-hour (0.5 hour), he drives at 30 mph.
   - Distance covered in 0.5 hours = 30 mph × 0.5 hours = 15 miles.

5. After this, he drives the remaining 2 hours at 80 mph.
   - Distance covered in 2 hours = 80 mph × 2 hours = 160 miles.

6. Total distance traveled after turning around = Initial trip + Traffic delay + Remaining trip.
   - Total distance = 180 miles + 15 miles + 160 miles = 355 miles.

7. Since John started 180 miles away from home initially,
   - Final distance from home = Initial distance + Additional distance traveled.
   - Final distance = 180 miles + 355 miles = 535 miles.

Answer: 535
