EXPLANATION
To determine how much Janet makes every day at the farmers' market, we need to calculate the total number of eggs laid per day, subtract the eggs eaten for breakfast and used to bake muffins, and then multiply by the price per egg.

Step 1: Calculate the total number of eggs laid per day.
Janet's ducks lay 16 eggs per day.

Step 2: Subtract the eggs eaten for breakfast.
Janet eats 3 eggs for breakfast every morning.
Total eggs eaten for breakfast = 3

Step 3: Subtract the eggs used to bake muffins.
Janet uses 4 eggs to bake muffins for her friends every day.
Total eggs used for baking muffins = 4

Step 4: Calculate the remaining eggs after accounting for breakfast and baking.
Remaining eggs = Total eggs - Eggs eaten for breakfast - Eggs used for baking muffins
Remaining eggs = 16 - 3 - 4 = 9

Step 5: Calculate the earnings from selling the remaining eggs.
Each egg is sold for $2.
Earnings from selling remaining eggs = Remaining eggs * Price per egg
Earnings from selling remaining eggs = 9 * $2 = $18

Therefore, Janet makes $18 every day at the farmers' market.
