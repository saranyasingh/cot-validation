To determine how much Janet makes every day at the farmers' market, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the total number of eggs laid per day.
2. Subtract the number of eggs eaten for breakfast.
3. Subtract the number of eggs used to bake muffins for friends.
4. Determine the number of eggs remaining after subtracting the above amounts.
5. Calculate the revenue from selling the remaining eggs.

Let's go through each step:

1. Total eggs laid per day = 16 eggs

2. Eggs eaten for breakfast = 3 eggs

3. Eggs used to bake muffins = 4 eggs

4. Remaining eggs = Total eggs - Eggs eaten for breakfast - Eggs used to bake muffins
                 = 16 - 3 - 4
                 = 9 eggs

5. Revenue from selling the remaining eggs = Number of remaining eggs * Price per egg
                                         = 9 * $2
                                         = $18

Therefore, Janet makes $18 every day at the farmers' market.

The answer is: $18
