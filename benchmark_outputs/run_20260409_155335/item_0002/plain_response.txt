To determine the total number of bolts of fabric required to make a robe, we need to calculate the amount of each color separately and then sum them up.

1. The robe requires 2 bolts of blue fiber.
2. It also requires half as much white fiber as blue fiber. Therefore, the amount of white fiber needed is:
   \[
   \frac{2}{2} = 1 \text{ bolt}
   \]

3. To find the total number of bolts of fabric required, we add the bolts of blue fiber and the bolts of white fiber together:
   \[
   2 + 1 = 3 \text{ bolts}
   \]

Thus, the total number of bolts of fabric needed for the robe is 3. 

The answer is: 3
