EXPLANATION
To determine how many bolts of fabric are needed to make a robe, we need to consider both the blue and white fibers required.

1. **First Premise:** A robe requires 2 bolts of blue fiber.
   - This directly states the quantity of blue fiber needed.

2. **Second Premise:** Half as much white fiber as there is blue fiber is required.
   - From the first premise, we know there are 2 bolts of blue fiber. Therefore, the amount of white fiber needed would be half of this, which is \( \frac{2}{2} = 1 \) bolt of white fiber.

3. **Total Quantity Calculation:**
   - We add the quantities of both types of fiber together to find the total number of bolts used.
     \[
     \text{Total bolts} = \text{Blue fiber} + \text{White fiber}
     \]
     Substituting the values:
     \[
     \text{Total bolts} = 2 \text{ (blue)} + 1 \text{ (white)}
     \]

4. **Final Answer:**
   - By adding these two quantities, we get the total number of bolts needed.
     \[
     \text{Total bolts} = 2 + 1 = 3
     \]

Therefore, the answer is: 3.
