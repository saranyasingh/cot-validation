To calculate Eliza's earnings for the week when she worked 45 hours, we need to break down the calculation into two parts:

1. Calculate the earnings from the first 40 hours at her regular rate.
2. Calculate the earnings from the additional 5 hours (overtime) at her overtime rate.

**Step 1: Earnings from the first 40 hours**

Regular rate = $10/hour
Hours worked at regular rate = 40

Earnings from regular hours = Regular rate × Hours worked
= $10/hour × 40 hours
= $400

**Step 2: Earnings from overtime hours**

Overtime rate = Regular rate × 1.2
= $10/hour × 1.2
= $12/hour

Hours worked at overtime rate = 5

Earnings from overtime hours = Overtime rate × Hours worked
= $12/hour × 5 hours
= $60

**Total Earnings**

Total earnings = Earnings from regular hours + Earnings from overtime hours
= $400 + $60
= $460

Therefore, Eliza's total earnings for this week are **$460**.
