EXPLANATION
To determine Eliza's earnings for the week, we need to break down her work into two parts: her regular hours (the first 40 hours) and her overtime hours (any additional hours beyond 40).

1. **Calculate Regular Earnings:**
   - Eliza's regular hourly rate is $10.
   - She worked for 40 hours at this rate.
   - Regular earnings = Hourly rate × Hours worked
   - Regular earnings = $10/hour × 40 hours = $400

2. **Calculate Overtime Earnings:**
   - Overtime hours are any hours worked beyond the first 40 hours.
   - Eliza worked 45 hours in total, so her overtime hours = Total hours worked - Regular hours
   - Overtime hours = 45 hours - 40 hours = 5 hours
   - Her overtime pay rate is 1.2 times her regular hourly rate.
   - Overtime pay rate = $10/hour × 1.2 = $12/hour
   - Overtime earnings = Overtime pay rate × Overtime hours
   - Overtime earnings = $12/hour × 5 hours = $60

3. **Total Earnings:**
   - To find the total earnings, add the regular earnings and the overtime earnings.
   - Total earnings = Regular earnings + Overtime earnings
   - Total earnings = $400 + $60 = $460

**Conclusion:** Eliza's total earnings for the week are $460.

Answer: 460
