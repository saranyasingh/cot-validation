EXPLANATION

Next, we need to calculate how many of these glasses are discounted (every second glass). Since there are 16 glasses in total, half of them would be discounted. Therefore, there are 8 discounted glasses and 8 non-discounted glasses.

Now, let's find out the price of one discounted glass. Since they are 60% of the original price, we calculate this as follows:
\[ \text{Discounted Price} = \frac{60}{100} \times \$5 = 0.60 \times \$5 = \$3 \]

So, the price for one discounted glass is $3.

Since there are 8 discounted glasses, the total cost for the discounted glasses is:
\[ \text{Total Cost for Discounted Glasses} = 8 \times \$3 = \$24 \]

For the non-discounted glasses, since they all have the same price ($5), the total cost for the non-discounted glasses is:
\[ \text{Total Cost for Non-Discounted Glasses} = 8 \times \$5 = \$40 \]

Finally, we add the total cost of the discounted glasses and the non-discounted glasses together to get the overall amount Kylar needs to pay:
\[ \text{Total Amount} = \$24 + \$40 = \$64 \]

Therefore, Kylar needs to pay a total of $64 for the glasses.
