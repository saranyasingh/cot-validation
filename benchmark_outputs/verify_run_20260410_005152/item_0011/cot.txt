EXPLANATION

1. **First Month**: The program received 60 downloads.
   - Premise 1: A new program had 60 downloads in the first month.

2. **Second Month**: The number of downloads increased to three times that of the first month.
   - Calculation: \(3 \times 60 = 180\) downloads.
   - Premise 2: The number of downloads in the second month was three times as many as the downloads in the first month.

3. **Third Month**: The number of downloads decreased by 30% compared to the second month's count.
   - Calculation: \(180 \times (1 - 0.3) = 180 \times 0.7 = 126\) downloads.
   - Premise 3: Then, it was reduced by 30%.

4. **Total Downloads Over Three Months**:
   - Sum up all the downloads: \(60 + 180 + 126\).
   - Total: \(60 + 180 + 126 = 366\) downloads.

Therefore, the total number of downloads over the three months is 366.

The answer is: 366
