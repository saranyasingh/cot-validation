Explanation:

1. Let's denote the amount of blue fiber as B.
2. According to the problem, B = 2 bolts.
3. Now, let's denote the amount of white fiber as W.
4. We know that W = 0.5 * B.
5. Substituting the value of B from step 2 into this equation, we get W = 0.5 * 2 = 1 bolt.
6. Therefore, the total amount of fiber required is B + W = 2 + 1 = 3 bolts.

Thus, the answer is: 3.
