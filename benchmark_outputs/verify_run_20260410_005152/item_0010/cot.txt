EXPLANATION
To determine Eliza's earnings for the week when she worked 45 hours, we need to break down her total earnings into two parts: her regular earnings (for the first 40 hours) and her overtime earnings.

1. **Calculate Regular Earnings:**
   - Eliza's regular rate per hour is $10.
   - For the first 40 hours, she earns at her regular rate.
   - Therefore, her regular earnings = 40 hours * $10/hour = $400.

2. **Calculate Overtime Earnings:**
   - Eliza worked 45 hours in total, so she worked 5 hours of overtime.
   - Her overtime rate is 1.2 times her regular hourly rate.
   - Therefore, her overtime rate = $10/hour * 1.2 = $12/hour.
   - For the 5 hours of overtime, she earns at her overtime rate.
   - Therefore, her overtime earnings = 5 hours * $12/hour = $60.

3. **Total Earnings:**
   - Total earnings = Regular earnings + Overtime earnings
   - Total earnings = $400 + $60 = $460.

**The answer is:** 460
