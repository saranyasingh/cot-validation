EXPLANATION
To determine Josh's profit after buying the house and making improvements, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total cost of the house:**  
   - Cost of the house = $80,000  
   - Amount spent on repairs = $50,000  
   - Total cost = $80,000 + $50,000 = $130,000  

2. **Determine the increase in the house's value due to the renovations:**  
   - Value increase percentage = 150%  
   - Increase in value = 150% of $130,000  
   - Increase in value = 1.5 * $130,000 = $195,000  

3. **Calculate the actual sale price of the house:**  
   - Sale price before renovation = $130,000 (original cost) + $195,000 (increase) = $325,000  

4. **Calculate the profit made by subtracting the original cost from the sale price:**  
   - Profit = Sale price - Original cost  
   - Profit = $325,000 - $130,000 = $195,000  

Therefore, the profit that Josh made is $195,000.
