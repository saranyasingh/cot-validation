To determine the percentage of students who enrolled in hip-hop dance, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the number of students enrolled in contemporary dance**:
   - Total students = 20
   - Percentage enrolled in contemporary dance = 20%
   \[
   \text{Number of students in contemporary dance} = 20\% \times 20 = 0.20 \times 20 = 4
   \]

2. **Determine the number of students remaining after those enrolled in contemporary dance**:
   - Remaining students = Total students - Students in contemporary dance
   \[
   \text{Remaining students} = 20 - 4 = 16
   \]

3. **Calculate the number of students enrolled in jazz dance**:
   - Percentage enrolled in jazz dance = 25% of the remaining students
   \[
   \text{Number of students in jazz dance} = 25\% \times 16 = 0.25 \times 16 = 4
   \]

4. **Determine the number of students enrolled in hip-hop dance**:
   - Since all other students have already been accounted for (contemporary and jazz), the remaining students are enrolled in hip-hop dance.
   \[
   \text{Number of students in hip-hop dance} = 16 - 4 = 12
   \]

5. **Calculate the percentage of students enrolled in hip-hop dance**:
   - Percentage enrolled in hip-hop dance = (Number of students in hip-hop dance / Total students) × 100%
   \[
   \text{Percentage enrolled in hip-hop dance} = \left(\frac{12}{20}\right) \times 100\% = 0.60 \times 100\% = 60\%
   \]

Thus, the percentage of the entire students enrolled in hip-hop dance is \(60\%\).

The answer is: 60
