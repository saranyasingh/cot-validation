To determine how many total meters James runs per week, we need to break down the problem into smaller parts and then combine them.

1. **Determine the distance for one sprint:**
   - James runs 60 meters each sprint.
   
2. **Calculate the distance for three sprints:**
   - Since he runs 3 sprints per session, we multiply the distance of one sprint by 3.
     \[
     \text{Distance for three sprints} = 60 \text{ meters/sprint} \times 3 \text{ sprints}
     \]
     \[
     \text{Distance for three sprints} = 180 \text{ meters}
     \]

3. **Calculate the total weekly distance:**
   - He runs this distance 3 times a week (once in the morning and once in the afternoon).
     \[
     \text{Total weekly distance} = 180 \text{ meters/session} \times 3 \text{ sessions/week}
     \]
     \[
     \text{Total weekly distance} = 540 \text{ meters}
     \]

Therefore, the answer is: 540.
