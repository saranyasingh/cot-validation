EXPLANATION
To determine how much Janet makes every day at the farmers' market, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the total number of eggs laid per day.
   - Janes ducks lay 16 eggs per day.

2. Subtract the number of eggs eaten for breakfast.
   - She eats 3 eggs for breakfast every morning.
   - Eggs left after breakfast = Total eggs - Eggs eaten for breakfast
     = 16 - 3 = 13 eggs.

3. Subtract the number of eggs used for baking muffins.
   - She uses 4 eggs to bake muffins for her friends every day.
   - Eggs left after baking muffins = Eggs left after breakfast - Eggs used for muffins
     = 13 - 4 = 9 eggs.

4. Calculate the revenue from selling the remaining eggs.
   - Each egg sold at $2 per egg.
   - Revenue from selling remaining eggs = Number of remaining eggs * Price per egg
     = 9 * 2 = $18.

5. Determine the amount Janet makes every day at the farmers' market.
   - The amount made is the revenue from selling the remaining eggs.

Therefore, Janet makes $18 every day at the farmers' market.

The answer is: 18
