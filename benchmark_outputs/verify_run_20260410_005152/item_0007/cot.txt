EXPLANATION

Let's denote the number of sheep in Seattle as S. According to the problem, S = 20.

Next, let's determine how many sheep are in Charleston based on the information given. It states that Charleston has 4 times as many sheep as Seattle. Therefore, we can write:
Charleston Sheep (C) = 4 * S

Substituting the value of S:
C = 4 * 20 = 80

Now, let's calculate how many sheep are in Toulouse. The problem states that Toulouse has twice as many sheep as Charleston. Hence,
Toulouse Sheep (T) = 2 * C

Substituting the value of C:
T = 2 * 80 = 160

To find the total number of sheep among all three cities, we add up their respective counts:
Total Sheep = T + C + S

Substituting the values:
Total Sheep = 160 + 80 + 20 = 260

Therefore, the total number of sheep among Toulouse, Charleston, and Seattle is 260.

The answer is: 260
