EXPLANATION
1. Let's denote the total number of vacuum cleaners Melanie started with as \( V \).

2. According to the problem, after selling some vacuum cleaners at the green house, she had \( \frac{V}{3} \) remaining.

3. Then, she sold 2 more vacuum cleaners at the red house, leaving her with \( \frac{V}{3} - 2 \).

4. After that, she sold half of what was left at the orange house. This means she sold \( \frac{1}{2} \left( \frac{V}{3} - 2 \right) \), which leaves her with \( \frac{1}{2} \left( \frac{V}{3} - 2 \right) \).

5. We know that after these sales, she had 5 vacuum cleaners left. Therefore, we can set up the equation:
   \[
   \frac{1}{2} \left( \frac{V}{3} - 2 \right) = 5
   \]

6. Solving this equation for \( V \):
   \[
   \frac{1}{2} \left( \frac{V}{3} - 2 \right) = 5 \\
   \frac{V}{3} - 2 = 10 \\
   \frac{V}{3} = 12 \\
   V = 36
   \]

7. Thus, Melanie started with 36 vacuum cleaners.

THE ANSWER IS: 36
