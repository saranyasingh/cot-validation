EXPLANATION
To determine how long it will take Carlos to start earning money from his lemon tree, we need to calculate the net profit per year after accounting for both the initial investment and ongoing expenses.

First, let's establish the costs and revenue involved:
- Initial cost to plant the tree: $90
- Annual growth rate: 7 lemons/year
- Selling price per lemon: $1.50
- Annual watering and feeding cost: $3

Next, we'll break down the annual income and expenses:

1. **Annual Growth Revenue**: 
   - Number of lemons grown per year = 7
   - Price per lemon = $1.50
   - Annual growth revenue = \( 7 \times \$1.50 = \$10.50 \)

2. **Annual Expenses**:
   - Watering and feeding cost = $3

Now, we subtract the annual expenses from the annual growth revenue to find the net profit per year:
\[ \text{Net Profit} = \text{Annual Growth Revenue} - \text{Annual Expenses} \]
\[ \text{Net Profit} = \$10.50 - \$3 = \$7.50 \]

This means that each year, Carlos makes a net profit of $7.50 from selling lemons.

Finally, to find out when Carlos will start earning money, we divide the total initial investment by the net profit per year:
\[ \text{Number of Years} = \frac{\text{Total Investment}}{\text{Net Profit per Year}} \]
\[ \text{Number of Years} = \frac{\$90}{\$7.50} = 12 \]

Therefore, it will take Carlos 12 years to start earning money from the lemon tree.

The answer is: 12
