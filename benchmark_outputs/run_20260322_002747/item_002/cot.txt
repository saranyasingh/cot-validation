- Premise 1: The library has a total of T = 360 books.
- Premise 2: There are N = 9 shelves.
- Premise 3: The books are divided equally among the shelves, so there exists a number p such that T = p × N (each shelf has p books).
- Premise 4: From Premise 3, p = T / N.
- Premise 5: Substitute values: p = 360 / 9.
- Premise 6: Since 360 = 9 × 40, we have p = 40.
- Conclusion: Each shelf has p = 40 books.
