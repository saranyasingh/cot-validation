EXPLANATION

1. Gene starts his journey at age 23.
2. He continues to go on vacations until he reaches age 34.
   - Calculation: \(34 - 23 = 11\) years passed.
3. Since he went on four vacations per year, we calculate the total number of vacations as follows:
   - Total vacations = Number of vacations per year \(\times\) Years passed = \(4 \times 11 = 44\)
4. Each vacation results in one unique shirt being used for the quilt.
5. Therefore, the total number of quilt blocks (which correspond to the shirts) is equal to the total number of vacations.

The answer is: 44
