EXPLANATION
To determine how many copies Harald sold, we start by defining variables for the quantities involved. Let \( H \) represent the number of copies Harald sold. According to the problem, Marilyn sold 10 times as many copies as Harald, so we can express this relationship mathematically as \( M = 10H \).

We also know that together, Marilyn and Harald sold 88,000 copies. This gives us another equation: \( M + H = 88,000 \).

Now, substitute the expression for \( M \) into the second equation:
\[ 10H + H = 88,000 \]

Combine like terms:
\[ 11H = 88,000 \]

Solve for \( H \) by dividing both sides by 11:
\[ H = \frac{88,000}{11} \]
\[ H = 8,000 \]

Thus, Harald sold 8,000 copies.

The answer is: 8000
