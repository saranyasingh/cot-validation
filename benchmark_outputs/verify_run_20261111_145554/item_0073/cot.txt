EXPLANATION
1. To find out how much money Tommy raises, we need to calculate the total amount raised from both brownies and cheesecakes.
2. First, let's calculate the revenue from brownies. Since each brownie costs $3, and Tommy sold 43 brownies, we multiply these two numbers together: \( 43 \times 3 = 129 \) dollars.
3. Next, let's calculate the revenue from cheesecakes. Each cheesecake costs $4, and Tommy sold 23 cheesecakes, so we multiply these two numbers together: \( 23 \times 4 = 92 \) dollars.
4. Now that we have calculated the revenue from both items, we add them together to get the total amount of money Tommy raised: \( 129 + 92 = 221 \) dollars.

Therefore, the answer is: 221.
