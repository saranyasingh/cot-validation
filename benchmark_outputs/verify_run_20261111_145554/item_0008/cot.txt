To determine how long it takes Carla to download the 200 GB file after restarting due to the update installation, we need to break down the problem into manageable parts.

1. **Calculate the time Carla spends downloading before the restart:**
   - Carla's normal download speed is 2 GB per minute.
   - To find out how much data she can download in one minute at this speed, we use the formula:
     \[
     \text{Time} = \frac{\text{Data}}{\text{Speed}}
     \]
     Substituting the given values:
     \[
     \text{Time} = \frac{2 \text{ GB}}{2 \text{ GB/min}} = 1 \text{ minute}
     \]

2. **Determine the total amount of data downloaded so far before the restart:**
   - Since Carla downloads 2 GB per minute and it took her 1 minute to download 2 GB:
     \[
     \text{Total Data Downloaded Before Restart} = 2 \text{ GB}
     \]

3. **Calculate the remaining data to be downloaded after the restart:**
   - Total file size: 200 GB
   - Data already downloaded: 2 GB
   - Remaining data:
     \[
     \text{Remaining Data} = 200 \text{ GB} - 2 \text{ GB} = 198 \text{ GB}
     \]

4. **Calculate the new download rate after the restart:**
   - After the restart, Carla needs to start again from scratch. Her initial speed was reduced by 40%, so her new speed is:
     \[
     \text{New Speed} = 2 \text{ GB/min} \times (1 - 0.4) = 2 \text{ GB/min} \times 0.6 = 1.2 \text{ GB/min}
     \]

5. **Determine the time required to download the remaining data:**
   - Using the same formula as before, calculate the time needed to download 198 GB at 1.2 GB per minute:
     \[
     \text{Time} = \frac{198 \text{ GB}}{1.2 \text{ GB/min}} = 165 \text{ minutes}
     \]

6. **Add the time spent downloading before the restart and the time needed to download the remaining data:**
   - Time spent downloading before the restart: 1 minute
   - Time needed to download the remaining data: 165 minutes
   - Total time:
     \[
     \text{Total Time} = 1 \text{ minute} + 165 \text{ minutes} = 166 \text{ minutes}
     \]

Therefore, the total time it takes Carla to download the file after restarting due to the update installation is 166 minutes.

**Answer:** 166
