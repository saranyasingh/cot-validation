To determine how much more revenue the mechanic earned on the day with higher revenue, we need to calculate the total revenue for both days and then find the difference.

**Step 1: Calculate the revenue for Thursday**
- Number of truck tires repaired on Thursday = 6
- Charge per truck tire = $60
- Revenue from truck tires on Thursday = \(6 \times 60 = \$360\)

- Number of car tires repaired on Thursday = 4
- Charge per car tire = $40
- Revenue from car tires on Thursday = \(4 \times 40 = \$160\)

- Total revenue on Thursday = \(360 + 160 = \$520\)

**Step 2: Calculate the revenue for Friday**
- Number of car tires repaired on Friday = 12
- Charge per car tire = $40
- Revenue from car tires on Friday = \(12 \times 40 = \$480\)

- Number of truck tires repaired on Friday = 0 (since none were repaired)
- Revenue from truck tires on Friday = \(0 \times 60 = \$0\)

- Total revenue on Friday = \(480 + 0 = \$480\)

**Step 3: Determine which day had higher revenue**
- Total revenue on Thursday = \$520
- Total revenue on Friday = \$480
- Higher revenue occurred on Thursday

**Step 4: Calculate the difference in revenue between the two days**
- Difference in revenue = \(520 - 480 = \$40\)

**Conclusion:** The mechanic earned $40 more on Thursday compared to Friday.
