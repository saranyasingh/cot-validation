   - Contemporary dancers = 20% of total students
   - Number of contemporary dancers = 0.20 * 20 = 4

2. Next, we'll determine how many students remain after accounting for those enrolled in contemporary dance.
   - Remaining students = Total students - Contemporary dancers
   - Remaining students = 20 - 4 = 16

3. Now, let's calculate how many students enroll in jazz dance.
   - Jazz dancers = 25% of remaining students
   - Number of jazz dancers = 0.25 * 16 = 4

4. Finally, we need to find out what percentage of the entire student body enrolls in hip-hop dance.
   - Hip-hop dancers = Remaining students - Jazz dancers
   - Hip-hop dancers = 16 - 4 = 12
   - Percentage of hip-hop dancers = (Hip-hop dancers / Total students) * 100%
   - Percentage of hip-hop dancers = (12 / 20) * 100% = 60%

Therefore, the percentage of the entire students who enrolled in hip-hop dance is 60%.
