## VOCABULARY
Predicates:
MaterialCost(x, n) — x spent n on material
ConstructionCost(x, n) — x spent n on construction
InsuranceRate(x, r) — x insures the brooch at rate r of its total value
TotalCost(x, n) — x's total cost before insurance is n
TotalPaid(x, n) — x's final total payment is n

Constants:
janet, 500, 800, 0.10, 1300, 130, 1430

## FACTS
FACT-1: Janet spends $500 on the material :: MaterialCost(janet, 500)
FACT-2: Janet pays $800 to have the brooch constructed :: ConstructionCost(janet, 800)
FACT-3: Janet insures the brooch at 10% of its total value :: InsuranceRate(janet, 0.10)

## OPERATIONS
OP-1: 500 + 800 = 1300 :: Sum(500, 800, 1300)
OP-2: 0.10 × 1300 = 130 :: Product(0.10, 1300, 130)

## RULES
RULE-1: If x's material cost is m, construction cost is c, and m + c = t, then x's total cost before insurance is t :: ∀x∀m∀c∀t (MaterialCost(x,m) ∧ ConstructionCost(x,c) ∧ Sum(m,c,t) → TotalCost(x,t))
RULE-2: If x's total cost is t, insurance rate is r, material cost is m, construction cost is c, r × t = i, and m + c + i = p, then x's final total payment is p :: ∀x∀t∀r∀m∀c∀i∀p (TotalCost(x,t) ∧ InsuranceRate(x,r) ∧ MaterialCost(x,m) ∧ ConstructionCost(x,c) ∧ Product(r,t,i) ∧ Sum(m,c,i,p) → TotalPaid(x,p))
