## VOCABULARY
Predicates:
InitialCars(n) — n cars were initially on the motorway
ExitedCars(n) — n cars exited the line
RemainingCars(n) — n cars remained on the motorway after the exit/after 15 minutes
DroveThrough(n) — n cars drove through the traffic jam in the first 15 minutes

Constants:
30, 5, 25

## FACTS
FACT-1: Initially there are 30 cars on the motorway :: InitialCars(30)
FACT-2: After driving through the traffic jam, 5 cars exit the line :: ExitedCars(5)

## OPERATIONS
OP-1: 30 minus 5 is 25 :: Difference(30, 5, 25)
OP-2: 30 minus 25 is 5 :: Difference(30, 25, 5)

## RULES
RULE-1: If there are i initial cars and e cars exit, and i − e = r, then r cars remain :: ∀i∀e∀r (InitialCars(i) ∧ ExitedCars(e) ∧ Difference(i,e,r) → RemainingCars(r))
RULE-2: If there are i initial cars and r cars remain, and i − r = d, then d cars drove through the traffic jam in the first 15 minutes :: ∀i∀r∀d (InitialCars(i) ∧ RemainingCars(r) ∧ Difference(i,r,d) → DroveThrough(d))

## INFERENCES
INF-1: 25 cars remain on the motorway, from FACT-1, FACT-2, OP-1, and RULE-1 by Universal Instantiation and Modus Ponens :: RemainingCars(25)
INF-2: 5 cars drove through the traffic jam in the first 15 minutes, from FACT-1, INF-1, OP-2, and RULE-2 by Universal Instantiation and Modus Ponens :: DroveThrough(5)
