EXPLANATION
1. Let's denote the four schools as A, B, C, and D.
2. Each school sends a girls' team (with 5 players) and a boys' team (also with 5 players), totaling \(5 + 5 = 10\) players per school.
3. Additionally, each school sends a coach for both teams, making it 2 coaches per school.
4. Therefore, for each school, there are \(10 \text{ players} + 2 \text{ coaches} = 12\) individuals.
5. Since this setup applies to all four schools, we multiply the number of individuals per school by the number of schools: \(12 \times 4 = 48\).

The answer is: 48
