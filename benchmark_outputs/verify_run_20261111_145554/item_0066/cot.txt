EXPLANATION
1. Jim spends 2 hours watching TV.
2. After watching TV, he reads for half as long as he spent watching TV. Therefore, he reads for \( \frac{2}{2} = 1 \) hour.
3. In total, for one session (watching TV and reading), Jim spends \( 2 + 1 = 3 \) hours.
4. Jim repeats this process 3 times a week.
5. To find out how many hours he spends on TV and reading in one week, we multiply the total time per session by the number of sessions: \( 3 \times 3 = 9 \) hours.
6. Finally, to find out how many hours he spends over 4 weeks, we multiply the weekly total by 4: \( 9 \times 4 = 36 \) hours.

The answer is: 36
