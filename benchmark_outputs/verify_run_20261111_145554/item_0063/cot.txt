EXPLANATION

1. **Premise 1:** Marcy works for the same company for 40 years.
   - Premise 2 (from Premise 1): Marcy gets an annual pension of $50,000/year.

2. **Premise 3:** After working for 20 years, she becomes entitled to 5% of the value of the pension per year.
   - This implies that starting at age 20, she receives a 5% increase on her original pension amount ($50,000).

3. **Premise 4:** She quits after 30 years.
   - From Premise 2 and Premise 3 combined, we know she has been receiving a 5% increase since age 20.

4. **Conclusion:** Since she quit after 30 years, she would have received this 5% increase over the last 10 years (from age 20 to age 30).
   - Therefore, her annual pension after quitting would be $50,000 plus 5% of $50,000.

5. **Calculation:**  
   \( \text{Annual Pension} = \$50,000 + (0.05 \times \$50,000) \)
   \( \text{Annual Pension} = \$50,000 + \$2,500 \)
   \( \text{Annual Pension} = \$52,500 \)

**The answer is: 52500**
