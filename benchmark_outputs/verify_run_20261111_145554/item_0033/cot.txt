EXPLANATION

To determine how many hours John spends taking care of dogs each week, we need to break down the problem into smaller steps.

First, let's calculate the total time spent walking and caring for one dog per day.
- Each dog takes 0.5 hours to walk and care for its business each day.

Next, we find out how much time this process takes over an entire week (7 days).
- Total daily time = 0.5 hours/day * 7 days/week = 3.5 hours/week

Now that we know the weekly time it takes to take care of one dog, we can calculate the total time for all 10 dogs.
- Total weekly time for 10 dogs = 3.5 hours/week/dog * 10 dogs = 35 hours/week

Therefore, the answer is: 35.
