## VOCABULARY
Predicates:
TimePerDay(x, n) — dog x takes n hours per day to be walked and cared for
DaysPerWeek(n) — there are n days in a week
WeeklyTimePerDog(x, n) — dog x requires n hours of care per week
NumDogs(x, n) — person x has n dogs
TotalWeeklyTime(x, n) — person x spends n hours per week caring for all dogs

Constants:
john, dog, 0.5, 7, 3.5, 10, 35

## FACTS
FACT-1: Each dog takes 0.5 hours to walk and care for its business each day :: TimePerDay(dog, 0.5)
FACT-2: There are 7 days in a week :: DaysPerWeek(7)
FACT-3: John has 10 dogs :: NumDogs(john, 10)

## OPERATIONS
OP-1: 0.5 hours/day times 7 days/week is 3.5 hours/week :: Product(0.5, 7, 3.5)
OP-2: 3.5 hours/week/dog times 10 dogs is 35 hours/week :: Product(3.5, 10, 35)

## RULES
RULE-1: If a dog takes n hours per day and there are d days per week, and n times d equals w, then the dog requires w hours of care per week :: ∀x∀n∀d∀w (TimePerDay(x,n) ∧ DaysPerWeek(d) ∧ Product(n,d,w) → WeeklyTimePerDog(x,w))
RULE-2: If a dog requires w hours of care per week and person x has k dogs, and w times k equals t, then x spends t hours per week caring for all dogs :: ∀x∀y∀w∀k∀t (WeeklyTimePerDog(y,w) ∧ NumDogs(x,k) ∧ Product(w,k,t) → TotalWeeklyTime(x,t))

## INFERENCES
INF-1: Each dog requires 3.5 hours of care per week, from FACT-1, FACT-2, OP-1, and RULE-1 by Universal Instantiation and Modus Ponens :: WeeklyTimePerDog(dog, 3.5)
INF-2: John spends 35 hours per week caring for all his dogs, from INF-1, FACT-3, OP-2, and RULE-2 by Universal Instantiation and Modus Ponens :: TotalWeeklyTime(john, 35)
