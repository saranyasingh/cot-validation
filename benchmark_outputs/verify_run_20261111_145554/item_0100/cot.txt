EXPLANATION

1. **Premise 1**: Mary receives 18 new potted plants yesterday.
   - This establishes the initial quantity of potted plants.

2. **Premise 2**: Mary already has 2 potted plants on each of the 40 window ledges.
   - This provides the total count of potted plants Mary currently owns before receiving any more.

3. **Conclusion**: To find out how many potted plants Mary will have after giving away some, we need to subtract the number of potted plants she gives away from her current total.

4. **Calculation**:
   - Total number of potted plants Mary currently has = \( 2 \times 40 \)
   - Number of potted plants given away = 1 per ledge \(\times\) 40 ledges
   - Remaining potted plants = Current total - Given away

5. **Final Calculation**:
   - Remaining potted plants = \( (2 \times 40) - 40 \)

6. **Simplification**:
   - Remaining potted plants = \( 80 - 40 \)
   - Remaining potted plants = 40

Therefore, the answer is: 40.
