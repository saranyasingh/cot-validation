Explanation:

To determine how much shorter the candle will be after burning from 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM, we need to calculate the total time the candle has been burning and then use the rate at which it melts.

1. **Identify the duration**: From 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM is a span of 4 hours.
   
   - Start time: 1:00 PM
   - End time: 5:00 PM
   
   Duration = End time - Start time = 5:00 PM - 1:00 PM = 4 hours

2. **Determine the melting rate**: The candle melts at a rate of 2 centimeters per hour.

3. **Calculate the total melting**: Multiply the duration (in hours) by the melting rate (in centimeters per hour).

   Total melted = Duration × Melting rate
                  = 4 hours × 2 cm/hour
                  = 8 cm

Therefore, the candle will be 8 centimeters shorter after burning from 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM.

The answer is: 8
