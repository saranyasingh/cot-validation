EXPLANATION
To determine how much Christina will spend on gift bags, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the actual number of guests who will attend.
2. Determine the total number of gift bags needed.
3. Calculate the total cost based on the price per gift bag.

Step 1: Calculate the actual number of guests who will attend.
- Christina invited 16 friends.
- 1/4 of the invited guests don't show up.
- Therefore, 3/4 of the invited guests will attend.
\[ \text{Number of attending guests} = 16 \times \frac{3}{4} = 12 \]

Step 2: Determine the total number of gift bags needed.
- Christina plans to give 0.75 gift bags per guest.
- Number of guests attending = 12.
- Total number of gift bags required = \( 12 \times 0.75 = 9 \)

Step 3: Calculate the total cost.
- Each gift bag costs $2.
- Total cost = \( 9 \times 2 = \$18 \).

Therefore, the amount Christina will spend is: 18
