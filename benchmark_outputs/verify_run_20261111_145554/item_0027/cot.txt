EXPLANATION
To determine how much Mishka spent on all the clothing items, we need to calculate the total cost of each type of item separately and then sum them up.

First, let's find out how much Mishka spent on shorts. We know that one pair of shorts costs $16.50, and she bought 3 pairs. Therefore, the total cost for shorts is calculated as follows:
\[ \text{Total cost of shorts} = 3 \times \$16.50 = \$49.50 \]

Next, we'll calculate the total cost for pants. We know that one pair of pants costs $22.50, and she also bought 3 pairs. Thus, the total cost for pants is:
\[ \text{Total cost of pants} = 3 \times \$22.50 = \$67.50 \]

Finally, we'll calculate the total cost for shoes. We know that one pair of shoes costs $42, and she bought 3 pairs. Hence, the total cost for shoes is:
\[ \text{Total cost of shoes} = 3 \times \$42 = \$126 \]

Now, we add up the total costs of all the items to get the overall expenditure:
\[ \text{Total expenditure} = \$49.50 + \$67.50 + \$126 = \$243 \]

Therefore, Mishka spent a total of $243 on all the clothing items.

The answer is: 243
