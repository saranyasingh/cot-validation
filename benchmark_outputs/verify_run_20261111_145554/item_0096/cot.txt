1. We start with the total number of Grade 5 students, which is 200.
2. Out of these 200 students, \( \frac{2}{5} \) are boys. To find out how many boys there are, we calculate \( \frac{2}{5} \times 200 = 80 \). Therefore, there are 80 boys.
3. This leaves us with \( 200 - 80 = 120 \) girls because the rest must be girls (since all other students are either boys or girls).
4. Now, we know that \( \frac{2}{3} \) of the girls are in the Girl Scout organization. To find out how many girls are in the Girl Scout organization, we calculate \( \frac{2}{3} \times 120 = 80 \). Thus, 80 girls are in the Girl Scout organization.
5. Finally, to find out how many girls are not in the Girl Scout organization, we subtract the number of girls who are in it from the total number of girls. That gives us \( 120 - 80 = 40 \).

Therefore, the answer is: 40.
