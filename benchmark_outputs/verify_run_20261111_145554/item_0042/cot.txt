Let's break this down into a logical syllogism:

1. **Premise 3 Analysis**: Since Polly throws the javelin 400 feet normally (no gemstone), we need to determine what happens when she holds the sapphire gemstone. According to Premise 3, "when Polly holds the sapphire gemstone, she can throw the javelin three times farther than when not holding it." This means if she doesn't hold the gemstone, her maximum throw distance is 400 feet. Holding the gemstone increases this distance by a factor of three.

2. **Calculating Distance with Gemstone**:
   - Without the gemstone: 400 feet
   - With the gemstone: \( 400 \text{ feet} \times 3 = 1200 \text{ feet} \)

Therefore, when Polly holds the sapphire gemstone, she can throw the javelin for a distance of 1200 feet.

3. **Determining Reach Outside the Dragon’s Flames**:
   - The dragon breathes fire up to 1000 feet.
   - To find out how much further Polly can throw the javelin compared to the dragon's reach, subtract the dragon's reach from the javelin's potential throw distance with the gemstone:
     \( 1200 \text{ feet} - 1000 \text{ feet} = 200 \text{ feet} \)

Thus, when Polly stands outside the dragon's flames but still able to throw the javelin with the gemstone, she can do so as long as she is more than 200 feet away from the dragon.

Conclusion: Polly can stand at least 200 feet outside of the reach of the dragon's flames and still hit the dragon with the javelin when she holds the sapphire gemstone.
