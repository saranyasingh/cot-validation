Explanation:
1. First, we need to calculate the total cost of the cell phones before any interest.
   - Sheila bought 5 cell phones at $150 each.
   - Total cost = 5 * $150 = $750.

2. Next, we calculate the total interest based on the given rate (2%) per unit over a period of 3 months.
   - Interest = Number of units * Rate / 100 * Time period in years.
   - Since there are 3 months in a quarter, time period in years = 3/12 = 0.25 years.
   - Interest = 5 * 2 / 100 * 0.25 = $0.25.

3. Now, we add the total cost including interest to get the overall amount paid.
   - Overall amount = Cost + Interest = $750 + $0.25 = $750.25.

4. Finally, we divide this total amount by the number of quarters (3) to find out how much Sheila pays each month.
   - Monthly payment = Overall amount / 3 = $750.25 / 3 ≈ $250.08.
