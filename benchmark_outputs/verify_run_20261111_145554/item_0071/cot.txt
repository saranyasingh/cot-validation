To determine how much money Judy makes in one week, we need to break down the problem into smaller steps.

### Step 1: Calculate the total number of dance classes Judy teaches in one week.
- Judy teaches 5 dance classes daily on weekdays (Monday through Friday).
- She also teaches 8 dance classes on Saturdays.

Total weekday classes = 5 classes/day * 5 days/week = 25 classes/week
Total weekend classes = 8 classes/Saturday * 1 Saturday/week = 8 classes/week

Total classes in one week = Total weekday classes + Total weekend classes
= 25 classes/week + 8 classes/week
= 33 classes/week

### Step 2: Calculate the total number of students who attend all the classes in one week.
Each class has 15 students, so:

Total students = Number of classes * Students per class
= 33 classes/week * 15 students/class
= 495 students/week

### Step 3: Calculate the total amount of money Judy earns in one week.
She charges $15.00 per student, so:

Total earnings = Total students * Price per student
= 495 students * $15.00/student
= $7,425.00

### Conclusion:
The total amount of money Judy makes in one week is $7,425.00.

**Answer:** 7425
