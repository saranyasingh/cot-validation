To determine how many pounds of potato salad Ted needs to bring, we first calculate the total amount consumed by the adults and then by the children.

1. **Calculate the amount of potato salad eaten by adults:**
   - Each adult eats 10 lbs.
   - There are 20 adults.
   \[
   10 \text{ lbs/adult} \times 20 \text{ adults} = 200 \text{ lbs}
   \]

2. **Calculate the amount of potato salad eaten by children:**
   - Each child eats half as much as an adult, which is \( \frac{10}{2} = 5 \) lbs.
   - There are 5 children.
   \[
   5 \text{ lbs/child} \times 5 \text{ children} = 25 \text{ lbs}
   \]

3. **Sum the amounts needed for both adults and children:**
   \[
   200 \text{ lbs (adults)} + 25 \text{ lbs (children)} = 225 \text{ lbs}
   \]

Thus, Ted needs to bring 225 pounds of potato salad to the picnic to ensure everyone gets their share.

**Answer:** 225
