To determine how long it takes Tom to return his journey, we need to calculate the time taken for both parts of his trip (the outbound journey and the return journey).

### Step-by-Step Logical Reasoning:

**Premise 1:** Tom's ship can travel at 10 miles per hour.

This establishes the speed at which Tom can move.

**Premise 2:** Tom sails from location 1 to location 4 between 1 PM and 4 PM.

This specifies the starting point and duration of his outward journey.

**Premise 3:** Tom returns from location 4 to location 1 at a speed of 6 miles per hour.

This details the direction and speed of his return journey.

**Premise 4:** We need to find out the total time taken for the round trip.

**Premise 5:** To find this, we must first calculate the distance traveled during the outbound journey.

Let’s denote the distance from location 1 to location 4 as \( D \) miles.

**Calculation for Outbound Journey:**

\[ \text{Time}_{\text{out}} = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Speed}} \]

Given that Tom’s ship can travel at 10 miles per hour, the time taken for the outbound journey would be:

\[ \text{Time}_{\text{out}} = \frac{D}{10} \]

**Calculation for Return Journey:**

Since the distance is the same (\( D \)) but in reverse direction, the speed changes to 6 miles per hour. Therefore, the time taken for the return journey is:

\[ \text{Time}_{\text{return}} = \frac{D}{6} \]

**Total Time Calculation:**

The total time taken for the round trip is the sum of the times taken for both journeys:

\[ \text{Total Time} = \text{Time}_{\text{out}} + \text{Time}_{\text{return}} \]

Substituting the expressions derived above:

\[ \text{Total Time} = \frac{D}{10} + \frac{D}{6} \]

**Simplifying the Equation:**

To add these fractions, we need a common denominator. The least common multiple of 10 and 6 is 30. Thus:

\[ \text{Total Time} = \frac{3D}{30} + \frac{5D}{30} \]

Combining the terms gives us:

\[ \text{Total Time} = \frac{8D}{30} \]

Simplify further:

\[ \text{Total Time} = \frac{4D}{15} \]

**Conclusion:**

Therefore, the total time taken for Tom to complete his round trip is \( \frac{4D}{15} \) hours.

**Answer:** This expression represents the total time taken for the round trip based on the given distances and speeds.
