## VOCABULARY
Predicates:
HourlyRate(x, a, r) — x earns r dollars per hour for activity a
HoursPerWeek(x, a, h) — x works h hours per week on activity a
WeeksPerYear(x, w) — x works w weeks per year
WeeklyEarning(x, a, e) — x earns e dollars per week from activity a
AnnualEarning(x, a, e) — x earns e dollars per year from activity a

Constants:
jill, teaching, coaching, total, 20, 35, 30, 15, 50, 700, 450, 35000, 22500, 57500

## FACTS
FACT-1: Jill earns $20 per hour teaching :: HourlyRate(jill, teaching, 20)
FACT-2: Jill teaches 35 hours per week :: HoursPerWeek(jill, teaching, 35)
FACT-3: Jill earns $30 per hour coaching :: HourlyRate(jill, coaching, 30)
FACT-4: Jill coaches 15 hours per week :: HoursPerWeek(jill, coaching, 15)
FACT-5: Jill works 50 weeks a year :: WeeksPerYear(jill, 50)

## OPERATIONS
OP-1: $20/hour × 35 hours = $700/week :: Product(20, 35, 700)
OP-2: $30/hour × 15 hours = $450/week :: Product(30, 15, 450)
OP-3: $700/week × 50 weeks = $35,000/year :: Product(700, 50, 35000)
OP-4: $450/week × 50 weeks = $22,500/year :: Product(450, 50, 22500)
OP-5: $35,000 + $22,500 = $57,500 :: Sum(35000, 22500, 57500)

## RULES
RULE-1: If x earns r per hour for activity a and works h hours per week on a, and r×h=e, then x earns e per week from a :: ∀x∀a∀r∀h∀e (HourlyRate(x,a,r) ∧ HoursPerWeek(x,a,h) ∧ Product(r,h,e) → WeeklyEarning(x,a,e))
RULE-2: If x earns e per week from activity a and works w weeks per year, and e×w=p, then x earns p per year from a :: ∀x∀a∀e∀w∀p (WeeklyEarning(x,a,e) ∧ WeeksPerYear(x,w) ∧ Product(e,w,p) → AnnualEarning(x,a,p))
RULE-3: If x earns e1 per year from teaching and e2 per year from coaching, and e1+e2=s, then x earns s per year in total :: ∀x∀e1∀e2∀s (AnnualEarning(x,teaching,e1) ∧ AnnualEarning(x,coaching,e2) ∧ Sum(e1,e2,s) → AnnualEarning(x,total,s))

## INFERENCES
