EXPLANATION
To determine Jill's annual salary, we need to calculate her earnings from both teaching and coaching separately and then sum them up.

First, let's calculate her weekly earnings from teaching:
- She earns $20 per hour.
- She teaches 35 hours per week.

Weekly earning from teaching = $20/hour × 35 hours/week = $700/week

Next, let's calculate her weekly earnings from coaching:
- She earns $30 per hour.
- She coaches 15 hours per week.

Weekly earning from coaching = $30/hour × 15 hours/week = $450/week

Now, let's find out how much she earns annually from teaching:
- She works 50 weeks a year.
- Her weekly earning from teaching is $700.

Annual earning from teaching = Weekly earning from teaching × Number of weeks per year
= $700/week × 50 weeks/year = $35,000/year

Similarly, let's find out how much she earns annually from coaching:
- Her weekly earning from coaching is $450.
- She works 50 weeks a year.

Annual earning from coaching = Weekly earning from coaching × Number of weeks per year
= $450/week × 50 weeks/year = $22,500/year

Finally, let's add the two amounts together to get her total annual salary:
Total annual salary = Annual earning from teaching + Annual earning from coaching
= $35,000 + $22,500 = $57,500

Therefore, Jill's annual salary is $57,500. The answer is: 57500.
