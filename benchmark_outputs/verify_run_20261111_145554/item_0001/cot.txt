To determine how much Janet makes every day at the farmers' market, we need to follow these steps:

**Step 1:** Calculate the total number of eggs laid per day.
- Janet's ducks lay 16 eggs per day.

**Step 2:** Determine the number of eggs eaten for breakfast.
- Janet eats 3 eggs for breakfast every morning.

**Step 3:** Calculate the remaining eggs after breakfast consumption.
- Remaining eggs = Total eggs - Eggs eaten for breakfast
- Remaining eggs = 16 - 3 = 13 eggs

**Step 4:** Subtract the eggs used for baking muffins.
- Janet uses 4 eggs for baking muffins.

**Step 5:** Find out how many eggs are left after baking muffins.
- Remaining eggs after baking = Remaining eggs - Eggs used for baking
- Remaining eggs after baking = 13 - 4 = 9 eggs

**Step 6:** Calculate the earnings from selling the remaining eggs.
- Janet sells the remaining eggs at $2 per egg.

**Step 7:** Compute the total earnings.
- Earnings = Number of eggs sold × Price per egg
- Earnings = 9 × 2 = $18

**Explanation:**
We started with the total number of eggs laid (16) and subtracted the eggs eaten for breakfast (3). Then, we accounted for the eggs used for baking muffins (4), leaving us with 9 eggs. Finally, since each egg was sold for $2, we multiplied the number of eggs by the price to get the total earnings of $18.

The answer is: 18
