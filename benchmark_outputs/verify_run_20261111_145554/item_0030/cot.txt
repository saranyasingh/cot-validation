To determine the cost of the boots, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Identify the given information:**
   - One pair of heels costs $33.
   - Another pair of heels costs twice as much as the first pair.

2. **Calculate the cost of the second pair of heels:**
   \[
   \text{Cost of second pair} = 2 \times 33 = 66 \text{ dollars}
   \]

3. **Determine the total cost of both pairs of heels:**
   \[
   \text{Total cost of heels} = 33 + 66 = 99 \text{ dollars}
   \]

4. **Set up an equation based on the condition provided:**
   According to the problem, the combined cost of the two pairs of heels is $5 less than the cost of the boots.

5. **Let \( B \) represent the cost of the boots:**
   Therefore,
   \[
   \text{Boots cost} = B
   \]
   
6. **Express the relationship mathematically:**
   \[
   B = 99 + 5 = 104 \text{ dollars}
   \]

7. **Conclusion:**
   The boots cost $104.

**Explanation:**

- We started by identifying all relevant information about the prices of the shoes.
- Next, we calculated the individual price of the second pair of heels using the given ratio.
- Then, we determined the total cost of both pairs of heels.
- Finally, we set up an equation relating the total cost of the heels to the boots' cost, solving it to find the boots' price.

Thus, the answer is: 104
