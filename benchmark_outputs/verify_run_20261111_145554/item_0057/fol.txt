## VOCABULARY
Predicates:
Budget(x, n) — x's total weekly entertainment budget is n dollars
CostPerVisit(x, n) — the cost of one movie session (ticket + popcorn) for x is n dollars
Visits(x, n) — x can make n movie visits with the budget

Constants:
peter, 42, 7, 6

## FACTS
FACT-1: Peter's total weekly budget for entertainment is $42 :: Budget(peter, 42)
FACT-2: The cost of one movie session (ticket + popcorn) for Peter is $7 :: CostPerVisit(peter, 7)

## OPERATIONS
OP-1: 42 divided by 7 is 6 :: Quotient(42, 7, 6)

## RULES
RULE-1: If x has a budget b, the cost per visit is c, and b ÷ c = v, then x can make v visits :: ∀x∀b∀c∀v (Budget(x,b) ∧ CostPerVisit(x,c) ∧ Quotient(b,c,v) → Visits(x,v))

## INFERENCES
INF-1: Peter can make 6 movie visits, from FACT-1, FACT-2, OP-1, and RULE-1 by Universal Instantiation and Modus Ponens :: Visits(peter, 6)
