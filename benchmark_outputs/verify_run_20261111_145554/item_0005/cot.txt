EXPLANATION

To determine how many cups of feed Wendi needs to give her chickens in the final meal of the day, we first need to calculate the total amount of feed given to all chickens over the course of an entire day.

1. **Total feed given per day:**  
   Wendi gives her chickens 15 cups of feed in the morning and 25 cups in the afternoon.
   
   \[
   15 + 25 = 40 \text{ cups}
   \]

2. **Number of chickens:**  
   Wendi has 20 chickens.

3. **Feed per chicken per day:**  
   Each chicken gets fed 3 cups of mixed feed daily.

4. **Total feed needed per day for all chickens:**  
   To find out how much feed is needed for all chickens, multiply the total daily feed (40 cups) by the number of chickens (20).

   \[
   40 \times 20 = 800 \text{ cups}
   \]

5. **Cups of feed in the final meal:**  
   Since Wendi divides the total daily feed into three equal parts throughout the day, we divide the total daily feed by 3 to get the amount of feed given during the final meal.

   \[
   \frac{800}{3} \approx 266.67 \text{ cups}
   \]

Therefore, Wendi needs approximately **266.67** cups of feed to give her chickens in the final meal of the day.

The answer is: 266.67
