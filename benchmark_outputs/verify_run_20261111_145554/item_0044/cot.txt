To determine how many grams of chips you can eat while staying within your daily calorie limit, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total number of calories in the entire bag of chips**.
   - Each serving contains 250 calories.
   - There are 5 servings in a 300g bag.
   \[
   \text{Total calories} = 250 \, \text{calories/serving} \times 5 \, \text{serving} = 1250 \, \text{calories}
   \]

2. **Determine the remaining calories needed to meet the daily goal**.
   - Your daily calorie target is 2000 calories.
   - You have already consumed 1800 calories.
   \[
   \text{Remaining calories} = 2000 \, \text{calories} - 1800 \, \text{calories} = 200 \, \text{calories}
   \]

3. **Convert the remaining calories into grams of chips**.
   - Since 1250 calories corresponds to 300g (the entire bag), calculate the weight corresponding to 200 calories.
   - First, find out how much 1 gram represents in terms of calories:
     \[
     \text{Calories per gram} = \frac{1250 \, \text{calories}}{300 \, \text{grams}} = \frac{125}{30} \approx 4.17 \, \text{calories/gram}
     \]
   - Now, convert 200 calories to grams:
     \[
     \text{Grams of chips} = \frac{200 \, \text{calories}}{4.17 \, \text{calories/gram}} \approx 47.96 \, \text{grams}
     \]

Therefore, you can eat approximately 47.96 grams of chips while staying within your daily calorie limit.
