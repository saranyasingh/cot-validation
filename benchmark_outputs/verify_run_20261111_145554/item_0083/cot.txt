To solve this problem, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Identify the given information:**
   - Grace's weight = 125 pounds.

2. **Calculate Alex's weight based on the given relationship:**
   - According to the problem, Alex weighs 2 pounds less than 4 times what Grace weighs.
   - First, calculate 4 times Grace's weight:
     \[
     4 \times 125 = 500 \text{ pounds}
     \]
   - Next, subtract 2 pounds from this result to find Alex's weight:
     \[
     500 - 2 = 498 \text{ pounds}
     \]

3. **Combine the weights of Grace and Alex:**
   - Add Grace's weight and Alex's weight together:
     \[
     125 + 498 = 623 \text{ pounds}
     \]

### Explanation:
- We started by identifying Grace's weight as the base value (125 pounds).
- Then, we used the relationship provided in the problem to calculate Alex's weight. This involved multiplying Grace's weight by 4 and then subtracting 2 pounds.
- Finally, we added both weights to get the total combined weight.

Therefore, the answer is:
**The answer is: 623**
