EXPLANATION
To determine how many more hours Harry slept compared to James, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate how much time James slept.
   - James slept \(\frac{2}{3}\) of what Harry slept.
   - Since Harry slept 9 hours, we calculate James' sleep as \(9 \times \frac{2}{3} = 6\) hours.

2. Find out how many more hours Harry slept than James.
   - Harry slept 9 hours.
   - James slept 6 hours.
   - Therefore, Harry slept \(9 - 6 = 3\) hours more than James.

THE ANSWER IS: 3
