Predicates:
Planted(x, n) — x planted n rose bushes
RosesPerBush(x, n) — each of x's rose bushes has n roses
ThornsPerRose(x, n) — each of x's roses has n thorns
Total(x, n) — x has a total of n of something (roses or thorns, determined by context)

dan, 3, 25, 8, 75, 600

## RULES
RULE-1: If x planted n bushes with m roses per bush and n × m = p, then x has a total of p roses :: ∀x∀n∀m∀p (Planted(x,n) ∧ RosesPerBush(x,m) ∧ Product(n,m,p) → Total(x,p))
RULE-2: If x has a total of p roses with t thorns per rose and p × t = q, then x has a total of q thorns :: ∀x∀p∀t∀q (Total(x,p) ∧ ThornsPerRose(x,t) ∧ Product(p,t,q) → Total(x,q))
