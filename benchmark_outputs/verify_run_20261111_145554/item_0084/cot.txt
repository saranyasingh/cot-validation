Explanation:
1. First, we need to determine how many roses Dan has in total.
   - He planted 3 rose bushes.
   - Each rose bush has 25 roses.

   Therefore, the total number of roses is calculated as follows:
   \[
   3 \text{ bushes} \times 25 \text{ roses per bush} = 75 \text{ roses}
   \]

2. Next, we need to find out how many thorns are on all these roses combined.
   - Each rose has 8 thorns.

   To find the total number of thorns, we multiply the total number of roses by the number of thorns per rose:
   \[
   75 \text{ roses} \times 8 \text{ thorns per rose} = 600 \text{ thorns}
   \]

Therefore, the total number of thorns is 600.

The answer is: 600
