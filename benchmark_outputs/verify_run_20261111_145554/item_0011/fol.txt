RULE-2: If month y's downloads are reduced by fraction r compared to month x (with n being x's downloads), and r*n=d, and n-d=p, then month y's downloads are p :: ∀x∀y∀n∀r∀d∀p (Downloads(x,n) ∧ ReducedBy(y,x,r) ∧ Product(r,n,d) ∧ Difference(n,d,p) → Downloads(y,p))
RULE-3: If the downloads in three months x, y, z are n, m, p respectively, and n+m+p=t, then the total downloads over the three-month period is t :: ∀x∀y∀z∀n∀m∀p∀t (Downloads(x,n) ∧ Downloads(y,m) ∧ Downloads(z,p) ∧ Sum(n,m,p,t) → TotalDownloads(three_months,t))
