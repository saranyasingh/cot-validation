To solve this problem logically, let's break it down into steps.

**Step 1:** Calculate the number of downloads in the second month.
- The number of downloads in the second month was three times as many as the downloads in the first month.
- First month's downloads = 60
- Second month's downloads = 3 * 60 = 180

**Step 2:** Calculate the number of downloads in the third month.
- In the third month, the number of downloads was reduced by 30% compared to the second month.
- Reduction amount = 30% of 180 = (30/100) * 180 = 54
- Third month's downloads = 180 - 54 = 126

**Step 3:** Sum up all the downloads over the three months.
- Total downloads = First month + Second month + Third month
- Total downloads = 60 + 180 + 126 = 366

Therefore, the total number of downloads over the three months is **366**.
