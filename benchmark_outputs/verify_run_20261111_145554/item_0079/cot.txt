To determine how much Vincent saves by buying 18 flowers at the better price, we need to compare the cost of purchasing these flowers using both package options.

**Premise 1:** Vincent buys 18 flowers.
- This establishes our starting point and the total quantity of flowers needed.

**Premise 2:** Vincent can buy flowers in two different ways:
   - Packages of 3 for $2.50 each.
   - Packages of 2 for $1 each.
- These are the only available choices given.

**Premise 3:** We need to find out which option costs less per flower when purchasing 18 flowers.
- This comparison helps us decide on the more economical choice.

**Step-by-step Logical Reasoning:**

1. **Calculate the cost if Vincent buys all 18 flowers as packages of 3:**
   - Cost per package of 3 = $2.50
   - Number of packages needed = Total flowers / Flowers per package
     \[
     \text{Number of packages} = \frac{18}{3} = 6
     \]
   - Total cost = Number of packages * Cost per package
     \[
     \text{Total cost} = 6 \times \$2.50 = \$15.00
     \]

2. **Calculate the cost if Vincent buys all 18 flowers as packages of 2:**
   - Cost per package of 2 = $1
   - Number of packages needed = Total flowers / Flowers per package
     \[
     \text{Number of packages} = \frac{18}{2} = 9
     \]
   - Total cost = Number of packages * Cost per package
     \[
     \text{Total cost} = 9 \times \$1 = \$9.00
     \]

3. **Compare the total costs:**
   - Cost of buying 18 flowers as packages of 3: $15.00
   - Cost of buying 18 flowers as packages of 2: $9.00
   
4. **Determine the savings:**
   - Savings = Total cost of first option - Total cost of second option
     \[
     \text{Savings} = \$15.00 - \$9.00 = \$6.00
     \]

**Conclusion:** By buying 18 flowers at the better price (packages of 2), Vincent saves $6.00.

Therefore, the answer is: 6
