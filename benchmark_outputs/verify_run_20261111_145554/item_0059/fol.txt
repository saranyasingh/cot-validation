## VOCABULARY
Predicates:
InitialBill(x, n) — x's initial bill amount is n
FeePercent(x, n) — x is charged a fee of n (as a decimal fraction) on the bill
DeliveryFee(x, n) — x is charged a delivery fee of n
Tip(x, n) — x pays a tip of n
Total(x, n) — x's running total (bill, adjusted total, or final total) is n

Constants:
stephen, 40, 0.25, 10, 50, 3, 47, 4, 51

FACT-1: Stephen's initial bill is $40.00 :: InitialBill(stephen, 40)
FACT-2: Stephen is charged a 25% fee on the bill :: FeePercent(stephen, 0.25)

## RULES
RULE-1: If x's initial bill is n, x's fee percent is p, and p times n equals f, and n plus f equals t, then x's total is t :: ∀x∀n∀p∀f∀t (InitialBill(x,n) ∧ FeePercent(x,p) ∧ Product(p,n,f) ∧ Sum(n,f,t) → Total(x,t))
RULE-2: If x's total is t, x's delivery fee is d, and t minus d equals a, then x's total is a :: ∀x∀t∀d∀a (Total(x,t) ∧ DeliveryFee(x,d) ∧ Difference(t,d,a) → Total(x,a))
RULE-3: If x's total is a, x's tip is g, and a plus g equals s, then x's total is s :: ∀x∀a∀g∀s (Total(x,a) ∧ Tip(x,g) ∧ Sum(a,g,s) → Total(x,s))
