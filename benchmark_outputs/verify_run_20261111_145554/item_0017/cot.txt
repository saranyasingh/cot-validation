Explanation:

Let's break down the problem into its components and apply logical reasoning to find the solution.

**Premise 1:** Two trains leave San Rafael at the same time.
- This establishes the starting point and timing of the journey.

**Premise 2:** Both trains travel westward for 80 miles on the first day.
- This specifies the initial direction and distance traveled by each train during their first leg of the trip.

**Premise 3:** On the second day, both trains travel northward for 150 miles.
- This describes the direction and additional distance traveled by each train after their initial westward journey.

To calculate the total distance covered by each train over the two days, we need to add up all the distances traveled in both directions.

**Step-by-step Calculation:**

1. **First Day (Westward):**
   - Distance traveled = 80 miles
   - Total distance for this day = 80 miles

2. **Second Day (Northward):**
   - Distance traveled = 150 miles
   - Total distance for this day = 150 miles

3. **Total Distance Covered:**
   - Sum of the distances from both days = 80 miles + 150 miles
   - Total distance covered by each train = 230 miles

Therefore, the distance covered by each train in the two days is 230 miles.

**Answer:** 230
