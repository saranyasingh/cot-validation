## VOCABULARY
Predicates:
TravelsWest(x, n) — x travels westward n miles on the first day
TravelsNorth(x, n) — x travels northward n miles on the second day
TotalDistance(x, n) — x covers a total distance of n miles over the two days

Constants:
train, 80, 150, 230

## FACTS
FACT-1: Each train travels westward 80 miles on the first day :: TravelsWest(train, 80)
FACT-2: Each train travels northward 150 miles on the second day :: TravelsNorth(train, 150)

## OPERATIONS
OP-1: 80 miles plus 150 miles is 230 miles :: Sum(80, 150, 230)

## RULES
RULE-1: If x travels westward n miles on day one and northward m miles on day two, and n + m = s, then x covers a total distance of s miles over the two days :: ∀x∀n∀m∀s (TravelsWest(x,n) ∧ TravelsNorth(x,m) ∧ Sum(n,m,s) → TotalDistance(x,s))

## INFERENCES
INF-1: Each train covers a total distance of 230 miles over the two days, from FACT-1, FACT-2, OP-1, and RULE-1 by Universal Instantiation and Modus Ponens :: TotalDistance(train, 230)
