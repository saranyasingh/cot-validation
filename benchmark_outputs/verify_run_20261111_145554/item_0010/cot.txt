EXPLANATION
To determine Eliza's total earnings for the week, we need to break down her work into two parts: her regular working hours (first 40 hours) and her overtime hours (the remaining 5 hours).

1. **Calculate the earnings for the first 40 hours:**
   - Regular hourly rate = $10/hour
   - Earnings for the first 40 hours = 40 hours * $10/hour = $400

2. **Calculate the overtime earnings:**
   - Overtime rate = 1.2 times the regular hourly rate = 1.2 * $10 = $12/hour
   - Overtime hours = 5 hours
   - Earnings for overtime = 5 hours * $12/hour = $60

3. **Add the earnings from both parts to get the total earnings:**
   - Total earnings = Earnings for the first 40 hours + Earnings for overtime
   - Total earnings = $400 + $60 = $460

Therefore, Eliza's total earnings for the week amount to $460.

THE ANSWER IS: 460
