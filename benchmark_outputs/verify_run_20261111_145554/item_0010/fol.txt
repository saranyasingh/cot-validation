RULE-4: If x earns a from regular work and b from overtime and a+b=s, then x's total earnings are s :: ∀x∀a∀b∀s (Earnings(x,regular,a) ∧ Earnings(x,overtime,b) ∧ Sum(a,b,s) → Earnings(x,total,s))
