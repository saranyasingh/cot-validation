EXPLANATION

To determine how many Lego sets John still has after selling them and purchasing video games, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the total amount of money John earned from selling the Legos.
2. Determine the total cost of the video games John bought.
3. Subtract the cost of the video games from the money earned to find out how much money was left over.
4. Use the remaining money to calculate how many more Legos John could have sold if he had not purchased the video games.

Step 1: Calculate the total amount of money John earned from selling the Legos.
John has 13 Lego sets and sells each for $15.
\[ \text{Total earnings} = 13 \times 15 = \$195 \]

Step 2: Determine the total cost of the video games John bought.
He buys 8 video games at $20 each.
\[ \text{Cost of video games} = 8 \times 20 = \$160 \]

Step 3: Subtract the cost of the video games from the money earned to find out how much money was left over.
\[ \text{Money left} = 195 - 160 = \$35 \]

Step 4: Use the remaining money to calculate how many more Legos John could have sold if he had not purchased the video games.
Since John only needs to cover the cost of the video games and has $35 left, he would have been able to sell an additional set of Legos if he hadn't spent any on video games.
Therefore, the number of additional Legos he could have sold is equal to the number of video games he bought.
\[ \text{Additional Legos} = 8 \]

So, the number of Legos John still has is:
\[ \text{Remaining Legos} = 13 - 8 = 5 \]

Thus, the answer is: 5.
