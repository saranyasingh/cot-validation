EXPLANATION
Let's break down the problem step-by-step.

1. **Initial Investment**: Josh buys the house for $80,000.
2. **Repairs Cost**: He spends $50,000 on repairs.
3. **Total Initial Investment**: Adding these together gives us $80,000 (initial cost) + $50,000 (repairs) = $130,000 total initial investment.
4. **Value Increase**: The house increases in value by 150% after repairs.
   - To calculate this increase, we multiply the original value ($130,000) by 150% (or 1.5).
   - Calculation: $130,000 * 1.5 = $195,000.
5. **Final Value of House**: After the repairs, the house is worth $195,000.
6. **Profit Calculation**: Profit is calculated as the difference between the final value of the house and the total initial investment.
   - Calculation: $195,000 (final value) - $130,000 (total initial investment) = $65,000 profit.

Therefore, the answer is: 65000.
