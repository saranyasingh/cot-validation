EXPLANATION
To determine the total number of bolts of fiber needed to make a robe, we need to calculate how much white fiber is required first.

1. **Identify the amount of blue fiber**: The problem states that a robe requires 2 bolts of blue fiber.
   
   Premise 1: A robe requires 2 bolts of blue fiber.

2. **Determine the amount of white fiber**: According to the information provided, a robe needs half as much white fiber as it does blue fiber.

   Calculation: Half of 2 bolts = \( \frac{2}{2} = 1 \) bolt of white fiber.

3. **Sum up the total number of bolts**: Add the bolts of blue fiber to the bolts of white fiber.

   Total bolts = Blue fiber + White fiber  
   Total bolts = 2 + 1  
   Total bolts = 3

Therefore, the total number of bolts of fiber needed to make the robe is 3.

The answer is: 3
