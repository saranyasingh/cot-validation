## VOCABULARY
Predicates:
RequiresBlue(x, n) — robe x requires n bolts of blue fiber
RequiresWhite(x, n) — robe x requires n bolts of white fiber
RequiresTotal(x, n) — robe x requires n bolts of fiber in total

Constants:
robe, 2, 1, 3

## FACTS
FACT-1: A robe requires 2 bolts of blue fiber :: RequiresBlue(robe, 2)

## OPERATIONS
OP-1: Half of 2 is 1 :: Quotient(2, 2, 1)
OP-2: 2 plus 1 is 3 :: Sum(2, 1, 3)

## RULES
RULE-1: If a robe requires n bolts of blue fiber and n divided by 2 is m, then the robe requires m bolts of white fiber :: ∀x∀n∀m (RequiresBlue(x,n) ∧ Quotient(n,2,m) → RequiresWhite(x,m))
RULE-2: If a robe requires n bolts of blue fiber and m bolts of white fiber, and n + m = p, then the robe requires p bolts of fiber in total :: ∀x∀n∀m∀p (RequiresBlue(x,n) ∧ RequiresWhite(x,m) ∧ Sum(n,m,p) → RequiresTotal(x,p))

## INFERENCES
INF-1: The robe requires 1 bolt of white fiber, from FACT-1, OP-1, and RULE-1 by Universal Instantiation and Modus Ponens :: RequiresWhite(robe, 1)
INF-2: The robe requires 3 bolts of fiber in total, from FACT-1, INF-1, OP-2, and RULE-2 by Universal Instantiation and Modus Ponens :: RequiresTotal(robe, 3)
