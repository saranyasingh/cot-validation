\[ \text{Current Speed} = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Time}} = \frac{6 \text{ miles}}{3 \text{ hours}} = 2 \text{ miles per hour} \]

\[ \text{Average Speed} = \frac{\text{Total Distance}}{\text{Total Time}} \]

\[ 4 = \frac{12}{\text{Total Time}} \]

\[ \text{Remaining Time} = \frac{12 - 6}{4} = \frac{6}{4} = 1.5 \text{ hours} \]

Thus, to achieve an average speed of 4 miles per hour over the entire 12-mile trail, Marissa needs to cover the remaining 6 miles in 1.5 hours. Converting this into a speed:

\[ v = \frac{\text{Remaining Distance}}{\text{Remaining Time}} = \frac{6 \text{ miles}}{1.5 \text{ hours}} = 4 \text{ miles per hour} \]
