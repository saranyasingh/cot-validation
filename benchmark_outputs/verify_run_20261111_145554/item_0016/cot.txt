To determine the profit maximization scenario, we need to consider the growth rates of both markets over time.

**Premise 1:** The jewelry market will grow by 2.5% within the same month.
- Jewelry value at the start: $5,000
- Growth rate: 2.5%

**Premise 2:** The electronic gadgets market will grow by 1.2% within the same month.
- Electronic gadgets value at the start: $8,000
- Growth rate: 1.2%

Let's calculate the future values of both items using their respective growth rates.

**Step-by-step calculation for jewelry:**
1. Calculate the increase in value due to growth:
   \[
   \text{Increase} = \text{Initial Value} \times \text{Growth Rate}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Increase}_{\text{jewelry}} = \$5,000 \times 0.025 = \$125
   \]

2. Add the increase to the initial value to find the future value:
   \[
   \text{Future Value}_{\text{jewelry}} = \text{Initial Value} + \text{Increase}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Future Value}_{\text{jewelry}} = \$5,000 + \$125 = \$5,125
   \]

**Step-by-step calculation for electronic gadgets:**
1. Calculate the increase in value due to growth:
   \[
   \text{Increase} = \text{Initial Value} \times \text{Growth Rate}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Increase}_{\text{gadgets}} = \$8,000 \times 0.012 = \$96
   \]

2. Add the increase to the initial value to find the future value:
   \[
   \text{Future Value}_{\text{gadgets}} = \text{Initial Value} + \text{Increase}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Future Value}_{\text{gadgets}} = \$8,000 + \$96 = \$8,096
   \]

Now, let's compare the future values to decide which item yields more profit.

**Profit Calculation:**

- Profit from jewelry:
  \[
  \text{Profit}_{\text{jewelry}} = \text{Future Value}_{\text{jewelry}} - \text{Initial Value}
  \]
  \[
  \text{Profit}_{\text{jewelry}} = \$5,125 - \$5,000 = \$125
  \]

- Profit from electronic gadgets:
  \[
  \text{Profit}_{\text{gadgets}} = \text{Future Value}_{\text{gadgets}} - \text{Initial Value}
  \]
  \[
  \text{Profit}_{\text{gadgets}} = \$8,096 - \$8,000 = \$96
  \]

**Conclusion:**
By choosing to invest in the jewelry market, the merchant would achieve a higher profit at the end of the month compared to investing in electronic gadgets.

Therefore, the answer is: 125
