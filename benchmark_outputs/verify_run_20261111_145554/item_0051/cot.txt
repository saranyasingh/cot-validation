1. First, we need to calculate how many dozens of eggs Lloyd's chickens produce per day.
   - Since there are 12 eggs in a dozen, we divide the total number of eggs by 12.
     \( \frac{252}{12} = 21 \) dozens per day.

2. Next, we calculate the daily revenue from selling these eggs at $2 per dozen.
   - We multiply the number of dozens by the price per dozen.
     \( 21 \times 2 = 42 \) dollars per day.

3. To find out how much Lloyd makes in a week, we multiply his daily earnings by the number of days in a week (7).
   - We then perform this calculation.
     \( 42 \times 7 = 294 \) dollars per week.

Therefore, the answer is: 294
