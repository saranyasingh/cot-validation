## VOCABULARY
Predicates:
EggsPerDay(x, n) — x's chickens produce n eggs per day
DozensPerDay(x, n) — x's chickens produce n dozens of eggs per day
PricePerDozen(x, n) — x sells eggs at n dollars per dozen
EarnsPerDay(x, n) — x earns n dollars per day
EarnsPerWeek(x, n) — x earns n dollars per week

Constants:
lloyd, 252, 12, 2, 7, 21, 42, 294

## FACTS
FACT-1: Lloyd's chickens produce 252 eggs per day :: EggsPerDay(lloyd, 252)
FACT-2: Lloyd sells eggs at 2 dollars per dozen :: PricePerDozen(lloyd, 2)

## OPERATIONS
OP-1: 252 divided by 12 is 21 :: Quotient(252, 12, 21)
OP-2: 21 times 2 is 42 :: Product(21, 2, 42)
OP-3: 42 times 7 is 294 :: Product(42, 7, 294)

## RULES
RULE-1: If x's chickens produce e eggs per day and e ÷ 12 = d, then x's chickens produce d dozens of eggs per day :: ∀x∀e∀d (EggsPerDay(x,e) ∧ Quotient(e,12,d) → DozensPerDay(x,d))
RULE-2: If x produces d dozens of eggs per day and sells at p dollars per dozen, and d × p = r, then x earns r dollars per day :: ∀x∀d∀p∀r (DozensPerDay(x,d) ∧ PricePerDozen(x,p) ∧ Product(d,p,r) → EarnsPerDay(x,r))
RULE-3: If x earns r dollars per day and r × 7 = w, then x earns w dollars per week :: ∀x∀r∀w (EarnsPerDay(x,r) ∧ Product(r,7,w) → EarnsPerWeek(x,w))

## INFERENCES
INF-1: Lloyd's chickens produce 21 dozens of eggs per day, from FACT-1, OP-1, and RULE-1 by Universal Instantiation and Modus Ponens :: DozensPerDay(lloyd, 21)
