## VOCABULARY
Predicates:
TimesAsOld(x, y, n) — x's iPhone is n times as old as y's iPhone
AgeOf(x, n) — x's iPhone is n years old

Constants:
brandon, ben, suzy, 4, 2, 1, 8

## FACTS
FACT-1: Brandon's iPhone is four times as old as Ben's iPhone :: TimesAsOld(brandon, ben, 4)
FACT-2: Ben's iPhone is two times older than Suzy's iPhone :: TimesAsOld(ben, suzy, 2)
FACT-3: Suzy's iPhone is 1 year old :: AgeOf(suzy, 1)

## OPERATIONS
OP-1: 2 times 1 is 2 :: Product(2, 1, 2)
OP-2: 4 times 2 is 8 :: Product(4, 2, 8)

## RULES
RULE-1: If x's iPhone is n times as old as y's iPhone, y's iPhone is a years old, and n × a = b, then x's iPhone is b years old :: ∀x∀y∀n∀a∀b (TimesAsOld(x,y,n) ∧ AgeOf(y,a) ∧ Product(n,a,b) → AgeOf(x,b))

## INFERENCES
INF-1: Ben's iPhone is 2 years old, from FACT-2, FACT-3, OP-1, and RULE-1 by Universal Instantiation and Modus Ponens :: AgeOf(ben, 2)
INF-2: Brandon's iPhone is 8 years old, from FACT-1, INF-1, OP-2, and RULE-1 by Universal Instantiation and Modus Ponens :: AgeOf(brandon, 8)
