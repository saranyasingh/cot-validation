EXPLANATION

1. Let's denote the initial amount of money Bailey had as \( B \).

2. Bailey received a weekly allowance of $5 for 8 weeks. Therefore, the total amount of money she earned from her allowance over these 8 weeks is calculated as:
   \[
   5 \text{ dollars/week} \times 8 \text{ weeks} = 40 \text{ dollars}
   \]

3. After receiving her allowance, Bailey's total amount of money is the sum of her initial amount (\( B \)) plus the amount she earned from her allowance (40 dollars). According to the problem, this total amount at the end of the 8 weeks is $100. Thus, we have:
   \[
   B + 40 = 100
   \]

4. To find out how much money Bailey started with (\( B \)), we need to solve for \( B \) in the equation above:
   \[
   B = 100 - 40
   \]
   \[
   B = 60
   \]

Therefore, Bailey started with $60.

The answer is: 60
