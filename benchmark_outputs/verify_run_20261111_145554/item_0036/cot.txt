EXPLANATION
1. Mike played ping pong for a total of 40 minutes.
   Premise: Mike's total playing time is given as 40 minutes.

2. For the first 20 minutes, Mike scored 4 points.
   Premise: According to the problem statement, Mike scored 4 points during the first 20 minutes.

3. During the next 20 minutes (second half), Mike scored 25% more points than what he scored in the first half.
   Calculation: First calculate 25% of 4 points: \( 4 \times 0.25 = 1 \) point.
   Conclusion: Therefore, Mike scored an additional 1 point during the second 20 minutes.

4. To find the total points scored, add the points from both halves of his game:
   Total Points = Points in first half + Points in second half
                 = 4 + 1
                 = 5

THE ANSWER IS: 5
