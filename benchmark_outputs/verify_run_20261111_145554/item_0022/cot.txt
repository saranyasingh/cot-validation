To determine how many years ago Raymond's son was born, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Determine Raymond's birth year:**  
   - Since Samantha is currently 31 years old, and she was born when her father Raymond was 23 (as per the information given), we calculate Raymond's birth year as:
     \[
     \text{Samantha's current age} = \text{Raymond's current age} + 6 \quad (\text{since Raymond was born 6 years before Samantha})
     \]
     Therefore,
     \[
     31 = \text{Raymond's current age} + 6 \implies \text{Raymond's current age} = 31 - 6 = 25
     \]

2. **Calculate the time difference between Raymond and Samantha:**  
   - Since they were both born 6 years apart, the total time elapsed since their births is:
     \[
     \text{Time elapsed} = \text{Samantha's current age} - \text{Raymond's current age}
     \]
     Substituting the known values:
     \[
     \text{Time elapsed} = 31 - 25 = 6 \text{ years}
     \]

3. **Determine the birth year of Raymond's son:**  
   - Given that Raymond's son was born 23 years after his own age, we find:
     \[
     \text{Birth year of Raymond's son} = \text{Raymond's current age} + 23
     \]
     Substituting the value we found earlier:
     \[
     \text{Birth year of Raymond's son} = 25 + 23 = 48
     \]

Thus, Raymond's son was born 48 years ago.
