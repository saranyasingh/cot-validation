Explanation:
To determine how many oranges are good, we need to calculate the total number of unripe and sour oranges first, as these make up 30% (1 + 2 = 3) of the total oranges.

Step 1: Calculate the number of unripe oranges.
20% of 25 oranges are unripe.
Number of unripe oranges = 25 * 20/100 = 5 oranges.

Step 2: Calculate the number of sour oranges.
2 out of the 25 oranges are sour.
Number of sour oranges = 2 oranges.

Step 3: Add the number of unripe and sour oranges together.
Total unripe and sour oranges = 5 + 2 = 7 oranges.

Step 4: Subtract the total number of unripe and sour oranges from the total number of oranges to find the number of good oranges.
Number of good oranges = Total oranges - Total unripe and sour oranges
Number of good oranges = 25 - 7 = 18 oranges.

Therefore, the number of good oranges is 18.

The answer is: 18
