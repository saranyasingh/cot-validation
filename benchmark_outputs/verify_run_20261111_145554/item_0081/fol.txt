## VOCABULARY
Predicates:
Total(w, n) — the total quantity of water w is n liters
SharePortion(x, w, d) — x's share of water w is 1/d of the total
Got(x, n) — x received n liters of water
Used(w, n) — a cumulative total of n liters of water w has been accounted for/used
Remaining(w, n) — water w has n liters remaining

## RULES
RULE-1: If the total of water w is n liters and x's share is 1/d of it, and n ÷ d = m, then x got m liters :: ∀x∀w∀n∀d∀m (Total(w,n) ∧ SharePortion(x,w,d) ∧ Quotient(n,d,m) → Got(x,m))
RULE-2: If x got n1 liters and y got n2 liters, and n1 + n2 = n3, then n3 liters of water w have been used :: ∀x∀y∀w∀n1∀n2∀n3 (Got(x,n1) ∧ Got(y,n2) ∧ Sum(n1,n2,n3) → Used(w,n3))
RULE-3: If n1 liters of water w have been used and x got n2 more liters, and n1 + n2 = n3, then n3 liters of water w have been used :: ∀x∀w∀n1∀n2∀n3 (Used(w,n1) ∧ Got(x,n2) ∧ Sum(n1,n2,n3) → Used(w,n3))
RULE-4: If the total of water w is n liters and u liters have been used, and n − u = r, then r liters of water w remain :: ∀w∀n∀u∀r (Total(w,n) ∧ Used(w,u) ∧ Difference(n,u,r) → Remaining(w,r))
