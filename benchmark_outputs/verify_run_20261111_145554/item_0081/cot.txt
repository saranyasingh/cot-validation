EXPLANATION
Let's break down the problem step-by-step using a logical syllogism.

**Premise 1:** Two girls each got 1/6 of the 24 liters of water.
- This means that each girl received \( \frac{1}{6} \times 24 = 4 \) liters of water.
- Therefore, together, they received \( 4 + 4 = 8 \) liters of water.

**Premise 2:** A boy then got 6 liters of water.
- Adding this to the total we calculated so far gives us \( 8 + 6 = 14 \) liters of water accounted for.

**Premise 3:** Subtracting the amount of water used from the initial amount leaves us with \( 24 - 14 = 10 \) liters of water remaining.

Therefore, the answer is: 10 liters of water were left.
