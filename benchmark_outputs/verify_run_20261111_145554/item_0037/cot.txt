EXPLANATION

1. Terry consumes 2 yogurts per day.
   - This establishes our daily consumption rate.

2. Over 30 days, Terry would consume:
   - 2 yogurts/day * 30 days = 60 yogurts

3. At the current price of 4 yogurts for $5.00,
   - We need to determine how many sets of 4 yogurts Terry buys to get 60 yogurts.
   - 60 yogurts / 4 yogurts/set = 15 sets

4. Each set costs $5.00,
   - Therefore, the total cost for 15 sets is:
     15 sets * $5.00/set = $75.00

The answer is: 75
