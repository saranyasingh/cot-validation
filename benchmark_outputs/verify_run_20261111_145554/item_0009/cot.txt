EXPLANATION

1. **First leg of the journey**: John drives for 3 hours at a speed of 60 mph.
   - Distance = Speed × Time = 60 mph × 3 hours = 180 miles.

2. **Second leg of the journey**: John returns home, which takes him 4 hours total (including the 2 hours spent in traffic).
   - Since he has already driven 180 miles out, he needs to cover another 180 miles to return home.
   - However, he spends the first 2 hours in traffic, so he only drives for 2 hours after that.
   - During these 2 hours, he travels at 30 mph.
   - Distance covered in the second leg = Speed × Time = 30 mph × 2 hours = 60 miles.

3. **Total distance traveled**: Adding both legs together gives us the total distance John has traveled.
   - Total distance = Outbound trip + Return trip = 180 miles + 60 miles = 240 miles.

4. **Distance from home**: Since he started from home and drove 240 miles away, his position relative to home is 240 miles away.
   
Therefore, the answer is: 240.
