EXPLANATION
To determine the total cost of grooming John's dog after applying the discount, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the amount of the discount.
2. Subtract the discount from the original price.

Step 1: Calculate the discount amount
The original price of grooming is $100. The discount offered is 30%. To find out how much this discount amounts to, we multiply the original price by the discount rate (expressed as a decimal):
$100 * 0.30 = $30

So, the discount amount is $30.

Step 2: Subtract the discount from the original price
Now that we know the discount amount ($30), we subtract it from the original price of $100 to get the discounted price:
$100 - $30 = $70

Therefore, the total cost of grooming after applying the discount is $70.

The answer is: 70
