EXPLANATION
To determine Sylvie's annual salary after three more years of service, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Identify the initial salary**: According to the problem, the initial monthly salary is $600.

2. **Calculate the increase per year**: Each employee receives a 10% raise on their current salary at the end of each year. This means that if the current salary is S, the next year's salary will be \( S + 0.10S = 1.10S \).

3. **Determine the salary after four years**:
   - After the first year, the salary becomes \( 600 \times 1.10 = 660 \).
   - After the second year, the salary becomes \( 660 \times 1.10 = 726 \).
   - After the third year, the salary becomes \( 726 \times 1.10 = 800 \).
   - After the fourth year, the salary becomes \( 800 \times 1.10 = 880 \).

4. **Apply the fifth-year increment**: Since Sylvie has been with the company for five years, she gets another 10% raise based on her new salary of $880.
   
   - Her new salary after this raise would be \( 880 \times 1.10 = 968 \).

Therefore, Sylvie's annual salary after three more years of service (i.e., six years total) is $968.

The answer is: 968
