EXPLANATION
1. Jean starts with 30 lollipops.
2. Jean eats 2 lollipops out of these 30.
   - Calculation: 30 - 2 = 28 lollipops left.
3. Jean wants to package 2 lollipops per bag.
4. To find out how many bags Jean can fill, we need to divide the total number of lollipops by the number of lollipops per bag.
5. Calculation: 28 ÷ 2 = 14 bags.

Therefore, the answer is: 14.
