## VOCABULARY
Predicates:
Eats(x, n) — x eats n servings of ice cream per day
Period(x, n) — x considers a period of n days
CartonSpec(s, c) — each carton contains s servings and costs c dollars
Consumes(x, n) — x consumes n servings of ice cream in total over the period
Spends(x, n) — x spends n dollars in total

Constants:
cynthia, 1, 60, 15, 4.00, 16.00, 4

## FACTS
FACT-1: Cynthia eats one serving of ice cream per night (per day) :: Eats(cynthia, 1)
FACT-2: Cynthia considers a period of 60 days :: Period(cynthia, 60)
FACT-3: Each carton contains 15 servings and costs $4.00 :: CartonSpec(15, 4.00)

## OPERATIONS
OP-1: 60 days × 1 serving per day = 60 servings :: Product(60, 1, 60)
OP-2: 60 servings ÷ 15 servings per carton = 4 cartons :: Quotient(60, 15, 4)
OP-3: 4 cartons × $4.00 per carton = $16.00 :: Product(4, 4.00, 16.00)

## RULES
RULE-1: If x eats n servings per day over a period of d days, and d × n = t, then x consumes t servings in total :: ∀x∀n∀d∀t (Eats(x,n) ∧ Period(x,d) ∧ Product(d,n,t) → Consumes(x,t))
RULE-2: If x consumes t servings in total, each carton contains s servings and costs c dollars, t ÷ s = k, and k × c = m, then x spends m dollars in total :: ∀x∀t∀s∀c∀k∀m (Consumes(x,t) ∧ CartonSpec(s,c) ∧ Quotient(t,s,k) ∧ Product(k,c,m) → Spends(x,m))

## INFERENCES
INF-1: Cynthia consumes 60 servings in total, from FACT-1, FACT-2, OP-1, and RULE-1 by Universal Instantiation and Modus Ponens :: Consumes(cynthia, 60)
INF-2: Cynthia spends $16.00 in total, from INF-1, FACT-3, OP-2, OP-3, and RULE-2 by Universal Instantiation and Modus Ponens :: Spends(cynthia, 16.00)
