EXPLANATION
To determine how much Cynthia will spend on ice cream after 60 days, we need to calculate the total number of servings she consumes over that period and then multiply it by the price per serving.

First, let's find out how many servings Cynthia eats in one day. Since she eats one serving every night, in one day she consumes 1 serving.
Next, we need to determine how many servings she consumes in 60 days. This can be calculated as follows:
Servings consumed in 60 days = Number of days × Servings per day
= 60 × 1
= 60 servings

Now, we know that each carton contains 15 servings and costs $4.00. To find out how many cartons she needs to buy, we divide the total number of servings by the number of servings per carton:
Cartons needed = Total servings ÷ Servings per carton
= 60 ÷ 15
= 4 cartons

Finally, to find out how much she spends on these cartons, we multiply the number of cartons by the cost per carton:
Total spending = Cartons × Cost per carton
= 4 × $4.00
= $16.00

Therefore, Cynthia will spend $16.00 on ice cream after 60 days. The answer is: 16
