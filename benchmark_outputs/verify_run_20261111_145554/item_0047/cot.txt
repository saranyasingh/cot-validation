To determine how many post-it notes were in the package Candice purchased, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Initial Count**: Candice started with 80 post-it notes.
2. **Post-it Notes Placed at Work**: She placed 220 post-it notes on the cups of coffee.

Let's denote the total number of post-it notes purchased as \( P \).

### Step-by-Step Reasoning:

1. **Total Post-it Notes Used**:
   - Initial post-it notes: 80
   - Post-it notes used at work: 220
   
   Therefore, the total number of post-it notes used is:
   \[
   80 + 220 = 300
   \]

2. **Remaining Post-it Notes**:
   - Candice had 23 post-it notes remaining after placing them on the cups of coffee.

3. **Calculating the Total Number of Post-it Notes Purchased**:
   - Let \( P \) represent the total number of post-it notes purchased.
   
   According to the problem statement, the sum of the initial post-it notes (80), the post-it notes used at work (220), and the remaining post-it notes (23) must equal the total number of post-it notes purchased (\( P \)).

4. **Setting Up the Equation**:
   \[
   80 + 220 + 23 = P
   \]

5. **Solving the Equation**:
   \[
   80 + 220 + 23 = P \\
   323 = P
   \]

Thus, the total number of post-it notes in the package that Candice purchased was 323.

The answer is: 323
