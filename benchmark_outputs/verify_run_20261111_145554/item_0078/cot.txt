EXPLANATION

1. Let's start by defining the variables based on the information given:
   - Let \( D \) represent the amount of laundry done by David.
   - According to the problem, Sarah does 4 times as much laundry as David. Therefore, we have:
      \[
      S = 4D
      \]
     where \( S \) represents the amount of laundry done by Sarah.

2. We know that Sarah does 400 pounds of laundry. Using this information, we can find out how much laundry David did:
   \[
   S = 400 \text{ pounds}
   \]

3. Now, using the relationship between Sarah's and David's laundry amounts:
   \[
   4D = 400 \text{ pounds}
   \]

4. Solving for \( D \):
   \[
   D = \frac{400}{4} = 100 \text{ pounds}
   \]

5. Next, let's determine how much laundry Raymond does:
   - Raymond does half as much laundry as Sarah. Thus:
      \[
      R = \frac{S}{2} = \frac{400}{2} = 200 \text{ pounds}
      \]

6. Finally, to find the difference in the amount of laundry done by Raymond and David:
   \[
   \text{Difference} = R - D = 200 \text{ pounds} - 100 \text{ pounds} = 100 \text{ pounds}
   \]

CONCLUSION

The answer is: 100
