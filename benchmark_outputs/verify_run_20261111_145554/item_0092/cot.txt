Explanation:
1. Let's start by defining the variables:
   - Let \( C \) represent the number of pets Cindy has.
   - According to the problem, \( C = 4 \).

2. Next, we know that Marcia has two more pets than Cindy:
   - Therefore, \( M = C + 2 \).
   - Substituting the value of \( C \), we get \( M = 4 + 2 = 6 \).

3. Jan has three times the number of pets as Marcia:
   - Thus, \( J = 3M \).
   - Substituting the value of \( M \), we get \( J = 3 \times 6 = 18 \).

4. Now, let's calculate the total number of pets:
   - Total pets = Pets of Jan + Pets of Marcia + Pets of Cindy
   - Total pets = \( J + M + C \)
   - Substituting the values, we get \( \text{Total} = 18 + 6 + 4 \)

5. Performing the addition:
   - \( \text{Total} = 18 + 6 + 4 = 28 \)
