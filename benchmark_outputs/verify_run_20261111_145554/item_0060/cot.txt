EXPLANATION
To determine the total number of raspberries on the bush, we need to consider both the clustered fruits and the individual fruits.

1. **Clusters of Raspberries**: There are 6 clusters, and each cluster contains 20 fruits.
   - Number of fruits in clusters = \( 6 \text{ clusters} \times 20 \text{ fruits/cluster} = 120 \text{ fruits} \).

2. **Individual Fruits**: In addition to the clustered fruits, there are 67 individual fruits scattered across the bush.
   - Total number of fruits = Number of fruits in clusters + Individual fruits.
     \[
     120 \text{ (from clusters)} + 67 \text{ (individual)} = 187 \text{ fruits}
     \]

**Conclusion**: The total number of raspberries on the bush is 187.

Therefore, the answer is: 187
