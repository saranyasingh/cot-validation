EXPLANATION
1. We start with the initial length of wire Tracy has, which is 4 feet.
2. To convert this length into inches, we know that 1 foot = 12 inches.
3. Therefore, 4 feet * 12 inches/foot = 48 inches.
4. The wire needs to be cut into pieces that are 6 inches long.
5. To find out how many 6-inch pieces can be obtained from 48 inches, we divide 48 by 6.
6. 48 ÷ 6 = 8.

Therefore, the answer is: 8.
