Explanation:

1. Let's denote the first friend's guess as \( G_1 \). According to the problem, \( G_1 = 80 \).

2. The second friend says "20 more than half the first one." This means we need to calculate half of \( G_1 \) and then add 20.
   - Half of \( G_1 \): \( \frac{G_1}{2} = \frac{80}{2} = 40 \)
   - Adding 20 to this result: \( 40 + 20 = 60 \)

3. The third friend says "25% more than the first one." To find out what this means, we need to calculate 25% of \( G_1 \) and then add it to \( G_1 \).
   - 25% of \( G_1 \): \( 0.25 \times 80 = 20 \)
   - Adding this to \( G_1 \): \( 80 + 20 = 100 \)

4. Now that we have the guesses from all three friends:
   - First friend: \( G_1 = 80 \)
   - Second friend: \( G_2 = 60 \)
   - Third friend: \( G_3 = 100 \)

5. To find the average guess, we sum these values and divide by the number of guesses (which is 3):
   \[
   \text{Average guess} = \frac{G_1 + G_2 + G_3}{3} = \frac{80 + 60 + 100}{3}
   \]

6. Performing the addition inside the parentheses:
   \[
   80 + 60 + 100 = 240
   \]

7. Dividing by 3:
   \[
   \frac{240}{3} = 80
   \]

Therefore, the average guess is: 80.
