EXPLANATION
To determine the total number of bolts of fiber needed to make a robe, we first need to calculate how much white fiber is required.

1. **Determine the amount of white fiber**: According to the problem, the robe requires half as much white fiber as blue fiber.
   - Given: Blue fiber = 2 bolts
   - Therefore, White fiber = 0.5 * (Blue fiber) = 0.5 * 2 = 1 bolt

2. **Calculate the total number of bolts**: To find the total number of bolts used, add the blue fiber to the white fiber.
   - Total bolts = Blue fiber + White fiber = 2 + 1 = 3 bolts

**Conclusion**
The total number of bolts of fiber needed to make the robe is 3 bolts.

Therefore, the answer is: 3
